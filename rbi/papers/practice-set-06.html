A bat and a ball together cost Rs. 100. The bat costs Rs. 20 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?
A. Rs. 40
B. Rs. 50
C. Rs. 60
D. Rs. 70
Correct Answer: A
Let's say the ball costs x. The bat costs x + 20. The total cost is x + (x + 20) = 100. Simplifying the equation, we get 2x + 20 = 100. Subtracting 20 from both sides, we get 2x = 80. Dividing both sides by 2, we get x = 40.
