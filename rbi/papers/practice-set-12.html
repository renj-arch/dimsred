Q1. Reasoning
A and B are siblings. C is the father of B. D is the mother of A. E is the son of D. Who is E to C?
A. Son
B. Grandson
C. Brother
D. Nephew
Correct Answer: A
D is the mother of A, so D is also the mother of B (since A and B are siblings). C is the father of B, so C is married to D. E is the son of D, which makes E the son of C.

Q8. English Language

B. B) stuck with the bill
C. C) stuck in a rut
D. D) stuck for an answer
Correct Answer: B

'Stuck in a rut' implies boredom/lack of progress, which contradicts the sudden financial crisis described. 5. The other options do not fit the context of being unable to pay debts due to a specific action.

Q10. Reasoning
In a certain code language, words are encoded based on the sum of their digits. The rule is: If a number's digit-sum (repeatedly adding digits until a single digit is obtained) is odd, write the letter corresponding to that sum (A=1, B=2... Z=26). If even, write the word 'EVE'. Example: '7' -> 7 (Odd) -> G '8' -> 8 (Even) -> EVE '5' -> 5 (Odd) -> E '4' -> 4 (Even) -> EVE What is the code for the number 29?
A. A
B. I
C. EVE
D. K
Correct Answer: C
Step 1: Sum digits of 29 = 2 + 9 = 11. Step 2: Since 11 > 9, repeat process: 1 + 1 = 2. Step 3: Final single digit is 2. Step 4: 2 is Even. Step 5: Apply rule for even -> Code is 'EVE'.
