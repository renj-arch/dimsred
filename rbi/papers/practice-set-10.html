Q5. [+1, -0.25]
Monthly Premium made by India citizen at 18 years of age, under Atal Pension Yojana, opting for Rs. 5000 monthly pension after attaining 60 years of age, is Rs. ______.
A. 185
B. 210
C. 225
D. 305
E. 324
Correct Answer: B
Q6. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
In a certain code, 'FLOWER' is written as '649537' and 'WONDER' is written as '539278'. How is 'REFLOW' coded?
A. 729645
B. 785649
C. 789645
D. 789465
Correct Answer: C
By comparing the letters and their corresponding codes in 'FLOWER' and 'WONDER', we can deduce the code for each letter. R=7, E=8, F=6, L=4, O=9, W=5. Therefore, 'REFLOW' would be coded as 789645.
Q7. [+1, -0.25]
As per the Migration & Development brief Report, in the fourth quarter of 2023, the global average costs of sending USD 200 continued to be high at 6.4 per cent, 0.2 percentage points higher than the previous year. SDG 10.C underscores the commitment of countries to reducing the transaction costs associated with migrant remittances to less than _____. This target aims to establish a global average for sending USD 200.
A. 5%
B. 3.5%
C. 3%
D. 2.5%
E. 1%
Correct Answer: C

D. 9, 55

Q10. [+1, -0.25]

B. Rs 5 lakh
C. Rs 15 lakh
D. Rs 3 lakh
E. Rs 2 lakh
Correct Answer: B
Q12. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
If it takes 5 machines 5 minutes to make 5 widgets, how long would it take 100 machines to make 100 widgets?
A. 5 minutes
B. 50 minutes
C. 100 minutes
D. 500 minutes
Correct Answer: A
If 5 machines can make 5 widgets in 5 minutes, then 1 machine can make 1 widget in 5 minutes. Therefore, 100 machines can make 100 widgets in 5 minutes.
Q13. [+1, -0.25]

C. Thisam Phanit
D. Tsukhenyie
E. None of these
