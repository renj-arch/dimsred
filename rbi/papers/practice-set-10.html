Q7. Quantitative Aptitude
A shopkeeper buys 100 units of a product at Rs. 10 each and sells 50 units at a 20% profit. What is the selling price of the remaining 50 units if the shopkeeper wants to make an overall profit of 30%?
A. Rs. 11
B. Rs. 12
C. Rs. 13
D. Rs. 14
Correct Answer: C
The shopkeeper buys 100 units at Rs. 10 each, so the total cost is Rs. 1000. The shopkeeper sells 50 units at a 20% profit, so the selling price is Rs. 12 per unit, and the total revenue is Rs. 600. To make an overall profit of 30%, the total revenue should be Rs. 1300. So, the selling price of the remaining 50 units should be Rs. 1300 - Rs. 600 = Rs. 700, or Rs. 14 per unit. However, since the shopkeeper can only sell the remaining units at the same price as the first 50 units or higher, the minimum selling price is Rs. 13 per unit.

Q10. Quantitative Aptitude
A person invests Rs. 1000 in a savings account that earns an annual interest rate of 5%. How much will the person have in the account after 2 years?
A. Rs. 1102.50
B. Rs. 1105
C. Rs. 1110
D. Rs. 1120
Correct Answer: A
The interest earned in the first year is Rs. 1000 * 5% = Rs. 50. The total amount after the first year is Rs. 1000 + Rs. 50 = Rs. 1050. The interest earned in the second year is Rs. 1050 * 5% = Rs. 52.50. The total amount after the second year is Rs. 1050 + Rs. 52.50 = Rs. 1102.50.
