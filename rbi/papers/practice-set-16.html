A. RuPay debit card
B. Overdraft facility up to Rs. 5,000
C. Insurance cover of Rs. 30,000 for accidental death
D. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) facility
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Recall the standard features of PMJDY – it offers RuPay card, basic insurance and DBT, but overdraft is a later add‑on, not automatic. B. Large manufacturing units with turnover above Rs. 500 crore
C. Export‑oriented units with foreign exchange earnings above Rs. 100 crore
D. All scheduled commercial banks irrespective of borrower type
Correct Answer: A

Q3. General Awareness
As per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016, and subsequent notifications issued by the Central Government, what is the current minimum amount of default required for a creditor to initiate the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) against a corporate debtor?
A. A. ₹1 Lakh
B. B. ₹10 Lakh
C. C. ₹1 Crore
D. D. ₹10 Crore
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut (Legislative Timeline Elimination): The original threshold (2016) was ₹1 Lakh (Option A). A 2020 COVID-19 notification permanently raised it to ₹1 Crore (Option C). Proposals for ₹10 Crore (Option D) exist but are not yet notified; ₹10 Lakh (Option B) was never a threshold. Step-by-step: Section 4 of IBC empowers the Central Government to notify the minimum default. Initially notified as ₹1 Lakh. Via Notification S.O. 1205(E) dated 24th March 2020, the threshold was enhanced to ₹1 Crore to exclude small defaults during the pandemic. This ₹1 Crore limit remains the current operative statutory threshold as no subsequent notification has revised it further.

Q7. Reasoning
Statements: All roses are flowers. Some flowers are lilies. No lily is a shrub. Conclusions: I. Some roses are lilies. II. No rose is a shrub. III. Some flowers are not shrubs. Which of the conclusions logically follow?
A. Only I follows
B. Only II follows
C. Only III follows
D. Both II and III follow
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Only conclusion III must be true because the ‘some flowers are lilies’ together with ‘no lily is a shrub’ guarantees at least those lilies are flowers that are not shrubs. Full solution: Draw Venn diagrams; roses ⊂ flowers; some flowers overlap lilies; lilies and shrubs are disjoint. Hence some flowers (the lilies) are not shrubs → III follows. I and II are not forced.
Q8. Reasoning
Statements: All pens are pencils. Some pencils are erasers. No eraser is a marker. Conclusions: I. Some pens are erasers. II. No pen is a marker. III. Some pencils are not markers. Which of the conclusions logically follow?
A. Only I follows
B. Only II follows
C. Only III follows
D. None follows
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Draw mental Venn. All pens ⊂ pencils. Some pencils overlap erasers. Erasers and markers are disjoint. I: Pens may not be in the eraser overlap → not definite. II: Pencils could be markers → not definite. III: The pencils that are erasers are not markers, so at least some pencils are not markers → definite. Hence only III follows.

Find the smallest three-digit number which when divided by 13 leaves a remainder of 7 and when divided by 17 leaves a remainder of 10.
A. 163
B. 176
C. 189
D. 202
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Test options using divisibility. 163 ÷ 13 = 12 rem 7; 163 ÷ 17 = 9 rem 10. Only Option A satisfies both. Option B (176) gives rem 6 with 17. Option C (189) gives rem 2 with 17. Option D (202) gives rem 15 with 17. Full: Number N = 13a+7 = 17b+10 => 13a - 17b = 3. Smallest positive integer solution: a=12, b=9 (156-153=3). N = 13(12)+7 = 163. General form N = 163 + 221k; smallest 3-digit is 163.

Q11. Reasoning
In a coded blood relation language, the following symbols are used: 'P @ Q' means 'P is the father of Q'. 'P # Q' means 'P is the mother of Q'. 'P $ Q' means 'P is the brother of Q'. 'P % Q' means 'P is the sister of Q'. Based on the expression 'K @ L $ M # N % O', how is K related to O?
A. Paternal Grandfather
B. Maternal Uncle
C. Maternal Grandfather
D. Father
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut (Generation Gap Method): Assign values: Father/Mother = +1; Brother/Sister = 0. Trace path K -> L (+1), L -> M (0), M -> N (+1), N -> O (0). Total Gap = +2 (Grandparent). Gender of K is Male (@). Path contains Mother (#) and Sister (%), indicating Maternal line. Hence, Maternal Grandfather. Step-by-step: K @ L => K is Father of L. L $ M => L is Brother of M => K is Father of M. M # N => M is Mother of N => K is Maternal Grandfather of N. N % O => N is Sister of O => K is Maternal Grandfather of O.

Correct Answer: B

Q14. Quantitative Aptitude
Two trains of lengths 180 m and 220 m run on parallel tracks. When running in opposite directions, they cross each other in 10 seconds. When running in the same direction, the faster train crosses the slower train in 40 seconds. What is the speed of the faster train in km/h?
A. 72 km/h
B. 90 km/h
C. 108 km/h
D. 54 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Total length = 400 m. Opposite relative speed = 400/10 = 40 m/s (Sum of speeds). Same direction relative speed = 400/40 = 10 m/s (Difference of speeds). Faster speed = (Sum + Diff)/2 = (40+10)/2 = 25 m/s. Convert: 25 × 18/5 = 90 km/h. Full: Let speeds be x, y m/s (x>y). x+y = 400/10 = 40. x-y = 400/40 = 10. Adding: 2x = 50 => x = 25 m/s = 25 × 18/5 = 90 km/h.
Q15. Reasoning
A is the brother of B. C is the father of A. D is the sister of C. E is the mother of D. How is E related to B?
A. Grandmother
B. Mother
C. Aunt
D. Great‑grandmother
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Trace the parental line upward twice – B’s father is C, and C’s mother is E, so E is B’s grandmother. Full solution: A and B share father C; D is C’s sister, so D is aunt of B; E is mother of C and D, making her grandmother of B.
