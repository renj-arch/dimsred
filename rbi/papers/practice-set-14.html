The radius of a right circular cylinder is increased by 20% and its height is decreased by 25%. What is the percentage change in the volume of the cylinder?
A. Decrease by 10%
B. Decrease by 28%
C. Increase by 8%
D. No change
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Volume V ∝ r²h. Treat r² as a single entity. % change in r² = 20 + 20 + (20×20)/100 = 44% increase. Net % change in V = 44 + (-25) + (44×-25)/100 = 19 - 11 = 8% increase. Full: Let original radius = r, height = h. Original Volume = πr²h. New radius = 1.2r, New height = 0.75h. New Volume = π(1.2r)²(0.75h) = πr²h × 1.44 × 0.75 = 1.08 πr²h. Percentage change = (1.08 - 1) × 100 = 8% increase.

What is the unit digit of the expression (17^43 + 23^57)?
A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 8
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Unit digit of powers follows cycles of 4. 17^43 → 7^43, 43 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit 3. 23^57 → 3^57, 57 mod 4 = 1 → unit digit 3. Sum of unit digits = 3+3 = 6.

If 'P @ Q' means 'P is the mother of Q', 'P # Q' means 'P is the father of Q', 'P $ Q' means 'P is the brother of Q', and 'P % Q' means 'P is the sister of Q', then in the expression 'K @ L # M $ N % O', how is K related to O?
A. Mother
B. Grandmother
C. Aunt
D. Great Grandmother
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the Generation Gap method. Assign values: Parent (@, #) = +1; Sibling ($, %) = 0. Calculate cumulative gap from K to O: K@L (+1), L#M (+1), M$N (0), N%O (0). Total gap = +2. Determine gender of K: '@' implies Mother (Female). A female with +2 gap is Grandmother. Full Solution: K is mother of L. L is father of M. M is brother of N, so M and N are siblings. N is sister of O, so N and O are siblings. Thus M, N, O are siblings with father L. K is mother of L, making K the grandmother of O.
