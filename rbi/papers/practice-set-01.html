Q7. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
A train travels from City A to City B at a speed of 60 km/hr and returns from City B to City A at a speed of 90 km/hr. What is the average speed of the train for the entire journey?
A. 70 km/hr
B. 72 km/hr
C. 75 km/hr
D. 80 km/hr
Correct Answer: B
The average speed is calculated using the harmonic mean formula for two speeds over the same distance: Average Speed = 2 * (Speed1 * Speed2) / (Speed1 + Speed2). Plugging in the values: Average Speed = 2 * (60 * 90) / (60 + 90) = 2 * 5400 / 150 = 10800 / 150 = 72 km/hr.
