A rate hike cannot "base itself on data" → Eliminate (A) and (D). 4. **Check Idiomatic Usage:** Option (B) uses "Basing on". A motorboat covers a certain distance downstream in 4 hours and returns upstream to the starting point in 6 hours. If the speed of the stream is 2 km/h, what is the speed of the boat in still water?
A. 8 km/h
B. 10 km/h
C. 12 km/h
D. 14 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Ratio of Time (Downstream : Upstream) = 4 : 6 = 2 : 3. Since distance is constant, Ratio of Speeds (Downstream : Upstream) = 3 : 2 (inverse ratio). Let Downstream speed = 3x, Upstream speed = 2x. Speed of Boat = (Downstream + Upstream)/2 = (3x + 2x)/2 = 2.5x. Speed of Stream = (Downstream - Upstream)/2 = (3x - 2x)/2 = 0.5x. Ratio Boat : Stream = 2.5x : 0.5x = 5 : 1. Given Stream = 2 km/h, Boat = 5 × 2 = 10 km/h. Full Solution: Let speed of boat = B km/h, stream = 2 km/h. Downstream speed = B+2, Upstream speed = B-2. Distance = Speed × Time. Distance Downstream = 4(B+2). Distance Upstream = 6(B-2). Equating distances: 4(B+2) = 6(B-2) → 4B + 8 = 6B - 12 → 2B = 20 → B = 10 km/h.
