A car travels from city X to city Y at a speed of 40 km/h and returns from Y to X at 60 km/h. What is the average speed for the entire round trip?
A. 48 km/h
B. 50 km/h
C. 45 km/h
D. 52 km/h
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For equal distances, average speed is the harmonic mean = 2ab/(a+b) = 2×40×60/(40+60) = 48 km/h. Full method: Let one-way distance be d. Time onward = d/40, return = d/60. Total distance = 2d, total time = d/40 + d/60 = 5d/120 = d/24. Average speed = 2d ÷ (d/24) = 48 km/h.
