The difference between compound interest and simple interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is ₹200. Find the principal amount.
A. ₹10,000
B. ₹20,000
C. ₹15,000
D. ₹25,000
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For 2 years, CI − SI = P × (R/100)². Here 200 = P × (10/100)² = P × 0.01 ⇒ P = 20,000. Full: SI = P × 2 × 10/100 = 0.2P; CI = P(1.1)² − P = 0.21P; difference = 0.01P = 200 ⇒ P = 20,000.
