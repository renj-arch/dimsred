Q1. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
⚠ Negative Marking: -0.25 marks per wrong answer
A person invests Rs. 1000 in a savings account that earns an annual interest rate of 5%. How much will the person have in the account after 2 years?
A. Rs. 1102.50
B. Rs. 1105
C. Rs. 1110
D. Rs. 1120
Correct Answer: A
The interest earned in the first year is Rs. 1000 * 5% = Rs. 50. The total amount after the first year is Rs. 1000 + Rs. 50 = Rs. 1050. The interest earned in the second year is Rs. 1050 * 5% = Rs. 52.50. The total amount after the second year is Rs. 1050 + Rs. 52.50 = Rs. 1102.50.
