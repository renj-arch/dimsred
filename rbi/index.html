RBI Grade B is one of the most prestigious banking exams in India. Phase 1 (prelims) consists of 200 objective questions across General Awareness, English, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability. Phase 2 includes Descriptive Economics/Finance & Management papers. Selection rate is ~0.1%.
