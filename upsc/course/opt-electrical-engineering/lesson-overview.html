UPSC Mains Optional Electrical Engineering - Overview</h3> <h3>Paper I — Core Electrical Engineering</h3> <ul> <li><strong>Electromagnetic Theory:</strong> Electrostatics (Coulomb's law, Gauss's law, potential, capacitance, energy), Magnetostatics (Biot-Savart law, Ampere's law, magnetic dipole, magnetic materials), Maxwell's equations (differential and integral forms), electromagnetic waves (wave equation, Poynting vector, reflection/reflection at boundaries), displacement current.</li> <li><strong>Electromagnetic Theory Applications:</strong> Transmission lines (characteristic impedance, reflection coefficient, VSWR, Smith chart), waveguides (rectangular and circular waveguides, TE/TM modes, cutoff frequency), cavity resonators, antenna fundamentals (radiation pattern, gain, directivity, antenna types: dipole, Yagi, parabolic reflector).</li> <li><strong>Control Systems:</strong> Open-loop vs closed-loop systems, transfer functions, block diagrams, signal flow graphs (Mason's rule), time domain analysis (steady state error, steady state error for step/ramp/parabolic inputs), Routh-Hurwitz stability criterion, root locus, Bode plots, Nyquist stability criterion, lead-lag compensators, PID controllers, state space analysis (state equations, controllability, observability, canonical forms).</li> <li><strong>Control Systems Applications:</strong> Servo systems, process control, robotics basics, flight control systems, power system stabilizers.</li> </ul> <h3>Electrical Measurements & Instrumentation</h3> <ul> <li><strong>Measurement Systems:</strong> Static characteristics (accuracy, precision, resolution, sensitivity, reproducibility), dynamic characteristics (speed of response, fidelity, lag), errors in measurement (gross errors, systematic errors, random errors), uncertainty analysis.</li> <li><strong>Electrical Measurements:</strong> Measurement of low resistance (Kelvin double bridge), medium resistance (Wheatstone bridge, substitution method), high resistance (Megger, loss of charge method), inductance (Maxwell's bridge, Heaviside's bridge), capacitance (De Sauty bridge, Schering bridge), frequency (Wien bridge), Q factor measurement.</li> <li><strong>Instruments:</strong> Moving coil (PMMC), moving iron (attraction/repulsion type), dynamometer type, electrostatic instruments, electrostatic voltmeters, electrostatic voltmeters, electrostatic voltmeters, electrostatic voltmeters, electrostatic voltmeters.</li> <li><strong>Digital Instruments:</strong> Digital voltmeters (ramp type, integrating type, successive approximation), digital multimeters, frequency counters, time interval measurement, phase measurement, frequency measurement.</li> </ul> <h3>Power Systems</h3> <ul> <li><strong>Power System Analysis:</strong> Per unit system, base values, impedance diagrams, reactance diagrams, load flow studies (Gauss-Seidel, Newton-Raphson, fast decoupled methods), load flow analysis for radial and mesh networks.</li> <li><strong>Fault Analysis:</strong> Symmetrical faults (three-phase short circuit), unsymmetrical faults (line-to-ground, line-to-line, double line-to-ground), sequence networks (positive, negative, zero sequence), fault current calculation, fault level calculation.</li> <li><strong>Power System Stability:</strong> Steady state stability, transient stability (equal area criterion), dynamic stability, methods of improving stability (fast valving, turbine governing, HVDC links, series capacitor, series capacitor).</li> <li><strong>Power System Protection:</strong> Protection zones, overcurrent protection (definite time, inverse time), distance protection (impedance, reactance, mho), differential protection (differential protection of transformers, generators, busbars), distance protection (impedance, reactance, mho zones), overcurrent protection, earth fault protection.</li> <li><strong>Power System Protection Devices:</strong> Circuit breakers (air blast, oil, SF6, vacuum), circuit breaker ratings (making current, breaking capacity, short-time current), auto-reclosing, auto-reclosing schemes.</li> <li><strong>EHV Transmission:</strong> Extra high voltage transmission (765 kV, 1200 kV), compact transmission lines, gas insulated substations (GIS), gas insulated switchgear (GIS), high voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission, back-to-back HVDC, multi-terminal HVDC.</li> </ul> <div class="example-box"><div class="ex-title">Technique: Thevenin's Theorem</div><div class="ex-text">Question: Find Thevenin equivalent across terminals A-B of a circuit with 12V source, 4Ω resistor in series with parallel combination of 4Ω and 8Ω resistors.</div><div class="ex-soln">Answer: V_th = open circuit voltage = voltage across 4Ω resistor = (4/(4+4))×12 = 2V. R_th = 4||4 = 2Ω. Thevenin equivalent: 2V source in series with 2Ω resistor.</div></div> <h3>Memory Tricks</h3> <ul> <li><strong>Kirchhoff's Laws:</strong> KCL - sum of currents at node = 0 (charge conservation); KVL - sum of voltages around loop = 0 (energy conservation).</li> <li><strong>Thevenin/Norton:</strong> V_th = V_oc (open circuit voltage), I_sc = V_th/R_th (short circuit current), R_th = V_oc/I_sc.</li> <li><strong>Maximum Power Transfer:</strong> Maximum power transferred when load resistance = source resistance (R_L = R_th).</li> <li><strong>Resonance:</strong> Series resonance - impedance minimum, current maximum, power factor = 1; Parallel resonance - impedance maximum, current minimum.</li> </ul> <div class="tip-box"><div class="tip-title">Mains strategy</div><div class="tip-text">Electrical Engineering answers: draw circuit diagrams clearly, label all nodes/branches/nodes, show step-by-step derivation of circuit equations, state assumptions clearly, cite IS codes (IS 3025, IS 4722, IS 13234, IS 7098), quote standard values (copper resistivity 1.72×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, copper density 8.96 g/cm³, aluminum resistivity 2.83×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, copper density 2.7 g/cm³).</div></div> <script> (function(){try{ var m=document.getElementById("lesson-meta");if(!m)return;
