Key Concepts

Current Affairs for UPSC

Current Affairs is crucial for UPSC Prelims, Mains, and Interview. It covers national and international events across the entire syllabus — polity, economy, environment, science & tech, social issues, international relations, and culture. Unlike SSC-level coverage which is mostly factual, UPSC demands analytical understanding of issues, their interlinkages, and government interventions. Current affairs should be studied systematically from sources like The Hindu, Indian Express, PIB, Yojana, Economic Survey, and Budget documents.

Key Concepts

Government Schemes & Programmes (2024-25): Ayushman Bharat — PM Jan Arogya Yojana (₹5 lakh/family/year cover for 10.7 crore poor families). PM-KISAN — ₹6,000/year in 3 installments to farmers (~11.8 crore beneficiaries). PM Awas Yojana (Urban + Rural/Gramin) — 'Housing for All by 2022' (target: 2.95 crore houses). Jal Jeevan Mission — tap water connection to every rural household (target: 15.3 crore households; ~78% covered as of Oct 2024). Swachh Bharat Mission (Phase 2) — ODF+ and ODF++ status for villages, liquid waste management. National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 — 5+3+3+4 structure, vocational from Class 6, multiple entry-exit in higher education, Academic Bank of Credits (ABC). PM Mudra Yojana — loans up to ₹10 lakh to micro-enterprises; Shishu (up to ₹50K), Kishor (₹50K-₹5L), Tarun (₹5L-₹10L). PM SVANidhi — working capital loan up to ₹10,000 for street vendors. PM Vishwakarma Yojana (2023) — skill development and loan (up to ₹3 lakh) for traditional artisans and craftspeople in 18 trades. PM e-Bus Sewa — 10,000 e-buses in cities. PM PRANAM (Promotion of Alternate Nutrients for Agriculture Management) — to reduce urea subsidy, promote nano-urea/alternative fertilisers. SAMARTH (Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture) — advanced horticulture clusters. Amrit Dharohar — wetland conservation. Green Credit Programme (2023) — tradable credits for environmental-positive actions (afforestation, water conservation, waste management). PM JANMAN (2023) — development of Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs) in 75 districts, ₹24,000 crore outlay. Vibrant Villages Programme — border villages in 4 states/UTs replacing Border Area Development Programme.

International Relations: India-USA — iCET (Initiative on Critical & Emerging Technologies — semiconductors, AI, space, defence). INDUS-X — defence innovation bridge. India-US joint exercises: Yudh Abhyas (army), Malabar (naval — with Japan, Australia), Cope India (air force). India-China — border tensions in Eastern Ladakh (Galwan 2020, Depsang, Demchok); ongoing military standoff; trade deficit of ~$85 billion; India's 'Act East' vs China's 'String of Pearls'. India-Russia — special and privileged strategic partnership; BrahMos, S-400 (deal completed despite CAATSA), joint military exercise (INDRA), trade $65 billion target by 2030, rupee-rouble mechanism. India-Japan — 'Special Strategic and Global Partnership'; Shinkansen (Mumbai-Ahmedabad HSR); joint exercises (Dharma Guardian, JIMEX, Shinyuu Maitri). India-Australia — Comprehensive Strategic Partnership; ECTA (Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement 2022); joint exercise (AUSINDEX, Pitch Black). India-ASEAN — Act East Policy; ASEAN-India Summit; Comprehensive Strategic Partnership; trade $131 billion (2022-23); RCEP not joined due to trade deficit concerns. India-European Union — trade agreement negotiations (FTA + IPR + Geographical Indications); Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) — concern for Indian exports; EU's deforestation regulation. India-France — strategic autonomy; Rafale jets, Scorpene submarines; Jaitapur nuclear plant (EPR technology); joint exercise (Garuda, Varuna, Shakti). India-Gulf — Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreements (CEPA) with UAE (2022); India-Middle East-Europe Corridor (IMEC — announced at G20 2023, connectivity via UAE, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Israel to Europe). India-Central Asia — 'Connect Central Asia' policy; Chabahar port (Iran) bypasses Pakistan; International North-South Transport Corridor (INSTC — St Petersburg to Mumbai via Iran). India-Africa — Africa-India Forum Summit (4th held in 2025?), Indian diaspora in Africa; lines of credit (~$12 billion); Pan African e-Network (tele-medicine, tele-education). I2U2 (India, Israel, UAE, USA) — joint infrastructure, technology, water projects. Quad (India, US, Japan, Australia) — Indo-Pacific; Malabar exercise, Quad Vaccine Partnership, critical technologies, infrastructure (PIP — Quad Ports for Pacific Islands). UN & Multilateral — India's permanent seat ambition (UNSC reforms, G4 group); UN Peacekeeping (largest troop contributor historically); India's Presidency of G20 (2023) — New Delhi Leaders' Declaration, inclusion of AU in G20, Global Biofuels Alliance (GBA), INDUS (Initiative for Digital Public Infrastructure). SCO (Shanghai Cooperation Organisation) — India's role, counter-terrorism (RATS), connectivity, India-China tensions within SCO. BRICS — New Development Bank (NDB), BRICS Contingent Reserve Arrangement (CRA), expansion (2023 — added Egypt, Iran, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Ethiopia, Argentina postponed). India-Myanmar — border security, free movement regime (FMR reviewed post-coup 2021), Kaladan multi-modal project, conflict in Myanmar affecting Manipur/Mizoram.

Environment & Climate Change: Climate change — global warming (1.1°C above pre-industrial), IPCC AR6 (2023) — 'code red for humanity', 1.5°C limit likely breached by 2030-35. UNFCCC — COP28 (Dubai 2023): Global Stocktake — countries called for transition away from fossil fuels (not phase-out), tripling renewables, doubling energy efficiency by 2030; Loss and Damage Fund operationalised (initial pledges ~$700 million). India's NDCs (updated 2022): reduce emission intensity by 45% (from 2005 levels) by 2030; 50% cumulative electric power from non-fossil sources by 2030; net-zero by 2070. LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment) — Mission LiFE launched by India at COP26. Green Hydrogen Mission (SIGHT incentive) — 5 MMT by 2030, National Green Hydrogen Hub. National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) — 20-30% reduction in PM2.5/PM10 by 2024 (target revised to 40% by 2026 from 2019 baseline). Forest (Conservation) Act 2023 — amended; exemption for up to 0.1 ha near border areas; linear projects (roads/railways) exempted; concerns about reduced transparency. Wildlife — Project Tiger (1973 → 53 reserves, 3,682 tigers according to All India Tiger Estimation 2022, 75% of world's tigers). Project Elephant. Cheetah reintroduction (Kuno National Park, Madhya Pradesh — from Namibia/South Africa, 20 cheetahs translocated as of 2024). Conservation reserves — biosphere reserves (18), Ramsar sites (80 — highest in Asia, led by Tamil Nadu), UNESCO World Heritage sites (43). National Green Tribunal (NGT) — 2010, expert body for environmental disputes. Compensatory Afforestation Fund (CAF) Act 2016. Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) notification 2019. Plastic waste management — single-use plastic ban (2022). Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules 2017. Organic farming — Sikkim first organic state (2016). Zero Budget Natural Farming (ZBNF, now 'Natural Farming' under NITI Aayog promotion).

Social Issues: Poverty — multidimensional poverty index (NITI Aayog): 11.3% of India poor in 2022-23 (down from 29.2% in 2013-14); fastest MPI reduction globally. Inequality — top 10% holds 57% of wealth (World Inequality Lab). Caste census — demand for new caste census 2024; OBC reservation in local bodies; SC/ST Atrocities Act amendments. Gender — sex ratio improved (1024 females per 1000 males in 2024 NFHS-5?), female labour force participation rising (~37% in 2022-23 PLFS). Women's reservation — Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam (2023) — 33% reservation for women in Lok Sabha and state assemblies (implementation after delimitation, likely 2029). Tribal issues — PVTGs 75 communities, PESA Act 1996 (Panchayat Extension to Scheduled Areas) not fully implemented, land alienation. Health — Ayushman Bharat; Maternal Mortality Ratio (MMR) 97 (2018-20), Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) 28 (2022), Under-5 mortality 32 (2020). Malnutrition — NFHS-5 (2019-21): 35% children stunted, 32% underweight, 19% wasted (improving but high). Anemia — 57% women (15-49), 67% children (6-59 months). Poshan Abhiyaan (National Nutrition Mission). Education — NEP 2020; Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER) — higher education 28.4%; school drop-out rate declining; ASER 2023 report — learning levels improved post-COVID but below 2016 levels. Mental health — National Mental Health Programme, Tele-MANAS helpline (14416). Skill development — Skill India Mission, Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY 4.0). Employment — PLFS 2023-24: unemployment rate ~3.2% (CWS approach), but youth unemployment higher. Gig economy — growing platform work; code on social security (2020) includes gig/platform workers. Labour reforms — 4 labour codes (Wages 2019, Industrial Relations 2020, Social Security 2020, Occupational Safety 2020) — implementation pending rules by many states.

Culture & Art: UNESCO intangible heritage — Yoga (2016), Kumbh Mela (2017), Durga Puja (2021), Garba (2023). Classical dances — 8: Bharatanatyam (TN), Kathak (UP), Kathakali (Kerala), Kuchipudi (AP), Manipuri, Mohiniyattam (Kerala), Odissi (Odisha), Sattriya (Assam — since 2000). Classical music — Hindustani (North) and Carnatic (South); gharanas. Fairs & Festivals — National festivals (Republic 26 Jan, Independence 15 Aug, Gandhi Jayanti 2 Oct); religious festivals (Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas, Gurupurab, Pongal, Onam, Bihu, Losar, Hemis, Parsi New Year). Tribal festivals — Medaram Jatara (Telangana, largest tribal fair), Sarhul (Jharkhand), Hornbill (Nagaland), Konark Dance Festival, Khajuraho Dance Festival. Museums — National Museum Delhi, Indian Museum Kolkata (oldest), Salar Jung Hyderabad, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya Mumbai. Built heritage — World Heritage Sites, ASI protected monuments, heritage cities (Jaipur — world heritage city 2019, Ahmedabad — 2017). Museums, archives, and libraries — National Archives, Khuda Bakhsh Library (Patna), Rampur Raza Library.

Solved Examples

Example 1 (Prelims 2023)
Consider the following statements about G20:
1. India hosted the G20 Summit in 2023.
2. The African Union was made a permanent member of the G20 during India's presidency.
3. G20 leaders signed a declaration on cryptocurrency regulation.

How many of the above statements are correct?

a) Only one b) Only two c) All three d) None
Solution: b) Only two. Statement 1: India hosted the G18th G20 Summit in September 2023 in New Delhi — correct. Statement 2: The African Union was granted permanent membership of G20 during India's presidency (India had pushed for this) — correct. Statement 3: The New Delhi Leaders Declaration included a section on crypto assets but called for a 'comprehensive policy framework' and FATF implementation — it did NOT 'sign a declaration on cryptocurrency regulation' as a binding commitment. So statement 3 is not accurate. Thus statements 1 and 2 are correct — only two.
Example 2 (Mains 2022, GS II)
Discuss the role of the QUAD in the Indo-Pacific region. How has India's participation in the QUAD evolved in recent years?
Solution: The Quadrilateral Security Dialogue (QUAD — India, US, Japan, Australia) aims to ensure a free, open, and inclusive Indo-Pacific. Role: (1) Maritime security — freedom of navigation, respect for UNCLOS, countering China's assertive claims in South China Sea and militarisation in Indian Ocean; Malabar naval exercises. (2) Infrastructure — Quad Ports for Pacific Islands (PIP), humanitarian assistance/disaster relief (HADR) coordination. (3) Technology — Critical and emerging technologies (5G, AI, quantum, semiconductors); Quad Technology Incubator. (4) Health — Quad Vaccine Partnership ($1.2 billion for COVID vaccine delivery in Indo-Pacific). (5) Climate — Quad climate action, green shipping, clean energy. (6) Counter-terrorism and cyber security. India's evolving role: initial caution (Quad 1.0 2007-08, India stepped back after Chinese concerns). Quad revived in 2017 after India-Australia-US-Japan consultations. India now actively engages — balancing Act East, Neighbourhood First, and maintaining strategic autonomy. India hosts Malabar exercise (2024), leads Quad working group on HADR and maritime security. India's participation is calibrated — not a military alliance but a consultative forum; avoids direct confrontation with China; maintains BRICS, SCO, and Russia partnership alongside Quad. Critics argue Quad is an 'Asian NATO' — India maintains that Quad is not directed against any country.
Example 3 (Prelims 2024)
With reference to the PM Vishwakarma Yojana, consider the following statements:
1. It covers all traditional artisans and craftspeople regardless of their occupation.
2. The scheme provides collateral-free credit up to ₹3 lakh.
3. It also provides skill training and a toolkit incentive.

How many of the above statements are correct?

a) Only one b) Only two c) All three d) None
Solution: b) Only two — statements 2 and 3 are correct. The PM Vishwakarma Yojana was launched in September 2023 for traditional artisans and craftspeople in 18 specific trades (carpenter, blacksmith, potter, tailor, barber, mason, etc.) — not ALL occupations, so statement 1 is incorrect. The scheme provides: (1) Collateral-free credit — up to ₹1 lakh (first tranche) and ₹2 lakh (second tranche) = total up to ₹3 lakh — so statement 2 is correct; (2) Skill training (5-7 days, with ₹500/day stipend); (3) Toolkit incentive of ₹15,000; (4) Digital literacy, marketing support, Udyam registration. So statements 2 and 3 are correct.
