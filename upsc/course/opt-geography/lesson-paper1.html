Key Concepts

Geography Paper I — Physical Geography

Geomorphology

  • Earth's interior: Structure of earth (crust, mantle, core - outer & inner), evidence from seismology (P-waves & S-waves behavior), density, temperature, pressure conditions. Isostasy (Airy's & Pratt's hypotheses), isostatic adjustment, isostatic rebound (post-glacial rebound).
  • Plate tectonics: Plate tectonics theory (Wilson, Morgan, McKenzie, Parker 1967-68) - lithosphere broken into plates (major: Pacific, North American, South American, Eurasian, African, Indo-Australian, Antarctic; minor: Nazca, Cocos, Caribbean, Philippine, Arabian, Scotia), plate boundaries (divergent, convergent, transform), plate movements (rates ~2-15 cm/year), causes of plate movement (ridge push, slab pull, mantle convection currents), Wilson cycle (ocean basin opening & closing).
  • Earthquakes: Focus & epicenter, seismic waves (P-waves - primary/compressional, S-waves - secondary/shear, surface waves - Love & Rayleigh), seismograph & seismogram, Richter scale & moment magnitude scale (Mw), intensity (Modified Mercalli Intensity - MMI), distribution of earthquakes (circum-Pacific belt, Alpine-Himalayan belt, mid-ocean ridges), causes & effects, earthquake prediction & preparedness, seismic zoning of India (BIS zones II-V), earthquake-resistant design.
  • Volcanoes: Types (shield, composite/strato, cinder cone, lava dome, fissure), types of eruptions (Hawaiian, Strombolian, Vulcanian, Pelean, Plinian, Surtseyan, phreatic, phreatomagmatic), volcanic products (lava - pahoehoe & aa, pyroclastic materials - ash, lapilli, bombs, blocks, volcanic gases), volcanic hazards & mitigation, major volcanic belts (circum-Pacific ring of fire - ~75% of volcanoes, mid-ocean ridges, intraplate hotspots - Hawaii, Yellowstone), famous eruptions (Mount St. Helens 1980, Mount Pinatubo 1991, Krakatoa 1883, Mount Vesuvius 79 AD).
  • Weathering, mass wasting & landforms: Weathering (physical/mechanical - frost action, insolation, exfoliation, block disintegration, granular disintegration; chemical - solution, carbonation, hydration, oxidation, reduction, hydrolysis, leaching; biological - plants, animals, humans), factors affecting weathering (climate, rock type, topography, vegetation, time), mass wasting (landslides, slump, creep, earthflow, mudflow, rockfall, avalanche - causes & prevention), soils (soil profile - A, B, C, R horizons; soil formation factors - parent rock, climate, topography, organisms, time; soil texture - sand, silt, clay; soil types - zonal, intrazonal, azonal), soil erosion (sheet, rill, gully, sheet, splash), soil conservation (contour bunding, terracing, afforestation, check dams).
  • Geomorphic processes & landforms: Fluvial (river erosion - hydraulic action, corrosion/abrasion, attrition, solution; transportation - traction, saltation, suspension, solution; deposition - alluvial fans, floodplains, meanders, oxbow lakes, natural levees, deltas - arcuate, bird's foot, cuspate, estuarine), glacial (erosion - plucking, abrasion; deposition - moraines, drumlins, eskers, kames, outwash plains; features - cirque, arete, horn, U-shaped valley, hanging valley, fjord, fiord), glacial depositional & erosional landforms, glacial lakes, moraines, glacier types (valley/alpine, continental/ice sheet, piedmont), periglacial processes (frost action, solifluction, patterned ground), aeolian (wind erosion - deflation, abrasion; deposition - sand dunes - barchan, seif, star, transverse, longitudinal; loess, desert pavement, yardang, zeugen), glacial & aeolian landforms.
  • Coastal geomorphology: Waves (constructive & destructive), tides (spring & neap), currents, erosion (hydraulic action, corrosion, attrition, solution), deposition (beaches, bars, spits, tombolos, barrier islands, lagoons), coastal landforms (sea cliffs, sea caves, sea arches, sea stacks, wave-cut platforms, headlands & bays), depositional coasts (mudflats, salt marshes, mangroves, coral reefs - fringing, barrier, atoll, atoll formation - Darwin's theory), coral reefs (conditions for growth, types - fringing, barrier, atoll, theories of coral reef formation - Darwin's subsidence theory, Daly's glacial control theory, Daly's & Murray's theories), coastal management & protection.
  • Karst topography: Limestone topography - dissolution of limestone (carbonation), surface features (sinkholes/swallow holes, dolines, poljes, karst windows, disappearing streams), underground features (caves, caverns, stalactites, stalagmites, pillars, flowstone), distribution (Kentucky, Slovenia, Guangxi, Vindhyan & Bastar regions in India).
  • Gradation & denudation: Gradation (degradation & aggradation), base level of erosion, peneplain, pediplain, panplain, rejuvenation (knick points, waterfalls, river terraces), drainage patterns (dendritic, trellis, radial, rectangular, parallel, annular, centripetal, barbed, discordant), drainage patterns & their significance.
Technique: Plate tectonics answer
Question: 'Explain plate tectonics theory & its evidence.'
Answer: Plate tectonics - lithosphere broken into ~7 major & several minor plates floating on asthenosphere. Evidence: (1) Jigsaw fit of continents (South America & Africa), (2) Fossil evidence (Mesosaurus, Glossopteris, Lystrosaurus across continents), (3) Rock continuity across oceans, (4) Paleomagnetism & sea floor spreading (magnetic stripes symmetrical about mid-ocean ridges), (5) Earthquake & volcano distribution along plate boundaries, (6) GPS measurements of plate movement. Mechanisms: ridge push, slab pull, mantle convection. Results: fold mountains, rift valleys, volcanoes, earthquakes at plate boundaries.

Memory Tricks

  • Plate boundaries: Divergent = move apart (ridges), Convergent = move together (subduction/collision), Transform = slide past (faults).
  • Earthquake waves: P-waves = Primary/fastest, travel through solids & liquids; S-waves = Secondary/slower, solids only.
  • Weathering types: Physical = mechanical breakdown; Chemical = chemical change (solution, oxidation, carbonation).
  • Drainage patterns: Dendritic = tree-like (uniform rock), Radial = from central cone/volcano, Trellis = folded mountains (alternate hard/soft), Rectangular = jointed/faulted rocks.
Mains strategy
Physical geography answers need diagrams (plate boundaries, fault types, river meander, glacial features, karst features, atmospheric layers, heat budget, monsoon mechanism, ocean currents map). Draw neat labeled diagrams. Quote facts: Earth's radius ~6,371 km, crust thickness (oceanic 5-10 km, continental 30-50 km), mantle ~2,900 km thick, core ~3,485 km radius (outer core liquid, inner core solid). Quote data: earthquakes ~500,000 detectable/year, ~100,000 felt, volcanism ~1,500 potentially active volcanoes worldwide.
