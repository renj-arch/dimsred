3. Conjuncts (Sanyuktakshar)

Two+ consonants combining — क्ष (क+ष), त्र (त+र), ज्ञ (ज+ञ), श्र (श+र), द्वि (द+व). UPSC asks which is NOT a conjunct — know क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र द्वि explicitly.

5. Vyanjan sandhi (consonant sandhi)

  • त् + च → च्च: वाक् + च → वाच्च? Actually त् + च → च्च gives वाग्च? — standard: वाक् + च ≠ — the rule: त् + च → च्च? Keep to: क् + ष → क्ष (वाक् + इंद्र → वागिंद्र via visarga), श् + च → श्च.
  • Visarga sandhi: visarga + क/ख/प/फ → ष/स (निः + काल → निश्चल? — निःशब्द + कृत → निश्शब्द).
  • Final common exam pair: विद्या + आलय = विद्यालय (dirgha), महा + ऋषि = महर्षि (guna), सदा + अवतु = सदैवतु (vriddhi).

4. Upasarga and Pratyaya

6. Upasarga (prefixes) — 20 standard

अति, अधि, अनु, अप, अभि, अव, आ, उप, प्रति, परा, परि, प्र, वि, सम्, सु, नि, दुर्, दव्?, अपि?, निस् — the standard list examiners use: उपसर्ग 20: अति, अधि, अनु, अप, अभि, अव, आ, उत्, उप, प्रति, परा, परि, प्र, वि, सम्, सु, नि, दुर्, अपि, निस्? The commonly cited Hindi list is 20 including these; verify against your prescribed list.

Example: आ + गमन = आगमन; उत् + नयन? — उत्थान; प्र + गोप = प्रगोप.

Eight karaks with their vibhakti markers — the most-tested mapping:

The 8 karaks

Karta (कर्ता)ने (transitive past) / zeroराम ने खायाDoer
Karma (कर्म)कोसीता को पत्रObject/recipient
Karana (करण)सेकलम से लिखाInstrument
Sampradana (सम्प्रदान)के लिए / कोमाँ के लिए दूधPurpose/recipient
Apadana (अपादान)सेपेड़ से गिराSeparation
Sambandha (संबंध)का/के/कीराम का घरPossession

Critical distinction: Karta uses ने only in transitive past tense — "राम ने खाया" (transitive) vs "राम खाता है" (intransitive/present — no ने). This is the #1 karak trap.

6. Vakya-bhed — sentence types

Sentence types
TypeStructureExample
Saral (simple)One independent clauseराम घर गया।
Sanyukta (compound)Two+ independent clauses joined by conjunctionराम गया और सीता रुकी।
Misra (complex)Main + subordinate clauseराम गया क्योंकि वह थक गया था।

Some grammars add a fourth: complex-compound mixed (sanyukt-misra). UPSC typically tests the 3-way distinction: does the sentence have one clause, co-ordinate clauses, or a main+subordinate pair?

Paryayavachi (synonyms) — common clusters: पानी: जल, वारि, नीर, तोय, अम्बु; आग: अग्नि, अनल, पावक, हुताशन; सूर्य: रवि, भानु, दिवाकर, आदित्य; चाँद: शशि, इन्दु, चन्द्र, हिमांशु; पेड़: वृक्ष, तरु, द्रुम, विटप.

Vilom (antonyms) — pairs: बड़ा↔छोटा, Sugam↔कठिन, प्रगति↔पतन, जीत↔हार, आदर↔अनादर, प्रसन्न↔निराश, स्वर्ग↔नरक.

9. Examiner traps

Traps that cost marks
  • Karta ने vs no ने: ने appears only in transitive past — "राम ने लिखा" vs "राम लिखता है" (no ने). Present/future never take ने.
  • से for karana vs apadana: same word से — instrument (कलम से) vs separation (पेड़ से). Context decides.
  • को for karma vs sampradana: को = object (letter TO Sita) vs purpose (milk FOR mother) — sometimes both fit; the verb's meaning disambiguates.
  • Karmadharya vs bahuvrihi: same test as Sanskrit — does the compound name the thing or its possessor?
  • Savarna vs dirgha: अ+अ→आ is called dirgha sandhi in Hindi terminology (not "savarna" — that's Sanskrit usage).
  • Muhavara literal translation: never translate idioms word-for-word — "आँखों का तारा" is NOT "the star of the eyes".
  • Conjunct classification: क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र द्वि are the 5 standard — know them explicitly.

10. Practice plan

Phase 1 — classification: 20 random Hindi words daily — classify each sandhi, samas, karak, vakya type.

Phase 2 — mapping tables: karak↔vibhakti, samas subtypes, paryayavachi clusters — drill until instant recall.

Phase 3 — muhavare: 10 new idioms daily, used in sentences — idioms are tested in both recognition and usage.

Phase 4 — mixed mock: previous-year Hindi Paper I grammar questions under time. Grammar is the highest-ROI section — pure rule knowledge, no interpretation needed.
