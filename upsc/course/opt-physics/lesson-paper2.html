Key Concepts

Physics Paper II — Thermodynamics, Statistical Mechanics & Quantum Mechanics

Thermodynamics

  • Zeroth Law: Thermodynamic equilibrium, temperature as a fundamental property.
  • First Law of Thermodynamics: Conservation of energy, ΔU = Q - W (or ΔU = Q + W depending on sign convention), internal energy U as a state function, heat capacity (Cp, Cv), relation Cp - Cv = R for ideal gas.
  • Second Law of Thermodynamics: Kelvin-Planck statement, Clausius statement, entropy as a state function, entropy change calculations (dS = δQ_rev/T), entropy change for ideal gas, isolated systems, irreversibility.
  • Third Law of Thermodynamics: Third law (Nernst heat theorem), absolute zero unattainability, absolute entropy scale, residual entropy.
  • Thermodynamic Potentials: Internal energy U, enthalpy H = U + pV, Helmholtz free energy F = U - TS, Gibbs free energy G = H - TS, relations between them, Maxwell relations, Gibbs-Duhem equation, Clapeyron equation.
  • Thermodynamic Relations: T-dS equations (TdS = C_v dT + T(∂P/∂T)_V dV, TdS = C_p dT - T(∂V/∂T)_p dP), Joule-Thomson coefficient, inversion temperature.
  • Phase Equilibrium: Phase rule (F = C - P + 2), phase diagrams (P-T diagram, P-T diagram for water), phase transitions (first order, second order), Clausius-Clapeyron equation (dP/dT = L/(TΔv)).

Statistical Mechanics

  • Microcanonical Ensemble: Isolated system (N, V, E fixed), microcanonical ensemble, entropy S = k_B ln Ω (Boltzmann's formula), fundamental postulate of statistical mechanics (equal a priori probabilities).
  • Canonical Ensemble: Canonical ensemble (N, V, T fixed), Boltzmann distribution p_i = (1/Z) e^(-βE_i), partition function Z = Σ e^(-βE_i), thermodynamic relations (F = -k_B T ln Z, S = k_B ln Z + U/T, U = -∂lnZ/∂β, C_V = k_B β² ⟨(E - ⟨E⟩)²⟩).
  • Grand Canonical Ensemble: Grand canonical ensemble (μ, V, T fixed), grand partition function Ξ = Σ exp(-β(E_i - μN_i)), grand potential Ω = -k_B T ln Ξ, grand potential relations.
  • Quantum Statistics: Bose-Einstein statistics (Bose-Einstein statistics, Bose-Einstein condensation, Bose-Einstein condensate), Fermi-Dirac statistics (Fermi-Dirac statistics, Fermi energy, Fermi temperature), Maxwell-Boltzmann statistics (classical limit).
  • Blackbody Radiation: Blackbody radiation, Planck's radiation law (Planck's law), Stefan-Boltzmann law (j* = σT⁴), Wien's displacement law (λ_max T = b), Rayleigh-Jeans law, ultraviolet catastrophe resolved by Planck.
  • Fermi Gas: Fermi gas (ideal Fermi gas), Fermi energy E_F, Fermi temperature T_F, density of states, low temperature specific heat of electrons (C_v ∝ T), white dwarfs (electron degeneracy pressure).

Blackbody Radiation

  • Blackbody Radiation: Blackbody radiation (blackbody spectrum), Planck's law (Planck's radiation law: u(ν,T) = (8πhν³/c³)/(e^(hν/kT) - 1)), Stefan-Boltzmann law (j* = σT⁴), Wien's displacement law (λ_max T = b = 2.898 × 10⁻³ m·K), Wien's displacement law (λ_max T = 2.898 × 10⁻³ m·K).
  • Photoelectric Effect: Photoelectric effect (photoelectric effect), Einstein's photoelectric equation (E_k = hν - φ), threshold frequency, stopping potential.
  • Compton Effect: Compton effect (Compton effect), Compton wavelength shift (Δλ = (h/m_ec)(1 - cos θ)), Compton wavelength (λ_C = h/(m_e c) ≈ 2.426 × 10⁻¹² m).
  • De Broglie Hypothesis: de Broglie hypothesis (matter waves), de Broglie wavelength (λ = h/p), electron diffraction, Davisson-Germer experiment.
  • Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle: Heisenberg uncertainty principle (Δx Δp ≥ ℏ/2, ΔE Δt ≥ ℏ/2), interpretation, applications (zero-point energy, tunneling).
  • Wave-Particle Duality: Wave-particle duality (light as particle and wave, matter as wave and particle), quantum mechanics (wave mechanics, matrix mechanics), Schrödinger equation (time-dependent Schrödinger equation: iℏ ∂ψ/∂t = Ĥψ, time-independent Schrödinger equation: Ĥψ = Eψ).
Technique: Blackbody Radiation
Question: Calculate the peak wavelength of blackbody radiation at T = 3000 K.
Answer: Wien's displacement law: λ_max T = b = 2.898 × 10⁻³ m·K. λ_max = 2.898 × 10⁻³ / 3000 = 9.66 × 10⁻⁷ m = 966 nm (infrared region).

Memory Tricks

  • Thermodynamics Laws: Zeroth law (temperature), First law (energy conservation: ΔU = Q - W), Second law (entropy increases, entropy of isolated system never decreases), Third law (S → 0 as T → 0, absolute zero unattainable).
  • Quantum Mechanics: Schrödinger equation: iℏ ∂ψ/∂t = Ĥψ (time-dependent), Ĥψ = Eψ (time-independent), Heisenberg uncertainty principle: Δx Δp ≥ ℏ/2, ΔE Δt ≥ ℏ/2.
  • Thermodynamic Potentials: Internal energy U = Q - W, Enthalpy H = U + pV, Helmholtz free energy F = U - TS, Gibbs free energy G = H - TS = U + pV - TS, differentials: dU = TdS - pdV, dH = TdS + VdP, dF = -SdT - pdV, dG = -SdT + Vdp.
  • Thermodynamic Potentials Relations: Maxwell relations: (∂S/∂V)_T = (∂P/∂T)_V, (∂S/∂P)_T = -(∂V/∂T)_P, (∂T/∂V)_S = -(∂P/∂S)_V, (∂T/∂P)_S = (∂V/∂S)_P.
Mains strategy
Physics answers: derive equations step by step, show all steps clearly, verify assumptions before applying theorems, draw diagrams (graphs, surfaces, curves), quote constants (ħ, c, G, k_B, e, m_e, m_p), quote experiments (Michelson-Morley, photoelectric effect, Stern-Gerlach, double-slit experiment). Data: fundamental constants (ħ = 1.055 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s, G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ m³/(kg·s²), k_B = 1.381 × 10⁻²³ J/K, e = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C, m_e = 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg, m_p = 1.673 × 10⁻²⁷ kg), quote experiments (Michelson-Morley, photoelectric effect, Stern-Gerlach, double-slit experiment).
