Technique: Crystal Field Splitting
Question: 'Explain crystal field splitting in octahedral complexes.'
Answer: In octahedral crystal field, five d-orbitals split into two sets: t2g (dxy, dyz, dxz - lower energy) and eg (dx2-y2, dz2 - higher energy). Energy difference = Δo (crystal field splitting energy). Magnitude depends on: nature of ligand (strong field: CN-, CO → large Δo, low spin; weak field: I-, Cl- → small Δo, high spin), oxidation state of metal (higher oxidation state → larger Δo), geometry of complex.

Memory Tricks

  • Hybridization: sp3 (tetrahedral), sp2 (trigonal planar), sp (linear), dsp2 (square planar), sp3d (trigonal bipyramidal), sp3d2 (octahedral).
  • Spectrochemical Series: I- < Br- < Cl- < F- < OH- < H2O < NH3 < en < NO2- < CN- (increasing field strength).
  • Spectrochemical Series: Weak field ligands: I-, Br-, Cl-, F-, OH-, H2O; Strong field ligands: CN-, CO, NO2-, en.
  • Crystal Field Splitting: Octahedral: t2g (lower), eg (higher); Tetrahedral: e (lower), t2 (higher), Δt = 4/9 Δo.
Mains strategy
Inorganic chemistry answers: draw structures, show reactions step by step, cite IUPAC names, mention reaction conditions (catalysts, temperature, pressure). Draw reaction mechanisms (curved arrow mechanisms). Quote standard textbooks (J.D. Lee for inorganic chemistry), quote standard data (bond energies, bond lengths, bond angles, lattice energies, standard electrode potentials).
