Key Concepts

Civil Engineering Paper II — Design, Water Resources & Geotechnics

Design of Steel Structures

  • Steel structures: Limit state method (strength & serviceability), partial safety factors, design of tension members (gross & net area, block shear), compression members (buckling, slenderness ratio, buckling curves), built-up columns, lacing & battens, tension members (splices), beams (laterally stable & unstable, lateral-torsional buckling, shear, deflection, web buckling & crippling), plate girders (components, design of web & flange, stiffeners), gantry girders, roof trusses (loads: dead, live, wind — IS 875, snow), connections (bolt types — bearing & friction type, high-strength friction grip bolts HSFG, weld types — fillet & butt, welding symbols, design of bolted & welded connections), plastic design of steel structures (plastic moment, shape factor, mechanism method).

Design of Concrete Structures (IS 456)

  • Limit state method: Partial safety factors for materials (γc = 1.5, γs = 1.15) & loads (DL = 1.5, LL = 1.5, combination 1.5(DL+LL)), characteristic strength, design strength, design loads.
  • Limit state of collapse: Flexure (singly reinforced — depth of neutral axis, lever arm, moment of resistance, balanced/under/over-reinforced, minimum & maximum steel, doubly reinforced), shear (design shear stress, shear reinforcement — vertical stirrups, bent-up bars, minimum shear reinforcement), torsion (equivalent shear, equivalent moment, transverse & longitudinal reinforcement), bond & anchorage (development length, lap splices).
  • Limit state of serviceability: Deflection limits (span/250 for simply supported up to 10m, span/350 for cantilever, span/300-350 for others), cracking, durability (minimum cover, minimum cement content, maximum w/c ratio by exposure condition — mild, moderate, severe, very severe, extreme).
  • Slabs: One-way & two-way slabs, design of simply supported one-way slab, cantilever slab, two-way slab (simply supported corners not held down vs held down), flat slab.
  • Columns: Short & long columns (slenderness ratio limits), design of axially loaded short column (Pu = 0.4fck·Ac + 0.67fy·Asc), minimum eccentricity (20 mm or D/500, whichever greater), combined axial load & uniaxial/biaxial bending (interaction diagram, load contour method), slender columns (additional moment, slenderness effect), isolated & combined footings (size based on soil bearing pressure, check for one-way & two-way shear, punching shear, development length), combined footing (rectangular & trapezoidal), raft foundation concept.
  • Pre-stressed concrete: Pre-tensioning & post-tensioning, losses (elastic shortening, creep, shrinkage, relaxation, anchorage slip, friction), degree of pre-stressing, kern distance, pressure line, losses estimation.

Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics

  • Fluid properties: Density, specific weight, specific gravity, viscosity (dynamic & kinematic), surface tension, capillarity, vapor pressure, compressibility (bulk modulus).
  • Fluid statics: Pressure variation, hydrostatic forces on plane & curved surfaces, center of pressure, buoyancy & floatation (metacentre, metacentric height, stability of floating & submerged bodies), manometers.
  • Fluid kinematics: Types of flow (steady/unsteady, uniform/non-uniform, laminar/turbulent — Reynolds number), streamlines, path lines, streak lines, continuity equation, circulation & vorticity, stream function & velocity potential, flownet.
  • Fluid dynamics: Bernoulli's equation (assumptions, applications — venturimeter, orifice meter, pitot tube), energy line & hydraulic grade line, impulse-momentum principle (force on pipe bends, nozzle force), moment of momentum.
  • Flow through pipes: Darcy-Weisbach equation (head loss due to friction), minor losses (entrance, exit, contraction, enlargement, bends, valves), hydraulic gradient & total energy lines, pipes in series & parallel, equivalent pipe, siphon, water hammer (surge analysis, surge tanks).
  • Open channel flow: Types of flow (uniform, non-uniform, critical, subcritical, supercritical), Chezy & Manning equations, most economical sections (rectangular — depth = half width, triangular — half angle 45°), specific energy & specific force, critical depth, hydraulic jump (elements, sequent depths, energy loss, types based on Froude number), gradually varied flow (GVF — dynamic equation, surface profiles — M1, M2, M3, S1, S2, S3, C1, C2, C3), rapidly varied flow.
  • Hydraulic machines: Turbines (Pelton, Francis, Kaplan — working principle, specific speed, unit quantities, characteristic curves), pumps (centrifugal — working principle, manometric head, efficiency, specific speed, cavitation, NPSH, multistage), positive displacement pumps.

Hydrology & Water Resources

  • Hydrology: Hydrologic cycle, precipitation (types — convectional, orographic, cyclonic), measurement (rain gauges — recording & non-recording), mean areal precipitation (arithmetic average, Thiessen polygon, isohyetal), infiltration (Horton's equation, φ-index, W-index), runoff (factors affecting, rational method — Q = CIA/3.6, unit hydrograph — unit hydrograph theory, assumptions, unit hydrograph derivation, S-curve, synthetic unit hydrograph), floods (flood frequency analysis — Gumbel's method, log-Pearson type III, design flood, flood routing — reservoir & channel routing, Muskingum method), drought.
  • Water resources: Water resources of India (surface & ground), water demand (domestic, irrigation, industrial, power, environmental), consumptive & non-consumptive use, water balance, water conservation (watershed management, rainwater harvesting, artificial recharge), inter-linking of rivers, National Water Policy, water disputes (Cauvery, Krishna, Godavari, Narmada), water pricing.

Irrigation Engineering

  • Irrigation: Necessity & benefits, ill-effects (waterlogging & salinity), crop seasons (kharif, rabi, zaid), crop water requirements (consumptive use, duty, delta, base period, duty-delta-area relationship, overlap allowance), irrigation efficiencies (conveyance, application, distribution, storage), canal system (components — headworks, distributaries, minors, outlets), canal lining (advantages, types), canal falls, cross-drainage works (aqueduct, super passage, level crossing, inlet & outlet, super passage), canal outlets (proportional, semi-modular, non-modular), canal drops, cross regulators & head regulators.
  • Groundwater: Occurrence (aquifers — confined, unconfined, perched), specific yield & specific retention, Darcy's law, transmissivity, storage coefficient, well hydraulics (theis equation — Theis method for unconfined aquifer, Dupuit's equation for steady flow — confined & unconfined), tube wells (types — cavity, strainer, gravel pack), artificial recharge (spreading basins, recharge shafts, recharge wells), saline water intrusion.
  • Dams & spillways: Dams (types — gravity, earthen, rock-fill, arch, buttress — selection factors), gravity dam forces (hydrostatic, uplift, wave, ice, silt, earthquake, wind), stability analysis (sliding, overturning, tension & compression stresses), foundation & drainage (drainage gallery, relief wells), spillways (types — ogee, side channel, chute, shaft/siphon, labyrinth), energy dissipation (hydraulic jump stilling basin, ski-jump), spillway crest gates (radial, vertical lift, drum).
  • Cross drainage works: Aqueduct, super passage, level crossing, inlet & outlet, super passage (canal below drainage), design considerations.
  • Waterlogging & reclamation: Causes (excessive irrigation, seepage from canals, impervious layer, inadequate drainage), effects (waterlogging, salinity/alkalinity), prevention & reclamation (surface & subsurface drainage — open drains, tile drains, mole drains, leaching for salinity).

Environmental Engineering

  • Water supply: Water demand (per capita demand — domestic, industrial, commercial, public, fire; factors affecting demand), design period, population forecasting (arithmetic, geometric, incremental increase, decreased rate of growth, graphical methods), sources of water (surface — rivers, lakes, reservoirs; ground — wells, springs), intake works, conveyance, treatment (screening, aeration, coagulation — alum, flocculation, sedimentation — plain & with coagulant aids, filtration — slow sand, rapid gravity, pressure filters, disinfection — chlorination, breakpoint chlorination, UV, ozonation, softening — lime-soda process, zeolite process, fluoridation & defluoridation, iron & manganese removal), distribution system (layouts — dead end, grid iron, circular, radial), storage reservoirs (service reservoirs), valves & appurtenances.
  • Wastewater engineering: Sewage characteristics (physical, chemical, biological — BOD, COD, DO, TSS, ammonia, nitrogen, phosphorus), sewerage systems (separate, combined, partially separate), sewer design (self-cleansing velocity, minimum & maximum velocities, Manning's formula), appurtenances (manholes, drop manholes, inverted siphon, flushing tanks, storm water inlets), sewage treatment (primary — screening, grit chamber, sedimentation; secondary — biological — trickling filter, activated sludge process (ASP), oxidation pond, oxidation ditch, aerated lagoon; advanced — nutrient removal), sludge treatment & disposal (thickening, digestion — anaerobic, dewatering, drying beds, incineration, land application), septic tank & Imhoff tank (on-site sanitation), sullage & grey water, rural sanitation (soak pit, leach pit), ecological sanitation (ecosan), solid waste management (collection, transport, disposal — landfill, composting, incineration, vermicomposting, waste-to-energy, Plastic Waste Management Rules, e-waste), air pollution (sources, effects, control — ESP, baghouse filter, scrubber, catalytic converter, National Ambient Air Quality Standards), noise pollution (sources, effects, control, decibel scale).

Geotechnical Engineering

  • Soil formation & classification: Soil formation (weathering — physical & chemical), soil structure & fabric, phase diagram (void ratio, porosity, degree of saturation, water content, density — bulk, dry, saturated, submerged), density index, relative density, consistency limits (Atterberg limits — liquid, plastic, shrinkage limits, plasticity index, flow index, toughness index), soil classification (IS classification — coarse grained & fine grained, USCS), soil moisture & suction.
  • Permeability & seepage: Darcy's law, coefficient of permeability (factors affecting — temperature, void ratio, impurities, degree of saturation), laboratory tests (constant head & falling head), field tests (pumping out & pumping in), permeability of stratified soils (parallel & perpendicular to layers), seepage (discharge, seepage pressure, uplift pressure, quicksand condition — critical hydraulic gradient), flownet (properties, construction, applications — uplift pressure, exit gradient), protective filters.
  • Effective stress principle: Terzaghi's principle — effective stress = total stress - pore water pressure, capillary rise, quicksand condition, frost heave, boiling condition.
  • Stress distribution: Boussinesq's equation (point load), Westergaard's equation, Newmark's influence chart (vertical stress beneath point, line & strip loads), contact pressure distribution (flexible & rigid footings on sand & clay).
  • Consolidation: Terzaghi's one-dimensional consolidation theory (assumptions, differential equation, degree of consolidation, time factor), consolidation test (oedometer — e-log p curve, preconsolidation pressure, overconsolidation ratio OCR), normally consolidated & overconsolidated soils, secondary consolidation.
  • Shear strength: Mohr-Coulomb failure theory (τ = c + σ tan φ), cohesion & angle of internal friction, laboratory tests (direct shear, triaxial — UU, UU, CU, CD, unconfined compression, vane shear), stress path, pore pressure parameters (Skempton's A & B), sensitivity & thixotropy of clays.
  • Compaction: Factors affecting compaction, Proctor compaction test (standard & modified), compaction curve (dry density vs water content — optimum moisture content OMC & maximum dry density MDD), factors affecting compaction (water content, compactive effort, soil type, mixing time, method), field compaction methods (rollers — smooth wheel, sheep foot, pneumatic-tired, vibratory), compaction control (field density measurement — sand replacement, core cutter, nuclear density gauge), relative compaction.
  • Shear strength: (see above) - triaxial tests (UU, CU, CD), unconfined compression test, vane shear test, direct shear test.
  • Bearing capacity: Terzaghi's bearing capacity theory (ultimate bearing capacity for general & local shear failure, factors — cohesion, surcharge, weight terms), Skempton's equation for saturated clays, Meyerhof's & Hansen's equations, factors affecting bearing capacity (width, depth, water table, shape, inclination), settlement (elastic settlement, consolidation settlement — calculation using e-log p curve, coefficient of volume compressibility mv, differential settlement), allowable bearing pressure, plate load test.
  • Retaining walls: Earth pressure theories (Rankine & Coulomb — active, passive, earth pressure at rest), Rankine's theory (active & passive earth pressure coefficients Ka & Kp for cohesionless & cohesive soils), Culmann's graphical method, stability of retaining walls (sliding, overturning, bearing capacity), drainage behind retaining walls, reinforced earth.
  • Slope stability: Infinite slopes (dry & submerged), finite slopes (method of slices — ordinary/Fellenius, simplified Bishop's method), factor of safety, improvement of stability (drainage, retaining structures, rock bolts, soil nailing).
  • Ground improvement: Preloading & vertical drains (sand drains, wick drains), stone columns, vibroflotation, dynamic compaction, grouting (permeation, compaction, jet grouting), soil reinforcement (geotextiles, geogrids, soil nailing), soil stabilization (lime, cement, fly ash, chemical).

Transportation Engineering

  • Highway engineering: Highway development in India (Nagpur plan — 20-year plans), road classification (NH, SH, MDR, ODR, village roads), highway alignment (factors controlling), road geometrics (design speed, sight distance — stopping sight distance SSD & overtaking sight distance OSD, super elevation, extra width on curves, transition curves, gradients & grade compensation, sight distance at intersections), traffic engineering (traffic volume studies, speed studies — spot speed & space speed, origin-destination studies, parking studies, accident studies, traffic signs, markings, signals — fixed time & vehicle-actuated, channelization, rotary intersections, level of service LOS), highway capacity (basic capacity, practical capacity, design capacity, PCU values), flexible pavement design (flexible pavement — layers: subgrade, sub-base, base course, surface course; CBR method — IRC method for flexible pavement design), rigid pavement design (cement concrete slab, joint types — expansion, contraction, construction, longitudinal, dowel bars & tie bars, IRC method), hill road considerations, roadside development, street lighting, traffic impact assessment.
  • Railway engineering: Permanent way components (rails — types, wear, creep; sleepers — types & functions; ballast — functions & types; ballastless track), creep of rail, gradient & grade compensation, curves (superelevation/cant, cant deficiency & excess, cant gradient, transition curve — length, shift), points & crossings (switches, stock rails, crossing nose, heel of switch, flange way clearance), stations & yards (types of stations, types of yards — passenger, goods, marshalling), signaling & interlocking (absolute block system, automatic block system, color light signaling), track modernization (LWR — long welded rails, prestressed concrete sleepers, high capacity wagons), high-speed rail concepts, metro rail systems (components, stations, signaling — CBTC).
  • Airport engineering: Airport components (runway, taxiway, apron, terminal building, hangars), runway orientation (wind rose), runway length (basic, actual, corrected for elevation, temperature, gradient), geometric design (runway width, safety areas, shoulders, taxiway width, separation distances), visual aids (markings, lights, windsock), airport capacity & delay, zoning laws, noise control.
  • Docks & harbors: Types of harbors (natural, semi-natural, artificial), components (breakwaters, wharves, jetties, quays, piers, docks — wet & dry, slipways, locks), littoral drift, tide, wave action, dredging, coastal protection structures (groynes, sea walls, breakwaters — rubble mound, caisson).
  • Tunnel engineering: Tunnel classification & purposes, surveying for tunnels, methods of drilling & blasting (cut & cover, drilling & blasting cycle — drilling, charging, blasting, ventilation, mucking), lining methods (timber, steel, concrete, shotcrete), ventilation & lighting, drainage, tunneling through soft ground & rock, shield method, TBM (tunnel boring machine), NATM (New Austrian Tunneling Method), muck disposal.
Technique: Flexible pavement design (IRC CBR method)
Question: Design flexible pavement thickness using CBR method.
Answer: IRC method — cumulative standard axles (CSA) = A × [(1+r)^n - 1]/r × 365 × VDF / lanes, where A = initial traffic (CVPD), r = annual traffic growth rate, n = design life, VDF = vehicle damage factor, lanes = number of lanes. Then read thickness of each layer (sub-base, base, base course, wearing course) from IRC:37 design charts based on CBR of subgrade & CSA. Design includes granular sub-base (GSB), wet mix macadam (WMM), dense bituminous macadam (DBM), bituminous concrete (BC).

Memory Tricks

  • Steel vs RCC design: Steel — limit state, partial safety factor γm=1.15 (steel), γc=1.5 (concrete); concrete — limit state, γc=1.5.
  • Bearing capacity: Terzaghi's q_u = cN_c + γD_fN_q + 0.5γBN_γ — factors N_c, N_q, N_γ depend on φ.
  • Permeability: Darcy's law — Q = kiA (k = coefficient of permeability, i = hydraulic gradient, A = area).
  • Traffic capacity: PCU — car = 1.0, motorcycle = 0.5, bicycle = 0.5, auto-rickshaw = 1.2, truck/bus = 3.0 (on urban roads).
