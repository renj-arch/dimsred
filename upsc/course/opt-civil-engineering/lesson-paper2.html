Design of Steel Structures

  • Plastic Analysis: Plastic moment capacity, plastic hinge, plastic hinge mechanism, plastic collapse mechanism, plastic moment capacity M_p = f_y * Z_p where Z_p = plastic section modulus.
  • Plastic Design: Plastic hinge formation, collapse mechanism, load factor (load factor = collapse load / working load), plastic hinge formation sequence, plastic moment capacity M_p = f_y * Z_p.
  • Steel Structures: Limit state design (limit state of strength, limit state of serviceability), partial safety factors (γ_m for material, γ_f for loads), design strength in tension (T_d = min(f_y * A_g / γ_0, 0.9 * f_u * A_e / γ_1)), design strength in compression (design compressive strength f_d = χ * f_y / γ_0 where χ = elastic critical buckling stress / f_y = π² E / (f_y * (KL/r)²)), design of tension members, design of compression members (columns), design of tension members, design of compression members (columns), design of beams (flexural members), design of beam-columns (combined axial and bending).
  • Connections: Bolted connections (bearing type bolts, friction grip bolts, high strength friction grip bolts - HSFG, prying action, shear capacity of bolt P_sf = n * f_ub * A_n / (γ_b * γ_m0) where n = number of bolts, f_ub = ultimate tensile strength of bolt, A_n = net area of bolt at threads, γ_b = partial safety factor for bolts = 1.25, γ_m0 = 1.25), welds (fillet welds - throat thickness t = 0.7 * s where s = leg length, design strength of weld = f_u * t_e * l_eff / (√3 * γ_m0) where t_e = effective throat thickness = k * s where k depends on angle of bevel, maximum weld size = t - 1.5 mm for t ≤ 10 mm, t - 1.5 mm for t > 10 mm).
  • Columns: Axially loaded compression members (design compressive strength f_d = χ * f_y / γ_m0 where χ = elastic critical buckling stress / f_y = π² E / (f_y * (KL/r)²), λ = effective slenderness ratio = KL / r, φ = 0.5 * [1 + α(λ - 0.2) + λ²], χ = 1 / (φ + sqrt(φ² - λ²)), χ = 1 for λ ≤ 0.2).
  • Beams: Laterally supported beams (plastic moment capacity M_p = Z_p * f_y / γ_m0 where Z_p = plastic section modulus), laterally unsupported beams (lateral torsional buckling, moment capacity M_d = β_b * Z_p * f_y / γ_m0 where β_b = bend correction factor = 1.0 for I-section loaded on major axis), deflection limits (L/300 for simply supported, L/250 for cantilever).
  • Columns: Slenderness ratio λ = kL / r where k = effective length factor, L = actual length, r = radius of gyration = √(I/A), slenderness ratio limits (180 for compression members carrying load from floors, 180 for compression members of buildings subject to wind, 180 for compression members of buildings supporting floors, 180 for industrial buildings subject to cranes, 180 for industrial buildings subject to cranes).
  • Plate Girders: Plate girder (built-up section), components (web plate, flange plates, stiffeners - transverse stiffeners, longitudinal stiffeners, bearing stiffeners, bearing stiffeners at supports and concentrated loads, intermediate stiffeners), tension field action, design of web panel (shear buckling), design of flange (flexural strength), design of stiffeners (transverse stiffeners at supports and at point loads, longitudinal stiffeners for deep girders), end bearing stiffeners (bearing stress, buckling of web).
  • Connections: Bolted connections (HSFG bolts - high strength friction grip bolts, slip resistance = μ * N * n where μ = friction coefficient = 0.5 for milled surface, N = clamp force = 0.7 * f_ub * A_b where A_b = shank area of bolt, f_ub = ultimate tensile strength of bolt, n = number of bolts), bearing type bolts (bearing stress = P / (d * t) where d = bolt diameter, t = thickness of thinner plate), weld fillet welds (throat thickness t = 0.7 * s where s = leg length, design strength of weld = f_u * t_e * l_eff / (√3 * γ_m0)), groove welds (complete joint penetration, partial joint penetration).

Steel Design Examples:

Technique: Tension Member Design
Question: Design a tension member to carry a factored load of 400 kN using double angle section. Use Fe 410 steel (f_y = 250 MPa, f_u = 410 MPa).
Answer: Design tension T_d = min(f_y * A_g / γ_0, 0.9 * f_u * A_n / γ_1). Assume A_g = 4000 mm², try ISA 100 x 100 x 10 (A_g = 1910 mm²). Check: T_d = min(250 * 4000 / 1.0, 0.9 * 410 * A_g / 1.25). For 4000 mm²: T_d = min(1000 kN, 0.9 * 410 * 4000 / 1.25) = min(1000 kN, 1168 kN) = 1000 kN > 400 kN OK. Design is adequate.

Memory Tricks

  • Limit State Design: Partial safety factors: γ_m (material) = 1.0 for shop fabrication, 1.25 for field fabrication; γ_f (loads) = 1.5 for DL, 1.5 for LL, 1.2 for wind/seismic.
  • Slenderness Ratio Limits: Compression member carrying load from floors: λ_max = 180; tension member: λ_max = 180; member carrying wind/seismic loads: λ_max = 250; tie member: λ_max = 350.
  • Slenderness Limits for Beams: λ_w / t_w ≤ 110 for web (Class 1), λ_f / t_f = 9.3 * ε for flange (Class 1), where ε = sqrt(250 / f_y).
  • Weld Size: Minimum fillet weld size = 3 mm for t ≤ 10 mm, 5 mm for 10 < t ≤ 20 mm, 6 mm for t > 20 mm.
Mains strategy
Design of steel structures: draw free body diagrams, show load paths, calculate effective lengths for columns based on end conditions, check slenderness limits, use IS 800:2007 clauses, quote codes (IS 800:2007 for steel design, IS 875 for loads), draw typical connection details (gusset plate, splice, column base). Data: IS 800:2007 partial safety factors: γ_m0 = 1.0 for shop fabrication, 1.25 for field fabrication; γ_f = 1.5 for DL, 1.5 for LL, 1.5 for wind/seismic; steel grades: Fe250 (250 MPa), Fe410 (410 MPa), Fe490 (490 MPa); common steel grades: Fe 250, Fe 410, Fe 490.
