Key Concepts

Kashmiri Paper I — History of the Language, Movements, Authors and Works

History of the Language

Kashmiri is a Dardic language of the Indo-Aryan family. Its literary tradition begins with the 14th-century mystic Lal Ded; Persian and Sufi influences shaped the medieval and modern periods.

Literary Periods and Movements

  • Mystic period (14th–16th c.): Lal Ded’s vakhs and Nund Rishi’s shruks.
  • Medieval and Sufi period (16th–18th c.): Habba Khatoon, Mahmud Gami and Rasul Mir.
  • Modern period (19th–20th c.): Mahjoor, Zinda Kaul and Dina Nath Nadim; modern poetry and prose.
  • Contemporary period: Diaspora writing, conflict literature and new media.

Major Authors

  • Lal Ded (Lalleshwari): 14th-century Shaiva mystic poet; her vakhs are foundational to Kashmiri literature.
  • Nund Rishi: 14th–15th century Sufi saint and poet; the Rishi order.
  • Habba Khatoon: 16th-century poet known for love lyrics.
  • Ghulam Ahmad Mahjoor: Pioneer of modern Kashmiri poetry.
  • Dina Nath Nadim: Modern poet and lyricist.

Major Works

  • Lalla Vakyani — Lal Ded (Lalleshwari): Mystic verses (vakhs) attributed to Lal Ded.
  • Shruks — Nund Rishi: Devotional verses of the Rishi order.
  • Habba Khatoon’s lyrics — Habba Khatoon: Love lyrics of separation and longing.
  • Modern Kashmiri poetry — Ghulam Ahmad Mahjoor: Mahjoor pioneered modern Kashmiri poetry.

Solved Example: Author and Period
Question: Place Lal Ded (Lalleshwari) in the literary history of Kashmiri and assess their contribution.
Answer: Lal Ded (Lalleshwari) belongs to the Mystic period (14th–16th c.) phase. 14th-century Shaiva mystic poet; her vakhs are foundational to Kashmiri literature. The contribution should be assessed through language, theme and form, and through influence on later writers.

Memory Tricks

  • Timeline: Mystic period (14th–16th c.) → Medieval and Sufi period (16th–18th c.) → Modern period (19th–20th c.) → Contemporary period.
  • Authors: Lal Ded (Lalleshwari), Nund Rishi, Habba Khatoon, Ghulam Ahmad Mahjoor, Dina Nath Nadim.
  • Works: Lalla Vakyani, Shruks, Habba Khatoon’s lyrics, Modern Kashmiri poetry.
