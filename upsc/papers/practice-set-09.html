Q2. General Studies
Consider the following statements regarding the National Green Tribunal (NGT): 1. It is a specialized body set up to expeditiously deal with environmental related litigation. 2. The head of the NGT is known as the Chairperson, who is appointed by the President of India. 3. The decisions of the NGT are not subject to appeal in any court of law. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
Correct Answer: A
Statement 1 is correct as the NGT was established under the National Green Tribunal Act, 2010 to handle environmental disputes quickly. Statement 2 is correct; the Chairperson of the NGT is appointed by the President of India. Statement 3 is incorrect because Section 14 of the NGT Act allows for appeals to be filed directly to the Supreme Court of India against any order or decision of the Tribunal.

Q8. Current Affairs
As per the table below showing Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR) in usual status (ps+ss) for persons aged 15 years and above as per the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) 2023-24, which category exhibits the highest absolute gender gap (difference between Male and Female LFPR in percentage points)?
CategoryMale LFPR (%)Female LFPR (%)
Rural79.547.6
Urban77.228.0
All India78.841.7
A. Rural
B. Urban
C. All India
D. The gap is equal across all categories
Correct Answer: B
**Shortcut (Conceptual Elimination):** Male LFPR is relatively stable across Rural (~79.5%) and Urban (~77.2%) areas. However, Female LFPR differs drastically: Rural females participate significantly more (~47.6%) due to agricultural/allyied engagement, while Urban female participation is very low (~28.0%). Since Gap = Male % - Female %, the gap is widest where Female % is lowest. Urban Female LFPR (28.0%) < All India Female LFPR (41.7%) < Rural Female LFPR (47.6%). Therefore, Urban Gap > All India Gap > Rural Gap. Select **Urban**. **Worked Solution:** 1. **Rural Gap** = 79.5 - 47.6 = **31.9 percentage points** 2. **Urban Gap** = 77.2 - 28.0 = **49.2 percentage points** 3. **All India Gap** = 78.8 - 41.7 = **37.1 percentage points** Comparing the gaps: 49.2 (Urban) > 37.1 (All India) > 31.9 (Rural). The highest gap is in the **Urban** category.

Q15. General Studies
Under Article 368 of the Constitution of India, which of the following categories of amendments requires ratification by the Legislatures of not less than one-half of the States?
A. Election of the President of India
B. Extent of the executive power of the Union and the States
C. Representation of States in Parliament
D. All of the above
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Article 368 proviso lists specific federal provisions requiring state ratification. If a candidate identifies that even two of the listed options (e.g., Election of President and Representation of States) are entrenched federal provisions, 'All of the above' becomes the logical choice without verifying the third. Full Solution: Article 368 stipulates that amendments to provisions affecting the federal structure (listed in the proviso) require special majority of Parliament plus ratification by half the State Legislatures. The list explicitly includes: (a) Articles 54 and 55 (Election of President); (b) Articles 73 and 162 (Extent of executive power); (c) Chapter IV of Part V, Chapter V of Part VI, Chapter I of Part XI (Judiciary, Legislative Relations); (d) Any of the Lists in Seventh Schedule; (e) Representation of States in Parliament; (f) Proviso to Article 368 itself. Since all three options (A, B, C) are explicitly mentioned in the proviso to Article 368, option D is correct.
