Q1. CSAT
As per the table, a shopkeeper marks his goods 50% above the Cost Price and offers a discount of 20% on the Marked Price. What is his profit percentage?
StageValue (₹)Calculation
Cost Price (CP)100Base
Marked Price (MP)150CP × 1.5
Selling Price (SP)120MP × 0.8
Profit %20%(SP-CP)/CP × 100
A. 15%
B. 20%
C. 25%
D. 30%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use Multiplying Factors. CP = 100. MP = 100 × 1.5 = 150. SP = 150 × 0.8 = 120. Profit % = (120-100)/100 × 100 = 20%. Alternatively, Effective % change = 50 - 20 + (50×-20)/100 = 30 - 10 = 20%. Step-by-step: Let CP = ₹100. Marked Price = ₹100 + 50% of ₹100 = ₹150. Discount = 20% of ₹150 = ₹30. Selling Price = ₹150 - ₹30 = ₹120. Profit = ₹20. Profit % = (20/100) × 100 = 20%.
Q2. CSAT
A man starts from point O, walks 6 km North, turns right and walks 8 km, turns left and walks 6 km, and finally turns right and walks 8 km. What is the straight-line distance from his final position to the starting point O?
A. 10 km
B. 15 km
C. 18 km
D. 20 km
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Track net displacement on axes. North: +6 +6 = +12 km. East: +8 +8 = +16 km. Distance = √(12² + 16²) = √(144+256) = √400 = 20 km (3-4-5 triplet scaled by 4). Step-by-step: Coordinates: Start (0,0). Move N6 → (0,6). Right(E)8 → (8,6). Left(N)6 → (8,12). Right(E)8 → (16,12). Distance = √(16² + 12²) = 20 km.
Q3. CSAT
A shopkeeper marks up the price of an article by 40% and then offers a discount of 25% on the marked price. What is his profit or loss percentage?
A. 5% Profit
B. 5% Loss
C. 10% Profit
D. 10% Loss
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (Successive Percentage Formula): Net % change = a + b + (ab/100). Here a = +40 (markup), b = -25 (discount). Net = 40 - 25 + (40 * -25)/100 = 15 - 10 = +5%. Positive indicates Profit. Full Method: Let CP = 100. MP = 140. SP = 140 * (1 - 0.25) = 140 * 0.75 = 105. Profit = 5. Profit % = (5/100)*100 = 5%.
Q4. CSAT
Rearrange the following four sentences (P, Q, R, S) to form a coherent paragraph. P: However, the rate of deforestation has slowed down in recent years due to conservation efforts. Q: Forests cover about 31% of the land area on our planet. R: They play a crucial role in mitigating climate change by absorbing carbon dioxide. S: Despite their importance, forests continue to be threatened by agricultural expansion and logging.
A. Q-R-S-P
B. Q-S-R-P
C. R-Q-S-P
D. S-Q-R-P
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (Mandatory Pairs & Flow): Q is the best opening sentence (general fact/definition). R follows Q explaining 'crucial role' (elaboration on importance). S follows R with contrast 'Despite their importance' (threats). P concludes with current status 'However, rate slowed...'. Sequence: Q-R-S-P. Step-by-step: 1. Identify opening: Q introduces the subject (Forests coverage). R and S cannot open as they use pronouns/references ('They', 'Despite their importance'). 2. Link Q-R: 'They' in R refers to Forests in Q. 3. Link R-S: 'Despite their importance' in S contrasts the role mentioned in R. 4. Link S-P: 'However' in P contrasts the threat in S with positive recent trend. Order: Q-R-S-P.
Q5. CSAT
A container holds 80 litres of pure milk. 8 litres of milk is drawn out and replaced with water. This process is repeated two more times (three times in total). What is the quantity of milk remaining in the container?
A. 58.32 litres
B. 56.24 litres
C. 54.88 litres
D. 52.16 litres
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the replacement formula $V_{final} = V_{initial} \times (1 - \frac{replaced}{total})^n$. Fraction replaced = 8/80 = 1/10. Milk left = $80 \times (9/10)^3 = 80 \times 729/1000 = 58.32$ litres. Step-by-step: After 1st replacement, milk = $80 \times 9/10 = 72$ L. After 2nd, milk = $72 \times 9/10 = 64.8$ L. After 3rd, milk = $64.8 \times 9/10 = 58.32$ L.
Q6. CSAT
Refer to the table showing a journey split into two equal distances. If the average speed for the whole journey is 48 km/h and the speed for the first half is 40 km/h, what is the speed for the second half?
SegmentDistanceSpeed (km/h)Time (h)
First Halfd40d/40
Second Halfd?d/x
Total2d48 (Avg)2d/48
A. 55 km/h
B. 60 km/h
C. 65 km/h
D. 70 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For equal distances, Average Speed = Harmonic Mean of speeds = 2ab/(a+b). Given Avg = 48, a = 40. 48 = 2×40×b/(40+b) => 48(40+b) = 80b => 1920 + 48b = 80b => 32b = 1920 => b = 60. Step-by-step: Let total distance = 2d. Time for first half = d/40. Time for second half = d/x. Total time = d(1/40 + 1/x). Avg Speed = 2d / [d(1/40 + 1/x)] = 2 / (1/40 + 1/x) = 48. Solving gives x = 60 km/h.
Q7. CSAT
As per the table below, the price of a commodity increases by 25%. By what percentage must a household reduce its consumption to keep the total expenditure on the commodity unchanged?
ParameterInitial ValueChange
Price (₹/kg)100+25%
Consumption (kg)10?
Expenditure (₹)1000Constant
A. 16.67%
B. 20%
C. 25%
D. 33.33%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the Product Constancy rule (A × B = Constant). If Price increases by 1/4 (25%), Consumption must decrease by 1/(4+1) = 1/5 = 20%. Step-by-step: Let initial Price = P, Consumption = C, Expenditure = E = P×C. New Price = 1.25P. New Consumption = C'. Since E is constant, 1.25P × C' = P × C => C' = C/1.25 = 0.8C. Reduction = (C - 0.8C)/C × 100 = 20%.

Correct Answer: D

Correct Answer: A

Q14. CSAT
A sum of money becomes 1.5 times itself in 5 years at simple interest. What is the annual rate of interest?
A. 8%
B. 10%
C. 12%
D. 15%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Rate = ((Amount/Principal - 1) × 100) / Time = ((1.5 - 1) × 100) / 5 = (0.5 × 100) / 5 = 10%. Full method: Let principal = P. Amount = 1.5P, so SI = 0.5P. SI = P×R×T/100 => 0.5P = P×R×5/100 => R = (0.5×100)/5 = 10%.
Q15. CSAT
Two persons start simultaneously from points A and B towards each other. The ratio of their speeds is 4:5. They meet at a point 80 km from A. What is the distance between A and B?
A. 160 km
B. 180 km
C. 200 km
D. 220 km
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: When time is constant, Distance Ratio = Speed Ratio. Speed ratio = 4:5, so Distance covered from A : Distance covered from B = 4:5. Distance from A (4 parts) = 80 km → 1 part = 20 km. Total distance = 9 parts = 9 × 20 = 180 km. Full Method: Let speeds be 4x and 5x km/h. Time to meet = t. Distance by first = 4xt = 80 → xt = 20. Distance by second = 5xt = 5 × 20 = 100 km. Total distance = 80 + 100 = 180 km.
