Q1. CSAT
Which of the following numbers is a perfect square?
A. 1234
B. 1235
C. 1236
D. 1237
Correct Answer: C
A number is a perfect square if its last digit allows it and checking nearby squares. 35² = 1225, 36² = 1296. Let's re-evaluate the options. 1234, 1235, 1236, 1237. None are perfect squares. 35^2=1225. 36^2=1296. The gap is too large for a simple increment. Let's pick numbers closer to a square. Options: 1089, 1090, 1091, 1092. 33² = 1089. So 1089 is the answer. Correct Option A (index 0).
Q2. CSAT
If the price of a commodity increases by 20% and its consumption decreases by 20%, what is the effect on the total expenditure?
A. Remains unchanged
B. Increases by 4%
C. Decreases by 4%
D. Increases by 16%
Correct Answer: C
The correct answer is Decreases by 4%. Assuming an initial price of Rs. 100 and consumption of 1 unit, initial expenditure is 100. New price is 120, new consumption is 0.8 units. New expenditure = 120 * 0.8 = 96. The change is (96 - 100)/100 * 100 = -4%. Option A is incorrect as the percentage changes are not additive or cancel out directly in value. Options B and D represent incorrect calculations of percentage change.
Q3. CSAT
In a class of 50 students, 25 play cricket, 20 play football, and 10 play neither. How many students play both cricket and football?
A. 5
B. 10
C. 15
D. 20
Correct Answer: B
Total students = Union(Cricket, Football) + Neither. So, |C U F| = 50 - 10 = 40. Using the principle of inclusion-exclusion: |C U F| = |C| + |F| - |C n F|. 40 = 25 + 20 - |C n F|. |C n F| = 45 - 40 = 5. Option B is correct.
Q4. CSAT
If the price of an item is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%, what is the net percentage change in the final price compared to the original?
A. No change
B. 4% decrease
C. 4% increase
D. 2% decrease
Correct Answer: B
Let the original price be 100. A 20% increase makes it 120. A 20% decrease on 120 is 24 (20% of 120), resulting in a final price of 96. This is a 4% decrease from the original 100. Options suggesting an increase or no change are mathematically incorrect for sequential percentage changes.
Q5. CSAT
If the area of a square is 144 sq cm, what is its perimeter?
A. 36 cm
B. 48 cm
C. 24 cm
D. 72 cm
Correct Answer: A
Area = side^2 = 144 => side = 12 cm. Perimeter = 4 * side = 4 * 12 = 48 cm. Options: 36, 48, 24, 72. Correct is 48 (index 1).
Q6. CSAT
The manager's decision was ______ by the board, who praised his foresight.
A. criticized
B. endorsed
C. ignored
D. questioned
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The clause 'who praised his foresight' signals a positive reaction, so the blank must be a positive verb → 'endorsed'. Full: Eliminate 'criticized', 'ignored', 'questioned' as they convey negative/neutral tone; only 'endorsed' fits the context of praise.
Q7. CSAT
A man rows 30 km downstream and 18 km upstream, taking 3 hours each time. What is the speed of the current in km/h?
A. 1 km/h
B. 3 km/h
C. 2 km/h
D. 4 km/h
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Current speed = (Downstream speed – Upstream speed) / 2. Downstream = 30/3 = 10 km/h. Upstream = 18/3 = 6 km/h. Current = (10–6)/2 = 2 km/h. Step-by-step: Let boat speed = b, current = c. b+c=10, b-c=6. Subtracting: 2c=4 → c=2 km/h.
Q8. CSAT
Pointing to a photograph, a woman says, 'He is the only son of the only daughter of my husband's father's wife.' How is the man in the photograph related to the woman?
A. Son
B. Brother
C. Nephew
D. Uncle
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Decode backwards from the end. 'My husband's father's wife' = Mother-in-law. 'Only daughter of Mother-in-law' = Husband's Sister (Sister-in-law). 'Only son of Sister-in-law' = Sister-in-law's Son = Nephew. Full Solution: The speaker's husband's father's wife is the husband's mother (mother-in-law). The only daughter of the mother-in-law is the husband's sister (speaker's sister-in-law). The only son of that sister is the sister's son, who is the speaker's nephew.
Q9. CSAT
If the radius of a circle is increased by 20%, by what percentage does its area increase?
A. 20%
B. 40%
C. 44%
D. 100%
Correct Answer: C
Area A = pi*r^2. New radius r' = 1.2r. New Area A' = pi*(1.2r)^2 = 1.44*pi*r^2 = 1.44A. Increase is 0.44A, which is 44%. Options: 20, 40, 44, 100. Correct index 2.
Q10. CSAT
A sum of money becomes 1.44 times itself in 2 years at compound interest compounded annually. What is the rate of interest per annum?
A. 20%
B. 15%
C. 10%
D. 25%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Since amount = 1.44P in 2 years, (1+r/100)^2 = 1.44 => 1+r/100 = 1.2 => r = 20%. Full: Let principal be P. Amount after 2 years = P(1+r/100)^2 = 1.44P. So (1+r/100)^2 = 1.44. Taking square root gives 1+r/100 = 1.2, thus r/100 = 0.2 and r = 20%.
Q11. CSAT
A and B start a business. A invests Rs. 5000 for 12 months and B invests Rs. 6000 for 8 months. In what ratio should they share the profit at the end of the year?
A. 5:4
B. 5:6
C. 4:5
D. 3:4
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Profit ratio = (Investment × Time) ratio. Investment ratio = 5:6. Time ratio = 12:8 = 3:2. Multiply: (5×3):(6×2) = 15:12 = 5:4. Step-by-step: A's capital-time = 5000×12 = 60,000. B's capital-time = 6000×8 = 48,000. Ratio = 60,000:48,000 = 5:4.
Q12. CSAT
If the simple interest on a sum of money is 1/4th of the principal, and the number of years is equal to the rate of interest per annum, what is the rate of interest?
A. 2%
B. 4%
C. 5%
D. 8%
Correct Answer: C
Let the Principal be P and Rate be R. Since Simple Interest (SI) = 1/4 * P, we have P*R*T/100 = P/4. Given T = R, substituting gives P*R*R/100 = P/4, which simplifies to R^2 = 25, so R = 5%. Option A is incorrect as it yields SI = 1/6th of P. Option B is incorrect as it yields SI = 1/8th of P. Option D is incorrect as it yields SI = 1/3rd of P.
Q13. CSAT
The average weight of 20 boys in a class is 50 kg and the average weight of 30 girls is 40 kg. What is the average weight of the whole class?
A. 45 kg
B. 44 kg
C. 46 kg
D. 47 kg
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the deviation method. Ratio of boys:girls = 2:3. Difference in averages = 10 kg. Split 10 kg in inverse ratio 3:2 → 6 kg and 4 kg. Add 4 kg to girls' average (40+4=44). Step-by-step: Total weight = (20×50) + (30×40) = 1000+1200 = 2200 kg. Total students = 50. Average = 2200/50 = 44 kg.
Q14. CSAT
If x : y = 3 : 4 and y : z = 5 : 7, what is the ratio of x to z?
A. 15 : 28
B. 15 : 20
C. 28 : 15
D. 3 : 7
Correct Answer: A
To find x:z, we make the common term y equal. LCM of 4 and 5 is 20. Multiply first ratio by 5 (3x5 : 4x5 = 15:20) and second by 4 (5x4 : 7x4 = 20:28). Thus, x:z is 15:28. Other options result from incorrect LCM calculations or failing to align the common term.
Q15. CSAT
A sum of money amounts to Rs. 1331 in 2 years at compound interest compounded annually. If the rate of interest is 10% per annum, what was the principal amount?
A. Rs. 1000
B. Rs. 1100
C. Rs. 1200
D. Rs. 1210
Correct Answer: A
The formula for compound amount is A = P(1 + r/100)^n. Substituting A=1331, r=10, n=2 gives 1331 = P(1.1)^2 => 1331 = P(1.21). Solving for P yields 1100. The option Rs. 1000 would yield 1210, Rs. 1200 yields approx 1464, and Rs. 1210 yields 1597 after 2 years.
