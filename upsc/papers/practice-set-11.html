Consider the following statements regarding the 'Kigali Amendment' to the Montreal Protocol: 1. It aims to phase down Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), which are potent greenhouse gases. 2. It legally binds developed and developing countries to the same phase-down schedule. 3. India has ratified the Kigali Amendment and its phase-down schedule begins earlier than that of China. 4. B. 1, 2 and 3 only
