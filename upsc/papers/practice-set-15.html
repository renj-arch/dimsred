Q1. General Studies
With reference to the cultural history of India, consider the following statements regarding the 'Sarvodaya' movement: 1. The term 'Sarvodaya' was first coined by Mahatma Gandhi as the title of his translation of John Ruskin's 'Unto This Last'. 2. Vinoba Bhave's Bhoodan Movement was a direct offshoot of the Sarvodaya philosophy. 3. Jayaprakash Narayan's 'Total Revolution' in the 1970s was explicitly based on Sarvodaya principles. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use 'Person-Idea Association' verification. Statement 1: Gandhi coined 'Sarvodaya' (Welfare of All) for his 1908 Gujarati translation of Ruskin's 'Unto This Last' (Correct). Statement 2: Vinoba Bhave is the quintessential Sarvodaya leader; Bhoodan (Gift of Land) was the practical application of Sarvodaya (Correct). Statement 3: JP Narayan's 'Total Revolution' (1974) was deeply rooted in Gandhian Sarvodaya ideals of non-violent societal reconstruction (Correct). All three statements are factually accurate.
