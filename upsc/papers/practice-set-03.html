A company has 15 machines, and each machine produces 20 units per hour. If the company operates for 8 hours a day, how many units will it produce in a day?
A. 2400 units
B. 3000 units
C. 3600 units
D. 4800 units
Correct Answer: A
To find the total units produced in a day, we multiply the number of machines (15), the units produced per hour per machine (20), and the number of hours (8): 15 * 20 * 8 = 2400 units.

Q5. [+2, -0.66] CSAT
If a car travels from city A to city B at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h, what is its average speed?
A. 48 km/h
B. 50 km/h
C. 55 km/h
D. 60 km/h
Correct Answer: A
The average speed is calculated as total distance divided by total time. Let's assume the distance between city A and city B is 120 km. The car takes 2 hours to travel from A to B and 3 hours to return. The total distance traveled is 240 km, and the total time taken is 5 hours. The average speed is 240 km / 5 hours = 48 km/h.
