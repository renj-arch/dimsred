Q1. CSAT
If a car travels from city A to city B at an average speed of 60 km/h and returns at an average speed of 40 km/h, what is the average speed for the entire trip?
A. 48 km/h
B. 50 km/h
C. 55 km/h
D. 60 km/h
Correct Answer: A
To find the average speed for the entire trip, we need to find the total distance traveled and the total time taken. Since the distance from A to B is the same as the distance from B to A, we can let the distance be x. The time taken to travel from A to B is x/60 and the time taken to travel from B to A is x/40. The total time is x/60 + x/40 = (2x + 3x)/120 = 5x/120 = x/24. The average speed is total distance/total time = 2x/(x/24) = 48 km/h.

Q6. CSAT

A. 48 km/h
B. 50 km/h
C. 55 km/h
D. 60 km/h
Correct Answer: A
The average speed is calculated as total distance divided by total time. Let's assume the distance between city A and city B is 120 km. The car takes 2 hours to travel from A to B and 3 hours to return. The total distance traveled is 240 km, and the total time taken is 5 hours. The average speed is 240 km / 5 hours = 48 km/h.

Q8. CSAT
A bakery sells 250 loaves of bread per day. They make a profit of Rs. 2 per loaf. If the cost price of 100 loaves is Rs. 500, what is the total daily profit?
A. Rs. 500
B. Rs. 1000
C. Rs. 1500
D. Rs. 2000
Correct Answer: A
The cost price of 250 loaves is Rs. 1250. The selling price is Rs. 2 more than the cost price per loaf, so the total revenue is Rs. 250 x (cost price + Rs. 2) = Rs. 250 x (5 + 2) = Rs. 1750. The total daily profit is Rs. 1750 - Rs. 1250 = Rs. 500.

Q12. CSAT
A company has 100 employees, and the average salary is Rs. 50,000 per month. If 20 new employees are hired at an average salary of Rs. 55,000 per month, what is the new average salary of all employees?
A. Rs. 50,500
B. Rs. 50,900
C. Rs. 51,000
D. Rs. 52,000
Correct Answer: C
The total salary of the original 100 employees is Rs. 50,000 x 100 = Rs. 5,000,000. The total salary of the 20 new employees is Rs. 55,000 x 20 = Rs. 1,100,000. The total salary of all 120 employees is Rs. 5,000,000 + Rs. 1,100,000 = Rs. 6,100,000. The new average salary is Rs. 6,100,000 / 120 = Rs. 50,833.33, which is approximately Rs. 51,000.
Q13. CSAT
A bat and a ball together cost Rs. 100. The bat costs Rs. 20 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?
A. Rs. 40
B. Rs. 50
C. Rs. 60
D. Rs. 70
Correct Answer: A
Let the cost of the ball be x. Then the cost of the bat is x + 20. The total cost is x + (x + 20) = 100. Solving for x, we get 2x + 20 = 100, 2x = 80, x = 40.
