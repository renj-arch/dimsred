Q1. General Studies
Which of the following Biosphere Reserves of India is **NOT** part of the UNESCO World Network of Biosphere Reserves (WNBR)?
A. Nilgiri
B. Nanda Devi
C. Agasthyamalai
D. Manas
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: India has 18 Biosphere Reserves but only 12 in UNESCO WNBR. Eliminate the famous 'early entries': Nilgiri (1st, 2000), Nanda Devi (2004), Agasthyamalai (2016). Manas (Assam) is a National Park, Tiger Reserve & UNESCO World Heritage Site, but its Biosphere Reserve is *not* in the WNBR. Worked Solution: The 12 Indian sites in UNESCO WNBR are: Nilgiri, Gulf of Mannar, Sunderbans, Nanda Devi, Nokrek, Pachmarhi, Simlipal, Achanakmar-Amarkantak, Great Nicobar, Agasthyamalai, Kanchenjunga, Panna. Manas BR is absent from this list.
Q2. General Studies
As per the demographic data table below, State X has a Total Fertility Rate (TFR) of 1.6 and a Contraceptive Prevalence Rate (CPR) of 68%. State Y has a TFR of 2.8 and CPR of 45%. Using the inverse approximation shortcut (TFR ≈ k / CPR), which state's data aligns better with the expected inverse relationship, and what is the approximate constant 'k' for State X?
StateTFRCPR (%)
State X1.668
State Y2.845
A. A State X, k ≈ 109
B. B State Y, k ≈ 126
C. C State X, k ≈ 108
D. D State Y, k ≈ 108
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The inverse relationship implies TFR × CPR ≈ Constant (k). Calculate k for both states mentally. State X: 1.6 × 68. 1.6 × 70 = 112, minus 1.6×2 = 3.2 → 108.8 ≈ 109. State Y: 2.8 × 45. 2.8 × 50 = 140, minus 2.8×5 = 14 → 126. Since k should be stable for the model, State X (109) is closer to typical demographic constants (~100-110) than State Y (126). Option Elimination: Options for State X are 109 (A) and 108 (C). 108.8 rounds to 109. Worked Solution: For State X, k = TFR × CPR = 1.6 × 68 = 108.8 ≈ 109. For State Y, k = 2.8 × 45 = 126. The constant 'k' is more consistent for State X with standard demographic approximation models.

Q6. General Studies
The price of a commodity is increased by 20% and subsequently decreased by 20%. What is the net percentage change in the price?
A. 4% increase
B. 4% decrease
C. No change
D. 2% decrease
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For successive changes of +x% and -x%, net effect is always a decrease of x²/100 %. Here, (20)²/100 = 4% decrease. Worked: Assume initial price = 100. After 20% increase = 120. After 20% decrease = 120 × 0.80 = 96. Net change = -4. Percentage change = (4/100) × 100 = 4% decrease.
Q7. General Studies
Consider the following pairs of Constitutional Bodies and their corresponding Articles in the Constitution of India: 1. Election Commission : Article 324 2. Union Public Service Commission : Article 315 3. Comptroller and Auditor General of India : Article 148 4. Finance Commission : Article 280 Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
A. 1, 2 and 3 only
B. 1, 3 and 4 only
C. 2, 3 and 4 only
D. 1, 2, 3 and 4
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Option Elimination. Verify the most distinct articles first. Article 148 (CAG) and Article 280 (Finance Commission) are unique and correct. Article 324 (Election Commission) is a standard fact. Article 315 (UPSC) is the correct founding article (Chapter II, Part XIV). Since all four are standard constitutional provisions, the option '1, 2, 3 and 4' must be correct. Worked Solution: Article 324 vests superintendence of elections in EC. Article 315 provides for UPSC and State PSCs. Article 148 establishes CAG. Article 280 provides for Finance Commission. All pairs are correctly matched.

Q10. General Studies
Which of the following provisions was **NOT** introduced by the Constitution (Forty-fourth Amendment) Act, 1978?
A. Removal of Right to Property from the list of Fundamental Rights.
B. Provision that the President shall act on the advice of the Council of Ministers.
C. Insertion of Fundamental Duties in Part IVA.
D. Replacement of "internal disturbance" with "armed rebellion" for National Emergency.
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The 44th Amendment (1978) was a 'corrective' amendment to undo the 42nd Amendment (1976). Fundamental Duties were *added* by the 42nd Amendment; the 44th Amendment did not insert them. Worked Solution: The 44th Amendment: 1) Deleted Art 31 (Right to Property) making it a legal right under Art 300A. 2) Amended Art 74 to make President bound by CoM advice. 3) Amended Art 352 replacing 'internal disturbance' with 'armed rebellion'. Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Art 51A) were inserted by the 42nd Amendment, 1976.

Q14. General Studies
Which of the following provisions is **NOT** mandated by the Constitution (Seventy-third Amendment) Act, 1992 (Panchayati Raj)?
A. Reservation of not less than one-third seats for women in Panchayats at all levels.
B. Reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in proportion to their population in the Panchayat area.
C. Reservation of seats for Other Backward Classes (OBCs) in Panchayats.
D. Constitution of a State Finance Commission every five years to review the financial position of Panchayats.
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Elimination technique. The 73rd Amendment *mandates* Women reservation (Art 243D), SC/ST reservation (Art 243D), and State Finance Commission (Art 243I). OBC reservation under Art 243D(6) is discretionary ('State may by law provide'), not mandatory. Full: Article 243D mandates reservation for SC/ST (proportionate) and Women (1/3). Article 243D(6) enables States to provide OBC reservation voluntarily. Article 243I mandates SFC constitution every 5 years.
Q15. General Studies
As per the table below showing Major Fiscal Indicators for a hypothetical Union Budget (in ₹ Lakh Crore), what is the Primary Deficit?
ItemAmount (₹ Lakh Crore)
Revenue Receipts20.0
Non-Debt Capital Receipts0.5
Total Expenditure35.0
Interest Payments9.0
A. 4.5
B. 5.5
C. 6.5
D. 7.5
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit – Interest Payments. Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure – (Revenue Receipts + Non-Debt Capital Receipts). Compute FD = 35.0 – (20.0 + 0.5) = 14.5. Then PD = 14.5 – 9.0 = 5.5. Full: Non-debt receipts = 20.5. Fiscal Deficit = 35.0 – 20.5 = 14.5. Primary Deficit = 14.5 – 9.0 = 5.5 Lakh Crore.
