Q2. General Studies
Which Constitutional Amendment Act inserted the Tenth Schedule, containing provisions for disqualification on grounds of defection, into the Constitution of India?
A. 52nd Amendment Act, 1985
B. 73rd Amendment Act, 1992
C. 91st Amendment Act, 2003
D. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Link Schedule number to subject. 10th Schedule = Anti-Defection = 52nd Amendment (1985). Eliminate 42nd (Mini Constitution), 73rd (Panchayati Raj), 91st (Strengthened Anti-Defection later). Worked: The Tenth Schedule was added by the 52nd Amendment Act, 1985, during the Rajiv Gandhi government to curb political defections. The 91st Amendment Act, 2003, later amended the Tenth Schedule to make disqualification more stringent. The 73rd Amendment (1992) added Part IX (Panchayats) and the 42nd Amendment (1976) is known as the 'Mini Constitution'.
Q3. General Studies
Which of the following rivers is a major right-bank tributary of the River Ganga?
A. Ghaghara
B. Gandak
C. Son
D. Kosi
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Classify by origin. Right-bank tributaries (Peninsular origin): Yamuna, Son. Left-bank tributaries (Himalayan origin): Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Gomati. Eliminate the three Himalayan rivers (Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi) to select Son. Worked: The Ganga receives tributaries from the Himalayas (left bank/North) and the Peninsular Plateau (right bank/South). Ghaghara, Gandak, and Kosi are major snow-fed Himalayan rivers joining from the left. The Son originates from the Amarkantak plateau in Madhya Pradesh and joins the Ganga near Patna (Bihar) from the right bank, making it a principal right-bank tributary along with the Yamuna.

Q5. General Studies
What is the unit digit of the expression (7^95 + 3^58)?
A. 0
B. 2
C. 4
D. 6
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits (cycle of 4). For 7^95, 95 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit of 7^3 = 3. For 3^58, 58 mod 4 = 2 → unit digit of 3^2 = 9. Sum = 3 + 9 = 12 → unit digit 2. Worked: Cyclicity of 7 is 7, 9, 3, 1. 95 = 4×23 + 3, so 3rd position = 3. Cyclicity of 3 is 3, 9, 7, 1. 58 = 4×14 + 2, so 2nd position = 9. 3 + 9 = 12, unit digit is 2.
Q6. General Studies

A. Karnataka
B. Madhya Pradesh
C. Odisha
D. Assam
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Map famous Tiger Reserves/Parks to States. Panna = Khajuraho/Ken River = Madhya Pradesh. Eliminate Karnataka (Bandipur/Nagarhole), Odisha (Simlipal), Assam (Kaziranga/Manas). Worked: Panna National Park, located in Panna and Chhatarpur districts of Madhya Pradesh, was declared a Tiger Reserve in 1994 and a Biosphere Reserve in 2011. Q7. Current Affairs
According to the Climate Change Performance Index (CCPI) 2024 released at COP28, which of the following statements regarding India's performance is correct?
A. India slipped to 10th rank due to increased reliance on coal-fired power plants.
B. India secured the 7th rank, maintaining its position among the top 10 performers.
C. India achieved the 4th rank, becoming the highest-ranked G20 country.
D. India was ranked 15th, categorized as a 'Low' performer in the GHG Emissions category.
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Elimination by Rank Knowledge. CCPI leaves top 3 ranks empty (no country meets 1.5°C path). Denmark is usually 4th. India has consistently been in top 10 (8th in 2023). Option C (4th) is impossible (top 3 empty, Denmark 4th). Option A (10th) and D (15th) indicate a drop, but news highlighted India *improving* or maintaining high rank. Option B (7th) matches the reported improvement from 8th to 7th. Full Solution: CCPI 2024 (published Nov 2023 by Germanwatch, CAN, NewClimate Institute) ranked India 7th (up from 8th in 2023). The top three ranks remain vacant. Denmark ranked 4th. India received 'High' ratings in GHG Emissions and Energy Use, 'Medium' in Climate Policy and Renewable Energy. It was the highest-ranked G20 country (but at 7th, not 4th).
Q8. General Studies
In a certain code language, 'PLANT' is written as 'KOZMG'. How will 'FARMER' be written in that code?
A. UZINVI
B. UZINVJ
C. UZIMVI
D. UZINUI
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Detect reverse alphabet pairing (Atbash cipher) where sum of positions = 27 (A=1, Z=26). Verify: P(16)+K(11)=27, L(12)+O(15)=27. Apply to FARMER: F(6)→U(21), A(1)→Z(26), R(18)→I(9), M(13)→N(14), E(5)→V(22), R(18)→I(9). Worked: Each letter is replaced by its reverse counterpart (A↔Z, B↔Y...). F→U, A→Z, R→I, M→N, E→V, R→I. Code is UZINVI.

Q13. Current Affairs
With reference to the Constitution (One Hundred and Sixth Amendment) Act, 2023 (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam), which of the following statements is correct?
A. It provides for reservation of seats for women in the Rajya Sabha and State Legislative Councils.
B. The reservation shall come into effect immediately upon the assent of the President.
C. The reservation will cease to have effect on the expiration of a period of fifteen years from the commencement of the Act.
D. The rotation of reserved seats for women shall take effect after every general election to the Lok Sabha.
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Eliminate options using key constraints. Option A is wrong because Rajya Sabha/Legislative Councils are excluded. Option B is wrong because implementation is contingent on the first Census after commencement and subsequent delimitation, not immediate. Option D is wrong because rotation occurs after each *delimitation* exercise, not every general election. Option C matches the explicit sunset clause (Article 334A) stating 15 years. Full Solution: The 106th Amendment inserts Articles 330A, 332A, and 334A. It reserves 1/3rd seats in Lok Sabha and State Assemblies (not Upper Houses). Article 334A stipulates the reservation ceases after 15 years from commencement. Implementation requires a Census and Delimitation exercise (not immediate). Rotation of seats occurs after each subsequent delimitation.
