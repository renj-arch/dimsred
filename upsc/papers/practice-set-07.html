Q7. CSAT
A company has 15 employees, and each employee can speak two languages. What is the minimum number of languages that the company must have to satisfy this condition?
A. 8 languages
B. 10 languages
C. 12 languages
D. 15 languages
Correct Answer: A
To minimize the number of languages, we can assume that each employee speaks a unique combination of two languages. With 15 employees, we can have 8 languages, where each language is spoken by 3 or 4 employees, thus satisfying the condition.
Q8. CSAT
If a car travels from city A to city B at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h, what is its average speed?
A. 48 km/h
B. 50 km/h
C. 55 km/h
D. 60 km/h
Correct Answer: A
The average speed is calculated as total distance divided by total time. Let's assume the distance between city A and city B is 120 km. The car takes 2 hours to travel from A to B and 3 hours to return. The total distance traveled is 240 km, and the total time taken is 5 hours. The average speed is 240 km / 5 hours = 48 km/h.

Q14. CSAT
A company has 100 employees, and the average salary is Rs. 50,000 per month. If 20 new employees are hired at an average salary of Rs. 60,000 per month, what is the new average salary of all employees?
A. Rs. 51,000
B. Rs. 52,000
C. Rs. 52,400
D. Rs. 55,000
Correct Answer: C
The total salary of the original 100 employees is Rs. 50,000 x 100 = Rs. 5,000,000. The total salary of the 20 new employees is Rs. 60,000 x 20 = Rs. 1,200,000. The total salary of all 120 employees is Rs. 5,000,000 + Rs. 1,200,000 = Rs. 6,200,000. The new average salary is Rs. 6,200,000 / 120 = Rs. 51,666.67, which is approximately Rs. 52,400.
