Q3. [+2, -0.66] CSAT
A company has 100 employees, and the average salary is Rs. 50,000 per month. If 20 new employees are hired at an average salary of Rs. 60,000 per month, what is the new average salary of all employees?
A. Rs. 51,200
B. Rs. 51,500
C. Rs. 52,000
D. Rs. 55,000
Correct Answer: C
The total salary of the original 100 employees is Rs. 50,000 x 100 = Rs. 5,000,000. The total salary of the 20 new employees is Rs. 60,000 x 20 = Rs. 1,200,000. The total salary of all 120 employees is Rs. 5,000,000 + Rs. 1,200,000 = Rs. 6,200,000. The new average salary is Rs. 6,200,000 / 120 = Rs. 51,666.67, which is approximately Rs. 52,000.
Q4. [+2, -0.66] CSAT
If a car travels from city A to city B at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h, what is its average speed?
A. 48 km/h
B. 50 km/h
C. 55 km/h
D. 60 km/h
Correct Answer: A
The average speed is calculated as total distance divided by total time. Let's assume the distance between city A and city B is 120 km. The car takes 2 hours to travel from A to B and 3 hours to return. The total distance traveled is 240 km, and the total time taken is 5 hours. The average speed is 240 km / 5 hours = 48 km/h.

Q7. [+2, -0.66] CSAT
A bat and a ball together cost Rs. 100. The bat costs Rs. 20 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?
A. Rs. 40
B. Rs. 50
C. Rs. 60
D. Rs. 70
Correct Answer: A
Let the cost of the ball be x. Then the cost of the bat is x + 20. The total cost is x + (x + 20) = 100. Solving for x, we get 2x + 20 = 100, 2x = 80, x = 40.
