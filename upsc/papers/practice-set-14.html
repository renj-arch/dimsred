Q1. CSAT
A train 180 metres long crosses a telegraph pole in 12 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
A. 45 km/h
B. 54 km/h
C. 60 km/h
D. 72 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Speed (km/h) = (Length in metres ÷ Time in seconds) × 18/5. Here (180/12)×18/5 = 15×3.6 = 54 km/h. Step‑by‑step: Distance = 180 m, Time = 12 s → Speed = 15 m/s. Convert: 15 × 18/5 = 54 km/h.

Q3. CSAT
A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above cost price and offers a discount of 15% on the marked price. What is his profit percent?
A. 19%
B. 20%
C. 21%
D. 22%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Multiply the successive factors (1 + 0.40) × (1 – 0.15) = 1.40 × 0.85 = 1.19, giving a 19% profit. Full method: Assume CP = ₹100; MP = ₹140; SP = 85% of ₹140 = ₹119; Profit = ₹19 → 19%.

Q4. CSAT
If 5 men can complete a piece of work in 20 days, how many days will it take for 10 men to complete the same work?
A. 10 days
B. 40 days
C. 15 days
D. 5 days
Correct Answer: A
The total work is constant. If 5 men take 20 days, the man-days required are 5 × 20 = 100. With 10 men working, the time taken will be 100 ÷ 10 = 10 days. Option B implies an inverse relationship where more workers take longer, which is incorrect. Options C and D do not result from the correct calculation of total man-days divided by the number of workers.
Research Team · ~3s · Double Men Halve Days
  1. Men double from 5 to 10
  2. Halve 20 days to 10 days
Q5. CSAT
A rectangular park has a length that is three times its breadth. If the area of the park is 192 square meters, what is the perimeter of the park in meters?
A. 36
B. 48
C. 50
D. 64
Correct Answer: B
Side of square = sqrt(144) = 12 cm. Perimeter = 4 * 12 = 48 cm. Option A (36) is side*3, C (50) is approx diagonal related, D (64) is double perimeter. Correct is B.

Q7. CSAT
If the price of an article increases by 20% and its consumption decreases by 20%, what is the net effect on the expenditure?
A. No change
B. Increase by 4%
C. Decrease by 4%
D. Increase by 16%
Correct Answer: B
Let initial price be P and consumption be C. New price is 1.2P and new consumption is 0.8C. The new expenditure is 1.2 * 0.8 = 0.96 times the original, which represents a decrease of 4%.
Q8. CSAT
A and B can complete a work in 12 days and 18 days respectively. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?
A. 8 days
B. 6 days
C. 10 days
D. 12 days
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Take LCM of 12 and 18 = 36 units. A does 3 units/day, B does 2 units/day. Together 5 units/day. In 4 days they finish 20 units, leaving 16 units. B alone does 2 units/day → 8 days. Full: Work = 1 unit. A's rate = 1/12, B's = 1/18. Combined = 5/36 per day. In 4 days = 20/36 = 5/9 done. Remaining = 4/9. B's time = (4/9)/(1/18)=8 days.

Q11. CSAT
A rectangular field has a length that is twice its breadth. If the area of the field is 288 square meters, what is the perimeter of the field?
A. 60 meters
B. 72 meters
C. 84 meters
D. 96 meters
Correct Answer: B
Let breadth be b and length be 2b. Area = 2b * b = 2b^2 = 288 => b^2 = 144 => b = 12 meters. Length = 24 meters. Perimeter = 2*(12+24) = 72 meters. Option A (60) implies dimensions of roughly 15x20, Area=300. Option C implies larger dimensions.

Q13. CSAT
If a clock shows 3:15, what is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand?
A. 0 degrees
B. 7.5 degrees
C. 90 degrees
D. 45 degrees
Correct Answer: B
At 3:15, the minute hand is at the 3 (90 degrees from 12). The hour hand moves 0.5 degrees per minute. From 3:00 to 3:15 is 15 minutes, so the hour hand moves 7.5 degrees past the 3. The angle between them is exactly 7.5 degrees.
Q14. CSAT
A train travels a certain distance at an average speed of 60 km/h and returns over the same distance at an average speed of 40 km/h. What is the average speed for the entire round trip?
A. 50 km/h
B. 48 km/h
C. 42 km/h
D. 45 km/h
Correct Answer: B
The correct answer is 48 km/h because when covering equal distances at two different speeds, the average speed is the harmonic mean: 2 * (60 * 40) / (60 + 40) = 48 km/h. Option A represents the simple arithmetic mean which is incorrect for unequal time intervals. Options C and D are mathematically inconsistent with the given speeds.
Q15. CSAT
A number when divided by 35 leaves a remainder of 28. What is the remainder when this number is divided by 7?
A. 0
B. 2
C. 4
D. 6
Correct Answer: A
Let the number be N. We can write N = 35k + 28 for some integer k. Since 35 is a multiple of 7 (35 = 7 × 5) and 28 is also a multiple of 7 (28 = 7 × 4), N can be rewritten as N = 7(5k + 4). This means N is exactly divisible by 7, leaving a remainder of 0. The other options are incorrect because any number of the form 35k + 28 is inherently a multiple of 7.
