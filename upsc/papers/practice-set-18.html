Q2. CSAT
A can complete a piece of work in 12 days and B can complete the same work in 18 days. They work together for 4 days. What fraction of the total work remains unfinished?
A. 1/3
B. 5/9
C. 2/3
D. 4/9
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Take LCM of 12 and 18 = 36 units of work. A does 3 units/day, B does 2 units/day. Together 5 units/day. In 4 days they complete 20 units. Remaining work = 16 units = 16/36 = 4/9. Full method: A's 1 day work = 1/12, B's = 1/18. Combined 1 day work = 5/36. In 4 days = 20/36 = 5/9. Work left = 1 - 5/9 = 4/9.
Q3. CSAT
Read the following passage and answer the question: "Urbanization has led to a surge in waste generation. Municipal corporations struggle with segregation at source. While waste-to-energy plants offer a solution, high capital costs and operational challenges hinder their widespread adoption. Decentralized composting and recycling units managed by local communities have shown promise in reducing the burden on landfills." Which of the following is the most logical and rational inference that can be drawn from the passage?
A. Waste-to-energy plants are the only viable solution for metro cities.
B. Municipal corporations should stop collecting waste from households.
C. Landfills are the most efficient way to handle urban waste.
D. Community-led decentralized waste management can effectively supplement municipal efforts.
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Elimination technique. Option A uses extreme word 'only' – passage says WtE plants face hindrances. Option C suggests 'stop collecting' – not supported, municipalities struggle but don't cease function. Option D claims landfills are 'most efficient' – passage says decentralized units reduce burden on landfills, implying landfills are problematic. Option B is balanced ('supplement', 'effectively') and directly supported by 'shown promise in reducing the burden'. Full Method: The passage highlights limitations of centralized systems (municipal struggles, WtE costs) and presents decentralized community units as a promising alternative ('shown promise'). Thus, the inference that community-led management can supplement municipal efforts is the only logically supported statement.
Q4. CSAT
Two trains start simultaneously from stations A and B towards each other. The ratio of their speeds is 3:2. They meet at a point 60 km from station A. What is the distance between stations A and B?
A. 80 km
B. 100 km
C. 120 km
D. 150 km
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: When time is constant, Distance ∝ Speed. Ratio of distances covered = Ratio of speeds = 3:2. Distance from A (3 parts) = 60 km → 1 part = 20 km. Total distance (5 parts) = 5 × 20 = 100 km. Full Solution: Let speeds be 3x and 2x km/h. Let meeting time be t hours. Distance from A = 3x × t = 60 km. Distance from B = 2x × t. Dividing, Dist_B / 60 = 2/3 → Dist_B = 40 km. Total Distance = 60 + 40 = 100 km.
Q5. CSAT
The radius of a right circular cylinder is doubled and its height is halved. What is the percentage change in its volume?
A. 50% increase
B. No change
C. 50% decrease
D. 100% increase
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Volume ∝ r²h. Radius factor = 2² = 4. Height factor = 1/2. Net factor = 4 × 0.5 = 2. Volume doubles → 100% increase. Step-by-step: Original V = πr²h. New V = π(2r)²(h/2) = π·4r²·h/2 = 2πr²h = 2V. % Increase = ((2V-V)/V)×100 = 100%.
Q6. CSAT
If the area of a square is 64 sq cm, what is its perimeter?
A. 16 cm
B. 32 cm
C. 48 cm
D. 64 cm
Correct Answer: B
The side length of a square with area 64 sq cm is the square root of 64, which is 8 cm. The perimeter of a square is 4 times the side length, so 4 * 8 = 32 cm. Option A (16) is half the perimeter, Option C (48) is incorrect, and Option D (64) is the area itself.
Q7. CSAT
A rectangular park has a length of 120 meters and a breadth of 80 meters. A path of uniform width 4 meters runs along the inside edge of the park. What is the area of this path in square meters?
A. 3,680
B. 3,840
C. 4,000
D. 4,160
Correct Answer: B
The inner dimensions are (120 - 2*4) = 112 m and (80 - 2*4) = 72 m. The area of the path is the difference between the outer rectangle area (9,600 sq m) and the inner rectangle area (8,064 sq m), which equals 3,840 sq m.
Q8. CSAT
A shopkeeper sells an article for Rs. 1050 and makes a profit of 20%. What was the cost price of the article?
A. Rs. 840
B. Rs. 875
C. Rs. 900
D. Rs. 950
Correct Answer: B
Selling Price (SP) = Cost Price (CP) + Profit. Here SP = 1.2 * CP. So, 1050 = 1.2 * CP => CP = 1050 / 1.2 = 875. Option A is too low, Option C corresponds to a different profit percentage, and Option D is incorrect.
Q9. CSAT
Find the next term in the series: 1, 3, 7, 15, 31, ?
A. 55
B. 63
C. 47
D. 59
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (Pattern Recognition): Observe the terms are one less than powers of 2: 2^1-1=1, 2^2-1=3, 2^3-1=7, 2^4-1=15, 2^5-1=31. Next is 2^6-1 = 64-1 = 63. Alternative Shortcut (Difference Method): Differences are 2, 4, 8, 16 (doubling each time). Next difference = 32. Next term = 31 + 32 = 63. Full Method: The series follows the pattern T_n = 2^n - 1. For n=6, T_6 = 64 - 1 = 63.

Q13. CSAT
The price of a commodity increases by 20%. By what percentage must a consumer reduce his consumption so that his expenditure on the commodity remains unchanged?
A. 15%
B. 16.67%
C. 18%
D. 20%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the formula for constant expenditure: % Reduction = (r / (100 + r)) × 100, where r is % increase in price. Here r=20. % Reduction = (20 / 120) × 100 = 16.67%. Full Solution: Let initial price = ₹100/unit and initial consumption = 100 units. Initial Expenditure = ₹10,000. New Price = ₹120/unit. To keep expenditure ₹10,000, New Consumption = 10,000 / 120 = 83.33 units. Reduction in consumption = 100 - 83.33 = 16.67 units. % Reduction = (16.67 / 100) × 100 = 16.67%.
Q14. CSAT
In what ratio must a grocer mix two varieties of tea costing Rs. 60 per kg and Rs. 80 per kg so that the mixture costs Rs. 70 per kg?
A. 2:1
B. 1:1
C. 1:2
D. 3:1
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Alligation – difference from mean: 80‑70 = 10, 70‑60 = 10 → ratio 10:10 = 1:1. Full: Let quantities be x and y. (60x+80y)/(x+y)=70 → 60x+80y=70x+70y → 10y=10x → x:y=1:1.
Q15. CSAT
A train travels at a constant speed of 60 km/h for 2 hours and then covers the remaining distance to its destination in 3 hours at a speed of 40 km/h. What is the total distance between the starting point and the destination?
A. 180 km
B. 240 km
C. 300 km
D. 360 km
Correct Answer: C
The first part of the journey covers 60 km/h * 2 h = 120 km. The second part covers 40 km/h * 3 h = 120 km. Adding these gives a total distance of 240 km.
