With reference to the Fundamental Duties enshrined in Article 51A of the Constitution of India, consider the following statements: 1. They were incorporated by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976 on the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee. 2. The duty to provide opportunities for education to children between the age of six and fourteen years was added by the 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002. 3. D. 1, 2 and 3
Correct Answer: A

A. Only one pair
B. Only two pairs
C. Only three pairs
D. All four pairs
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the 'Hemisphere Temperature Rule' for quick elimination. In the Northern Hemisphere, currents flowing towards the pole (North) are Warm (Kuroshio, Gulf Stream); towards the equator (South) are Cold (Labrador, California). In the Southern Hemisphere, towards the pole (South) are Warm (East Australian, Brazil); towards equator (North) are Cold (Benguela, Peru/Humboldt). Pair 1: Labrador flows South (Equator-ward) in NH -> Cold (Correct). Pair 2: Kuroshio flows North (Pole-ward) in NH -> Warm (Incorrectly labeled Cold). Pair 3: Benguela flows North (Equator-ward) in SH -> Cold (Incorrectly labeled Warm). Pair 4: East Australian flows South (Pole-ward) in SH -> Warm (Correct). Only Pairs 1 and 4 are correct. Answer: Two pairs.

With reference to the special majorities required in the Indian Parliament, which of the following provisions requires a majority of two-thirds of the members present and voting only, without requiring a majority of the total membership of the House?
A. Passage of a Constitutional Amendment Bill under Article 368
B. Passage of a resolution by Rajya Sabha for creation of All India Services under Article 312
C. Removal of a Judge of the Supreme Court under Article 124(2)
D. Approval of a Proclamation of National Emergency under Article 352(6)
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Elimination by 'Total Membership' clause. Articles 368, 124(2), and 352(6) explicitly mandate 'a majority of the total membership of the House' PLUS 'two-thirds of members present and voting'. Article 312 (Rajya Sabha resolution for All India Services) requires only 'not less than two-thirds of the members present and voting' (Special Majority under Art 249), with no total membership requirement. Worked Solution: 1. Art 368 (Constitutional Amendment): Requires majority of total membership + 2/3rd present and voting. 2. Art 124(2) (Removal of SC Judge): Requires same majority as Art 368 (majority of total membership + 2/3rd present and voting). 3. Art 352(6) (Emergency Approval): Requires same majority as Art 368 (majority of total membership + 2/3rd present and voting). 4. Art 312 (All India Services): Rajya Sabha resolution supported by 'not less than two-thirds of the members present and voting'. No mention of total membership. Hence, Option B is correct.
