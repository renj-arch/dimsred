Q1. CSAT
A company has 15 machines, and each machine produces 20 units per hour. If the company operates for 8 hours a day, how many units will it produce in a day?
A. 2400 units
B. 3000 units
C. 3600 units
D. 4800 units
Correct Answer: A
To find the total units produced in a day, we multiply the number of machines (15), the units produced per hour per machine (20), and the number of hours (8): 15 * 20 * 8 = 2400 units.

Q4. CSAT
A snail is at the bottom of a 20-foot well. Each day, it climbs up 3 feet, but at night, it slips back 2 feet. How many days will it take for the snail to reach the top of the well?
A. 18 days
B. 20 days
C. 22 days
D. 25 days
Correct Answer: A
The snail climbs 3 feet up and slips 2 feet back each day, making a net progress of 1 foot per day. On the 18th day, the snail will climb 3 feet and reach the top of the 20-foot well.

Q13. CSAT
A bat and a ball together cost $1.10. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?
A. $0.05
B. $0.10
C. $0.15
D. $0.20
Correct Answer: A
Let's assume the cost of the ball is x. Since the bat costs $1.00 more than the ball, the cost of the bat is x + $1.00. The total cost is $1.10, so we can write the equation: x + (x + $1.00) = $1.10. Solving for x, we get x = $0.05.
Q14. CSAT
If it takes five machines five minutes to make five widgets, how long would it take 100 machines to make 100 widgets?
A. 5 minutes
B. 10 minutes
C. 50 minutes
D. 100 minutes
Correct Answer: A
If it takes five machines five minutes to make five widgets, then one machine can make one widget in five minutes. Therefore, 100 machines can make 100 widgets in five minutes.
Q15. CSAT
If a bakery sells 250 loaves of bread per day and each loaf costs $2.50, what is the total daily revenue?
A. $500
B. $625
C. $750
D. $1000
Correct Answer: B
To find the total daily revenue, we multiply the number of loaves sold (250) by the cost per loaf ($2.50): 250 * $2.50 = $625.
