What is the value of tan(45°)?
A. 0
B. 1
C. Undefined
D. None of these
Correct Answer: B
The tangent of an angle in a right-angled triangle is defined as the ratio of the opposite side to the adjacent side. For a 45-degree angle in an isosceles right triangle, the opposite and adjacent sides are equal, making their ratio 1. Therefore, tan(45°) equals 1. Options A and C correspond to tan(0°) and tan(90°) respectively.
Research Team · ~5s · Unit Circle Recall
  1. Visualize 45° on unit circle: coordinates (√2/2, √2/2)
  2. Compute tan = y/x = (√2/2)/(√2/2)
  3. Result is 1

Q11. CSATmodel-generated · 2026-11-18
A rectangular garden has length twice its breadth. If the perimeter is increased by 20 % while keeping the shape same, what is the percentage increase in its area?
A. 44%
B. 48%
C. 50%
D. 52%
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For similar figures, area scales as the square of linear scale. 20% increase in perimeter ⇒ linear dimensions increase by 20% (since perimeter ∝ length). Hence area increase = (1.20)²‑1 = 1.44‑1 = 0.44 → 44%. Option A. Wait index mismatch. Correct index 0. Adjust: correct should be 0. Full solution: Let breadth = x, length = 2x. Perimeter = 2(2x+x)=6x. New perimeter = 1.2·6x = 7.2x ⇒ new breadth = 1.2x, new length = 2.4x. New area = 1.2x·2.4x = 2.88x². Original area = 2x². Increase = (2.88‑2)/2 = 0.44 = 44%. Hence option A.
