Merit total
What counts. Only the Essay, GS I–IV and the two Optional papers count for the merit list — 1,750 written marks. Paper A (Indian language) and Paper B (English) are 300 marks each but are qualifying: you must score at least 25% in each, and those marks are not added to your total. The Interview adds 275. Many candidates clear Prelims and still lose a year to a language paper; treat the qualifying papers as real.

How to use this pack

1 · Write before you read
Pick a question, start the timer, and finish without looking at the model answer. Reading a model answer first creates the feeling of knowing the answer; it does not create the answer.
2 · Compare, then rewrite one paragraph
The model answer is a comparison, not a key. Find the single paragraph you handled worst, rewrite it, and keep both attempts. Improvement lives in the second version.
3 · Keep a paper-wise log
The workspace saves attempts in this browser. Look for papers you avoid — that avoidance is usually the syllabus gap, not a scheduling problem.
4 · Protect the qualifying papers
Ten minutes of language practice a day is enough to stay safe. Do not discover a language problem in the week before the exam.
