Direction
The rule. Paper A is one Indian language from the Eighth Schedule. It is 300 marks and three hours; you need 25% (75/300) to qualify, and the marks are not counted in the merit list. A candidate who fails Paper A is not ranked, however well the scored papers went.

The Devanagari script

U+0900–U+097F

Marathi is written in Devanagari. It has its own vocabulary and grammatical forms; a Devanagari passage is not automatically Hindi. The paper tests standard written Marathi, including comprehension, precis, usage and translation.

Direction and characters
Devanagari runs left to right. The page uses the Unicode range U+0900–U+097F. Write the language in its own script, not in a Latin transliteration.
Traps in Marathi
  • Use Marathi vocabulary and verb forms, not Hindi equivalents.
  • Use ळ where standard Marathi requires it; it is a distinct letter.
  • Watch agreement and honorific forms; Marathi uses them differently from Hindi.

Your Marathi course path

5 modules · do them in order

Paper A is five skills, not one. Work through these modules, then use the workbook and script checker below. Each module links to the section of this page that carries the material.

1. Script and decoding
Do not read Marathi through a transliteration. Work directly in Devanagari (U+0900–U+097F, left-to-right). Spend the first sessions learning the conjuncts and half-forms you meet most: a slow first read costs minutes in the exam, a fast second read is the skill that scores.
2. Comprehension method
Read the passage once for the argument, once for the detail. Before answering, write the claim in one line in Marathi. Answer in the language of the question paper, cite the passage rather than your opinion, and keep each answer to two or three sentences. Inference questions reward "the passage suggests", not outside knowledge.
3. Precis method
Compress to roughly one-third of the original. Keep the claim, the reasons and the tone; drop examples and repetition. Do not add a conclusion the author did not write. After drafting, check every sentence still belongs to the Marathi register of the original — a precis that drifts into essay style loses marks.
4. Translation drills
Translate the same English paragraph both ways. Meaning and register beat word order. Watch the traps listed under the script section — for Marathi they are not generic: Use Marathi vocabulary and verb forms, not Hindi equivalents. Check your Marathi output with the script checker before comparing meaning.
5. Essay structure
250 words in Marathi: one opening that states the position, two developed points with an example each, one closing. Plan in Marathi for five minutes before writing. The examiner reads organisation and language control first; a correct but shapeless answer scores less than a structured one.

Suggested pace for a December attempt: one module per week, workbook every other day, script check on every written answer.

Practice workbook

Original study prompts · not PYQs

These are original tasks, not previous-year questions. Write your answers in Marathi; use the script checker below before you ask for feedback.

1. Comprehension
Read a 300–400 word Marathi passage. Answer in Marathi:
  1. What is the main claim?
  2. What are the two strongest supporting reasons?
  3. What does the writer want the reader to do?
Then close the passage and write a three-sentence summary.
2. Precis
Write a precis of the same passage in about one-third of its length. Keep the claim, the reasons and the tone. Do not add your opinion. Check the script before you compare with the original.
3. Usage and vocabulary
Choose ten words from the passage that you would not use in everyday speech. Write one Marathi sentence for each and note the English meaning. Then write one sentence using a common idiom from the passage.
4. Translate English → Marathi
Translate the paragraph below into Marathi. Then translate your version back into English without looking at the original. Compare meaning, not word order.
Reading a newspaper carefully is a skill that improves with practice. A reader who notices the main claim, the evidence and the tone can summarize an article in a few sentences. This habit helps in the examination because it saves time and prevents answers from becoming a list of disconnected points. It also builds vocabulary, which is useful in the translation section. Ten minutes a day is enough if the reading is active.
5. Translate Marathi → English
Take the first paragraph of your passage. Translate it into English. Underline the phrases that had no direct equivalent. Rewrite those phrases in plain English.
6. Short essay
Write 250 words in Marathi on one of these topics: the value of public libraries; why local languages matter in a democracy; how a city can protect its trees. Use a beginning, two developed points and a close.

Advanced Grammar Patterns

Mastering complex sentence structures is essential for the descriptive paper. Focus on compound‑complex sentences that combine coordination and subordination. Practice converting simple statements into sentences that use relative clauses, conditional clauses, and participial phrases.

  • Identify the main clause and embed a relative clause with जो or जे.
  • Use जर … तर for real‑conditionals and असल्यास … तर for hypothetical ones.
  • Employ present participles (करत, राहत) to show simultaneous actions.

Essay Structuring Strategies

A well‑organized essay scores higher on coherence and logical flow. Adopt a three‑stage framework: introduction, body, conclusion. Within the body, allocate one paragraph per major argument and use discourse markers to guide the examiner.

  1. Open with a clear thesis statement that addresses the prompt directly.
  2. Develop each argument with evidence, examples, and a counter‑point followed by a rebuttal.
  3. Close by restating the thesis in light of the discussed points and suggest a forward‑looking remark.

Vocabulary Enrichment Techniques

Expanding lexical range improves precision and reduces repetition. Combine reading of editorial columns, literary excerpts, and official reports with active note‑taking.

Translation Nuances for Administrative Texts

Translating government circulars into Marathi demands exact equivalence of legal terminology. Words such as अधिसूचना (notification), आदेश (order) and परिपत्रक (circular) carry procedural weight that cannot be replaced by synonyms. The translator must preserve the hierarchical tone: the source uses passive constructions to denote authority; Marathi prefers the कर्मणी प्रयोग (passive voice) with the auxiliary जाणे. Maintaining the original clause order avoids ambiguity in implementation deadlines.

Example: Passive Construction
Original: “The order shall be implemented by 31 March.”
Marathi: “आदेश ३१ मार्चपर्यंत कार्यान्वित होईल.”
The Marathi sentence uses the future passive “होईल” to mirror the obligatory tone of “shall be”.

Precision in Official Correspondence

Official letters follow a fixed template: प्रेषक, प्रतिपाद्य, संदर्भ, निर्देश. Each segment has a prescribed vocabulary set. For instance, “regarding” is always rendered as विषये rather than बाबत. The closing formula “Yours faithfully” translates to “आपला विश्वासू,” not “आपला शुभेच्छु”. Consistency across departments ensures that automated indexing tools retrieve the correct records.

Tip
Create a personal glossary of the 30 most frequent administrative terms and review it weekly; this reduces lookup time during the exam.

Critical Reading of Marathi Editorials

Editorials in leading Marathi dailies employ rhetorical devices such as अनुप्रास (alliteration), उपमा (simile) and व्यंग्य (satire) to shape public opinion. Identifying the writer’s stance requires tracking the progression from समस्या (problem) to उपाय (solution) and noting any विरोधाभास (contradiction). Practice summarising each paragraph in one sentence; this sharpens the ability to extract the core argument under time pressure.

संवैधानिक शब्दावली आणि व्याख्या

मराठीमध्ये संवैधानिक दस्तऐवजांचे भाषांतर करताना विशिष्ट पदांचे सटीक अर्थ समजणे आवश्यक आहे. उदाहरणार्थ, “प्रभुसत्ता”, “मौलिक अधिकार”, “नीति निर्देशक तत्वे” यांचे मराठी पर्याय संविधानातील मूळ अंग्रेजी पदांशी संगत असणे अपेक्षित आहे. हे पद समाजप्रबोधन, न्यायनिर्णय आणि प्रशासकीय आदेशांमध्ये बारशीर वापरले जातात. शब्दकोशातील विविध अर्थांमधून संदर्भानुसार योग्य अर्थ निवडणे लेखकाचे कौशल्य आहे.

  • मूळ अंग्रेजी पद ओळखा.
  • संविधानातील परिभाषा वाचा.
  • मराठी भाषेतील प्रचलित प्रयोग तपासा.
  • लेखनात सुसंगतता राखा.

प्रशासकीय लेखनशैली: स्पष्टता आणि संक्षिप्तता

शासकीय पत्र, परिपत्रक आणि आदेश यथार्थ, अव्यवस्थित नसता, स्पष्ट आणि संक्षिप्त असावेत. वाक्यरचना सक्रिय ध्वनीत, काळजीपूर्वक विरामचिन्हांकित आणि अनावश्यक विशेषणांकित वाक्यांशांकित नसावी. उदाहरणार्थ, “कृपया यथाशीघ्र आवश्यक कारवाई करावी” असे लिहिणे “आपण यथाशीघ्र आवश्यक कारवाई करावी” पेक्षा अधिक व्यावसायिक आहे.

  1. सक्रिय ध्वनीचा वापर.
  2. एक वाक्यात एकच मुख्य विचार.
  3. तांत्रिक शब्दांचे स्पष्टीकरण द्या.
  4. पुनरावलोकन करून अनावश्यक शब्द काढा.

मराठी भाषेतील उच्चारण विविधता आणि लेखन मानकीकरण

मराठीमध्ये प्रदेशिक उच्चारणभेद (उदा. विदर्भ, कोंकण, मराठवाडा) लेखनात प्रतिबिंबित होऊ शकतात. मानक देवनागरी लिपीमध्ये यथार्थ उच्चारणाचे प्रतिबिंब करणारा एकच स्वीकृत रूप ठेवणे आवश्यक आहे. उदाहरणार्थ, “शहर” आणि “शहर” यथार्थच समान लिहिले जातात, पण उच्चारणात फरक असू शकतो. शैक्षणिक आणि प्रशासकीय ग्रंथांमध्ये मानक शब्दरूप आणि विरामचिन्हांचे पालन करणे भाषिक एकरूपता सुनिश्चित करते.

  • मानक शब्दकोश (उदा. महाराष्ट्र राज्य शब्दकोश) अवलंबा.
  • लिपीमानकीकरण समितीची शिफारसी अनुसरा.
  • प्रदेशिक शब्दांचे स्पष्टीकरण टिप्पणीत द्या.

संवैधानिक संशोधन प्रक्रिया आणि मराठी भाषेतील प्रभाव

भारतीय संविधानातील संशोधन प्रक्रिया अनुच्छेद ३६८ अंतर्गत निर्दिष्ट आहे. संसद् दोन्ही सदनांमधील विशेष बहुमताने संशोधन पारित करू शकते, तर काही प्रकरणांमध्ये राज्य विधानमंडळांची मान्यता देखील अपेक्षित असते. मराठी भाषेतील प्रभाव यथार्थतः तब आढळतो जेव्हा संशोधनांमुळे राजभाषा, शिक्षण माध्यम किंवा प्रशासकीय पत्रव्यवहाराचे स्वरूप बदलते. उदाहरणार्थ, १९७६ चे ४२ वे संशोधनाने राजभाषा संबंधी उपबंधांची पुनर्रचना झाली असून, याचे मराठी भाषिक दस्तऐवजीकरणावर परिणाम झाला. संशोधन प्रक्रियेची यथार्थता समजून घेण्यासाठी विद्यार्थ्यांनी संसदीय चर्चा, समिती अहवाल आणि राष्ट्रपतीचे अनुमोदन यांचे क्रमवार अभ्यास करावे.

प्रशासकीय निर्णय लेखनातील तांत्रिक शब्दावली

प्रशासकीय निर्णय (orders, notifications, circulars) यांचे लेखन करताना स्पष्टता, संक्षिप्तता आणि कानूनी बंधनकारकता यांचे संतुलन साधणे आवश्यक आहे. मराठीत "प्रस्ताव", "निर्देश", "अधिसूचना", "परिपत्रक" यांचे सटीक वापर संदर्भानुसार केले पाहिजे.
