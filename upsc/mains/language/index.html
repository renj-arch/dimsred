UPSC Mains · Paper A · Indian language

One qualifying paper. Twenty-two languages.

Paper A is 300 marks and three hours, and you need 25% to qualify. The marks do not count in the merit list, but failing the paper ends the attempt. Pick your language below for a script guide, a practice workbook and a Unicode script checker.

What is on each page

Script guide
The script, its direction, its Unicode range and the traps that cost marks.
Practice workbook
Comprehension, precis, vocabulary, two-way translation and a short essay. Original prompts, not PYQs.
Script checker
Paste an answer and see whether it uses the expected Unicode script. It flags Latin letters; it does not grade grammar.

Low-resource languages

Label read

Santali, Bodo, Dogri and Kashmiri pages carry a short model-generated practice passage. The passage is labelled, script-checked and marked as pending native-speaker review. The other pages ask you to source a passage from a newspaper or an official paper instead of pretending that a short model text is a substitute.
