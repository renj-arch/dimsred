Direction
The rule. Paper A is one Indian language from the Eighth Schedule. It is 300 marks and three hours; you need 25% (75/300) to qualify, and the marks are not counted in the merit list. A candidate who fails Paper A is not ranked, however well the scored papers went.

The Devanagari script

U+0900–U+097F

Nepali is written in Devanagari. It shares the script with Hindi but has its own vocabulary, spelling conventions and grammar. The paper tests standard written Nepali, so do not submit a Hindi answer in Devanagari.

Direction and characters
Devanagari runs left to right. The page uses the Unicode range U+0900–U+097F. Write the language in its own script, not in a Latin transliteration.
Traps in Nepali
  • Use Nepali spellings and vocabulary, not Hindi.
  • Watch the verb forms and honorifics used in the passage.
  • Keep the vowel signs and conjuncts of standard written Nepali.

Your Nepali course path

5 modules · do them in order

Paper A is five skills, not one. Work through these modules, then use the workbook and script checker below. Each module links to the section of this page that carries the material.

1. Script and decoding
Do not read Nepali through a transliteration. Work directly in Devanagari (U+0900–U+097F, left-to-right). Spend the first sessions learning the conjuncts and half-forms you meet most: a slow first read costs minutes in the exam, a fast second read is the skill that scores.
2. Comprehension method
Read the passage once for the argument, once for the detail. Before answering, write the claim in one line in Nepali. Answer in the language of the question paper, cite the passage rather than your opinion, and keep each answer to two or three sentences. Inference questions reward "the passage suggests", not outside knowledge.
3. Precis method
Compress to roughly one-third of the original. Keep the claim, the reasons and the tone; drop examples and repetition. Do not add a conclusion the author did not write. After drafting, check every sentence still belongs to the Nepali register of the original — a precis that drifts into essay style loses marks.
4. Translation drills
Translate the same English paragraph both ways. Meaning and register beat word order. Watch the traps listed under the script section — for Nepali they are not generic: Use Nepali spellings and vocabulary, not Hindi. Check your Nepali output with the script checker before comparing meaning.
5. Essay structure
250 words in Nepali: one opening that states the position, two developed points with an example each, one closing. Plan in Nepali for five minutes before writing. The examiner reads organisation and language control first; a correct but shapeless answer scores less than a structured one.

Suggested pace for a December attempt: one module per week, workbook every other day, script check on every written answer.

Practice workbook

Original study prompts · not PYQs

These are original tasks, not previous-year questions. Write your answers in Nepali; use the script checker below before you ask for feedback.

1. Comprehension
Read a 300–400 word Nepali passage. Answer in Nepali:
  1. What is the main claim?
  2. What are the two strongest supporting reasons?
  3. What does the writer want the reader to do?
Then close the passage and write a three-sentence summary.
2. Precis
Write a precis of the same passage in about one-third of its length. Keep the claim, the reasons and the tone. Do not add your opinion. Check the script before you compare with the original.
3. Usage and vocabulary
Choose ten words from the passage that you would not use in everyday speech. Write one Nepali sentence for each and note the English meaning. Then write one sentence using a common idiom from the passage.
4. Translate English → Nepali
Translate the paragraph below into Nepali. Then translate your version back into English without looking at the original. Compare meaning, not word order.
Reading a newspaper carefully is a skill that improves with practice. A reader who notices the main claim, the evidence and the tone can summarize an article in a few sentences. This habit helps in the examination because it saves time and prevents answers from becoming a list of disconnected points. It also builds vocabulary, which is useful in the translation section. Ten minutes a day is enough if the reading is active.
5. Translate Nepali → English
Take the first paragraph of your passage. Translate it into English. Underline the phrases that had no direct equivalent. Rewrite those phrases in plain English.
6. Short essay
Write 250 words in Nepali on one of these topics: the value of public libraries; why local languages matter in a democracy; how a city can protect its trees. Use a beginning, two developed points and a close.

Advanced Grammar Patterns

Mastering complex sentence structures is essential for scoring well in the language paper. Focus on conditional clauses, relative pronouns, and passive constructions that frequently appear in formal writing. Practice converting direct speech into indirect speech while preserving tense and honorific markers. Regularly rewrite newspaper editorials using these patterns to internalise their rhythm.

Example: Conditional Construction
यदि तपाईं समयमा पुग्नुहुन्छ भने, हामी कक्षा सुरु गर्नेछौं।
This sentence uses the 'यदि ... भने' pattern to express a real condition in future tense.

Additionally, work on nominalising verbs with the suffix ‘‑नु’ to create abstract nouns, which enriches analytical essays. Combine these techniques with varied sentence openings to avoid monotony.

Essay Structuring for Mains

A well‑organised essay follows a clear logical flow: introduction, body, and conclusion. The introduction should present the thesis and outline the main arguments. Each body paragraph must develop a single idea supported by evidence, examples, or data. Transition words such as ‘अतएव’, ‘यसकारण’, and ‘अर्कोतर्फ’ guide the reader between points. The conclusion restates the thesis in light of the discussion and may suggest policy implications.

Tip: Outline First
Before writing, sketch a three‑part outline: introduction, body arguments, conclusion. This keeps the essay coherent and within the word limit.

Time management is critical; allocate roughly 10 minutes for planning, 35 minutes for writing, and 5 minutes for revision. Practise timed essays on previous topics to build speed without sacrificing depth.

Vocabulary Enrichment Strategies

Expanding lexical range involves systematic reading of diverse genres — literature, government reports, scientific articles, and opinion pieces. Maintain a personal glossary of unfamiliar words with definitions, synonyms, and example sentences. Use spaced‑repetition flashcards to reinforce retention. Incorporate newly learned terms into daily writing exercises to move them from passive to active vocabulary.

Translation Techniques for Mains

Effective translation between Nepali and English is a core skill for Paper A. The examiner expects precise rendering of idioms, cultural references, and administrative terminology without loss of nuance. A systematic approach reduces errors and saves time during the exam.

  • Identify the register of the source text – formal, literary, or colloquial – and match it in the target language.
  • Segment long sentences into logical units; translate each unit before re‑assembling.
  • Maintain grammatical gender and honorifics where Nepali requires them.
  • Use bilingual glossaries for domain‑specific vocabulary (e.g., governance, economics).
  • Proofread for word‑order differences: Nepali follows SOV while English follows SVO.
Example
सरकारले नयाँ नीति घोषणा गर्यो।
The government announced a new policy.

Answer Writing Strategies for Nepali

Structuring answers in Nepali demands clarity, logical flow, and appropriate script conventions. The following steps help candidates produce coherent, high‑scoring responses.

  1. Read the question twice; underline keywords that dictate the required format (explain, compare, evaluate).
  2. Draft an outline in Romanised Nepali or English; then convert each point into Devanagari.
  3. Begin with a concise thesis statement (मुख्य विचार).
  4. Develop each paragraph around a single idea; use connective particles (र, तर, किनभने).
  5. Conclude by restating the thesis and summarising key evidence.
  6. Allocate the last five minutes for script check – verify matras, halanta, and punctuation.
Tip
Practice writing 250‑word answers within 30 minutes; timed drills improve speed and legibility.

Literary Devices in Nepali Prose

नेपाली गद्यमा अलंकारहरूको प्रयोग लेखकको भावना र शैलीलाई समृद्ध बनाउँछ। रूपक, उत्प्रेक्षा, अनुप्रास र यमक जस्ता उपकरणहरू पाठकको मानसिक चित्रणलाई तीव्र गर्छन्। रूपकले दुई असमान वस्तुहरूको तुलना गरी अर्थको गहिराई दिन्छ भने उत्प्रेक्षाले कल्पनाको पंख लगाउँछ। अनुप्रास ध्वनिमयता सिर्जना गरी वाक्यलाई संगीतमय बनाउँछ र यमकले शब्दको पुनरावृत्ति गरी जोड दिन्छ। यी उपकरणहरूको सटीक प्रयोग गर्नका लागि लेखकले शब्द चयनमा सावधानी राख्नुपर्छ।

प्रमुख अलंकारहरू र उदाहरण

  • रूपक: “जीवन यो एक नदी हो” – जीवन र नदीको तुलना।
  • उत्प्रेक्षा: “चन्द्रमा जस्तो मुख देखेको छु” – मुखलाई चन्द्रसम मान्ने कल्पना।
  • अनुप्रास: “सुनौलो सूर्य सानो साँझमा” – ‘स’ ध्वनिको पुनरावृत्ति।
  • यमक: “राम राम भन्छ, राम राम सुन्छ” – शब्दको पुनरावृत्ति।

यसप्रकारका अलंकारहरूको पहिचान र प्रयोग गर्न सक्ने क्षमता परीक्षामा निबन्ध र व्याख्याको गुणस्तर बढाउँछ।

उदाहरण: रूपक प्रयोग
“शिक्षा यो एक ज्योति हो जसले अन्धकारलाई भगाउँछ।”
यहाँ ‘शिक्षा’ लाई ‘ज्योति’ भनी रूपक प्रयोग गरिएको छ। ज्योतिले अन्धकार भगाउँछ भन्ने विशेषता शिक्षाको प्रभावलाई स्पष्ट पार्छ।

Critical Reading Strategies for Mains

मुख्य परीक्षामा गद्यांश विश्लेषण गर्दा पाठकले केवल अर्थ बुझ्न मात्र नभई, लेखकको दृष्टिकोण, तर्क संरचना र भाषा शैलीको सूक्ष्म परीक्षण गर्नुपर्छ। पहिलो चरणमा शीर्षक, उपशीर्षक र पहिलो अनुच्छेदबाट मुख्य विषय पहिचान गर्नुहोस्। दोश्रो चरणमा प्रत्येक अनुच्छेदको केन्द्रीय विचार र यसको सहायक प्रमाणहरू छान्नुहोस्। तेस्रो चरणमा लेखकको पूर्वधारणा, पक्षपात र तर्कको बलाबल मूल्यांकन गर्नुहोस्। अन्तिम चरणमा आफ्नो निष्कर्ष र प्रतिक्रिया लेख्नुहोस्। यो क्रमबद्ध पद्धति समय व्यवस्थापन र उत्तरको स्पष्टता द दुईवटै बढाउँछ।

व्यावहारिक टिप्स

  1. पाठको मुख्य वाक्य (topic sentence) लाई चिन्हित गर्नुहोस्।
  2. संकेत शब्दहरू (अतः, तर, किनभने) बाट तर्कको दिशा बुझ्नुहोस्।
  3. प्रश्नको मांगअनुसार साक्ष्य छान्नुहोस्।
  4. उत्तर लेख्दा संक्षिप्त, तार्किक र सन्दर्भसहित हुनुहोस्।
परीक्षा कक्षमा समय बचत
पाठको पहिलो र अन्तिम अनुच्छेदलाई धेरै ध्यान दिनुहोस्; यी प्रायः मुख्य विचार र निष्कर्ष समेटेका हुन्छन्।
