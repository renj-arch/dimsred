About UPSC

UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) is conducted by Union Public Service Commission for recruitment to IAS, IPS, IFS and other Group A & Group B services. The Prelims consists of General Studies (GS) Paper 1 and CSAT Paper 2. The Mains has 9 papers including essay, GS I-IV, optional subject, and language papers. It is considered one of the toughest exams in India.
