How this issue is made

Story links are aggregated from publisher RSS and carried with their own title, source and timestamp. Photography comes from Wikimedia Commons with the licence and photographer named on every frame. Books come from Project Gutenberg's public-domain catalogue. Nothing here is ghost-written by a model, and no article is reproduced.
