About CDS

CDS (Combined Defence Services) exam is conducted by UPSC twice a year for recruitment into IMA, INA, AFA, and OTA. The written exam includes English (100 marks), General Knowledge (100 marks), and Elementary Mathematics (100 marks) for IMA/INA/AFA. OTA candidates need only English and GK. Candidates must be 19-25 years old (varies by academy).
