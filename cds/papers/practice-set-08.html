Which of the following diseases is caused by a protozoan parasite?
A. Tuberculosis
B. Cholera
C. Typhoid
D. Malaria
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Eliminate bacterial diseases first. TB (Mycobacterium), Cholera (Vibrio), Typhoid (Salmonella) are bacterial. Malaria (Plasmodium) is protozoan. Q3. General Knowledgemodel-generated · 2026-11-18
With reference to the liquidity management tools of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), which rate denotes the interest rate at which the RBI borrows money from commercial banks overnight against government securities?
A. Repo Rate
B. Reverse Repo Rate
C. Bank Rate
D. Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) Rate
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Direction of money flow. 'Repo' (Repurchase Option) = RBI lends TO banks (injects liquidity) — Banks borrow. 'Reverse Repo' = Reverse flow — RBI borrows FROM banks (absorbs liquidity). Eliminate: Bank Rate is the penal rate for long-term lending (no collateral strictly required historically); MSF Rate is the emergency window for banks to borrow from RBI (higher than Repo). Full Solution: Under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF), Reverse Repo Rate is the rate at which RBI borrows funds from commercial banks by selling government securities with an agreement to repurchase. It is a tool to suck out excess liquidity. Currently, it stands linked to the Repo Rate (Repo Rate minus 35 basis points under the Fixed Rate Reverse Repo / SDF framework).
Q4. General Knowledgemodel-generated · 2026-11-18
The dimensional formula of Planck's constant (h) is identical to that of:
A. Angular Momentum
B. Linear Momentum
C. Work
D. Force
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the 'Unit Analysis' trick. Unit of h is Joule-second (J·s). Angular Momentum = kg·m²/s = J·s. Work = Joule (J). Linear Momentum = kg·m/s. Force = Newton (kg·m/s²). Only Angular Momentum has the 'second' in the numerator matching J·s. Full Solution: Planck's constant h = Energy / Frequency. Dimensional formula of Energy = [ML²T⁻²], Frequency = [T⁻¹]. Thus h = [ML²T⁻²] / [T⁻¹] = [ML²T⁻¹]. Angular Momentum (L) = r × p = [L] × [MLT⁻¹] = [ML²T⁻¹]. Linear Momentum = [MLT⁻¹], Work = [ML²T⁻²], Force = [MLT⁻²]

Which one of the following writs is specifically issued by a higher court (Supreme Court or High Court) to challenge a person's right to hold a public office?
A. Habeas Corpus
B. Mandamus
C. Certiorari
D. Quo Warranto
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use Latin meaning association. 'Quo Warranto' translates to 'By what authority'. Link 'Quo' to 'Question' — questioning the authority to hold office. Eliminate others: Habeas Corpus ('Have the body') deals with illegal detention; Mandamus ('We command') compels performance of duty; Certiorari ('To be certified') reviews judicial/quasi-judicial decisions. Full Solution: Article 32/226 empowers courts to issue writs. Quo Warranto is the specific remedy against usurpation of a substantive public office. It does not apply to private or ministerial offices. The court directs the person to show by what authority they hold the office; if invalid, they are ousted.
