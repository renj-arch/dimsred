Q1. Mathematics
A man travels from point A to B at a speed of 30 km/h and returns from B to A at 60 km/h. What is his average speed for the entire journey?
A. 30 km/h
B. 40 km/h
C. 45 km/h
D. 50 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For equal distances, average speed = Harmonic Mean = 2ab/(a+b) = (2×30×60)/(30+60) = 3600/90 = 40 km/h. Full: Let distance = d km. Time for onward = d/30 hr, return = d/60 hr. Total time = d/30 + d/60 = d/20 hr. Total distance = 2d km. Average speed = Total Distance / Total Time = 2d / (d/20) = 40 km/h.
Q2. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of the expression (7^95 - 3^58)?
A. 0
B. 2
C. 4
D. 6
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits. For 7, cycle is 7,9,3,1 (length 4); 95 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit 3. For 3, cycle is 3,9,7,1 (length 4); 58 mod 4 = 2 → unit digit 9. Subtract: 3 - 9 requires borrow → 13 - 9 = 4. Full: 7^95 = 7^(4×23+3) → unit digit 3. 3^58 = 3^(4×14+2) → unit digit 9. Unit digit of difference = (10+3) - 9 = 4.
Q3. Mathematics
A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 15% on the marked price. What is his gain percent?
A. 19%
B. 21%
C. 23%
D. 25%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use successive percentage change formula: \(a + b + \frac{ab}{100}\). Here \(a = +40\) (markup), \(b = -15\) (discount). Effective % = \(40 - 15 + \frac{40 \times (-15)}{100} = 25 - 6 = 19\%\). Full: Let CP = 100. MP = 140. SP = 140 - 15% of 140 = 140 - 21 = 119. Gain = 19. Gain% = 19%.
Q4. Mathematics
The legs of a right triangle are 6 cm and 8 cm. What is the inradius of the triangle?
A. 3 cm
B. 4 cm
C. 2 cm
D. 5 cm
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For a right triangle, inradius r = (a+b-c)/2. Hypotenuse c=10, so r=(6+8-10)/2=2 cm. Full: Hypotenuse = √(6²+8²)=10. Semiperimeter s=(6+8+10)/2=12. Area = ½×6×8=24. Inradius r = Area/s = 24/12 = 2 cm.
Q5. Mathematics
A train 150 m long running at 60 km/h crosses another train 200 m long running at 40 km/h in opposite directions. The time taken to cross each other is:
A. 10.8 s
B. 12.6 s
C. 14.4 s
D. 16.2 s
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Relative speed (opposite) = (60+40) km/h = 100 km/h = 100 × 5/18 = 250/9 m/s. Total distance = 150+200 = 350 m. Time = Distance/Speed = 350 ÷ (250/9) = 350 × 9/250 = 12.6 s. Full: Speed1 = 60×5/18 = 50/3 m/s. Speed2 = 40×5/18 = 100/9 m/s. Relative speed = 50/3 + 100/9 = 250/9 m/s. Time = 350 / (250/9) = 12.6 s.
Q6. Mathematics
Two circles touch each other externally. The distance between their centres is 13 cm and the sum of their areas is \(97\pi\) cm². What is the radius of the smaller circle?
A. 3 cm
B. 4 cm
C. 5 cm
D. 6 cm
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Let radii be \(r_1, r_2\). \(r_1 + r_2 = 13\) and \(r_1^2 + r_2^2 = 97\). Check integer pairs summing to 13: (4,9) \(\rightarrow 16+81=97\). Smaller radius = 4. Full: \(r_1 + r_2 = 13 \Rightarrow (r_1+r_2)^2 = 169\). \(r_1^2 + r_2^2 + 2r_1r_2 = 169 \Rightarrow 97 + 2r_1r_2 = 169 \Rightarrow r_1r_2 = 36\). Roots of \(x^2 - 13x + 36 = 0\) are 4 and 9. Smaller = 4 cm.
Q7. Mathematics
From a point P outside a circle of radius 5 cm, the length of the tangent drawn to the circle is 12 cm. Find the distance of P from the centre of the circle.
A. 12 cm
B. 14 cm
C. 13 cm
D. 15 cm
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Tangent radius perpendicular → right triangle with legs 5 and 12, hypotenuse = √(5^2+12^2)=13. Full: OP^2 = r^2 + PT^2 = 25+144=169 → OP=13 cm.
Q8. Mathematics
A solid metallic sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into a solid right circular cylinder of radius 4 cm. The height of the cylinder is:
A. 12 cm
B. 14 cm
C. 18 cm
D. 20 cm
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Volume remains constant. (4/3)πR³ = πr²h → h = (4/3 × 6³) / 4² = (4/3 × 216) / 16 = 288 / 16 = 18 cm. Full: Volume of sphere = (4/3)π(6)³ = 288π cm³. Volume of cylinder = π(4)²h = 16πh cm³. Equating: 16πh = 288π → h = 18 cm.
Q9. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a cricket bat with a marked price of ₹5,000. He offers two alternatives: Scheme A: 15% discount followed by a further 10% discount on the reduced price. Scheme B: A single flat discount of x%. To make the final selling price identical in both schemes, what is the value of x?
A. 25%
B. 23.5%
C. 22.5%
D. 24%
Correct Answer: B
Discount A = 15%. Remaining = 85%. Discount B = 10%. Remaining = 90%. Combined Remaining = 85% x 90% = 0.85 x 0.90 = 0.765. Total Discount = 1 - 0.765 = 0.235. x = 23.5%.
Q10. Mathematics
What is the remainder when 3^100 is divided by 7?
A. 4
B. 2
C. 1
D. 6
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The cyclicity of 3^n mod 7 is 6 (remainders: 3, 2, 6, 4, 5, 1). 100 mod 6 = 4, so the 4th remainder in the cycle is 4. Full method: By Fermat's Little Theorem, 3^6 ≡ 1 (mod 7). 3^100 = 3^(6×16+4) = (3^6)^16 × 3^4 ≡ 1^16 × 81 ≡ 4 (mod 7).
Q11. Mathematics
A bank offers a special 'Festival Savings Scheme' where the interest rate for the first year is 5% per annum on the principal. However, the interest earned in the first year is added to the principal at the end of the year. If a customer deposits ₹10,000 and closes the account after exactly one year, what is the total amount received?
A. ₹10,500
B. ₹10,525
C. ₹10,600
D. ₹10,400
Correct Answer: A
Simple Interest = (P * R * T) / 100. Substituting values: P = 10000, R = 5%, T = 1 year. SI = (10000 * 5 * 1) / 100. SI = 500. Total Amount = Principal + Interest = 10000 + 500 = 10500.
Q12. Mathematics
A retailer marks up a commodity by 25% above its cost price. He then offers a discount of x% on this marked price. If the final selling price equals the original cost price plus half of the initial profit he would have made had there been no discount, what is the value of x?
A. 10
B. 12
C. 15
D. 20
Correct Answer: A
Let Cost Price (CP) = Rs. 100. Initial Profit (if no discount) = Marked Price - CP = 125 - 100 = 25. Half of this initial profit = 25 / 2 = 12.5. Target Selling Price = CP + Half Initial Profit = 100 + 12.5 = 112.5. Marked Price (MP) = 125. Discount Amount = MP - SP = 125 - 112.5 = 12.5. Discount Percentage (x) = (Discount / MP) * 100 = (12.5 / 125) * 100 = 10%.
Q13. Mathematics
If the roots of the equation x² - 7x + 12 = 0 are α and β, find the value of α² + β².
A. 23
B. 21
C. 19
D. 25
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: α+β = 7, αβ = 12. Then α²+β² = (α+β)² - 2αβ = 49 - 24 = 25. Full: Roots of x²-7x+12=0 are 3 and 4. Squares are 9 and 16, sum = 25.
Q14. Mathematics
In triangle ABC, AD is a median of length 12 cm. G is the centroid. The length of AG is:
A. 4 cm
B. 6 cm
C. 10 cm
D. 8 cm
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Centroid divides median in ratio 2:1 from vertex. AG = (2/3) × AD = (2/3) × 12 = 8 cm. Full: By centroid property, AG : GD = 2 : 1. Total parts = 3. AD = 12 cm = 3 parts → 1 part = 4 cm. AG = 2 parts = 8 cm.
Q15. Mathematics
A shopkeeper offers two successive discounts of 20% and 10% on an article. What is the equivalent single discount percentage?
A. 28%
B. 30%
C. 25%
D. 32%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Equivalent discount = 1 - (0.8 × 0.9) = 0.28 → 28%. Full: After 20% discount price becomes 80% of original; a further 10% discount leaves 90% of 80% = 72% of original, so total discount = 28%.
