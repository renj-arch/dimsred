Q1. Mathematics
A vessel contains 80 litres of pure milk. 16 litres of milk is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated two more times. What is the quantity of milk left in the vessel?
A. 40.96
B. 41.20
C. 42.50
D. 38.40
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use formula Final Amount = Initial * (1 - Replaced/Total)^n. Milk left = 80 * (1 - 16/80)^3 = 80 * (4/5)^3 = 80 * 64/125 = 40.96 litres. Full Solution: After 1st replacement: Milk = 80 * 64/80 = 64L. After 2nd: 64 * 4/5 = 51.2L. After 3rd: 51.2 * 4/5 = 40.96L.
Research Team · ~5s · Formula Shortcut
  1. Compute fraction removed: 16/80 = 1/5
  2. Remaining fraction each time: 4/5
  3. Raise to power 3: (4/5)^3 = 64/125
  4. Multiply by 80: 80 * 64/125 = 40.96
Q2. Mathematics
If the quadratic equation 2x² − 5x + k = 0 has equal roots, what is the value of k?
A. 25/4
B. 5/2
C. 25/8
D. 5/8
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For equal roots, discriminant D = b² − 4ac = 0. Here a=2, b=−5, c=k ⇒ 25 − 8k = 0 ⇒ k = 25/8. Full steps: The condition for equal roots is b² = 4ac. Substituting gives (−5)² = 4×2×k ⇒ 25 = 8k ⇒ k = 25/8.
Research Team · ~5s · Discriminant Zero
  1. Δ = b² - 4ac = 0
  2. 25 - 8k = 0
  3. k = 25/8
Q3. Mathematics
If α and β are the roots of the quadratic equation x² - 5x + 6 = 0, what is the value of α² + β²?
A. 13
B. 11
C. 17
D. 19
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use identity α²+β² = (α+β)² - 2αβ. Sum of roots (α+β) = 5, Product (αβ) = 6. Value = 5² - 2×6 = 25 - 12 = 13. Step-by-step: For ax²+bx+c=0, α+β = -b/a = 5, αβ = c/a = 6. α²+β² = (α+β)² - 2αβ = 25 - 12 = 13.
Research Team · ~5s · Vieta's Identity
  1. Sum α+β = 5, product αβ = 6
  2. Compute 5² - 2×6 = 25 - 12 = 13
Q4. Mathematics
A chord of length 16 cm is at a distance of 6 cm from the centre of a circle. What is the radius of the circle?
A. 8 cm
B. 12 cm
C. 14 cm
D. 10 cm
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Perpendicular from centre bisects chord. Half chord = 8 cm, Distance = 6 cm. Recognize 6-8-10 Pythagorean triplet ⇒ Radius = 10 cm. Step-by-step: Radius² = (Distance)² + (Half Chord)² = 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100. Radius = √100 = 10 cm.
Research Team · ~3s · Pythagorean Triple
  1. Half chord = 16/2 = 8 cm
  2. Distance from center = 6 cm
  3. Recognize 6-8-10 right triangle
  4. Radius = 10 cm
Q5. Mathematics
A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 25% on the marked price. What is his profit percentage?
A. 10%
B. 15%
C. 5%
D. 20%
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use the successive percentage change formula for +40% (markup) and -25% (discount): Net % = 40 - 25 - (40*25/100) = 15 - 10 = 5%. Full Solution: Let CP = ₹100. Marked Price = ₹140. Selling Price = 75% of 140 = ₹105. Profit = ₹5 on ₹100, so Profit% = 5%.
Research Team · ~3s · Multiplier Chain
  1. SP = CP × (1+0.40) × (1-0.25)
  2. SP = CP × 1.4 × 0.75 = CP × 1.05
  3. Profit% = (1.05 - 1) × 100 = 5%
Q6. Mathematics
The difference between Compound Interest and Simple Interest on a certain sum at 10% per annum for 2 years is Rs. 120. Find the principal sum.
A. 10000
B. 11000
C. 12000
D. 13000
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For 2 years, Difference = P * (R/100)^2. Here 120 = P * (10/100)^2 = P/100 => P = 12,000. Full Solution: SI for 2 years = P*10*2/100 = 0.2P. CI for 2 years = P(1.1)^2 - P = 0.21P. Difference = 0.21P - 0.2P = 0.01P = 120. Therefore, P = 120 / 0.01 = 12,000.
Q7. Mathematics
An article is sold at a 20% profit. If the cost price is increased by 10% and the selling price is increased by Rs 10, the profit percentage becomes 10%. Find the original cost price.
A. 800
B. 1000
C. 1200
D. 1500
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use fraction method. 20% profit ⇒ SP/CP = 6/5. 10% CP increase ⇒ New CP = 11/10 CP. 10% New Profit ⇒ New SP = 11/10 New CP = 121/100 CP. Increase in SP = (121/100 - 120/100) CP = 1/100 CP = 10 ⇒ CP = 1000. Step-by-step: Let CP = x. SP = 1.2x. New CP = 1.1x. New SP = 1.2x + 10. New Profit 10% ⇒ New SP = 1.1 × 1.1x = 1.21x. Equate: 1.21x = 1.2x + 10 ⇒ 0.01x = 10 ⇒ x = 1000.
Q8. Mathematics
The radius of a right circular cylinder is increased by 20% and its height is decreased by 10%. What is the percentage change in its volume?
A. 8% increase
B. 29.6% increase
C. 8% decrease
D. 29.6% decrease
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Volume factor = (1+0.20)² × (1−0.10) = 1.44 × 0.9 = 1.296 ⇒ 29.6% increase. Full steps: Original volume V = πr²h. New radius = 1.2r, new height = 0.9h. New volume = π(1.2r)²(0.9h) = π × 1.44r² × 0.9h = 1.296 πr²h = 1.296V. Percentage change = (1.296−1)×100 = 29.6% increase.
Q9. Mathematics
PAB is a secant and PT is a tangent to a circle with centre O. If PT = 12 cm and PB = 16 cm, find the length of PA.
A. 6
B. 7
C. 8
D. 9
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Tangent-Secant Theorem: PT^2 = PA * PB. 12^2 = PA * 16 => 144 = 16 * PA => PA = 9 cm. Full Solution: By the Tangent-Secant theorem, the square of the tangent segment equals the product of the whole secant segment and its external segment. PT^2 = PA * PB. Substituting values: 144 = PA * 16. Solving gives PA = 9 cm.
Q10. Mathematics
The LCM of two numbers is 180 and their HCF is 6. If one of the numbers is 30, find the other number.
A. 42
B. 36
C. 48
D. 54
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Product of two numbers = LCM × HCF. So 30 × x = 180 × 6 = 1080 ⇒ x = 1080/30 = 36. Full steps: Let the other number be x. We know LCM × HCF = product of numbers. Hence 180 × 6 = 30 × x ⇒ 1080 = 30x ⇒ x = 36.
Q11. Mathematics
A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. B and C together can complete the same work in 15 days. C and A together can complete it in 20 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?
A. 30 days
B. 40 days
C. 60 days
D. 24 days
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Take total work as LCM(12,15,20)=60 units. Then A+B=5, B+C=4, C+A=3 units/day. Adding gives 2(A+B+C)=12 ⇒ A+B+C=6. So A = 6−4 = 2 units/day. Hence A alone takes 60/2 = 30 days. Full steps: Let work = 60 units. Combined efficiencies: A+B=60/12=5, B+C=60/15=4, C+A=60/20=3. Sum = 2(A+B+C)=12 ⇒ A+B+C=6. Subtract B+C=4 from total to get A=2. Days for A = 60/2 = 30.
Q12. Mathematics
Two trains of lengths 150 m and 200 m run on parallel tracks. When running in opposite directions, they cross each other in 10 seconds. When running in the same direction, the faster train crosses the slower train in 70 seconds. What is the speed of the faster train (in m/s)?
A. 15
B. 17.5
C. 20
D. 22.5
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Relative speed (opposite) = (150+200)/10 = 35 m/s. Relative speed (same) = (150+200)/70 = 5 m/s. Speed of faster train = (35+5)/2 = 20 m/s. Full Solution: Let speeds be u and v (u > v). Opposite direction: u+v = 350/10 = 35. Same direction: u-v = 350/70 = 5. Adding equations: 2u = 40 => u = 20 m/s.
Q13. Mathematics
In triangle ABC, DE is drawn parallel to BC such that D and E lie on AB and AC respectively. If AD = 3 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 4 cm, find EC.
A. 6 cm
B. 10 cm
C. 12 cm
D. 8 cm
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: By Basic Proportionality Theorem, AD/DB = AE/EC ⇒ 3/6 = 4/EC ⇒ EC = 8 cm. Full steps: Since DE ∥ BC, AD/DB = AE/EC. Substitute AD=3, DB=6, AE=4 ⇒ 3/6 = 4/EC ⇒ 1/2 = 4/EC ⇒ EC = 8 cm.

Q15. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of the product (7^95 × 3^58)?
A. 1
B. 3
C. 7
D. 9
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits. For 7: cycle 7,9,3,1 (length 4). 95 mod 4 = 3 → 3rd digit = 3. For 3: cycle 3,9,7,1 (length 4). 58 mod 4 = 2 → 2nd digit = 9. Product unit digit = 3 × 9 = 27 → 7. Step-by-step: 7^95 = 7^(4×23+3) → unit digit of 7^3 = 343 → 3. 3^58 = 3^(4×14+2) → unit digit of 3^2 = 9. 3 × 9 = 27. Unit digit is 7.
