Q1. General Knowledgemodel-generated · 2026-11-15
The Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer (1987) primarily targets the phase-out of which category of chemicals?
A. Greenhouse Gases (Carbon Dioxide, Methane)
B. Persistent Organic Pollutants (DDT, PCBs)
C. Heavy Metals (Mercury, Lead, Arsenic)
D. Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) and Halons
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Treaty-Chemical Mapping. Montreal -> **Ozone** -> **CFCs/HCFCs/Halons** (Ozone Depleting Substances). Kyoto/Paris -> **GHGs** (CO2, CH4). Stockholm -> **POPs**. Minamata -> **Mercury**. Full Solution: The Montreal Protocol was designed specifically to protect the stratospheric ozone layer by phasing the production and consumption of Ozone Depleting Substances (ODS), chiefly Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), Halons, Carbon Tetrachloride, and Methyl Chloroform. It is widely regarded as the most successful international environmental treaty.

Correct Answer: A

Q3. General Knowledgemodel-generated · 2026-11-15
'Regur' soil, characterized by high clay content, high moisture retention, and the development of deep cracks during dry weather, is most ideally suited for the cultivation of which major crop?
A. Wheat
B. Rice
C. Cotton
D. Tea
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: 'Regur' is the local/technical name for Black Cotton Soil. The keyword 'Cotton' is embedded in the common identity of the soil. Eliminate Wheat (Alluvial soil), Rice (Alluvial/Deltaic), Tea (Laterite/Hill slopes). Full Solution: Regur (Black Soil) is formed from the weathering of Deccan Trap basalt. It is rich in lime, iron, magnesia, and alumina but deficient in phosphorus, nitrogen, and organic matter. Its high clay content (montmorillonite) causes swelling when wet and deep cracking when dry (self-ploughing), creating ideal aeration and moisture conditions for Cotton, a deep-rooted crop.

A. Nilgiri Hills
B. Palani Hills
C. Cardamom Hills
D. Anaimalai Hills
Correct Answer: D

B. Sixth Schedule
C. Seventh Schedule
D. Ninth Schedule
Correct Answer: A

The Sixth Schedule applies only to Tribal Areas in the four NE states (Art 244(2)). The 7th Schedule contains the Three Lists (Union/State/Concurrent). The 9th Schedule protects land reform laws from judicial review. **Step-by-step:** 1. Identify the subject: Administration of Scheduled Areas & STs. 2. Recall Article 244. Clause (1) invokes Fifth Schedule for states *excluding* Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram. Clause (2) invokes Sixth Schedule for those four states. 3. Eliminate 7th Schedule (Distribution of Powers) and 9th Schedule (Validation of Acts). 4. Select Fifth Schedule.

A. One month
B. Two months
C. Three months
D. Six months
Correct Answer: B

Full Solution: Article 356(3) stipulates that a Proclamation under Article 356(1) shall cease to operate at the expiration of two months unless before the expiration of that period it has been approved by resolutions of both Houses of Parliament.

As per Article 279A of the Constitution (inserted by the 101st Amendment Act, 2016), who acts as the ex-officio Chairperson of the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council?
A. Minister of State for Finance
B. Chairman, Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC)
C. Union Finance Minister
D. Prime Minister of India
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Constitutional Designation Logic. The GST Council is a federal body balancing Centre-States; the Chair must be a political executive representing the Centre's fiscal authority, not the administrative head (CBIC) or the Head of Government (PM) directly. Full Solution: Article 279A(3) explicitly designates the Union Finance Minister as the ex-officio Chairperson. The Vice-Chairperson is chosen from amongst the State Finance Ministers. The Union Minister of State for Finance is a member, not the Chair.

Under Article 368, which of the following constitutional provisions requires a Special Majority of Parliament **and** ratification by the Legislatures of not less than one-half of the States for amendment?
A. Election of the President
B. Fundamental Rights (General)
C. Directive Principles of State Policy
D. Admission or establishment of new States
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the 'Federal Provisions List' mnemonic (7 items: President election, Executive powers, Judiciary, Distribution of powers, State representation in Parliament, Article 368 itself). 'Admission of new States' (Art 2/3) requires only Simple Majority. FR/DPSP usually need only Special Majority. Full Solution: Article 368(2) mandates that amendments affecting the federal structure—specifically the election of the President (Art 54/55), extent of executive powers, Supreme Court/High Courts, distribution of legislative powers, State representation in Parliament, or Article 368 itself—require Special Majority plus State ratification.
