Q4. General Knowledgemodel-generated · 2026-11-25
Which vitamin is synthesized by the human skin when exposed to ultraviolet B (UVB) radiation from sunlight?
A. Vitamin D
B. Vitamin A
C. Vitamin B12
D. Vitamin C
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Vitamin D is uniquely called the 'Sunshine Vitamin'; it is synthesized from 7-dehydrocholesterol in the skin. Vitamins A, B12, and C are essential dietary nutrients not produced by sun exposure. Full: UVB radiation (290–315 nm) converts cutaneous 7-dehydrocholesterol to pre-vitamin D3, which isomerizes to Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol), later hydroxylated in liver and kidney to active form.

A. Plasmodium vivax
B. Plasmodium ovale
C. Plasmodium malariae
D. Plasmodium falciparum
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: 'Falciparum' sounds like 'Fatal'; it causes malignant/cerebral malaria. Vivax/Ovale cause relapsing malaria (hypnozoites); Malariae causes quartan malaria. Step-by-step: P. falciparum infects RBCs of all ages, leading to high parasitemia, cytoadherence in brain capillaries (cerebral malaria), and highest mortality.

Which writ is issued by a higher court to a lower court or tribunal to review a judicial decision?
A. Habeas Corpus
B. Mandamus
C. Quo Warranto
D. Certiorari
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use meaning elimination. Habeas Corpus = 'Produce the body' (detention); Mandamus = 'We command' (duty); Quo Warranto = 'By what authority' (office). Certiorari = 'To be certified' (review). Full Solution: Certiorari is a curative writ issued by the Supreme Court or High Courts to review a decision of a lower court, tribunal, or quasi-judicial body. It ensures judicial decisions are within jurisdiction and follow natural justice.

Correct Answer: A
