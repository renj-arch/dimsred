Q3. English
Improve the underlined segment in the sentence. If no improvement is required, select 'No improvement'. **I wish I was** a bird so that I could fly to you.
A. I wish I were
B. I wish I am
C. I wish I have been
D. No improvement
Correct Answer: A
Elimination shortcut: The sentence expresses an **unreal/impossible wish** (Subjunctive Mood). In the Subjunctive Mood, the verb 'to be' always takes **'were'** for all persons (I, he, she, it, we, they). Option A uses 'were'. Option B ('am') is present indicative. Option C ('have been') is present perfect indicative. Option D is incorrect because 'was' is indicative past, not subjunctive.

B. have submitted
C. their nominations
D. for the post
Correct Answer: B
Elimination shortcut: 'Neither of' is always followed by a plural noun/pronoun but takes a **singular verb**. Option A ('Neither of the two candidates') is the standard structure. Option C ('their nominations') correctly uses plural pronoun 'their' with plural noun 'nominations' (modern usage accepts 'their' for singular antecedent 'neither' to avoid gendered 'his/her'). Option D is a correct prepositional phrase. Option B uses plural verb 'have' with singular subject 'Neither'; it must be 'has submitted'.
