What is the unit digit of 7^{2023}?
A. 7
B. 9
C. 3
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The unit digit of powers of 7 repeats every 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. 2023 mod 4 = 3, so the third digit in the cycle is 3. Full: 7^1=7, 7^2=49→9, 7^3=343→3, 7^4=2401→1; cycle length 4. 2023 = 4×505 + 3 ⇒ unit digit = 3.

  2. 2023 mod 4 = 3 → pick 3rd entry: 3
Q2. Mathematics
If a + b = 10 and ab = 21, what is the value of a² + b²?
A. 42
B. 58
C. 79
D. 100
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: (a+b)² = a²+b²+2ab ⇒ a²+b² = (a+b)² − 2ab = 100 − 42 = 58. Full: Substitute a+b=10 and ab=21 into the identity a²+b² = (a+b)² − 2ab to get 58.
Research Team · ~5s · Square Sum Identity
  1. Compute (a+b)² = 10² = 100
  2. Compute 2ab = 2×21 = 42
  3. Subtract: 100 − 42 = 58
Q3. Mathematics
In a circle of radius 10 cm, a chord subtends an angle of 60° at the centre. What is the length of the chord?
A. 10√3 cm
B. 20 cm
C. 10 cm
D. 5√3 cm
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Chord length = 2r sin(θ/2). Here r=10, θ=60°, so length = 2×10×sin30° = 20×0.5 = 10 cm. Full solution: The chord, two radii form an isosceles triangle with vertex angle 60°. Dropping perpendicular bisects chord and angle, giving right triangle with hypotenuse 10 and angle 30°. Half-chord = 10 sin30° = 5, so full chord = 10 cm.
Research Team · ~3s · Equilateral Triangle
  1. Draw radii to chord endpoints forming triangle with sides 10,10,chord.
  2. Central angle 60° implies triangle is equilateral.
  3. Chord = radius = 10 cm.
Q4. Mathematics
A pipe can fill a tank in 6 hours and another pipe can empty the same tank in 8 hours. If both pipes are opened simultaneously, how long will it take to fill the tank?
A. 20 hours
B. 24 hours
C. 28 hours
D. 14 hours
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: LCM Method. Take tank capacity = LCM(6, 8) = 24 units. Filling efficiency = 4 units/hr, Emptying efficiency = 3 units/hr. Net efficiency = 1 unit/hr. Time = 24/1 = 24 hours. Full method: Net part filled in 1 hour = 1/6 - 1/8 = (4-3)/24 = 1/24. Time required to fill the tank = 1 / (1/24) = 24 hours.
Research Team · ~4s · Reciprocal Difference
  1. Formula: Time = (6*8)/(8-6)
  2. Calculate: 48/2 = 24 hours
Q5. Mathematics
In triangle ABC, medians AD, BE, and CF intersect at centroid G. If the area of triangle ABC is 72 cm², what is the area of triangle BGD?
A. 6 cm²
B. 8 cm²
C. 16 cm²
D. 12 cm²
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Centroid divides triangle into 6 equal-area triangles. Area(ABC) = 72 → each small triangle = 72/6 = 12 cm². Triangle BGD is one of them. Full: Median AD divides area into 36 cm² each (ABD, ADC). Centroid G divides median AD in ratio AG:GD = 2:1. Triangles AGB and BGD share vertex B; area ratio = base ratio = 2:1. Area(BGD) = (1/3) × Area(ABD) = (1/3) × 36 = 12 cm².
Research Team · ~3s · Six Equal Triangles
  1. Recall three medians divide any triangle into 6 smaller triangles of equal area.
  2. Divide total area 72 by 6 to get 12 cm².

Q7. Mathematics
The sum of the first 10 terms of an Arithmetic Progression is 100 and the sum of the first 20 terms is 400. What is the sum of the first 30 terms?
A. 600
B. 700
C. 800
D. 900
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: In an AP, S_n/n forms an AP. S_10/10 = 10, S_20/20 = 20. The common difference is 10, so S_30/30 = 30 ⇒ S_30 = 900. Full method: S_n = n/2[2a + (n-1)d]. From S_10=100: 2a+9d=20. From S_20=400: 2a+19d=40. Solving gives d=2, a=1. S_30 = 15[2 + 58] = 15 × 60 = 900.
Research Team · ~5s · Block Sum AP
  1. S₁₀ = 100, S₂₀ = 400
  2. First block sum = 100
  3. Second block sum = 300
  4. Common difference = 200
  5. Third block sum = 500
  6. S₃₀ = 400 + 500 = 900
Q8. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of \(6^{50} + 4^{50}\)?
A. 0
B. 2
C. 6
D. 8
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Any power of 6 ends with 6; any even power of 4 ends with 6. Sum of the two unit digits 6+6=12 → unit digit 2. Full: \(6^{50}\) ends with 6. \(4^{50} = (4^2)^{25} = 16^{25}\) ends with 6. 6+6=12, so the unit digit is 2.

  3. 50 is even → 4^50 ends in 6
  4. 6+6=12 → unit digit 2
Q9. Mathematics
The difference between Compound Interest and Simple Interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is Rs 50. The sum is:
A. 5000
B. 4000
C. 6000
D. 7000
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Difference for 2 years = P × (R/100)². 50 = P × (10/100)² = P/100 → P = 5000. Full: SI = P×10×2/100 = 0.2P. Amount CI = P(1+10/100)² = 1.21P. CI = 0.21P. Difference = 0.21P - 0.2P = 0.01P = 50 → P = 5000.

Q12. Mathematics
In a right-angled triangle ABC, right-angled at B, BD is the altitude drawn to the hypotenuse AC. If AD = 4 cm and CD = 9 cm, what is the length of BD?
A. 5 cm
B. 6 cm
C. 7 cm
D. 8 cm
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (Altitude Theorem): In a right triangle, the altitude on the hypotenuse is the geometric mean of the segments it creates. BD² = AD × DC. BD² = 4 × 9 = 36 => BD = 6 cm. Step-by-step: Triangles ADB and BDC are similar (AAA similarity). Therefore, AD/BD = BD/CD. Cross-multiplying gives BD² = AD × CD = 4 × 9 = 36. Taking the positive square root (length), BD = 6 cm.
Q13. Mathematics
What is the highest power of 12 that divides 50! ?
A. 20
B. 22
C. 23
D. 24
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Factorize 12 = 2² × 3. Use Legendre's formula to find exponents of 2 and 3 in 50!. Exponent of 2 = 25+12+6+3+1 = 47 → available (2²) pairs = ⌊47/2⌋ = 23. Exponent of 3 = 16+5+1 = 22. The limiting factor is the number of 3s, so the highest power is 22. Full: Calculate prime factor exponents in 50!. For 2: Σ⌊50/2ᵏ⌋ = 47. For 3: Σ⌊50/3ᵏ⌋ = 22. Since 12 = 2²·3, each factor of 12 consumes two 2s and one 3. Max possible factors = min(⌊47/2⌋, 22) = min(23, 22) = 22.

Q15. Mathematics
What is the remainder when 5^100 is divided by 13?
A. 5
B. 8
C. 12
D. 1
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: By Fermat's little theorem, 5¹² ≡ 1 (mod 13). 100 = 12×8 + 4, so 5¹⁰⁰ ≡ (5¹²)⁸ × 5⁴ ≡ 1 × 5⁴ ≡ 625 ≡ 1 (mod 13). Full: 5²=25≡-1 (mod 13), so 5⁴≡1. Hence remainder is 1.
