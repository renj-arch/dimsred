Q4. General Knowledge

A. 1764
B. 1757
C. 1761
D. 1765
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Timeline elimination. 1757 is Battle of Plassey. 1761 is Third Battle of Panipat. 1765 is Treaty of Allahabad (post-Buxar). The battle itself occurred in 1764. Full Solution: The Battle of Buxar was fought on 22 October 1764 between the forces of the British East India Company under Hector Munro and the combined armies of Mir Qasim (Nawab of Bengal), Shuja-ud-Daulah (Nawab of Awadh), and Shah Alam II (Mughal Emperor). Q5. General Knowledge
Night blindness is caused by the deficiency of which vitamin?
A. Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)
B. Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)
C. Vitamin A (Retinol)
D. Vitamin D (Calciferol)
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Mnemonic association 'A' for 'A' (Night Blindness/Retinol). Eliminate B1 (Beriberi), C (Scurvy), D (Rickets/Osteomalacia). Full Solution: Vitamin A (Retinol) is essential for the synthesis of rhodopsin, the visual pigment in the retina's rod cells responsible for low-light vision. Its deficiency leads to impaired dark adaptation known as night blindness (nyctalopia), and prolonged deficiency causes xerophthalmia and keratomalacia.

Q9. General Knowledge
The decisive Battle of Talikota (1565 CE), which led to the catastrophic decline of the Vijayanagara Empire, was fought between the Vijayanagara forces and a confederacy of which powers?
A. Mughal Empire and Maratha Confederacy
B. Deccan Sultanates (Bijapur, Golconda, Ahmadnagar, Bidar)
C. Bahmani Kingdom and Portuguese
D. Chola Empire and Pandya Kingdom
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: **Talikota (1565)** is historically synonymous with the combined attack by the **Deccan Sultanates**. Use chronological elimination: Mughals (Akbar) were in North India; Marathas (Shivaji) rose ~70 years later; Portuguese were coastal; Cholas/Pandyas had declined by the 13th century. Only the Deccan Sultanates were the immediate powerful neighbours of Vijayanagara in the 16th century. Worked: The Battle of Talikota (also called Rakshasa-Tangadi) was fought on 23 January 1565 between the Vijayanagara Empire under Aliya Rama Raya and the united forces of the four major Deccan Sultanates: Hussain Nizam Shah I of Ahmadnagar, Ali Adil Shah I of Bijapur, Ibrahim Qutb Shah Wali of Golconda, and Ali Barid Shah I of Bidar. The defeat resulted in the sack of the capital Hampi and the end of Vijayanagara's imperial dominance.

Q12. General Knowledge
Which of the following plant hormones is primarily responsible for inducing dormancy in seeds and buds, and helps plants withstand water stress by triggering stomatal closure?
A. Auxin (IAA)
B. Gibberellic Acid (GA3)
C. Cytokinin (Kinetin)
D. Abscisic Acid (ABA)
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: **Abscisic Acid (ABA)** -> Think **"Abscission" / "Abort" / "Anti-growth"**. It is the **stress hormone** (closes stomata during drought) and induces **dormancy**. The other three are primarily **growth promoters**: Auxin (cell elongation/apical dominance), Gibberellin (breaks dormancy/stem elongation), Cytokinin (cell division/delays senescence). Worked: Abscisic Acid (ABA) accumulates in leaves under water stress (drought), signaling guard cells to close stomata, reducing transpiration. It also enforces seed and bud dormancy by inhibiting germination/growth processes during unfavorable conditions (winter/drought), ensuring survival. Conversely, Gibberellins are known to *break* seed dormancy.
Q13. General Knowledge
Under Article 352 of the Constitution of India, a Proclamation of National Emergency must be approved by both Houses of Parliament within what period from the date of its issue?
A. One month
B. Two months
C. Six months
D. One year
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The 44th Amendment (1978) reduced the parliamentary approval period for National Emergency (Art 352) from **2 months to 1 month**. Options "6 months" and "1 year" typically refer to the maximum duration for President's Rule (Art 356) extension or the period for which an approved Emergency can continue without fresh approval. Eliminate longer periods; the initial approval window is the shortest. Worked: Article 352 stipulates that a Proclamation of Emergency must be laid before each House of Parliament. It ceases to operate at the expiration of **one month** unless approved by resolutions of both Houses passed by a special majority. Prior to the 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978, this period was two months.
