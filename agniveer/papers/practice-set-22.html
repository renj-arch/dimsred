Q1. Science
What is the power of a convex lens having a focal length of 50 cm?
A. 0.5 D
B. 2 D
C. 5 D
D. 0.2 D
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the formula P(D) = 100 / f(cm) for quick calculation without converting to meters. 100 / 50 = 2 D. Worked Solution: Power (P) = 1 / f(m). Focal length f = 50 cm = 0.5 m. P = 1 / 0.5 = 2 Dioptres. Since the lens is convex, power is positive.
Q2. Reasoning
Select the number that will come next in the following series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
A. 40
B. 41
C. 42
D. 44
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Check differences between terms: 4, 6, 8, 10. Differences increase by 2 each time. Next difference = 12. 30 + 12 = 42. Full Method: The series follows the pattern n × (n+1): 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30. The 6th term is 6×7 = 42. Alternatively, successive differences are consecutive even numbers (4, 6, 8, 10), so next difference is 12, giving 30+12=42.
Q3. Mathematics
If the radius of a sphere is increased by 20%, what is the percentage increase in its volume?
A. 72.8%
B. 44%
C. 20%
D. 60%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Volume ∝ r³. New radius factor = 1.2. Volume factor = (1.2)³ = 1.728. % Increase = (1.728 - 1) × 100 = 72.8%. Worked: Original Volume V₁ = (4/3)πr³. New radius = 1.2r. New Volume V₂ = (4/3)π(1.2r)³ = 1.728 × (4/3)πr³ = 1.728 V₁. Increase = 0.728 V₁ = 72.8%.

Q6. Science
The filtration of blood in the human nephron takes place specifically in which capillary network under high pressure?
A. Proximal Convoluted Tubule
B. Loop of Henle
C. Bowman's Capsule
D. Glomerulus
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Remember the functional sequence: Filtration → Glomerulus (high pressure capillary bed); Selective Reabsorption → PCT; Concentration of Urine → Loop of Henle. Filtration occurs only in the Glomerulus. Full Solution: The glomerulus is a tuft of capillaries formed by the afferent arteriole (wider) and efferent arteriole (narrower). This difference in diameter creates high hydrostatic pressure inside the glomerular capillaries, forcing water and solutes out of the blood into the Bowman's capsule (ultrafiltration). The PCT and Loop of Henle are tubular structures involved in reabsorption, not the primary site of filtration.
Q7. Science
What is the primary function of red blood cells in the human body?
A. Fighting infections
B. Transporting oxygen
C. Clotting blood
D. Producing hormones
Correct Answer: B
Red blood cells contain hemoglobin, a protein that binds to oxygen in the lungs and transports it to tissues throughout the body. Fighting infections is the role of white blood cells, clotting is performed by platelets, and hormone production is primarily a function of endocrine glands.

B. Ten
C. Twelve
D. Fourteen
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Remember the baseline 'Original 8, Current 12'. The 9th Schedule was added in 1951 (1st Amendment), 10th in 1985 (52nd Amendment), 11th & 12th in 1992 (73rd & 74th Amendments). Option A (Eight) is the only figure matching the 1949 status. Worked Solution: The Constitution of India, when adopted on 26 November 1949, had 395 Articles, 22 Parts, and 8 Schedules. The 9th Schedule was inserted by the 1st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1951. The 10th Schedule (Anti-Defection) was added by the 52nd Amendment Act, 1985. The 11th Schedule (Panchayats) and 12th Schedule (Municipalities) were added by the 73rd and 74th Amendment Acts, 1992 respectively, bringing the current total to 12.

Q12. Mathematics
A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. B and C together can complete it in 15 days. C and A together can complete it in 20 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?
A. 20 days
B. 24 days
C. 30 days
D. 40 days
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: LCM Method. Total Work = LCM(12, 15, 20) = 60 units. Efficiency (A+B) = 5 units/day, (B+C) = 4 units/day, (C+A) = 3 units/day. Adding: 2(A+B+C) = 12 units/day => (A+B+C) = 6 units/day. Efficiency of A = 6 - 4 = 2 units/day. Time for A = 60/2 = 30 days. Full Method: 1/A + 1/B = 1/12, 1/B + 1/C = 1/15, 1/C + 1/A = 1/20. Adding gives 2(1/A+1/B+1/C) = 1/5 => 1/A+1/B+1/C = 1/10. Subtracting 1/B+1/C=1/15 gives 1/A = 1/10 - 1/15 = 1/30. Time = 30 days.

The Tapti river originates from the Satpura range near Multai in Betul district, Madhya Pradesh, which is geographically distinct from the Amarkantak plateau.

Q15. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of the expression \((12^{41} + 13^{31} + 17^{21} + 18^{11})\)?
A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 8
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of 4 for unit digits 2,3,7,8. Powers 41,31,21,11 give remainders 1,3,1,3 yielding unit digits 2,7,7,2; sum=18→8. Full: \(12^{41}\to2^{41}\to2^1=2\); \(13^{31}\to3^{31}\to3^3=7\); \(17^{21}\to7^{21}\to7^1=7\); \(18^{11}\to8^{11}\to8^3=2\); total unit digit \(=(2+7+7+2)=18\to8\).
