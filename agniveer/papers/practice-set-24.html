A body starts from rest and moves with a uniform acceleration of 5 m/s². What will be its velocity after covering a distance of 20 m?
A. 10 m/s
B. 10√2 m/s
C. 20 m/s
D. 100 m/s
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use v² = u² + 2as. Since u=0, v = √(2as) = √(2×5×20) = √200 = 10√2 m/s. Option elimination: √2 ≈ 1.414, so value ≈ 14.14 m/s; only option B matches the exact surd form. Step-by-step: Initial velocity u = 0, Acceleration a = 5 m/s², Displacement s = 20 m. Using third equation of motion: v² = 0² + 2(5)(20) = 200. Therefore, v = √200 = 10√2 m/s.

Q5. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-25
The difference between Compound Interest (compounded annually) and Simple Interest on a sum of ₹1600 for 2 years is ₹16. What is the rate of interest per annum?
A. 5%
B. 8%
C. 10%
D. 12%
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For 2 years, Difference (CI - SI) = P × (R/100)². Substituting values: 16 = 1600 × (R/100)² ⇒ (R/100)² = 16/1600 = 1/100 ⇒ R/100 = 1/10 ⇒ R = 10%. Full Solution: SI for 2 years = (1600 × R × 2)/100 = 32R. Amount in CI = 1600(1 + R/100)² = 1600(1 + 2R/100 + R²/10000). CI = Amount - P = 32R + 1600×R²/10000 = 32R + 0.16R². Difference = CI - SI = 0.16R² = 16 ⇒ R² = 100 ⇒ R = 10%.

What is the pH of a 0.001 M hydrochloric acid (HCl) solution? (Given log 10 = 1)
A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: HCl is a strong monoprotic acid, so [H⁺] = Molarity = 1 × 10⁻³ M. pH = -log[H⁺] = -log(10⁻³) = 3. Step-by-step: Concentration = 0.001 M = 10⁻³ M. pH = -log₁₀(10⁻³) = - (log₁₀1 + log₁₀10⁻³) = - (0 - 3) = 3.

In a mixture of 60 litres, the ratio of milk to water is 2:1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1:2?
A. 20 L
B. 30 L
C. 40 L
D. 60 L
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Milk remains constant at 40 L. For final ratio 1:2, water should be 2×40 = 80 L. Additional water = 80−20 = 60 L. Full: Initial milk = (2/3)×60 = 40 L, water = 20 L. Let x be added water. Then 40/(20+x) = 1/2 → 80 = 20+x → x = 60 L.

Deficiency of which vitamin causes Rickets in children?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin B1
C. Vitamin C
D. Vitamin D
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: 'D' for 'D' (Rickets/D). Sunlight helps synthesize Vitamin D; lack of it softens bones (Rickets in kids, Osteomalacia in adults). A. 5%
B. 6%
C. 7%
D. 8%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: SI = Amount − Principal = 1200; Rate = (SI×100)/(P×T) = (1200×100)/(5000×4) = 6%. Full: Simple Interest = 6200−5000 = 1200; using formula SI = (P×R×T)/100 → 1200 = (5000×R×4)/100 → R = 6%.
