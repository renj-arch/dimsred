Q1. General Knowledge
The 'Thomas Cup' is associated with which sport?
A. Badminton
B. Tennis
C. Table Tennis
D. Golf
Correct Answer: A
The Thomas Cup is an international badminton competition among teams representing member nations of the Badminton World Federation (BWF).
Q2. General Knowledge
Which of the following rivers flows through the state of Rajasthan and is known as the 'Saraswati' of the west?
A. Sabarmati
B. Luni
C. Mahi
D. Chambal
Correct Answer: B
The Luni River originates in the Aravalli Range and flows through Rajasthan before disappearing into the Rann of Kutch in Gujarat. It is often referred to as the 'Saraswati' of the west due to its historical significance and its role as a lifeline in an arid region.
Q3. General Knowledge
Who was the first Indian woman to win a medal at the Olympics?
A. P.T. Usha
B. Saina Nehwal
C. Mary Kom
D. Karnam Malleswari
Correct Answer: D
Karnam Malleswari won a bronze medal in weightlifting at the 2000 Sydney Olympics, becoming the first Indian woman to achieve this feat.
Q4. Reasoning
Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "I have no brother or sister, but that man's father is my father's son." Whose photograph was it?
A. His own son
B. His father
C. His uncle
D. His nephew
Correct Answer: A
The phrase 'my father's son' refers to the man himself, as he has no brothers or sisters. Therefore, 'that man's father is me'. This means the man in the photograph is his son.
Q5. Science
What is the SI unit of electric current?
A. Volt
B. Ohm
C. Ampere
D. Watt
Correct Answer: C
The SI unit of electric current is Ampere (A). Volt is the unit for electric potential difference, Ohm for electrical resistance, and Watt for power.
Q6. General Knowledge

B. Ashoka

D. Samudragupta
Correct Answer: C
Chandragupta Maurya founded the Maurya Empire in ancient India around 322 BCE after overthrowing the Nanda Dynasty.
Q7. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells an item for Rs. 800 after giving a discount of 20% on its marked price. What is the marked price of the item?
A. Rs. 960
B. Rs. 1000
C. Rs. 900
D. Rs. 1020
Correct Answer: B
Let the Marked Price (MP) be X. A 20% discount means the selling price (SP) is 80% of the MP. So, 0.80 * X = Rs. 800. X = 800 / 0.80 = Rs. 1000. The marked price is Rs. 1000.
Q8. Science
Which of the following is an example of a non-renewable resource?
A. Solar energy
B. Wind energy
C. Natural gas
D. Hydropower
Correct Answer: C
Non-renewable resources are those that are consumed much faster than they can be replenished. Natural gas is a fossil fuel formed over millions of years and is finite. Solar, wind, and hydropower are renewable energy sources.
Q9. Science
Which part of the human body is responsible for filtering blood and producing urine?
A. Liver
B. Lungs
C. Kidneys
D. Heart
Correct Answer: C
The kidneys are vital organs that filter waste products and excess water from the blood to produce urine, maintaining the body's fluid and electrolyte balance.
Q10. Reasoning
Complete the series: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
A. 37
B. 35
C. 39
D. 38
Correct Answer: A
The pattern involves adding consecutive odd numbers: 2 (+3) = 5, 5 (+5) = 10, 10 (+7) = 17, 17 (+9) = 26. The next number to add is 11. So, 26 + 11 = 37.
