A sum of money amounts to ₹ 12,100 in 2 years at 10% per annum compound interest compounded annually. Find the principal.
A. ₹ 10,000
B. ₹ 10,500
C. ₹ 11,000
D. ₹ 11,500
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For 2 years at 10%, the amount factor is (1.1)² = 1.21; principal = 12,100 ÷ 1.21 = 10,000. Full solution: Using A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ, we have 12,100 = P(1 + 10/100)² = P × 1.21, so P = 12,100 / 1.21 = ₹ 10,000.

Rahul walks 10 km North, then turns right and walks 6 km. Then he turns right again and walks 10 km. Finally, he turns left and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point?
A. 16 km
B. 5 km
C. 11 km
D. 21 km
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Net Displacement Method. Cancel opposite vertical movements (North 10 − South 10 = 0). Add horizontal movements in same direction (East 6 + East 5 = 11). Distance = 11 km. Full Solution: Start (0,0). North 10 → (0,10). Right (East) 6 → (6,10). Right (South) 10 → (6,0). Left (East) 5 → (11,0). Distance from origin = √(11²+0²) = 11 km.

C. Sensory Neuron → Receptor → Interneuron → Motor Neuron → Effector

Q8. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-27
A car travels from point A to point B at a speed of 60 km/h and returns from B to A at a speed of 40 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire journey?
A. 50 km/h
B. 48 km/h
C. 52 km/h
D. 45 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For equal distances, average speed is the Harmonic Mean of speeds: 2ab/(a+b) = (2 * 60 * 40) / (60 + 40) = 4800 / 100 = 48 km/h. Step-by-step: Let distance be d km. Time for onward = d/60 hrs, return = d/40 hrs. Total distance = 2d. Total time = d/60 + d/40 = 5d/120 = d/24. Average speed = Total Distance / Total Time = 2d / (d/24) = 48 km/h.

A. 1:2
B. 1:1
C. 2:1
D. 3:1
Correct Answer: B

Step-by-step: Let quantities be x kg and y kg. (120x + 150y) / (x + y) = 135 => 120x + 150y = 135x + 135y => 15y = 15x => x : y = 1 : 1.
Q13. Sciencemodel-generated · 2026-11-27
Two electric bulbs rated 60 W–220 V and 100 W–220 V are connected in series across a 220 V supply. Which bulb will glow brighter?
A. 60 W bulb
B. 100 W bulb
C. Both equally bright
D. Neither will glow
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: At rated voltage, Resistance R = V²/P. Lower power rating means higher resistance. In a series circuit, current (I) is same; actual power dissipated P_actual = I²R ∝ R. Hence, the bulb with higher resistance (60 W) glows brighter. Worked: R₆₀ = 220²/60 ≈ 807 Ω; R₁₀₀ = 220²/100 = 484 Ω. Since R₆₀ > R₁₀₀ and current is common, the 60 W bulb dissipates more power.
