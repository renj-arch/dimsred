Q1. Mathematics
A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price. He allows two successive discounts of 20% and 10% on the marked price. What is his profit or loss percentage?
A. 0.8% Profit
B. 1.2% Profit
C. 0.8% Loss
D. 1.2% Loss
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Net discount for successive 20% and 10% = 20 + 10 - (20×10/100) = 28%. Effective SP = MP × (100-28)/100 = 1.4 CP × 0.72 = 1.008 CP. Profit = 0.8%. Full: Let CP = 100. MP = 140. After 20% discount: 140 × 0.8 = 112. After 10% discount: 112 × 0.9 = 100.8. Profit = 0.8 on CP 100 → 0.8% Profit.
Q2. Mathematics
A person covers one-third of his journey at 20 km/h, the next one-third at 30 km/h, and the remaining one-third at 60 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?
A. 32 km/h
B. 30 km/h
C. 33 km/h
D. 35 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For equal distances, average speed = Harmonic Mean of speeds = n / Σ(1/speed) = 3 / (1/20 + 1/30 + 1/60) = 3 / (6/60) = 30 km/h. Full: Let each part = 60 km (LCM). Time for 1st part = 3 h, 2nd = 2 h, 3rd = 1 h. Total distance = 180 km, Total time = 6 h. Average speed = 180/6 = 30 km/h.
Q3. Science
The process of conversion of atmospheric nitrogen into ammonia by bacteria in the soil is called:
A. Nitrogen fixation
B. Nitrification
C. Denitrification
D. Ammonification
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The term 'fixation' directly indicates converting inert N₂ into a fixed form like NH₃. Step-by-step: Nitrogen fixation is carried out by nitrogen‑fixing bacteria (e.g., Rhizobium) converting N₂ → NH₃. Nitrification oxidizes NH₃ to NO₂⁻/NO₃⁻, denitrification reduces NO₃⁻ to N₂, and ammonification decomposes organic nitrogen to NH₃. Thus the correct process is nitrogen fixation.
Q4. Science
A 5 kg object is lifted vertically through a height of 10 m. Taking g = 10 m/s², what is the work done against gravity?
A. 50 J
B. 250 J
C. 500 J
D. 1000 J
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Work against gravity = weight × height = (mg) × h. With g = 10 m/s², weight = 5 × 10 = 50 N. Multiply by 10 m gives 500 J. Full solution: Work done = force × displacement in direction of force. Force = mg = 5 kg × 10 m/s² = 50 N. Displacement = 10 m. Work = 50 N × 10 m = 500 J.
Q5. Reasoning
In a certain code language, 'MANGO' is written as 'NZMTL'. How is 'APPLE' written in that code?
A. ZKKOU
B. ZKLOV
C. ZKKOV
D. ZKKPV
Correct Answer: C
**Shortcut (Atbash Cipher Rule):** In a reverse alphabet code (Atbash), the sum of the positional values of a plain letter and its coded letter is always 27 (since A=1, Z=26; 1+26=27). To find the code for any letter, subtract its position value from 27. **Step-by-Step Solution:** 1. **Analyze the given example:** M(13) → N(14), A(1) → Z(26), N(14) → M(13), G(7) → T(20), O(15) → L(12). 2. **Identify the pattern:** 13+14=27, 1+26=27, 14+13=27, 7+20=27, 15+12=27. The pattern is Reverse Alphabet (Atbash Cipher). 3. **Apply to 'APPLE':** - A (1) → 27-1 = 26 → **Z** - P (16) → 27-16 = 11 → **K** - P (16) → 27-16 = 11 → **K** - L (12) → 27-12 = 15 → **O** - E (5) → 27-5 = 22 → **V** 4. **Final Code:** **ZKKOV**.
Q6. Mathematics
The sum of two numbers is 45 and their difference is 15. What is the product of the numbers?
A. 400
B. 425
C. 450
D. 500
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For two numbers with sum S and difference D, product = (S² – D²)/4. Here S=45, D=15 → (2025 – 225)/4 = 1800/4 = 450. Full: Let the numbers be x and y. x + y = 45 and x – y = 15. Adding gives 2x = 60 → x = 30, then y = 15. Product = 30 × 15 = 450.
Q7. Reasoning
A soldier starts from his base camp and walks 10 km North. He turns right and walks 15 km. He turns right again and walks 10 km. Finally, he turns left and walks 5 km. What is the straight-line distance between his final position and the base camp?
A. 15 km
B. 18 km
C. 20 km
D. 25 km
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Track Net Displacement. North (10 km) and South (10 km) cancel each other out. East movements (15 km + 5 km) add up to 20 km. No net North/South displacement remains. Distance = 20 km. Worked: Let Base be origin (0,0). 1. Walk 10 km North → (0, 10). 2. Turn Right (East), walk 15 km → (15, 10). 3. Turn Right (South), walk 10 km → (15, 0). 4. Turn Left (East), walk 5 km → (20, 0). Final coordinates (20, 0). Distance from (0,0) = √(20² + 0²) = 20 km.
Q8. General Knowledge
The First Battle of Panipat, which established the Mughal Empire in India, was fought in which year?
A. 1526
B. 1556
C. 1761
D. 1192
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Eliminate 1192 (Second Battle of Tarain, Ghori vs Prithviraj), 1556 (Second Battle of Panipat, Akbar vs Hemu), and 1761 (Third Battle of Panipat, Marathas vs Abdali). The only remaining option is 1526. Full Solution: The First Battle of Panipat was fought on 21 April 1526 between Babur and Ibrahim Lodi, marking the beginning of the Mughal Empire.

Which of the following is the source of the Brahmaputra River (known as Yarlung Tsangpo in Tibet)?
A. Gangotri Glacier
B. Yamunotri Glacier
C. Mansarovar Lake
D. Amarkantak Plateau

Mansarovar (near Mount Kailash) is the traditional source region for the Trans-Himalayan rivers Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra, and Karnali. Q10. General Knowledge
Which is the highest wartime gallantry award in India?
A. Ashoka Chakra
B. Maha Vir Chakra
C. Vir Chakra
D. Param Vir Chakra
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: 'Param' means Highest/Supreme. 'Vir' means Brave. Param Vir Chakra (PVC) = Highest Wartime. Ashoka Chakra = Highest Peacetime. Maha Vir Chakra = 2nd Wartime. Vir Chakra = 3rd Wartime. Eliminate Peacetime (Ashoka) and lower Wartime (Maha Vir, Vir). Full Solution: The Param Vir Chakra (PVC) is India's highest military decoration awarded for displaying distinguished acts of valour during wartime. It is the wartime equivalent of the Ashoka Chakra, which is the highest peacetime gallantry award.
Q11. General Knowledge
Deficiency of which vitamin is the primary cause of Night Blindness (Nyctalopia)?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin B12
C. Vitamin C
D. Vitamin D
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use Disease-Vitamin mapping elimination. Vitamin C deficiency causes Scurvy. Vitamin D deficiency causes Rickets/Osteomalacia. Vitamin B12 deficiency causes Pernicious Anemia. By eliminating the three known pairs, Vitamin A is the only option left for Night Blindness. Worked Solution: Vitamin A (Retinol) is a critical component of rhodopsin, the light-sensitive pigment in retinal rod cells essential for low-light vision. Its deficiency leads to xerophthalmia and night blindness. Vitamin B12 deficiency leads to megaloblastic anemia and neurological issues. Vitamin C deficiency causes scurvy (bleeding gums, poor wound healing). Vitamin D deficiency causes rickets in children and osteomalacia in adults.
Q12. Science
What is the pH of a 0.001 M NaOH solution at 298 K?
A. 3
B. 4
C. 11
D. 10
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For strong bases, pOH = -log[OH⁻]. Since [OH⁻] = 1 × 10⁻³ M, pOH = 3. Then pH = 14 - pOH = 11. Worked Solution: NaOH is a strong base, so [OH⁻] = 0.001 M = 10⁻³ M. pOH = -log₁₀(10⁻³) = 3. Using Kw = 10⁻¹⁴ at 298 K, pH + pOH = 14. Therefore, pH = 14 - 3 = 11.
Q13. Mathematics
A train 150 m long passes a pole in 15 seconds. How long will it take to pass a platform 250 m long?
A. 30 seconds
B. 40 seconds
C. 50 seconds
D. 60 seconds
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Speed = train length / time to cross pole = 150/15 = 10 m/s. Time to cross platform = (train length + platform length) / speed = (150+250)/10 = 40 s. Full solution: The train's speed is 10 m/s. To cross the platform it must cover its own length plus the platform length, i.e., 400 m. At 10 m/s this takes 400/10 = 40 seconds.
Q14. Reasoning
If 'A # B' means 'A is the father of B', 'A @ B' means 'A is the mother of B', 'A $ B' means 'A is the brother of B', and 'A % B' means 'A is the sister of B', then which of the following expressions shows that P is the paternal grandfather of Q?
A. P # R # Q
B. P # R @ Q
C. P @ R # Q
D. P @ R @ Q
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Paternal grandfather requires P to be male (#) and two generations above Q via male line. Scan options for P # ... # Q. Option A fits perfectly. Full Solution: In Option A: P # R means P is father of R. R # Q means R is father of Q. Thus P is father of Q's father, making P the paternal grandfather. Option B makes P maternal grandfather (R @ Q). Options C and D make P female (@), so grandmother.
Q15. General Knowledge
Which of the following is the highest mountain peak located entirely within India's territory?
A. Mount Everest
B. K2 (Godwin-Austen)
C. Kanchenjunga
D. Nanda Devi
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Eliminate Everest (Nepal/China border) and K2 (PoK/Pakistan/China). Kanchenjunga (8,586m) lies on the Sikkim-Nepal border but its main summit is the highest point *in* India. Nanda Devi (7,816m) is the second highest entirely within India. Full Solution: Kanchenjunga is the third highest peak in the world and the highest peak located in India. K2 is higher but lies in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir (Gilgit-Baltistan), while Mount Everest lies on the Nepal-China border.
