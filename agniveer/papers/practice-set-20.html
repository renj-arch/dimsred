Q1. Mathematics
A copper wire is bent in the form of a circle of radius 14 cm. If the same wire is bent into a square, what is the area of the square? (Use π = 22/7)
A. 121 cm²
B. 196 cm²
C. 484 cm²
D. 1764 cm²
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Perimeter remains constant. Circumference = 2 × (22/7) × 14 = 88 cm. Side of square = 88/4 = 22 cm. Area = 22² = 484 cm². Full: Length of wire = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 14 = 88 cm. This length becomes perimeter of square: 4a = 88 → a = 22 cm. Area = a² = 22 × 22 = 484 cm².
Q2. Mathematics
Find the unit digit of the expression (7^95 + 3^58).
A. 0
B. 2
C. 4
D. 6
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits (cycle of 4). For 7^95: 95 mod 4 = 3, 3rd position in cycle (7,9,3,1) is 3. For 3^58: 58 mod 4 = 2, 2nd position in cycle (3,9,7,1) is 9. Sum of unit digits = 3 + 9 = 12, unit digit is 2. Full: 7^95 = 7^(4*23+3) → unit digit of 7^3=343 is 3. 3^58 = 3^(4*14+2) → unit digit of 3^2=9 is 9. 3+9=12 → unit digit 2.
Q3. Reasoning
In a certain code language, vowels are coded as the next letter and consonants are coded as the previous letter in the English alphabet. How will 'ORANGE' be written in that code?
A. PQBMFE
B. PQCMFF
C. PQBMFF
D. PQBMFG
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Identify vowels (A,E,I,O,U) -> Next letter (+1). Consonants -> Previous letter (-1). Apply quickly without writing full alphabet. Worked Solution: O(vowel)->P; R(cons)->Q; A(vowel)->B; N(cons)->M; G(cons)->F; E(vowel)->F. Result: PQBMFF.
Q4. General Knowledge
Who is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha?
A. President of India
B. Vice President of India
C. Prime Minister of India
D. Speaker of Lok Sabha
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use Option Elimination based on Constitutional designations. The President is Head of State (not RS Chair), the PM is Head of Government (usually LS member), and the Speaker presides over Lok Sabha. Only the Vice President holds the ex-officio Chairmanship of Rajya Sabha. Step-by-step: Article 89(1) of the Indian Constitution explicitly states that the Vice President of India shall be the ex-officio Chairman of the Council of States (Rajya Sabha). The President summons Parliament but does not chair either house. The Prime Minister leads the Lower House typically. The Speaker is the presiding officer of Lok Sabha only.
Q5. General Knowledge
Who was the first Governor-General of Independent India?
A. Lord Mountbatten
B. C. Rajagopalachari
C. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
D. Warren Hastings
Correct Answer: A
Lord Mountbatten served as the first Governor-General of the Dominion of India from 15 August 1947 to 21 June 1948. C. Warren Hastings was the first Governor-General of *Bengal* (1773).
Q6. Science
Which organelle is known as the 'powerhouse of the cell'?
A. Mitochondria
B. Ribosome
C. Nucleus
D. Chloroplast
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The term 'powerhouse' directly refers to mitochondria because it generates ATP through cellular respiration. Full solution: Mitochondria are double-membraned organelles where aerobic respiration occurs, producing ATP, the energy currency of the cell. Ribosomes synthesize proteins, the nucleus stores genetic material, and chloroplasts perform photosynthesis in plant cells. Hence, mitochondria is the correct answer.
Q7. Mathematics
The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point on the ground 30 m away from the foot of the tower is 30°. Find the height of the tower.
A. 10√3 m
B. 15√3 m
C. 20√3 m
D. 30√3 m
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use standard ratio for 30°-60°-90° triangle (1 : √3 : 2). tan 30° = 1/√3 = Height / 30 → Height = 30/√3 = 10√3 m. Full: In right triangle, tan(30°) = Perpendicular / Base = Height / 30. 1/√3 = h/30 → h = 30/√3. Rationalizing denominator: h = (30√3)/3 = 10√3 m.

B. 11

D. 9
Correct Answer: B

Q11. Reasoning
Rahul starts from point A and walks 10 km North, then turns right and walks 5 km, then turns right again and walks 10 km. How far is he from the starting point?
A. 10 km
B. 15 km
C. 0 km
D. 5 km
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: North and South movements cancel (10 km each), leaving only 5 km East displacement. Step-by-step: Plot on coordinates: start (0,0) → North to (0,10) → right (East) to (5,10) → right (South) to (5,0); distance from origin = √(5²+0²) = 5 km.
Q12. Reasoning
In a certain code language, 'PENCIL' is written as 'KVMXRO'. How will 'SHARP' be written in that language?
A. HSZIK
B. HSZKI
C. HZSIK
D. HSZIL
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Recognize the Atbash cipher (Reverse Alphabet) where each letter is replaced by its mirror position (A↔Z, B↔Y). Sum of positions = 27. For 'SHARP': S(19)→H(8), H(8)→S(19), A(1)→Z(26), R(18)→I(9), P(16)→K(11). Full Solution: PENCIL→KVMXRO confirms P(16)→K(11), E(5)→V(22), N(14)→M(13), etc., each pair summing to 27. Applying 27−Position to SHARP gives H, S, Z, I, K → HSZIK.
Q13. General Knowledge
What is the minimum age required to become the Prime Minister of India?
A. 30 years
B. 35 years
C. 25 years
D. 40 years
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Link PM eligibility to Lok Sabha membership (Article 84). The PM must be a member of Parliament; the lower house (Lok Sabha) minimum age is 25 years. Rajya Sabha is 30 years. Since PM can be from Lok Sabha, the effective minimum is 25. Worked Solution: As per Article 84 of the Constitution, a person must be a citizen of India and not less than 25 years of age to be a member of the Lok Sabha. Since the Prime Minister must be a member of either House of Parliament (or get elected within 6 months), the minimum constitutional age is 25 years. Age for President/Vice President/Governor is 35 years; Rajya Sabha/State Legislative Council is 30 years.
Q14. Science
A straight wire of length 0.5 m carries a current of 2 A in a uniform magnetic field of 0.4 T. If the wire is oriented perpendicular to the field direction, what is the magnitude of the magnetic force acting on the wire?
A. 0.2 N
B. 0.4 N
C. 0.8 N
D. 1.6 N
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use F = BIL sinθ. Since θ = 90°, sinθ = 1. Multiply B × I × L = 0.4 × 2 × 0.5. Notice 2 × 0.5 = 1, so Force = 0.4 × 1 = 0.4 N instantly. Full Solution: Magnetic force on a current-carrying conductor is given by F = B I L sinθ. Substituting B = 0.4 T, I = 2 A, L = 0.5 m, θ = 90°: F = 0.4 × 2 × 0.5 × 1 = 0.4 N.
Q15. Science
Which hormone, secreted by the beta cells of the Islets of Langerhans in the pancreas, lowers the blood glucose level by promoting its uptake into cells?
A. Insulin
B. Glucagon
C. Adrenaline
D. Cortisol
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Mnemonic — 'Insulin puts sugar **In** cells'. Glucagon, Adrenaline, and Cortisol all **increase** blood glucose (hyperglycemic). Only Insulin decreases it (hypoglycemic). Eliminate the three 'stress/raise' hormones. Full Solution: Insulin is a peptide hormone secreted by β-cells of pancreatic Islets of Langerhans. It binds to tyrosine kinase receptors triggering GLUT4 translocation, facilitating glucose uptake in muscle/adipose tissue, lowering blood glucose. Glucagon (α-cells), Adrenaline (adrenal medulla), and Cortisol (adrenal cortex) act via cAMP to increase hepatic glucose output.
