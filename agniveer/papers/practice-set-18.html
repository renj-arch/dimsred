Q2. Science
Which of the following elements is essential for the process of photosynthesis in plants?
A. Nitrogen
B. Phosphorus
C. Magnesium
D. Sodium
Correct Answer: C
Magnesium is a central component of chlorophyll, the pigment that captures light energy for photosynthesis. Nitrogen and Phosphorus are vital macronutrients but do not directly form the light-capturing structure. Sodium is generally not required by plants in significant amounts and is not part of the photosynthetic machinery.

A. FKDLU
B. FKDUL
C. FLKDU
D. FDKLU
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Compare the first letters of the given word and code (T → W). The shift is +3 positions forward in the alphabet. Apply this constant shift (+3) to each letter of 'CHAIR' to get the answer instantly. Step-by-step: T(20)+3=W(23), A(1)+3=D(4), B(2)+3=E(5), L(12)+3=O(15), E(5)+3=H(8). Pattern confirmed as +3. For CHAIR: C(3)+3=F(6), H(8)+3=K(11), A(1)+3=D(4), I(9)+3=L(12), R(18)+3=U(21). Result: FKDLU.
Q4. Mathematics
A shopkeeper gives two successive discounts of 20% and 10% on a marked price of ₹500. What is the selling price?
A. 350
B. 340
C. 330
D. 360
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Successive discounts of a% and b% equal a+b−ab/100 =20+10−2 =28% net discount. SP = 500×(1−0.28)=360. Full method: After 20% discount price =500×0.8=400. After further 10% discount =400×0.9=360.
Q5. Mathematics
The LCM of two numbers is 180 and their HCF is 6. If one number is 30, find the other number.
A. 45
B. 36
C. 54
D. 60
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For any two numbers, product = LCM × HCF. Hence other number = (180×6)/30 = 36. Full method: Let the numbers be 30 and x. LCM(30,x)=180, HCF(30,x)=6. Since 30×x = 180×6 =1080, x =1080/30 =36.

D. 5
Correct Answer: C
The common element between '247' (sky is blue) and '689' (sea is deep) is the digit '8', which represents 'is'. Q9. General Knowledge

A. Ganga
B. Brahmaputra
C. Godavari
D. Yamuna
Correct Answer: A
The Ganga is the longest river flowing entirely within India (approx. 2,525 km). While the Indus and Brahmaputra are longer in total length, they flow through other countries. The Godavari is the longest peninsular river but shorter than the Ganga. This distinction between 'total length' and 'length within India' is a common geography trap.
Q10. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of 7^95?
A. 3
B. 7
C. 9
D. 1
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The unit digit of powers of 7 repeats every 4 (7, 9, 3, 1). 95 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 3, so the unit digit is the 3rd in the cycle = 3. Full method: 7^1=7, 7^2=49→9, 7^3=343→3, 7^4=2401→1, cycle length 4; 95=4×23+3 → unit digit = 3.
Q11. Science
In a dihybrid cross (AaBb × AaBb), what is the phenotypic ratio of offspring showing both dominant traits?
Genotype CombinationFrequency
A_B_9
A_bb3
aaB_3
aabb1
A. 9:16
B. 9:7
C. 9:3
D. 9:1
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a dihybrid cross AaBb × AaBb, the classic phenotypic ratio is 9 (both dominant) : 3 (dominant A, recessive b) : 3 (recessive a, dominant B) : 1 (both recessive) = 9:7 for the combined dominant‑dominant vs all others. Thus the ratio of offspring showing both dominant traits to the total is 9/(9+3+3+1)=9/16, but the question asks for the ratio of "both dominant" to "others" which is 9:7. Full: Total parts = 9+3+3+1 = 16. Both dominant = 9 parts. Others = 3+3+1 = 7 parts. Hence ratio = 9:7.
Q12. General Knowledge
A person with blood group 'O' is considered a universal donor for red blood cell transfusion because their red cells lack which antigens?
A. A and B antigens
B. Rh factor only
C. A antigen only
D. B antigen only
Correct Answer: A
Blood group O negative is the universal donor for red cells because the red blood cells lack both A and B antigens on their surface, preventing an immune reaction in recipients of any ABO type. However, the plasma of group O contains both anti-A and anti-B antibodies, so plasma compatibility is reversed. The question specifically asks about red cell transfusion.

Q15. Mathematics
A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 12 seconds. What is its speed in km/h?
A. 40
B. 50
C. 45
D. 55
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Speed (m/s) = length/time =150/12=12.5 m/s. Convert to km/h by multiplying 18/5 → 12.5×18/5 =45 km/h. Full method: Distance =150 m, time=12 s → speed=12.5 m/s. 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h, so 12.5×3.6 =45 km/h.
