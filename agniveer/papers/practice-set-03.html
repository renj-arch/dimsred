If 'ORANGE' is coded as 'PSBOHF', how is 'LEMON' coded?

B. MFOPN
C. MEPNO
D. NFNPO
Correct Answer: A
Each letter is shifted by +1: O→P, R→S, A→B, N→O, G→H, E→F. So ORANGE→PSBOHF. Similarly, L→M, E→F, M→N, O→P, N→O = MFNPO.

Q12. Reasoning
Statements: All cats are mammals. Some mammals are black. Conclusions: I. Some cats are black. II. All mammals are cats. Which conclusion(s) follow?
A. Only I follows
B. Only II follows
C. Neither I nor II follows
D. Both I and II follow
Correct Answer: C
From the statements: All cats are mammals (true). Some mammals are black. It does not necessarily mean some cats are black — the black mammals could be non-cats. Conclusion II is clearly false. So neither follows.

Q24. Reasoning
How many letters are there in the word 'MATHEMATICS' that come before M in the alphabet?
A. 3
B. 4
C. 5
D. 6
Correct Answer: B
Letters before M in the alphabet: A, E, I (3 letters). Wait, also H? Let me check: M-A-T-H-E-M-A-T-I-C-S. Letters before M: A (×2), E, H, I. That's A, A, E, H, I = 5 occurrences. But distinct letters before M: A, E, H, I = 4 distinct letters. The question asks "letters" — likely meaning distinct letters: A, E, H, I = 4.

Q50. Mathematics
If a triangle has sides 6 cm, 8 cm, and 10 cm, what type of triangle is it?
A. Equilateral
B. Isosceles
C. Right-angled
D. Scalene
Correct Answer: C
6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100 = 10². Since a² + b² = c², the triangle satisfies Pythagoras theorem, so it is right-angled (3-4-5 triple scaled by 2).
