Q8. [+2, -0.25] Reasoning
If a snail is at the bottom of a 20-foot well, and it climbs 3 feet up the well each day, but slips back 2 feet each night, how many days will it take for the snail to reach the top of the well?
A. 18 days
B. 20 days
C. 22 days
D. 18 days, but on the 18th day it will reach the top and not slip back
Correct Answer: D
The snail climbs 3 feet up the well each day and slips back 2 feet each night, so it effectively climbs 1 foot per day. To climb 20 feet, it will take 18 days to climb 18 feet, and on the 18th day, it will climb the final 2 feet to reach the top, but it will not slip back because it has reached the top.

Q15. [+2, -0.25] Reasoning
A person has two coins that add up to 30 pence. One coin is twice the value of the other. What are the two coins?
A. 10p and 20p
B. 5p and 25p
C. 15p and 15p
D. 20p and 10p
Correct Answer: A
Let's say the smaller coin is x pence. Since the larger coin is twice the value of the smaller coin, the larger coin is 2x pence. The total value of the two coins is 30 pence, so we can set up the equation: x + 2x = 30. Simplifying the equation, we get 3x = 30. Dividing both sides by 3 yields x = 10. So, the smaller coin is 10p and the larger coin is 20p.
