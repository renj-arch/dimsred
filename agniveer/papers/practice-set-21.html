Q7. Sciencemodel-generated · 2026-11-20
A body starts from rest and moves with a uniform acceleration of 4 m/s². The distance travelled by the body in the 5th second of its motion is:
A. 18 m
B. 12 m
C. 16 m
D. 20 m
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For a body starting from rest (u=0), distance travelled in the nth second = a × (n - 0.5). Here, a = 4 m/s² and n = 5. Distance = 4 × (5 - 0.5) = 4 × 4.5 = 18 m. Full Solution: Using the formula for displacement in the nth second: S_n = u + (a/2)(2n - 1). Given u = 0, a = 4 m/s², n = 5. S_5 = 0 + (4/2)(2×5 - 1) = 2 × 9 = 18 m.

D. The solution turns green and the nail dissolves completely
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Recall reactivity series mnemonic '... Fe ... (H) ... Cu ...'. Iron (Fe) is above Copper (Cu), so Fe displaces Cu²⁺. Reaction: Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu. It displaces copper from its salt solution forming ferrous sulphate (light green) and copper metal (reddish-brown). The blue colour of CuSO₄ fades as Cu²⁺ ions are reduced to Cu(s), which coats the nail.

Q13. Sciencemodel-generated · 2026-11-20
The oxide of an element 'X' has the formula X₂O₃. What will be the formula of its chloride?
A. XCl
B. XCl₃
C. XCl₂
D. XCl₄
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the cross-valency rule. In X₂O₃, oxygen has valency 2. Cross-multiplying gives valency of X = 3. Since chlorine has valency 1, the formula of chloride is XCl₃. Full Solution: In X₂O₃, let oxidation state of X be x. Oxygen is -2. 2x + 3(-2) = 0 → 2x = 6 → x = +3. Valency of X is 3. Chlorine (Cl) has valency 1. To balance valencies, one atom of X combines with three atoms of Cl. Formula = XCl₃.
Q14. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-20
A solid metallic sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into a solid right circular cylinder of radius 4 cm. The height of the cylinder is:
A. 12 cm
B. 18 cm
C. 24 cm
D. 36 cm
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Volume remains constant during melting. Equate Volume of Sphere = Volume of Cylinder. (4/3)πr³ = πR²h. Cancel π. h = (4/3 × 6³) / 4² = (4/3 × 216) / 16 = 288 / 16 = 18 cm. Step-by-step: Volume of sphere = (4/3)π(6)³ = (4/3)π(216) = 288π cm³. Volume of cylinder = π(4)²h = 16πh cm³. Equating: 16πh = 288π => h = 288/16 = 18 cm.
