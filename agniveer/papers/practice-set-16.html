Q5. Sciencemodel-generated · 2026-11-14
As per the chemical equation $N_2 + 3H_2 \rightarrow 2NH_3$, if 14 grams of Nitrogen gas reacts completely with excess Hydrogen, how many moles of Ammonia ($NH_3$) are produced? (Atomic masses: N=14, H=1)
A. A. 1 mole
B. B. 2 moles
C. C. 0.5 mole
D. D. 1.5 moles
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Notice the mass ratio. 28g of $N_2$ (2 moles) produces 2 moles of $NH_3$. Therefore, 14g ($N_2$) is exactly half that amount and will produce exactly 1 mole of $NH_3$. Step-by-step: Molar mass of $N_2$ = $14 \times 2 = 28$ g/mol. Moles of $N_2$ used = $14/28 = 0.5$ mol. From the stoichiometry ($1 N_2 \rightarrow 2 NH_3$), $0.5$ mol $N_2$ yields $0.5 \times 2 = 1$ mol $NH_3$.

If all 'Clouds' are 'Rain', and some 'Rain' is 'Wind', then which of the following conclusions logically follows? I. Some Clouds are Wind. II. Some Rain is not Cloud.
A. Only I follows
B. Only II follows
C. Either I or II follows
D. Neither I nor II follows
Correct Answer: D
From 'All Clouds are Rain', we know the set of Clouds is inside Rain. From 'Some Rain is Wind', there is an intersection between Rain and Wind, but this does not guarantee any overlap with Clouds; thus, I does not follow. Similarly, the statement 'Some Rain is Wind' tells us nothing about whether some Rain is NOT Cloud (it could be that all Rain is Cloud), so II does not follow. Therefore, neither conclusion follows.

Q15. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-14
A shopkeeper marks an article 50% above its cost price and offers a discount of 20% on the marked price. If the final selling price is Rs. 600, what is the profit percentage?
A. 10%
B. 20%
C. 30%
D. 40%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Let CP = 100x. MP = 150x. SP = 80% of 150x = 120x. Given SP = 600, so 120x = 600 => x=5. Profit = SP - CP = 600 - 500 = 100. Profit% = (100/500)*100 = 20%. Alternatively, use the formula: Effective % change = 50 - 20 - (50*20)/100 = 30 - 10 = +20% profit.
