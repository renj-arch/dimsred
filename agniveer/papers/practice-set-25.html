If \( x + \frac{1}{x} = 4 \), what is the value of \( x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} \)?

B. 60
C. 52
D. 64
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use the identity \( (x + \frac{1}{x})^3 = x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} + 3(x + \frac{1}{x}) \). Substitute the given value: \( 4^3 = x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} + 3(4) \Rightarrow 64 = x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} + 12 \Rightarrow x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = 52 \). Step-by-step: Cube both sides of the given equation \( x + \frac{1}{x} = 4 \). The expansion of \( (a+b)^3 \) is \( a^3 + b^3 + 3ab(a+b) \). Here \( a=x, b=\frac{1}{x} \), so \( ab=1 \). Thus \( (x + \frac{1}{x})^3 = x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} + 3(1)(x + \frac{1}{x}) \). Substitute \( x + \frac{1}{x} = 4 \): \( 64 = x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} + 12 \). Solving gives \( x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = 52 \).

Q15. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-26
The sides of a triangle are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. If the perimeter of the triangle is 48 cm, what is its area (in \(\text{cm}^2\))?
A. 96
B. 48
C. 72
D. 100
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Recognize 3:4:5 as a Pythagorean triplet (right-angled triangle). Let sides be \(3x, 4x, 5x\). Perimeter \(12x = 48 \Rightarrow x = 4\). Sides are 12 cm, 16 cm, 20 cm. Area = \(\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 16 = 96\). Step-by-step: Sum of ratio terms = 3+4+5 = 12. Value of one part = 48/12 = 4. Sides = 12, 16, 20. Check right angle: \(12^2 + 16^2 = 144 + 256 = 400 = 20^2\). Area = 0.5 * 12 * 16 = 96 \(\text{cm}^2\).
