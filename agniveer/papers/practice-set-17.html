Correct Answer: C

What is the remainder when (12^13 + 13^12) is divided by 5?
A. 0
B. 1
C. 2
D. 3
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use modular arithmetic (Unit digit pattern for division by 5). 12 ≡ 2 (mod 5) and 13 ≡ 3 (mod 5). Expression ≡ 2^13 + 3^12 (mod 5). Cyclicity of 2 mod 5: 2, 4, 3, 1 (cycle 4). 13 mod 4 = 1 => 2^13 ≡ 2^1 ≡ 2. Cyclicity of 3 mod 5: 3, 4, 2, 1 (cycle 4). 12 mod 4 = 0 => 3^12 ≡ 3^4 ≡ 1. Sum = 2 + 1 = 3. Step-by-step: Remainder of 12^13 / 5 = Remainder of 2^13 / 5. 2^4 = 16 ≡ 1. 2^13 = (2^4)^3 * 2 ≡ 1^3 * 2 = 2. Remainder of 13^12 / 5 = Remainder of 3^12 / 5. 3^4 = 81 ≡ 1. 3^12 = (3^4)^3 ≡ 1^3 = 1. Total remainder = (2 + 1) = 3.

Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Direct formula for intersection of internal angle bisectors: ∠BOC = 90° + (∠A / 2). Substituting ∠A = 80°: ∠BOC = 90° + 40° = 130°. Step-by-step: In ΔABC, ∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180° => 80° + ∠B + ∠C = 180° => ∠B + ∠C = 100°. In ΔBOC, ∠OBC = ∠B/2 and ∠OCB = ∠C/2. Sum of angles in ΔBOC: ∠BOC + ∠B/2 + ∠C/2 = 180°. ∠BOC + (∠B + ∠C)/2 = 180°. ∠BOC + 100°/2 = 180°. ∠BOC = 180° - 50° = 130°.

Q10. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 25% on the marked price. What is his profit percentage?
A. 5%
B. 10%
C. 15%
D. 20%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use successive percentage change formula: Net % = a + b + (ab/100). Here a = +40 (markup), b = -25 (discount). Net % = 40 - 25 + (40 * -25 / 100) = 15 - 10 = 5% profit. Step-by-step: Let CP = ₹100. Marked Price (MP) = 100 + 40% of 100 = ₹140. Selling Price (SP) = MP - 25% of MP = 140 - 35 = ₹105. Profit = SP - CP = 105 - 100 = ₹5. Profit % = (5/100) * 100 = 5%.
