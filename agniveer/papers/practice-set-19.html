A. 8 : 27
B. 4 : 9
C. 2 : 3

Correct Answer: A

Q3. Sciencemodel-generated · 2026-11-17
A student suffering from Myopia (near-sightedness) has a far point of 50 cm. What is the nature and power of the corrective lens required to enable him to see distant objects clearly?
A. Convex lens of power +2.0 D
B. Convex lens of power -2.0 D
C. Concave lens of power +2.0 D
D. Concave lens of power -2.0 D
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Myopia correction always requires a Diverging (Concave) lens. Concave lenses have Negative focal length and Negative Power. Eliminate Convex options (A, B) and Positive Power Concave option (C). Only Concave lens with Negative Power remains. Step-by-step: In Myopia, the far point is finite (50 cm = 0.5 m). To see objects at infinity clearly, the lens must form a virtual image at the far point. Using Lens Formula P = 1/f (f in meters). Object at infinity (u = ∞), Image at far point (v = -0.5 m, virtual). 1/f = 1/v - 1/u = 1/(-0.5) - 0 = -2. Power P = -2 Dioptres. Negative power implies Concave lens.

Q8. Sciencemodel-generated · 2026-11-18
Which of the following compounds contains nitrogen in the +5 oxidation state?
A. NO
B. N2O5
C. NO2
D. N2O
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: In a neutral compound, sum of oxidation states = 0. Oxygen is -2 each. For N2O5, total O = 5 × (-2) = -10. Let oxidation state of N be x. Then 2x + (-10) = 0 → 2x = +10 → x = +5. Full solution: Assign oxidation numbers: O = -2. For each option calculate N's oxidation state. NO: x -2 = 0 → x = +2. N2O5: 2x -10 = 0 → x = +5. NO2: x -4 = 0 → x = +4. N2O: 2x -2 = 0 → x = +1. Only N2O5 gives +5.

Q11. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-18
If x = √3 + √2, find the value of x² + 1/x².
A. 6
B. 8
C. 4
D. 10
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Rationalize 1/x = 1/(√3+√2) = √3-√2. Then x + 1/x = (√3+√2) + (√3-√2) = 2√3. Use identity x² + 1/x² = (x + 1/x)² - 2 = (2√3)² - 2 = 12 - 2 = 10. Full: x = √3+√2. 1/x = √3-√2 (rationalizing). x + 1/x = 2√3. Squaring both sides: x² + 1/x² + 2 = 12. Therefore, x² + 1/x² = 10.

A train 150 metres long crosses an electric pole in 15 seconds. How much time will it take to cross a platform 350 metres long?
A. 40 sec
B. 50 sec
C. 60 sec
D. 70 sec
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Speed = Train Length / Time = 150/15 = 10 m/s. Total distance to cross platform = Train + Platform = 150 + 350 = 500 m. Time = Distance / Speed = 500/10 = 50 s. Full: Speed of train = 150 m / 15 s = 10 m/s. Distance to cover platform = 150 m + 350 m = 500 m. Required time = 500 m / 10 m/s = 50 seconds.
