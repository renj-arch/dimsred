Q4. General Knowledge
Under which Article of the Indian Constitution is the 'Right to Constitutional Remedies' enshrined?
A. Article 14
B. Article 19
C. Article 21
D. Article 32
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar called Article 32 the 'Heart and Soul of the Constitution'. Remember '32' for 'Remedy/Teeth' to the Fundamental Rights. Full Solution: Article 32 provides the right to move the Supreme Court by appropriate proceedings for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights. It empowers the Supreme Court to issue directions, orders, or writs (Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Quo Warranto, Certiorari). Article 226 grants similar power to High Courts. Article 14 is Equality before Law, Article 19 is Freedom of Speech etc., Article 21 is Protection of Life and Personal Liberty.
Q5. General Knowledge
How many Schedules are there in the Constitution of India?
A. 8
B. 12
C. 10
D. 14
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use elimination based on amendment history. The original Constitution had 8 Schedules. Four were added later: 9th (1951), 10th (1985), 11th (1992), 12th (1992). Total = 8 + 4 = 12. Full Solution: The Constitution of India originally contained 8 Schedules. Through various Constitutional Amendment Acts, four more Schedules were added: the 9th Schedule (Land Reforms), 10th Schedule (Anti-Defection Law), 11th Schedule (Powers of Panchayats), and 12th Schedule (Powers of Municipalities). No further Schedules have been added since 1992, making the current total 12.

Q8. Mathematics
If a : b = 3 : 4 and b : c = 5 : 6, then a : c is equal to
A. 3 : 5
B. 4 : 7
C. 9 : 10
D. 5 : 8
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Multiply the two ratios: a/c = (a/b) × (b/c) = (3/4) × (5/6) = 15/24 = 5/8 → a : c = 5 : 8. Full: Make b common. a:b = 3:4 = 15:20 (multiply by 5). b:c = 5:6 = 20:24 (multiply by 4). Hence a:b:c = 15:20:24 → a:c = 15:24 = 5:8.
Q9. Science
A wire of resistance R is stretched uniformly until its length becomes double the original length. Assuming the volume and resistivity remain constant, what is the new resistance of the wire?
A. 2R
B. 4R
C. R/2
D. R/4
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a wire of constant volume and resistivity, Resistance (R) is directly proportional to the square of Length (L). Since Length doubles (2L), Resistance becomes 2² = 4 times the original (4R). Step-by-step: Resistance formula R = ρL/A. Volume V = A × L = constant. New Length L' = 2L. New Area A' = V/L' = (A×L)/(2L) = A/2. New Resistance R' = ρL'/A' = ρ(2L) / (A/2) = 4(ρL/A) = 4R.
Q10. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of (7^2023 + 3^2024)?
A. 0
B. 2
C. 4
D. 6
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits. 7 has cycle 4 (7,9,3,1); 2023 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit 3. 3 has cycle 4 (3,9,7,1); 2024 mod 4 = 0 → unit digit 1. Sum = 3+1 = 4. Full: 7^1=7, 7^2=49→9, 7^3=343→3, 7^4=2401→1 repeats every 4. 2023 = 4×505+3 → unit digit of 7^2023 = 3. 3^1=3, 3^2=9, 3^3=27→7, 3^4=81→1 repeats every 4. 2024 = 4×506 → unit digit of 3^2024 = 1. 3+1=4.
Q11. Science
In a grassland food chain (Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake), the energy available at the producer level (Grass) is 10,000 J. Applying the 10% law of energy transfer, what is the energy available to the secondary consumer (Frog)?
A. 10 J
B. 100 J
C. 1,000 J
D. 10,000 J
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Energy transfers twice (Producer → Primary → Secondary). Apply 10% twice: 10,000 × 0.1 × 0.1 = 100 J. Step-by-step: Energy at Producer = 10,000 J. Energy at Primary Consumer (Grasshopper) = 10% of 10,000 = 1,000 J. Energy at Secondary Consumer (Frog) = 10% of 1,000 = 100 J.

Correct Answer: B

Q13. Mathematics
Find the smallest positive integer n such that the 10‑digit number 123456789n is divisible by 11.
A. 3
B. 5
C. 7
D. 9
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Divisibility by 11 → difference between sum of digits in odd positions and even positions must be 0 or multiple of 11. For 123456789: odd positions (1,3,5,7,9) sum = 1+3+5+7+9 = 25; even positions (2,4,6,8) sum = 2+4+6+8 = 20. Difference = 5. Adding n at position 10 (even) makes even sum = 20+n. New difference = 25 − (20+n) = 5−n. For divisibility, 5−n = 0 ⇒ n = 5. Full: Check n=5 gives difference 0, so 1234567895 is divisible by 11.
Q14. Mathematics
A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above cost price and then allows a discount of 10%. What is his effective profit percent?
A. 26%
B. 20%
C. 30%
D. 34%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Successive percentage change formula: net% = a + b + ab/100, where a = +40% (markup), b = −10% (discount). Net = 40 − 10 + (40×−10)/100 = 30 − 4 = 26%. Full: Let CP = 100. MP = 140. SP after 10% discount = 140 × 0.9 = 126. Profit = 26 → Profit% = 26%.
Q15. General Knowledge
Which vitamin deficiency causes 'Night Blindness' (Nyctalopia)?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin B12
C. Vitamin C
D. Vitamin D
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (Elimination Technique): Vitamin B12 deficiency causes Pernicious Anemia/Neurological issues. Vitamin C deficiency causes Scurvy. Vitamin D deficiency causes Rickets/Osteomalacia. Vitamin A is the only one directly linked to Rhodopsin (visual pigment) synthesis and retinal health. Step-by-step: Vitamin A (Retinol) is a critical component of Rhodopsin, the photopigment in rod cells of the retina responsible for vision in low-light conditions. A deficiency impairs the regeneration of rhodopsin, leading to Nyctalopia (Night Blindness). It is also the leading cause of preventable childhood blindness (Xerophthalmia).
