Key Concepts

What is Sports GK?

Sports General Knowledge is an important section in Agniveer and defence exams. It covers major sports tournaments, trophies and awards, venues, famous sports personalities, sports terminology, and international sporting events. Questions are typically straightforward and test awareness of popular sports like cricket, football, hockey, tennis, badminton, and Olympics.

Key Topics in Sports GK

1. Major Sports Tournaments: Cricket: ICC Cricket World Cup (ODI), T20 World Cup, IPL, Champions Trophy, Ashes (England-Australia). Football: FIFA World Cup, UEFA Champions League, Premier League, La Liga, Indian Super League (ISL). Hockey: Hockey World Cup, FIH Pro League, Sultan Azlan Shah Cup.

2. Trophies and Cups: Cricket: World Cup, Border-Gavaskar Trophy, Duleep Trophy, Ranji Trophy, Irani Cup, Vijay Hazare Trophy. Football: World Cup trophy, European Cup, FA Cup. Hockey: World Cup, Champions Trophy, Asia Cup. Tennis: Grand Slams — Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, US Open. Badminton: Thomas Cup (men), Uber Cup (women), Sudirman Cup (mixed).

3. Sports Awards in India: Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (highest sporting honour), Arjuna Award, Dronacharya Award (coaching), Dhyan Chand Award (lifetime achievement), Rashtriya Khel Protsahan Puruskar (promotion of sports).

4. International Events: Summer Olympics (held every 4 years), Winter Olympics, Asian Games, Commonwealth Games, Para Olympics, Youth Olympics. Latest editions: Tokyo 2020 (held in 2021), Paris 2024, Los Angeles 2028.

5. Sports Terminology: Cricket: Stump, LBW, Googly, Silly point, Powerplay, DRS. Football: Offside, Dribble, Hat-trick, Free kick, Penalty, Yellow/Red card. Hockey: Bully, Penalty corner, Stroke, Dribble. Tennis: Love, Deuce, Advantage, Break point, Grand Slam. Badminton: Smash, Drop, Lob, Net shot, Rally.

6. Stadiums and Venues: Wankhede Stadium (Mumbai), Eden Gardens (Kolkata), M.A. Chidambaram (Chennai), Lords (London), MCG (Melbourne), Salt Lake Stadium (Kolkata, football), SAI centres, JLN Stadium (Delhi).

Solved Examples

Example 1
Which country has won the most FIFA World Cup titles?

a) Germany b) Argentina c) Brazil d) Italy
Solution: c) Brazil has won the FIFA World Cup 5 times (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002), the most by any country.
Example 2
The term 'Love' is associated with which sport?

a) Cricket b) Football c) Tennis d) Hockey
Solution: c) Tennis. 'Love' means zero in tennis scoring. It originates from the French word 'l'oeuf' meaning egg, which resembles zero.
Example 3
Who was the first Indian to win an Olympic individual gold medal?

a) P.V. Sindhu b) Neeraj Chopra c) Saina Nehwal d) Abhinav Bindra
Solution: d) Abhinav Bindra won the gold medal in men's 10m air rifle shooting at the 2008 Beijing Olympics. Neeraj Chopra won gold in javelin at Tokyo 2020.

Shortcut Techniques

  • Associate sports with their major countries — e.g., Cricket: India, Australia, England; Football: Brazil, Argentina, Germany; Hockey: India, Netherlands, Australia.
  • Remember trophies by the sport they belong to — e.g., Ranji Trophy (cricket), Durand Cup (football), Agha Khan Cup (hockey).
  • Group awards by category: Team sports, Individual sports, Olympic events, Asian Games events.
  • Create a year-wise timeline of recent Olympics, Asian Games, and Commonwealth Games winners.
Pro Tip
In Agniveer exams, sports GK questions are basic — focus on the most well-known tournaments, trophies, and personalities. Make sure you know all Indian Olympic medal winners and current world champions in major sports.
