About LDC

LDC (Lower Division Clerk) exam is conducted by various state PSCs for recruitment of clerical staff in government departments. Our comprehensive practice paper covers all subjects from both Paper-I and Paper-II in a single set: General Intelligence, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, General Studies, Ethics, English, Computer Knowledge, Science, Commerce, Economics, Geography, History, Polity, and Constitution.
