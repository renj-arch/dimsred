Key Concepts

Précis Writing — Compression to One-Third

A précis is a concise summary that keeps the argument, tone, and main claims of the original in about one-third of the words. It appears in UPSC Mains Paper A/B, RBI Phase 2, IBPS PO Mains, and state PSC descriptive papers.

  • Definition: Précis = clear, concise summary in your own words, roughly one-third length, retaining essence, tone, and logical structure.
  • Précis is NOT: A mere outline, paraphrase, abstract with opinion, or critical essay. Do NOT add your views, examples, or criticism.
  • Steps:
    1. Read the passage twice — first for gist, then for structure.
    2. Identify the main claim/argument and supporting reasons.
    3. Delete examples, illustrations, repetitions, digressions, adjectives of emphasis.
    4. Note key facts (names, dates, figures, places) to retain.
    5. Draft in your own words — one-third length.
    6. Check: does it stand alone? Is tone preserved? Is word count right?
  • What to keep: Main claim, supporting reasons, conclusion, key data, author's stance/tone.
  • What to drop: Examples, anecdotes, repetitions, rhetorical questions, emotive adjectives, quotations unless crucial, your own opinion.
  • Word count: Target ≈ one-third of original (e.g., 300-word passage → ~100-word précis). UPSC accepts reasonable range (~one-third ± small margin).
  • Language: Third person, indirect/reported speech, past tense often, formal, objective, no contractions, no first person ('I think', 'in my opinion').
  • Structure: One paragraph usually; opening states theme/author and claim; body gives supporting logic; closing states conclusion/implication.
    • Technique: Compression example
    Original (60 words): 'The government has launched several schemes to improve rural employment. MGNREGA guarantees 100 days of wage work. PMKVY trains youth in skills. Together these reduce distress migration.' Précis it.
    Answer: 'Rural employment schemes—MGNREGA's 100-day wage guarantee and PMKVY's skill training—aim to reduce distress migration.' (~18 words, ~one-third, keeps claims, drops 'has launched', 'several').
    Technique: Tone preservation
    Original is critical of a policy. Précis must remain critical, not neutral or praising.
    Answer: Use words like 'faulty', 'questionable', 'fails to' reflecting author's stance; do not soften to 'interesting' or 'notable'.

    Common Traps

    • Adding your own opinion ('I think the policy is good').
    • Including examples/illustrations from the passage.
    • Copying sentences verbatim instead of paraphrasing.
    • Exceeding one-third by too much (or writing too little, losing argument).
    • Changing the tone (e.g., making a critical passage sound neutral).
    • Using first person or direct speech.

    Memory Tricks

    • KISS: Keep It Short and Simple — one-third, own words, same argument.
    • 3R: Read → Retain main claim → Rewrite (own words).
    • Drop list: Examples, repetitions, your opinion, emphatic adjectives.
    • One-third: Original word count ÷ 3 ≈ target précis length.
    Précis exam strategy
    Count original words, divide by 3 for target. Draft, count words, trim/expand to hit target. Read aloud to check flow and tone. Ensure no new opinion crept in. Compare against passage: did you keep the main claim and conclusion?
