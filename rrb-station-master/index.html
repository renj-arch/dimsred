About RRB Station Master

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) recruits Station Masters through the NTPC graduate-level notification, CEN 08/2026, which advertised 1,265 Station Master posts. Selection runs through CBT 1 (100 questions, 120 minutes: 30 Mathematics, 30 General Intelligence and Reasoning, 40 General Awareness), CBT 2 (120 questions, 90 minutes: 35 Mathematics, 35 Reasoning, 50 General Awareness), and a computer typing test at 30 words per minute in English or 25 in Hindi. Station Master is a Level 6 post, so the mathematics is graduate level. Both CBTs carry negative marking of one-third.
