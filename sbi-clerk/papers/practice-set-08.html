Q1. English Language
Identify the error in the following sentence and choose the best revision: "Neither the manager nor the employees was aware of the new compliance regulations that were introduced last week."
A. Replace 'was' with 'were'
B. Replace 'employees' with 'employee'
C. Replace 'new' with 'latest'
D. No change needed
Correct Answer: A
The subject is a compound with 'neither...nor', and the verb must agree with the nearer noun 'employees' (plural). Hence 'was' should be changed to 'were'. Option 1 (index 0) is correct.
Q2. Quantitative Aptitude
Two pipes A and B can fill a tank in 12 hours and 15 hours respectively. A third pipe C can empty the full tank in 20 hours. If all three pipes are opened simultaneously, how long will it take to fill the tank?
A. 10 hrs
B. 8 hrs
C. 12 hrs
D. 15 hrs
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Net rate = 1/12 + 1/15 – 1/20 = (5+4–3)/60 = 6/60 = 1/10 tank per hour → 10 hours. Step‑by‑step: LCM of 12,15,20 is 60. A fills 5 units/hr, B fills 4 units/hr, C empties 3 units/hr. Net fill = 6 units/hr. Tank capacity = 60 units. Time = 60/6 = 10 hours.
Q3. Reasoning Ability
A person starts facing East. He turns 90° clockwise, walks 10 m, turns 180° anticlockwise, walks 5 m, turns 90° clockwise, walks 15 m. Which direction is he facing at the end?
A. North
B. South
C. East
D. West
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Track only the facing direction. Start East → 90° clockwise = South → 180° anticlockwise = North → 90° clockwise = East. Final facing is East. Full steps: Initial facing East. First turn: 90° clockwise → South. Second turn: 180° anticlockwise from South → North. Third turn: 90° clockwise from North → East. Distances walked do not affect final orientation. Therefore he faces East.

Q5. Quantitative Aptitude
The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year, decreases by 10% in the second year, and then increases by 20% in the third year. If the present population is 1,00,000, what will be the population after 3 years?
A. 118800
B. 119800
C. 117800
D. 120000
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Multiply successive factors: 1.10 × 0.90 × 1.20 = 1.188. Final population = 1,00,000 × 1.188 = 1,18,800. Step‑by‑step: After 1st year: 1,00,000 × 1.10 = 1,10,000. After 2nd year: 1,10,000 × 0.90 = 99,000. After 3rd year: 99,000 × 1.20 = 1,18,800.
Q6. Reasoning Ability
Find the next term in the following alphanumeric series: A2, C5, E10, G17, ?
A. I26
B. I25
C. J26
D. I27
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The letters move forward by 2 positions each time (A→C→E→G→I). The numbers follow the pattern n²+1: 1²+1=2, 2²+1=5, 3²+1=10, 4²+1=17, so the 5th term is 5²+1=26. Hence the next term is I26. Full solution: Observing the letter sequence gives I; the numeric differences are 3,5,7 (odd numbers increasing by 2), so the next difference is 9, making 17+9=26, confirming I26.

Q8. English Language
Select the most appropriate conjunction to complete the sentence: "The project was delayed, __________ the team worked overtime to meet the deadline."
A. although
B. because
C. however
D. despite
Correct Answer: C
The second clause contrasts with the first; a conjunctive adverb showing contrast is needed. 'However' fits, while 'although' and 'despite' need a clause/subordinate structure, and 'because' indicates cause. Therefore option 3 (index 2) is correct.
Q9. Quantitative Aptitude
Three partners A, B, C invest in a business in the ratio 3:4:5. After 6 months, A withdraws half of his capital. At the end of the year, the total profit is Rs. 1,44,000. What is B's share of the profit?
A. 48000
B. 51200
C. 54000
D. 57600
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Compute capital‑months for each partner. A = 3×6 + 1.5×6 = 27; B = 4×12 = 48; C = 5×12 = 60. Ratio = 27:48:60 = 9:16:20. B's share = (16/45)×144000 = 51200. Step‑by‑step: A's investment for first 6 months = 3x, next 6 months = 1.5x → total 27x. B invests 4x for 12 months → 48x. C invests 5x for 12 months → 60x. Total parts = 135x = 45 units. Each unit = 144000/45 = 3200. B gets 16 units = 51200.
Q10. Reasoning Ability
In a certain code language, 'TABLE' is written as 'UBCMF'. How will 'CHAIR' be written in that code?
A. DIBJT
B. DIBJS
C. DIBJR
D. DIBJU
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Observe each letter of TABLE shifts forward by 1 (T→U, A→B, B→C, L→M, E→F). Apply same +1 shift to CHAIR: C→D, H→I, A→B, I→J, R→S → DIBJS. Full steps: Write alphabet positions, add 1 to each, wrap after Z. For TABLE: 20+1=21(U), 1+1=2(B), 2+1=3(C), 12+1=13(M), 5+1=6(F). For CHAIR: 3+1=4(D), 8+1=9(I), 1+1=2(B), 9+1=10(J), 18+1=19(S). Hence code is DIBJS.
Q11. Reasoning Ability
Pointing to a photograph, a man says, 'She is the only daughter of my father's only son.' How is the woman related to the man?
A. Mother
B. Sister
C. Daughter
D. Wife
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: 'My father's only son' refers to the man himself (assuming he is the only son). The 'only daughter of him' is therefore his daughter. Full steps: Identify the man's father's only son → the man. Then the only daughter of that person → his daughter. Hence the woman is the man's daughter.
Q12. Quantitative Aptitude
A sum of money amounts to Rs. 7,200 in 2 years and Rs. 8,640 in 4 years at simple interest. What is the principal amount?
A. 5400
B. 5600
C. 6000
D. 5760
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Interest for 2 years = 8640–7200 = 1440 → annual interest = 720. Principal = Amount after 2 years – 2×720 = 7200–1440 = 5760. Step‑by‑step: Simple interest is same every year. Interest earned in years 3‑4 = 8640–7200 = 1440 for 2 years, so yearly interest = 720. Principal = 7200 – (2×720) = 5760.
Q13. Quantitative Aptitude
A boat travels 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 hours. It can also travel 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream in 13 hours. Find the speed of the boat in still water.
A. 6 km/h
B. 7 km/h
C. 8 km/h
D. 9 km/h
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Let u = 1/(b–s), v = 1/(b+s). Solve 30u+44v=10 and 40u+55v=13 → v=1/11, u=1/5 → b–s=5, b+s=11 → b=8 km/h. Step‑by‑step: Upstream speed = b–s, downstream = b+s. From given data: 30/(b–s)+44/(b+s)=10 and 40/(b–s)+55/(b+s)=13. Solving yields 1/(b–s)=1/5 and 1/(b+s)=1/11. Hence b–s=5, b+s=11. Adding gives 2b=16 → b=8 km/h.

Q15. Reasoning Ability
Statements: All pens are pencils. Some pencils are erasers. No eraser is a sharpener. Conclusions: I. Some pens are erasers. II. No pencil is a sharpener. Which of the following is correct?
A. Only conclusion I follows
B. Only conclusion II follows
C. Both conclusions follow
D. Neither conclusion follows
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Draw mental Venn – pens inside pencils; pencils and erasers overlap; erasers and sharpeners are separate. Conclusion I is not forced because the pencil‑eraser overlap may not include pens. Conclusion II is not forced because pencils that are not erasers could be sharpeners. Thus neither conclusion follows. Full steps: From 'All pens are pencils' we know pen ⊆ pencil. 'Some pencils are erasers' gives an overlap between pencil and eraser, but pens could be outside that overlap. 'No eraser is sharpener' only separates eraser and sharpener; pencils outside the eraser set may still be sharpeners. Hence both conclusions are invalid.
