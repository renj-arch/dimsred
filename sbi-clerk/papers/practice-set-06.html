Q4. Quantitative Aptitude
A car travels from city A to city B at an average speed of 60 km/h and returns at an average speed of 40 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire trip?
A. 45
B. 48
C. 50
D. 55
Correct Answer: B
To find the average speed for the entire trip, we need to find the total distance and the total time. Let's assume the distance between city A and city B is x km. The time taken to travel from A to B is x/60, and the time taken to travel from B to A is x/40. The total time is x/60 + x/40 = (2x + 3x)/120 = 5x/120 = x/24. The average speed is the total distance divided by the total time, which is 2x / (x/24) = 48 km/h.

Q6. Quantitative Aptitude
If x + 1/x = 3, find the value of x^2 + 1/x^2.
A. 7
B. 9
C. 11
D. 5
Correct Answer: A
(x + 1/x)^2 = x^2 + 1/x^2 + 2 => 9 = x^2 + 1/x^2 + 2 => x^2 + 1/x^2 = 7.

Q10. Reasoning
If today is Monday, what was the day 473 days ago?
A. Wednesday
B. Thursday
C. Tuesday
D. Monday
Correct Answer: B
1. Divide the total days (473) by 7 to find full weeks. 2. Calculation: 473 ÷ 7 = 67 weeks with a remainder of 4 days. 3. Since we are going backwards, subtract the remainder from 'Monday'. 4. Monday - 1 day = Sunday; - 2 days = Saturday; - 3 days = Friday; - 4 days = Thursday. 5. The year's leap status does not affect day calculation as it depends purely on the modulo arithmetic of 7.
Q11. Quantitative Aptitude
The ratio of the ages of two friends is 6:5. If the sum of their ages is 44, what is the age of the elder friend?
A. 24
B. 22
C. 26
D. 28
Correct Answer: A
Let the ages of the two friends be 6x and 5x. Given that the sum of their ages is 44, we can write the equation 6x + 5x = 44. Solving for x, we get 11x = 44, x = 4. The age of the elder friend is 6x = 6*4 = 24.

Q15. Reasoning
If it is given that 'p' is greater than 'q' and 'q' is greater than 'r', then which of the following is true?
A. p < q
B. p > r
C. q < r
D. r > p
Correct Answer: B
Given 'p' is greater than 'q' and 'q' is greater than 'r', we can deduce that 'p' is greater than 'r'. This is a basic transitive property where if p > q and q > r, then p > r.
