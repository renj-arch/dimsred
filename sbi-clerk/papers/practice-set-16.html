A. 30
B. 24
C. 28
D. 32
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (LCM method): Total work = LCM(12,20)=60 units. A+B = 60/12 =5 units/day. A = 60/20 =3 units/day. Hence B = 2 units/day → time = 60/2 =30 days. Stepwise: 1/12 - 1/20 = (5-3)/60 = 2/60 = 1/30 → 30 days.

A. 960
B. 1008
C. 1152
D. 1080
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Length : Breadth = 120 : 100 = 6 : 5. Let sides be 6x and 5x. Perimeter = 2(11x) = 22x = 132 ⇒ x = 6. Area = 6x × 5x = 30x² = 30 × 36 = 1080. Unit digit of area must be 0 (30 × 36), eliminating 960, 1008, 1152. Full: Let breadth = b. Length = 1.2b. 2(b + 1.2b) = 132 ⇒ 4.4b = 132 ⇒ b = 30. Length = 36. Area = 30 × 36 = 1080 cm².
