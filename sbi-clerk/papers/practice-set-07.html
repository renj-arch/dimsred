Q7. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-15
A train 200 metres long crosses a platform in 40 seconds. If the speed of the train is 54 km/h, what is the length of the platform?
A. 300 m
B. 400 m
C. 500 m
D. 600 m
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Convert speed to m/s (54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s). Total distance = speed × time = 15 × 40 = 600 m. Platform length = total distance – train length = 600 – 200 = 400 m. Full steps: Speed = 54 km/h = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s. In 40 seconds the train covers 15 × 40 = 600 m. This distance equals the length of the train plus the length of the platform. Hence platform length = 600 – 200 = 400 m.

Q9. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-15
What is the unit digit of (7^95 × 3^58)?
A. 1
B. 3
C. 7
D. 9
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Unit digit of powers follows cycles of 4. 7^95 → 95 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit = 3 (7^3=343). 3^58 → 58 mod 4 = 2 → unit digit = 9 (3^2=9). Product unit digit = 3×9 = 27 → 7. Full steps: Cyclicity of 7 is 7,9,3,1; 95÷4 remainder 3 gives 3. Cyclicity of 3 is 3,9,7,1; 58÷4 remainder 2 gives 9. Multiplying the unit digits 3 and 9 gives 27, so final unit digit is 7.

Q13. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-15
After allowing two successive discounts of 20% and 10% on the marked price, an article is sold for Rs. 1296. What is the marked price of the article?
A. Rs. 1800
B. Rs. 2000
C. Rs. 2200
D. Rs. 2400
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Two successive discounts of 20% and 10% are equivalent to a single discount of 28% (1 – 0.8×0.9 = 0.28). Hence selling price = 72% of marked price → Marked price = 1296 / 0.72 = 1800. Full steps: Let marked price be x. After 20% discount price = 0.8x. After further 10% discount price = 0.9 × 0.8x = 0.72x. Given 0.72x = 1296, so x = 1296/0.72 = 1800.

Q15. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-15
A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. B and C together can complete it in 15 days. C and A together can complete it in 20 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?
A. 20 days
B. 24 days
C. 30 days
D. 40 days
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Add the three pairwise efficiencies: (1/12+1/15+1/20) = (5+4+3)/60 = 12/60 = 1/5 per day for 2(A+B+C). So A+B+C = 1/10 per day. A's efficiency = (A+B+C) - (B+C) = 1/10 - 1/15 = 1/30 per day. Hence A alone takes 30 days. Full: Let total work = 60 units. A+B=5, B+C=4, C+A=3 units/day. Sum=12 => A+B+C=6. A=6-4=2 units/day. Time=60/2=30 days.
