Q3. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning

A. All beds are tables
B. Some tables are beds
C. All chairs are beds

Correct Answer: B
Given that all tables are chairs and some chairs are beds, we can conclude that some tables are beds, since some chairs (which include tables) are beds.

Q7. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
If "PENCIL" is coded as "RGP EKN", how is "ERASER" coded?
A. GTCUGT
B. GTBUGT
C. GTCVGT
D. GTCUHT
Correct Answer: A
PENCIL: each letter +2: P+2=R, E+2=G, N+2=P, C+2=E, I+2=K, L+2=N => RGP EKN. Similarly ERASER: E+2=G, R+2=T, A+2=C, S+2=U, E+2=G, R+2=T => GTCUGT.
Q8. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
The average of 5 numbers is 40. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 38. What is the excluded number?
A. 42
B. 46
C. 48
D. 44
Correct Answer: C
Sum of 5 numbers = 5*40 = 200. Sum of 4 numbers = 4*38 = 152. Excluded number = 200 - 152 = 48.

Q10. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
Find the missing number: 3, 12, 27, 48, ?

B. 75
C. 81
D. 84
Correct Answer: B
3 = 3*1^2, 12 = 3*2^2, 27 = 3*3^2, 48 = 3*4^2, so next = 3*5^2 = 3*25 = 75.
Q11. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
A can do a piece of work in 12 days, B in 15 days. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves. How many more days will B take to finish the remaining work?
A. 4 days
B. 5 days
C. 6 days
D. 7 days
Correct Answer: C
LCM of 12,15 = 60. A does 5/day, B does 4/day. Together 9/day. In 4 days: 36 done. Remaining: 24. B needs 24/4 = 6 days.

Q13. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
If x + 1/x = 3, find the value of x^2 + 1/x^2.
A. 7
B. 9
C. 11
D. 5
Correct Answer: A
(x + 1/x)^2 = x^2 + 1/x^2 + 2 => 9 = x^2 + 1/x^2 + 2 => x^2 + 1/x^2 = 7.
Q14. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
A sum of Rs. 8000 becomes Rs. 9261 in 3 years at compound interest. Find the rate of interest per annum.
A. 4%
B. 5%
C. 6%
D. 7%
Correct Answer: B
A = P(1+R/100)^T => 9261 = 8000(1+R/100)^3 => (1+R/100)^3 = 9261/8000 = 1.157625 => 1+R/100 = 1.05 => R = 5%.
Q15. [+1, -0.25] English
Identify the voice: "The letter was written by him."
A. Active Voice
B. Passive Voice
C. Direct Speech
D. Indirect Speech
Correct Answer: B
The sentence has the structure: object + was + past participle + by + subject. This is passive voice.
