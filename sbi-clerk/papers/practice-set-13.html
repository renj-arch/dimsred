Q9. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-24
A boat takes 8 hours to cover a certain distance upstream and 6 hours to cover the same distance downstream. If the speed of the stream is 4 km/h, what is the speed of the boat in still water?
A. 28
B. 24
C. 32
D. 36
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Time ratio Up:Down = 8:6 = 4:3 → Speed ratio Down:Up = 4:3 (inverse). Let Downstream = 4x, Upstream = 3x. Stream speed = (4x-3x)/2 = x/2 = 4 → x=8. Boat speed = (4x+3x)/2 = 3.5x = 28 km/h. Step-by-step: Distance = Speed × Time. Up×8 = Down×6 → Up/Down = 3/4. Down = Up + 2×Stream. Solving gives Up=24, Down=32. Boat = (24+32)/2 = 28.

Q14. Reasoning Abilitymodel-generated · 2026-11-20
If 'A @ B' means 'A is the father of B', 'A # B' means 'A is the sister of B', 'A $ B' means 'A is the mother of B', and 'A % B' means 'A is the brother of B', then how is P related to T in the expression: P @ Q # R $ S % T?
A. Maternal Grandfather
B. Paternal Grandfather
C. Maternal Uncle
D. Paternal Uncle
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Trace generation gaps backwards from T. S % T ⇒ S is brother of T (Same gen). R $ S ⇒ R is mother of S (Gen +1). Q # R ⇒ Q is sister of R (Same gen). P @ Q ⇒ P is father of Q (Gen +1). Total Gen gap = +2 (Grandfather). Line: P → Q/R (Mother side) ⇒ Maternal Grandfather. Full Solution: Decode step-by-step: 1. S % T means S is the brother of T. 2. R $ S means R is the mother of S, so R is the mother of T. 3. Q # R means Q is the sister of R, so Q is the maternal aunt of T. 4. P @ Q means P is the father of Q, so P is the father of T's maternal aunt. Therefore, P is the Maternal Grandfather of T.
