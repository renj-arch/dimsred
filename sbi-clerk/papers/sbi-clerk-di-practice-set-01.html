What was the percentage increase in production from 2022 to 2023?
A. 40%
B. 4.29%
C. 45.83%
D. 28.57%
Correct Answer: A
Increase = 70 − 50 = 20; % = 20/50 × 100 = 40%.
Q2.
What was the average production over the five years?
A. 65 MT
B. 64.6 MT
C. 80.75 MT
D. 82 MT
Correct Answer: B
Average = (48 + 50 + 70 + 73 + 82) / 5 = 64.6 MT.
