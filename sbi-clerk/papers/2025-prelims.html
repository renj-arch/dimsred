Q12. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
A shopkeeper buys 100 kg of rice at Rs. 20 per kg and 50 kg of wheat at Rs. 30 per kg. What is the average cost price per kg of the mixture?
A. 22
B. 24
C. 26
D. 28
Correct Answer: A
The total cost of rice is 100 * 20 = 2000, and the total cost of wheat is 50 * 30 = 1500. The total cost is 2000 + 1500 = 3500, and the total weight is 100 + 50 = 150 kg. The average cost price per kg is 3500 / 150 = 23.33, which is approximately 22 when rounded to the nearest whole number, but since the options do not have the exact average, the closest is 22.

A. A B C D E
B. A B E C D
C. A B E D C
D. A D E B C
Correct Answer: D
From the given conditions, we know A is to the left of B, and C is to the right of B. Also, D is to the left of E, and E is to the left of C. Combining these, the order that satisfies all conditions is A D E B C.

Q15. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
The ratio of the ages of two friends is 6:5. If the sum of their ages is 44, what is the age of the elder friend?
A. 24
B. 22
C. 26
D. 28
Correct Answer: A
Let the ages of the two friends be 6x and 5x. Given that the sum of their ages is 44, we can write the equation 6x + 5x = 44. Solving for x, we get 11x = 44, x = 4. The age of the elder friend is 6x = 6*4 = 24.
