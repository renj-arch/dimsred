Starter (iii) 'With the interest rates having been hiked' functions as an absolute phrase denoting the prevailing circumstance/reason, effectively combining the sentences. Thus, only (i) and (iii) are valid.

Q13. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-19
Two trains of lengths 140 m and 210 m are running on parallel tracks in opposite directions. The speed of the shorter train is 54 km/h. If they cross each other completely in 10 seconds, what is the speed of the longer train?
A. 60 km/h
B. 72 km/h
C. 66 km/h
D. 54 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Relative Speed (km/h) = (Sum of lengths in metres × 18) / (Time in seconds × 5). Here, Relative Speed = (350 × 18) / (10 × 5) = 126 km/h. Speed of longer train = 126 − 54 = 72 km/h. Step-by-step: Total distance = 140 + 210 = 350 m. Time = 10 s. Relative speed = 350/10 = 35 m/s. Convert to km/h: 35 × 18/5 = 126 km/h. Since directions are opposite, relative speed = Sum of speeds. Speed of longer train = 126 − 54 = 72 km/h.

A contractor employs 12 men and 18 women to complete a work in 20 days. The efficiency of a man is 1.5 times that of a woman. After 8 days, 4 men leave and 6 women join. In how many more days will the remaining work be finished?
A. 10 days
B. 12 days
C. 14 days
D. 16 days
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The total efficiency before and after the change remains 36 units/day, so remaining days = total days – days elapsed = 20 – 8 = 12 days. Full: Let woman’s efficiency = 1 unit/day, man’s = 1.5 units/day. Initial daily work = 12×1.5 + 18×1 = 36 units. Total work = 36×20 = 720 units. Work done in 8 days = 36×8 = 288 units. Remaining = 432 units. New daily work = 8×1.5 + 24×1 = 36 units. Days needed = 432/36 = 12 days.
