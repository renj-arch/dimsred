B. 2

Correct Answer: B

Full Method: 7^82 = (7^4)^20 × 7^2 → unit digit 1^20 × 49 = 9. 8^93 = (8^4)^23 × 8^1 → unit digit 6^23 × 8 = 6 × 8 = 48 → 8. 6^47 → unit digit 6. Product unit digit = 9 × 8 × 6 = 432 → 2.

A. Only I follows
B. Only II follows
C. Only III follows
D. None follows
Correct Answer: D
Using the rule that 'All + Some' yields no definite conclusion, we see that 'All pens are pencils' and 'Some pencils are erasers' does not guarantee some pens are erasers. 'No eraser is sharpener' combined with 'Some pencils are erasers' gives 'Some pencils are not sharpeners', not 'No pencil is sharpener'. Since no statement links sharpeners to pencils directly, conclusion III also fails. Hence none follows.

Q12. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-23
Two trains of lengths 160 m and 240 m are running on parallel tracks. When running in opposite directions, they cross each other in 10 seconds. When running in the same direction, the faster train crosses the slower train in 40 seconds. Find the speed of the slower train (in m/s).
A. 10
B. 12
C. 15
D. 18
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Total length = 400 m. Relative speed (opposite) = 400/10 = 40 m/s. Relative speed (same) = 400/40 = 10 m/s. Speed of slower train = (Relative speed opposite - Relative speed same) / 2 = (40 - 10) / 2 = 15 m/s. Full: Let speeds be S1 (faster) and S2 (slower). Opposite: S1 + S2 = 400/10 = 40. Same: S1 - S2 = 400/40 = 10. Adding equations: 2S1 = 50 → S1 = 25 m/s. Substituting: S2 = 40 - 25 = 15 m/s.

B. 1450
C. 1550
D. 1500
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use the direct formula for annual installment under SI: Installment = (100 × Principal) / [100n + r×n(n-1)/2]. Here P=6450, n=4, r=5. Denominator = 400 + 5×6 = 430. Installment = 645000 / 430 = 1500. Step-by-step: Let installment be X. Amount paid = X(1+0.15) + X(1+0.10) + X(1+0.05) + X = X(4.30). Equate to 6450: 4.3X = 6450 → X = 1500.
