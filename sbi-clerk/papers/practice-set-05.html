Q5. Quantitative Aptitude
The ratio of the ages of two friends is 6:5. If the sum of their ages is 44, what is the age of the elder friend?
A. 24
B. 22
C. 26
D. 28
Correct Answer: A
Let the ages of the two friends be 6x and 5x. Given that the sum of their ages is 44, we can write the equation 6x + 5x = 44. Solving for x, we get 11x = 44, x = 4. The age of the elder friend is 6x = 6*4 = 24.
Q6. Quantitative Aptitude
A car travels from city A to city B at an average speed of 60 km/h and returns at an average speed of 40 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire trip?
A. 45
B. 48
C. 50
D. 55
Correct Answer: B
To find the average speed for the entire trip, we need to find the total distance and the total time. Let's assume the distance between city A and city B is x km. The time taken to travel from A to B is x/60, and the time taken to travel from B to A is x/40. The total time is x/60 + x/40 = (2x + 3x)/120 = 5x/120 = x/24. The average speed is the total distance divided by the total time, which is 2x / (x/24) = 48 km/h.

What is the value of x in the equation x/4 + 3 = 7?

B. 16
C. 20
D. 24

Q15. Reasoning
If 'a' is coded as 'd', 'b' is coded as 'e', and so on, what is the code for 'y'?
A. a
B. b
C. c
D. b
Correct Answer: D
Given the pattern of coding where 'a' is coded as 'd', 'b' is coded as 'e', and so on, it seems that each letter is shifted 3 positions forward in the alphabet. Applying this pattern to 'y', we get 'y' coded as 'b' since 'y' is the 25th letter and 'b' is the 2nd letter in the alphabet, with 'y' shifted 3 positions back resulting in 'b'.
