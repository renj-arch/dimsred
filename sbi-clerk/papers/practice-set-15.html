Q1. English Language
Rearrange the following four sentences (A), (B), (C), and (D) in the correct sequence to form a coherent paragraph. A. However, the rapid pace of urbanization has strained these resources to a breaking point. B. Indian cities have historically been engines of economic growth and cultural exchange. C. Consequently, urban local bodies are now mandated to adopt sustainable waste management practices. D. This pressure has led to severe air pollution, water scarcity, and unmanageable solid waste. Choose the correct order from the options below.
A. BACD
B. BDAC
C. BADC
D. DABC
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Spot the opening sentence (independent, general statement) → B. 'However' in A contrasts B, so A follows B. 'This pressure' in D refers to 'strain' in A, so D follows A. 'Consequently' in C concludes the result, so C is last. Order: BADC. Step-by-step: Sentence B introduces the topic 'Indian cities'. Sentence A begins with 'However', signaling a contrast to the positive history in B, making B-A a mandatory pair. Sentence D starts with 'This pressure', a demonstrative pronoun referencing the 'strain' mentioned in A, fixing A-D as the next link. Sentence C starts with 'Consequently', indicating the final outcome of the pressure described in D. Thus, the logical flow is B → A → D → C.
Q2. Quantitative Aptitude

C. 240

Correct Answer: B

Q4. Quantitative Aptitude
A boat covers 36 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 3 hours. What is the speed of the stream (in km/h)?
A. 2
B. 4
C. 3
D. 5
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Downstream speed = 36/2 = 18 km/h. Upstream speed = 36/3 = 12 km/h. Speed of stream = (Downstream - Upstream)/2 = (18 - 12)/2 = 3 km/h. Full: Let speed of boat in still water be b and stream be s. b + s = 18 and b - s = 12. Adding equations: 2b = 30 ⇒ b = 15. Substituting: s = 18 - 15 = 3 km/h.

Q6. English Language

A. had the meeting started when
B. the meeting had started than
C. did the meeting start when
D. No improvement
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Trigger word 'Scarcely' (or 'Hardly') at the beginning of a sentence mandates Subject-Auxiliary Inversion (Aux + Subject + Main Verb) and is followed by 'when', not 'than'. Step-by-step: The original sentence lacks inversion ('the meeting had started'). Option A provides correct inversion ('had the meeting started') and correct conjunction ('when'). Option B uses 'than' (incorrect with Scarcely). Option C uses 'did... start' (wrong auxiliary for Past Perfect context).

Q8. Reasoning Ability
In a row of students, Ankit is 12th from the left end and 18th from the right end. If 5 new students join the row and stand at the right end, what will be Ankit's new position from the right end?
A. 22
B. 23
C. 24
D. 25
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Position from right increases by the number of people added at the right end. Old position from right = 18. 5 added at right -> New position = 18 + 5 = 23. Full: Total students initially = 12 + 18 - 1 = 29. After 5 join at right, total = 34. Ankit's position from left remains 12. New position from right = 34 - 12 + 1 = 23.
Q9. Quantitative Aptitude
The ratio of the present ages of A and B is 3 : 2. After 10 years, the ratio of their ages will become 7 : 5. What was the ratio of their ages 5 years ago?
A. 10 : 7
B. 9 : 5
C. 11 : 7
D. 8 : 5
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use reverse option elimination. Since the time gap between '5 years ago' and '10 years hence' is 15 years, add 15 years to the ages assumed from each option (in ratio form) and check which yields the future ratio 7:5. For option C (11:7), let ages 5 years ago be 11k and 7k. Present ages = 11k+5, 7k+5. Given present ratio 3:2 ⇒ 2(11k+5)=3(7k+5) ⇒ k=5. Ages 10 years hence = 60+10, 40+10 = 70:50 = 7:5 (matches). Full Solution: Let present ages be 3x and 2x. (3x+10)/(2x+10) = 7/5 ⇒ 15x+50 = 14x+70 ⇒ x=20. Present ages = 60, 40. Ages 5 years ago = 55, 35 = 11:7.
Q10. Reasoning Ability
In a family of seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F, and G, there are two married couples. B is the son of C. A is the mother of B. D is the brother of E. F is the mother-in-law of A. G is the grandfather of D. E is the sister of D. How is E related to G?
A. Granddaughter
B. Grandson
C. Daughter
D. Niece
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: 'Mother-in-law of A' (F) implies F is mother of A's husband. Since B is son of C and A is mother of B, C is A's husband. So F is mother of C. 'Grandfather of D' (G) implies G is father of D's parent. As F is mother of C, G is husband of F and father of C. Thus C is child of G. B, D, E are children of A and C (siblings). Since E is sister of D, E is female. Hence E is granddaughter of G. Full Solution: Couple 1: F (Mother-in-law of A) & G. So F is mother of C (A's husband). Couple 2: A & C. Children of A & C: B (Son), D, E (Siblings, E is sister). G is father of C. Therefore E is granddaughter of G.
Q11. Reasoning Ability
Rohan walks 15 metres West, turns left and walks 20 metres, turns left again and walks 15 metres, then turns right and walks 10 metres. How far is he from the starting point?
A. 20 metres
B. 25 metres
C. 30 metres
D. 35 metres
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Track net horizontal and vertical displacement. Horizontal: 15m West (-15) then 15m East (+15) = 0. Vertical: 20m South (-20) then 10m South (-10) = 30m South. Distance = 30m. Full: Start (0,0). West 15 -> (-15,0). Left (South) 20 -> (-15,-20). Left (East) 15 -> (0,-20). Right (South) 10 -> (0,-30). Distance = √(0² + 30²) = 30 metres.

Q13. English Language
Identify the segment in the sentence that contains a grammatical error. If the sentence is error-free, select the segment labelled 'No error' (though all segments below are parts of the sentence). The committee is comprised of experts from various fields, who are expected to deliberate on the proposal and submitting their report by next month.
A. The committee is comprised of
B. experts from various fields, who are
C. expected to deliberate on the proposal and
D. submitting their report by next month
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Scan for parallelism after 'to' (infinitives). The structure 'expected to [verb1] and [verb2]' requires both verbs in base form. 'Deliberate' (base) pairs with 'submit' (base), not 'submitting' (gerund). Step-by-step: 1) Segment A: 'Comprised of' is often debated but accepted in modern usage; 'comprises' is stricter but not the primary error here. 2) Segment B: Correct relative clause usage. 3) Segment C: 'Expected to deliberate' is correct. 4) Segment D: 'Expected to... and submitting' violates parallelism. The bare infinitive 'submit' is required after 'to'. Error lies in Segment D.
Q14. Quantitative Aptitude
Find the missing term in the following number series: 12, 13, 17, 26, 42, ?
A. 62
B. 67
C. 72
D. 77
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Check differences between consecutive terms: 1, 4, 9, 16 (perfect squares 1², 2², 3², 4²). Next difference is 5² = 25. Missing term = 42 + 25 = 67. Step-by-step: 13-12=1; 17-13=4; 26-17=9; 42-26=16; Pattern n²; Next diff=25; 42+25=67.
