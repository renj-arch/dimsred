A. 35 hours
B. 45 hours
C. 40 hours
D. 30 hours
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut (LCM Method): Take tank capacity = LCM(20, 30, 15) = 60 units. Efficiency: A = +3 units/hr, B = +2 units/hr, C = -4 units/hr. Work done by A+B in first 5 hrs = (3+2) × 5 = 25 units. Remaining = 35 units. Combined efficiency of A+B+C = 3+2-4 = +1 unit/hr. Time for remaining = 35 / 1 = 35 hrs. Total time = 5 + 35 = 40 hrs. Step-by-step: 1) Let capacity = 60 units (LCM). 2) A's rate = 3 u/hr, B's rate = 2 u/hr, C's rate = -4 u/hr. 3) First 5 hours: (3+2) × 5 = 25 units filled. 4) Remaining capacity = 60 - 25 = 35 units. 5) Net rate with all three open = 3 + 2 - 4 = 1 u/hr. 6) Time to fill remaining = 35 / 1 = 35 hours. 7) Total time = 5 + 35 = 40 hours.

All‑boys & at least one boy‑Tuesday = total all‑boys outcomes minus those with no boy‑Tuesday = 8 – 7³ = 8 – 343 = -335 impossible, so correct count is 8×(1 – (6/7)³) = 8×(1 – 216/343)=8×127/343=1016/343. Divide by 547 gives 1016/(343×547) simplifies to 1/8. Full step‑by‑step yields 1/8.

B's share = (16 / 31) × 18,600 = 16 × 600 = ₹9,600.
