Key Concepts

What is Sentence Correction & Para Jumbles?

Sentence Correction involves identifying and fixing grammatical errors in sentences — spot the error, fill in the blank, and improve the sentence questions. Para Jumbles require rearranging jumbled sentences (usually 5-6) into a coherent paragraph. Both are high-weightage topics in SSC CGL Tier 2 English.

Key Concepts

1. Error Detection (Spot the Error): Read the entire sentence before identifying the error. Common error types: Subject-Verb disagreement, wrong tense sequence, incorrect preposition, misplaced modifier, parallelism error, pronoun-antecedent disagreement, incorrect article usage, and wrong conditional construction.

2. Sentence Improvement/Phrase Replacement: Identify the part that needs replacement and choose the option that corrects it without changing the intended meaning. Check for conciseness, grammatical correctness, and idiomatic usage.

3. Para Jumbles (Sentence Rearrangement): Identify the first sentence (usually an introduction — introduces a subject, person, or concept), look for linking words (however, therefore, furthermore, consequently, as a result), pronoun references (he, she, it, they — must refer back to a noun in a previous sentence), and chronological/ logical sequence.

Solved Examples

Example 1 (Spot the Error)
Find the error part in the sentence:

Neither the manager nor his colleagues (A)/ was aware of the (B)/ decision taken by the (C)/ board of directors. (D)

a) A b) B c) C d) No error
Solution: b) B. With 'Neither...nor', the verb must agree with the nearest subject. Here 'colleagues' is plural, so the verb should be 'were' (not 'was'). Hence, error in part B.
Example 2 (Para Jumble)
Rearrange the following sentences (A-E) into a meaningful paragraph:

A. Eventually, it became a symbol of national pride.
B. The Qutub Minar was built in the early 13th century.
C. It is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
D. Qutb-ud-din Aibak began its construction.
E. Iltutmish completed the tower later.

a) B-D-E-A-C b) B-A-E-D-C c) D-B-A-E-C d) B-C-D-E-A
Solution: a) B-D-E-A-C. B introduces Qutub Minar. D says who started construction. E says who completed it. A gives the outcome. C gives the current status.

Shortcut Techniques

  • For error detection, the first scan should always check Subject-Verb agreement (most common SSC error). Then check tense sequence (if the main verb is past, the subordinate verb should generally be past).
  • For para jumbles, identify the independent sentence that doesn't refer to any previous pronoun or 'this/that/these/those'. That is typically S1.
  • Mandatory pairs: Look for mandatory linkages (e.g., 'On one hand... On the other hand...', 'Firstly... Secondly...', cause-effect pairs). These must be adjacent (or at least ordered correctly).
Pro Tip: In Para Jumbles, look for the sentence with the most general introduction — it is almost always the first sentence. The last sentence usually has a concluding remark, a result, or a present-tense observation. Practice 10 para jumbles daily for speed.

Advanced Grammar

Conditional sentences, subject-verb agreement rules, and advanced vocabulary building techniques.

Key S-V Agreement Rules

  • Each/every/either/neither take singular verbs
  • With either-or/neither-nor, verb agrees with the nearer subject
  • Collective nouns can be singular or plural based on context
  • Mathematics, news, physics are singular despite ending in s
Pro Tip
For RC, read questions FIRST then scan passage. For error detection, read sentence from right to left.
