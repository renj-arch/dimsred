Quantum Field Theory — Fields, Canonical Quantization, Feynman Diagrams & Renormalization

Deep dive into Quantum Field Theory — the framework that merges quantum mechanics with special relativity: classical fields and the Lagrangian formalism (the Klein-Gordon equation for scalar fields, the Dirac equation for spinors, the Proca equation for vector fields), canonical quantization (promoting fields to operators, commutation and anticommutation relations, creation and annihilation operators, the Fock space, normal ordering, the Hamiltonian), the Feynman path integral approach, Wick's theorem and Feynman diagrams (propagators, vertices, the expansion of the S-matrix), scattering amplitudes and cross sections, the S-matrix and the LSZ reduction formula, Noether's theorem and symmetries (energy-momentum conservation, charge conservation), quantum electrodynamics (QED) (the gauge-invariant Lagrangian, running coupling, the anomalous magnetic moment of the electron), non-Abelian gauge theories (Yang-Mills theory, Faddeev-Popov ghosts, asymptotic freedom), spontaneous symmetry breaking and the Higgs mechanism, and renormalization (regularization schemes like dimensional regularization, counterterms, the Callan-Symanzik equation, the renormalization group).
