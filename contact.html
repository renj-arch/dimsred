Frequently Asked Questions

Is vlymbooq really free?

Yes. Every feature on this platform — practice questions, mock tests, performance analytics, streak tracking, and study guides — is completely free. There are no paid plans, subscriptions, or hidden charges.

Which exams do you cover?

We cover 14 exams: SSC CGL, RBI Grade B, JEE Main & Advanced, NEET UG, GATE, Agniveer, UPSC Civil Services, IBPS PO, SBI Clerk, SSC GD, CTET, CDS, NDA, and CLAT. We continuously add more exams based on demand.

How often is content updated?

Current affairs content is updated daily from official government sources. Practice questions and mock tests are updated weekly. Study guides and exam information are updated monthly to reflect the latest exam patterns.

Can I use vlymbooq on mobile?

Yes. The platform is designed to work on all devices including smartphones and tablets. You can practice on the go without installing any app — just open the website in your browser.
