Q1. [1 or 2 marks] General Aptitude
⚠ Negative Marking: -1/3 mark for 1-mark MCQ, -2/3 for 2-mark MCQ (no negative for NAT)
A person walks at 5 km/h and runs at 8 km/h. If total time for 13 km is 2 hours, how much did he walk?
A. 5 km
B. 5 km
C. 8 km
D. 3 km
Correct Answer: B
Let walk time = t hr, run time = 2-t. 5t + 8(2-t) = 13 ? t = 1 hr. Walk distance = 5 km.
Q2. [1 or 2 marks] General Aptitude
The number of diagonals in a pentagon is:
A. 3
B. 4
C. 5
D. 6
Correct Answer: C
n(n-3)/2 = 5(2)/2 = 5 diagonals.
Q3. [1 or 2 marks] General Aptitude
Select the synonym of "Ephemeral":
A. Permanent
B. Long-lasting
C. Transient
D. Eternal
Correct Answer: C
Ephemeral = short-lived/transient. Opposite = permanent.
Q4. [1 or 2 marks] General Aptitude
If a:b = 2:3 and b:c = 4:5, then a:c is:
A. 2:5
B. 8:15
C. 8:15
D. 3:5
Correct Answer: C
a/b = 2/3, b/c = 4/5. a/c = (2/3)(4/5) = 8/15 ? 8:15.
Q5. [1 or 2 marks] General Aptitude
A die is rolled once. Probability of getting an even number is:
A. 1/6
B. 1/2
C. 2/3
D. 1/3
Correct Answer: B
Even: 2,4,6 (3 outcomes). P = 3/6 = 1/2.
Q6. [1 or 2 marks] General Aptitude
If x + y = 10 and xy = 21, then x² + y² is:
A. 100
B. 79
C. 58
D. 42
Correct Answer: C
x²+y² = (x+y)² - 2xy = 100 - 42 = 58.

Q8. [1 or 2 marks] Structural
A simply supported beam with UDL has maximum bending moment at:
A. Ends
B. Mid-span
C. Quarter span

Correct Answer: B
Mmax = wL²/8 at mid-span for simply supported beam with UDL.
Q9. [1 or 2 marks] Structural
The factor of safety is defined as:
A. Ultimate load/Working load
B. Ultimate strength/Working stress
C. Yield stress/Working stress
D. Both A and B
Correct Answer: D
FoS = ultimate load / allowable load = ultimate stress / working stress.
Q10. [1 or 2 marks] Structural
The Euler's buckling load for a column depends on:
A. Material only
B. Length only
C. Length, E, I, and end conditions
D. Cross-section only
Correct Answer: C
Pcr = p²EI/(Le)². Depends on E, I, effective length (end conditions).
Q11. [1 or 2 marks] Structural
Concrete is strong in:
A. Tension
B. Compression
C. Shear
D. Torsion

Concrete has high compressive strength but low tensile strength (reinforced with steel).
