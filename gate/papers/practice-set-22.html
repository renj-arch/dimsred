Q1. Core Subject
A shaft subjected to pure torsion develops a maximum shear stress of 100 MPa. The yield strength of the material in uniaxial tension is 300 MPa. According to the Maximum Shear Stress Theory (Tresca), the factor of safety is:
A. 2.0
B. 1.5
C. 1.0
D. 3.0
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For pure torsion, max shear stress τ_max = τ. Tresca criterion: τ_max = S_yt / (2 × FS). Thus FS = S_yt / (2 × τ_max) = 300 / (2 × 100) = 1.5. Full Solution: Pure torsion stress state: σ₁ = τ, σ₂ = 0, σ₃ = -τ. Max shear stress = (σ₁ - σ₃)/2 = τ = 100 MPa. Yielding occurs when max shear stress = S_yt/2 = 150 MPa. Factor of Safety = Yield Shear Stress / Working Shear Stress = 150 / 100 = 1.5.
Q2. Core Subject
A helical spring of stiffness k is cut into two equal halves. The same mass m is attached to one of the halves. The ratio of the new natural frequency to the original natural frequency is:
A. 1/√2
B. 1
C. √2
D. 2
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Spring stiffness is inversely proportional to its length; halving the length doubles the stiffness (k' = 2k). Since natural frequency ω ∝ √k, the new frequency becomes √2 times the original. Full solution: Original ω = √(k/m). After cutting, each half has stiffness 2k. New ω' = √(2k/m) = √2 √(k/m) = √2 ω. Hence the ratio is √2.
Q3. Core Subject
A centrifugal pump running at 1500 rpm delivers 0.1 m³/saga inst a head of 30 m. If the speed is increased to 1800 rpm, the new discharge (m³/s) and head (m) respectively, assuming affinity laws hold, are:
A. 0.10, 30.0
B. 0.12, 36.0
C. 0.12, 43.2
D. 0.144, 43.2
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Affinity laws: Q ∝ N, H ∝ N². Speed ratio = 1800/1500 = 1.2. Q₂ = 0.1 × 1.2 = 0.12 m³/s. H₂ = 30 × (1.2)² = 30 × 1.44 = 43.2 m. Full: Q₂/Q₁ = N₂/N₁ → Q₂ = 0.1(1800/1500) = 0.12. H₂/H₁ = (N₂/N₁)² → H₂ = 30(1.2)² = 43.2.
Q4. Core Subject
In a vapor compression refrigeration cycle, the enthalpy at compressor inlet is 250 kJ/kg, at compressor outlet is 280 kJ/kg, and at condenser outlet is 100 kJ/kg. The throttling process is isenthalpic. The COP of the cycle is:
A. 3.5
B. 4.5
C. 5.0
D. 6.0
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: COP = (h₁ - h₄)/(h₂ - h₁). Since throttling is isenthalpic, h₄ = h₃ = 100 kJ/kg. RE = 250 - 100 = 150 kJ/kg. WC = 280 - 250 = 30 kJ/kg. COP = 150/30 = 5.0. Full method: Refrigeration effect q_L = h₁ - h₄ = 250 - 100 = 150 kJ/kg. Compressor work w_c = h₂ - h₁ = 280 - 250 = 30 kJ/kg. COP_R = q_L/w_c = 150/30 = 5.0.
Q5. Core Subject
A solid circular shaft transmits 80 kW at 1000 rpm. The allowable shear stress is 40 MPa. The required shaft diameter (in mm) is approximately:
A. 30
B. 40
C. 50
D. 60
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Torque T = 9550·P(kW)/N(rpm) = 9550·80/1000 = 764 Nm. Diameter from τ = 16T/(πd^3) → d^3 = 16·764/(π·40×10^6) ≈ 9.73×10^-5 m^3 → d ≈ 0.0458 m ≈ 50 mm. Full solution: Compute torque, then solve torsion formula for d.

Q8. General Aptitude
What is the unit digit of 7^2023?
A. 7
B. 9
C. 3
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The unit digit of powers of 7 follows a cycle of 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. Since 2023 mod 4 = 3, the unit digit is the 3rd in the cycle, which is 3. Full solution: 7^1=7, 7^2=49, 7^3=343, 7^4=2401; the pattern repeats every 4. 2023 = 4×505 + 3, so unit digit of 7^2023 equals unit digit of 7^3 = 3.
Q9. Core Subject
In a parallel flow heat exchanger, the hot fluid enters at 120°C and leaves at 80°C. The cold fluid enters at 30°C and leaves at 60°C. The log mean temperature difference (LMTD) in °C is:
A. 42.5
B. 44.5
C. 48.5
D. 46.5
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: For parallel flow, ΔT₁ = T_h,in - T_c,in = 90°C, ΔT₂ = T_h,out - T_c,out = 20°C. LMTD = (ΔT₁ - ΔT₂)/ln(ΔT₁/ΔT₂) = 70/ln(4.5) ≈ 70/1.504 ≈ 46.5°C. Full method: ΔT₁ = 120 - 30 = 90°C. ΔT₂ = 80 - 60 = 20°C. LMTD = (90 - 20)/ln(90/20) = 70/ln(4.5) = 70/1.504077 = 46.54°C ≈ 46.5°C.
Q10. General Aptitude
In a certain code language, 'TABLE' is written as 'UBCMF'. How is 'CHAIR' written in that code?
A. DIBJS
B. DIBJT
C. DIBJR
D. DIBJU
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Each letter in the code is shifted forward by one position in the alphabet (Caesar cipher +1). Applying this to CHAIR gives C→D, H→I, A→B, I→J, R→S → DIBJS. Full solution: Comparing TABLE and UBCMF shows T→U, A→B, B→C, L→M, E→F, confirming a uniform +1 shift. Using the same rule on CHAIR yields DIBJS.

Q12. Core Subject
Heat Q = 1000 kJ is transferred from a reservoir at 500 K to another at 300 K. The ambient temperature is 300 K. The exergy destruction (in kJ) during this process is:
A. 133.3
B. 200
C. 266.7
D. 400
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Exergy destruction = T₀ × Q × (1/T_L - 1/T_H) = 300 × 1000 × (1/300 - 1/500) = 300 × 1000 × (2/1500) = 400 kJ. Full Solution: Entropy generation S_gen = Q/T_L - Q/T_H = 1000/300 - 1000/500 = 10/3 - 2 = 4/3 kJ/K. Exergy destruction (Irreversibility) I = T₀ × S_gen = 300 × (4/3) = 400 kJ.
Q13. Core Subject
A long cylindrical fin of diameter 5 mm and thermal conductivity 200 W/m·K is exposed to a convection environment with h = 50 W/m²·K. Using the infinite fin approximation, the fin effectiveness is approximately:
A. 28
B. 40
C. 56
D. 80
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For a long fin, effectiveness ε = √(kP/hAc). For a cylinder, P/Ac = 4/d. Thus ε = 2√(k/hd) = 2√(200/(50×0.005)) = 2√800 ≈ 56.6. Full: m = √(hP/kAc) = √(4h/kd) = √(4×50/(200×0.005)) = √200 ≈ 14.14 m⁻¹. mL = 14.14×0.1 = 1.414. Although mL < 2.65, the problem states 'infinite fin approximation'. Effectiveness ε = √(kP/hAc) = √(k×(πd)/(h×πd²/4)) = √(4k/hd) = 2√(200/(50×0.005)) = 2√800 = 56.57 ≈ 56.
Q14. Core Subject
A rotor with a moment of inertia of 0.5 kg·m² spins at 300 rad/s. The shaft precesses at 10 rad/s. If the spin speed is doubled and the precession speed is halved, the new gyroscopic couple (in N·m) will be:
A. 1500
B. 750
C. 3000
D. 6000
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Gyroscopic couple C = I·ω·ω_p. The product ω·ω_p changes by factor (2)×(0.5)=1, so couple remains unchanged at 1500 N·m. Step-by-step: Original couple = 0.5 × 300 × 10 = 1500 N·m. New spin = 600 rad/s, new precession = 5 rad/s. New couple = 0.5 × 600 × 5 = 1500 N·m.
Q15. Core Subject
A simply supported beam of span L carries a uniformly distributed load w over its entire length. The same beam carries a central point load equal to the total distributed load (W = wL). The ratio of the maximum deflection under UDL to that under the central point load is:
A. 5/8
B. 8/5
C. 1/2
D. 2/5
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Known formula ratio δ_udl/δ_point = (5/384)/(1/48) = 5/8. Full solution: δ_udl = 5wL^4/(384EI), δ_point = (wL)L^3/(48EI) = wL^4/(48EI). Ratio = (5/384)/(1/48) = 5/8.
