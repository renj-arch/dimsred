Q1. Core Subject
An ideal Brayton cycle operates with a pressure ratio of 10 and specific heat ratio γ = 1.4. Using the shortcut η ≈ 1 − (1 / r_p^{(γ−1)/γ}), the approximate thermal efficiency (in %) is:
A. 48%
B. 45%
C. 51%
D. 54%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: η ≈ 1 − 1/r_p^{(γ−1)/γ}. Here (γ−1)/γ = 0.4/1.4 ≈ 0.2857. r_p^0.2857 = 10^0.2857 ≈ 1.93. η ≈ 1 − 1/1.93 ≈ 0.482 → 48%. Full: η = 1 − (1/10)^{0.2857} = 0.482 ≈ 48%.
Q2. Core Subject
In a compound gear train, gear A (20 teeth) drives gear B (40 teeth). Gear B is mounted on the same shaft as gear C (30 teeth), which drives gear D (60 teeth). The overall velocity ratio ω_A/ω_D is:
A. 4
B. 2
C. 1/2
D. 1/4
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Velocity ratio = (N_B/N_A) × (N_D/N_C) = (40/20) × (60/30) = 2 × 2 = 4. Full: ω_A/ω_B = N_B/N_A = 2; ω_C/ω_D = N_D/N_C = 2; since ω_B = ω_C, ω_A/ω_D = 2 × 2 = 4.
Q3. Core Subject
In a counter-flow heat exchanger, the heat capacity rates of the hot and cold fluids are equal (C_h = C_c). If the Number of Transfer Units (NTU) is 2.0, the effectiveness (ε) of the heat exchanger is:
A. 0.50
B. 0.67
C. 0.75
D. 0.80
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For balanced flow (C_r = C_min/C_max = 1), effectiveness ε = NTU / (1 + NTU) = 2 / (1 + 2) = 2/3 ≈ 0.67. Full method: The general effectiveness formula for counter-flow is ε = [1 - exp(-NTU(1-C_r))] / [1 - C_r exp(-NTU(1-C_r))]. For C_r = 1, this is indeterminate (0/0). Applying L'Hôpital's rule or the known limit for C_r → 1 gives ε = NTU / (1 + NTU). Substituting NTU = 2 gives ε = 2/3 = 0.666...
Q4. Core Subject
The maximum shear stress in a solid circular shaft of diameter d subjected to a torque T is τ. If the shaft diameter is increased to 2d while the torque remains the same, the new maximum shear stress is:
A. τ/2
B. τ/4
C. τ/8
D. τ/16
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: τ_max = 16T/(πd³) ∝ 1/d³; doubling d reduces stress by 2³ = 8 times. Full: τ' = 16T/(π(2d)³) = 16T/(8πd³) = τ/8.
Q5. General Aptitude
Find the missing term in the series: 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ?
A. 125
B. 129
C. 127
D. 131
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The series follows the pattern 2^(n+1)−1 (3, 7, 15, 31, 63, …). The next term is 2^7−1 = 127. Full solution: Differences are 4, 8, 16, 32 (doubling each time), so the next difference is 64; 63 + 64 = 127.
Q6. Core Subject
A rotating shaft made of steel (ultimate tensile strength S_ut = 600 MPa) is subjected to a completely reversed bending stress of amplitude 250 MPa. The fatigue strength fraction f = 0.9 at 10^3 cycles. Using Basquin's equation σ_a = σ_f' (2N_f)^b with the shortcut that the slope b ≈ -1/3 log10(f S_ut / σ_f') and σ_f' ≈ f S_ut, the estimated fatigue life N_f (in cycles) is approximately:
A. 1.2 × 10^5
B. 3.5 × 10^5
C. 8.7 × 10^5
D. 2.1 × 10^6
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For completely reversed loading, σ_f' ≈ f S_ut = 0.9 × 600 = 540 MPa. Slope b = -1/3 log10(σ_f' / σ_a) = -1/3 log10(540/250) ≈ -1/3 × 0.334 ≈ -0.111. Then 2N_f = (σ_a/σ_f')^(1/b) = (250/540)^(1/-0.111) = (0.463)^(-9) ≈ 7.0 × 10^5. Thus N_f ≈ 3.5 × 10^5 cycles. Full method: Basquin's equation σ_a = σ_f' (2N_f)^b. At 10^3 cycles, σ_a = f S_ut = 540 MPa ⇒ 540 = σ_f' (2000)^b. At failure σ_f' ≈ 540 MPa (standard approximation). Then 250 = 540 (2N_f)^b. Also 540 = 540 (2000)^b ⇒ (2000)^b = 1 ⇒ b = 0? Correction: Use two-point method. At N=10^3, σ=540; at N=10^6, σ=0.5 S_ut=300 (endurance limit approx). Slope b = log(540/300)/log(2×10^6/2×10^3) = log(1.8)/log(1000) ≈ 0.255/3 = 0.085? Standard approach: b = -1/3 log10(f S_ut / σ_f') with σ_f' = f S_ut gives b ≈ -0.085. Then 250 = 540 (2N_f)^(-0.085) ⇒ (2N_f)^0.085 = 2.16 ⇒ 2N_f = 2.16^(1/0.085) ≈ 2.16^11.76 ≈ 7×10^5 ⇒ N_f ≈ 3.5×10^5.
Q7. Core Subject
In a fully developed laminar flow through a circular pipe of diameter D, the pressure drop is ΔP. If the diameter is halved (D/2) and the volumetric flow rate is doubled (2Q), keeping length and viscosity constant, the new pressure drop is approximately:
A. 8 ΔP
B. 16 ΔP
C. 64 ΔP
D. 32 ΔP
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Hagen-Poiseuille law: ΔP ∝ Q/D⁴. Q doubles (×2), D halves (1/D⁴ becomes 16×). Net factor = 2 × 16 = 32. New ΔP = 32 ΔP. Full Solution: For laminar flow, ΔP = (128 μ L Q)/(π D⁴). Thus ΔP ∝ Q/D⁴. Let initial state be ΔP₁, Q₁, D₁. New state: Q₂ = 2Q₁, D₂ = D₁/2. ΔP₂/ΔP₁ = (Q₂/Q₁) × (D₁/D₂)⁴ = 2 × (2)⁴ = 2 × 16 = 32. Hence ΔP₂ = 32 ΔP₁.
Q8. Core Subject
In a laminar flow through a horizontal circular pipe, the pressure drop ΔP is given by the Hagen–Poiseuille equation. If the pipe diameter is halved while the flow rate, length, and fluid viscosity remain unchanged, the new pressure drop becomes:
A. 2ΔP
B. 4ΔP
C. 8ΔP
D. 16ΔP
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: ΔP ∝ 1/D⁴ from Hagen–Poiseuille; halving D increases ΔP by 2⁴ = 16 times. Full: ΔP = 128μLQ/(πD⁴); new ΔP' = 128μLQ/(π(D/2)⁴) = 16 × ΔP.
Q9. Core Subject
A ball bearing (life exponent p = 3) rated for 8000 hours at a load of 2 kN is subjected to an increased load of 4 kN. The new rated life L10 (in hours) is approximately:
A. 1000
B. 2000
C. 4000
D. 16000
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Bearing life varies inversely with the cube of the load (L ∝ P⁻³). Load doubles (×2), so life reduces by 2³ = 8 times. 8000 / 8 = 1000 hours. Full method: Using the load-life relationship L₁₀ = (C/P)ᵖ. For the same bearing, C is constant. Ratio L₂/L₁ = (P₁/P₂)ᵖ = (2/4)³ = (1/2)³ = 1/8. L₂ = 8000 × (1/8) = 1000 hours.
Q10. Core Subject
In an orthogonal cutting process, the rake angle (α) is 10° and the friction angle (β) is 20°. Using Merchant's minimum energy principle (φ = 45° + α/2 - β/2), the shear angle φ (in degrees) is:
A. 40
B. 35
C. 45
D. 50
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Direct substitution into Merchant's formula: φ = 45° + (10°/2) - (20°/2) = 45° + 5° - 10° = 40°. Full Solution: Merchant's shear angle relationship for minimum energy consumption is φ = 45° + (α - β)/2. Given α = 10°, β = 20°. φ = 45° + (10 - 20)/2 = 45° - 5° = 40°.
Q11. Core Subject
A Rankine cycle operates with steam entering the turbine at 8 MPa (saturation temperature ≈ 295°C) and condensing at 10 kPa (saturation temperature ≈ 45°C). The Carnot efficiency between these temperature limits is approximately:
A. 30%
B. 44%
C. 55%
D. 60%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: η_Carnot = 1 - T_low/T_high (Kelvin). T_high = 295+273 = 568 K, T_low = 45+273 = 318 K. η = 1 - 318/568 ≈ 0.44 = 44%. Full: 318/568 = 0.56; 1 - 0.56 = 0.44.
Q12. Core Subject
In a multiple‑choice exam, a candidate knows that the correct answer for a certain question is always an even number. The four options are 13, 27, 42, and 55. Using the parity shortcut, the candidate selects:
A. 13
B. 27
C. 42
D. 55
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Only the even number satisfies the given condition. Among the options, 42 is even, so it must be correct. Full steps: Check each option for parity – 13 (odd), 27 (odd), 42 (even), 55 (odd). Hence the answer is 42.
Q13. General Aptitude
A can complete a piece of work in 12 days and B can complete the same work in 18 days. They work on alternate days starting with A. In how many days will the work be completed?
A. 14 2/3 days
B. 14 1/3 days
C. 15 days
D. 15 1/2 days
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Assume total work = LCM(12,18)=36 units; A's rate=3 units/day, B's=2 units/day. In each 2-day cycle they complete 5 units, so after 14 days (7 cycles) 35 units are done. The remaining 1 unit is finished by A in 1/3 day, giving 14 1/3 days total.
Q14. Core Subject
For a laminar boundary layer on a flat plate, the Blasius solution gives the 99% thickness δ ≈ 5.0x/√Re_x. If the boundary layer thickness at a distance x₁ from the leading edge is δ₁, the thickness at x₂ = 4x₁ is:
A. δ₁
B. 1.5δ₁
C. 2δ₁
D. 4δ₁
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: From δ ∝ x/√Re_x and Re_x ∝ x, we get δ ∝ √x. Since x₂ = 4x₁, δ₂/δ₁ = √(x₂/x₁) = √4 = 2. Thus δ₂ = 2δ₁. Full Solution: δ = 5.0x / √(Ux/ν) = 5.0 √(νx/U). This shows δ is proportional to √x. Ratio δ₂/δ₁ = √(x₂/x₁) = √(4x₁/x₁) = 2. Therefore, δ₂ = 2δ₁.
Q15. Core Subject
A steel rod of length 2 m and cross‑sectional area 1 × 10⁻⁴ m² is heated uniformly so that its temperature rises from 20 °C to 120 °C. Using the linear thermal expansion shortcut ΔL ≈ α L₀ ΔT, where α = 12 × 10⁻⁶ °C⁻¹ for steel, the increase in length (in mm) is:
A. 1.2
B. 2.4
C. 12.0
D. 24.0
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: ΔL ≈ α L₀ ΔT. Compute α·ΔT = 12×10⁻⁶ × 100 = 1.2×10⁻³. Multiply by L₀ = 2 m → ΔL = 2 × 1.2×10⁻³ = 2.4×10⁻³ m = 2.4 mm. Full steps: ΔT = 120‑20 = 100 °C. αΔT = 12×10⁻⁶×100 = 1.2×10⁻³. ΔL = αΔT·L₀ = 1.2×10⁻³·2 = 2.4×10⁻³ m = 2.4 mm.
