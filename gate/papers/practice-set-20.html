B. A₁/A₂
C. 1
D. 1 - A₁/A₂
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Reciprocity gives F₂₁ = (A₁/A₂)F₁₂ = A₁/A₂. Summation rule for surface 2 (box) requires F₂₁ + F₂₂ = 1 (since it sees only the sphere and itself). Thus F₂₂ = 1 - F₂₁ = 1 - A₁/A₂. Substitute F₂₁: F₂₂ = 1 - A₁/A₂.

B. 0.12, 72
