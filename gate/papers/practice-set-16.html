Q1. Core Subject
A simply supported beam of length 4 m carries a point load of 10 kN at mid‑span. The flexural rigidity EI is 2×10⁶ N·m². The maximum deflection (in mm) is approximately:
A. 7 mm
B. 5 mm
C. 10 mm
D. 15 mm
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: δ = PL³/(48EI). Plug P=10 kN=10⁴ N, L=4 m → L³=64, numerator=6.4×10⁵ N·m³. Denominator=48×2×10⁶=9.6×10⁷. δ=6.4×10⁵/9.6×10⁷=0.00667 m=6.7 mm ≈ 7 mm. Full: Using the standard formula for central point load on a simply supported beam, the maximum deflection is PL³/(48EI). Substituting the given values yields 6.7 mm, closest to 7 mm.
Q2. Core Subject
A ball bearing is rated for a life of 10,000 hours at a radial load of 5 kN. If the load is increased to 10 kN, the expected life (in hours) is approximately? (Assume load-life exponent k = 3 for ball bearings)
A. 2500
B. 5000
C. 1250
D. 8000
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Bearing life L ∝ 1/Pᵏ. Load doubles (P₂/P₁ = 2), so life reduces by factor 2³ = 8. 10,000 / 8 = 1,250 hours. Full Solution: The load-life relationship is L₁₀ = (C/P)ᵏ or L₁P₁ᵏ = L₂P₂ᵏ. Given L₁ = 10,000 hrs, P₁ = 5 kN, P₂ = 10 kN, k = 3. L₂ = L₁ × (P₁/P₂)ᵏ = 10,000 × (5/10)³ = 10,000 × (1/8) = 1,250 hours.

Q4. Core Subject
A steam pipe is insulated with a material of thermal conductivity 0.1 W/m·K. The outer surface convection heat transfer coefficient is 10 W/m²·K. The critical radius of insulation (in mm) is approximately:
A. 5 mm
B. 10 mm
C. 20 mm
D. 50 mm
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a cylinder, critical radius r_c = k_ins / h. Here r_c = 0.1 / 10 = 0.01 m = 10 mm. Full method: Heat loss from insulated pipe increases with insulation thickness until r = k/h, then decreases. Substituting k = 0.1 W/m·K and h = 10 W/m²·K gives r_c = 0.01 m = 10 mm.
Q5. General Aptitude
A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. B and C together can complete it in 15 days. C and A together can complete it in 20 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?
A. 30 days
B. 40 days
C. 50 days
D. 60 days
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Take LCM of 12, 15, 20 = 60 as total work. Then daily work of (A+B)=5, (B+C)=4, (C+A)=3. Sum = 12 = 2(A+B+C) => A+B+C=6. So A's daily work = 6-4=2. Time for A = 60/2 = 30 days. Full solution: Let total work = 60 units. Efficiency of A+B = 60/12 = 5 units/day, B+C = 60/15 = 4, C+A = 60/20 = 3. Adding gives 2(A+B+C)=12 => A+B+C=6. Subtract B+C (4) from total (6) gives A=2 units/day. Hence A alone takes 60/2 = 30 days.
Q6. Core Subject
A Carnot refrigerator operates between −10 °C and 30 °C. Its coefficient of performance (COP) is approximately:
A. 5.5
B. 6.5
C. 7.5
D. 8.5
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: COP = T_L/(T_H−T_L) with temperatures in Kelvin. T_L = 263 K (−10 °C), T_H = 303 K (30 °C), ΔT = 40 K → COP = 263/40 ≈ 6.58 ≈ 6.5. Full: Convert Celsius to Kelvin, apply the Carnot refrigerator COP formula, compute 263/40 = 6.575, nearest option 6.5.
Q7. Core Subject
In a turning operation using a carbide tool, the tool life is 80 minutes at a cutting speed of 30 m/min. If the Taylor's tool life exponent (n) is 0.5, the cutting speed (in m/min) required to achieve a tool life of 20 minutes is approximately:
A. 42
B. 60
C. 75
D. 90
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the ratio form of Taylor's equation V₂ = V₁(T₁/T₂)ⁿ. Speed ratio = (80/20)⁰·⁵ = √4 = 2. New speed = 30 × 2 = 60 m/min. Full: Taylor's equation VTⁿ = C gives V₁T₁ⁿ = V₂T₂ⁿ. Substituting V₁=30, T₁=80, T₂=20, n=0.5: V₂ = 30 × (80/20)⁰·⁵ = 30 × √4 = 60 m/min.
Q8. General Aptitude
Find the missing term in the following series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
A. 40
B. 44
C. 42
D. 46
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Calculate successive differences: 4, 6, 8, 10. Differences increase by 2 constantly. Next difference = 12. Missing term = 30 + 12 = 42. Full: The series follows the pattern n(n+1): 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, 6×7=42.
Q9. Core Subject
A cubic casting of side 50 mm solidifies in 5 minutes. Using Chvorinov's rule (t = B(V/A)^2), the solidification time (in minutes) for a cubic casting of the same material with side 100 mm is approximately:
A. 10
B. 40
C. 80
D. 20
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: For a cube, V/A = a/6, so solidification time t ∝ a². Doubling the side length (50 mm → 100 mm) increases time by factor 2² = 4. 5 min × 4 = 20 min. Full Solution: Chvorinov's rule states t = B(V/A)². For a cube of side 'a', Volume = a³, Surface Area = 6a², so V/A = a/6. Thus t = B(a/6)² ∝ a². For a₁ = 50 mm, t₁ = 5 min. For a₂ = 100 mm = 2a₁, t₂/t₁ = (a₂/a₁)² = 4. Therefore t₂ = 4 × 5 = 20 min.
Q10. Core Subject
A simply supported beam of length 4 m carries a uniformly distributed load of 10 kN/m. The maximum bending moment (in kN·m) occurs at mid-span and equals:
A. 40
B. 60
C. 20
D. 80
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For UDL on simply supported beam, M_max = wL^2/8 = 10×4^2/8 = 20 kNm. Full: Reactions = wL/2 = 20 kN each; moment at centre = 20×2 – 10×2×1 = 40 – 20 = 20 kNm.
Q11. Core Subject
An ideal Brayton cycle operates with a pressure ratio of 8. Assuming specific heat ratio γ = 1.4, the thermal efficiency (in %) is approximately:
A. 35
B. 55
C. 45
D. 65
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Brayton efficiency η = 1 - (1/rₚ)^((γ-1)/γ). Here (γ-1)/γ = 0.4/1.4 = 2/7 ≈ 0.286. rₚ^0.286 = 8^(2/7) = (2³)^(2/7) = 2^(6/7) ≈ 1.81. η ≈ 1 - 1/1.81 ≈ 0.447 ≈ 45%. Full Solution: For an ideal Brayton cycle, thermal efficiency depends only on pressure ratio rₚ and γ. η = 1 - 1/(rₚ)^((γ-1)/γ). Substituting rₚ = 8, γ = 1.4: Exponent = 0.4/1.4 = 2/7. (8)^(2/7) = 2^(6/7) ≈ 1.811. η = 1 - 1/1.811 = 0.4478 = 44.78% ≈ 45%.
Q12. Core Subject
An ideal Diesel cycle operates with a compression ratio of 16 and a cut-off ratio of 2. Taking the specific heat ratio γ = 1.4, the air-standard thermal efficiency (in %) is approximately:
A. 55
B. 58
C. 61
D. 67
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Compute 1/r^(γ-1) ≈ 1/16^0.4 ≈ 1/3.03 ≈ 0.33. Cut-off term (ρ^γ-1)/[γ(ρ-1)] ≈ (2^1.4-1)/1.4 ≈ 1.64/1.4 ≈ 1.17. Product ≈ 0.386. Efficiency ≈ 1 - 0.386 = 61.4%. Full: η = 1 - (1/r^(γ-1)) * [(ρ^γ - 1)/(γ(ρ - 1))]. r=16, ρ=2, γ=1.4. r^(γ-1)=16^0.4≈3.031. ρ^γ=2^1.4≈2.639. Term = (1/3.031) * (1.639/1.4) = 0.33 * 1.171 = 0.386. η = 1 - 0.386 = 0.614 ≈ 61%.
Q13. Core Subject
A centrifugal pump running at 1000 rpm delivers 0.1 m³/s against a head of 50 m. If the pump speed is increased to 1500 rpm, the new discharge (in m³/s) and head (in m) are respectively:
A. 0.15, 112.5
B. 0.15, 75
C. 0.225, 112.5
D. 0.225, 75
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Affinity laws: Q ∝ N, H ∝ N². Speed ratio = 1500/1000 = 1.5. Q₂ = 0.1 × 1.5 = 0.15 m³/s. H₂ = 50 × (1.5)² = 50 × 2.25 = 112.5 m. Full: For dynamically similar conditions, Q₁/N₁ = Q₂/N₂ → Q₂ = 0.1 × (1500/1000) = 0.15 m³/s. H₁/N₁² = H₂/N₂² → H₂ = 50 × (1500/1000)² = 50 × 2.25 = 112.5 m.
