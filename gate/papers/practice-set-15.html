What is the unit digit of the expression \((7^{95} - 3^{58})\)?
A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 8
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (Cyclicity Method): The unit digits of powers of 7 repeat in a cycle of 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. For \(7^{95}\), divide exponent by 4: \(95 \bmod 4 = 3\), so unit digit is the 3rd in cycle = 3. The unit digits of powers of 3 repeat in a cycle of 4: 3, 9, 7, 1. For \(3^{58}\), \(58 \bmod 4 = 2\), so unit digit is the 2nd in cycle = 9. Since 3 < 9, borrow 10: \(13 - 9 = 4\). Step-by-step: \(7^1=7, 7^2=49, 7^3=343, 7^4=2401\) → cycle (7,9,3,1). \(95 = 4 \times 23 + 3\) → unit digit of \(7^{95}\) is 3. \(3^1=3, 3^2=9, 3^3=27, 3^4=81\) → cycle (3,9,7,1). \(58 = 4 \times 14 + 2\) → unit digit of \(3^{58}\) is 9. Unit digit of difference = \((10+3) - 9 = 4\).
Q3. General Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-17
The price of an article is increased by 20% and subsequently decreased by 20%. What is the net percentage change in the final price compared to the original price?
A. No change (0%)
B. 4% decrease
C. 4% increase
D. 2% decrease
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For successive changes of a% and b%, net change = a + b + ab/100. Here, 20 + (-20) + (20 * -20)/100 = -4% (decrease). Full: Assume original price = 100. After 20% increase = 120. After 20% decrease = 120 * 0.80 = 96. Net change = (96-100)/100 * 100 = -4%.

B. 56.5%
C. 62.4%
D. 38.7%
Correct Answer: B
