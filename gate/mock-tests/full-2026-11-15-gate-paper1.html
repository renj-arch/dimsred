General Aptitude 10 Q · 15 marks

Q1General Aptitude
If x + y = 10 and xy = 21, then x^2 + y^2 is:
Answer: C

x^2+y^2 = (x+y)^2 - 2xy = 100 - 42 = 58.

Q2General Aptitude
A man buys a book for 500 and sells it for 600. Profit percentage is:
Answer: B

Profit = 100, CP = 500. Profit% = 100/500 x 100 = 20%.

Q3General Aptitude
Find the next term: 3, 9, 27, 81, ?
Answer: C

Geometric progression with ratio 3: 35 = 243.

Q4General Aptitude
Choose the correct spelling:
Answer: C

Correct spelling: Mischievous.

Q5General Aptitude
If 3x + 2 = 11, then x = ?
Answer: B

3x = 9 ⇒ x = 3.

Q6General Aptitude
A train 150 m long passes a pole in 15 seconds. Find the speed of the train in km/h.
Answer: C

Speed = distance/time = 150/15 = 10 m/s. In km/h: 10 x (18/5) = 36 km/h.

Q7General Aptitude
A die is rolled once. Probability of getting an even number is:
Answer: B

Even: 2,4,6 (3 outcomes). P = 3/6 = 1/2.

Q8General Aptitude
Find the odd one out: 2, 4, 8, 16, 24, 32
Answer: C

All others are powers of 2 (2, 2^2, 2, 2, 2). 24 is not a power of 2.

Q9General Aptitude
If 5 men can build a wall in 12 days, how many days will 10 men take?
Answer: C

MD = MD -> 5x12 = 10xD -> D = 6 days.

Q10General Aptitude
The simple interest on 1000 at 5% per annum for 2 years is:
Answer: B

SI = PxRxT/100 = 1000x5x2/100 = 100.

Q27Core Subject
What is the value of the integral of x^2 dx from 0 to 2?
Answer: B

The integral of x^2 dx from 0 to 2 is (1/3)x^3 from 0 to 2, which is (1/3)(2^3) - (1/3)(0^3) = (1/3)(8) = 8/3.
