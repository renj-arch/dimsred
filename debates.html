What the Salt March was really fighting over.

The march is remembered as a walk. It was also a fight inside a fight: over strategy, representation, and who had the right to speak for India.

An eye-opener. A tax on salt looks small. That is the point. A small tax on a thing everyone needs is a quiet way to make a whole population pay — and a quiet way to make them obey.
