Q1. General Knowledge
Which of the following peaks is the highest in the Western Ghats?
A. Doddabetta
B. Mullayanagiri
C. Anamudi
D. Kalsubai
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Anamudi (2,695 m) is the only Western Ghats peak exceeding 2,600 m, while Doddabetta (2,637 m), Mullayanagiri (1,930 m) and Kalsubai (1,646 m) are lower; thus eliminate the three lower options. Detailed: Anamudi in Kerala is the highest point of the Western Ghats and of South India; Doddabetta is the highest in the Nilgiris but slightly lower; Mullayanagiri is the highest in Karnataka; Kalsubai is the highest in Maharashtra. Hence Anamudi is the correct answer.

Q3. English
Improve the bracketed part of the sentence. Choose 'No improvement' if the sentence is correct. [Walking down the street], the flowers smelled lovely.
A. Walking down the street, the flowers smelled lovely.
B. The flowers smelled lovely walking down the street.
C. As I walked down the street, the flowers smelled lovely.
D. Walking down the street, I smelled the lovely flowers.
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Dangling Modifier Check. The introductory participial phrase 'Walking down the street' must logically modify the subject of the main clause. Flowers cannot walk. Eliminate options where 'flowers' is the subject (A, B). Option C corrects the clause structure but retains 'flowers' as subject of main clause (still illogical for the modifier). Option D supplies the correct subject 'I' for the action 'Walking'. Worked Solution: In (A) and (B), 'flowers' is the subject, creating a dangling modifier (flowers don't walk). In (C), 'As I walked...' is a dependent clause, but the main clause subject remains 'flowers', disconnecting the action from the smeller. In (D), 'I' is the subject of the main clause, correctly modified by 'Walking down the street'.

Q5. General Knowledge
The 'Mission Shakti' conducted by India in March 2019 demonstrated India's capability in which domain?
A. Anti-Satellite (ASAT) Missile Test
B. Hypersonic Cruise Missile Test
C. Intercontinental Ballistic Missile (ICBM) Test
D. Anti-Ballistic Missile (ABM) Interceptor Test
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Keyword association. 'Mission Shakti' (March 2019) + 'Satellite' = ASAT. 'Mission Divyastra' (March 2024) = Agni-5 MIRV/ICBM tech. 'HSTDV' = Hypersonic. 'PDV/AD-1/AD-2' = ABM/Interceptor. The name 'Shakti' (Power) was specifically assigned to the ASAT test (Mission Shakti) where DRDO destroyed a live satellite (Microsat-R) in Low Earth Orbit. Full Solution: Mission Shakti was the code name for India's first successful Anti-Satellite (ASAT) missile test conducted on March 27, 2019. A DRDO Ballistic Missile Defence (BMD) interceptor (PDV Mk-II) successfully engaged and destroyed the Microsat-R satellite at an altitude of ~300 km in Low Earth Orbit (LEO), making India the 4th nation with demonstrated ASAT capability. It was not a Hypersonic, ICBM, or ABM target test (though it used BMD tech, the *demonstrated capability* was ASAT).

A. RPSQ
B. SRPQ
C. RSPQ

Q8. General Knowledge
Which of the following is the longest river flowing entirely within Peninsular India?
A. Krishna
B. Godavari
C. Kaveri
D. Mahanadi
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Basin Area/Length ranking elimination. Godavari is the largest peninsular river system (length ~1465 km, basin ~3.12 lakh km²). Krishna (~1400 km) is second. Kaveri (~805 km) and Mahanadi (~858 km) are significantly shorter. 'Dakshin Ganga' epithet confirms Godavari's dominance. Worked Solution: The Godavari River rises at Trimbakeshwar (Maharashtra) and flows east for 1,465 km, draining the second-largest basin in India after the Ganga. The Krishna River is approx 1,400 km long. The Kaveri is approx 805 km. The Mahanadi is approx 858 km. Thus, Godavari is the longest peninsular river.
Q9. English
Select the word most nearly similar in meaning to the word given in capital letters: EPHEMERAL
A. Eternal
B. Transient
C. Permanent
D. Enduring
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Antonym elimination. Three options (Eternal, Permanent, Enduring) are synonyms of each other meaning 'lasting forever'. Ephemeral means 'lasting for a very short time'. The odd one out matching this definition is Transient. Worked Solution: Ephemeral describes something short-lived (e.g., a mayfly). Transient means lasting only for a short time; impermanent. Eternal, Permanent, and Enduring are all antonyms of Ephemeral. Thus, Transient is the correct synonym.

Q12. General Knowledge
Deficiency of which vitamin causes Pellagra, classically characterized by the '3 Ds' – Dermatitis, Diarrhoea, and Dementia?
A. Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)
B. Vitamin B3 (Niacin)
C. Vitamin B12 (Cobalamin)
D. Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Mnemonic 'Pellagra = 3 Ds = Niacin (B3)'. Beriberi (B1) = Wet/Dry; Pernicious Anemia (B12) = Neuro/Megaloblastic; Scurvy (C) = Gums/Bleeding. Full Solution: Pellagra is caused by Niacin (Vitamin B3) deficiency (or tryptophan deficiency). The classic triad is Dermatitis (photosensitive), Diarrhoea, and Dementia; untreated leads to Death (4th D). Thiamine (B1) causes Beriberi. Cobalamin (B12) causes Megaloblastic anemia. Ascorbic Acid (C) causes Scurvy.
Q13. General Knowledge
Consider the following statements regarding the 'Kaziranga National Park': 1. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. 2. It is the single largest undisturbed habitat of the Indian One-Horned Rhinoceros. 3. It is located in the Karbi Anglong district of Assam. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Verify location/district knowledge to eliminate. Kaziranga is located in Golaghat and Nagaon districts (partly), not Karbi Anglong (Karbi Anglong hills are south of the park, distinct district). Statement 3 is incorrect. Eliminate Options B, C, D (all contain 3). Only Option A remains. Full Solution: Kaziranga National Park (Assam) was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1985 (Statement 1 correct). It hosts ~2,400+ Indian One-Horned Rhinos, the largest population globally (Statement 2 correct). It is situated in the Golaghat and Nagaon districts. Karbi Anglong is a separate district to the south, known for the Karbi Anglong Hills which act as a refuge during floods, but the park itself is not *in* Karbi Anglong district (Statement 3 incorrect).

Correct Answer: A

Q15. General Knowledge
Which of the following is the highest peak of the Western Ghats?
A. Doddabetta
B. Kalsubai
C. Anamudi
D. Guru Shikhar
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Map-based elimination. Guru Shikhar is the highest in the Aravallis (Rajasthan). Doddabetta is the highest in the Nilgiris (Tamil Nadu). Kalsubai is the highest in Maharashtra (Sahyadris). Anamudi (2,695 m) in Kerala is the overall highest peak of the entire Western Ghats range. Step-by-step: The Western Ghats run north-south. Anamudi is located in the Anaimalai Hills (Kerala/Tamil Nadu border) and is the highest point in India south of the Himalayas. Doddabetta (2,637 m) is the second highest.
