Q4. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-17
The value of the definite integral ∫_0^{π/2} (sin x)/(sin x + cos x) dx is:
A. π/2
B. π/4
C. π/8
D. π/6
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Apply King's Property (∫_0^a f(x) dx = ∫_0^a f(a-x) dx). I = ∫ cos x/(sin x+cos x) dx. Adding, 2I = ∫_0^{π/2} 1 dx = π/2, so I = π/4. Full Solution: Let I = ∫_0^{π/2} sin x/(sin x+cos x) dx. Using property, I = ∫_0^{π/2} sin(π/2-x)/(sin(π/2-x)+cos(π/2-x)) dx = ∫_0^{π/2} cos x/(cos x+sin x) dx. Adding both forms: 2I = ∫_0^{π/2} (sin x+cos x)/(sin x+cos x) dx = ∫_0^{π/2} 1 dx = π/2. Hence I = π/4.

The product of the first 9 terms of a Geometric Progression (GP) is 512. The 5th term of the GP is:
A. 4
B. 8
C. 2
D. 16
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: In a GP with odd number of terms (n), the product of all terms equals the (middle term)^n. Here n=9, middle term is the 5th term. So (T₅)⁹ = 512 = 2⁹ ⇒ T₅ = 2. Worked: Let terms be a, ar, ..., ar⁸. Product = a⁹ r^(0+1+...+8) = a⁹ r³⁶ = (a r⁴)⁹. Since 5th term T₅ = a r⁴, we have (T₅)⁹ = 512 ⇒ T₅ = 2.

C. 22.5%
D. 24%
Correct Answer: B

C. 2
D. 4
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: 2^3 = 8 ≡ 1 (mod 7). Hence 2^{100} = 2^{3×33+1} = (2^3)^{33}·2 ≡ 1^{33}·2 = 2 (mod 7). Full steps: powers of 2 mod 7 cycle as 2,4,1; 100 mod 3 = 1 → remainder 2.
Q14. Mathematics
A bank offers a special 'Festival Savings Scheme' where the interest rate for the first year is 5% per annum on the principal. However, the interest earned in the first year is added to the principal at the end of the year. If a customer deposits ₹10,000 and closes the account after exactly one year, what is the total amount received?
A. ₹10,500
B. ₹10,525
C. ₹10,600
D. ₹10,400
Correct Answer: A
Simple Interest = (P * R * T) / 100. Substituting values: P = 10000, R = 5%, T = 1 year. SI = (10000 * 5 * 1) / 100. SI = 500. Total Amount = Principal + Interest = 10000 + 500 = 10500.
