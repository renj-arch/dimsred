Q1. General Knowledge
Which of the following rivers flows through South Africa?
A. Nile
B. Amazon
C. Orange
D. Yangtze
Correct Answer: C
The Orange River flows through Southern Africa. The Nile is in Egypt and Sudan, the Amazon is in South America, and the Yangtze is in China.
Q2. General Knowledge
Who painted the famous artwork 'The Last Supper'?
A. Vincent van Gogh
B. Pablo Picasso
C. Leonardo da Vinci
D. Michelangelo
Correct Answer: C
Leonardo da Vinci painted 'The Last Supper' in the late 15th century. Vincent van Gogh is famous for 'Starry Night'. Pablo Picasso created many cubist works but not this painting. Michelangelo is known for the Sistine Chapel ceiling and the statue of David.
Q3. General Knowledge
What is the currency of Japan?
A. Won
B. Yuan
C. Yen
D. Ringgit
Correct Answer: C
The Yen is the official currency of Japan. The Won is used in South Korea and North Korea, the Yuan is the currency of China, and the Ringgit is used in Malaysia.
Q4. English
Identify the synonym of the word 'Pernicious'.
A. Harmless
B. Beneficial
C. Destructive
D. Useful
Correct Answer: C
'Pernicious' means having a harmful effect, especially in a gradual or subtle way. 'Destructive' aligns with this meaning. 'Harmless', 'beneficial', and 'useful' are antonyms.
Q5. English
Identify the synonym of the word 'Obstinate'.
A. Flexible
B. Reasonable
C. Stubborn
D. Compliant
Correct Answer: C
'Obstinate' means unwilling to change one's opinion or course of action; 'Stubborn' is a direct synonym. 'Flexible', 'Reasonable', and 'Compliant' describe someone who is adaptable or willing to change, which are antonyms.
Q6. General Knowledge
Which planet in our solar system has the longest day (rotation period)?
A. Mars
B. Jupiter
C. Uranus
D. Venus
Correct Answer: D
Venus has a rotation period of approximately 243 Earth days, which is longer than its year and longer than any other planet's day. Mars rotates in about 24.6 hours, Jupiter in about 10 hours, and Uranus in about 17 hours. Thus, Venus holds the record for the longest day among all planets.
Q7. English
Identify the part of speech for the word 'Beautifully'.
A. Noun
B. Adjective
C. Adverb
D. Verb
Correct Answer: C
'Beautifully' describes how an action is performed (e.g., danced beautifully), making it an adverb. 'Noun', 'Adjective', and 'Verb' are incorrect grammatical categories for this word ending in '-ly'.
Q8. English
Identify the word that is an adjective in the following list: 'The _____ dog barked loudly.'
A. barking
B. loudly
C. dog
D. bone
Correct Answer: A
'Barking' functions as an adjective here (participle) modifying 'dog'. 'Loudly' is an adverb, 'dog' is a noun, and 'bone' is a noun. Note: In standard grammar, participles act as adjectives.
Q9. General Knowledge
Who painted the iconic work 'Mona Lisa'?
A. Vincent van Gogh
B. Pablo Picasso
C. Leonardo da Vinci
D. Michelangelo
Correct Answer: C
Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa during the Italian Renaissance. Vincent van Gogh is famous for 'Starry Night'. Pablo Picasso was a key figure in Cubism. Michelangelo is renowned for sculptures like the David and the Sistine Chapel ceiling.
Q10. General Knowledge
Which of the following is the capital city of France?
A. Berlin
B. Madrid
C. Paris
D. Rome
Correct Answer: C
Paris is the capital and most populous city of France. Berlin is the capital of Germany, Madrid is the capital of Spain, and Rome is the capital of Italy.
Q11. General Knowledge
Which of the following rivers is known as the 'Sorrow of Bihar'?
A. Ganga
B. Kosi
C. Yamuna
D. Godavari
Correct Answer: B
The Kosi River is widely known as the 'Sorrow of Bihar' due to its frequent and devastating floods caused by shifting course and heavy sediment load. The Ganga is the longest river in India but not specifically called this. Yamuna flows through northern India, and Godavari is the largest river basin in peninsular India.
Q12. General Knowledge
In the Indian Parliament, which house has the power to initiate money bills?
A. Rajya Sabha
B. Lok Sabha
C. Supreme Court
D. State Assembly
Correct Answer: B
The Lok Sabha (House of the People) has the exclusive power to initiate money bills. The Rajya Sabha can only recommend amendments or reject them within a month but cannot originate such bills. The Supreme Court is the judiciary, and State Assemblies have their own legislative powers but not over national money bills.
Q13. English
Select the word that is a noun: 'The _____ of the story moved everyone to tears.'
A. ending
B. happily
C. sadly
D. quickly
Correct Answer: A
'Ending' refers to the conclusion or result, which is a noun and fits the sentence structure. 'Happily', 'sadly', and 'quickly' are adverbs describing manner, not the subject of the sentence.
Q14. General Knowledge
Which of the following is a renewable source of energy?
A. Coal
B. Crude Oil
C. Natural Gas
D. Solar Energy
Correct Answer: D
Solar energy is renewable as it is derived from the sun's radiation which is inexhaustible. Coal, Crude Oil, and Natural Gas are fossil fuels formed over millions of years and are non-renewable resources.
Q15. General Knowledge
The national animal of India is the:
A. Lion
B. Elephant
C. Bengal Tiger
D. Peacock
Correct Answer: C
The Bengal Tiger (Panthera tigris) was declared the National Animal of India in 2010 by the Prime Minister. The Lion represents Gujarat, the Elephant is a state animal of several states like Kerala and Karnataka, and the Peacock is the National Bird of India.
