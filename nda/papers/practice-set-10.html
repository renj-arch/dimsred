Q2. English
Choose the word that best completes the sentence: 'Despite the heavy rain, they _____ to the stadium.'
A. did not go
B. went
C. have gone
D. gone
Correct Answer: B
'Went' is the simple past tense verb needed here. The clause 'Despite the heavy rain' suggests an action occurred regardless of conditions. 'Did not go' contradicts the implied success of reaching the destination usually tested in such contexts unless negation is intended, but 'went' is the direct completion. 'Have gone' requires 'have', and 'gone' cannot stand alone as a verb.
Q3. English
Convert the following Direct Speech into Indirect Speech: He said, "I am writing a letter now."
A. He said that he is writing a letter now.
B. He said that he was writing a letter then.
C. He said that he wrote a letter then.
D. He said that he has been writing a letter now.
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Reporting verb 'said' (Past) triggers backshifting. Present Continuous ('am writing') becomes Past Continuous ('was writing'). 'Now' changes to 'then'. Pronoun 'I' changes to 'he' (subject of reporting verb). Full Solution: Direct: He said, "I am writing a letter now." Indirect: Conjunction 'that' added. Pronoun 'I' -> 'he'. Verb 'am writing' (Pres. Cont.) -> 'was writing' (Past Cont.). Adverb 'now' -> 'then'. Result: He said that he was writing a letter then.

Correct Answer: A

Q14. General Knowledge
Which of the following physical quantities is a scalar quantity?
A. Force
B. Velocity
C. Displacement
D. Work
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Remember 'Work' is energy transferred (dot product of Force and Displacement); dot product yields a scalar. Cross product yields vector (Torque). Step-by-step: Force, Velocity, and Displacement all have both magnitude and direction, making them vector quantities. Work done is defined as the dot product of Force and Displacement vectors (W = F·s = |F||s|cosθ). The dot product of two vectors results in a scalar quantity (magnitude only). Therefore, Work is a scalar.
Q15. English
Change the following sentence into Passive Voice: "The committee will submit the report by Friday."
A. The report would be submitted by the committee by Friday.
B. The report will have been submitted by the committee by Friday.
C. The report is being submitted by the committee by Friday.
D. The report will be submitted by the committee by Friday.
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Preserve the tense. Active Future Indefinite (will + V1) converts to Passive Future Indefinite (will be + V3). Object 'report' becomes subject. Add 'by' + agent 'committee'. Full Solution: Active: Subject (Committee) + will submit (Future Indefinite) + Object (Report). Passive: Subject (Report) + will be submitted (Future Indefinite Passive) + by agent (by the committee). The time expression 'by Friday' remains unchanged. Options A, B, C incorrectly shift tense to Conditional, Future Perfect, and Present Continuous respectively.
