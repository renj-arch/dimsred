Q1. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of 7^{2023} + 3^{2024}?
A. 0
B. 2
C. 6
D. 4
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: The unit digit of powers of 7 repeats every 4 (7,9,3,1); 2023 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit 3. For 3, the cycle is (3,9,7,1); 2024 mod 4 = 0 → unit digit 1. Sum of unit digits = 3+1 = 4. Full steps: compute cycles, find remainders, add the resulting unit digits.
Research Team · ~4s · Modulo 10 Shortcut
  1. For a coprime to 10, a^4 ≡ 1 (mod 10)
  2. 7^2023 = 7^(4*505+3) ≡ 7^3 ≡ 343 ≡ 3 (mod 10)
  3. 3^2024 = 3^(4*506) ≡ 1 (mod 10)
  4. Sum ≡ 3+1=4 (mod 10) → unit digit 4 → D

If the sum of the interior angles of a polygon is 1260°, how many sides does the polygon have?

Correct Answer: C

The principal argument of (1 + i)^12 is:
A. 0
B. π/2
C. π
D. 3π/2
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: arg(1+i)=π/4; arg(z^n)=n·arg(z) mod 2π → 12·π/4 = 3π ≡ π (mod 2π). Full: (1+i) = √2 e^{iπ/4}; raising to 12 gives (√2)^{12} e^{i3π} = 64 e^{iπ}, principal argument = π.

Q5. Mathematics
The value of the definite integral ∫_0^1 (3x^2 + 2x + 1) dx is:
A. 3
B. 4
C. 5
D. 6
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Integrate each term using power rule: ∫3x^2 dx = x^3, ∫2x dx = x^2, ∫1 dx = x. Evaluate from 0 to 1: (1^3+1^2+1) - 0 = 3. Full solution: The antiderivative is x^3 + x^2 + x. Substituting the limits gives 1+1+1 = 3.

Q6. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a cricket bat with a marked price of ₹5,000. He offers two alternatives: Scheme A: 15% discount followed by a further 10% discount on the reduced price. Scheme B: A single flat discount of x%. To make the final selling price identical in both schemes, what is the value of x?
A. 25%
B. 23.5%
C. 22.5%
D. 24%
Correct Answer: B
Discount A = 15%. Remaining = 85%. Discount B = 10%. Remaining = 90%. Combined Remaining = 85% x 90% = 0.85 x 0.90 = 0.765. Total Discount = 1 - 0.765 = 0.235. x = 23.5%.

If α and β are the roots of the quadratic equation 2x² - 5x + 3 = 0, what is the value of α² + β²?
A. 17/4
B. 13/4
C. 19/4
D. 21/4
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use Vieta's formulas: α+β = 5/2, αβ = 3/2. Then α²+β² = (α+β)² - 2αβ = (25/4) - 3 = 13/4. Full: Sum of roots = -b/a = 5/2, product = c/a = 3/2. Compute (5/2)² - 2*(3/2) = 25/4 - 3 = 13/4.
Research Team · ~4s · Discriminant Shortcut
  1. Formula: α²+β² = (b²-2ac)/a²
  2. Plug a=2,b=-5,c=3: (25-12)/4 = 13/4

Find the distance between the points (2, 3) and (5, 7).

B. 6
C. 5
D. 7
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Δx = 3, Δy = 4 → 3-4-5 right triangle → distance = 5. Full: distance = √((5-2)² + (7-3)²) = √(9+16) = √25 = 5.
Q13. Mathematics
A bank offers a special 'Festival Savings Scheme' where the interest rate for the first year is 5% per annum on the principal. However, the interest earned in the first year is added to the principal at the end of the year. If a customer deposits ₹10,000 and closes the account after exactly one year, what is the total amount received?
A. ₹10,500
B. ₹10,525
C. ₹10,600
D. ₹10,400
Correct Answer: A
Simple Interest = (P * R * T) / 100. Substituting values: P = 10000, R = 5%, T = 1 year. SI = (10000 * 5 * 1) / 100. SI = 500. Total Amount = Principal + Interest = 10000 + 500 = 10500.
Q14. Mathematics
A retailer marks up a commodity by 25% above its cost price. He then offers a discount of x% on this marked price. If the final selling price equals the original cost price plus half of the initial profit he would have made had there been no discount, what is the value of x?
A. 10
B. 12
C. 15
D. 20
Correct Answer: A
Let Cost Price (CP) = Rs. 100. Initial Profit (if no discount) = Marked Price - CP = 125 - 100 = 25. Half of this initial profit = 25 / 2 = 12.5. Target Selling Price = CP + Half Initial Profit = 100 + 12.5 = 112.5. Marked Price (MP) = 125. Discount Amount = MP - SP = 125 - 112.5 = 12.5. Discount Percentage (x) = (Discount / MP) * 100 = (12.5 / 125) * 100 = 10%.
