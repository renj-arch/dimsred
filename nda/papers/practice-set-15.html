Q1. Mathematics
The value of the definite integral <span class="math">∫₀^π/2 \frac{\sin x}{\sin x + \cos x} dx</span> is:
A. π/2
B. π/4
C. π
D. π/8
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use King's Property <span class="math">I = ∫_a^b f(x)dx = ∫_a^b f(a+b-x)dx</span>. Here <span class="math">f(π/2 - x) = \cos x/(\sin x + \cos x)</span>. Add original <span class="math">I</span> and transformed <span class="math">I</span>: <span class="math">2I = ∫_0^{\pi/2} 1 dx = π/2 ⇒ I = π/4</span>. Full Solution: Let <span class="math">I = ∫_0^{\pi/2} \frac{\sin x}{\sin x + \cos x} dx</span>. Applying the property, <span class="math">I = ∫_0^{\pi/2} \frac{\sin(\pi/2-x)}{\sin(\pi/2-x)+\cos(\pi/2-x)} dx = ∫_0^{\pi/2} \frac{\cos x}{\cos x + \sin x} dx</span>. Adding the two expressions for <span class="math">I</span>: <span class="math">2I = ∫_0^{\pi/2} \frac{\sin x + \cos x}{\sin x + \cos x} dx = ∫_0^{\pi/2} 1 dx = [x]_0^{\pi/2} = \pi/2</span>. Therefore, <span class="math">I = \pi/4</span>.
Q2. Mathematics
lim_{x→0} (sin5x)/x equals:
A. 1
B. 0
C. 25
D. 5
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Standard limit lim_{x→0} sin(kx)/x = k. Here k = 5, so the limit is 5. Full solution: rewrite as 5·(sin5x)/(5x) and use lim_{u→0} sin u / u = 1.
Q3. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a cricket bat with a marked price of ₹5,000. He offers two alternatives: Scheme A: 15% discount followed by a further 10% discount on the reduced price. Scheme B: A single flat discount of x%. To make the final selling price identical in both schemes, what is the value of x?
A. 25%
B. 23.5%
C. 22.5%
D. 24%
Correct Answer: B
Discount A = 15%. Remaining = 85%. Discount B = 10%. Remaining = 90%. Combined Remaining = 85% x 90% = 0.85 x 0.90 = 0.765. Total Discount = 1 - 0.765 = 0.235. x = 23.5%.
Q4. Mathematics
lim_{x→0} (e^x - 1 - x)/x^2 equals:
A. 0
B. 1/2
C. 1
D. ∞
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use Maclaurin series e^x = 1 + x + x^2/2 + x^3/6 + …; then numerator ≈ x^2/2, so limit = 1/2. Full: e^x = 1 + x + x^2/2 + o(x^2). Substituting, (e^x - 1 - x)/x^2 = (x^2/2 + o(x^2))/x^2 → 1/2 as x→0.
Q5. Mathematics
A bank offers a special 'Festival Savings Scheme' where the interest rate for the first year is 5% per annum on the principal. However, the interest earned in the first year is added to the principal at the end of the year. If a customer deposits ₹10,000 and closes the account after exactly one year, what is the total amount received?
A. ₹10,500
B. ₹10,525
C. ₹10,600
D. ₹10,400
Correct Answer: A
Simple Interest = (P * R * T) / 100. Substituting values: P = 10000, R = 5%, T = 1 year. SI = (10000 * 5 * 1) / 100. SI = 500. Total Amount = Principal + Interest = 10000 + 500 = 10500.
Q6. Mathematics
A retailer marks up a commodity by 25% above its cost price. He then offers a discount of x% on this marked price. If the final selling price equals the original cost price plus half of the initial profit he would have made had there been no discount, what is the value of x?
A. 10
B. 12
C. 15
D. 20
Correct Answer: A
Let Cost Price (CP) = Rs. 100. Initial Profit (if no discount) = Marked Price - CP = 125 - 100 = 25. Half of this initial profit = 25 / 2 = 12.5. Target Selling Price = CP + Half Initial Profit = 100 + 12.5 = 112.5. Marked Price (MP) = 125. Discount Amount = MP - SP = 125 - 112.5 = 12.5. Discount Percentage (x) = (Discount / MP) * 100 = (12.5 / 125) * 100 = 10%.
Q7. Mathematics
The value of the definite integral ∫₀^{π/2} sin² x dx is:
A. π/2
B. π/8
C. π/6
D. π/4
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use symmetry ∫₀^{π/2} sin²x dx = ∫₀^{π/2} cos²x dx. Adding gives ∫₀^{π/2} 1 dx = π/2, so each integral = π/4. Full solution: Integrate sin²x = (1-cos2x)/2 from 0 to π/2 → [x/2 - sin2x/4]₀^{π/2} = π/4.

A. 20/9
B. 10/3

Correct Answer: D

Q10. Mathematics
A suburban train covers a distance of 120 km in 2 hours at its scheduled speed. On the return journey, due to signal disruptions at exactly 5 platforms encountered, the train incurs a fixed 'delay penalty' of 4 minutes for every platform passed. If the train maintains the exact same average speed between these platforms as it did on the outbound journey, how much total time is required for the entire return trip?
A. 2 hours
B. 2 hours 10 minutes
C. 2 hours 20 minutes
D. 2 hours 30 minutes
Correct Answer: C
1. Outbound average speed = 120 km / 2 hrs = 60 km/hr. 2. Return journey distance is also 120 km. 3. Ideal time for return trip at 60 km/hr = 120/60 = 2 hours (120 mins). 4. Delay penalty = 4 minutes per platform * 5 platforms = 20 minutes. 5. Total time = Ideal time + Penalty = 120 mins + 20 mins = 140 mins.
Q11. Mathematics
Two fair dice are rolled. The probability that the sum of the numbers is 9 is:
A. 1/6
B. 1/9
C. 1/12
D. 1/18
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Total outcomes = 36. Favorable pairs for sum 9: (3,6),(4,5),(5,4),(6,3) → 4 outcomes. Probability = 4/36 = 1/9. Full: List all 36 equally likely outcomes, count those with sum 9, divide by 36.
Q12. Mathematics
If A = [[2,1,1],[1,2,1],[1,1,2]], what is det(A)?
A. 0
B. 4
C. 2
D. 8
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a 3×3 matrix with diagonal a and off-diagonal b, det = (a-b)^2 (a+2b). Here a=2, b=1 → (1)^2 × 4 = 4. Full solution: apply the formula or perform row operations to obtain an upper‑triangular matrix and multiply the diagonal entries.
Q13. Mathematics
The modulus of (1 + i)^8 is:
A. 8
B. 32
C. 16
D. 64
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: |1+i| = √2, so |(1+i)^8| = (√2)^8 = 2^4 = 16. Full solution: Compute modulus of base: √(1^2+1^2)=√2; raise to power 8: (√2)^8 = (2^{1/2})^8 = 2^4 = 16.

Q15. Mathematics
The value of lim_{x→0} (1 - cos x)/x^2 is:
A. 0
B. 1/2
C. 1
D. 2
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the standard limit (1 - cos x) ~ x^2/2 as x→0, so the ratio tends to 1/2. Full solution: Apply the series expansion cos x = 1 - x^2/2 + x^4/24 - …; then (1 - cos x)/x^2 = (x^2/2 - x^4/24 + …)/x^2 → 1/2.
