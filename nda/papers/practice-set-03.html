Q1. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a cricket bat with a marked price of ₹5,000. He offers two alternatives: Scheme A: 15% discount followed by a further 10% discount on the reduced price. Scheme B: A single flat discount of x%. To make the final selling price identical in both schemes, what is the value of x?
A. 25%
B. 23.5%
C. 22.5%
D. 24%
Correct Answer: B
Discount A = 15%. Remaining = 85%. Discount B = 10%. Remaining = 90%. Combined Remaining = 85% x 90% = 0.85 x 0.90 = 0.765. Total Discount = 1 - 0.765 = 0.235. x = 23.5%.
Q2. Mathematics
A bank offers a special 'Festival Savings Scheme' where the interest rate for the first year is 5% per annum on the principal. However, the interest earned in the first year is added to the principal at the end of the year. If a customer deposits ₹10,000 and closes the account after exactly one year, what is the total amount received?
A. ₹10,500
B. ₹10,525
C. ₹10,600
D. ₹10,400
Correct Answer: A
Simple Interest = (P * R * T) / 100. Substituting values: P = 10000, R = 5%, T = 1 year. SI = (10000 * 5 * 1) / 100. SI = 500. Total Amount = Principal + Interest = 10000 + 500 = 10500.
Q3. Mathematics
The probability of getting at least one head in two tosses of a fair coin is:
A. 1/4
B. 1/2
C. 3/4
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
Total outcomes = 4 (HH, HT, TH, TT). Favorable for 'at least one head' are HH, HT, TH (3 outcomes). Probability = 3/4. Option A is P(TT). Option B is P(exactly one head).

  2. Notice only one combination (TT) fails the 'at least one head' condition.
  3. Conclude the probability is 3 out of 4 immediately.
  4. Select option C representing 3/4.

Q5. Mathematics
The area of a triangle with vertices at $(1, 2)$, $(4, 5)$, and $(7, 3)$ is:
A. $6.5$ square units
B. $7.5$ square units
C. $8.0$ square units
D. $9.5$ square units
Correct Answer: B
Using the determinant formula Area $= rac{1}{2} |x_1(y_2-y_3) + x_2(y_3-y_1) + x_3(y_1-y_2)|$, substituting the coordinates yields $ rac{1}{2} |1(5-3) + 4(3-2) + 7(2-5)| = rac{1}{2} |2 + 4 - 21| = rac{1}{2} |-15| = 7.5$. The other options result from calculation errors or incorrect coordinate substitutions.
Research Team · ~5s · Shoelace Formula
  1. List vertices in order: (1,2), (4,5), (7,3).
  2. Apply formula: Area = 0.5 * |(x1y2 + x2y3 + x3y1) - (y1x2 + y2x3 + y3x1)|.
  3. Substitute values: 0.5 * |(1*5 + 4*3 + 7*2) - (2*4 + 5*7 + 3*1)|.
  4. Calculate sums: x-sum = 31, y-sum = 46. Difference is 15.
  5. Multiply by 0.5: Result is 7.5, matching option B.
Q6. Mathematics
A bag contains 4 white balls and 6 black balls. If two balls are drawn at random without replacement, what is the probability that both balls are of the same color?
A. 1/5
B. 7/15
C. 8/15
D. 3/10
Correct Answer: C
Total ways to draw 2 balls from 10 is C(10,2) = 45. Ways to draw 2 white balls is C(4,2) = 6. Ways to draw 2 black balls is C(6,2) = 15. Total favorable outcomes = 6 + 15 = 21. Probability = 21/45 = 7/15. Option A (1/5) and D (3/10) correspond to partial calculations or incorrect combinations.
Q7. Mathematics
A retailer marks up a commodity by 25% above its cost price. He then offers a discount of x% on this marked price. If the final selling price equals the original cost price plus half of the initial profit he would have made had there been no discount, what is the value of x?
A. 10
B. 12
C. 15
D. 20
Correct Answer: A
Let Cost Price (CP) = Rs. 100. Initial Profit (if no discount) = Marked Price - CP = 125 - 100 = 25. Half of this initial profit = 25 / 2 = 12.5. Target Selling Price = CP + Half Initial Profit = 100 + 12.5 = 112.5. Marked Price (MP) = 125. Discount Amount = MP - SP = 125 - 112.5 = 12.5. Discount Percentage (x) = (Discount / MP) * 100 = (12.5 / 125) * 100 = 10%.
Q8. Mathematics
In a triangle ABC, if angle A = 90 degrees, b = 3, and c = 4, then the length of side a is:
A. 5
B. 6
C. 7
D. 8
Correct Answer: A
By Pythagoras theorem in a right-angled triangle, a^2 = b^2 + c^2. So a^2 = 3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25. Thus a = 5. Options 6, 7, 8 are incorrect as they do not satisfy the theorem.
Research Team · ~2s · Pythagorean Recall
  1. Identify angle A=90 makes it a right triangle.
  2. Recall standard Pythagorean triple (3, 4, 5).
  3. Match given sides b=3 and c=4 to the pair.
  4. Conclude hypotenuse a is 5.
Q9. Mathematics
A continuous random variable X has a probability density function f(x) = kx² for 0 ≤ x ≤ 2 and 0 otherwise. Find the value of k and calculate P(X > 1).
A. k=3/8, P(X>1)=5/8
B. k=3/4, P(X>1)=7/8
C. k=1/2, P(X>1)=3/4
D. k=3/8, P(X>1)=3/8
Correct Answer: A
For f(x) to be a valid PDF, the integral from 0 to 2 of kx² dx must equal 1. ∫₀² kx² dx = [kx³/3]₀² = 8k/3 = 1 => k = 3/8. Then P(X > 1) = ∫₁² (3/8)x² dx = (3/8)[x³/3]₁² = (1/8)(8 - 1) = 7/8. Option B has the wrong k, Option C has both wrong, and Option D has the correct k but wrong probability.
Q10. Mathematics
The value of the integral ∫(from 0 to π) sin(x) dx is:
A. 0
B. 1
C. 2
D. π
Correct Answer: C
The antiderivative of sin(x) is -cos(x). Evaluating from 0 to π gives [-cos(π)] - [-cos(0)] = [-(-1)] - [-1] = 1 + 1 = 2. Option A (0) is the result for sin² or integral over full period 0 to 2π. Option B (1) is half the value. Option D (π) is the area of a semicircle radius 1, not sine wave area.
Research Team · ~3s · Antiderivative
  1. Recall integral of sin(x) is -cos(x).
  2. Evaluate at limits: -cos(π) - (-cos(0)).
  3. Simplify: -(-1) + 1 = 2.
  4. Select option C.
Q11. Mathematics
If a circle of radius r has a chord subtending a right angle at the center, what is the length of the minor arc corresponding to this chord?
A. πr/2
B. πr
C. πr/4
D. 3πr/2
Correct Answer: A
The angle subtended at the center is 90° or π/2 radians. The length of an arc is given by L = rθ, where θ is in radians. Substituting θ = π/2, we get L = r(π/2) = πr/2. Option A is correct. Option B corresponds to a semi-circle (180°), Option C is too small, and Option D corresponds to 270°.
Research Team · ~3s · Direct Formula
  1. Identify central angle θ as π/2 radians (90 degrees).
  2. Apply arc length formula s = r * θ.
  3. Substitute θ: s = r * (π/2) to get πr/2
Q12. Mathematics
The variance of the data set {4, 6, 8, 10, 12} is multiplied by what factor if each number is increased by 5 and then divided by 2?
A. 1/4
B. 1/2
C. 1
D. 4
Correct Answer: A
Adding a constant (5) to every observation does not change the variance. Dividing every observation by a constant (2) scales the variance by the square of that constant, i.e., 1/2² = 1/4. Options B and C incorrectly assume linear scaling or no change, while D is the inverse operation.
Research Team · ~3s · Linear Transform Rule
  1. Recall variance scales by $k^2$ when data is transformed to $ax+b$.
  2. Identify transformation: add 5 (shift, no effect) then divide by 2 ($a=1/2$).
  3. Calculate factor: $(1/2)^2 = 1/4$.
  4. Select option A.

Q14. Mathematics
If the probability of rain on any given day is 0.3, what is the probability that it will NOT rain on a specific day?
A. 0.3
B. 0.5
C. 0.7
D. 1.0
Correct Answer: C
The sum of probabilities of all possible outcomes for an event is 1. The complement of 'it rains' is 'it does not rain'. Thus, P(not rain) = 1 - P(rain) = 1 - 0.3 = 0.7. Option A repeats the probability of rain; Options B and D are mathematically incorrect for this specific subtraction.
Research Team · ~2s · Complement Rule
  1. Identify probability of rain: 0.3.
  2. Calculate complement by subtracting from 1: 1 - 0.3 = 0.7.
  3. Select option matching result: C
Q15. Mathematics
A solid cylinder has radius r and height h. If both dimensions are doubled, the new volume is how many times the original volume?
A. 2 times
B. 4 times
C. 8 times
D. 16 times
Correct Answer: C
Volume V = πr²h. New volume V' = π(2r)²(2h) = π(4r²)(2h) = 8πr²h. Thus, the new volume is 8 times the original. Options A and B underestimate the cubic scaling effect, while D overestimates it.
Research Team · ~3s · Dimension Scaling
  1. Recall volume formula V = πr²h.
  2. Identify scaling factor k=2 for both r and h.
  3. Calculate new volume factor as k² × k = 2³ = 8.
