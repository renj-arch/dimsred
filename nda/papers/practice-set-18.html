Correct Answer: B

Q4. General Knowledge
Deficiency of which vitamin specifically leads to impaired blood clotting and increased bleeding tendency?
A. Vitamin D
B. Vitamin E
C. Vitamin A
D. Vitamin K
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Mnemonic **'K** for **K**oagulation' (German spelling). Vitamin K is essential for gamma-carboxylation of clotting factors II, VII, IX, X. Eliminate D (Calcium/Bones), E (Antioxidant/Neuromuscular), A (Vision/Epithelial). Step-by-step: Vitamin K (Phylloquinone/Menaquinone) acts as a cofactor for the enzyme gamma-glutamyl carboxylase, which modifies glutamate residues on clotting factors to bind Calcium ions. Without it, factors remain inactive, causing hemorrhagic disease. Newborns are routinely given Vitamin K prophylaxis.
Q5. English
Fill in the blank with the most appropriate word: "She has been working here ______ 2015."
A. since
B. for
C. from
D. by
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: "Since" is used with a specific point in time (2015), while "for" is used with a duration. The other options do not fit the present perfect continuous structure. Full solution: The sentence uses present perfect continuous ("has been working") to indicate an action started at a point in the past and continuing. The correct preposition for a starting point is "since". "For" would require a period (e.g., "for eight years"), and "from"/"by" are ungrammatical here.

Q11. General Knowledge
Match the following Agni missile variants with their correct range classification: 1. Agni-I 2. Agni-III 3. Agni-V Range Classes: (a) Short Range Ballistic Missile (SRBM) (b) Intermediate Range Ballistic Missile (IRBM) (c) Intercontinental Ballistic Missile (ICBM)
A. 1-a, 2-b, 3-c
B. 1-b, 2-a, 3-c
C. 1-a, 2-c, 3-b
D. 1-b, 2-c, 3-a
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Range increases with the missile number (I < II < III < IV < V). Classification thresholds: SRBM < 1,000 km; IRBM 3,000–5,500 km; ICBM > 5,500 km. Agni-I (~700–1,200 km) fits SRBM (Option a). Agni-III (~3,500–5,000 km) fits IRBM (Option b). Agni-V (~5,000–8,000+ km) fits ICBM (Option c). Sequence: 1-a, 2-b, 3-c. Full Solution: The Agni series follows a progressive range enhancement. Agni-I is a single-stage, solid-fuel, road/rail mobile missile with a range of 700–1,200 km, classifying it as an SRBM. Agni-III is a two-stage, solid-fuel IRBM with a range of 3,000–5,000 km. Agni-V is a three-stage, solid-fuel, canisterised ICBM with a proven range exceeding 5,000 km (tested up to 7,000+ km), placing it firmly in the ICBM category. Therefore, the correct matching is 1-a, 2-b, 3-c.

Q13. General Knowledge
Which of the following was explicitly REJECTED by the Cabinet Mission Plan (1946) during its negotiations with Indian political leaders?
A. Grouping of provincial assemblies into Sections A, B, and C
B. Formation of a Constituent Assembly elected by Provincial Assemblies
C. The demand for a separate sovereign state of Pakistan
D. A three-tier federal structure with a weak Centre controlling only Defence, Foreign Affairs, and Communications
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The Cabinet Mission (May 1946) was the *last major attempt* to keep India united. Its defining feature was the **rejection of Partition/Pakistan**. The acceptance of Pakistan happened over a year later via the Mountbatten Plan (June 3, 1947). Options A, B, and D were core *recommendations* of the Mission. Full Solution: The Cabinet Mission Plan, announced on 16 May 1946, proposed a united India with a federal structure. It rejected the Muslim League's demand for a separate sovereign Pakistan, arguing that it would not solve the communal problem (large non-Muslim minorities would remain in Pakistan and vice versa) and would entail economic/administrative dislocation. Instead, it proposed: (1) A Union of India controlling Defence, Foreign Affairs, Communications (Option D); (2) Grouping of existing provinces into three Sections (A, B, C) (Option A); (3) A Constituent Assembly elected by Provincial Legislatures to frame the Constitution (Option B). The Mission's rejection of Pakistan is a standard historical fact distinguishing it from the subsequent Mountbatten Plan.
Q14. General Knowledge
The 'Basic Structure' doctrine of the Indian Constitution, which limits Parliament's power to amend the Constitution, was first propounded by the Supreme Court in which landmark case?
A. Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973)
B. Golaknath v. State of Punjab (1967)
C. Minerva Mills v. Union of India (1980)
D. Shankari Prasad v. Union of India (1951)
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (Option Elimination): Use chronological logic. Shankari Prasad (1951) and Golaknath (1967) pre-date the doctrine's formulation; they dealt with whether Fundamental Rights are amendable. Minerva Mills (1980) applied the doctrine to strike down parts of the 42nd Amendment. Only Kesavananda Bharati (1973) fits as the originating 13-judge bench verdict. Step-by-step: The doctrine evolved through conflict between Parliament and Judiciary. Early cases (Shankari Prasad, Sajjan Singh) upheld absolute amendment power. Golaknath (1967) reversed this, making FRs unamendable. The 24th Amendment nullified Golaknath. In Kesavananda Bharati (1973), the SC ruled Parliament can amend any article but cannot alter the 'Basic Structure' (supremacy of Constitution, republican/democratic form, secularism, separation of powers, federalism). Minerva Mills (1980) later reinforced this by striking down clauses removing judicial review.
