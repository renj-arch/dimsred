Q1. General Knowledge
What is the chemical symbol for the element Gold?
A. Ag
B. Au
C. Fe
D. Cu
Correct Answer: B
The chemical symbol for Gold is Au, derived from the Latin word 'Aurum'. Ag is the symbol for Silver. Fe stands for Iron (from Ferrum), and Cu stands for Copper.
Q2. English
Select the word that best completes the sentence: 'The teacher _____ the students to be punctual.'
A. advised
B. warned
C. taught
D. asked
Correct Answer: A
'Advised' fits best as it implies giving counsel or recommendation, which aligns with encouraging punctuality. While 'taught', 'warned', and 'asked' are possible, 'advised' is the most natural collocation for urging a behavioral change like punctuality without implying a threat ('warned') or just instruction ('taught').
Q3. English
Choose the option that best completes the sentence: 'She has been working here _____ 2019.'
A. since
B. for
C. until
D. by
Correct Answer: A
'Since' is used with a specific point in time (2019) to indicate an action starting then and continuing. 'For' requires a duration. 'Until' implies stopping at that time. 'By' indicates completion before a time.
Q4. English
Select the correctly spelt word.
A. Acommodate
B. Accomodate
C. Accommodate
D. Accommoddate
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Apply the double consonant rule for 'accommodate': prefix 'ad-' assimilates to 'ac-' before 'c' (double c), and 'com-' retains double 'm' before 'modate'. Only option C has both double 'c' and double 'm'. Full Solution: The word derives from Latin 'accommodare' (ad- + commodare). The prefix 'ad-' changes to 'ac-' before 'c', giving 'acc-'. The root 'commodare' starts with 'comm-'. Thus, the correct spelling is 'Accommodate' (double c, double m).
Q5. General Knowledge
The 'Akash' weapon system, indigenously developed by DRDO for the Indian Armed Forces, is classified as a:
A. Air-to-Air missile
B. Surface-to-Air missile
C. Anti-Tank Guided Missile
D. Submarine-launched Ballistic Missile
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Name association — **Akash** means **Sky**. It defends the sky/airspace from the ground. Eliminate Air-to-Air (Astra), Anti-Tank (Nag/Helina), SLBM (K-15/K-4). Step-by-step: Akash is a medium-range mobile Surface-to-Air Missile (SAM) system developed under the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP). It uses a Ramjet-rocket propulsion system, can engage multiple targets simultaneously (aircraft, helicopters, drones) up to 25-30 km range and 18 km altitude. It is operational with both the Indian Air Force and Indian Army.

B. Article 356
C. Article 360
D. Article 365
Correct Answer: B

Article 365 deals with the effect of failure to comply with directions given by the Union. Article 356 specifically empowers the President to assume state functions if governance cannot be carried on per the Constitution.

Q8. General Knowledge
The Battle of Buxar (1764) was fought between the British East India Company and the combined armies of which three Indian rulers?
A. Mir Jafar, Shuja-ud-Daulah, Shah Alam II
B. Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daulah, Shah Alam II
C. Siraj-ud-Daulah, Shah Alam II, Nizam of Hyderabad
D. Mir Qasim, Siraj-ud-Daulah, Shuja-ud-Daulah
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Eliminate options containing Siraj-ud-Daulah (died 1757, Battle of Plassey) or Mir Jafar (installed after Plassey, not fighting at Buxar). The trio at Buxar is the 'Q-S-S' combination: **Q**asim (Nawab of Bengal), **S**huja-ud-Daulah (Nawab of Awadh), **S**hah Alam II (Mughal Emperor). Step-by-step: Mir Qasim, the deposed Nawab of Bengal, allied with Shuja-ud-Daulah of Awadh and the Mughal Emperor Shah Alam II to expel the British. Their defeat by Hector Munro led to the Treaty of Allahabad (1765), granting Diwani rights to the Company.
Q9. General Knowledge
The Bering Strait separates which two major landmasses?
A. Asia and North America
B. Europe and Africa
C. Australia and Antarctica
D. South America and Antarctica
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: 'Bering' sounds like 'Bearing' -> Navigation -> Russia (Asia) to Alaska (North America). Eliminate others: Europe/Africa separated by Mediterranean/Gibraltar; Australia/Antarctica by Southern Ocean; South America/Antarctica by Drake Passage. Step-by-step: The Bering Strait is a Pacific strait between Russia (Chukchi Peninsula, Asia) and the USA (Seward Peninsula, Alaska, North America). It connects the Arctic Ocean (Chukchi Sea) with the Pacific Ocean (Bering Sea). It is named after Vitus Bering.
Q10. General Knowledge
Which Article of the Constitution of India guarantees the 'Protection of Life and Personal Liberty'?
A. Article 14
B. Article 19
C. Article 21
D. Article 32
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use the 'Golden Triangle' mnemonic (Articles 14, 19, 21). Article 14 = Equality before law; Article 19 = Six Freedoms; Article 21 = Life & Personal Liberty. Article 32 = Right to Constitutional Remedies (Heart & Soul). Step-by-step: Article 14 ensures equality. Article 19 protects freedoms like speech and movement. Article 21 explicitly states: 'No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law'. Article 32 provides the remedy to enforce these rights. Hence, Article 21 is the correct match.
Q11. English
Identify the synonym of the word 'Cacophony'.
A. Harmony
B. Melody
C. Noise
D. Silence
Correct Answer: C
'Cacophony' means a harsh, discordant mixture of sounds or noise. 'Noise' is the closest synonym among the choices. 'Harmony' and 'Melody' refer to pleasant sounds, while 'Silence' refers to the absence of sound, making them antonyms or unrelated concepts.
Q12. General Knowledge
In the context of Indian Polity, which Article of the Constitution empowers the Parliament to form a new State by separation of territory from any State or by uniting two or more States?
A. Article 2
B. Article 3
C. Article 4
D. Article 1
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the 'Territory vs State' distinction trick. Article 1 defines India as a Union of States and its territory. Article 2 deals with admission/establishment of *new* states from *outside* the Union (e.g., Sikkim). Article 3 deals with formation of new states *internal* to the Union (altering boundaries/names of existing states). Article 4 contains supplemental laws for Art 2/3. The question describes internal reorganization (separation/uniting existing states). Full Solution: Article 3 authorizes Parliament to form a new State by separation of territory from any State, by uniting two or more States, or by uniting any territory to a part of any State. Article 2 relates to acquiring foreign territory. Article 1 defines the Union. Article 4 provides for consequential amendments.

B. Lothal – Citadel and Lower Town division only

D. Rakhigarhi – Ploughed field evidence exclusively
Correct Answer: A

Rakhigarhi (Haryana) is the largest Harappan site. Thus, only Option A matches correctly.

Q15. General Knowledge
Which river is known as the 'Ganges of South India'?
A. Krishna
B. Godavari
C. Kaveri
D. Vaigai
Correct Answer: C
The Kaveri (or Cauvery) river is popularly referred to as the Ganges of South India due to its cultural and religious significance in the region. The Krishna and Godavari are major peninsular rivers but do not carry this specific epithet. The Vaigai is a smaller river flowing through Tamil Nadu.
