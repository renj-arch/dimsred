Q1. Mathematics
A fair die is rolled three times. The probability of getting at least one six is:
A. 125/216
B. 91/216
C. 1/6
D. 1/216
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: P(at least one 6) = 1 − P(no 6) = 1 − (5/6)³ = 1 − 125/216 = 91/216. Full: Probability of no six in one roll = 5/6; for three independent rolls = (5/6)³ = 125/216; complement gives 91/216.

Q3. Mathematics
The maximum value of f(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 + 4 on the interval [0, 3] is:
A. 0
B. 4
C. 2
D. 6
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: f'(x)=3x^2-6x=3x(x-2). Critical points x=0,2. Evaluate f(0)=4, f(2)=0, f(3)=4 ⇒ maximum = 4. Full: Check endpoints and critical points; f(0)=4, f(2)=8-12+4=0, f(3)=27-27+4=4. Hence max value is 4.

Q5. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a cricket bat with a marked price of ₹5,000. He offers two alternatives: Scheme A: 15% discount followed by a further 10% discount on the reduced price. Scheme B: A single flat discount of x%. To make the final selling price identical in both schemes, what is the value of x?
A. 25%
B. 23.5%
C. 22.5%
D. 24%
Correct Answer: B
Discount A = 15%. Remaining = 85%. Discount B = 10%. Remaining = 90%. Combined Remaining = 85% x 90% = 0.85 x 0.90 = 0.765. Total Discount = 1 - 0.765 = 0.235. x = 23.5%.
Q6. Mathematics
The sum of the first 15 even natural numbers is:
A. 210
B. 240
C. 270
D. 300
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Sum of first n even numbers = n(n+1). For n=15, 15×16=240. Full: Even numbers are 2,4,6,…,30. This is an AP with first term 2, last term 30, number of terms 15. Sum = n/2 (first+last) = 15/2 × 32 = 240.
Q7. Mathematics
A bank offers a special 'Festival Savings Scheme' where the interest rate for the first year is 5% per annum on the principal. However, the interest earned in the first year is added to the principal at the end of the year. If a customer deposits ₹10,000 and closes the account after exactly one year, what is the total amount received?
A. ₹10,500
B. ₹10,525
C. ₹10,600
D. ₹10,400
Correct Answer: A
Simple Interest = (P * R * T) / 100. Substituting values: P = 10000, R = 5%, T = 1 year. SI = (10000 * 5 * 1) / 100. SI = 500. Total Amount = Principal + Interest = 10000 + 500 = 10500.
Q8. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of 2^{100} + 5^{100}?
A. 1
B. 3
C. 5
D. 7
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The unit digit of powers of 2 repeats every 4 (2,4,8,6); 100 mod 4 = 0 so 2^100 ends in 6. Any power of 5 ends in 5. 6+5=11 → unit digit 1. Full: 2^1=2, 2^2=4, 2^3=8, 2^4=16→6, cycle length 4. 100÷4=25 remainder 0 → last digit 6. 5^n always ends in 5. Sum=…1.
Q9. Mathematics
The odds in favor of an event are 3:5. The probability of the event is:
A. 5/8
B. 3/5
C. 5/3
D. 3/8
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Odds in favor = P(event) : P(not event) = 3:5 → P(event) = 3/(3+5) = 3/8. Full solution: Let P = p, then p/(1-p) = 3/5 → 5p = 3 - 3p → 8p = 3 → p = 3/8.
Q10. Mathematics
A retailer marks up a commodity by 25% above its cost price. He then offers a discount of x% on this marked price. If the final selling price equals the original cost price plus half of the initial profit he would have made had there been no discount, what is the value of x?
A. 10
B. 12
C. 15
D. 20
Correct Answer: A
Let Cost Price (CP) = Rs. 100. Initial Profit (if no discount) = Marked Price - CP = 125 - 100 = 25. Half of this initial profit = 25 / 2 = 12.5. Target Selling Price = CP + Half Initial Profit = 100 + 12.5 = 112.5. Marked Price (MP) = 125. Discount Amount = MP - SP = 125 - 112.5 = 12.5. Discount Percentage (x) = (Discount / MP) * 100 = (12.5 / 125) * 100 = 10%.
Q11. Mathematics
If the sum of two numbers is 15 and their product is 56, what is the larger number?
A. 7
B. 8
C. 9
D. 10
Correct Answer: B
Let numbers be x and y. x+y=15, xy=56. They are roots of t² - 15t + 56 = 0. Factoring: (t-7)(t-8)=0. Roots are 7 and 8. The larger number is 8. Option A is the smaller number. Options C and D do not satisfy the product condition (9*6=54, 10*5=50).

Q13. Mathematics
How many diagonals does a convex polygon of 12 sides have?
A. 54
B. 48
C. 60
D. 66
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Number of diagonals = n(n-3)/2. For n=12: 12×9/2 = 54. Full steps: each vertex joins n-3 diagonals, total n(n-3) counts each twice, divide by 2.
Q14. Mathematics
A suburban train covers a distance of 120 km in 2 hours at its scheduled speed. On the return journey, due to signal disruptions at exactly 5 platforms encountered, the train incurs a fixed 'delay penalty' of 4 minutes for every platform passed. If the train maintains the exact same average speed between these platforms as it did on the outbound journey, how much total time is required for the entire return trip?
A. 2 hours
B. 2 hours 10 minutes
C. 2 hours 20 minutes
D. 2 hours 30 minutes
Correct Answer: C
1. Outbound average speed = 120 km / 2 hrs = 60 km/hr. 2. Return journey distance is also 120 km. 3. Ideal time for return trip at 60 km/hr = 120/60 = 2 hours (120 mins). 4. Delay penalty = 4 minutes per platform * 5 platforms = 20 minutes. 5. Total time = Ideal time + Penalty = 120 mins + 20 mins = 140 mins.
Q15. Mathematics
The value of sin 15° sin 75° is:
A. 1/4
B. 1/2
C. √3/4
D. 1/8
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: sin 75° = cos 15°. Product = sin 15° cos 15° = 1/2 sin 30° = 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/4. Full Solution: Using complementary angle identity, sin 75° = sin(90°-15°) = cos 15°. The expression becomes sin 15° cos 15°. Multiply and divide by 2: (1/2)(2 sin 15° cos 15°) = (1/2) sin 30° = (1/2)(1/2) = 1/4.
