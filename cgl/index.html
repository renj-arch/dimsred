About SSC CGL

Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL) exam is conducted annually for recruitment to Group B and Group C posts in government ministries. Tier 1 consists of 100 objective questions (200 marks, 60 minutes) across General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, and English Comprehension.
