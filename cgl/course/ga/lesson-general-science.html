Key Concepts

What is General Science?

General Science for SSC CGL covers basic concepts from Physics (laws of motion, work-energy, light, sound, electricity), Chemistry (atomic structure, chemical bonding, acids-bases, periodic table, common compounds), and Biology (cell biology, human body systems, plant biology, diseases). Questions are typically at the high school level.

Key Concepts

Physics: Newton's Laws of Motion, Work-Energy theorem, Gravitation (g = 9.8 m/s²), Sound (speed 340 m/s in air, echo, ultrasound), Light (reflection, refraction, lenses, mirrors, human eye, dispersion — rainbow), Electricity (Ohm's Law V=IR, series/parallel circuits, electric power P=VI).

Chemistry: Atomic structure (proton, neutron, electron), Periodic Table (groups 1-18, periods 1-7), Chemical bonding (ionic, covalent, metallic), Acids (pH < 7) and Bases (pH > 7), Common compounds (NaCl, H₂O, CO₂, CaCO₃, H₂SO₄, NH₃).

Biology: Cell (structure, organelles — nucleus, mitochondria, ribosomes, cell membrane), Human body systems (digestive, respiratory, circulatory, nervous, excretory), Vitamins (A-K — sources, deficiency diseases), Diseases (bacterial — typhoid, TB; viral — flu, COVID; vector-borne — malaria, dengue).

Solved Examples

Example 1
What is the SI unit of electric current?

a) Volt b) Ampere c) Ohm d) Watt
Solution: b) Ampere (A). Electric current is the rate of flow of charge (I = Q/t). SI unit: Ampere. 1 A = 1 C/s. Voltage is measured in Volts (V). Resistance in Ohms (Ω). Power in Watts (W).
Example 2
Which vitamin is produced by the human body when exposed to sunlight?

a) Vitamin A b) Vitamin B12 c) Vitamin C d) Vitamin D
Solution: d) Vitamin D (Cholecalciferol). The skin synthesizes Vitamin D when exposed to UV-B rays from sunlight. It is essential for calcium absorption and bone health. Deficiency causes rickets in children and osteomalacia in adults.

Shortcut Techniques

  • For Vitamin deficiency diseases: 'B-Beriberi, C-Scurvy, D-Rickets, K-Hemorrhage' — B for Beriberi (double B), C for Scurvy (sea voyages lacking citrus), D for Rickets (D for deformation), K for Koagulation (blood clotting).
  • For the electromagnetic spectrum: 'Raman's Microwaves, Infra-red Visible Ultra X-ray Gamma' — Radio, Microwave, Infrared, Visible, Ultraviolet, X-ray, Gamma (increasing frequency, decreasing wavelength).
  • For chemical formula of common compounds: Washing Soda (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O), Baking Soda (NaHCO₃), Bleaching Powder (CaOCl₂), Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O).
Pro Tip: Biology is the most asked section in General Science for SSC CGL (40% questions). Focus on human body systems (organs, functions, diseases) and vitamins. For Physics, focus on units, laws, and everyday applications. For Chemistry, focus on atomic number, valency, and common compounds.
