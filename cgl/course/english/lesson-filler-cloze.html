Fillers & Cloze Test

Fill in the blanks, cloze tests, and phrase replacement.
Single Fillers · Double Fillers · Cloze Test · Phrase Replacement

Learning Objectives

  • Choose correct words for fill-in-the-blanks
  • Solve cloze tests with contextual understanding
  • Replace phrases without changing meaning

Key Concepts

What are Fillers & Cloze Test?

Fillers (Fill in the Blanks) require you to choose the correct word or phrase to complete a sentence from given options. Cloze Test presents a passage with missing words (usually every 5th-7th word is blank) and you must select the most appropriate word for each blank from the choices provided. Both test vocabulary, grammar, and contextual understanding.

Key Concepts

1. Single Fillers (Single Blank): One word is missing from the sentence. Consider grammar first (what part of speech fits?), then meaning (which word makes logical sense in the context?). Check collocations (words that naturally go together — 'strong coffee' not 'powerful coffee', 'heavy rain' not 'strong rain').

2. Double Fillers (Two Blanks): Two words are missing. Check each blank independently, but ultimately both words must fit the sentence. Frequently the options pair words, so eliminating one bad choice in the first blank can remove multiple answer choices.

3. Cloze Test Strategy: Read the entire passage first (without filling blanks) to understand the theme and tone. The passage is usually from a newspaper editorial or informative article. Determine the part of speech needed for each blank. Eliminate clearly wrong options. Use context clues from surrounding sentences.

Solved Examples

Example 1 (Single Filler)
Fill in the blank:

The government's new policy aims to ___ economic growth.

a) hamper b) boost c) reduce d) ignore
Solution: b) boost. Government policies generally aim to 'boost' (promote/stimulate) economic growth. 'Hamper', 'reduce', and 'ignore' are negative and don't align with the positive intent implied by 'aims to'.
Example 2 (Cloze Test)
Passage: 'Climate change is one of the most (1) ___ challenges of our time. Rising temperatures have already (2) ___ severe weather patterns across the globe. Scientists (3) ___ that immediate action is necessary.'

1. a) trivial b) pressing c) minor d) outdated
2. a) reduced b) prevented c) disrupted d) stabilized
3. a) warn b) hope c) deny d) question
Solution: 1-b (pressing — urgent/serious), 2-c (disrupted — disturbed negatively), 3-a (warn — caution about danger). The passage has a serious tone about climate change, so all three blanks require words that convey urgency and negative impact.

Shortcut Techniques

  • For single fillers, read the sentence and predict your own word for the blank before looking at the options. This prevents you from being misled by wrong options.
  • For double fillers, check the easier blank first. Often, eliminating 2-3 options based on the first blank makes the answer obvious.
  • For cloze tests, use the 'POS method': Determine the Part of Speech needed for each blank (noun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition). This immediately eliminates grammatically wrong options.
Pro Tip: In cloze tests, pay attention to the tone of the passage (positive/negative/neutral) and the time frame (past/present/future). The answer must be consistent with both. Practice 2-3 cloze tests per week — they improve vocabulary, grammar, and contextual reading simultaneously.
