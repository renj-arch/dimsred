Key Concepts

What is Percentage?

Percentage means "per hundred" (parts out of 100). It is one of the most important topics in SSC CGL Tier 1 quant section, forming the basis for profit-loss, simple interest, and data interpretation questions.

Key Concepts

1. Basic Formula: Percentage = (Part / Whole) * 100. To convert a fraction to a percentage, multiply by 100. To convert a percentage to a fraction, divide by 100.

2. Fraction-Percentage Equivalents: Memorise these: 1/2 = 50%, 1/3 = 33.33%, 1/4 = 25%, 1/5 = 20%, 1/6 = 16.67%, 1/7 = 14.28%, 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/9 = 11.11%, 1/10 = 10%, 1/11 = 9.09%, 1/12 = 8.33%.

3. Percentage Change: If a value increases from A to B, % increase = ((B-A)/A) * 100. If it decreases, % decrease = ((A-B)/A) * 100.

4. Successive Percentage Change: If a number is changed by x% and then by y%, net change = x + y + (xy/100)%. A single discount equivalent to two successive discounts of x% and y% is: x + y - (xy/100).

Solved Examples

Example 1 (Basic)
What is 12.5% of 640?

a) 60 b) 70 c) 80 d) 90
Solution: c) 80. 12.5% = 1/8. So 1/8 of 640 = 640 / 8 = 80.
Example 2 (Successive Change)
A number is first increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net percentage change?

a) 0% b) 1% increase c) 1% decrease d) 2% decrease
Solution: c) 1% decrease. Net change = 10 + (-10) + (10*(-10))/100 = 0 - 1 = -1%.
Example 3 (Election)
In an election, candidate A got 60% of the total valid votes. If total votes were 50,000 and 10% of votes were invalid, how many valid votes did A get?

a) 24,000 b) 27,000 c) 30,000 d) 32,000
Solution: b) 27,000. Valid votes = 50,000 * 90% = 45,000. A votes = 60% of 45,000 = 27,000.

Shortcut Techniques

  • Use fraction equivalents instead of decimals for faster mental math. 12.5% = 1/8, 33.33% = 1/3.
  • For "x% of y = y% of x" — always switch to the easier side. 8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2.
  • For successive discounts, apply the formula directly rather than calculating step by step.
  • In population increase/decrease questions, multiply by factors: 10% increase = multiply by 1.1, 10% decrease = multiply by 0.9.
Pro Tip: For SSC CGL, mastering fraction-percentage equivalents up to 1/20 saves 30-40 seconds per question. Practice converting fractions to percentages in your head instantly.
