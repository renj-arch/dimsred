Profit, Loss & Discount

Marked price, discounts, successive discounts, and false weight problems.
CP/SP · Markup & Discount · Successive Discount · False Weight

Learning Objectives

  • Calculate profit/loss percentage accurately
  • Solve marked price and discount problems
  • Handle successive discounts and false weight

Key Concepts

What is Profit, Loss & Discount?

Profit and loss is a core commercial arithmetic topic that deals with buying and selling goods. Questions from this topic appear regularly in SSC CGL Tier 1 and involve cost price (CP), selling price (SP), profit percentage, loss percentage, discounts, and marked price (MP).

Key Concepts

1. Basic Formulas: Profit = SP - CP (when SP > CP). Loss = CP - SP (when CP > SP). Profit% = (Profit/CP) * 100. Loss% = (Loss/CP) * 100.

2. Discount: Discount is always calculated on the Marked Price (MP). Discount% = (Discount/MP) * 100. SP = MP - Discount. Alternatively, SP = MP * (100 - d%)/100.

3. Successive Discounts: If two discounts of x% and y% are offered, single equivalent discount = x + y - (xy/100). SP = MP * (100-x)/100 * (100-y)/100.

4. False Weight / Dishonest Shopkeeper: Profit% = [(True Weight - False Weight) / False Weight] * 100. If a shopkeeper sells at cost price but uses a false weight, his profit% is the percentage of the weight difference.

Solved Examples

Example 1 (Basic Profit)
A shopkeeper buys an article for Rs.800 and sells it for Rs.960. What is his profit percentage?

a) 15% b) 18% c) 20% d) 25%
Solution: c) 20%. Profit = 960 - 800 = 160. Profit% = (160/800) * 100 = 20%.
Example 2 (Discount)
The marked price of a shirt is Rs.1200. A 15% discount is offered. What is the selling price?

a) Rs.1000 b) Rs.1020 c) Rs.1050 d) Rs.1080
Solution: b) Rs.1020. Discount = 15% of 1200 = 180. SP = 1200 - 180 = 1020.
Example 3 (Dishonest Shopkeeper)
A shopkeeper sells rice at cost price but uses a weight of 900g instead of 1kg. What is his profit percentage?

a) 10% b) 11.11% c) 12.5% d) 9.09%
Solution: b) 11.11%. Profit% = (1000 - 900)/900 * 100 = 100/9 = 11.11%.

Shortcut Techniques

  • If CP and SP are both given, profit% can be found by dividing profit by CP and multiplying by 100.
  • For "if sold at Rs.x there is a loss/profit of p%" type questions, solve using CP as 100 units.
  • When two items are sold at the same price, one at x% profit and one at x% loss, there is always a net loss of (x^2/100)%.
  • For discount problems, use the formula: MP = SP * 100/(100 - d%) directly.
Pro Tip: SSC CGL frequently asks questions involving "cost price of x items = selling price of y items". Use the ratio method: Profit% = [(x - y)/y] * 100%.
