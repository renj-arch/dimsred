Q2. Quantitative Aptitude
Two vessels A and B contain spirit and water in the ratio 5:2 and 7:6 respectively. In what ratio should these mixtures be mixed to obtain a new mixture in vessel C containing spirit and water in the ratio 8:5?
A. 7:9
B. 9:7
C. 4:3
D. 3:4
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (Alligation Rule): Compare the fraction of spirit only. Spirit in A = 5/7, in B = 7/13, in C = 8/13. Convert to denominator 91: A = 65/91, B = 49/91, C = 56/91. Differences: (65-56)=9 and (56-49)=7. Required ratio A:B = 7:9 (inverse of differences). Full Method: Let the mixtures be mixed in ratio x:y. Equation: (5/7)x + (7/13)y = (8/13)(x+y). Solving: (5/7 - 8/13)x = (8/13 - 7/13)y -> (65-56)/91 x = 1/13 y -> 9x/91 = 7y/91 -> x/y = 7/9.
Q3. Quantitative Aptitude
From a point P, 13 cm away from the centre O of a circle of radius 5 cm, two tangents PQ and PR are drawn to the circle. What is the area of quadrilateral PQOR (in cm²)?
A. 30
B. 40
C. 60
D. 65
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Tangent length = √(d² - r²) = √(13² - 5²) = 12 cm (5-12-13 triplet). Area of quad = r × tangent length = 5 × 12 = 60 cm². Step-by-step: In right triangle PQO (radius ⟂ tangent), OQ = 5 cm (radius), OP = 13 cm. By Pythagoras, PQ = √(OP² - OQ²) = √(169 - 25) = √144 = 12 cm. Area of ΔPQO = ½ × OQ × PQ = ½ × 5 × 12 = 30 cm². Quadrilateral PQOR consists of two congruent triangles ΔPQO and ΔPRO. Total Area = 2 × 30 = 60 cm².
Q4. Quantitative Aptitude
A shopkeeper marks his goods 50% above the cost price. He allows two successive discounts of 20% and 10% on the marked price. What is his profit percentage?
A. 5% profit
B. 8% profit
C. 10% profit
D. 2% loss
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the successive percentage change multiplier: 1.50 (markup) × 0.80 (20% discount) × 0.90 (10% discount) = 1.08, indicating an 8% profit directly. Step-by-step: Let CP = ₹100. MP = ₹150. After 20% discount, price = ₹120. After further 10% discount, SP = ₹108. Profit = ₹8 on CP ₹100, so Profit% = 8%.
Q5. General Awareness
Under Article 360 of the Constitution of India, which of the following statements regarding Financial Emergency is correct?
A. It requires Parliamentary approval within one month of proclamation.
B. The salaries and allowances of the Judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts cannot be reduced during its operation.
C. A proclamation of Financial Emergency must be approved by both Houses of Parliament within two months.
D. It remains in force for a maximum period of three years unless revoked earlier.
Correct Answer: C
(a) Shortcut - Article Comparison Table: Memorize the trio: Art 352 (National Emergency) = 1 month approval; Art 356 (President's Rule) = 2 months approval, max 3 years; Art 360 (Financial Emergency) = 2 months approval, NO maximum duration. Option A (1 month) belongs to Art 352. Option D (3 years max) belongs to Art 356. Option B is factually wrong as Art 360 explicitly allows reduction of salaries of Judges. Option C is the only statement matching Art 360 provisions. (b) Step-by-step: Article 360(2) states the proclamation must be laid before each House of Parliament and ceases to operate at the expiration of two months unless approved by resolutions of both Houses. There is no fixed maximum duration under Art 360(3); it continues until revoked. Article 360(4)(b) empowers the President to direct reduction of salaries of all Union/State employees including Judges of SC/HCs.
Q6. Quantitative Aptitude
Find the unit digit of the expression \( (127)^{43} + (183)^{52} + (246)^{61} \).
A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 0
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits. For 7: cycle 4 (7,9,3,1), 43%4=3 → 3. For 3: cycle 4 (3,9,7,1), 52%4=0 → 1. For 6: always 6. Sum = 3+1+6=10 → unit digit 0. Step-by-step: Unit digit of 127^43 = unit digit of 7^43. Cyclicity of 7 is 4. 43 = 4×10 + 3 → 7^3 = 343 → unit digit 3. Unit digit of 183^52 = unit digit of 3^52. Cyclicity of 3 is 4. 52 = 4×13 → 3^4 = 81 → unit digit 1. Unit digit of 246^61 = 6 (any power of 6 ends in 6). Sum of unit digits = 3 + 1 + 6 = 10. Required unit digit = 0.
Q7. English Language
Choose the word that best substitutes the given group of words: One who walks in sleep
A. Somnambulist
B. Insomniac
C. Somniloquist
D. Philatelist
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use root words. 'Somnus' (Sleep) + 'Ambulare' (Walk) = Sleepwalker. Eliminate 'Insomniac' (In+Somnus = No sleep), 'Somniloquist' (Loqui = Speak), 'Philatelist' (Stamps). Step-by-step: A Somnambulist is a person who walks while sleeping. An Insomniac suffers from inability to sleep. A Somniloquist talks in sleep. A Philatelist collects stamps. Only 'Somnambulist' matches the definition 'One who walks in sleep'.

Q10. English Language
Convert the following sentence into Indirect Speech: He said to me, "When did you arrive?"
A. He asked me when I had arrived.
B. He asked me when did I arrive.
C. He asked me when I have arrived.
D. He said me when I arrived.
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Eliminate options with incorrect reporting verb ('said' instead of 'asked' in D), interrogative word order ('did I' in B), or wrong tense ('have arrived' in C). The correct structure is Reporting Verb (asked) + Wh-word (when) + Subject (I) + Past Perfect Verb (had arrived). Full Solution: 1. Reporting verb 'said to' changes to 'asked' for interrogative sentences. 2. Conjunction is the 'Wh-' word itself ('when'). 3. Pronoun 'you' changes to 'I' (subject of reported speech). 4. Tense changes: Simple Past ('did arrive') → Past Perfect ('had arrived'). 5. Interrogative form changes to Assertive form (Subject + Verb). Thus: 'He asked me when I had arrived.'
Q11. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Find the missing term in the following series: 2, 12, 36, 80, 150, ?
A. 250
B. 252
C. 254
D. 256
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (Method of Differences): Calculate successive differences until constant. 1std iff: 10, 24, 44, 70. 2nd diff: 14, 20, 26. 3rd diff: 6, 6 (Constant). Since 3rd diff is constant, series is cubic. Next 2nd diff = 26+6=32. Next 1std iff = 70+32=102. Missing term = 150+102=252. Full: General term is n³ + n². For n=1: 1+1=2; n=2: 8+4=12; n=3: 27+9=36; n=4: 64+16=80; n=5: 125+25=150; n=6: 216+36=252.

In a certain code language, each letter of a word is replaced by the next letter in the English alphabet (e.g., 'A' becomes 'B', 'Z' becomes 'A'). If 'PLANT' is coded as 'QMBOU', how will 'FLOWER' be coded in the same language?
A. GMPXFS
B. GMPVFS
C. GMNXFS
D. GMPXDS
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The coding rule is a simple Caesar shift of +1 for every letter. Apply +1 to each letter of FLOWER: F→G, L→M, O→P, W→X, E→F, R→S → GMPXFS. Full steps: Write the alphabet positions, add 1 to each position (with Z wrapping to A), convert back to letters; the result matches option A.
Q13. General Intelligence & Reasoning
A is the brother of B. C is the father of A. D is the sister of C. How is D related to B?
A. Mother
B. Aunt
C. Sister
D. Grandmother
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Draw a quick family tree. Since A and B are siblings, C (father of A) is also father of B. D is sister of C, making D the aunt of both A and B. Full steps: 1) A brother of B ⇒ same parents. 2) C father of A ⇒ C father of B. 3) D sister of C ⇒ D is female sibling of B's father ⇒ D is B's aunt.
Q14. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Find the missing number in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
A. 36
B. 40
C. 42
D. 48
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Observe the differences between consecutive terms: 4, 6, 8, 10. The differences increase by 2 each time, so the next difference is 12. Add 12 to the last term 30 → 42. Full steps: The series follows the pattern n(n+1) for n=1,2,3,4,5,6: 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, 6×7=42. Hence the missing number is 42.
Q15. General Awareness
Which of the following works is the earliest extant Tamil grammar text, dated generally between the 3rd century BCE and 5th century CE?
A. Silappadikaram
B. Tolkappiyam
C. Manimekalai
D. Tirukkural
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Eliminate the epics (Silappadikaram, Manimekalai) and the ethical treatise (Tirukkural). Tolkappiyam is the only grammatical treatise (Ilakkanam) among the options. Full Solution: Tolkappiyam, authored by Tolkappiyar, is the most ancient Tamil grammar text available today. It deals with orthography, phonology, morphology, semantics, and prosody. Silappadikaram and Manimekalai are the 'Twin Epics' of Sangam literature, while Tirukkural by Thiruvalluvar is a classic work on ethics and morality.
