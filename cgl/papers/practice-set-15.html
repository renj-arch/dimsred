Q1. Quantitative Aptitude
If the cost price of an article is Rs. 600 and it is sold for Rs. 720, what is the profit percentage?
A. 15%
B. 20%
C. 25%
D. 30%
Correct Answer: B
Profit is calculated as Selling Price minus Cost Price, which is 720 - 600 = Rs. 120. The profit percentage is (Profit / Cost Price) * 100 = (120 / 600) * 100 = 20%. Options A, C, and D are incorrect as they do not match this calculation.

  2. Fraction = 120/600 = 1/5
  3. 1/5 = 20%
Q2. Quantitative Aptitude
A shopkeeper allows two successive discounts of 20% and 15% on the marked price of an article. What is the single equivalent discount percentage?
A. 32%
B. 35%
C. 30%
D. 28%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For two successive discounts of a% and b%, the single equivalent discount = a + b - (a×b)/100. Here, 20 + 15 - (20×15)/100 = 35 - 3 = 32%. Step-by-step: Let Marked Price = ₹100. After 1st discount (20%), Price = ₹80. After 2nd discount (15% on ₹80), Price = 80 × (85/100) = ₹68. Total Discount = 100 - 68 = ₹32. Discount % = (32/100) × 100 = 32%.
Q3. English Language
Select the most appropriate one-word substitution for: "A person who knows many languages."
A. Polyglot
B. Linguist
C. Grammarian
D. Bibliophile
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use etymology/root words: 'Poly' (Greek: many) + 'glot' (Greek: glotta, tongue/language). Step-by-step: A Linguist is a scholar who studies the structure of language scientifically, not necessarily a speaker of many. A Grammarian specializes in grammar rules. A Bibliophile is a lover of books. Only Polyglot specifically denotes a person who speaks, writes, or reads several languages.
Q4. General Awareness
On the recommendation of which committee were the Fundamental Duties incorporated into the Indian Constitution through the 42nd Amendment Act, 1976?
A. Swaran Singh Committee
B. Sarkaria Committee
C. Punchhi Committee
D. Balwant Rai Mehta Committee
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Committee Mapping trick: Swaran Singh -> Fundamental Duties; Sarkaria/Punchhi -> Centre-State Relations; Balwant Rai Mehta -> Panchayati Raj (3-tier structure). Worked Solution: The Swaran Singh Committee (1976) recommended the inclusion of Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Article 51A) to remind citizens of their obligations. The 42nd Amendment implemented this. The Sarkaria (1983) and Punchhi (2007) Commissions dealt with Centre-State relations, while the Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957) recommended democratic decentralization.
Q5. Quantitative Aptitude
Pipe A can fill a tank in 12 hours and Pipe B can empty the full tank in 18 hours. If both pipes are opened simultaneously, in how many hours will the empty tank be filled?
A. 30 hours
B. 36 hours
C. 24 hours
D. 42 hours
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (LCM Method): Take LCM of 12 and 18 = 36 units (Total Capacity). Efficiency of A = 36/12 = +3 units/hr. Efficiency of B = 36/18 = -2 units/hr. Net Efficiency = 3 - 2 = 1 unit/hr. Time = 36/1 = 36 hours. Step-by-step: Part filled by A in 1 hour = 1/12. Part emptied by B in 1 hour = 1/18. Net part filled in 1 hour = 1/12 - 1/18 = (3-2)/36 = 1/36. Time required to fill the tank = 1 / (1/36) = 36 hours.
Research Team · ~5s · Product/Difference
  1. Multiply fill and empty times: 12 × 18 = 216
  2. Subtract fill from empty: 18 - 12 = 6
  3. Divide product by difference: 216 / 6 = 36

Correct Answer: B

Q8. Quantitative Aptitude
A train of length 150 m crosses a telegraph post in 10 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
A. 54 km/h
B. 72 km/h
C. 90 km/h
D. 36 km/h
Correct Answer: A
The distance covered to cross a post equals the train's length (150 m). Speed = Distance/Time = 150 m / 10 s = 15 m/s. Converting to km/h by multiplying by 18/5 gives 54 km/h. The other options result from incorrect unit conversions or calculation errors.
Q9. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Select the number that can replace the question mark (?) in the following series. 3, 10, 29, 66, 127, ?
A. 216
B. 218
C. 220
D. 222
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Observe that each term is 2 more than the cube of its position number (n). 1³+2=3, 2³+2=10, 3³+2=29, 4³+2=66, 5³+2=127. For the 6th term: 6³+2 = 216+2 = 218. Step-by-step: Find the first differences: 7, 19, 37, 61. Find the second differences: 12, 18, 24. Find the third differences: 6, 6 (constant). Since the third difference is constant, the series is cubic. Extend the pattern: next 2nd difference = 24+6=30; next 1st difference = 61+30=91; next term = 127+91=218.
Q10. English Language
Select the word that best completes the sentence: 'The sudden _______ of rain caused a major flood in the valley.'
A. A drizzle
B. B deluge
C. C shower
D. D mist
Correct Answer: B
'Deluge' refers to a heavy, overwhelming flow of water or rain, which fits the context of causing a major flood. 'Drizzle' and 'shower' imply light or moderate rain unlikely to cause such devastation immediately. 'Mist' is a thin cloud of droplets that does not equate to heavy rainfall. Thus, 'deluge' is the only appropriate choice.
Q11. General Awareness
The 'Right to Education' was included in which Article of the Indian Constitution?
A. Article 19
B. Article 21A
C. Article 32
D. Article 44
Correct Answer: B
Article 21A, inserted via the 86th Constitutional Amendment Act in 2002, mandates free and compulsory education for children aged 6 to 14. Article 19 deals with freedoms of speech and movement, Article 32 is about writ jurisdiction, and Article 44 pertains to linguistic uniformity.
Q12. General Awareness
Deficiency of which vitamin causes the disease 'Pellagra', characterized by the '3 Ds' (Dermatitis, Diarrhea, Dementia)?
A. Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)
B. Vitamin B3 (Niacin)
C. Vitamin B12 (Cobalamin)
D. Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Vitamin Deficiency Mnemonics: B1 -> Beriberi; B3 -> Pellagra (3 Ds); B12 -> Pernicious Anemia; C -> Scurvy. Worked Solution: Pellagra is caused by a deficiency of Niacin (Vitamin B3) or its precursor tryptophan. The classic symptoms are the '3 Ds': Dermatitis (photosensitive rash), Diarrhea, and Dementia; if untreated, it leads to Death (4th D). Vitamin B1 deficiency causes Beriberi (wet/dry), B12 causes Megaloblastic/Pernicious Anemia, and Vitamin C causes Scurvy (bleeding gums, poor wound healing).
Q13. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In a certain code language, '257' means 'Watch is fast', '193' means 'Time is slow', and '682' means 'Watch runs slow'. Which digit stands for the word 'runs'?
A. A. 1
B. B. 2
C. C. 3
D. D. 6
Correct Answer: A
Comparing '257' (Watch is fast) with '682' (Watch runs slow), the common digit is 2, representing 'Watch'. Comparing '193' (Time is slow) with '682' (Watch runs slow), the common digit is 3, representing 'slow'. Therefore, in '682', since 2 is Watch and 3 is slow, digit 6 must represent 'runs'.
Q14. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In a certain code language, 'KITE' is written as 'JHSD' and 'BALL' is written as 'AZKK'. How will 'CODE' be written in that code language?
A. BNDC
B. ANCD
C. BNC D
D. ABDD
Correct Answer: A
Comparing KITE to JHSD, the pattern is shifting each letter backward by 1 position (K->J, I->H, T->S, E->D). Applying this same -1 shift to CODE results in BNC D. Option A shifts only the first two letters, Option C has a typo and incorrect last letter, and Option D does not follow the consistent pattern.
Q15. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Statements: Some flowers are red. All red things are beautiful. Conclusions: I. Some flowers are beautiful. II. No flower is beautiful.
A. Only Conclusion I follows
B. Only Conclusion II follows
C. Either I or II follows
D. Neither I nor II follows
Correct Answer: A
From the statements, 'Some flowers are red' and 'All red things are beautiful' implies that the intersection of flowers and red things exists, and since all red things are beautiful, those specific flowers must be beautiful. Therefore, Conclusion I ('Some flowers are beautiful') logically follows. Conclusion II contradicts this deduction. Thus, only Conclusion I is correct.
