Q1. Quantitative Aptitude
A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 7:3. When 10 litres of water is added, the ratio becomes 7:5. What was the initial quantity of the mixture?
A. 40 litres
B. 60 litres
C. 70 litres
D. 50 litres
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Let initial milk = 7x, water = 3x. After adding 10 L water, water = 3x+10. New ratio 7x/(3x+10) = 7/5 → 35x = 21x+70 → 14x=70 → x=5. Initial mixture = 10x = 50 L. Full: Set up equation from ratios, solve for x, compute total.
Q2. Quantitative Aptitude
A shopkeeper marks his goods 50% above the cost price. He allows a discount of 20% on the marked price and then an additional discount of 10% on the reduced price. What is his profit percentage?
A. 5%
B. 8%
C. 10%
D. 12%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use Multiplying Factors. MP/CP = 1.5. Net Spf actor = 0.8 × 0.9 = 0.72. SP/CP = 1.5 × 0.72 = 1.08 → 8% profit. Step-by-step: Let CP = ₹100. MP = ₹150. After 20% discount, price = ₹120. After further 10% discount, SP = ₹108. Profit = ₹8 on ₹100 CP = 8%.
Q3. Quantitative Aptitude
The sides of a right-angled triangle are in the ratio 5 : 12 : 13. If its perimeter is 120 cm, what is the radius (in cm) of the inscribed circle?
A. 8
B. 6
C. 10
D. 12
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Sides are \(5k, 12k, 13k\). Perimeter \(30k=120 \Rightarrow k=4\). Sides: 20, 48, 52. For a right triangle, inradius \(r = \frac{a+b-c}{2} = \frac{20+48-52}{2} = 8\). Full Solution: Let sides be \(5x, 12x, 13x\). Perimeter \(= 30x = 120 \Rightarrow x=4\). Sides are 20 cm, 48 cm, 52 cm. Area \(= \frac{1}{2} \times 20 \times 48 = 480 \text{ cm}^2\). Semi-perimeter \(s = 60 \text{ cm}\). Inradius \(r = \frac{\text{Area}}{s} = \frac{480}{60} = 8 \text{ cm}\).
Q4. General Awareness
Deficiency of which vitamin leads to impaired blood clotting and increased bleeding tendency due to reduced synthesis of prothrombin?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)
C. Vitamin C
D. Vitamin K
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use elimination by matching famous deficiency pairs: Vit A → Night Blindness; Vit B1 → Beri-Beri; Vit C → Scurvy. The only remaining option, Vitamin K, is the co-factor for gamma-carboxylation of clotting factors II, VII, IX, X. Step-by-step: Vitamin K (Phylloquinone/Menaquinone) is a fat-soluble vitamin essential for the hepatic synthesis of clotting factors II (prothrombin), VII, IX, and X. Its deficiency causes prolonged prothrombin time and hemorrhagic disease, particularly in newborns (VKDB) or due to malabsorption.
Q5. General Awareness
Which of the following was added as a Fundamental Duty by the 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002?
A. To protect and improve the natural environment including forests, lakes, rivers and wildlife
B. To provide opportunities for education to his child or ward between the age of six and fourteen years
C. To safeguard public property and to abjure violence
D. To promote harmony and the spirit of common brotherhood amongst all the people of India
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Link the 86th Amendment (2002) directly to the Right to Education (Article 21A). The corresponding duty on parents/guardians was inserted as Article 51A(k). Full Solution: The 42nd Amendment (1976) added the original 10 Fundamental Duties (Article 51A). The 86th Amendment Act, 2002 inserted Article 21A (Right to Education for 6-14 years) and consequently added clause (k) to Article 51A, making it a duty of every parent/guardian to provide education opportunities to their child/ward in that age group. Options A, C, and D were part of the original 1976 list.
Q6. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Rohan walks 6 kmno rth, turns right and walks 8 km, turns left and walks 6 km, then turns right and walks 8 km. What is the straight-line distance from his starting point?
A. 25 km
B. 28 km
C. 30 km
D. 20 km
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Net North displacement = 6 + 6 = 12 km. Net East displacement = 8 + 8 = 16 km. Recognize 12-16-20 as a 3-4-5 Pythagorean triplet multiplied by 4. Distance = 20 km. Full Solution: Movements: N(6), E(8), N(6), E(8). Final position is 12 kmno rth and 16 km East of start. Distance = √(12² + 16²) = √(144 + 256) = √400 = 20 km.

Q8. General Awareness
Under the Flexible Inflation Targeting Framework (FITF) adopted in India in 2016, who is responsible for notifying the inflation target (in terms of CPI) once in every five years?
A. The Central Government (in consultation with the RBI)
B. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
C. The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)
D. The Fifteenth Finance Commission
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Target Setting = Government (Executive); Target Achieving = RBI/MPC (Central Bank). The amended RBI Act (Section 45ZA) mandates the Central Govt to fix the target. Full Solution: The Finance Act, 2016 amended the RBI Act, 1934, establishing a statutory basis for FITF. Under Section 45ZA, the Central Government, in consultation with the RBI, notifies the inflation target (currently 4% with +/- 2% tolerance band) for a period of five years. The MPC (headed by RBI Governor) determines the policy interest rate (repo rate) required to achieve this target. The Finance Commission deals with Centre-State fiscal transfers.

Q12. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In a certain code language, 'GRAPE' is written as 'TIZKV'. How will 'MANGO' be written in that language?
A. NZMUL
B. NZMTL
C. NZMSL
D. NZMRL
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use Atbash cipher (Reverse alphabet positions: A=Z, B=Y...). G(7)->T(20), R(18)->I(9), A(1)->Z(26), P(16)->K(11), E(5)->V(22). Apply same to MANGO: M(13)->N(14), A->Z, N(14)->M(13), G->T, O(15)->L(12) = NZMTL. Step-by-step: Verify pattern with GRAPE -> TIZKV (each letter replaced by its reverse positional counterpart). Apply to M->N, A->Z, N->M, G->T, O->L. Result: NZMTL.
Q13. Quantitative Aptitude
A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. A alone can complete the same work in 20 days. In how many days can B alone complete the work?
A. 24
B. 36
C. 30
D. 40
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: 1/B = 1/12 - 1/20 = (5-3)/60 = 2/60 = 1/30 → B = 30 days. Full: Work done by A+B in 1 day = 1/12. Work done by A in 1 day = 1/20. So B's 1 day work = 1/12 - 1/20 = 1/30. Hence B alone takes 30 days.
Q14. General Intelligence & Reasoning
If 'P + Q' means 'P is the father of Q', 'P - Q' means 'P is the mother of Q', 'P × Q' means 'P is the brother of Q', and 'P ÷ Q' means 'P is the sister of Q', then in the expression 'A + B × C - D ÷ E', how is A related to E?
A. Maternal Grandfather
B. Paternal Grandfather
C. Maternal Uncle
D. Father
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Track generation gaps: '+'/'-' change generation by ±1; '×'/'÷' keep same generation. Path: A(+1)→B(0)→C(-1)→D(0)→E. Net gap = +2 (Grandparent). Gender check: A is Male (+), C is Female (-). Since the link to E passes through a female (C), A is Maternal Grandfather. Full: A+B ⇒ A father of B. B×C ⇒ B brother of C ⇒ A father of C. C-D ⇒ C mother of D ⇒ A maternal grandfather of D. D÷E ⇒ D sister of E ⇒ A maternal grandfather of E.
Q15. English Language
Choose the best improvement for the sentence: "She likes dancing, singing, and to paint."
A. She likes dancing, singing, and painting.
B. She likes to dance, sing, and paint.
C. She likes dancing, to sing, and painting.
D. No improvement
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Items in a list after "likes" must be parallel – all gerunds or all infinitives. Option A makes all three gerunds (dancing, singing, painting). Step‑by‑step: The original mixes gerunds (dancing, singing) with an infinitive (to paint). Converting the infinitive to a gerund yields a fully parallel structure, which is the standard correction.
