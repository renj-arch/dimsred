Q31. Quantitative Aptitude
If the cost price of 12 pens is equal to the selling price of 8 pens, then the gain percentage is:
A. 25%
B. 40%
C. 50%
D. 60%
Correct Answer: C
Let the cost price of one pen be CP and the selling price of one pen be SP. According to the question, 12 * CP = 8 * SP. This implies SP/CP = 12/8 = 3/2. The gain is SP - CP. The gain percentage is ((SP - CP) / CP) * 100. Substituting SP = (3/2)CP, we get ((3/2)CP - CP) / CP * 100 = (1/2)CP / CP * 100 = (1/2) * 100 = 50%.
