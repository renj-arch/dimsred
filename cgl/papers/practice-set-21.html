B. 6
C. 8
D. 2
Correct Answer: A

Identify the segment in the sentence which contains the grammatical error: Neither the principal nor the teachers was present at the meeting.
A. Neither the principal
B. nor the teachers
C. was present
D. at the meeting
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Proximity Rule (Verb agrees with the nearest subject). In 'Neither...Nor' / 'Either...Or' structures, the verb matches the number/person of the second subject ('teachers' - Plural). Hence, 'was' (Singular) is incorrect; it should be 'were'. Worked Solution: The sentence uses the correlative conjunction 'Neither...Nor'. Subject 1: 'The principal' (Singular). Subject 2: 'The teachers' (Plural). The verb must agree with the subject closest to it (Subject 2). Since 'teachers' is plural, the correct verb is 'were present'. The error lies in segment C ('was present').

Q14. English Languagemodel-generated · 2026-11-23
Change the following sentence into Indirect Speech: He said to me, "Please help me in this matter."
A. He ordered me to help him in this matter.
B. He requested me to help him in that matter.
C. He asked me if I could help him in that matter.
D. He told me to help him in this matter.
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: 'Please' + Imperative → Reporting verb changes to 'requested' + 'to' + V1. Change 'said to' to 'requested'. Pronouns: 'me' (listener) stays 'me'; 'me' (speaker) becomes 'him'. Demonstrative 'this' becomes 'that'. Worked Solution: Direct: He said to me, "Please help me in this matter." The word 'Please' indicates a request. Reporting verb 'said to' becomes 'requested'. Imperative 'help' changes to infinitive 'to help'. Pronoun 'me' (object of help) refers to the speaker (He), so it becomes 'him'. 'This' changes to 'that'. Result: He requested me to help him in that matter.
