Q2. English Language
Select the correct passive form of the given sentence: "The committee has approved the new policy."
A. The new policy is approved by the committee.
B. The new policy has been approved by the committee.
C. The new policy was approved by the committee.
D. The new policy has approved by the committee.
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For Present Perfect Active (has/have + V3), the passive structure is Object + has/have + been + V3 + by + Subject. Step-by-step: The active sentence is in Present Perfect tense ('has approved'). The object 'the new policy' becomes the passive subject. The verb changes to 'has been approved' (has/have + been + V3). The original subject 'the committee' becomes the agent introduced by 'by'.
Q3. English Language
Choose the correct indirect speech for the given sentence: He said, "I am writing a letter now."
A. He said that he is writing a letter now.
B. He said that he wrote a letter then.
C. He said that he was writing a letter then.
D. He said that he has written a letter now.
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: When the reporting verb is past ('said'), Present Continuous ('am writing') shifts to Past Continuous ('was writing') and 'now' changes to 'then'. Step-by-step: The reporting verb 'said' remains unchanged. The pronoun 'I' changes to 'he' (matching the subject of reporting verb). The tense backshifts: 'am writing' (Present Continuous) becomes 'was writing' (Past Continuous). The time adverb 'now' changes to 'then' to reflect the past perspective.

Q5. Quantitative Aptitude
From an external point P, a tangent PT and a secant PAB are drawn to a circle. If PT = 12 cm and PA = 8 cm, find the length of chord AB.
A. 10 cm
B. 12 cm
C. 14 cm
D. 16 cm
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Tangent-Secant Theorem: PT² = PA × PB. 12² = 8 × PB → PB = 144/8 = 18 cm. Chord AB = PB - PA = 18 - 8 = 10 cm. Full: By the Tangent-Secant theorem, the square of the length of the tangent segment equals the product of the lengths of the whole secant segment and its external segment. So, PT² = PA × PB. Substituting values: 144 = 8 × PB ⇒ PB = 18 cm. The chord AB is the internal part of the secant, so AB = PB - PA = 18 - 8 = 10 cm.

Correct Answer: C

Q9. Quantitative Aptitude
What is the unit digit of the expression (7^95 - 3^58)?
A. 0
B. 2
C. 4
D. 6
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut (Cyclicity of 4): Unit digit of powers repeats every 4. For 7^95: 95 mod 4 = 3 → 7^3 = 343 → Unit digit 3. For 3^58: 58 mod 4 = 2 → 3^2 = 9 → Unit digit 9. Unit digit of (3 - 9) = Unit digit of (-6) = 4 (borrowing 10). Step-by-step: Cyclicity of 7 is 7, 9, 3, 1. 95 = 4×23 + 3 → 3rd position → 3. Cyclicity of 3 is 3, 9, 7, 1. 58 = 4×14 + 2 → 2nd position → 9. Difference = 3 - 9 = -6. Since unit digit cannot be negative, add 10 → 10 - 6 = 4.
Q10. Quantitative Aptitude
Find the unit digit of the expression \((127)^{153} + (248)^{206} + (369)^{112}\).
A. 0
B. 2
C. 4
D. 6
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits. For 7: cycle 4 (7,9,3,1), 153%4=1 → 7. For 8: cycle 4 (8,4,2,6), 206%4=2 → 4. For 9: cycle 2 (9,1), 112%2=0 → 1. Sum = 7+4+1=12 → unit digit 2. Full: Unit digit of 127^153 = 7^153. Cyclicity of 7 is 4; 153 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 1, so unit digit is 7. Unit digit of 248^206 = 8^206. Cyclicity of 8 is 4; 206 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 2, so unit digit is 4. Unit digit of 369^112 = 9^112. Cyclicity of 9 is 2; 112 ÷ 2 leaves remainder 0, so unit digit is 1. Sum of unit digits = 7 + 4 + 1 = 12. Required unit digit is 2.

Q15. English Language
Choose the word that best substitutes the given group of words: A person who loves and collects books
A. Bibliophile
B. Philatelist
C. Numismatist
D. Cartographer
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use Greek root elimination. 'Biblio' = books (Bible), 'phile' = lover. 'Philately' = stamps, 'Numisma' = coins, 'Cartography' = maps. Only Option A matches 'books + lover'.
