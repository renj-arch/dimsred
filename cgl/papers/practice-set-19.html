Q3. Quantitative Aptitude
A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 15% on the marked price. What is his profit percentage?
A. 19%
B. 21%
C. 23%
D. 25%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use successive percentage change formula \(a + b + \frac{ab}{100}\) where \(a=+40\) (markup) and \(b=-15\) (discount). Net % = \(40 - 15 + \frac{40 \times (-15)}{100} = 25 - 6 = 19\%\). Step-by-step: Let CP = 100. MP = 140. SP = \(140 \times \frac{85}{100} = 119\). Profit = 119 - 100 = 19. Profit% = \(\frac{19}{100} \times 100 = 19\%\).

Q5. Quantitative Aptitude

A. 44 cm
B. 66 cm
C. 88 cm
D. 132 cm
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: r = √(A×7/22) = 14 cm, C = 2πr = 88 cm. Step: Area = πr² → r² = 616×7/22 = 196 → r = 14 → C = 2×22/7×14 = 88 cm.

Q7. General Awareness
Deficiency of which vitamin causes 'Rickets' in children and 'Osteomalacia' in adults, and is unique because it is synthesized by the human skin upon exposure to ultraviolet B (UVB) sunlight?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)
C. Vitamin C
D. Vitamin D
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: **Sunlight Vitamin = Vitamin D**. Rickets (children) & Osteomalacia (adults) = Bone softening/deformity = Calcium/Phosphate metabolism issue = Vitamin D. Eliminate Vit A (Night Blindness/Xerophthalmia), Vit B1 (Beri-Beri), Vit C (Scurvy). Step-by-step: Vitamin D (Calciferol) is a fat-soluble vitamin synthesized in the skin from 7-dehydrocholesterol under UVB radiation. It regulates calcium and phosphate absorption. Its deficiency leads to defective bone mineralization: Rickets (growth plate failure in children causing bowed legs) and Osteomalacia (bone pain/fragility in adults).
Q8. General Awareness
In the Mansabdari system of the Mughal Empire, the rank of a Mansabdar was determined by two numerical designations: 'Zat' and 'Sawar'. What did these terms respectively indicate?
A. Zat: Number of cavalrymen to be maintained; Sawar: Personal salary rank
B. Zat: Personal salary rank and status; Sawar: Number of cavalrymen to be maintained
C. Zat: Revenue assignment (Jagir); Sawar: Administrative territory
D. Zat: Religious status; Sawar: Military equipment grade
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Mnemonic: **Z**at = **Z**eroing in on **Self** (Personal Rank/Salary). **S**awar = **S**oldiers/**S**ipahis (Cavalry count). Eliminate Option A (reversed), C (confuses with Jagir/Iqta system), D (irrelevant). Step-by-step: The Mansabdari system graded officials via two ranks: 'Zat' determined the personal pay (talab) and hierarchical status of the officer, while 'Sawar' indicated the number of cavalrymen (sawars) the mansabdar was required to maintain for imperial service. A mansabdar's salary was calculated based on Zat, and his military obligation on Sawar.
Q9. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Rahul walks 20m North, turns right and walks 30m, turns right and walks 35m, turns left and walks 15m, turns left and walks 15m. How far is he from the starting point?
A. 30 m
B. 35 m
C. 45 m
D. 50 m
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Calculate Net North-South and Net East-West displacements. N-S: +20 -35 +15 = 0. E-W: +30 +15 = 45. Distance = 45m. Step-by-step: Start (0,0). North 20 → (0,20). Right (East) 30 → (30,20). Right (South) 35 → (30,-15). Left (East) 15 → (45,-15). Left (North) 15 → (45,0). Distance from origin = √(45²+0²) = 45m.

Q13. Quantitative Aptitude
The length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and its breadth is decreased by 10%. What is the percentage change in the area of the rectangle?
A. 8% decrease
B. 10% increase
C. 8% increase
D. 10% decrease
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut (Successive Percentage Change): For Area = Length × Breadth, %ΔArea ≈ %ΔL + %ΔB + (%ΔL × %ΔB)/100. Here, +20 + (-10) + (20 × -10)/100 = 10 - 2 = 8% increase. Step-by-step: Let original Length = L, Breadth = B. Original Area = LB. New Length = 1.2L. New Breadth = 0.9B. New Area = 1.2L × 0.9B = 1.08 LB. Change = 1.08LB - LB = 0.08LB. % Change = (0.08LB / LB) × 100 = 8% increase.

Q15. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In a certain code language, 'TABLE' is written as 'UZCKF'. How will 'CHAIR' be written in that language?
A. DGBHS
B. DGBHR
C. DGBJS
D. DGBHQ
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Observe the pattern in the given example 'TABLE' -> 'UZCKF'. The shifts are +1, -1, +1, -1, +1 (alternating). Apply this to 'CHAIR': C+1=D, H-1=G, A+1=B, I-1=H, R+1=S. The code is 'DGBHS'. Quick elimination: Last letter R+1=S, only Option A ends with 'S'. Step-by-step: Write alphabet positions. T(20)+1=U(21); A(1)-1=Z(26); B(2)+1=C(3); L(12)-1=K(11); E(5)+1=F(6). Pattern confirmed as alternate +1, -1 starting with +1. Apply to CHAIR: C(3)+1=D(4); H(8)-1=G(7); A(1)+1=B(2); I(9)-1=H(8); R(18)+1=S(19). Result: DGBHS.
