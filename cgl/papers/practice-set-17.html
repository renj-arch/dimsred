A. 70 km/h
B. 72 km/h
C. 65 km/h
D. 80 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Let total distance be 2d. Time for first half = d/50. Total time required = 2d/60. Time for second half = (2d/60) - (d/50) = d/300. Speed for second half = Distance/Time = d / (d/300) = 72 km/h. Option A is incorrect because average speed is not the arithmetic mean of speeds, and options Cand D do not satisfy the harmonic mean relationship.

If \( x + \frac{1}{x} = 5 \), find the value of \( x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} \).
A. 100
B. 105
C. 110
D. 115
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use identity \(x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = (x + \frac{1}{x})^3 - 3(x + \frac{1}{x})\). Substitute \(k=5\): \(5^3 - 3 \times 5 = 125 - 15 = 110\). Step-by-step: Cube the given equation: \((x + \frac{1}{x})^3 = x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} + 3(x + \frac{1}{x})\). \(125 = x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} + 15\). Therefore, \(x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = 125 - 15 = 110\).
Q7. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-16
A vessel contains 80 litres of pure milk. 16 litres of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated two more times. What is the quantity of milk (in litres) left in the vessel?
A. 38.40
B. 40.96
C. 42.50
D. 36.80
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the replacement formula $Final = Initial \times (1 - \frac{Replaced}{Total})^n$. Here, fraction remaining $= 1 - \frac{16}{80} = \frac{4}{5}$, and $n=3$. Milk left $= 80 \times (\frac{4}{5})^3 = 80 \times \frac{64}{125} = 40.96$ litres. Full Method: After 1st replacement, milk $= 80 - 16 = 64$ L. After 2nd, milk removed $= 16 \times \frac{64}{80} = 12.8$ L, remaining $= 51.2$ L. After 3rd, milk removed $= 16 \times \frac{51.2}{80} = 10.24$ L, final milk $= 51.2 - 10.24 = 40.96$ L.
Q8. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-17
The difference between Compound Interest and Simple Interest on a certain sum at 10% per annum for 2 years is Rs. 50. Find the principal sum.
A. Rs. 4000
B. Rs. 4500
C. Rs. 5000
D. Rs. 5500
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For 2 years, Difference (CI - SI) = Principal × (Rate/100)². Substituting values: 50 = P × (10/100)² = P × 1/100 ⇒ P = 50 × 100 = Rs. 5000. Step-by-step: Let Principal = P. SI for 2 years = (P × 10 × 2)/100 = 0.2P. Amount in CI = P(1 + 10/100)² = 1.21P. CI = 1.21P - P = 0.21P. Difference = 0.21P - 0.2P = 0.01P. Given 0.01P = 50, so P = 50/0.01 = Rs. 5000.

Q15. English Languagemodel-generated · 2026-11-17
Identify the segment containing the grammatical error: "The teacher asked the students / to listen her carefully / during the lecture / No error"
A. The teacher asked the students
B. to listen her carefully
C. during the lecture
D. No error
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The verb 'listen' is intransitive and mandatorily requires the preposition 'to' before an object (listen to someone). Spot the missing 'to'. Full Solution: The error lies in the second segment. The verb 'listen' cannot take a direct object; it must be followed by the preposition 'to' when specifying what or who is being heard.
