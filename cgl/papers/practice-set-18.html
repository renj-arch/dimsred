Convert the following direct speech into indirect speech: He said, "I am writing a letter now."
A. He said that he is writing a letter now.
B. He said that he was writing a letter then.
C. He said that he wrote a letter then.
D. He said that he has been writing a letter now.
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: When the reporting verb is in past tense ('said'), the reported speech tense backshifts: Present Continuous ('am writing') becomes Past Continuous ('was writing'). Time marker 'now' changes to 'then'. Eliminate options retaining present tense ('is', 'has been') or incorrect time marker ('now'). Full Solution: Direct: He said, "I am writing a letter now." Indirect: Conjunction 'that' added. Pronoun 'I' becomes 'he'. Verb 'am writing' (Present Continuous) shifts to 'was writing' (Past Continuous). Adverb 'now' changes to 'then'. Result: He said that he was writing a letter then.

The difference between Compound Interest (compounded annually) and Simple Interest on a certain sum of money for 2 years at 10% per annum is Rs. 50. What is the principal sum?
A. Rs. 4,000
B. Rs. 5,000
C. Rs. 6,000
D. Rs. 5,500
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For 2 years, Difference (D) = P × (R/100)². Here, 50 = P × (10/100)² = P × 1/100 ⇒ P = 50 × 100 = Rs. 5,000. Step-by-step: Let Principal = P. SI for 2 years = (P × 10 × 2)/100 = 0.2P. Amount in CI = P(1 + 10/100)² = P × 1.21 = 1.21P. CI = 1.21P - P = 0.21P. Difference = 0.21P - 0.2P = 0.01P. Given 0.01P = 50 ⇒ P = 50/0.01 = 5,000.

Q15. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-18
If \(x + \frac{1}{x} = 5\), then the value of \(x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3}\) is:
A. 100
B. 105
C. 110
D. 115
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use identity \(x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = (x + \frac{1}{x})^3 - 3(x + \frac{1}{x})\). Substitute \(k=5\): \(5^3 - 3(5) = 125 - 15 = 110\). Full: Cube the given equation: \((x+1/x)^3 = x^3 + 1/x^3 + 3(x+1/x)\). \(125 = x^3+1/x^3 + 15\). Therefore, \(x^3+1/x^3 = 110\).
