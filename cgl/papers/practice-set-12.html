Q10. English Language

A. blessing
B. curse
C. nuisance
D. boon
Correct Answer: B
A blessing or boon implies improvement; a nuisance is minor. The context describes a negative outcome (despair) from an expected positive event, making 'curse' the only logical synonym for a misfortune.
