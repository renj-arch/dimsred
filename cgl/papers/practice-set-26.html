Q1. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In a certain code language, 'MANGO' is written as 'PHOBN'. How will 'APPLE' be written in that language?
A. EMQQB
B. FMQQA
C. FNQQB
D. FMQQB
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: The pattern is 'Reverse the word, then replace each letter with its next letter in the alphabet (+1)'. For MANGO: Reverse → OGNAM → +1 → PHOBN. Apply to APPLE: Reverse → ELPPA → +1 → F (E+1), M (L+1), Q (P+1), Q (P+1), B (A+1) = FMQQB. Step-by-step: 1. Verify pattern with given example: M-A-N-G-O reversed is O-G-N-A-M. O+1=P, G+1=H, N+1=O, A+1=B, M+1=N → PHOBN (Matches). 2. Apply to APPLE: Reverse = E-L-P-P-A. Add 1 to positions: E→F, L→M, P→Q, P→Q, A→B. Result: FMQQB.
Q2. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Rahul walks 6 km North, turns right and walks 8 km, turns left and walks 6 km, finally turns right and walks 8 km. What is the straight-line distance (in km) from his starting point?
A. 18
B. 20
C. 22
D. 24
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Calculate net displacement on axes. North displacement = 6 + 6 = 12 km. East displacement (Right turns while facing North/South) = 8 + 8 = 16 km. Distance = √(12² + 16²) = √(144+256) = √400 = 20 km. (Recognize 3-4-5 triplet scaled by 4: 12-16-20). Full Solution: Plot movements on coordinate plane. Start (0,0). North 6 → (0,6). Right (East) 8 → (8,6). Left (North) 6 → (8,12). Right (East) 8 → (16,12). Distance from origin = √(16² + 12²) = √(256+144) = √400 = 20 km.
Q3. English Language
Convert the following sentence into Indirect Speech: The teacher said to the students, "Please maintain silence in the library."
A. The teacher requested the students to maintain silence in the library.
B. The teacher ordered the students that they should maintain silence in the library.
C. The teacher said to the students that please maintain silence in the library.
D. The teacher asked the students if they could maintain silence in the library.
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Spot 'Please' → indicates a request, not an order. Eliminate 'ordered' (B). Imperative sentences convert to Infinitive (to + V1), not a 'that' clause; eliminate (C). 'Asked if' changes meaning to a question; eliminate (D). Only (A) uses 'requested' + 'to maintain'. Worked Solution: In Direct Speech, 'said to' with an imperative 'Please maintain' changes the reporting verb to 'requested' (or 'asked'). The imperative verb 'maintain' changes to the infinitive 'to maintain'. The object 'the students' remains. 'Please' is dropped. Result: The teacher requested the students to maintain silence in the library.
Q4. Quantitative Aptitude
A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 20% on the marked price. Find his profit percentage.
A. 8%
B. 12%
C. 10%
D. 15%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Effective percentage change = a + b + ab/100. Here a=+40 (markup), b=-20 (discount). Effective % = 40 - 20 + (40×-20)/100 = 20 - 8 = 12%. Step-by-step: Let Cost Price (CP) = 100. Marked Price (MP) = 100 + 40% of 100 = 140. Selling Price (SP) = MP - 20% of MP = 140 - 28 = 112. Profit = SP - CP = 112 - 100 = 12. Profit % = (12/100) × 100 = 12%.
Q5. Quantitative Aptitude
A sum of money becomes 1.44 times itself in 2 years at compound interest compounded annually. What is the annual rate of interest?
A. 10%
B. 20%
C. 15%
D. 25%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: (1 + r/100)^2 = 1.44 → 1 + r/100 = √1.44 = 1.2 → r = 20%. Full: Let principal = P, amount = 1.44P. Using A = P(1+r/100)^2, we get (1+r/100)^2 = 1.44, so 1+r/100 = 1.2, r = 20%.
Q6. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Statements: All engineers are problem solvers. Some problem solvers are creative. Conclusions: I. Some engineers are creative. II. All creative are problem solvers. Choose the correct option:
A. Only conclusion I follows
B. Only conclusion II follows
C. Both conclusions I and II follow
D. Neither conclusion I nor II follows
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Draw a Venn diagram – Engineers circle entirely inside Problem Solvers; Creative overlaps Problem Solvers partially. Conclusion I is not certain because the creative problem solvers may lie outside Engineers. Conclusion II is not given as only some problem solvers are creative. Hence neither follows. Full solution: From 'All engineers are problem solvers', engineers ⊆ problem solvers. 'Some problem solvers are creative' means the intersection of problem solvers and creative is non‑empty, but it does not guarantee any engineer is creative nor that all creative are problem solvers. Therefore both conclusions are invalid.
Q7. English Language
Identify the segment in the sentence which contains a grammatical error. If there is no error, select 'No error'. The number of / participants in the marathon / have exceeded / the expected limit.
A. The number of
B. participants in the marathon
C. have exceeded
D. the expected limit
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: 'The number of' takes a singular verb (has), while 'A number of' takes a plural verb (have). Spot the subject 'The number' (singular) and match with singular verb 'has'. Step-by-step: The subject is 'The number' (singular head noun). The phrase 'of participants' is a prepositional phrase modifying the subject and does not affect verb agreement. The verb 'have exceeded' is plural, creating a subject-verb disagreement. The correct verb is 'has exceeded'. Error lies in segment 'have exceeded'.
Q8. General Awareness
Which of the following Commissions was boycotted by the Indian National Congress primarily because it did not include a single Indian member?
A. Cripps Mission (1942)
B. Cabinet Mission (1946)
C. Simon Commission (1927)
D. Wavell Plan / Simla Conference (1945)
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Remember the slogan 'Simon Go Back'. The Simon Commission (1927) was an all-white, 7-member British Parliamentary Commission under Sir John Simon to review the 1919 Act. Full Solution: The Indian Statutory Commission (Simon Commission) was constituted in 1927, two years ahead of schedule. Its all-British composition led to a nationwide boycott by the Congress (Madras Session 1927) and major Muslim League factions. The Cripps Mission (1942), Cabinet Mission (1946), and Wavell Plan (1945) were negotiation missions/plans involving Indian leaders, not statutory review commissions lacking Indian representation.
Q9. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Select the number that can replace the question mark (?) in the following series: 12, 13, 25, 38, 63, ?
A. 99
B. 101
C. 103
D. 105
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Test the Fibonacci pattern (Sum of previous two terms). 12 + 13 = 25 (3rd term). 13 + 25 = 38 (4th term). 25 + 38 = 63 (5th term). Next term = 38 + 63 = 101. Full Solution: The series follows the recursive rule T(n) = T(n-1) + T(n-2). Term 3 = 12+13=25. Term 4 = 13+25=38. Term 5 = 25+38=63. Term 6 = 38+63=101.
Q10. General Awareness
Which of the following soil types is characterized by the presence of 'Kankar' nodules and is typically found in the arid and semi-arid regions of Rajasthan and Gujarat?
A. Black Soil
B. Laterite Soil
C. Arid/Desert Soil
D. Alluvial Soil
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Keyword association. 'Kankar' (calcium carbonate nodules) + 'Arid/Semi-arid' + 'Rajasthan/Gujarat' = Arid/Desert Soil. Black soil has lime but is sticky/clayey; Laterite is leached/iron-rich; Alluvial is river-deposited/silty. Worked Solution: Arid and Desert soils are found in Western Rajasthan, parts of Gujarat, and Punjab. Due to high evaporation and low rainfall, calcium carbonate accumulates in the lower layers forming hard nodules called 'Kankar'. This layer restricts water infiltration. Black soil is known for moisture retention, Laterite for leaching, and Alluvial for fertility.
Q11. Quantitative Aptitude
The cost price of 10 articles is equal to the selling price of 8 articles. What is the profit percentage?
A. 25%
B. 20%
C. 30%
D. 15%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Profit% = ((CP quantity - SP quantity)/SP quantity) × 100 = ((10-8)/8)×100 = 25%. Full: Let CP of 1 article = ₹1, then CP of 10 = ₹10 = SP of 8, so SP of 1 = ₹1.25. Profit = ₹0.25 on ₹1 → 25%.
Q12. General Awareness
Which of the following Articles of the Constitution of India deals with the appointment and conditions of the office of the Attorney General for India?
A. Article 74
B. Article 75
C. Article 76
D. Article 77
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use the '70s series' elimination trick for Union Executive articles. Art 74 (Council of Ministers), Art 75 (PM/Ministers), Art 76 (Attorney General), Art 77 (Conduct of Business). The Attorney General is the only constitutional post in this specific sequence not part of the Council of Ministers. Worked Solution: Article 76(1) states the President shall appoint a person qualified to be a Supreme Court Judge as Attorney General. Article 74 deals with the Council of Ministers, Article 75 with their appointment/salaries, and Article 77 with the conduct of government business.
Q13. General Awareness
In the context of the Indian Economy, what does the term 'Fiscal Deficit' technically represent?
A. Total Expenditure minus Revenue Receipts
B. Total Expenditure minus Total Receipts (excluding borrowings)
C. Revenue Expenditure minus Revenue Receipts
D. Capital Expenditure minus Capital Receipts
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Definition elimination. Fiscal Deficit = Borrowings (approx). Borrowings are needed when Total Spending > Total Income (excluding loans). Option A ignores capital receipts (non-debt). Option C is Revenue Deficit. Option D is partial. Worked Solution: Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure (Revenue + Capital) - Total Receipts (Revenue + Non-debt Capital Receipts like disinvestment, loan recoveries). It indicates the total borrowing requirement of the government. Revenue Deficit is only Revenue Expenditure minus Revenue Receipts. Primary Deficit is Fiscal Deficit minus Interest Payments.

Q15. Quantitative Aptitude
A solid metallic cylinder of radius 4 cm and height 18 cm is melted and recast into solid spheres of radius 2 cm. How many spheres can be made?
A. 24
B. 27
C. 30
D. 36
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Equate total volume. Volume of cylinder = πr²h = π×16×18 = 288π. Volume of one sphere = ⁴/₃πr³ = ⁴/₃π×8 = ³²π/₃. Number of spheres = 288π ÷ (³²π/₃) = 288 × ³/₃₂ = 27. Trick: Cancel π mentally; compute (16×18×3)/32 = (288×3)/32 = 864/32 = 27.
