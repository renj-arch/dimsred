Q10. [+2, -0.50] Mathematics
If the length of the sides of a cube are doubled, what is the ratio of the new volume to the original volume?
A. 4:1
B. 6:1
C. 8:1
D. 9:1
Correct Answer: C
The volume of a cube is given by V = s^3, where s is the length of the side. If the side length is doubled, the new volume is V' = (2s)^3 = 8s^3. The ratio of the new volume to the original volume is V'/V = 8s^3 / s^3 = 8:1.
