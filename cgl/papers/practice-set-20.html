Change the following sentence into Passive Voice: 'They completed the project yesterday.'
A. The project is completed by them yesterday.
B. The project has been completed by them yesterday.
C. The project was completed by them yesterday.
D. The project had been completed by them yesterday.
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Identify the Active tense (Simple Past: V2). Rule: Object + was/were + V3 + by + Subject. 'They' (Subject) -> 'The project' (Object). V2 'completed' -> 'was completed'. Time marker 'yesterday' confirms Simple Past. Eliminate A (Present), B (Present Perfect + yesterday mismatch), D (Past Perfect). Full Solution: The active sentence is in Simple Past Tense (Subject + V2). To convert to passive: 1. Make the object ('the project') the new subject. 2. Use 'was/were' (singular subject -> 'was'). 3. Use Past Participle (V3) of main verb ('completed'). 4. Add 'by them'. Result: 'The project was completed by them yesterday.'

The sides of a right-angled triangle are 9 cm, 40 cm, and 41 cm. Find the inradius of the triangle.
A. 3 cm
B. 4 cm
C. 5 cm
D. 6 cm
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a right triangle, inradius \(r = \frac{a + b - c}{2}\) where \(c\) is hypotenuse. \(r = \frac{9 + 40 - 41}{2} = \frac{8}{2} = 4\) cm. Step-by-step: Verify triplet: \(9^2 + 40^2 = 81 + 1600 = 1681 = 41^2\). Semi-perimeter \(s = \frac{9+40+41}{2} = 45\). Area \(A = \frac{1}{2} \times 9 \times 40 = 180\). Inradius \(r = \frac{A}{s} = \frac{180}{45} = 4\) cm.
Research Team · ~5s · Right triangle shortcut

  2. Compute (9+40-41)/2 = 8/2 = 4

Q6. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-20
A solid metallic cylinder of radius 6 cm and height 10 cm is melted and recast into solid spheres of radius 3 cm each. The number of spheres formed is:
A. 8
B. 9
C. 10
D. 12
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Volume ratio method. Volume of Cylinder = \(\pi r^2 h = \pi \times 36 \times 10 = 360\pi\). Volume of one Sphere = \(\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi \times 27 = 36\pi\). Number of spheres = \(\frac{360\pi}{36\pi} = 10\). Full Method: Volume of cylinder = \(\pi \times 6^2 \times 10 = 360\pi \text{ cm}^3\). Volume of one sphere = \(\frac{4}{3} \pi \times 3^3 = 36\pi \text{ cm}^3\). Let \(n\) be number of spheres. \(n \times 36\pi = 360\pi \implies n = 10\).
Research Team · ~5s · Dimension Ratio Shortcut
  1. Note cylinder radius = 2 × sphere radius (6 = 2×3).
  2. Cylinder volume = π(2r)²h = 4πr²h.
  3. Sphere volume = (4/3)πr³.
  4. Divide: (4πr²h) / ((4/3)πr³) = 3h/r.
  5. Plug h=10, r=3 → 30/3 = 10 spheres.

A boat takes 90 minutes less to travel 36 km downstream than to travel the same distance upstream. If the speed of the boat in still water is 10 km/h, what is the speed of the stream?
A. 2 km/h
B. 3 km/h
C. 4 km/h
D. 5 km/h
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (Option Elimination): Test Option A (2 km/h). Downstream speed = 10+2 = 12 km/h, Time = 36/12 = 3 hours. Upstream speed = 10-2 = 8 km/h, Time = 36/8 = 4.5 hours. Difference = 1.5 hours = 90 minutes. Condition satisfied instantly. Full Solution: Let speed of stream = x km/h. Downstream speed = (10+x) km/h, Upstream speed = (10-x) km/h. Time difference = 90 min = 1.5 hours. Equation: 36/(10-x) - 36/(10+x) = 1.5. => 36(2x) / (100 - x^2) = 1.5 => 72x = 150 - 1.5x^2 => 1.5x^2 + 72x - 150 = 0 => x^2 + 48x - 100 = 0 => (x + 50)(x - 2) = 0 => x = 2 km/h (rejecting negative value).
Research Team · ~10s · Plug Options
  1. For each option x, compute downstream time = 36/(10+x), upstream = 36/(10-x).
  2. Find difference upstream - downstream.
  3. The option giving 1.5 hours (90 min) is correct.
  4. Option A (2 km/h) gives 36/12=3h downstream, 36/8=4.5h upstream, diff=1.5h.

What is the unit digit of the expression \((7^{95} \times 3^{58}) + 2^{47}\)?
A. 5
B. 3
C. 7
D. 9
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits (cycle of 4 for 2,3,7,8). \(7^{95}\): 95 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit of \(7^3\) is 3. \(3^{58}\): 58 mod 4 = 2 → unit digit of \(3^2\) is 9. \(2^{47}\): 47 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit of \(2^3\) is 8. Product unit digit = 3 × 9 = 27 → 7. Sum unit digit = 7 + 8 = 15 → 5. Full Method: Cyclicity of 7 is 7,9,3,1. 95 = 4×23 + 3 → 3rd position = 3. Cyclicity of 3 is 3,9,7,1. 58 = 4×14 + 2 → 2nd position = 9. Cyclicity of 2 is 2,4,8,6. 47 = 4×11 + 3 → 3rd position = 8. (3 × 9) + 8 = 27 + 8 = 35. Unit digit is 5.
Research Team · ~8s · Mod 10 Arithmetic

  2. 3^58 mod 10: 3^4≡1 → 3^(4×14+2)≡3^2≡9
  3. Product mod 10: 3×9=27≡7
  4. 2^47 mod 10: cycle 2,4,8,6 → 47 mod 4=3 → 2^3=8
  5. Sum mod 10: 7+8=15≡5

Rahul walks 15 km North, turns right and walks 20 km, turns left and walks 15 km, turns right and walks 20 km. How far is he from the starting point?
A. 40 km
B. 60 km
C. 70 km
D. 50 km
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Calculate net displacement coordinates. North: 15+15=30 km. East: 20+20=40 km. Recognize 30-40-50 Pythagorean triplet (3-4-5 × 10). Distance = 50 km. Step-by-step: Start (0,0). North 15 → (0,15). Right (East) 20 → (20,15). Left (North) 15 → (20,30). Right (East) 20 → (40,30). Distance = √(40² + 30²) = √2500 = 50 km.
