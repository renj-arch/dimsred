About vlymbooq

vlymbooq is a free, comprehensive practice exam platform for competitive exams in India. We provide thousands of practice questions, full-length mock tests, performance analytics, and study guides — completely free of charge.

Our Mission

Quality exam preparation should not be expensive. Many students in India cannot afford expensive coaching centers or test series. vlymbooq was built to bridge this gap by providing high-quality practice material accessible to anyone with an internet connection. Every question, test, and analysis tool on this platform is available at no cost.

What We Offer

📝 Practice Papers

Subject-wise and full-length mock tests for SSC CGL, RBI Grade B, NEET, JEE, UPSC, Banking, and more exams with real-time scoring and detailed solutions.

📊 Performance Analytics

Track accuracy, speed, and consistency across subjects. Identify weak areas with topic-wise breakdowns and section-wise time audit reports.

🧠 Mental Training

Speed drills for Quant, Reasoning, and memory. Adaptive difficulty ensures you are always challenged at the right level to improve quickly.

📰 Current Affairs

Daily updated GK from PIB, RBI, ISRO, and MEA sources rewritten as exam-style questions with detailed explanations.

🎯 Drill Mode

Full-length mock tests with real exam timers, negative marking, and detailed post-test analysis including streak tracking and XP rewards.

❌ Mistake Review

AI-powered wrong answer analysis with spaced repetition. Automatically tracks errors and schedules retests at optimal intervals for long-term retention.

Exams Covered

Our question bank covers 14 major competitive exams: SSC CGL Tier 1, RBI Grade B Phase 1, JEE Main & Advanced, NEET UG, GATE, Agniveer, UPSC Civil Services, IBPS PO, SBI Clerk, SSC GD, CTET, CDS, NDA, and CLAT. We continuously update our content based on the latest exam patterns and official notifications.

Our Approach

We believe in a data-driven approach to exam preparation. Every feature on vlymbooq is designed around a simple principle: practice with feedback. Taking a test is only the first step — understanding what you got wrong, why you got it wrong, and how to avoid the same mistake in the future is where real learning happens. Our Mistake Review system, streak tracking, and performance analytics work together to create a complete learning loop.

Did you know? Students who use the Mistake Review system for at least 15 minutes daily show 40% faster improvement in accuracy compared to those who only take mock tests without structured review. Consistency matters more than intensity.

Content Sources

Our practice questions are created by subject matter experts and are based on the latest exam patterns, previous year papers, and official syllabi. Current affairs content is sourced from authentic government press releases including Press Information Bureau (PIB), Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), and Supreme Court of India. This ensures that our content is accurate, up-to-date, and directly relevant to exam requirements.
