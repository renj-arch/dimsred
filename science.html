How to study a science topic

  1. Meaning first: say the idea in one plain sentence and give one everyday example.
  2. Then the rule: write the formula or law, and the unit of every quantity.
  3. Then one solved example: cover it and redo it from memory.
  4. Then 5–10 MCQs from the micro-test; log every mistake.
  5. Revise on day 1, 3 and 7 — recall beats rereading.
