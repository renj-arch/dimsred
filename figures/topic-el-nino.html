El Niño — Normal vs El Niño

Hand-drawn, label-first diagram — reproducible in the exam hall · GS1 Geography

Draw: Pacific ocean strip left (Asia/Australia) to right (South America). Normal: arrow left (trade winds), warm water on left, cold upwelling on right, rain on left. El Niño: arrow right/weakened, warm water shifts right, no upwelling on right, rain shifts right.
