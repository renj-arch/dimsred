Monsoon Seesaw (Indian Summer Monsoon)

Hand-drawn, label-first diagram — reproducible in the exam hall · GS1 Geography

Draw: two boxes (land, sea). Summer: sun over land → hot land → low pressure over land → arrow sea→land → rain. Winter: warm sea → high pressure over land → arrow land→sea → dry. Label: SW monsoon, NE monsoon, ITCZ shift.
