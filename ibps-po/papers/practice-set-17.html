Find the unit digit of the expression (7^95 - 3^58).
A. 4
B. 6
C. 2
D. 8
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (Cyclicity): Unit digit of powers of 7 repeats in cycle of 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. 95 ÷ 4 gives remainder 3 → 3rd position in cycle = 3. Unit digit of powers of 3 repeats in cycle of 4: 3, 9, 7, 1. 58 ÷ 4 gives remainder 2 → 2nd position in cycle = 9. Unit digit of (3 - 9) requires borrowing → (13 - 9) = 4. Step-by-step: 7^95 = 7^(4×23+3) → Unit digit same as 7^3 = 343 → 3. 3^58 = 3^(4×14+2) → Unit digit same as 3^2 = 9. Since 3 < 9, we borrow 1 from tens place: 13 - 9 = 4. Unit digit is 4.

Q6. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-26
A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 15% on the marked price. Find his gain percent.
A. 17%
B. 19%
C. 21%
D. 25%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (Effective Percentage Formula): For successive changes of x% and y%, net % change = x + y + (xy/100). Here, Markup = +40%, Discount = -15%. Net Gain % = 40 + (-15) + (40 × -15)/100 = 25 - 6 = 19%. Step-by-step: Let Cost Price (CP) = Rs. 100. Marked Price (MP) = 100 + 40% of 100 = Rs. 140. Selling Price (SP) after 15% discount = 140 - 15% of 140 = 140 × 0.85 = Rs. 119. Gain = SP - CP = 119 - 100 = Rs. 19. Gain % = (19/100) × 100 = 19%.
