Q1. Quantitative Aptitude
A contractor employs 6 men and 8 women to complete a work in 10 days. If 3 men are as efficient as 4 women, in how many days will 3 men and 4 women complete the same work?
A. 20
B. 15
C. 10
D. 5
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Given 3M = 4W, the first group (6M+8W) is exactly double the second group (3M+4W). Since efficiency is halved, time taken doubles. Answer = 10 × 2 = 20 days. Full: Let efficiency of 1 woman = 3 units/day → 1 man = 4 units/day. Work = (6×4 + 8×3) × 10 = 480 units. New team efficiency = 3×4 + 4×3 = 24 units/day. Days = 480/24 = 20 days.
Q2. Reasoning Ability
Pointing to a photograph, Rekha said, "He is the only son of the father of my mother's only daughter." How is the person in the photograph related to Rekha?
A. Brother
B. Son
C. Nephew
D. Father
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Decode backwards from the speaker. 'My mother's only daughter' = Rekha (speaker). 'Father of Rekha' = Rekha's father. 'Only son of Rekha's father' = Rekha's brother. Full Solution: Rekha's mother's only daughter is Rekha herself. The father of Rekha is her father. The only son of her father is Rekha's brother (assuming Rekha is female). Hence, the person is Rekha's brother.
Q3. Reasoning Ability

A. Brother
B. Son
C. Nephew
D. Husband
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The woman's mother's only daughter is the woman herself (assuming she is the only daughter). The only son of that daughter is the woman's son. Hence the man is her son. Step-by-step: Identify the woman's mother's only daughter = the woman. Then the only son of that daughter = the woman's son. So answer is Son.
Q4. Reasoning Ability
Point A is 10m North of Point B. Point C is 15m East of Point A. Point D is 20m South of Point C. Point E is 10m West of Point D. What is the shortest distance between Point B and Point E?
A. 5√5 m
B. 5 m
C. 10 m
D. 10√2 m
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Calculate net horizontal and vertical displacement from B to E. Vertical: +10 (B to A) -20 (C to D) = -10m (10m South). Horizontal: +15 (A to C) -10 (D to E) = +5m (5m East). Distance = √(10² + 5²) = √125 = 5√5 m. Full: B(0,0) -> A(0,10) -> C(15,10) -> D(15,-10) -> E(5,-10). Distance BE = √[(5-0)² + (-10-0)²] = √(25+100) = √125 = 5√5 m.
Q5. English Language
In the following question, a word is given along with its usage in four different sentences. Choose the sentence in which the usage of the word is INCORRECT or INAPPROPRIATE. Word: PRECLUDE
A. The new evidence does not preclude the possibility of a different outcome.
B. His busy schedule precludes him from attending the meeting.
C. The contract precludes any liability for damages caused by natural disasters.
D. The heavy rain precluded the match to continue.
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Check the verb pattern. 'Preclude' means prevent. Structure: 'Preclude + Object + from + V-ing' OR 'Preclude + Noun'. It is NEVER followed by 'to + infinitive'. Option 4 uses 'precluded the match to continue' (incorrect). Correct: 'precluded the match from continuing'. Step-by-step: 1. Sentence 1: 'preclude the possibility' (Noun object) - Correct. 2. Sentence 2: 'precludes him from attending' (Object + from + V-ing) - Correct. 3. Sentence 3: 'precludes any liability' (Noun object) - Correct. 4. Sentence 4: 'precluded the match to continue' uses infinitive 'to continue', which is grammatically incorrect with 'preclude'.
Q6. Quantitative Aptitude
A person invests Rs. 5000 at a 10% interest rate. How much will he get after 1 year?
A. Rs. 5000
B. Rs. 5500
C. Rs. 6000
D. Rs. 6500
Correct Answer: B
The interest earned in 1 year is 5000 * 10% = 500. Therefore, the total amount after 1 year is 5000 + 500 = 5500.
Q7. English Language
Rearrange the following four sentences (A), (B), (C), and (D) in the proper sequence to form a meaningful paragraph. (A) However, the success of these initiatives hinges on the active participation of local communities. (B) The government has launched several schemes to promote financial literacy in rural areas. (C) Without their involvement, the awareness drives remain mere formalities on paper. (D) These programmes aim to integrate the unbanked population into the formal financial system.
A. BADC
B. BDAC
C. BCDA
D. DABC
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Identify the opening sentence (B - independent introduction). Spot the mandatory pair: 'However' in (A) contrasts with the positive aim in (D), and 'Without their involvement' in (C) directly explains the consequence mentioned in (A). Sequence: B (Intro) -> D (Aim) -> A (Contrast/Condition) -> C (Elaboration). Step-by-step: 1. Sentence B introduces the topic (government schemes). 2. Sentence D elaborates on the aim of 'these programmes' (referencing B). 3. Sentence A introduces a contrast ('However') regarding success depending on participation. 4. Sentence C follows A logically ('Without their involvement' refers to 'participation of local communities' in A). Correct order: BDAC.

Q10. Quantitative Aptitude
If (a+b):(b+c):(c+a) = 6:7:8 and a+b+c = 14, then the value of c is:
A. 5
B. 7
C. 6
D. 4
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Let a+b=6k, b+c=7k, c+a=8k. Adding gives 2(a+b+c)=21k => a+b+c=10.5k=14 => k=4/3. Then c = ((b+c)+(c+a)-(a+b))/2 = (7k+8k-6k)/2 = 9k/2 = 9*(4/3)/2 = 6. Step-by-step: Set the three sums proportional to 6,7,8 with factor k. Sum all three equations: 2(a+b+c)=21k. Substitute given sum 14 to get k=4/3. Compute c using combination: c = ( (b+c)+(c+a)-(a+b) )/2 = (7k+8k-6k)/2 = 9k/2 = 6.
Q11. Reasoning Ability
In a row of students facing North, A is 12th from the left end and B is 18th from the right end. If they interchange their positions, A becomes 25th from the left end. How many students are there in the row?
A. 40
B. 41
C. 42
D. 43
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: When two persons interchange, Total Persons = (New Position of 1st from Left) + (Old Position of 2nd from Right) - 1. Total = 25 + 18 - 1 = 42. Full Solution: After interchange, A occupies B's original position. So B's original position from left = 25. B's original position from right = 18. Total = (Pos from Left + Pos from Right) - 1 = 25 + 18 - 1 = 42. Verification: A's old pos 12, B's old pos from left 25. Gap = 12 persons between. Total = 12 + 1 + 12 + 1 + 17 = 42.
Q12. Quantitative Aptitude
What approximate value should come in place of the question mark (?) in the following question? (You are not expected to calculate the exact value) 42.05² + 28.99² - 15.01² = ?
A. 2360
B. 2370
C. 2380
D. 2390
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Approximate to nearest integers: 42² + 29² - 15². Use unit digit method: 2²=4, 9²=1 (unit digit 1), 5²=5. Unit digit of result = 4 + 1 - 5 = 0. Only option ending in 0 is 2380. Verification: 42²=1764, 29²=841, 15²=225. 1764 + 841 - 225 = 2380. Full: 42.05² ≈ 1768.2, 28.99² ≈ 840.4, 15.01² ≈ 225.3. Sum ≈ 1768.2 + 840.4 - 225.3 = 2383.3 ≈ 2380.
Q13. English Language
Read the sentence to find out whether there is any grammatical error in it. The error, if any, will be in one part of the sentence. **Neither the Principal nor the teachers was present at the meeting.**
A. Neither the Principal
B. nor the teachers
C. was present at
D. the meeting
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Apply the Proximity Rule for 'Neither...nor' — the verb agrees with the nearest subject ('teachers', plural), so it must be 'were'. Scan the verb phrase in the options. Worked Solution: In correlative conjunctions like 'Neither...nor', the verb agrees with the noun/pronoun closest to it (Proximity Rule). Here, 'teachers' (plural) is nearest to the verb. Thus, the plural verb 'were' is required instead of the singular 'was'. The error lies in the third part: 'was present at'.
Q14. English Language
Which of the following phrases (A), (B), (C), (D) given below the sentence should replace the phrase printed in bold to make the sentence grammatically correct? If the sentence is correct as it is, mark (D) as the answer. Had he known about the traffic diversion, he **would reach** the venue on time.
A. will reach
B. will have reached
C. would have reached
D. would reach
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut/Trick: Recognize the Third Conditional structure (Impossible past condition). 'If' clause (or inverted 'Had' clause) uses Past Perfect (Had known). Main clause MUST use 'Would have + V3' (Perfect Infinitive). Eliminate 'would reach' (Second Conditional) and 'will' forms (First Conditional/Future). Step-by-step: 1. Identify structure: 'Had he known' = Past Perfect (Inverted Third Conditional 'If he had known'). 2. Rule: Third Conditional Result = Would/Could/Might + Have + Past Participle. 3. Check options: 'would reach' (Simple - wrong), 'will reach/will have reached' (Future - wrong for past hypothesis). 4. 'would have reached' fits the rule perfectly.
