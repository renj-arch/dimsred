C. 40
D. 41
Correct Answer: B

A. 7

C. 3
D. 9
Correct Answer: A

A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Let the numbers be 13x and 13y (where x, y are co-prime). Then 13(x+y)=156 → x+y=12. Find co-prime pairs (x,y) summing to 12: (1,11) and (5,7) only. Hence 2 pairs. Full Method: Since HCF is 13, numbers are 13a and 13b with gcd(a,b)=1. Sum = 13(a+b)=156 → a+b=12. Possible positive integer pairs (a,b) with a≤b: (1,11), (2,10), (3,9), (4,8), (5,7), (6,6). Only (1,11) and (5,7) are co-prime. Thus, exactly 2 valid pairs exist: (13, 143) and (65, 91).
