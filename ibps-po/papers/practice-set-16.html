C. 43
D. 40
Correct Answer: B

Q13. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-23
Pipe A can fill a tank in 12 hours and Pipe B can fill it in 15 hours. Pipe C can empty the full tank in 20 hours. If all three pipes are opened simultaneously when the tank is empty, in how many hours will the tank be filled?
A. 8 hours
B. 10 hours
C. 12 hours
D. 15 hours
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (LCM Method): Take LCM of 12, 15, and 20 = 60 units (Total Capacity). Efficiency of A = 60/12 = 5 units/hr. Efficiency of B = 60/15 = 4 units/hr. Efficiency of C = -60/20 = -3 units/hr (negative for outlet). Net Efficiency = 5 + 4 - 3 = 6 units/hr. Time = Capacity / Net Efficiency = 60 / 6 = 10 hours. Step-by-step: Part filled by A in 1 hour = 1/12. Part filled by B in 1 hour = 1/15. Part emptied by C in 1 hour = 1/20. Net part filled in 1 hour = 1/12 + 1/15 - 1/20 = (5 + 4 - 3) / 60 = 6/60 = 1/10. Therefore, time required to fill the tank = 1 / (1/10) = 10 hours.

D. Only I

Check III: B ≥ C ≤ D ⇒ Relation between B and D cannot be established (False). Thus Only I and II follow. Full Solution: 1. Decode: A # B → A > B; B @ C → B ≥ C; C % D → C ≤ D; D * E → D = E; E $ F → E < F. 2. Combined: A > B ≥ C ≤ D = E < F. 3. Evaluate I: A > C (True). 4. Evaluate II: F > D (True). 5.
