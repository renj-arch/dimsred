Q7. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-16
A boat takes 8 hours to travel a certain distance upstream and 6 hours to travel the same distance downstream. If the speed of the stream is 2 km/h, what is the speed of the boat in still water?
A. 12 km/h
B. 14 km/h
C. 16 km/h
D. 18 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (Ratio Method): Upstream Time : Downstream Time = 8 : 6 = 4 : 3. Since distance is constant, Speed Ratio (Up:Down) = 3 : 4. Let upstream speed = 3x, downstream speed = 4x. Speed of stream = (Down - Up)/2 = (4x - 3x)/2 = x/2. Given x/2 = 2 => x = 4. Speed of boat in still water = (Up + Down)/2 = (3x + 4x)/2 = 3.5x = 14 km/h. Full Method: Let boat speed = u, stream = 2. Distance = (u-2)*8 = (u+2)*6 => 8u - 16 = 6u + 12 => 2u = 28 => u = 14 km/h.
Research Team · ~5s · Formula Shortcut
  1. Let t₁=8 upstream, t₂=6 downstream, s=2 km/h.
  2. Use b = s(t₁+t₂)/(t₁−t₂).
  3. Compute b = 2×(8+6)/(8−6) = 2×14/2 = 14 km/h.

A train 240 metres long crosses a platform 360 metres long in 30 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
A. 60 km/h
B. 72 km/h
C. 84 km/h
D. 90 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Total distance = 240 + 360 = 600 m. Speed in m/s = 600/30 = 20 m/s. Convert to km/h by multiplying by 18/5 (or 3.6): 20 × 3.6 = 72 km/h. Full Method: Distance covered = Length of train + Length of platform = 600 m. Time = 30 s. Speed = Distance/Time = 20 m/s. 1 m/s = 18/5 km/h. Speed = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h.
