A person invests Rs. 5000 at a 10% interest rate. How much will he get after 1 year?
A. Rs. 5000
B. Rs. 5500
C. Rs. 6000
D. Rs. 6500
Correct Answer: B
The interest earned in 1 year is 5000 * 10% = 500. Therefore, the total amount after 1 year is 5000 + 500 = 5500.

Q7. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
The sum of three consecutive even integers is 60. What is the largest of the three integers?
A. 20
B. 22
C. 24
D. 26
Correct Answer: B
Let the three consecutive even integers be x, x+2, and x+4. We are given that their sum is 60, so x + (x+2) + (x+4) = 60. Simplifying this equation, we get 3x + 6 = 60. Subtracting 6 from both sides gives 3x = 54. Dividing both sides by 3 gives x = 18. Therefore, the three integers are 18, 20, and 22. The largest of the three integers is 22.
Q8. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
A shopkeeper gives a 20% discount on the marked price and still makes a 10% profit. If the cost price is Rs. 800, what is the marked price?
A. Rs. 1000
B. Rs. 1100
C. Rs. 1200
D. Rs. 900
Correct Answer: B
SP = CP + 10% of CP = 800 + 80 = 880. Discount = 20% on MP => SP = 80% of MP => 880 = 0.8 * MP => MP = 880/0.8 = 1100.

Q12. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
If the simple interest on a sum of money for 2 years at 5% per annum is Rs. 500, what is the compound interest on the same sum for the same period at the same rate?
A. Rs. 512.50
B. Rs. 525
C. Rs. 500
D. Rs. 550
Correct Answer: A
SI = P*R*T/100 => 500 = P*5*2/100 => P = 5000. CI = P*(1+R/100)^T - P = 5000*(1.05)^2 - 5000 = 5000*1.1025 - 5000 = 5512.50 - 5000 = 512.50.

Q14. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
If 5 @ 3 = 28 and 7 @ 4 = 53, then 6 @ 2 = ?
A. 34
B. 40
C. 36
D. 38
Correct Answer: B
5^2 + 3 = 25 + 3 = 28. 7^2 + 4 = 49 + 4 = 53. So 6^2 + 2 = 36 + 2 = 38.
