Q3. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
A car travels from city A to city B at an average speed of 50 km/h and returns at an average speed of 60 km/h. What is the average speed for the entire trip?
A. 54 km/h
B. 55 km/h
C. 56 km/h
D. 57 km/h
Correct Answer: A
The average speed for the entire trip is the total distance divided by the total time. Let the distance between the cities be x. The time taken to travel from A to B is x/50, and the time taken to travel from B to A is x/60. The total time is x/50 + x/60 = (6x + 5x)/300 = 11x/300. The average speed is 2x / (11x/300) = 600/11 = 54.55 km/h, which is approximately 54 km/h.

Q5. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
A car travels from city A to city B at an average speed of 60 km/h and returns at an average speed of 40 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire trip?
A. 45 km/h
B. 48 km/h
C. 50 km/h
D. 55 km/h
Correct Answer: B
To find the average speed for the entire trip, we need to find the total distance and the total time. Let's assume the distance from city A to city B is x km. The time taken to travel from A to B is x/60 hours, and the time taken to return is x/40 hours. The total time is x/60 + x/40 hours. The total distance is 2x km. The average speed is the total distance divided by the total time, which is 2x / (x/60 + x/40). To add the fractions in the denominator, we need a common denominator, which is 120. So, the average speed is 2x / (2x/120 + 3x/120) = 2x / (5x/120) = 2x * 120 / 5x = 240 / 5 = 48 km/h.

A person invests Rs. 1000 at a 5% interest rate. How much will he get after 2 years?
A. Rs. 1000
B. Rs. 1050
C. Rs. 1102.50
D. Rs. 1200
Correct Answer: C
The interest earned in the first year is 1000 * 5% = 50. The interest earned in the second year is 1050 * 5% = 52.50. Therefore, the total amount after 2 years is 1000 + 50 + 52.50 = 1102.50.

Q13. [+1, -0.25] General Awareness
The term 'Laffer Curve' is associated with which of the following?
A. Economic growth
B. Fiscal policy
C. Taxation and revenue
D. Monetary policy
Correct Answer: C
The Laffer Curve is a concept in economics that shows the relationship between tax rates and tax revenue. It suggests that as tax rates increase, tax revenue initially increases but eventually decreases as higher tax rates discourage economic activity. The curve is named after economist Arthur Laffer, who popularized the idea.
Q14. [+1, -0.25] Quantitative Aptitude
The average of 10 numbers is 20. If each number is increased by 2, what is the new average?
A. 20
B. 21
C. 22
D. 24
Correct Answer: C
The sum of the 10 numbers is 10 * 20 = 200. If each number is increased by 2, the new sum is 200 + 10 * 2 = 220. The new average is 220 / 10 = 22.
