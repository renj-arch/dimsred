Q1. English Language
Fill in the blanks with the most appropriate pair of words: The _______ of the rare manuscript in the library archives was a moment of great academic triumph; however, the _______ caused by decades of neglect had rendered several crucial pages illegible.
A. A) discovery, deterioration
B. B) invention, restoration
C. C) recovery, preservation
D. D) loss, conservation
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (Elimination by Context Polarity): The first blank follows 'moment of great academic triumph', requiring a positive word like 'discovery' or 'recovery'. This eliminates Option D ('loss'). The second blank follows 'decades of neglect' and results in 'illegible' pages, requiring a negative word like 'deterioration'. This eliminates Option B ('restoration' - positive) and Option C ('preservation' - positive/preventive). Option A ('discovery', 'deterioration') fits both the positive and negative contexts perfectly. Step-by-step: 1. Analyze Blank 1 context: 'triumph' signals a positive event. 'Discovery' and 'recovery' fit; 'invention' is illogical for a manuscript; 'loss' contradicts 'triumph'. 2. Analyze Blank 2 context: 'neglect' and 'illegible' signal damage. 'Deterioration' means worsening condition; 'restoration', 'preservation', 'conservation' imply care or improvement. 3. Only Option A satisfies both blanks simultaneously.
Q2. Quantitative Aptitude
Pipe A and Pipe B can fill a tank in 10 hours and 15 hours respectively. Pipe C can empty the full tank in 12 hours. All three pipes are opened simultaneously. After 3 hours, Pipe C is closed. How much additional time (in hours) will be required to fill the tank completely?
A. 4.5
B. 5
C. 4
D. 6
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (LCM Method): Take LCM of 10, 15, 12 = 60 units (Tank Capacity). Efficiency: A = 60/10 = 6 units/hr; B = 60/15 = 4 units/hr; C = -60/12 = -5 units/hr. Combined efficiency (A+B+C) = 5 units/hr. Work done in first 3 hours = 5 × 3 = 15 units. Remaining work = 60 - 15 = 45 units. After C closes, efficiency (A+B) = 10 units/hr. Time required = 45/10 = 4.5 hours. Full Step-by-Step: Let tank capacity be 60 litres (LCM of times). Pipe A fills 6 L/hr, Pipe B fills 4 L/hr, Pipe C empties 5 L/hr. Net fill rate with all open = (6+4-5) = 5 L/hr. In first 3 hours, volume filled = 5 × 3 = 15 litres. Remaining volume = 45 litres. Now only A and B work at combined rate 10 L/hr. Time = 45 / 10 = 4.5 hours.
Q3. Quantitative Aptitude
In the following questions, two equations numbered I and II are given. You have to solve both the equations and choose the correct option. **I.** 3x² - 14x + 16 = 0 **II.** 3y² - 16y + 21 = 0
A. x > y
B. x < y
C. x ≥ y
D. x = y or relationship cannot be established
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use the Product-Sum factorization trick. For Eq I (3x² - 14x + 16), product = 48, sum = -14 → split as -6 and -8. Roots: 2, 8/3 (2.67). For Eq II (3y² - 16y + 21), product = 63, sum = -16 → split as -9 and -7. Roots: 3, 7/3 (2.33). Comparing sets {2, 2.67} and {3, 2.33}: 2 < both y-values, but 2.67 > 2.33 and < 3. No consistent relation exists. Full Solution: I. 3x² - 6x - 8x + 16 = 0 → 3x(x-2) - 8(x-2) = 0 → (x-2)(3x-8)=0 → x = 2, 8/3. II. 3y² - 9y - 7y + 21 = 0 → 3y(y-3) - 7(y-3) = 0 → (y-3)(3y-7)=0 → y = 3, 7/3. Since x=2 < y=3, 7/3 but x=8/3 > y=7/3 and x=8/3 < y=3, the relationship between x and y cannot be established.

Q6. English Language
Two sentences are given below. Choose the connector from the options that can combine them into a single sentence **without changing the meaning**, maintaining grammatical correctness and formal tone. **Sentence 1:** The regulatory authority has mandated strict KYC norms for all digital wallets. **Sentence 2:** Several fintech startups have requested an extension of the compliance deadline.
A. Whereas the regulatory authority has mandated strict KYC norms for all digital wallets, several fintech startups have requested an extension of the compliance deadline.
B. The regulatory authority has mandated strict KYC norms for all digital wallets; moreover, several fintech startups have requested an extension of the compliance deadline.
C. Since the regulatory authority has mandated strict KYC norms for all digital wallets, several fintech startups have requested an extension of the compliance deadline.
D. The regulatory authority has mandated strict KYC norms for all digital wallets; nevertheless, several fintech startups have requested an extension of the compliance deadline.

A. that the report be submitted

Q12. Reasoning Ability
Statements: Some Pens are Pencils. All Pencils are Erasers. No Eraser is a Sharpener. Conclusions: I. Some Pens are not Sharpeners. II. All Pens are Erasers. Select the correct option:
A. Only Conclusion I follows
B. Only Conclusion II follows
C. Both Conclusions I and II follow
D. Neither Conclusion I nor II follows
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the chain rule (Some + All = Some; Some + No = Some Not). 1) Some Pens are Pencils + All Pencils are Erasers = Some Pens are Erasers. 2) Some Pens are Erasers + No Eraser is Sharpener = Some Pens are not Sharpeners (Conclusion I follows). Conclusion II ('All Pens are Erasers') is not definite because 'Some Pens are Pencils' does not cover all Pens. Full Solution: Draw minimal Venn diagrams. The 'Some Pens' overlapping 'Pencils' must overlap 'Erasers' and thus cannot touch 'Sharpeners', proving I. The non-overlapping part of Pens may or may not be Erasers, so II is possible but not definite.
Q13. Reasoning Ability
In a certain code language: 'P × Q' means 'P is the wife of Q'. 'P ÷ Q' means 'P is the father of Q'. 'P + Q' means 'P is the son of Q'. 'P – Q' means 'P is the sister of Q'. How is M related to K in the expression 'M + N ÷ O – K'?
A. Sister
B. Brother
C. Father
D. Nephew
Correct Answer: B
**Shortcut (Generation Gap & Gender Check):** 1. Identify gender of M: 'M + N' → M is **Son** (Male). Eliminate 'Sister'. 2. Find generation gap between M and K: - M + N (Gap +1, M lower) - N ÷ O (Gap -1, N higher) → Net Gap 0 (M, O same gen) - O – K (Gap 0, Siblings) → Net Gap 0 (M, K same gen). 3. Same generation + Male = **Brother**. **Step-by-Step:** - 'M + N' means M is the son of N. - 'N ÷ O' means N is the father of O. Thus, N is father of both M and O → M and O are siblings. - 'O – K' means O is the sister of K. Thus, O and K are siblings. - Since N is father of O and K, M, O, and K are all children of N (siblings). - M is male (Son). Therefore, M is the **Brother** of K.
