Q15. Quantitative Aptitudemodel-generated · 2026-11-15
In what ratio must a grocer mix two varieties of tea costing Rs. 60 per kg and Rs. 80 per kg so that the mixture is worth Rs. 72 per kg?
A. 3:2
B. 4:5
C. 5:4
D. 2:3
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Alligation rule: Cheaper=60, Dearer=80, Mean=72. Differences: 80-72=8, 72-60=12. Ratio (cheaper:dearer) = 8:12 = 2:3. Step-by-step: Write costs 60 and 80 with mean 72 in the middle. Subtract diagonally: 80-72=8, 72-60=12. The required mixing ratio is the inverse of these differences, i.e., 8:12 = 2:3 (cheaper to dearer).
