C. Aunt
D. Great Grandmother
Correct Answer: A

D. 95 kg
Correct Answer: B

Full Solution: Let the initial average weight be 'x' kg. Total weight of 8 persons initially = 8x kg. New average = (x + 2.5) kg. Total weight of 8 persons now = 8(x + 2.5) = 8x + 20 kg. Two trains of lengths 160 m and 200 m are running on parallel tracks in opposite directions at speeds of 60 km/h and 48 km/h respectively. In how many seconds will they completely cross each other?
A. 10
B. 12
C. 15
D. 18
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Relative speed (opposite direction) = (60 + 48) = 108 km/h. Convert to m/s: 108 × (5/18) = 30 m/s. Total distance = Sum of lengths = 160 + 200 = 360 m. Time = Distance/Speed = 360/30 = 12 seconds. Full: Speed of first train = 60 × 5/18 = 50/3 m/s. Speed of second train = 48 × 5/18 = 40/3 m/s. Relative speed = 50/3 + 40/3 = 30 m/s. Total length = 360 m. Required time = 360 / 30 = 12 seconds.
