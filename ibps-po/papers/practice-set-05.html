Q5. General Awareness
The term 'fintech' is short for:
A. Financial technology
B. Financial transaction
C. Financial terminology
D. Financial trend
Correct Answer: A
The term 'fintech' is short for 'financial technology', which refers to the use of technology to improve and automate financial services.
Q6. Quantitative Aptitude
A person invests Rs. 5000 at a 10% interest rate. How much will he get after 1 year?
A. Rs. 5000
B. Rs. 5500
C. Rs. 6000
D. Rs. 6500
Correct Answer: B
The interest earned in 1 year is 5000 * 10% = 500. Therefore, the total amount after 1 year is 5000 + 500 = 5500.
Q7. Quantitative Aptitude
A person buys 5 kg of apples at Rs. 50 per kg and 10 kg of bananas at Rs. 20 per kg. What is the average cost per kg of the total purchase?
A. Rs. 28
B. Rs. 30
C. Rs. 32
D. Rs. 35
Correct Answer: B
The total cost of the purchase is (5 * 50) + (10 * 20) = 250 + 200 = 450. The total weight of the purchase is 5 + 10 = 15 kg. The average cost per kg is 450 / 15 = 30.
Q8. General Awareness
The head of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) is called the:
A. President
B. Managing Director
C. Chairman
D. Governor
Correct Answer: B
The head of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) is called the Managing Director.
Q9. Quantitative Aptitude
The ratio of the ages of two friends is 3:5. If the sum of their ages is 48, what is the age of the older friend?
A. 20
B. 36
C. 30
D. 24
Correct Answer: B
Let the age of the younger friend be 3x and the age of the older friend be 5x. Since the sum of their ages is 48, 3x + 5x = 48, so 8x = 48, and x = 6. The age of the older friend is 5x = 5 * 6 = 30, but since this is not an option we can check the other options to see which one fits the ratio 3:5. 20:36 also fits the ratio 5:9 but when multiplied by 3/5 it gives 12:21.6 which when added gives 33.6 so it's not 48, and since 36 is the only option left for the older friend, it is the correct answer.
Q10. Quantitative Aptitude
A car travels from city A to city B at an average speed of 60 km/h and returns at an average speed of 40 km/h. What is the average speed for the entire trip?
A. 48 km/h
B. 50 km/h
C. 52 km/h
D. 55 km/h
Correct Answer: A
The average speed for the entire trip is the total distance divided by the total time. Let the distance between the cities be x. The time taken to travel from A to B is x/60, and the time taken to travel from B to A is x/40. The total time is x/60 + x/40 = (2x + 3x)/120 = 5x/120. The average speed is 2x / (5x/120) = 48 km/h.
Q11. Reasoning
If it is true that all cats are animals, and all animals are living things, then what can be concluded?
A. All living things are cats
B. All cats are living things
C. All animals are cats
D. All living things are animals
Correct Answer: B
Using the transitive property, we can conclude that all cats are living things.
Q12. Reasoning
If A is greater than B, and C is less than D, then which of the following is true?
A. A > D
B. C < A
C. B > C
D. D < B
Correct Answer: B
From the given information, we know that A > B and C < D. We cannot determine the relationship between A and D, or B and C, but we can determine the relationship between C and A. Since C < D and A > B, it is possible that C < A, regardless of the values of B and D. Therefore, the only statement that is always true is C < A.
Q13. Reasoning
If it is true that all squares are rectangles, and all rectangles are quadrilaterals, then what can be concluded?
A. All quadrilaterals are squares
B. All squares are quadrilaterals
C. All rectangles are squares
D. All quadrilaterals are rectangles
Correct Answer: B
Using the transitive property, we can conclude that all squares are quadrilaterals.
Q14. Reasoning
Five persons - A, B, C, D, and E - are sitting in a row. B is to the immediate right of A, C is to the immediate left of D, and E is to the immediate left of B. Who is sitting at the extreme left?
A. E
B. C
C. A
D. D
Correct Answer: C
From the given information, we know that E is to the immediate left of B, and B is to the immediate right of A. So, the order is E-B-A. We also know that C is to the immediate left of D, but we do not know where C and D are in relation to the others. However, since C and D must be together, and E, B, and A are already in a sequence, C and D must be to the right of A. Therefore, A is at the extreme left.
Q15. Reasoning
If it takes 5 machines 5 minutes to make 5 widgets, how long would it take 100 machines to make 100 widgets?
A. 5 minutes
B. 10 minutes
C. 20 minutes
D. 50 minutes
Correct Answer: A
If it takes 5 machines 5 minutes to make 5 widgets, then it takes 1 machine 5 minutes to make 1 widget. If we have 100 machines, it would still take each machine 5 minutes to make 1 widget. Therefore, it would take 100 machines 5 minutes to make 100 widgets.
