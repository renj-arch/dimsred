Two inlet pipes A and B can fill a tank in 24 minutes and 32 minutes respectively. An outlet pipe C can empty the full tank in 16 minutes. If all three pipes are opened simultaneously, in how many minutes will the tank be filled?
A. 72 minutes
B. 84 minutes
C. 96 minutes
D. 108 minutes
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut (LCM/Unit Method): Take tank capacity = LCM(24, 32, 16) = 96 units. Efficiency of A = 96/24 = 4 units/min; B = 96/32 = 3 units/min; C = -96/16 = -6 units/min. Net efficiency = 4 + 3 - 6 = 1 unit/min. Time = 96/1 = 96 minutes. Full Solution: Part filled by A in 1 min = 1/24; by B = 1/32; emptied by C = 1/16. Net part filled in 1 min = 1/24 + 1/32 - 1/16 = (4 + 3 - 6)/96 = 1/96. Therefore, time required to fill the tank = 96 minutes.

D. 40 days
Correct Answer: A

A's 1 day = 1/12 - 1/24 = 1/24. A alone = 24 days.
