Q3. Reasoning
Five persons - A, B, C, D, and E - are sitting in a row. B is to the immediate right of A, C is to the immediate left of D, and E is to the immediate left of B. Who is sitting at the extreme left?
A. E
B. C
C. A
D. D
Correct Answer: C
From the given information, we know that E is to the immediate left of B, and B is to the immediate right of A. So, the order is E-B-A. We also know that C is to the immediate left of D, but we do not know where C and D are in relation to the others. However, since C and D must be together, and E, B, and A are already in a sequence, C and D must be to the right of A. Therefore, A is at the extreme left.
Q4. Reasoning
A spy starts at a secure point 'X' facing North. His mission requires him to execute the following sequence of commands relative to his CURRENT direction, where every command rotates his internal compass by 90 degrees clockwise AFTER execution: 1. Move forward 5 units. 2. Turn Left (relative to current face) and move forward 5 units. 3. Turn Right (relative to current face) and move forward 5 units. 4. Turn Left (relative to current face) and move forward 5 units. However, due to a magnetic anomaly affecting Sector X, every time the spy executes a command that involves turning LEFT, his body rotates an additional 90 degrees clockwise immediately after the turn but BEFORE moving. If he turns RIGHT normally, no such rotation occurs. After completing all 4 steps, in which cardinal direction is the spy facing? A) North B) South C) East D) West
A. North
B. South
C. East
D. West
Correct Answer: C
Start: Facing North. Step 1 (Forward): Still facing North. Step 2 (Turn Left relative to North -> West; Magnetic anomaly adds +90 deg clockwise due to 'Left' turn command). New orientation: West rotated by +90 deg = North. Move 5 units while facing North. Step 3 (Turn Right relative to current North -> East). No anomaly for 'Right'. Orientation remains East. Move 5 units while facing East. Step 4 (Turn Left relative to East -> North; Magnetic anomaly adds +90 deg clockwise due to 'Left' turn command). New orientation: North rotated by +90 deg = East. Move 5 units. Final Facing Direction: East.

Q10. Quantitative Aptitude
A prudent investor splits a total sum of Rs. 20,000 into two parts. Part A is invested at 6% simple interest per annum, and Part B is invested at 8% simple interest per annum. If the combined annual interest earned from both parts is exactly equal to the simple interest on the entire Rs. 20,000 invested at a blended rate of 7% per annum for one year, what is the amount invested at 6%?
A. Rs. 10,000
B. Rs. 15,000
C. Rs. 8,000
D. Rs. 12,000
Correct Answer: A
1. Total Sum = Rs. 20,000. 2. Target Interest (Target Rate) = 7% of 20,000 = Rs. 1,400. 3. Let Part A = x (at 6%) and Part B = 20,000 - x (at 8%). 4. Equation: 0.06x + 0.08(20,000 - x) = 1,400. 5. Simplify: 0.06x + 1,600 - 0.08x = 1,400. 6. Rearrange: -0.02x = -200. 7. Solve: x = 200 / 0.02 = 10,000.
Q11. Reasoning
Find the odd one out: 27, 64, 125, 216, 341
A. 27
B. 341
C. 125
D. 216
Correct Answer: B
27 = 3^3, 64 = 4^3, 125 = 5^3 and 216 = 6^3 are perfect cubes. 341 is not a perfect cube (7^3 = 343), so 341 is the odd one out.

Q13. Quantitative Aptitude
A trader marks up the Cost Price (CP) of a commodity by 25% to get the Marked Price (MP). He then offers a 10% discount on this MP. If the final Selling Price (SP) is Rs. 270, what is the trader's profit percentage on the CP?
A. 15%
B. 12.5%
C. 10%
D. 8%
Correct Answer: B
Let CP = 100. MP = 100 + 25% = 125. SP = 125 - 10% = 125 * 0.9 = 112.5. Profit = SP - CP = 112.5 - 100 = 12.5. Profit % = (12.5 / 100) * 100 = 12.5%.
Q14. English Language
In a newly formed administrative zone, the Governor issued an order to prevent the duplication of duties among officials who are already working in the same department. This act of assigning two or more responsibilities to a single official is known as: A) Embezzlement B) Pluralism C) Congruence D) Accumulation
A. Embezzlement
B. Pluralism
C. Congruence
D. Accumulation
Correct Answer: B
The question describes the assignment of multiple roles to one person. In a bureaucratic context, holding or performing more than one public office is termed 'Pluralism' (or Plurality of offices), leading to redundancy. Embezzlement is theft; Congruence means agreement; Accumulation is gathering but lacks the specific administrative connotation of overlapping duties.
