What is inside every course

  • Script & sounds — the alphabet or writing system, stroke order, and the sound rules that change words in real speech.
  • Grammar reference — word order, particles or cases, verb forms, politeness, counters and the structures you actually reuse.
  • Topic chapters — greetings, introductions, numbers, time, food, directions, shopping, health, weather, family and opinions.
  • Drills — flashcards, listen-and-type practice, and four self-check quizzes per language.
  • Culture notes — register, gesture and usage traps that a phrasebook skips.

The site agent extends the thinnest chapters on each GitHub run, so a course you open today can be longer next week. Progress is stored in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
