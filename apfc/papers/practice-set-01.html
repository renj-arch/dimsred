Q18. Accountancy, Auditing & Insurance
A factory building valued at ₹20,00,000 is insured for ₹12,00,000 under a fire insurance policy subject to the Average Clause. A fire occurs causing a loss of ₹4,00,000. What is the amount of claim payable by the insurer?
A. ₹4,00,000
B. ₹2,40,000
C. ₹2,00,000
D. ₹1,20,000
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (Ratio Elimination): The Average Clause applies proportionally: Claim = (Sum Insured / Actual Value) × Loss. The insurance ratio is 12/20 = 3/5 (60%). Since the policy covers only 60% of the value, the insurer pays only 60% of the loss. 60% of ₹4,00,000 = ₹2,40,000. Eliminate Option A (full loss paid only if fully insured), Option D (sum insured amount, irrelevant to loss calculation), and Option C (50% of loss, incorrect ratio). Step-by-Step: 1. Identify Actual Value = ₹20,00,000; Sum Insured = ₹12,00,000; Loss = ₹4,00,000. 2. Apply Average Clause Formula: Claim = (Sum Insured / Actual Value) × Actual Loss. 3. Claim = (₹12,00,000 / ₹20,00,000) × ₹4,00,000. 4. Claim = (3/5) × ₹4,00,000 = ₹2,40,000.

Q23. Mathematics
A car covers a certain distance at a speed of 60 km/h and returns to the starting point at a speed of 40 km/h. What is the average speed (in km/h) for the entire journey?
A. 48
B. 50
C. 45
D. 52
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For equal distances, average speed is the Harmonic Mean of the speeds = 2ab/(a+b) = 2×60×40/(60+40) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h. Full: Let one-way distance be d km. Total distance = 2d. Time for first leg = d/60 hrs, return leg = d/40 hrs. Total time = d(1/60 + 1/40) = d(5/120) = d/24 hrs. Average speed = Total Distance / Total Time = 2d / (d/24) = 48 km/h.

Q25. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of the product (3^47) × (7^53)?
A. 1
B. 7
C. 3
D. 9
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Cyclicity of 3 is 4 (3,9,7,1); 47 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit 7. Cyclicity of 7 is 4 (7,9,3,1); 53 mod 4 = 1 → unit digit 7. Product unit digit = 7 × 7 = 49 → 9. Full: 3^47 = 3^(4×11+3), unit digit same as 3^3 = 27 → 7. 7^53 = 7^(4×13+1), unit digit same as 7^1 = 7. Multiplying unit digits: 7 × 7 = 49, hence unit digit is 9.
Q26. Accountancy, Auditing & Insurance
In an amalgamation in the nature of purchase, the purchase consideration is calculated under the Net Assets Method. The transferee company takes over assets of ₹50 Lakhs and liabilities of ₹15 Lakhs at agreed values. The purchase consideration is discharged by issuing 1,00,000 equity shares of ₹10 each at a premium of 20% and the balance in cash. What is the amount of cash paid?
A. ₹20 Lakhs
B. ₹23 Lakhs
C. ₹25 Lakhs
D. ₹18 Lakhs
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: PC (Net Asset Method) = Assets Taken Over – Liabilities Taken Over. Value of Shares = No. of Shares × Issue Price (FV + Premium). Cash = PC – Value of Shares. Step-by-step: 1. Purchase Consideration = ₹50 Lakhs (Assets) – ₹15 Lakhs (Liabilities) = ₹35 Lakhs. 2. Issue Price per Share = ₹10 (Face Value) + 20% Premium (₹2) = ₹12. 3. Value of Shares Issued = 1,00,000 shares × ₹12 = ₹12 Lakhs. 4. Cash Paid = ₹35 Lakhs – ₹12 Lakhs = ₹23 Lakhs.
Q27. Mathematics
The difference between Compound Interest (compounded annually) and Simple Interest on a certain principal for 2 years at 10% per annum is Rs. 50. What is the principal amount (in Rs.)?
A. 4000
B. 5000
C. 6000
D. 4500
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For 2 years, Difference (CI - SI) = P × (R/100)². Given Diff = 50, R = 10%. So, 50 = P × (10/100)² = P × 1/100. Therefore, P = 50 × 100 = 5000. Step-by-step: 1. Simple Interest for 2 years = P × 10 × 2 / 100 = 0.2P. 2. Compound Interest for 2 years: Amount = P(1 + 10/100)² = 1.21P. CI = 1.21P - P = 0.21P. 3. Difference = 0.21P - 0.2P = 0.01P. 4. Given 0.01P = 50 => P = 50 / 0.01 = 5000.

Q29. Mathematics
A vessel contains 40 litres of pure milk. 4 litres of milk is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated two more times (three times in total). What is the final quantity of milk (in litres) left in the vessel?
A. 29.16
B. 28.80
C. 30.00
D. 27.50
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the replacement formula: Final Quantity = Initial Quantity × (1 - Replaced Volume / Total Volume)^n. Here, 40 × (1 - 4/40)^3 = 40 × (9/10)^3 = 40 × 729/1000 = 29.16 litres. Step-by-step: 1. After 1st replacement: Milk left = 40 - 4 = 36 L. Concentration = 36/40 = 9/10. 2. After 2nd replacement: Milk removed = 4 × (9/10) = 3.6 L. Milk left = 36 - 3.6 = 32.4 L. 3. After 3rd replacement: Milk removed = 4 × (32.4/40) = 3.24 L. Milk left = 32.4 - 3.24 = 29.16 L.
