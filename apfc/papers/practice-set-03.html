Q7. Mathematics
In how many ways can 5 different books be arranged on a shelf such that two particular books are always together?
A. 24
B. 72
C. 48
D. 120
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Treat the two particular books as a single unit. Then we have 4 units to arrange, giving 4! = 24 arrangements. The two books can be arranged internally in 2! = 2 ways. Total = 24 × 2 = 48. Full steps: Consider the two books as one combined entity. The 4 entities (combined entity + 3 other books) can be permuted in 4! = 24 ways. Within the combined entity, the two books can be ordered in 2! = 2 ways. By the multiplication principle, total arrangements = 24 × 2 = 48.

Correct Answer: C

On 31st March 2024, the company issued bonus shares in the ratio of 1:1 (one bonus share for every one share held). The net profit for the year ended 31st March 2024 was ₹18,75,000. B. ₹10.00 and ₹10.00
C. ₹7.50 and ₹5.00
D. ₹7.50 and ₹6.67
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Bonus issue adjustment factor = (Shares after bonus / Shares before bonus) = 2. Q20. Mental Ability
At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles to each other for the first time?
A. 4:05 5/11
B. 4:38 2/11
C. 4:21 9/11
D. 4:32 8/11
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the formula |30H - 5.5M| = 90. For the first right angle between 4 and 5, the hour hand leads, so 30(4) - 5.5M = 90 → 120 - 5.5M = 90 → 5.5M = 30 → M = 60/11 = 5 5/11 minutes. Full Solution: At 4:00, the hour hand is at 120°. The minute hand gains 5.5° per minute on the hour hand. To form the first 90° angle, the minute hand must reduce the gap from 120° to 90°, i.e., cover 30° relatively. Time = Relative Distance / Relative Speed = 30° / 5.5° per min = 60/11 min = 5 5/11 min past 4.
Q21. Mathematics
What is the remainder when 7^103 is divided by 24?
A. 7
B. 1
C. 23
D. 0
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: 7^2 = 49 ≡ 1 (mod 24). Therefore, 7^103 = 7 × (7^2)^51 ≡ 7 × 1^51 = 7 (mod 24). Full Solution: 7^2 = 49 = 24×2 + 1. So 7^103 = 7 × (49)^51 = 7 × (24×2 + 1)^51. Expanding by Binomial Theorem, every term contains a factor of 24 except the last term 7×1. Hence, the remainder is 7.

Q23. Mathematics
What is the highest power of 5 that divides 50! (50 factorial) exactly?
A. 12
B. 10
C. 14
D. 11
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use Legendre's formula (successive division by 5). 50/5 = 10, 10/5 = 2, 2/5 = 0. Sum of quotients = 10 + 2 = 12. Full Solution: The exponent of a prime p in n! is given by [n/p] + [n/p^2] + ... For p=5, n=50: [50/5] = 10, [50/25] = 2, [50/125] = 0. Total power = 10 + 2 = 12.
