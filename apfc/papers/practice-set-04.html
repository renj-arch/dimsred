The length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and its breadth is decreased by 10%. What is the percentage change in the area of the rectangle?
A. 8% decrease
B. 8% increase
C. 10% increase
D. 2% increase
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Net % change in area = (L% + B% + (L% × B%)/100). Here, +20 + (-10) + (20 × -10)/100 = 10 - 2 = 8% increase. Step-by-step: Let original Length = L, Breadth = B. Original Area = L × B. New Length = 1.2L. New Breadth = 0.9B. New Area = 1.2L × 0.9B = 1.08 LB. Change in Area = 1.08LB - LB = 0.08LB. % Change = (0.08LB / LB) × 100 = 8%. Since positive, area increases by 8%.

A. 8 hours
B. 10 hours
C. 12 hours
D. 15 hours
Correct Answer: B
