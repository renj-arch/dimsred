Q3. Industrial Relations & Labour Codes
Under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020, what is the minimum percentage of workers' membership on the muster roll required for a Trade Union to be recognized as the sole Negotiating Union in an industrial establishment?
A. 30%
B. 51%
C. 66%
D. 75%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The Industrial Relations Code, 2020 establishes a clear majority threshold (51%) for a single union to act as the sole Negotiating Union. If no union achieves 51%, a Negotiating Council is formed comprising unions with at least 20% membership each. Eliminate 30% (too low for sole status), 66% (special majority, not required here), and 75% (constitutional amendment threshold). Full Solution: Section 14(1) of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 stipulates that where there is only one Trade Union in an industrial establishment, or where one Trade Union has not less than 51% of the workers on the muster roll as its members, that Trade Union shall be recognized as the Negotiating Union. If no Trade Union meets the 51% threshold, a Negotiating Council is constituted under Section 14(2) consisting of representatives of Trade Unions with at least 20% membership.

Q5. Mental Ability
A boat takes 4 hours to travel 24 km upstream and 3 hours to travel 27 km downstream. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
A. 6 km/h
B. 7 km/h
C. 7.5 km/h
D. 8 km/h
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Upstream speed = 24/4 = 6 km/h, Downstream speed = 27/3 = 9 km/h. Speed in still water = (Upstream + Downstream)/2 = (6+9)/2 = 7.5 km/h. Full method: Let boat speed = b, stream speed = s. Then b - s = 6 and b + s = 9; adding gives 2b = 15, so b = 7.5 km/h.

Q16. Mental Ability
A and B together can complete a piece of work in 12 days. B and C together can complete it in 15 days. C and A together can complete it in 20 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?
A. 20 days
B. 30 days
C. 40 days
D. 60 days
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (LCM/Efficiency Method): Take LCM of 12, 15, 20 = 60 units (Total Work). Efficiency of (A+B) = 60/12 = 5 units/day. Efficiency of (B+C) = 60/15 = 4 units/day. Efficiency of (C+A) = 60/20 = 3 units/day. Sum = 2(A+B+C) = 12 units/day => (A+B+C) = 6 units/day. Efficiency of A = 6 - 4 (B+C) = 2 units/day. Time for A = 60/2 = 30 days. Full Solution: 1 day work of (A+B) = 1/12, (B+C) = 1/15, (C+A) = 1/20. Adding: 2(A+B+C) = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = (5+4+3)/60 = 12/60 = 1/5. So (A+B+C) = 1/10. A's 1 day work = 1/10 - 1/15 = (3-2)/30 = 1/30. Hence A takes 30 days.

A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 15% on the marked price. What is his profit percentage?
A. 15%
B. 19%
C. 21%
D. 25%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (Successive Percentage Change Formula): Net % change = a + b + (ab/100), where a = +40 (markup) and b = -15 (discount). Net % = 40 - 15 + (40 × -15)/100 = 25 - 6 = 19% profit. Worked Solution: Let CP = ₹100. Marked Price = ₹140. Selling Price after 15% discount = 140 × (85/100) = ₹119. Profit = ₹19. Profit % = (19/100) × 100 = 19%.
Q26. Mathematics
A is twice as efficient as B. Working together, they can complete a piece of work in 12 days. In how many days can B alone complete the same work?
A. 18 days
B. 24 days
C. 36 days
D. 48 days
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut (Efficiency Ratio/LCM Method): Efficiency ratio A:B = 2:1. Total combined efficiency = 3 units/day. Total Work = 12 days × 3 units/day = 36 units. Time for B alone = Total Work / Efficiency of B = 36 units / 1 unit/day = 36 days. Worked Solution: Let B take 'x' days. A takes 'x/2' days. One day work of A = 2/x, B = 1/x. Combined one day work = 3/x = 1/12. Solving gives x = 36 days.

Q28. Mathematics
A man can row at 12 km/h in still water. He takes twice as long to row upstream as to row downstream for the same distance. What is the speed of the stream (in km/h)?
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Time ratio upstream:downstream = 2:1 ⇒ speed ratio upstream:downstream = 1:2. Let stream speed = x. Then (12−x):(12+x)=1:2 ⇒ 2(12−x)=12+x ⇒ x=4 km/h. Full solution: Let distance = d. Upstream time = d/(12−x), downstream = d/(12+x). Given d/(12−x)=2d/(12+x) ⇒ 12+x=24−2x ⇒ 3x=12 ⇒ x=4.
Q29. Accountancy, Auditing & Insurance
As per SA 705 (Modifications to the Opinion in the Independent Auditor's Report), which type of audit opinion is expressed when the auditor concludes that misstatements in the financial statements are both **material and pervasive**?
A. Unmodified Opinion
B. Qualified Opinion
C. Adverse Opinion
D. Disclaimer of Opinion
Correct Answer: C
**Shortcut (Materiality-Pervasiveness Matrix):** - Material but **Not** Pervasive → **Qualified** Opinion. - Material **AND** Pervasive → **Adverse** Opinion. - Unable to obtain evidence (Pervasive) → **Disclaimer** of Opinion. **Worked Solution:** SA 705 prescribes three types of modified opinions. A **Qualified Opinion** is given when misstatements are material but confined to specific items (not pervasive). An **Adverse Opinion** is required when the effect of misstatements is both material and pervasive, meaning the financial statements do not present a true and fair view as a whole. A **Disclaimer of Opinion** arises when the auditor cannot obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence (scope limitation) and the possible effects are pervasive. Since the question states misstatements are 'material and pervasive', the correct opinion is Adverse.
