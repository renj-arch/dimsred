As per AS 29 (Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets), which of the following conditions requires the recognition of a **Provision** in the financial statements?
A. A present obligation arising from a past event, where the outflow of resources is **remote**.
B. A **possible** obligation arising from a past event, where the outflow of resources is probable.
C. A present obligation arising from a past event, where the outflow of resources is **probable** and a reliable estimate can be made.
D. A present obligation arising from a past event, where no reliable estimate can be made.
Correct Answer: C
**Shortcut (The 3-P Rule):** A Provision requires three simultaneous conditions: **P**resent Obligation (not possible), **P**robable Outflow (>50%, not remote), **P**recise/Reliable Estimate. Scan options for all three. Only Option C satisfies all: Present Obligation + Probable Outflow + Reliable Estimate. Option A fails probability (Remote). Option B fails obligation type (Possible). Option D fails reliable estimate. **Step-by-Step:** 1. **AS 29 Para 14** states a provision is recognized only if: (a) An entity has a **present obligation** (legal or constructive) as a result of a **past event**; (b) It is **probable** (more likely than not) that an outflow of resources will be required to settle the obligation; (c) A **reliable estimate** can be made of the amount. 2. **Option A:** Outflow is 'remote' -> Disclose as Contingent Liability only if not remote, but here it is remote -> No recognition, no disclosure. 3. **Option B:** 'Possible' obligation -> Not a present obligation -> Treated as Contingent Liability (Disclosure only). 4. **Option C:** Matches all three criteria (Present, Probable, Reliable Estimate) -> **Recognize Provision**. 5. **Option D:** No reliable estimate -> Cannot recognize Provision -> Disclose as Contingent Liability.
