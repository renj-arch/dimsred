1. What the 55 GK questions actually test

General Knowledge is the heavyweight — 55 of 160 questions (34%). At APFC level it is overwhelmingly static GK: facts that don't change year to year. Current affairs has its own separate 15-question section, so GK here means the permanent map of Indian polity, history, geography, economy, science and culture.

Observed distribution in APFC papers:

2. High-yield static GK core

These are the facts that appear in nearly every APFC/SSC/RBI GK paper. Memorise the lists, not isolated facts.

Polity core

  • Constitution: adopted 26 Nov 1949, effective 26 Jan 1950; 395 Articles + 12 Schedules originally (now 470+ articles, 12 schedules); longest written constitution.
  • Key articles: 14 (equality), 19 (freedoms), 21 (life & liberty), 32 (constitutional remedies — "heart and soul" per Ambedkar), 368 (amendment), 370 (J&K special status — now abrogated Aug 2019).
  • Key schedules: 5th (Scheduled Tribes), 6th (tribal autonomous areas), 8th (official languages), 10th (anti-defection), 11th/12th (panchayats/municipalities), 12th (municipalities... actually 12th = municipalities, 11th = panchayats).
  • Institutions: UPSC (Art 315), Election Commission (Art 324), CAG (Art 148), Finance Commission (Art 280), NITI Aayog (2015, replaced Planning Commission).
  • Amendments you must know: 1st (speech restrictions), 42nd ("mini-constitution", 1976), 44th (property right removed, 1978), 52nd (anti-defection, 1985), 61st (voting age 18, 1989), 73rd/74th (panchayati raj/municipalities, 1992), 101st (GST, 2016), 103rd (10% EWS quota, 2019).

History core

  • Ancient: Indus Valley (Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Lothal, Dholavira; undeciphered script); Vedic period (Rigveda oldest); Mahajanapadas; Buddha (Lumbini, Bodh Gaya, Sarnath, Kushinagar — "Four sights"); Ashoka (Kalinga war 261 BCE, edicts, Dhamma); Gupta "Golden Age".
  • Medieval: Delhi Sultanate (Slave/Mamluk, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi); Mughals (Babur 1526 Panipat, Akbar's Din-i-Ilahi, Shah Jahan Taj Mahal, Aurangzeb); Bhakti/Sufi movements; Marathas under Shivaji.
  • Modern: 1857 revolt (Mangal Pandey, Rani Lakshmibai, Bahadur Shah Zafar); INC 1885 (A.O. Hume); Partition of Bengal 1905 (Curzon); Jallianwala Bagh 1919; Non-Cooperation 1920, Civil Disobedience 1930 (Dandi), Quit India 1942; INA (Subhas Bose); independence 15 Aug 1947.
  • Key dates: Plassey 1757, Buxar 1764, Regulating Act 1773, Pitt's India Act 1784, Sepoy Mutiny 1857, Government of India Act 1935.

Geography core

  • Physical: Himalayas (Purvanchal, Himadri, Himachal, Shivalik); Western/Eastern Ghats; Deccan plateau; Thar desert; Western Ghats = biodiversity hotspot.
  • Rivers: Ganga (Bhagirathi+Alaknanda at Devprayag; Gangotri glacier; Haridwar first city); Yamuna (longest tributary, joins at Prayagraj); Brahmaputra (Tsangpo in Tibet); Godavari (longest peninsular); Narmada & Tapi (west-flowing, rift valleys).
  • States/directions: Largest state by area = Rajasthan; smallest = Goa; most populous = UP; longest coastline = Gujarat; landlocked states = Bihar, Jharkhand, MP, Chhattisgarh, Telangana, Haryana; union territories count = 8 (incl. Ladakh, J&K, Delhi, Puducherry, Andaman, Lakshadweep, Chandigarh, Daman-Diu-Dadra-Nagar-Haveli).
  • Minerals/agriculture: Iron → Odisha/Chhattisgarh/Karnataka; bauxite → Odisha; coal → Jharkhand/Chhattisgarh/Odisha; black soil (regur) → Maharashtra/Gujarat cotton; alluvial → Ganga plain.

Economy core

  • Budget: Union Budget presented Feb 1 (since 2017); Finance Minister; types — balanced/surplus/deficit; revenue vs capital expenditure; FRBM Act 2003 (fiscal deficit target).
  • RBI: established 1935 (nationalised 1949); functions — monetary policy, banker's bank, lender of last resort, currency issue, forex reserves; repo rate (lending to banks), reverse repo, CRR, SLR.
  • Institutions: SEBI (capital markets), IRDAI (insurance), PFRDA (pension), NITI Aayog, GST Council (Article 279A), Finance Commission (fiscal devolution every 5 years).
  • Schemes (name + ministry): PM-KISAN (agriculture), Ayushman Bharat (health, ₹5 lakh cover), Ujjwala (LPG), Jan Dhan (banking), Make in India, Skill India, Digital India, Atmanirbhar Bharat, PM Awas Yojana.

Science core

  • Space: ISRO HQ Bengaluru; Chandrayaan-3 landed Aug 2023 (Shiv Shakti point); Gaganyaan (crewed mission); PSLV vs GSLV; NavIC = Indian regional navigation.
  • Defence: DRDO HQ Hyderabad; Agni missiles (Agni-V ICBM range); Tejas (light combat aircraft); INS Vikramaditya (aircraft carrier).
  • Everyday science: pH scale, Newton's laws, Ohm's law, photosynthesis equation, vitamin-deficiency diseases (A→night blindness, B1→beriberi, C→scurvy, D→rickets), blood groups (AB universal recipient, O universal donor), DNA/RNA, SI units.

Awards, books, sports

  • National awards: Bharat Ratna (highest civilian), Padma Vibhushan/Padma Bhushan/Padma Shri; Gallantry — Param Vir Chakra, Ashoka Chakra (highest peacetime), Mahavir Chakra, Vir Chakra.
  • Literature: Jnanpith (highest literary), Booker, Sahitya Akademi, Banu Prize; first Jnanpith = G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam, 1965).
  • Sports: Olympics/ CWG/Asian Games hosts; India's Olympic hockey golds; Tendulkar 100 international centuries; first Indian woman Grand Slam... (verify before memorising).

3. Subject-by-subject coverage map

Cover in this order — highest yield first:

  1. Week 1-2 — Polity: Constitution structure, fundamental rights/duties, directive principles, key articles, institutions, amendments list. One standard polity book or the NCERT Class 11-12 books.
  2. Week 3-4 — History: Ancient/medieval skim, deep modern (1857→1947). Timeline chart on one page.
  3. Week 5 — Geography: Physical map of India (rivers, passes, peaks, soil types), world map basics (continents, oceans, straits, capitals).
  4. Week 6 — Economy + Science: Budget/RBI/institutions; everyday science + ISRO/DRDO/current mission names.
  5. Week 7 — Awards/books/sports + revision cycles.
Pro Tip
Make three running lists as you study: (1) dates, (2) person↔work/institution, (3) place↔feature. 80% of GK MCQs are "match the pair" disguised as single facts — lists convert them into recognition, not recall.

4. Study method that sticks

  • 3-pass method: Pass 1 = read/learn the core lists; Pass 2 = MCQ drill on that subject (aim 70%+ before moving on); Pass 3 = mixed revision every 3 days.
  • NCERT-first: Class 6–10 NCERTs cover 60–70% of APFC static GK. Read them once with a GK eye, not a school eye.
  • Daily current-affairs note: even though CA is a separate section, last-6-months static events (summits, schemes, appointments, awards) leak into GK — one 15-min daily note covers both.
  • Elimination discipline: for unfamiliar GK questions, eliminate options that are "too extreme" (always, never, all) or from wrong categories before guessing.

5. Practice plan

Target: 40–45/55 in the real exam. That needs ~80% accuracy on static GK — achievable with the core lists above plus mock practice. Do 30 GK MCQs daily mixed across subjects; review every wrong answer by writing the correct fact in your list. In the last two weeks, switch to full mocks only — new GK facts have diminishing returns; accuracy on known material is the win.

Pro Tip
GK is where consistency beats intelligence: the same 500–800 facts recycle across APFC, SSC and banking papers. If you build the list once, it serves every exam on this site — that's why the micro-test General Knowledge pool now includes APFC questions too.
