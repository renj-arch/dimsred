Q1. Language II
A teacher writes the word 'ship' on the board, segments it into sounds /ʃ/ /ɪ/ /p/, asks students to repeat each sound, and then blend them to pronounce the word. This instructional strategy is a hallmark of which approach to teaching reading?
A. Whole Language Approach
B. Look-and-Say Method
C. Phonics Approach
D. Language Experience Approach
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Keywords 'segments into sounds', 'repeat each sound', 'blend them' define Synthetic Phonics. Eliminate Whole Language (meaning-focused), Look-and-Say (whole word memorization), Language Experience (learner-generated text). Worked: Phonics approach explicitly teaches grapheme-phoneme correspondences and the skill of blending (synthesising) sounds to decode words. The described steps—segmentation, articulation, blending—are the core instructional sequence of systematic synthetic phonics.
Q2. Language II
The lights in the house are off and there is no sound. The family ______ (go) to bed early last night.
A. must go
B. must have gone
C. should go
D. would go
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Spot the past time marker 'last night'. Modals of deduction for the past require perfect infinitive (have + V3). Only option B fits. Worked: 'Must' expresses strong certainty. For past deduction, structure is 'must have + past participle'. 'Must go' (present), 'should go' (advice), 'would go' (habitual past/future-in-past) do not indicate deduction about a completed past action.
Q3. Language I
A teacher demonstrates how to write a formal letter, then jointly constructs one with the class, and finally asks students to write independently while providing checklists. As students gain proficiency, the teacher withdraws the checklists. This instructional strategy aligns with which concept?
A. Drill and Practice
B. Scaffolding
C. Language Immersion
D. Phonics Instruction
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Eliminate options not involving temporary, adjustable support withdrawal. Drill relies on repetition; Immersion lacks explicit structured guidance; Phonics focuses on sound-symbol decoding. Scaffolding uniquely denotes temporary support (modelling, prompts, checklists) faded as competence grows. Full Solution: Scaffolding (Bruner/Vygotsky) is the process where a teacher provides structured support within the Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) to help a learner achieve a task they cannot do alone. The scenario describes the classic sequence: modelling (demonstration), guided practice (joint construction), independent practice with fading support (checklists withdrawn), matching the definition of scaffolding perfectly.
Q4. Mathematics
The perimeter of a rectangle is 60 cm. If its length is twice its breadth, what is the area of the rectangle?
A. 150 cm²
B. 200 cm²
C. 250 cm²
D. 300 cm²
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Perimeter = 2(L+B) = 60 → L+B = 30. Given L = 2B, so 3B = 30 → B = 10 cm, L = 20 cm. Area = L × B = 20 × 10 = 200 cm². Full Solution: Let breadth = b cm. Then length = 2b cm. Perimeter = 2(2b + b) = 6b = 60 cm. So, b = 10 cm. Length = 20 cm. Area = Length × Breadth = 20 cm × 10 cm = 200 cm².

Q5. Child Development & Pedagogy
A teacher provides temporary support through prompts, modeling, and guided practice to help a student complete a task that the student cannot yet do independently. As the student demonstrates increasing competence, the teacher systematically reduces the level of assistance until the student performs the task alone. This instructional process of adjustable and fading support is specifically termed:
A. Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)
B. Scaffolding
C. Private Speech
D. More Knowledgeable Other (MKO)
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Keywords 'temporary support', 'systematically reduces', 'fading assistance' indicate the *instructional strategy/process*, which is Scaffolding. ZPD is the *gap/zone* where this occurs. Step-by-step: Vygotsky defined ZPD as the distance between actual and potential development. Scaffolding (coined by Wood, Bruner, & Ross based on Vygotsky) refers to the dynamic support process provided by the MKO within the ZPD. The stem describes the teacher's action of providing and fading support, matching the definition of Scaffolding.
Q6. Language I
Choose the correct indirect speech conversion for the sentence: She said, 'I am reading this book now.'
A. She said that she was reading that book then.
B. She said that she is reading this book now.
C. She said that she was reading this book then.
D. She said that she is reading that book now.
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Apply backshifting rules simultaneously: Tense (Present Continuous → Past Continuous), Demonstrative (this → that), Time Adverb (now → then). Eliminate options retaining 'is', 'this', or 'now'. Only Option A applies all three mandatory shifts correctly. Full Solution: In reported speech with a past reporting verb ('said'), backshifting is mandatory. 1. Verb: 'am reading' (Present Continuous) shifts to 'was reading' (Past Continuous). 2. Demonstrative: 'this' (proximal) shifts to 'that' (distal). 3. Time: 'now' (proximal) shifts to 'then' (distal). Option A ('was reading that book then') is the only one satisfying all three rules.
Q7. Environmental Studies
Which of the following traditional water harvesting structures is specifically a 'stepwell' (locally called Vav or Baoli), featuring a series of steps descending to the water level, historically prevalent in the arid regions of Gujarat and Rajasthan?
A. Johad
B. Khadin
C. Eri
D. Vav
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Match structure to region and feature. Stepwell = Steps + Gujarat/Rajasthan = Vav/ Baoli. Johad (check dam), Khadin (runoff farming), Eri (tank system) lack descending steps. Full Solution: Vav (or Baoli) is the distinct stepwell architecture of Western India allowing access to fluctuating groundwater. Johads are small earthen check dams in Rajasthan. Khadins are structures for runoff agriculture in Jaisalmer. Eris are traditional tank irrigation systems of Tamil Nadu.

Q10. Mathematics

C. 30%
D. 32%
Correct Answer: A

Q11. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of the product \((3547)^{153} \times (251)^{72}\)?
A. 1
B. 3
C. 7
D. 9
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits. Unit digit of 3547 is 7; cycle of 7 is 4 (7, 9, 3, 1). 153 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 1 → unit digit = 7. Unit digit of 251 is 1; any power of 1 ends in 1. Final unit digit = 7 × 1 = 7. Step-by-step: The unit digit of \(3547^{153}\) is same as \(7^{153}\). Cyclicity of 7 is 4. \(153 = 4 \times 38 + 1\). So \(7^{153} = (7^4)^{38} \times 7^1\) → unit digit \(1^{38} \times 7 = 7\). The unit digit of \(251^{72}\) is \(1^{72} = 1\). Product unit digit = \(7 \times 1 = 7\).
Q12. Child Development & Pedagogy
According to Lev Vygotsky, when a 5-year-old child talks to themselves aloud while solving a puzzle (e.g., "Put the big piece here, now the corner..."), this phenomenon is best described as:
A. Egocentric speech
B. Private speech
C. Social speech
D. Inner speech
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Eliminate Option A (Piaget's term, not Vygotsky's). Eliminate Option C (speech is not directed at others). Eliminate Option D (inner speech is silent/internalized). Option B is Vygotsky's specific term for audible self-directed speech used for self-regulation. Full Solution: Vygotsky viewed private speech as a critical developmental milestone where children use language to plan and guide their own actions (self-regulation). It originates from social speech and eventually internalizes as inner speech. Piaget termed this 'egocentric speech', viewing it as a limitation, whereas Vygotsky saw it as a cognitive tool. Since the question asks for Vygotsky's perspective, 'Private speech' is the correct terminology.
Q13. Environmental Studies
As per the National Curriculum Framework (NCF) 2005, the subject 'Environmental Studies' (EVS) at the primary stage (Classes I–II) is structured around the child's immediate environment. Which of the following statements accurately reflects the curricular approach for EVS at this stage according to NCF 2005?
A. EVS is taught as separate Science and Social Science textbooks from Class I onwards to build disciplinary rigour early.
B. EVS is presented as an integrated curricular area drawing upon Science, Social Science, and Environmental Education concepts without rigid subject boundaries.
C. The focus is exclusively on natural environment components like plants, animals, and weather, delaying social environment themes until Class III.
D. Textbooks are avoided completely; the entire curriculum is transacted only through unstructured outdoor play and storytelling.
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Eliminate options suggesting 'separate subjects' (A), 'exclusive natural focus' (C), or 'no textbooks' (D) as NCF 2005 emphasizes integration, holistic environment (natural + social), and contextual materials. Worked Solution: NCF 2005 positions EVS (Classes I–II) as an integrated area combining insights from Science, Social Science, and Environmental Education. It focuses on the child's immediate environment (both natural and social) using themes like Family, Food, Water, Shelter, and Travel. It does not separate disciplines, exclude social themes, or discard textbooks.
Q14. Environmental Studies
Consider the following food chain in a grassland ecosystem: Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Hawk. Which organism in this chain functions as the secondary consumer?
A. Grass
B. Grasshopper
C. Frog
D. Hawk
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Count trophic levels: Producer (1) → Primary Consumer (2) → Secondary Consumer (3). The 3rd organism is the answer. Full Solution: Grass is the producer (autotroph). Grasshopper eats grass (Primary Consumer/Herbivore). Frog eats the grasshopper, making it the Secondary Consumer (Carnivore). Snake is the Tertiary Consumer, and Hawk is the Quaternary/Apex Consumer.
Q15. Child Development & Pedagogy
A citizen decides not to participate in a peaceful protest against a controversial law, reasoning: 'Even though I disagree with this law, I must obey it because laws are essential for maintaining social order. If everyone ignored laws they disliked, society would break down.' According to Lawrence Kohlberg's theory of moral development, this reasoning is characteristic of which stage?
A. Stage 1: Punishment and Obedience Orientation
B. Stage 2: Instrumental Relativist Orientation
C. Stage 4: Maintaining Social Order Orientation
D. Stage 5: Social Contract and Individual Rights Orientation
Correct Answer: C
The reasoning focuses on the duty to uphold laws to preserve the social system, which is the hallmark of Stage 4 (Law and Order). Stage 1 focuses on avoiding punishment; Stage 2 focuses on self-interest and exchange; Stage 5 views laws as flexible social contracts that can be changed if they violate rights.
