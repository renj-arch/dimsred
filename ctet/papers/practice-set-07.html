Q1. Language
In the quarterly review of a District Cooperative Bank's accounts, the Chairman noted that 'the financial statements failed to balance due to unrecorded entries'. Which single word best describes the state of the books mentioned?
A. A. Audited
B. B. Discrepant
C. C. Conciliatory
D. D. Rectified
Correct Answer: B
The phrase 'failed to balance' indicates a lack of agreement or consistency between two sets of figures (assets vs liabilities). In formal financial and legal contexts, a statement where the numbers do not match is described as 'discrepant'. 'Audited' implies a check was done, 'Conciliatory' refers to attitude, and 'Rectified' implies the error was fixed. Therefore, 'Discrepant' is the precise formal term for unbalanced accounts.
Q2. Language
Identify the sentence that is most appropriate for a formal letter of appointment from a government department: 'A. You are hereby appointed as a Junior Assistant, effective from 1st July 2024. B. They have made you the Junior Assistant starting July 1st. C. Get ready to work as a Junior Assistant from tomorrow. D. You are chosen for the job of Junior Assistant.
A. You are hereby appointed as a Junior Assistant, effective from 1st July 2024.
B. They have made you the Junior Assistant starting July 1st.
C. Get ready to work as a Junior Assistant from tomorrow.
D. You are chosen for the job of Junior Assistant.
Correct Answer: A
1. Option A uses the standard legal phrase 'hereby appointed' and 'effective from', which is the mandatory register for official government correspondence in India. 2. Option B uses 'made you' (colloquial) and lacks the precise date format ('July 1st' vs '1st July'). 3. Option C uses imperative 'Get ready' (inappropriate for an appointment letter). 4. Option D uses 'chosen' (informal) and vague phrasing. 5. Only Option A adheres to the passive/formal voice required by SSC, IBPS, and SBI guidelines for administrative writing.
Q3. Quantitative Aptitude
A bank offers a special 'Festival Savings Scheme' where the interest rate for the first year is 5% per annum on the principal. However, the interest earned in the first year is added to the principal at the end of the year. If a customer deposits ₹10,000 and closes the account after exactly one year, what is the total amount received?
A. A) ₹10,500
B. B) ₹10,525
C. C) ₹10,600
D. D) ₹10,400
Correct Answer: A
Simple Interest = (P * R * T) / 100. Substituting values: P = 10000, R = 5%, T = 1 year. SI = (10000 * 5 * 1) / 100. SI = 500. Total Amount = Principal + Interest = 10000 + 500 = 10500.

Q6. Language
Select the option that best replaces the phrase 'to bring the debate to an end temporarily, with a promise to resume later': 'A. To adjourn' 'B. To recess' C. To prorogue' 'D. To dissolve'
A. A. To adjourn
B. B. To recess
C. C. To prorogue
D. D. To dissolve
Correct Answer: A
Step 1: Identify the core meaning: temporary suspension of a meeting/debate. Step 2: Analyze 'To adjourn': Specifically means to end a meeting temporarily to resume later. Step 3: Analyze 'To recess': Means to take a break (often lunch), but in legislative terms, can imply a longer pause, though less precise than adjourn for a debate end. Step 4: Analyze 'To prorogue': Refers to ending a session of parliament entirely (usually by the governor/governor-general), effectively killing the session until a new one is summoned; not a temporary pause of a debate. Step 5: Analyze 'To dissolve': Means to end an assembly permanently or completely (e.g., dissolving the Lok Sabha). Conclusion: 'To adjourn' is the precise term for stopping a debate temporarily.

A. A) stating
B. B) had it stated
C. C) it was stated
D. D) which was stated
Correct Answer: A

Q11. Quantitative Aptitude
A basket contains 50 apples. If 20% are rotten, how many good apples are left?
A. 30
B. 40
C. 50
D. 20
Correct Answer: B
Rotten apples = 20% of 50 = 10. Good apples = 50 - 10 = 40.
