A. Evaporation
B. Condensation
C. Precipitation
D. Transpiration
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: 'Condensation' links to 'Dense' (gas to liquid); Evaporation is liquid to gas, Precipitation is falling water. Step-by-step: Evaporation converts liquid water to vapour. Which developmental milestone typically indicates that a child has entered Piaget's 'Concrete Operational Stage'?
A. The ability to think logically about concrete events and understand conservation
B. The ability to think abstractly and engage in hypothetical reasoning
C. The inability to conserve quantity when appearance changes
D. The belief that objects cease to exist when out of sight
Correct Answer: A

Abstract reasoning and hypothetical thinking belong to the subsequent Formal Operational stage; failing to conserve quantity characterizes the Pre-operational stage; believing objects cease to exist is a sign of lack of object permanence in the Sensorimotor stage.
