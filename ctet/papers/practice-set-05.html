Q1. Child Development & Pedagogy
According to Kohlberg, what is the highest stage of moral development?
A. Pre-conventional level
B. Conventional level
C. Post-conventional level
D. Transcendental level
Correct Answer: C
Kohlberg's theory of moral development proposes that the highest stage of moral development is the post-conventional level, where individuals develop a sense of social contract and universal principles.
Q2. Environmental Studies
What is the term for the natural processes that occur in an ecosystem, such as decomposition and nutrient cycling?
A. Biogeochemical cycles
B. Ecosystem services
C. Food chains
D. Habitat destruction
Correct Answer: A
Biogeochemical cycles refer to the natural processes that occur in an ecosystem, such as decomposition and nutrient cycling, which are essential for maintaining the health and balance of the ecosystem.
Q3. Environmental Studies
What is the main function of the atmosphere in the environment?
A. To regulate the Earth's temperature and climate
B. To produce oxygen and support life
C. To provide habitat and shelter for a diverse range of species
D. To control the water cycle and prevent flooding
Correct Answer: A
The main function of the atmosphere is to regulate the Earth's temperature and climate, which is essential for maintaining a stable and habitable environment.
