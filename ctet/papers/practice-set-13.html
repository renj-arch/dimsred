A grandmother prepares mango pickle (Aam ka Achar) using raw mangoes, salt, turmeric, mustard oil, and spices. The pickle remains edible for over a year without refrigeration. Which scientific principle primarily explains the preservation of this pickle?
A. Low temperature inhibits enzymatic reactions and microbial metabolism.
B. High concentration of salt and oil creates a hypertonic environment causing plasmolysis of microbial cells and anaerobic conditions.
C. Turmeric and spices act as antibiotics that sterilize the food matrix completely.
D. The acidic pH of raw mangoes denatures all proteins, preventing spoilage indefinitely.
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Eliminate modern methods (A - refrigeration), absolute claims (C - 'sterilize completely', D - 'indefinitely'). Traditional pickling uses osmosis (salt) and anaerobic barrier (oil). Worked Solution: Pickling preserves food primarily through high osmotic pressure created by salt (and sugar), which draws water out of microbial cells via plasmolysis, inhibiting their growth. The layer of oil creates an anaerobic seal, further preventing aerobic spoilage. While acidity (mango/turmeric) helps, the primary preservative mechanism in oil-based Indian pickles is the hypertonic environment and oxygen exclusion.
Q15. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-18
What is the unit digit of the number obtained from the expression (7^95)?
A. 7
B. 9
C. 3
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The unit digits of powers of 7 follow a cycle of 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. Divide the exponent 95 by 4; the remainder is 3. The 3rd value in the cycle (7→9→3) is 3. Full Solution: Cyclicity of 7 is 4 (7^1=7, 7^2=49, 7^3=343, 7^4=2401). 95 = 4×23 + 3. Unit digit of 7^95 = Unit digit of (7^4)^23 × 7^3 = 1 × 343 = 3.
