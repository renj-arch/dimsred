Q2. Child Development & Pedagogy
What is the term used to describe the tendency of young children to attribute their own thoughts and feelings to others, such as thinking that everyone sees what they see?
A. Theory of Mind
B. Egocentrism
C. Animism
D. Centration
Correct Answer: B
Egocentrism in Piaget's terms refers to the inability to distinguish between one's own perspective and the perspectives of others. Theory of Mind is a broader concept referring to the understanding that others have mental states different from one's own, which develops after egocentrism declines; Animism is attributing life to inanimate objects; Centration is focusing on only one aspect of a situation while ignoring others.
Q3. Language II
Despite the heavy downpour, the match continued because the drainage system of the new stadium ______ the water quickly.
A. drained out
B. drained off
C. drained away
D. drained up
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Test collocation with 'water' and directionality. 'Away' implies removal from a place (correct). 'Out' suggests from inside a container, 'off' from a surface, 'up' is non-standard for liquids. Worked: 'Drain away' is the standard phrasal verb for liquids (water, rain) disappearing from an area by flowing off. 'Drain out' is used for emptying a container (drain out the tank). 'Drain off' implies removing liquid from the surface of something solid. 'Drain up' is not a standard phrasal verb.
Q4. Environmental Studies
Which of the following pairs correctly matches the edible part of the plant with its common example?
A. Stem - Sweet Potato
B. Flower - Cauliflower
C. Root - Onion
D. Fruit - Potato
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut (Option Elimination): Eliminate options based on common misconceptions. Potato is a modified stem (tuber), not a fruit/root, eliminating D. Sweet potato is a modified root (tuberous root), not a stem, eliminating A. Onion is a modified leaf structure (bulb), not a root, eliminating C. Only Cauliflower is an inflorescence (flower) consumed as a vegetable. Step-by-step: 1. Identify Potato as stem tuber -> Option D wrong. 2. Identify Sweet Potato as root tuber -> Option A wrong. 3. Identify Onion as bulb (fleshy leaves) -> Option C wrong. 4. Confirm Cauliflower is an immature flower head (inflorescence) -> Option B correct.
Q5. Language I
Choose the correct passive voice construction for the sentence: 'The candidates must write the examination in blue ink.'
A. The examination must be written in blue ink by the candidates.
B. The examination must written in blue ink by the candidates.
C. The examination must be wrote in blue ink by the candidates.
D. The examination must being written in blue ink by the candidates.
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Passive voice with modals follows the fixed formula 'Modal + be + V3'. Eliminate Option B (missing 'be'), Option C (uses V2 'wrote' instead of V3 'written'), and Option D (uses 'being' instead of 'be'). Step-by-step: The object 'examination' becomes the subject. The modal 'must' is retained. Add the auxiliary 'be' followed by the past participle 'written'. The prepositional phrase 'in blue ink' and the agent 'by the candidates' remain unchanged.
Q6. Language II

A. To waste valuable resources
B. To work or study late into the night
C. To light a traditional lamp
D. To get extremely angry
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Eliminate literal meanings (Option C) and unrelated abstract meanings (Options A, D). The phrase historically refers to burning lamp oil at midnight for reading. Q7. Language II
According to Noam Chomsky's nativist theory of language acquisition, the innate, hypothetical neurological mechanism that enables children to acquire language naturally is termed as:
A. Language Acquisition Support System (LASS)
B. Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)
C. Language Acquisition Device (LAD)
D. Operant Conditioning Chamber
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Match theorists to concepts: Skinner → Operant Conditioning (D), Vygotsky → ZPD (B), Bruner → LASS (A). By elimination, Chomsky → LAD. Full Solution: Chomsky argued that humans possess an innate biological capacity for language, termed the Language Acquisition Device (LAD), which allows children to deduce grammatical rules from limited input, contrasting with behaviorist and social-interactionist models.
Q8. Child Development & Pedagogy
A Class V student refuses to cheat on a test even though they are unprepared and unlikely to be caught. They explain: 'Cheating isn't fair to the other students who studied hard, and I want to be the kind of person others can trust.' In Lawrence Kohlberg's theory, this reasoning aligns with which stage?
A. A. Stage 1: Punishment and Obedience Orientation
B. B. Stage 2: Instrumental Relativist Orientation
C. C. Stage 3: Good Interpersonal Relationships ("Good Boy/Good Girl")
D. D. Stage 4: Maintaining Social Order
Correct Answer: C
Stage 3 reasoning focuses on living up to social expectations, trust, loyalty, and being a 'good person' in the eyes of significant others (peers, family). Stage 1 (A) fears punishment. Stage 2 (B) calculates self-interest/exchange. Stage 4 (D) emphasizes duty to society/laws to prevent chaos.
Q9. Mathematics
If 3 pens cost ₹45, what is the cost of 7 pens?
A. ₹100
B. ₹105
C. ₹110
D. ₹115
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use unitary method – cost of 1 pen = 45/3 = ₹15; cost of 7 pens = 7×15 = ₹105. Full: 3 pens = ₹45 ⇒ 1 pen = ₹15 ⇒ 7 pens = 7×15 = ₹105.
Q10. Mathematics
The HCF of two numbers is 12 and their LCM is 144. If one of the numbers is 36, what is the other number?
A. 48
B. 72
C. 24
D. 96
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the relation Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM. The other number = (HCF × LCM) ÷ Given Number = (12 × 144) ÷ 36 = 12 × 4 = 48. Full Solution: Let the other number be x. We know that 36 × x = 12 × 144. Solving for x: x = (12 × 144) / 36 = 12 × 4 = 48.
Q11. Language I
A student reads a newspaper article quickly to get the general idea or gist, ignoring unfamiliar words and specific details. Which reading sub-skill is the student employing?
A. Scanning
B. Intensive Reading
C. Skimming
D. Extensive Reading
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Match the stated purpose to the skill definition. Scanning = locating specific details/keywords; Intensive Reading = detailed linguistic analysis; Extensive Reading = reading large amounts for fluency/pleasure. Skimming = rapid reading for main idea/gist. The stem explicitly mentions 'general idea or gist', which is the textbook definition of skimming. Full Solution: Skimming is a top-down processing strategy where the reader moves quickly through a text to grasp the central theme or gist without decoding every word. Scanning (A) targets specific information like names/dates. Intensive Reading (B) focuses on language structure and detail. Extensive Reading (D) involves reading longer texts for overall comprehension and enjoyment. The purpose 'general idea or gist' uniquely identifies Skimming.
Q12. Environmental Studies
In the water cycle, the process where water vapour cools and changes into tiny liquid water droplets to form clouds is technically termed:
A. Evaporation
B. Transpiration
C. Condensation
D. Precipitation
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut (Keyword Association): Cloud formation requires gas-to-liquid phase change. Evaporation (A) and Transpiration (B) are liquid-to-gas. Precipitation (D) is the falling of water (rain/snow). Condensation (C) is the only gas-to-liquid process listed. Step-by-step: 1. Water cycle stages: Evaporation (Liquid→Gas) → Condensation (Gas→Liquid droplets/clouds) → Precipitation (Fall). 2. Question asks for cloud formation (Gas→Liquid). 3. Match definition: Condensation is the change of state from vapour to liquid. 4. Select Option C.
Q13. Child Development & Pedagogy
In the context of moral development, what characterizes the 'Autonomous Morality' stage according to Piaget?
A. Rules are seen as flexible and can be changed through mutual agreement
B. Rules are absolute and unchangeable, handed down by authority figures
C. Punishment is viewed solely in terms of physical consequences
D. Actions are judged based on their intent rather than just the outcome
Correct Answer: D
Autonomous morality (or moral realism transitioning to relativism) involves understanding that rules are social agreements that can be changed and judging actions based on intent. Seeing rules as absolute describes Heteronomous Morality; focusing solely on physical consequences is part of early heteronomous thinking; while intent is important, the defining feature of autonomous morality includes the flexibility of rules themselves.
Q14. Mathematics
Which of the following fractions lies between 2/3 and 3/4?
A. 5/8
B. 7/10
C. 4/5
D. 1/2
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Convert the boundary fractions to decimals for quick comparison. 2/3 ≈ 0.667 and 3/4 = 0.75. Now check the options: 5/8 = 0.625 (less than 0.667), 7/10 = 0.7 (lies between 0.667 and 0.75), 4/5 = 0.8 (greater than 0.75), 1/2 = 0.5 (less than 0.667). Only 7/10 satisfies the condition. Full Solution: Find a common denominator for the boundaries, e.g., 12. 2/3 = 8/12 and 3/4 = 9/12. A fraction between them must have a value between 8/12 and 9/12. Convert options to denominator 60 (LCM of 3,4,8,10,5,2): 2/3 = 40/60, 3/4 = 45/60. Option A: 5/8 = 37.5/60 (low). Option B: 7/10 = 42/60 (between 40 and 45). Option C: 4/5 = 48/60 (high). Option D: 1/2 = 30/60 (low).
Q15. Language I
Which of the following word combinations is a standard English collocation?
A. Do a mistake
B. Make a mistake
C. Perform a mistake
D. Execute a mistake
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Collocations are predictable word partnerships; 'make a mistake' is a fixed strong collocation. 'Do' collocates with tasks/work (do homework), while 'perform/execute' collocate with formal actions/duties (perform surgery). Step-by-step: Eliminate Option A ('do' implies action process, not creation of error). Eliminate Options C and D ('perform/execute' are too formal and used for procedures, not errors). 'Make' implies creating or producing an outcome, fitting the semantics of 'mistake'.
