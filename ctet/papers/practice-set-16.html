Q2. Mathematics
A Class III student identifies a square correctly when its sides are horizontal and vertical. However, when the same square is rotated by 45 degrees, the student insists it is a 'diamond' and not a square. According to Van Hiele's levels of geometric thinking, this student is operating at which level?
A. Level 0: Visualization
B. Level 1: Analysis
C. Level 2: Informal Deduction
D. Level 3: Deduction
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Elimination by keywords. Level 0 (Visualization) is characterized by recognizing shapes by their overall visual appearance and orientation (e.g., 'it looks like a door', 'it stands on its base'). Level 1 (Analysis) involves identifying properties (sides, angles) regardless of orientation. The student's dependence on 'usual orientation' and visual look ('diamond') confirms Level 0. Step-by-step: 1. Level 0 (Visualization): Children recognize figures by their physical appearance as a whole. Orientation matters. A rotated square looks like a 'diamond'. 2. Level 1 (Analysis): Children identify properties of shapes (e.g., '4 equal sides, 4 right angles'). Orientation does not change classification. 3. Level 2 (Informal Deduction): Children understand relationships between properties and classes (e.g., 'A square is a rectangle'). 4. Level 3 (Deduction): Formal logic, proofs, axioms. 5. The student classifies based on visual orientation, not properties. Hence, Level 0.

Q6. Language II
Choose the correct verb form to complete the sentence applying the Proximity Rule: Neither the students nor the class teacher ______ aware of the sudden change in the timetable.
A. were
B. was
C. are
D. have been
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Identify the noun nearest to the verb ('class teacher' - singular). Select the singular verb option ('was'). Eliminate plural verbs ('were', 'are') and perfect tense ('have been') unless context demands it. Full Solution: In correlative conjunctions like 'Neither...Nor' or 'Either...Or', the verb agrees with the subject closest to it (Proximity Rule/Principle of Proximity). Here, 'class teacher' is singular and nearest to the blank. Therefore, the singular verb 'was' is correct. 'Were' and 'are' are plural; 'have been' is plural perfect.

Q10. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of 7 raised to the power 2023?
A. 7
B. 9
C. 3
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The unit digit of powers of 7 repeats every 4 (7, 9, 3, 1). 2023 mod 4 = 3, so the unit digit is the 3rd in the cycle, which is 3. Full solution: 7^1=7, 7^2=49→9, 7^3=343→3, 7^4=2401→1; the cycle length is 4. Since 2023 = 4×505 + 3, the unit digit of 7^2023 equals the unit digit of 7^3, i.e., 3.

Q15. Child Development & Pedagogy
A teacher designs an assessment task: 'Critique the effectiveness of the government's new environmental policy and propose a better alternative framework.' According to the Revised Bloom's Taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl), the primary cognitive process dimension targeted by the verb 'Critique' is:
A. Analyzing
B. Evaluating
C. Creating
D. Understanding
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Associate specific verbs with levels: 'Critique', 'Judge', 'Defend', 'Justify' = Evaluating. 'Generate', 'Plan', 'Propose' = Creating. The stem asks to 'Critique effectiveness' (judgment) as the primary command; 'propose alternative' is a secondary extension. Full Solution: In the Revised Taxonomy, Evaluating involves making judgments based on criteria and standards through checking and critiquing. Analyzing involves breaking material into parts (differentiating, organizing). Creating involves putting elements together to form a novel whole. Since the core demand is judging the value/effectiveness of an existing policy, it falls squarely under Evaluating.
