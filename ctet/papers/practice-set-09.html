Q1. Mathematics
If a rectangular garden has a length of 15 meters and a breadth of 10 meters, what is the minimum distance required to walk around its boundary once?
A. 25 meters
B. 40 meters
C. 50 meters
D. 60 meters
Correct Answer: C
The minimum distance to walk around the boundary once is the perimeter. The formula for the perimeter of a rectangle is 2 × (length + breadth). Substituting the values: 2 × (15 + 10) = 2 × 25 = 50 meters. Option A is incorrect because it represents half the sum of sides. Option B is the sum of length and breadth only, not the full perimeter. Option D incorrectly adds the area calculation logic or double counts the dimensions improperly.

Q6. Child Development & Pedagogy
According to John Bowlby's attachment theory, what is the primary outcome of a secure attachment formed in infancy?
A. The development of a strong internal working model for future relationships
B. The inability to form bonds with peers later in life
C. A heightened sensitivity to environmental stimuli causing anxiety
D. A complete reliance on authority figures throughout adulthood
Correct Answer: A
Secure attachment leads to the formation of a positive 'internal working model,' where individuals expect others to be responsive and trustworthy, facilitating healthy social interactions. Inability to form bonds is associated with insecure or disorganized attachment; heightened sensitivity to stimuli is not a direct definition of secure attachment outcomes; and complete reliance on authority contradicts the goal of developing independence found in secure attachments.

Q8. Child Development & Pedagogy
Which brain structure is primarily responsible for regulating emotions and forming memories associated with emotional experiences?
A. The Amygdala
B. The Hippocampus
C. The Cerebellum
D. The Frontal Lobe
Correct Answer: A
The amygdala plays a critical role in processing emotions, particularly fear, and linking them to memory formation. The hippocampus is primarily involved in the consolidation of declarative memories; the cerebellum coordinates motor control; and while the frontal lobe regulates impulse control and decision-making, the specific regulation of emotional responses and their immediate association with memory is the amygdala's core function.

Q10. Child Development & Pedagogy

A. Identity vs. Role Confusion
B. Industry vs. Inferiority
C. Initiative vs. Guilt
D. Trust vs. Mistrust
Correct Answer: B
The elementary school years correspond to the 'Industry vs. Inferiority' stage, where children develop a sense of competence through mastering skills and tasks; success leads to feelings of industry, while failure leads to inferiority. The other options represent different life stages: Trust vs. Mistrust (infancy), Initiative vs. Guilt (preschool), and Identity vs. Role Confusion (adolescence).

Q12. Environmental Studies
Which of the following gases is primarily responsible for the greenhouse effect that keeps the Earth's surface warm?
A. Nitrogen
B. Oxygen
C. Carbon Dioxide
D. Helium
Correct Answer: C
Carbon dioxide (CO2) is a major greenhouse gas that traps heat radiating from the Earth, maintaining temperatures suitable for life. Nitrogen and Oxygen make up most of the atmosphere but do not trap heat effectively. Helium is an inert noble gas present in trace amounts with no significant role in the greenhouse effect.
Q13. Environmental Studies
Which of the following animals is known to live in both water and on land?
A. Frog
B. Fish
C. Snake
D. Penguin
Correct Answer: A
A frog is an amphibian capable of breathing through its skin and lungs, allowing it to live in water as a tadpole and on land as an adult. Fish breathe only underwater using gills. Snakes are reptiles that primarily live on land (though some swim), and penguins are birds adapted specifically for swimming but cannot breathe underwater like fish.
