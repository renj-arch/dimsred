B. Object Permanence
C. Symbolic Function
D. Goal-Directed Behavior
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut/Trick: Keyword Association. The stem describes 'hidden object retrieved' -> Direct match with 'Object Permanence'. Eliminate 'Symbolic Function' (Pre-operational, language/pretend play), 'Deferred Imitation' (copying past actions), 'Goal-Directed Behavior' (means-end, 8-12 months, but permanence is the specific milestone for hidden objects). Full Solution: Object Permanence is the understanding that objects continue to exist even when they cannot be seen, heard, or touched.
