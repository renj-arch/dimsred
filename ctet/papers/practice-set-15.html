B. 5/7
C. 7/9
D. 9/11
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: In all given fractions, the difference between the denominator and numerator is constant (2). When the difference is the same, the fraction with the largest numerator (and denominator) is the greatest. Hence, 9/11 is the largest. Full Method: Cross-multiply adjacent options. A. Direction of the food source relative to the sun's position.
B. Approximate distance of the food source from the hive.

D. Height of the food source above the ground level.
Correct Answer: B

Q15. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-20
A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. A alone can do it in 20 days. In how many days can B alone complete the work?
A. 24 days
B. 30 days
C. 20 days
D. 15 days
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the formula 1/B = 1/(A+B) - 1/A; here 1/B = 1/12 - 1/20 = 1/30 → B = 30 days. Full solution: Combined rate = 1/12 work per day; A's rate = 1/20; B's rate = 1/12 - 1/20 = (5-3)/60 = 2/60 = 1/30; thus B alone takes 30 days.
