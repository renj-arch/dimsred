Q7. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-28
Find the missing digit 'x' in the number 5x342, if the number is divisible by 11.
A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 8
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Apply the divisibility rule for 11: (Sum of digits at odd places from right) - (Sum of digits at even places from right) = 0 or multiple of 11. Odd places: 2 + 3 + 5 = 10. Even places: 4 + x. Difference = 10 - (4 + x) = 6 - x. For divisibility, 6 - x = 0 ⇒ x = 6. Full Solution: Number the digits from right to left. Position 1 (units): 2 (Odd). Position 2 (tens): 4 (Even). Position 3 (hundreds): 3 (Odd). Position 4 (thousands): x (Even). Position 5 (ten thousands): 5 (Odd). Sum of odd positions = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10. Sum of even positions = 4 + x. Difference = 10 - 4 - x = 6 - x. For the number to be divisible by 11, the difference must be 0 or a multiple of 11. Since x is a single digit (0-9), 6 - x = 0 gives x = 6. Verification: 56342 ÷ 11 = 5122 (exact).
