Q34Mathematics
A contractor promises to complete a project in 40 days. He employs 60 men. However, due to supply shortages, the work rate of every single man drops by 25% immediately after day 20. If he wants to finish the job exactly on day 40 regardless of this slowdown, how many additional men must he hire at that moment?
Answer: C

Initial plan: 60 men complete work in 40 days. Halfway (day 20), half the total required work is done. Remaining work needs to be done in remaining 20 days. At day 20, efficiency of each man drops by 25% (becomes 75% of original). Let original rate be R. New rate = 0.75R. Effective men count available remains 60, but their total output is 0.75 * 60 * R = 45R equivalent to 45 normal-men-days per day. Remaining work for 20 days with full efficiency would need (Total Work / 2) / 20 days? No. Original plan: Total Work W = 60 men * 40 days * R = 2400 man-days. Half done in 20 days = 1200 man-days. Remaining 1200 man-days must be done in 20 days. Current effective capacity per day with 60 men at 75% efficiency = 60 * 0.75 * R = 45R (where 1R=1 normal man-day). Days needed for current 60 men = 1200 / 45 = 26.67 days. We have only 20 days left. Deficit = 6.67 days worth of work. Required capacity per day = 1200 / 20 = 60R (equivalent to 60 normal men). Current effective capacity is 45R. Need additional 15R. Since each new man adds 0.75R efficiency, let x be number of men. Total capacity = (60 + x) * 0.75R = 60R => 45R + 0.75xR = 60R => 0.75x = 15 => x = 20.

A boat travels between two ports, P and Q. On the first trip from P to Q (downstream), the current speed is 5 km/h, and the boat's speed in still water is V km/h. On the return trip from Q to P (upstream), the current speed increases to 6 km/h, but the distance between the ports remains unchanged. If the time taken for the downstream trip equals the time taken for the upstream trip, what is the value of V?
Answer: A

Let V be speed in still water. Downstream Speed = V + 5. Upstream Speed = V - 8. Time ratio Down:Up is 2:3 (since distances are equal). So, (V-8)/(V+5) = 2/3. Cross-multiply: 3(V-8) = 2(V+5). 3V - 24 = 2V + 10. V = 34 km/h.
