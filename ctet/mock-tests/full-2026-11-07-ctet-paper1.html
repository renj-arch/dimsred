Answer: A

Let Cost Price (CP) = Rs. 100. Initial Profit (if no discount) = Marked Price - CP = 125 - 100 = 25. Half of this initial profit = 25 / 2 = 12.5. Target Selling Price = CP + Half Initial Profit = 100 + 12.5 = 112.5. Marked Price (MP) = 125. Discount Amount = MP - SP = 125 - 112.5 = 12.5. Discount Percentage (x) = (Discount / MP) * 100 = (12.5 / 125) * 100 = 10%.
