Key Concepts

Basic Mathematics for CLAT

CLAT quantitative techniques basic mathematics covers number systems, LCM & HCF, simplification, surds & indices, logarithms, averages, and elementary algebra — all at Class 10 level. Speed and accuracy matter more than advanced concepts.

  • Number system:
    • Digits: 0–9; face value vs place value.
    • Types: natural, whole, integers, rational, irrational, real numbers.
    • Even/odd, prime/composite, co-prime numbers.
    • Divisibility rules: 2 (last digit even), 3 (sum of digits), 4 (last two digits), 5 (ends 0/5), 6 (2 & 3), 9 (sum of digits), 10 (ends 0), 11 (alternate sum difference).
  • LCM & HCF:
    • HCF (GCD): highest common factor — prime factorization or division method.
    • LCM: lowest common multiple — prime factorization.
    • Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM.
    • LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators / HCF of denominators; HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators.
    • Word problems: bells tolling together, largest tile size, etc.
  • Simplification (BODMAS): Brackets → Orders (powers/roots) → Division/Multiplication (left to right) → Addition/Subtraction (left to right).
  • Surds & Indices:
    • a^m × a^n = a^(m+n); a^m / a^n = a^(m−n); (a^m)^n = a^(mn); (ab)^n = a^n b^n; a^0 = 1; a^−n = 1/a^n.
    • √a × √b = √(ab); √(a/b) = √a/√b; rationalize denominator.
    • Surds: √2 ≈ 1.414, √3 ≈ 1.732, √5 ≈ 2.236.
  • Logarithms: log_a(m×n) = log_a m + log_a n; log_a(m/n) = log_a m − log_a n; log_a(m^n) = n log_a m; log_a a = 1; log_a 1 = 0; change of base: log_a b = log b / log a.
  • Averages: Mean = sum/count; weighted mean; combined average; average speed = total distance/total time (not average of speeds).
Technique: LCM & HCF word problem
Three bells toll at intervals of 6, 8, 12 minutes. When toll together again?
Answer: LCM(6,8,12) = 24 minutes — they toll together again after 24 minutes.
Technique: Indices
Simplify: (2^3 × 2^4) / 2^2.
Answer: 2^(3+4−2) = 2^5 = 32.
Technique: Average speed
Car travels 60 km at 30 km/h and 60 km at 60 km/h. Average speed?
Answer: Total distance 120 km; total time = 2 + 1 = 3 h; average = 120/3 = 40 km/h (NOT 45).

Common Traps

  • Average speed ≠ average of two speeds — always total distance ÷ total time.
  • BODMAS: division and multiplication have equal precedence — go left to right.
  • Logarithm of sum ≠ sum of logarithms: log(m+n) ≠ log m + log n.
  • LCM vs HCF confusion: LCM ≥ each number; HCF ≤ each number.
  • Square root of a² = |a|, not always a.

Memory Tricks

  • Product rule: Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM.
  • Indices: Multiply → add powers; Divide → subtract powers; Power of power → multiply powers.
  • Logs: log of product = sum; log of quotient = difference; log of power = power × log.
  • Average speed: total distance ÷ total time.
CLAT Basic Maths exam strategy
Apply BODMAS strictly. Cancel common factors before multiplying. Approximate roots/decimals when options are far apart. Read units carefully. Flag calculation-heavy questions and return if time permits.
