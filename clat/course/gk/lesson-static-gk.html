Key Concepts

Static General Knowledge for CLAT

Static GK forms a significant portion of the CLAT General Knowledge section. It covers facts that remain constant — history, geography, polity, awards, books and authors, national symbols, international organisations, sports, and important days.

  • History: Important dynasties (Maurya, Gupta, Mughal, Maratha), freedom movement leaders and events, constitutional developments (Regulating Act 1773 to Independence Act 1947), viceroys and governor-generals, battles (Plassey, Buxar, Panipat), ancient and medieval Indian history, world history (French Revolution, World Wars, Industrial Revolution).
  • Geography: Indian physical features (Himalayas, plains, plateaus, deserts, islands), river systems, climate, soil types, natural vegetation, mineral distribution, world geography (continents, oceans, major countries, capitals, currencies), international boundaries, demographic data.
  • Polity: Constitution — salient features, preamble, fundamental rights and duties, directive principles, union and state executive, legislature, judiciary, election commission, constitutional and non-constitutional bodies, amendment procedure, important articles.
  • Awards and Honours: Bharat Ratna, Padma awards, Nobel Prize (Indian laureates), Pulitzer, Booker Prize, Magsaysay Award, Sahitya Akademi, Dadasaheb Phalke, Arjuna/Dronacharya/Khel Ratna, national sports awards.
  • Books and Authors: Famous books by Indian and foreign authors, autobiographies of prominent personalities, literary classics.
  • National Symbols: National flag, anthem, song, emblem, animal (tiger), bird (peacock), flower (lotus), fruit (mango), tree (banyan), river (Ganga), calendar (Saka), currency (rupee), oath/pledge.
  • International Organisations: UN and its agencies (UNESCO, UNICEF, WHO, UNDP, FAO, ILO), World Bank, IMF, WTO, ASEAN, SAARC, BRICS, NATO, OPEC, G20, Commonwealth, Non-Aligned Movement — headquarters, heads, member countries, India's role.
  • Sports: Major tournaments (Olympics, Asian Games, Commonwealth Games, FIFA World Cup, cricket World Cup), Indian sportspersons, national sports, stadiums, recent championships, Olympics medals by India.
  • Important Days: National and international observance days (Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Human Rights Day, Environment Day, Women's Day, etc.) with dates and themes.
  • Science and Technology: Indian space programme (ISRO missions), nuclear programme, defence missiles, health and disease, Nobel laureates in science, inventions and discoveries.
Technique: Elimination in GK MCQs
Question: Which of the following is NOT a permanent member of the UN Security Council?
Answer: Options: USA, UK, Germany, France. Eliminate USA, UK, France (permanent members with veto). Germany is not a permanent member. Answer: Germany. Permanent members: USA, UK, France, Russia, China.
Technique: Date association
Question: When is World Environment Day observed?
Answer: 5 June every year, established by UN General Assembly in 1972, first celebrated 1974. Theme-based annual host country. Common confusion with Earth Day (22 April) — distinguish them.

Common Traps

  • Confusing similar awards (Bharat Ratna vs Padma Vibhushan vs Padma Bhushan vs Padma Shri — order of precedence).
  • Mixing up headquarters (e.g., WHO — Geneva, World Bank — Washington DC, ICJ — The Hague).
  • Incorrect dates for national/international days.
  • Assuming all Nobel laureates are Indian — know Indian Nobel winners specifically.
  • Mixing up national symbols (e.g., national aquatic animal — river dolphin; national heritage animal — elephant).

Memory Tricks

  • UNSC P5: USA, UK, France, Russia, China — "USA UK France Russia China" or remember the WWII victors.
  • National symbols: Tiger (animal), Peacock (bird), Lotus (flower), Mango (fruit), Banyan (tree), Ganga (river).
  • Key dates: Jan 26 (Republic), Aug 15 (Independence), Oct 2 (Gandhi), Dec 10 (Human Rights), June 5 (Environment), Apr 7 (Health), Mar 8 (Women).
CLAT GK strategy
CLAT GK now leans towards current affairs with static context. Read newspapers daily (The Hindu, Indian Express), note important events with dates, and revise static facts weekly. For passage-based GK, read the passage carefully — answers may be derivable even without prior knowledge.
