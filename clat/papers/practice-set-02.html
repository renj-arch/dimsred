Q1. Current Affairs & GK
The Government announces a policy to regularize all ad-hoc employees with 10 years of service. Relying on this, an employee declines a private job offer. Before regularization, the Government withdraws the policy citing fiscal constraints, without hearing the employee. The employee challenges the withdrawal. Which principle governs the outcome?
A. The employee has a substantive right to regularization because the policy created a legitimate expectation enforceable as a contract.
B. The withdrawal is valid because legitimate expectation is merely a moral claim with no legal enforceability against the State.
C. The withdrawal is invalid because legitimate expectation requires a hearing before frustration, unless overriding public interest is shown, which mere fiscal constraints may not satisfy.
D. The employee can claim damages for breach of promise under promissory estoppel, but cannot compel regularization.
Correct Answer: C
Correct answer: The withdrawal is invalid because legitimate expectation requires a hearing before frustration, unless overriding public interest is shown, which mere fiscal constraints may not satisfy..
