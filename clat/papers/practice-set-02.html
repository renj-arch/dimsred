Q1. Current Affairs & GK
In a passage the author writes: "Had the policy been implemented earlier, the crisis would have been averted." Which hidden premise must the author assume for this counterfactual to be logically valid?
A. The policy was the only factor influencing the crisis.
B. All other variables remained constant (ceteris paribus).
C. The crisis would have occurred regardless of the policy.
D. The policy was implemented later than planned.
Correct Answer: B
Correct answer: All other variables remained constant (ceteris paribus)..
Q2. English Language

Correct answer: An unstated belief necessary for the argument to hold..
Q3. English Language
A candidate encounters an inference question where one option is factually true in the real world but not supported by the passage. According to the Principle of Passage‑Only Reasoning, how should this option be treated?
