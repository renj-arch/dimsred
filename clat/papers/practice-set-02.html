Q1. Current Affairs & GK
A lower court refuses to decide a case differently from a higher court in the same jurisdiction. Which maxim describes this practice of following established decisions?
A. Caveat Emptor
B. Nemo Dat Quod Non Habet
C. Stare Decisis et non quieta movere
D. Actus Non Facit Reum Sine Intentione
Correct Answer: C
Correct answer: Stare Decisis et non quieta movere.
Q2. English Language
Passage: 'Despite the rapid adoption of digital payments, a significant portion of India's population still relies on cash transactions. The challenge lies in bridging the digital divide — ensuring internet access, smartphone availability, and digital literacy for all.' What challenge does the passage highlight?
A. Lack of digital payment apps
B. The digital divide in infrastructure and literacy
C. People prefer cash over digital payments
D. Lack of banking infrastructure
Correct Answer: B
Correct answer: The digital divide in infrastructure and literacy.
Q3. English Language
Passage: 'Financial literacy is essential for economic empowerment. When individuals understand concepts like budgeting, saving, and investing, they make better financial decisions. Governments worldwide are incorporating financial education into school curricula to equip young people with these skills.' What is the main idea of the passage?
A. Financial education is being added to school curricula
B. Financial literacy leads to better economic decisions
C. Governments control financial education
D. Saving money is the most important skill
Correct Answer: B
Correct answer: Financial literacy leads to better economic decisions.
