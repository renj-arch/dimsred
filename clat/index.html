About CLAT

CLAT (Common Law Admission Test) is a centralized national-level entrance exam for admission to 22 NLUs (National Law Universities). The 2-hour test has 120 MCQs covering English Language (28), Current Affairs & GK (35), Legal Reasoning (35), Logical Reasoning (12), and Quantitative Techniques (10). Candidates must have 45% marks in 12th standard (40% for SC/ST). No age limit.
