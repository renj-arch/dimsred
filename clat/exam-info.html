CLAT
Complete Exam Information

📋 Exam Pattern
120 MCQs (120 marks, 120 min). Sections: English, Current Affairs, Legal Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Techniques. Negative marking: 0.25 marks.
🎯 Eligibility
10+2 pass with 45% (40% for SC/ST). No age limit. Open to all streams.
📝 Selection Process
Written Exam → Counselling & Seat Allotment → Document Verification
📅 Important Dates
Notification in July. Exam in December. Results in January. Conducted once a year by CLAT Consortium.
