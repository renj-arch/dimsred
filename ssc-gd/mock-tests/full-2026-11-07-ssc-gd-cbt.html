If the retailer declares that his 'net profit percentage' is calculated on the 'final selling price' rather than the cost price, what is his net profit percentage (rounded to the nearest integer)?
Answer: A

1. Cost Price (CP) = 2000 * (1 - 0.12) = Rs. 1760. 2. Marked Price (MP) = 1760 * 1.25 = Rs. 2200. 3. Final Selling Price (SP) = 2200 * (1 - 0.10) = Rs. 1980. 4. Profit = SP - CP = 1980 - 1760 = Rs. 220. 5. Net Profit % on SP = (220 / 1980) * 100. 6. 220 / 1980 = 22 / 198 = 1 / 9. 7. (1 / 9) * 100 = 11.11%. 8. Rounded to nearest integer = 11%.

If the retailer declares that his 'net profit percentage' is calculated on the 'final selling price' rather than the cost price, what is his net profit percentage (rounded to the nearest integer)?
Answer: A
1. Cost Price (CP) = 2000 * (1 - 0.12) = Rs. 1760. 2. Marked Price (MP) = 1760 * 1.25 = Rs. 2200. 3. Final Selling Price (SP) = 2200 * (1 - 0.10) = Rs. 1980. 4. Profit = SP - CP = 1980 - 1760 = Rs. 220. 5. Net Profit % on SP = (220 / 1980) * 100. 6. 220 / 1980 = 22 / 198 = 1 / 9. 7. (1 / 9) * 100 = 11.11%. 8. Rounded to nearest integer = 11%.

The idiom 'oblivious' means unaware or unconscious of something, which fits perfectly for someone ignoring a situation without any physical inability (like being deaf or blind). 'Deaf' and 'blind' are literal; 'immune' is usually used for diseases or emotional reactions.
