Key Concepts

Speed, Distance & Time for SSC GD & Agniveer

Speed, distance, and time problems are frequently asked in SSC GD and Agniveer mathematics sections. The basic relationship is Distance = Speed � Time. Questions involve trains, relative speed, average speed, and boats & streams.

Key Concepts

Basic Formula: Speed = Distance/Time. Units: 1 km/h = 5/18 m/s; 1 m/s = 18/5 km/h. Average Speed: For equal distances at speeds a and b: avg = 2ab/(a+b). For equal times: avg = (a+b)/2. Relative Speed: Towards each other: add speeds. Same direction: subtract speeds. Train Problems: Passing a pole: distance = train length. Passing a platform: distance = train length + platform length. Boats & Streams: Downstream = boat+stream; Upstream = boat-stream. Boat speed = (down+up)/2; Stream speed = (down-up)/2.

Solved Examples

Example 1
A train 200 m long passes a pole in 10 seconds. Find its speed in km/h.

a) 54 km/h b) 60 km/h c) 72 km/h d) 80 km/h
Solution: c) 72 km/h. Speed = 200/10 = 20 m/s = 20�18/5 = 72 km/h.
