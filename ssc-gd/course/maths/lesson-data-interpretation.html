Key Concepts

Data Interpretation for SSC GD

Data interpretation involves extracting information from tables, bar graphs, line graphs, pie charts, and mixed diagrams to answer questions. For SSC GD, DI questions test basic arithmetic skills — percentages, averages, ratios — applied to real data sets.

Key Concepts

Tables: Read rows and columns carefully. Identify the required cell and perform the needed calculation (sum, difference, percentage, ratio). Bar Graphs: Heights of bars represent values. Compare lengths for relative values. Stacked bars show parts of a whole. Pie Charts: Each sector = (angle/360)×total. Percentage = (sector angle/360)×100. Line Graphs: Show trends over time. Slope indicates rate of change. Mixed DI: Two or more charts/tables combined; cross-reference data from both. Common calculations: percentage change = (new-old)/old×100; ratio comparison; average = sum/count.

Solved Examples

Example 1
A table shows monthly sales: Jan 500, Feb 600, Mar 450, Apr 700. What is the average monthly sales?

a) 525 b) 550 c) 562.5 d) 575
Solution: c) 562.5. Average = (500+600+450+700)/4 = 2250/4 = 562.5.
