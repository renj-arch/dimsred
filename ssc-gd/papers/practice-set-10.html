Q2. General Intelligence & Reasoning
If '+' means '÷', '÷' means '−', '−' means '×', and '×' means '+', then what is the value of 24 + 8 ÷ 4 − 2 × 6?
A. 0
B. 2
C. 1
D. 3
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Substitute symbols first, then apply BODMAS. Expression becomes 24 ÷ 8 − 4 × 2 + 6. Division: 3. Multiplication: 8. Then 3 − 8 + 6 = 1. Step-by-step: 1. Replace signs: 24 ÷ 8 − 4 × 2 + 6. 2. Division: 24 ÷ 8 = 3. 3. Multiplication: 4 × 2 = 8. 4. Expression: 3 − 8 + 6. 5. Left to right: 3 − 8 = -5; -5 + 6 = 1.
Q3. English/Hindi
Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error: "The book that you gave me are very interesting and informative."
A. The book
B. that you gave me
C. are very interesting
D. and informative
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Check subject‑verb agreement. The subject 'The book' is singular, so the verb must be singular 'is'; 'are' is plural, hence the error lies in the verb phrase. Step‑by‑step: 1) Split the sentence into parts. 2) Identify the main subject: 'The book'. 3) Locate the verb associated with it: 'are'. 4) Since 'book' is singular, 'are' is incorrect; it should be 'is'. 5) The erroneous part is 'are very interesting' (option C).
Q4. Elementary Mathematics
The radius of a right circular cylinder is increased by 20% and its height is decreased by 20%. What is the percentage change in the volume of the cylinder?
A. 4% decrease
B. 4% increase
C. 15.2% decrease
D. 15.2% increase
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Volume ∝ r²h. New factor = (1.2)² × 0.8 = 1.44 × 0.8 = 1.152 ⇒ 15.2% increase. Full: Original volume = πr²h. New radius = 1.2r, new height = 0.8h. New volume = π(1.2r)²(0.8h) = 1.152 πr²h. Percentage change = (1.152−1)×100 = 15.2% increase.
Q5. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Statements: (1) Some Pens are Pencils. (2) All Pencils are Erasers. (3) No Eraser is a Sharpener. Conclusions: I. Some Pens are Erasers. II. No Pencil is a Sharpener. III. Some Erasers are Pens.
A. Only I and II follow
B. Only II and III follow
C. Only I and III follow
D. All follow
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use Venn diagram logic. 'Some A are B + All B are C' implies 'Some A are C' (I) and its converse 'Some C are A' (III). 'All B are C + No C are D' implies 'No B are D' (II). All three follow. Step-by-step: 1. Stmt 1 & 2: Some Pens are Pencils, All Pencils are Erasers → Some Pens are Erasers (I true). Converse → Some Erasers are Pens (III true). 2. Stmt 2 & 3: All Pencils are Erasers, No Eraser is Sharpener → No Pencil is Sharpener (II true). 3. All conclusions follow.

Q7. Elementary Mathematics
The present ages of A and B are in the ratio 5:3. Four years ago, the ratio of their ages was 3:1. What is the present age of A?
A. 10 years
B. 8 years
C. 12 years
D. 14 years
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Let ages be 5x and 3x. Four years ago: (5x−4)/(3x−4)=3/1 ⇒ 5x−4=9x−12 ⇒ 4x=8 ⇒ x=2. A's age = 5x = 10 years. Full: Set up ratio equations, cross-multiply, solve for x, then compute 5x.
Q8. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Select the number that is different from the other three: 72, 135, 96, 120
A. 72
B. 135
C. 96
D. 120
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Check divisibility by 8 (last 3 digits rule). 72, 96, 120 are multiples of 8; 135 is not. Step-by-step: 1. 72 ÷ 8 = 9 (Divisible). 2. 96 ÷ 8 = 12 (Divisible). 3. 120 ÷ 8 = 15 (Divisible). 4. 135 ÷ 8 = 16.875 (Not divisible). 135 is the odd one out.
Q9. Elementary Mathematics
A sum of money amounts to ₹720 in 2 years and ₹870 in 5 years at simple interest. What is the principal amount?
A. ₹600
B. ₹620
C. ₹640
D. ₹660
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The difference ₹870−₹720 = ₹150 is the interest for 3 years, so yearly interest = ₹50. Principal = ₹720 − 2×₹50 = ₹620. Full: Let P be principal, R% rate. SI for 2 years = 720−P, for 5 years = 870−P. Subtracting gives 3 years SI = 150 ⇒ yearly SI = 50. Then P = 720 − 2×50 = 620.

Q15. Elementary Mathematics
Pipe A can fill a tank in 10 hours and pipe B in 15 hours. Both are opened together. After how many hours should pipe A be closed so that the tank is filled in exactly 9 hours?
A. 3 hours
B. 5 hours
C. 4 hours
D. 6 hours
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Take LCM(10,15)=30 as total work. A's rate=3 units/hr, B's=2 units/hr. Let A run t hours. Work = (3+2)t + 2(9−t) = 30 ⇒ 5t+18−2t=30 ⇒ 3t=12 ⇒ t=4 hrs. Full: Capacity = 30 units. Combined rate 5 units/hr. After t hours, remaining (9−t) hours only B works at 2 units/hr. Equation solves to t=4.
