Q8. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a cricket bat with a marked price of ₹5,000. He offers two alternatives: Scheme A: 15% discount followed by a further 10% discount on the reduced price. Scheme B: A single flat discount of x%. To make the final selling price identical in both schemes, what is the value of x?
A. 25%
B. 23.5%
C. 22.5%
D. 24%
Correct Answer: B
Discount A = 15%. Remaining = 85%. Discount B = 10%. Remaining = 90%. Combined Remaining = 85% x 90% = 0.85 x 0.90 = 0.765. Total Discount = 1 - 0.765 = 0.235. x = 23.5%.

Q14. Reasoning
In a specific logic-gate language, the symbols represent operations as follows: '+' means 'is less than or equal to' '- means 'is greater than' '*' means 'is not equal to' '/ means 'is exactly equal to' 'The statements below are true in this language: I. A + B (A <= B) II. B - C (B > C) III. D / B IV. E * A (E != A) Given these, which of the following conclusions MUST be true?
A. A < C and D < E
B. D > C and E < A
C. D >= C and E != A
D. Data Inadequate
Correct Answer: C
1. Translate statements: I(A<=B), II(B>C), III(D=B), IV(E!=A). 2. Combine I & II via B: Since A<=B and B>C, we have A<=B>C. No direct relation between A and C. 3. Substitute III into chain: D=B. Thus A<=D>C. Therefore D>=C is logically valid (since B>=C implies D>=C). 4. Analyze IV with substitution: E!=A. No fixed inequality exists between E and A, so 'E < A' or 'E > A' cannot be determined. 5. Evaluate options: Option C states 'D >= C and E != A'. This matches our derived facts perfectly.
