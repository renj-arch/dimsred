The cost price of 10 articles is equal to the selling price of 8 articles. What is the profit percentage?
A. 25%
B. 20%
C. 30%
D. 33.33%
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: If CP of x articles = SP of y articles, profit% = ((x-y)/y)×100. Here x=10, y=8 → (2/8)×100 = 25%. Full: Let CP of 1 article = ₹1, then CP of 10 = ₹10 = SP of 8 → SP of 1 = ₹10/8 = ₹1.25. Profit = ₹0.25 on ₹1 → 25%.

Ravi walks 12 km North, then turns right and walks 5 km. What is the straight‑line distance between his final position and the starting point?
A. 10 km
B. 11 km
C. 12 km
D. 13 km
Correct Answer: D
The path forms a right triangle with legs 12 km and 5 km. By Pythagoras, distance = √(12²+5²) = √(144+25) = √169 = 13 km. Shortcut: recognise the 5‑12‑13 Pythagorean triplet.
