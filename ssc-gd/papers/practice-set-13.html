Q1. Elementary Mathematics
What is the unit digit of (7^95 × 3^57)?
A. 1
B. 3
C. 7
D. 9
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Unit digit cycles – 7⁴=1, 95 mod 4 = 3 → 7³ ends with 3. 3⁴=1, 57 mod 4 = 1 → 3¹ ends with 3. 3 × 3 = 9. Full solution: 7^95 → cycle (7,9,3,1) position 3 → 3. 3^57 → cycle (3,9,7,1) position 1 → 3. Product unit digit = 3×3 = 9.
Q2. Elementary Mathematics
The present age of A is 1.5 times that of B. Four years ago, A's age was twice B's age. What is the present age of A?
A. 10 years
B. 12 years
C. 14 years
D. 16 years
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Let B's age = x, then A = 1.5x. Four years ago: 1.5x – 4 = 2(x – 4) → 0.5x = 4 → x = 8 → A = 12 years. Full solution: Set up equations A = 1.5B and A–4 = 2(B–4). Substitute A = 1.5B → 1.5B – 4 = 2B – 8 → 0.5B = 4 → B = 8, A = 12.
Q3. General Intelligence & Reasoning
A person walks 10 m north, then turns right and walks 15 m, then turns left and walks 10 m, then turns left and walks 15 m. What is the straight-line distance from the starting point?
A. 25 m
B. 30 m
C. 15 m
D. 20 m
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: The path traces a rectangle; the final position is directly north of the start by the sum of the two northward legs (10 m + 10 m = 20 m). Step‑by‑step: Start (0,0). North 10 → (0,10). Right (east) 15 → (15,10). Left (north) 10 → (15,20). Left (west) 15 → (0,20). Distance = 20 m.
Q4. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In a certain code language, each letter is replaced by its mirror image in the alphabet (A↔Z, B↔Y, C↔X, …). What will be the code for the word "MANGO"?
A. NZMTL
B. NZMUL
C. NZMTK
D. NZMSL
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the Atbash cipher – each letter maps to its mirror (A↔Z, B↔Y, …). For MANGO: M(13)→N(14), A(1)→Z(26), N(14)→M(13), G(7)→T(20), O(15)→L(12) → NZMTL. Full solution: Write alphabet positions 1‑26. Mirror position = 27 − original position. Compute each letter's mirror and convert back to letter. M=13 → 14=N; A=1 → 26=Z; N=14 → 13=M; G=7 → 20=T; O=15 → 12=L. Hence the code is NZMTL.
Q5. Elementary Mathematics
A sum of money becomes ₹1,331 in 3 years at 10% per annum compound interest compounded annually. What was the principal?
A. ₹1000
B. ₹1100
C. ₹1200
D. ₹1300
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: (1.1)³ = 1.331, so principal = 1331 ÷ 1.331 = ₹1000. Full solution: Amount = P(1 + 10/100)³ = P(1.1)³ = 1.331P. Given 1.331P = 1331 → P = 1331/1.331 = ₹1000.

Correct Answer: B

Q8. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In the following alphabet series, what will come in place of the question mark? A, C, F, J, O, ?
A. U
B. V
C. T
D. S
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The gaps between consecutive letters increase by 1 each time (+2, +3, +4, +5, +6). So the next gap is +6; O (15th) +6 = 21st = U. Step‑by‑step: A→C (+2), C→F (+3), F→J (+4), J→O (+5), therefore O→? = +6 → U.
Q9. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Who administers the oath of office to the President of India?
A. Prime Minister
B. Chief Justice of India
C. Vice President
D. Attorney General of India
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use option elimination — the oath is administered by the head of the judiciary (CJI), not the executive (PM, VP) or legal advisor (AG). Step-by-step: Article 60 of the Constitution mandates that the President's oath be administered by the Chief Justice of India, or in their absence, the senior-most Judge of the Supreme Court available.
Q10. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Which blood group is designated as the 'Universal Donor' for packed red blood cell transfusions in emergency situations?
A. O Negative
B. AB Positive
C. A Negative
D. B Positive
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Remember 'O Negative' lacks A, B, and Rh antigens (universal donor); 'AB Positive' has all (universal recipient). Eliminate AB+ (recipient), A- (has A antigen), B+ (has B & Rh antigens). Step-by-step: O Negative red cells possess no A, B, or Rh(D) antigens on their surface, minimizing the risk of hemolytic transfusion reactions when the recipient's blood type is unknown.
Q11. Elementary Mathematics
Two pipes A and B can fill a tank in 12 hours and 15 hours respectively. A third pipe C can empty the full tank in 20 hours. If all three pipes are opened together, how long will it take to fill the tank?
A. 8 hrs
B. 9 hrs
C. 10 hrs
D. 12 hrs
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Take LCM of 12, 15, 20 = 60 units (tank capacity). Pipe A fills 5 units/hr, B fills 4 units/hr, C empties 3 units/hr. Net fill = 6 units/hr → time = 60/6 = 10 hrs. Full solution: Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/15 – 1/20 = (5+4–3)/60 = 6/60 = 1/10 tank per hour, so 10 hours to fill.
Q12. English/Hindi
Choose the word that best substitutes the given group of words. **A person who hates mankind**
A. Philanthropist
B. Bibliophile
C. Misanthrope
D. Ascetic
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use root words. 'Miso-' means hatred (misogyny), 'anthro-' means mankind (anthropology). 'Phil-' means love (philanthropist), 'biblio-' means books (bibliophile), 'ascetic' denotes self-discipline. Full Solution: 'Misanthrope' is derived from Greek 'misos' (hatred) and 'anthropos' (mankind). A philanthropist loves mankind, a bibliophile loves books, and an ascetic practices severe self-discipline. Therefore, 'Misanthrope' is the correct one-word substitution.
Q13. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Which river is popularly known as the 'Sorrow of Bihar' due to its frequent devastating floods and frequent course changes?
A. Ganga
B. Gandak
C. Kosi
D. Mahananda
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Associate 'Sorrow' with high silt load and avulsion (course shifting); Kosi is infamous for shifting over 100km westward. Eliminate Ganga (main trunk), Gandak (relatively stable), Mahananda (smaller tributary). Step-by-step: The Kosi River carries massive sediment from the Himalayas, raising its bed level and causing frequent breaches of embankments, leading to catastrophic floods in North Bihar.
Q14. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Who was the first Indian woman to serve as the President of the United Nations General Assembly?
A. Sarojini Naidu
B. Vijaya Lakshmi Pandit
C. Indira Gandhi
D. Sucheta Kripalani
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use role elimination. Sarojini Naidu was a poet/governor; Indira Gandhi was PM; Sucheta Kripalani was first woman CM of a state (UP). The diplomatic role of UNGA President fits Vijaya Lakshmi Pandit, a career diplomat and sister of Nehru. Full Solution: Vijaya Lakshmi Pandit was elected President of the 8th session of the UN General Assembly in 1953, becoming the first woman and first Indian to hold the post. Sarojini Naidu was the first woman Governor of an Indian state (UP). Indira Gandhi was the first woman Prime Minister of India. Sucheta Kripalani was the first woman Chief Minister of an Indian state (UP).
