Q1. English/Hindi
Select the correct Passive Voice form of the sentence: "They will have completed the project by next Friday."
A. The project will be completed by them by next Friday.
B. The project would have been completed by them by next Friday.
C. The project will have been completed by them by next Friday.
D. The project is completed by them by next Friday.
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Tense preservation rule. Active Future Perfect (will have + V3) converts to Passive Future Perfect (will have been + V3). Eliminate options changing tense: 'will be' (Simple Future), 'would have been' (Conditional), 'is' (Present). Only option with 'will have been' is correct. Step-by-step: 1. Identify Active tense: Future Perfect ('will have completed'). 2. Apply rule: Object becomes Subject ('The project'). 3. Add 'be' form matching tense: 'will have been'. 4. Add V3 of main verb ('completed'). 5. Add 'by' + Agent ('by them'). 6. Retain time phrase ('by next Friday').
Q2. English/Hindi
Choose the word which best expresses the meaning of the given phrase: "A person who believes in the existence of God"
A. Theist
B. Atheist
C. Agnostic
D. Pantheist
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use prefix elimination. 'A-' means 'without/not'. 'Theist' comes from Greek 'theos' (God). So, A-theist = No God (incorrect). A-gnostic = No knowledge (incorrect). Pan-theist = All is God (incorrect). Theist = Believer in God (correct). Step-by-step: 1. Identify root 'theos' = God. 2. Analyse prefixes: 'A-' negates (Atheist, Agnostic). 'Pan-' means all (Pantheist). 3. Only 'Theist' directly denotes belief in God without negation or expansion.
Q3. Elementary Mathematics
A train 150 metres long passes a pole in 12 seconds. How much time will it take to pass a platform 350 metres long?
A. 30 seconds
B. 35 seconds
C. 45 seconds
D. 40 seconds
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Speed = 150/12 = 12.5 m/s. Total distance = 150 + 350 = 500 m. Time = 500/12.5 = 40 s. Full: Compute speed from pole crossing, then time = (train length + platform length) / speed.

Q5. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Deficiency of Vitamin D in growing children leads to which disease?
A. Scurvy
B. Beri-Beri
C. Rickets
D. Night Blindness
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Match Vitamin-Disease pairs. Vit C -> Scurvy, Vit B1 -> Beri-Beri, Vit A -> Night Blindness, Vit D -> Rickets (children) / Osteomalacia (adults). Q10. Elementary Mathematics
A can complete a piece of work in 10 days and B can complete the same work in 15 days. They work on alternate days, starting with A. In how many days will the work be completed?
A. 12 days
B. 12.5 days
C. 13 days
D. 13.5 days
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Take LCM of 10 and 15 = 30 units. A's efficiency = 3 units/day, B's = 2 units/day. In 2 days they complete 5 units. 30/5 = 6 cycles → 12 days. Full: A does 1/10 per day, B 1/15 per day. Two days work = 1/6. 6 such pairs = 1 work in 12 days.

Shortcut: Let CP = x. Original SP = 1.2x. New CP = x−100, new SP = 1.2x−100. Profit 25% → 1.2x−100 = 1.25(x−100). Solving gives 0.05x = 25 → x = 500. Full: Set up the equation as above and solve for x.

Q14. Elementary Mathematics
A sum of money becomes 1.44 times itself in 2 years at compound interest compounded annually. What is the annual rate of interest?
A. 15%
B. 20%
C. 25%
D. 30%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: 1.44 = (1.2)^2, so 1 + r/100 = 1.2 → r = 20%. Full: Amount = P(1 + r/100)^2 = 1.44P → (1 + r/100)^2 = 1.44 → 1 + r/100 = 1.2 → r = 20%.
