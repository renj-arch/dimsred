Q1. English/Hindi
Choose the correct Indirect Speech form of the following sentence: The officer said to the recruit, "Why did you arrive late yesterday?"
A. The officer asked the recruit why he had arrived late the previous day.
B. The officer asked the recruit why did he arrive late yesterday.
C. The officer asked the recruit why he arrived late the previous day.
D. The officer asked the recruit that why he had arrived late yesterday.
Correct Answer: A
**Shortcut/Trick (Exam Time):** 1. **Wh-word stays:** 'Why' remains 'why' (eliminate options using 'if/whether' or adding 'that'). 2. **Assertive Order:** Change interrogative order (V+S) to assertive order (S+V) → 'he had arrived' (eliminate B). 3. **Tense Backshift:** Past Simple ('did arrive') → Past Perfect ('had arrived') (eliminate C). 4. **Time Shift:** 'Yesterday' → 'the previous day' / 'the day before'. 5. **Pronoun:** 'You' → 'He' (subject of reporting). **Step-by-Step Solution:** - Reporting verb 'said to' changes to 'asked' (interrogative). - Conjunction: Wh-questions retain the Wh-word ('why'); 'that' is never used (Option D incorrect). - Pronoun 'you' (object of reporting) changes to 'he'. - Verb 'did arrive' (Past Indefinite) backshifts to 'had arrived' (Past Perfect). Option C fails this. - Word order must be Subject + Verb ('he had arrived'), not Verb + Subject ('did he arrive'). Option B fails this. - Adverb 'yesterday' changes to 'the previous day'. - Only Option A satisfies all rules.
Q2. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Select the number that will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following series: 5, 12, 27, 58, 121, ?
A. 246
B. 248
C. 250
D. 252
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Find successive differences: 7, 15, 31, 63. Each difference follows (Previous Diff × 2) + 1. Next difference = 63 × 2 + 1 = 127. Add to last term: 121 + 127 = 248. Unit-digit trick: differences end in 7, 5, 1, 3 (cycle of 4), so next ends in 7; 121 + ...7 gives unit digit 8. Only option with unit digit 8 is 248. Step-by-step: 12-5=7; 27-12=15 (7×2+1); 58-27=31 (15×2+1); 121-58=63 (31×2+1); Next diff=63×2+1=127; Missing term=121+127=248.

Q4. English/Hindi
Change the following sentence into Passive Voice. Open the door.
A. Let the door be opened.
B. The door should be opened.
C. The door is opened.
D. Let the door open.
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Imperative Passive Rule = 'Let + Object + be + V3'. Hidden subject 'You' is dropped. Object 'the door' becomes subject. Verb 'open' becomes 'be opened'. Only Option A follows this fixed structure. Eliminate B (modal 'should' changes tone), C (active voice), D (missing 'be'). Full Solution: The active sentence is an imperative (command) with understood subject 'You'. To convert: 1. Start with 'Let'. 2. Place object 'the door' next. 3. Use 'be' + Past Participle (V3) of main verb 'open' -> 'be opened'. Result: 'Let the door be opened.' Option A is the grammatically correct passive form for a direct order.
Q5. Elementary Mathematics
The monthly incomes of A and B are in the ratio 4:3 and their monthly expenditures are in the ratio 3:2. If each saves ₹6,000 per month, what is the monthly income of A?
A. ₹18,000
B. ₹20,000
C. ₹24,000
D. ₹28,000
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Since savings are equal, the difference in income ratio parts equals the difference in expenditure ratio parts. (4-3) = (3-2) = 1 part. This 1 part corresponds to the savings of ₹6,000. Income of A = 4 parts = 4 × 6,000 = ₹24,000. Step-by-step: Let incomes be 4x, 3x and expenditures be 3y, 2y. Savings: 4x - 3y = 6000 and 3x - 2y = 6000. Equating: 4x - 3y = 3x - 2y → x = y. Substitute x=y in first equation: 4x - 3x = 6000 → x = 6000. Income of A = 4x = ₹24,000.

Q7. Elementary Mathematics
Two trains of lengths 140 m and 160 m are running in opposite directions on parallel tracks at speeds of 60 km/h and 90 km/h respectively. In how many seconds will they completely cross each other?
A. 6.5
B. 7.5
C. 7.2
D. 8.0
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Relative speed = (60+90) km/h = 150 km/h = 150×5/18 = 125/3 m/s. Total length = 140+160 = 300 m. Time = 300 ÷ (125/3) = 300×3/125 = 7.2 s. Step‑by‑step: Convert speeds to m/s: 60 km/h = 60×5/18 = 50/3 m/s, 90 km/h = 90×5/18 = 25 m/s. Relative speed = 50/3 + 25 = 125/3 m/s. Distance = 300 m. Time = Distance/Speed = 300 / (125/3) = 7.2 seconds.
Q8. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Statements: All Pens are Books. Some Books are Pencils. No Pencil is Eraser. Conclusions: I. Some Books are not Erasers. II. All Pens being Pencils is a possibility. III. No Pen is an Eraser.
A. Only I follows
B. Only II follows
C. Only I and II follow
D. All follow
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use standard Venn rules. 'Some A are B + No B are C' => 'Some A are not C' (I follows). 'All A are B + Some B are C' => 'All A can be C' (Possibility II follows). III is not definite as Pens may be the part of Books not touching Pencils. Step-by-step: 1. Pens ⊂ Books. 2. Books ∩ Pencils ≠ ∅. 3. Pencils ∩ Eraser = ∅. I: The intersecting part of Books & Pencils is not Eraser => Follows. II: Pens can fit inside the intersection of Books & Pencils => Possibility Follows. III: Pens could be in Books but outside Pencils, where Erasers might exist => Does not follow.
Q9. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Which Article of the Indian Constitution explicitly provides for the Right to Education (RTE) as a Fundamental Right?
A. Article 21
B. Article 21A
C. Article 45
D. Article 51A
Correct Answer: B
**Shortcut Trick (Association & Elimination):** 1. **Article 21** = Protection of Life & Personal Liberty (Core Fundamental Right). 2. **Article 51A** = Fundamental Duties (Added by 42nd Amendment). 3. **Article 45** = Originally a DPSP for free education for children up to 14 years (now substituted for early childhood care). 4. **Article 21A** = Inserted by the **86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002** specifically to make "Right to Education" a Fundamental Right for children aged 6-14 years. **Step-by-Step Solution:** - The Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009 was enacted under **Article 21A**. - Article 21A states: "The State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children of the age of six to fourteen years in such manner as the State may, by law, determine." - Article 45 was amended to now read: "The State shall endeavour to provide early childhood care and education for all children until they complete the age of six years." - Therefore, the correct Article guaranteeing RTE as a Fundamental Right is Article 21A.

Q11. Elementary Mathematics
The length and breadth of a rectangular park are in the ratio 5:3. The cost of fencing the park at ₹25 per metre is ₹4,000. What is the area of the park?
A. 1200 sq m
B. 1800 sq m
C. 2000 sq m
D. 1500 sq m
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Perimeter = Total Cost / Rate = 4000 / 25 = 160 m. Sum of Length & Breadth = Perimeter / 2 = 80 m. Ratio parts sum = 5+3 = 8. One part = 80 / 8 = 10 m. Length = 50 m, Breadth = 30 m. Area = 50 × 30 = 1500 sq m. Step-by-step: Let length = 5x, breadth = 3x. Perimeter = 2(5x+3x) = 16x. Cost = 16x × 25 = 400x = 4000 → x = 10. Length = 50 m, Breadth = 30 m. Area = 50 × 30 = 1500 sq m.

Q14. Elementary Mathematics
Three bells toll at intervals of 36 seconds, 48 seconds, and 54 seconds respectively. If they start tolling together, after how many minutes will they toll together again?
A. 7.2 minutes
B. 6.5 minutes
C. 8.0 minutes
D. 7.5 minutes
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The bells toll together at the LCM of their intervals. LCM(36, 48, 54) = 2^4 × 3^3 = 16 × 27 = 432 seconds. Convert to minutes: 432 ÷ 60 = 7.2 minutes. Step-by-step: Prime factorize each interval: 36 = 2²×3², 48 = 2⁴×3, 54 = 2×3³. LCM takes highest powers: 2⁴ × 3³ = 432 seconds. 432 seconds = 7 minutes and 12 seconds = 7.2 minutes.
Q15. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Eight friends A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits third to the right of B. C sits second to the left of A. D is not an immediate neighbour of A or B. E sits second to the right of D. F sits third to the right of C. G is not an immediate neighbour of E. Who sits immediately to the left of H?
A. A
B. B
C. F
D. D
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Fix B at a position. Place A (3rd right/clockwise). Place C (2nd left/anti-clockwise of A). Place F (3rd right of C). Use negative condition for D (not neighbor A/B) to fix D and E. Place remaining. Step-by-step: 1. Fix B. A is 3rd right (clockwise). 2. C is 2nd left (anti-clockwise) of A. 3. F is 3rd right of C. 4. D cannot be next to A or B. Only valid spot for D leads to E 2nd right of D. 5. G not next to E fixes G. 6. H takes last spot. H's immediate left (anti-clockwise) is D.
