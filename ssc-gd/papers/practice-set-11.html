Q1. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Select the option that best completes the analogy: Cuboid : Volume :: Circle : ?
A. Area
B. Radius
C. Diameter
D. Circumference
Correct Answer: A
A cuboid is a 3‑dimensional figure measured by volume; a circle is a 2‑dimensional figure measured by area. The relationship is ‘3D shape : its measure :: 2D shape : its measure’. Hence Area is the answer.
Q2. Elementary Mathematics
If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 20% and the denominator is decreased by 10%, the fraction becomes 4/5. What was the original fraction?
A. 2/3
B. 3/5
C. 4/7
D. 5/8
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The multiplier for numerator is 1.2, for denominator 0.9, so overall multiplier = 1.2/0.9 = 4/3. Since new fraction = 4/5, original = (4/5) ÷ (4/3) = 3/5. Full: Let fraction = x/y. (1.2x)/(0.9y)=4/5 => (4/3)(x/y)=4/5 => x/y = (4/5)*(3/4)=3/5.
Q3. Elementary Mathematics
The perimeter of a rectangle is 60 cm and its length is twice its breadth. What is the area of the rectangle?
A. 150 cm²
B. 180 cm²
C. 200 cm²
D. 220 cm²
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Length:breadth = 2:1, so perimeter 2(2x+x)=6x=60 => x=10. Breadth=10, length=20, area=200 cm². Full: Let breadth = b, length = 2b. Perimeter = 2(2b+b)=6b=60 => b=10, length=20. Area = 20×10 = 200 cm².

Q6. Elementary Mathematics
A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. B and C together can complete it in 15 days. C and A together can complete it in 20 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?
A. 30 days
B. 24 days
C. 20 days
D. 18 days
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Add the three pairwise rates: 1/12+1/15+1/20 = 1/5. Half of this gives combined rate of A+B+C = 1/10. Subtract B+C rate (1/15) to get A's rate = 1/10-1/15 = 1/30, so A alone takes 30 days. Full: Let a,b,c be daily work. a+b=1/12, b+c=1/15, c+a=1/20. Adding gives 2(a+b+c)=1/5 => a+b+c=1/10. Then a = (a+b+c)-(b+c)=1/10-1/15=1/30. Hence 30 days.

Q8. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Pointing to a lady, a man said, ‘She is the daughter of the only son of my father’s wife.’ How is the lady related to the man?
A. Sister
B. Niece
C. Mother
D. Daughter
Correct Answer: D
The man’s father’s wife is his mother. The only son of his mother is the man himself (assuming he is the only son). The daughter of that son is the man’s daughter. Hence the lady is his daughter. Shortcut: break the statement step by step from the innermost relation.

Q10. Elementary Mathematics
What is the remainder when 7^95 is divided by 6?
A. 0
B. 5
C. 3
D. 1
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: 7 ≡ 1 (mod 6), so 7^95 ≡ 1^95 ≡ 1 (mod 6). Remainder = 1. Full: By modular arithmetic, 7 = 6+1, so 7^95 = (6+1)^95. All terms except 1^95 are multiples of 6, hence remainder 1.
Q11. English/Hindi
Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error: ‘She is married with a doctor since last year.’
A. She is married
B. with a doctor
C. since last year
D. No error
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The correct preposition after ‘married’ when indicating the spouse is ‘to’, not ‘with’; therefore the error lies in the second segment. Quick elimination: ‘She is married’ is grammatically sound, ‘since last year’ is a correct time expression, and ‘No error’ is false because the preposition is wrong. Full solution: In standard English, one says ‘married to someone’; ‘married with’ is used only when ‘with’ introduces an accompanying circumstance (e.g., ‘married with children’), not the spouse. Hence ‘with a doctor’ should be ‘to a doctor’.
Q12. English/Hindi
Choose the one word that best substitutes the phrase: 'A person who speaks many languages'.
A. Polyglot
B. Linguist
C. Orator
D. Rhetorician
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Eliminate options that do not imply speaking multiple languages – a linguist studies language, an orator is a public speaker, a rhetorician specializes in rhetoric. The term ‘polyglot’ directly means a person who knows and uses several languages. Full solution: ‘Polyglot’ derives from Greek ‘poly’ (many) + ‘glotta’ (tongue), precisely describing a multilingual person, whereas the other terms refer to language study, public speaking, or persuasive writing respectively.
Q13. English/Hindi
Select the meaning of the idiom ‘to burn the midnight oil’.
A. To waste time
B. To sleep early
C. To work late into the night
D. To light a lamp
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Recall the historical context – before electric lights, people burned oil lamps at midnight to continue working; thus the idiom means working late. Eliminate ‘waste time’, ‘sleep early’, and ‘light a lamp’ as they do not convey extended work hours. Full solution: The phrase originates from the practice of using oil lamps for illumination during late-night study or labor, so it figuratively means to work or study late into the night.

Q15. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Find the next term in the series: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
A. 35
B. 37
C. 39
D. 41
Correct Answer: B
The differences between consecutive terms are 3, 5, 7, 9 – consecutive odd numbers. The next difference is 11, so 26 + 11 = 37. Shortcut: check the pattern of differences; they increase by 2 each step.
