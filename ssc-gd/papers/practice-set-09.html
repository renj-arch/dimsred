Q11. General Intelligence & Reasoning
If today is Monday, what was the day 473 days ago?
A. Wednesday
B. Thursday
C. Tuesday
D. Monday
Correct Answer: B
1. Divide the total days (473) by 7 to find full weeks. 2. Calculation: 473 ÷ 7 = 67 weeks with a remainder of 4 days. 3. Since we are going backwards, subtract the remainder from 'Monday'. 4. Monday - 1 day = Sunday; - 2 days = Saturday; - 3 days = Friday; - 4 days = Thursday. 5. The year's leap status does not affect day calculation as it depends purely on the modulo arithmetic of 7.

Q13. Elementary Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a product at a 10% profit. If the cost price is Rs. 100, what is the selling price?
A. Rs. 90
B. Rs. 100
C. Rs. 110
D. Rs. 120
Correct Answer: C
The selling price is calculated by adding the profit to the cost price. 10% of Rs. 100 is Rs. 10, so the selling price is Rs. 100 + Rs. 10 = Rs. 110.
