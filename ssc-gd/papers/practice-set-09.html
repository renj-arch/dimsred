Q1. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Pointing to a photograph, a man said, 'She is the only daughter of the only son of my grandfather.' How is the woman in the photograph related to the man?
A. Sister
B. Mother
C. Daughter
D. Niece
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The only son of the man's grandfather is his father; the only daughter of that father is the man's sister. Step-by-step: Grandfather's only son = man's father. Father's only daughter = man's sister. Hence the woman is his sister.
Q2. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Deficiency of which vitamin leads to the condition known as 'Night Blindness' (Nyctalopia)?
A. Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)
B. Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)
C. Vitamin D (Calciferol)
D. Vitamin A (Retinol)
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Mnemonic 'Vitamin A for Eyes (Retinol/Rhodopsin)'. Night blindness is the hallmark deficiency disease of Vitamin A. B1 causes Beriberi, C causes Scurvy, D causes Rickets. Full Solution: Vitamin A (Retinol) is a component of rhodopsin, the photopigment in retinal rod cells essential for vision in low light. Its deficiency impairs dark adaptation, causing night blindness (nyctalopia) and can progress to xerophthalmia. Vitamin B1 deficiency causes Beriberi. Vitamin C deficiency causes Scurvy. Vitamin D deficiency causes Rickets in children and Osteomalacia in adults. Therefore, Vitamin A is the correct answer.
Q3. Elementary Mathematics
The difference between Compound Interest (compounded annually) and Simple Interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is Rs. 120. Find the principal sum.
A. Rs. 12,000
B. Rs. 10,000
C. Rs. 15,000
D. Rs. 8,000
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Difference for 2 years = P × (R/100)². 120 = P × (10/100)² = P × 1/100 → P = 12,000. Full: SI for 2 years = P×10×2/100 = 0.2P. CI for 2 years = P(1+0.1)² - P = 1.21P - P = 0.21P. Difference = 0.21P - 0.2P = 0.01P = 120 → P = 120/0.01 = 12,000.
Q4. General Knowledge & General Awareness
The Keibul Lamjao National Park, known as the only floating national park in the world, is located in which Indian state?
A. Assam
B. Manipur
C. Meghalaya
D. Tripura
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Keibul Lamjao is uniquely identified by 'floating park' (phumdis on Loktak Lake). Loktak Lake is the largest freshwater lake in Northeast India, situated in Manipur. Eliminate Assam (Kaziranga), Meghalaya (Nokrek), Tripura (Clouded Leopard). Full Solution: Keibul Lamjao National Park is located in the Bishnupur district of Manipur. It floats on the Loktak Lake due to decomposed plant biomass called 'phumdis'. It is the last natural habitat of the endangered Sangai deer (Brow-antlered deer). Assam hosts Kaziranga/Manas, Meghalaya hosts Balpakram/Nokrek, and Tripura hosts Clouded Leopard/Trishna National Parks. Thus, Manipur is the correct answer.
Q5. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Which letter will come in place of the question mark in the series: A, C, F, J, O, ?
A. T
B. U
C. V
D. W
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The gaps between consecutive letters increase by 1 each time (+2, +3, +4, +5), so the next gap is +6; O (15th) +6 = 21st letter = U. Step-by-step: A→C (+2), C→F (+3), F→J (+4), J→O (+5), therefore O→? (+6) = U.
Q6. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Which Article of the Indian Constitution is described by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar as the 'Heart and Soul of the Constitution'?
A. Article 32
B. Article 226
C. Article 14
D. Article 19
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar explicitly called Article 32 the 'Heart and Soul' because it guarantees the Right to Constitutional Remedies, allowing citizens to approach the Supreme Court directly for enforcement of Fundamental Rights. Full Solution: Article 32 provides the Right to Constitutional Remedies. Article 226 empowers High Courts to issue writs (wider scope but not 'Heart and Soul'). Article 14 guarantees Equality before Law. Article 19 protects six freedoms. Therefore, Article 32 is the correct answer.
Q7. General Intelligence & Reasoning
A man walks 10 km towards North, then turns right and walks 5 km, then turns right and walks 10 km, then turns left and walks 8 km. How far is he from the starting point and in which direction?
A. 13 km West
B. 15 km East
C. 13 km East
D. 15 km West
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: North and South movements cancel (10 km each), leaving only East displacement = 5 km + 8 km = 13 km East. Step-by-step: Plot on axes: start (0,0); North to (0,10); right (East) to (5,10); right (South) to (5,0); left (East) to (13,0). Final position is 13 km East of start.
Q8. English/Hindi
In the following sentence, choose the word that best replaces the underlined word: The officer's **adroit** handling of the crisis earned him commendation.
A. clumsy
B. skillful
C. hesitant
D. reckless
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Eliminate words that are antonyms of the underlined term. 'Clumsy', 'hesitant' and 'reckless' all convey lack of skill or care, while 'skillful' matches the meaning of 'adroit'. Full solution: 'Adroit' means clever or skillful in using the hands or mind. Among the options, only 'skillful' carries the same positive sense of expertise; the others are opposite in meaning. Hence the correct replacement is 'skillful'.

Q10. Elementary Mathematics
The ratio of the volumes of two solid spheres is 8 : 27. What is the ratio of their surface areas?
A. 8 : 27
B. 2 : 3
C. 16 : 81
D. 4 : 9
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Volume ratio = (r₁/r₂)³ = 8/27 → Radius ratio r₁/r₂ = 2/3. Surface Area ratio = (r₁/r₂)² = (2/3)² = 4/9. Full: Volume of sphere = (4/3)πr³. V₁/V₂ = r₁³/r₂³ = 8/27 → r₁/r₂ = ∛(8/27) = 2/3. Surface Area = 4πr². SA₁/SA₂ = r₁²/r₂² = (2/3)² = 4/9.
Q11. English/Hindi
Select the most appropriate conjunction to fill the blank: The committee decided to postpone the meeting ______ the key members were unavailable.
A. because
B. although
C. unless
D. while
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Identify the logical relation – the clause after the blank gives the reason for postponement, so a cause‑effect conjunction is needed. 'Because' expresses reason; 'although' shows contrast, 'unless' a condition, and 'while' simultaneity. Full solution: The sentence states that the meeting was postponed due to the unavailability of key members. The conjunction that introduces a reason is 'because'. The other options change the meaning to contrast, condition or time, which do not fit the context.
Q12. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Who was appointed as the first Governor-General of Bengal under the Regulating Act of 1773?
A. Robert Clive
B. Warren Hastings
C. Lord Cornwallis
D. Lord Wellesley
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: The Regulating Act 1773 created the post of Governor-General of Bengal; Warren Hastings (serving 1773–1785) was the first occupant. Robert Clive was Governor of Bengal (1765) before the post existed. Cornwallis (1786) and Wellesley (1798) succeeded Hastings. Full Solution: The Regulating Act of 1773 elevated the Governor of Bengal to Governor-General of Bengal. Warren Hastings, then Governor of Bengal, became the first Governor-General of Bengal in 1773. Lord Cornwallis became Governor-General in 1786, and Lord Wellesley in 1798. Robert Clive was the first Governor of Bengal (1765) but never held the title Governor-General of Bengal. Hence, Warren Hastings is the correct answer.
Q13. Elementary Mathematics
A starts a business with Rs. 5,000. After 3 months, B joins with a capital of Rs. 8,000. At the end of the year, the total profit is Rs. 33,000. What is the share of B in the profit?
A. Rs. 15,000
B. Rs. 18,000
C. Rs. 20,000
D. Rs. 12,000
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Profit ratio = (Capital × Time) ratio. A: 5000 × 12 = 60,000. B: 8000 × 9 = 72,000. Ratio A:B = 60:72 = 5:6. Total 11 parts = 33,000 → 1 part = 3,000. B's share = 6 × 3,000 = 18,000. Full: A invested for 12 months, B for (12-3)=9 months. Equivalent capital of A = 5000×12 = 60,000. Equivalent capital of B = 8000×9 = 72,000. Ratio = 60000:72000 = 5:6. B's profit = (6/11) × 33000 = 18000.
Q14. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Find the missing number in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
A. 40
B. 44
C. 46
D. 42
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Each term follows n×(n+1) for n=1,2,3…; for n=6 the term is 6×7=42. Step-by-step: 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, so the next term is 6×7=42.
Q15. Elementary Mathematics
What is the unit digit of the expression (7^95 × 3^58) + (9^41 × 2^33)?
A. 1
B. 3
C. 5
D. 0
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use cyclicity of unit digits (cycle of 4 for 2,3,7,8; cycle of 2 for 9). 7^95 → 95 mod 4 = 3 → unit digit 3 (7^3=343). 3^58 → 58 mod 4 = 2 → unit digit 9 (3^2=9). First product unit digit = 3×9 = 27 → 7. 9^41 → 41 mod 2 = 1 → unit digit 9. 2^33 → 33 mod 4 = 1 → unit digit 2. Second product unit digit = 9×2 = 18 → 8. Sum unit digit = 7 + 8 = 15 → 5. Full: Cyclicity of 7 is 7,9,3,1. 95=4×23+3 → 3rd position=3. Cyclicity of 3 is 3,9,7,1. 58=4×14+2 → 2nd position=9. 3×9=27→7. Cyclicity of 9 is 9,1. 41 odd→9. Cyclicity of 2 is 2,4,8,6. 33=4×8+1→1st position=2. 9×2=18→8. 7+8=15→5.
