A. 15 km
B. 10 km
C. 0 km
D. 5 km
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: The two 10 km legs are opposite (East then West) and cancel out. The only net displacement is the 5 km North from the first left turn. Distance = 5 km. Step-by-step: 1) Start at origin. 2) Walk 10 km East → (10,0). 3) Turn left (North) walk 5 km → (10,5). 4) Turn left (West) walk 10 km → (0,5). 5) Distance from origin = √(0²+5²) = 5 km.

B. He asked me where did I live.
C. He asked me where I live.
D. He asked me where do I live.
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: In Indirect Speech for Wh-questions: 1. Reporting verb 'said to' becomes 'asked'. 2. Remove quotation marks and 'that' is not used. 3. Change interrogative word order (Verb+Subject) to assertive order (Subject+Verb) — eliminate options with 'did I' or 'do I'. 4. Backshift tense: Simple Present ('do live') becomes Simple Past ('lived'). 5. Pronoun 'you' changes to 'I' (speaker's perspective). Step-by-step: Direct: He said to me, "Where do you live?" -> Reporting verb: 'said to' -> 'asked'. Conjunction: None (Wh-word 'where' joins). Subject/Verb order: 'do you live' -> 'I lived' (assertive + past tense). Pronoun: 'you' -> 'I'. Result: He asked me where I lived.

Present ages of A and B are in the ratio 3:4. Five years ago, the ratio of their ages was 2:3. What is the present age of A?
A. 12 years
B. 14 years
C. 15 years
D. 18 years
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Let present ages be 3x and 4x; five years ago (3x-5)/(4x-5)=2/3 → 9x-15=8x-10 → x=5 → A=15 years. Detailed: Ratio 3:4 gives ages 3x,4x; five years back ratio 2:3 gives equation (3x-5)/(4x-5)=2/3; cross-multiplying yields 9x-15=8x-10; solving gives x=5; hence A’s present age = 3×5 = 15 years.
