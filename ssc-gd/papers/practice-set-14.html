Q1. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Which mountain pass connects the Kashmir Valley (Srinagar) with the Ladakh region (Leh) across the Himalayas?
A. Banihal Pass
B. Rohtang Pass
C. Nathu La
D. Zoji La
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Regional elimination. Banihal connects Jammu to Srinagar; Rohtang connects Manali to Lahaul/Spiti; Nathu La connects Sikkim to Tibet. Zoji La is the gateway to Ladakh from Kashmir. Full Solution: Zoji La is a high mountain pass located on the National Highway 1 (Srinagar-Leh Highway) in the Union Territory of Ladakh, connecting the Kashmir Valley to the Drass and Leh regions.
Q2. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Find the next term in the series: 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ?
A. 95
B. 127
C. 111
D. 103
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Observe the differences between consecutive terms: 4, 8, 16, 32 – each difference doubles. The next difference is 64, so 63 + 64 = 127. Full solution: The series follows the pattern 2^{n+1} - 1 (for n starting at 1): 2^2-1=3, 2^3-1=7, 2^4-1=15, 2^5-1=31, 2^6-1=63, thus the 6th term is 2^7-1 = 127.

  2. Repeat: 7→15, 15→31, 31→63
  3. Apply to 63: 63*2+1 = 127
Q3. Elementary Mathematics
Three partners A, B and C invest in a business. A invests ₹50,000 for 12 months, B invests ₹60,000 for 10 months and C invests ₹70,000 for 8 months. If the total profit at the end of the year is ₹44,000, what is B's share?
A. ₹12,000
B. ₹15,000
C. ₹14,000
D. ₹13,000
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Compute capital×time for each partner: A=50,000×12=6,00,000; B=60,000×10=6,00,000; C=70,000×8=5,60,000. Ratio = 15:15:14. Total parts = 44. Each part = 44,000/44 = 1,000. B’s share = 15×1,000 = ₹15,000. Full method: Same calculation yields B’s share ₹15,000.
Q4. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Under which Article of the Constitution of India is the 'Right to Constitutional Remedies' provided?
A. Article 14
B. Article 19
C. Article 32
D. Article 21
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar famously called Article 32 the 'Heart and Soul of the Constitution'. Use elimination: Article 14 guarantees Equality before law, Article 19 guarantees six Freedoms, and Article 21 guarantees Protection of Life and Personal Liberty. Only Article 32 empowers citizens to move the Supreme Court directly for enforcement of Fundamental Rights. Worked Solution: Part III (Fundamental Rights) lists Article 32 under 'Right to Constitutional Remedies'. It provides the right to approach the Supreme Court (and High Courts under Art 226) for issuance of writs (Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto) to protect Fundamental Rights.

Q6. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Insulin, the hormone responsible for lowering blood glucose levels, is secreted by which organ?
A. Pancreas
B. Liver
C. Thyroid Gland
D. Adrenal Gland
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Functional elimination. Liver stores glycogen (does not secrete insulin); Thyroid secretes Thyroxine; Adrenal secretes Adrenaline/Cortisol. Pancreas (Islets of Langerhans/Beta cells) is the sole source of Insulin. Full Solution: Insulin is a peptide hormone produced by the beta cells of the pancreatic islets (Islets of Langerhans) in the pancreas. It regulates carbohydrate and fat metabolism by promoting glucose uptake from blood into liver, fat, and skeletal muscle cells.
Q7. Elementary Mathematics
The difference between compound interest and simple interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is ₹ 125. Find the principal amount.
A. ₹ 10,000
B. ₹ 12,500
C. ₹ 15,000
D. ₹ 20,000
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For 2 years, difference between CI and SI = P×(R/100)². Here 125 = P×(10/100)² = P×0.01 ⇒ P = 12,500. Full: SI = P×10×2/100 = 0.2P; CI = P(1.1)² - P = 0.21P; difference = 0.01P = 125 ⇒ P = 12,500.

Q8. Elementary Mathematics
Two trains of lengths 150 m and 200 m are running on parallel tracks in opposite directions at speeds of 54 km/h and 72 km/h respectively. In how many seconds will they completely cross each other?
A. 10 s
B. 12 s
C. 14 s
D. 16 s
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Relative speed = (54+72) km/h = 126 km/h = 126×5/18 = 35 m/s. Total length = 150+200 = 350 m. Time = 350/35 = 10 s. Full: Convert speeds to m/s, add for opposite direction, divide total distance by relative speed.
Q9. English/Hindi
Choose the word that can substitute the given group of words. A person who knows many languages
A. Linguist
B. Grammarian
C. Polyglot
D. Orator
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Break the word 'Polyglot' -> 'Poly' (many) + 'glot' (tongue/language). Eliminate 'Linguist' (studies language structure), 'Grammarian' (expert in grammar), 'Orator' (public speaker). Worked Solution: A 'Polyglot' specifically refers to a person who knows and is able to use several languages. A 'Linguist' is a person who studies linguistics (the scientific study of language), not necessarily a speaker of many languages. A 'Grammarian' is an expert in grammar. An 'Orator' is a skilled public speaker. Therefore, 'Polyglot' is the precise one-word substitution.
Q10. English/Hindi
In the following sentence, four segments are underlined and labelled (A), (B), (C) and (D). Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error. Despite of (A) / his hard work, (B) / he failed (C) / to qualify the exam. (D)
A. Despite of
B. his hard work,
C. he failed
D. to qualify the exam.
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Rule check - 'Despite' is a preposition meaning 'in spite of' and never takes 'of' after it. 'In spite of' takes 'of'. Spot the redundant 'of'. Worked Solution: The word 'Despite' functions as a preposition directly governing the noun phrase 'his hard work'. Adding 'of' after 'Despite' is a common grammatical error arising from confusion with the phrase 'in spite of'. The correct forms are 'Despite his hard work' or 'In spite of his hard work'. Segments (B), (C), and (D) are grammatically correct. Hence, the error lies in segment (A).
Q11. Elementary Mathematics
A wire is bent in the form of a circle of radius 14 cm. The same wire is then bent into a square. What is the area of the square? (Use π = 22/7)
A. 400 cm²
B. 441 cm²
C. 529 cm²
D. 484 cm²
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Circumference = 2πr = 2×22/7×14 = 88 cm = perimeter of square ⇒ side = 88/4 = 22 cm ⇒ area = 22² = 484 cm². Full: Wire length unchanged, equate circle circumference to square perimeter, compute side and area.
Q12. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Under Article 110 of the Constitution of India, who has the final authority to certify whether a Bill is a Money Bill or not?
A. President of India
B. Prime Minister
C. Speaker of the Lok Sabha
D. Chairperson of the Rajya Sabha
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Eliminate by constitutional role. The President gives assent but does not certify; the Prime Minister is executive head; Rajya Sabha has no power over Money Bills. Only the Speaker certifies under Art. 110(3). Full Solution: Article 110(3) explicitly states that if any question arises whether a Bill is a Money Bill, the decision of the Speaker of the Lok Sabha thereon shall be final.

B. 342
C. 380
D. 300
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Check unit digits. The logic is n × (n+1). For 18, calculate 18 × 19. Unit digit of 8×9 is 2. Only option with unit digit 2 is 342. Step-by-step: 14 × 15 = 210. Similarly, 18 × 19 = 342.
Q14. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In a certain code language, 'RAIL' is written as 'SBJM'. How will 'TRAIN' be written in that code?
A. USBJO
B. USBJN
C. USBKO
D. USBJP
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Each letter in the word is shifted one place forward in the alphabet (R→S, A→B, I→J, L→M). Apply the same rule to TRAIN: T→U, R→S, A→B, I→J, N→O → USBJO. Full solution: The coding rule is a constant forward shift of 1; encoding each letter of TRAIN gives USBJO.
Q15. English/Hindi
Select the correctly spelt word.
A. Accomodate
B. Privilege
C. Questionaire
D. Maintainance
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Eliminate options with common spelling traps—double consonants (cc/mm), ‘i before e’ patterns, and ‘ance/ence’ endings. Option A misses double ‘m’ (correct: Accommodate). Option C misses double ‘n’ and uses ‘aire’ (correct: Questionnaire). Option D uses ‘ance’ instead of ‘ence’ (correct: Maintenance). Option B ‘Privilege’ has no double letters and follows standard spelling. Full Solution: A is incorrect because ‘Accommodate’ requires double ‘c’ and double ‘m’. C is incorrect because ‘Questionnaire’ requires double ‘n’ and ends in ‘aire’. D is incorrect because ‘Maintenance’ ends with ‘ence’, not ‘ance’. Only B ‘Privilege’ is spelled correctly.
