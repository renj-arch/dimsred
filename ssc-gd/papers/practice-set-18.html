Q1. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In a certain code language, 'TRAIN' is written as 'VTCKP'. How will 'PLANE' be written in that code?
A. RNCPG
B. RNCPF
C. RNCPH
D. RNCPE
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Each letter of the word is shifted forward by two places in the alphabet (A→C, B→D, …). Applying this to PLANE: P→R, L→N, A→C, N→P, E→G gives RNCPG. Full solution: Compare TRAIN and VTCKP: T+2=V, R+2=T, A+2=C, I+2=K, N+2=P. The same +2 shift applied to PLANE yields RNCPG.
Q2. Elementary Mathematics
Find the value of the expression: (0.83³ + 0.17³) / (0.83² - 0.83×0.17 + 0.17²)
A. 0
B. 0.66
C. 1.33
D. 1
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Recognize the numerator as a³ + b³ and denominator as a² - ab + b² where a=0.83, b=0.17. Using identity a³ + b³ = (a+b)(a² - ab + b²), the expression simplifies to a + b = 0.83 + 0.17 = 1. Step-by-step: Let a = 0.83 and b = 0.17. The given expression is (a³ + b³) / (a² - ab + b²). We know the algebraic identity a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² - ab + b²). Substituting this in the numerator, the term (a² - ab + b²) cancels out with the denominator, leaving a + b = 0.83 + 0.17 = 1.

Q4. Elementary Mathematics
The average weight of 8 persons increases by 2.5 kg when a new person comes in place of one of them weighing 56 kg. What is the weight of the new person?
A. 72 kg
B. 76 kg
C. 74 kg
D. 78 kg
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Total weight increase = Increase in average × Number of persons = 2.5 × 8 = 20 kg. Weight of new person = Weight of replaced person + Total increase = 56 + 20 = 76 kg. Step-by-step: Let the initial total weight be W. Initial average = W/8. New total weight = W - 56 + x (where x is new weight). New average = (W - 56 + x)/8. Given new average = old average + 2.5. So (W - 56 + x)/8 = W/8 + 2.5. Multiplying by 8: W - 56 + x = W + 20. Cancelling W: x - 56 = 20, so x = 76 kg.
Q5. English/Hindi
Convert the following Direct Speech into correct Indirect Speech: He said to me, "Please wait here till I return."
A. He ordered me to wait here till he returns.
B. He told me that I should wait here till he returned.
C. He requested me to wait there till he returned.
D. He asked me to wait here until he would return.
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut (Elimination by Keywords): 'Please' indicates a Request, not Order/Tell. Eliminate A (ordered) and B (told). Pronoun 'I' changes to 'he' (speaker). Adverb 'here' changes to 'there'. Eliminate D (retains 'here'). Option C matches all rules: Requested + me + to wait (infinitive) + there + till he returned (backshift). Step-by-step: 1. Identify Reporting Verb: 'said to' + 'Please' -> 'requested'. 2. Change Imperative to Infinitive: 'wait' -> 'to wait'. 3. Change Pronouns: 'me' (object unchanged), 'I' -> 'he'. 4. Change Adverb of Place: 'here' -> 'there'. 5. Change Tense in time clause: 'return' (Present) -> 'returned' (Past). 6. Result: He requested me to wait there till he returned.
Q6. English/Hindi
Select the correct passive form of the sentence: "Who teaches you English?"
A. By whom are you taught English?
B. By whom were you taught English?
C. By whom you are taught English?
D. By whom is English taught by you?
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (Elimination): The sentence is in Simple Present Active (teaches). Passive requires 'is/am/are + V3'. Option B uses 'were' (Past) – eliminate. Option C misses auxiliary 'are' – eliminate. Option D uses 'by you' (retains original subject as agent) creating double passive – eliminate. Option A correctly uses 'are' (for 'you'), 'taught' (V3), and 'By whom'. Step-by-step: 1. Identify S-V-O: Who (Subject) + teaches (Verb) + you (Object) + English (Object). 2. Convert 'Who' to 'By whom'. 3. Make object 'you' the subject. 4. Change verb: Simple Present -> are taught (plural 'you'). 5. Retain 'English' as retained object. Result: By whom are you taught English?
Q7. General Intelligence & Reasoning
If '+' means '×', '-' means '÷', '×' means '-', and '÷' means '+', then what is the value of 16 + 4 ÷ 2 - 1 × 5?
A. 58
B. 61
C. 64
D. 55
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Decode symbols first. '+' becomes '×', so 16 + 4 → 16 × 4 = 64. The final answer will be slightly less than 64 due to the trailing subtraction (1 × 5 becomes 1 - 5 = -4, effectively subtracting 4 from the sum). Option 64 is the intermediate product, 55/58 are too low; 61 fits perfectly. Step-by-step: Replace symbols: 16 × 4 + 2 ÷ 1 - 5. Apply BODMAS: 16 × 4 = 64; 2 ÷ 1 = 2. Expression = 64 + 2 - 5 = 61.
Q8. Elementary Mathematics
The radius of a circular field is 21 metres. Find the cost of fencing the field at the rate of ₹15 per metre. (Use π = 22/7)
A. ₹1890
B. ₹1920
C. ₹1980
D. ₹2010
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Circumference = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 21 = 132 m. Cost = 132 × 15 = (132 × 10) + (132 × 5) = 1320 + 660 = 1980. Step-by-step: Radius r = 21 m. Length of fence required = Perimeter of circle = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 21 = 2 × 22 × 3 = 132 metres. Cost per metre = ₹15. Total cost = 132 × 15 = ₹1980.

Q14. Elementary Mathematics
The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 12 and 336 respectively. If one of the numbers is 84, find the other number.
A. 48
B. 44
C. 36
D. 40
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the formula Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM. The other number = (HCF × LCM) / Given Number = (12 × 336) / 84 = 12 × 4 = 48. Step-by-step: Let the other number be x. We know HCF × LCM = 84 × x. So, 12 × 336 = 84 × x. Dividing both sides by 84 gives x = (12 × 336) / 84. Since 336 ÷ 84 = 4, x = 12 × 4 = 48.
Q15. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Select the number that will come next in the following series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
A. 40
B. 42
C. 44
D. 46
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Observe the differences between consecutive terms: 4, 6, 8, 10. The differences increase by 2 each time, so the next difference is 12. Adding 12 to 30 gives 42. Full solution: The series follows the pattern n×(n+1) for n=1,2,3,4,5 giving 2,6,12,20,30. For n=6, 6×7=42.
