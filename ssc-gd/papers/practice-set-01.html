A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. What is its average speed?
A. 50 km/h
B. 60 km/h
C. 55 km/h
D. 65 km/h
Correct Answer: A
Average speed = Total distance / Total time = 250 km / 5 h = 50 km/h
Q2. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
Which of the following is the next number in the series: 2, 4, 8, 16, ?
A. 32
B. 36
C. 40
D. 48
Correct Answer: A
The pattern of the series is doubling the previous number, so the next number in the series is 32.
Q3. [+1, -0.25] English
Which of the following sentences is in the passive voice?
A. The dog bites the man
B. The man is bitten by the dog
C. The dog is biting the man
D. The man bites the dog
Correct Answer: B
The sentence 'The man is bitten by the dog' is in the passive voice, where the subject 'man' receives the action of the verb 'bites'.
Q4. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
Which of the following is the next number in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30?
A. 40
B. 42
C. 44
D. 46
Correct Answer: B
The pattern of the series is obtained by adding 4, 6, 8, 10, ... to the previous term, so the next number in the series would be 30 + 12 = 42.
Q5. [+1, -0.25] Mathematics
What is the value of x in the equation 2x + 5 = 11?
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
Correct Answer: B
To solve the equation, we need to isolate x. Subtracting 5 from both sides gives 2x = 6, then dividing both sides by 2 gives x = 3.

Q15. [+1, -0.25] Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a product at a 10% profit. If the cost price is Rs. 100, what is the selling price?
A. Rs. 90
B. Rs. 100
C. Rs. 110
D. Rs. 120
Correct Answer: C
The selling price is calculated by adding the profit to the cost price. 10% of Rs. 100 is Rs. 10, so the selling price is Rs. 100 + Rs. 10 = Rs. 110.
