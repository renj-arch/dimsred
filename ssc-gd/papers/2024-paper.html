Q1. [+1, -0.25] Mathematics
⚠ Negative Marking: -0.25 marks per wrong answer
A shopkeeper sells a book at a 10% discount. If the marked price is Rs. 200, what is the selling price?
A. Rs. 180
B. Rs. 190
C. Rs. 200
D. Rs. 210
Correct Answer: A
To find the selling price after a 10% discount, we calculate 10% of the marked price and subtract it from the marked price: 10% of Rs. 200 = 0.10 * Rs. 200 = Rs. 20. Selling price = Rs. 200 - Rs. 20 = Rs. 180.
Q2. [+1, -0.25] General Knowledge
Which of the following rivers is known as the 'Ganga of the South'?
A. Godavari
B. Krishna
C. Kaveri
D. Tungabhadra
Correct Answer: A
The Godavari River is often referred to as the 'Ganga of the South' due to its cultural and geographical significance in southern India.
Q3. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
Which of the following is the next letter in the series: A, C, E, G, I?
A. K
B. J
C. L
D. M
Correct Answer: A
The pattern of the series is obtained by adding 2 to the previous letter in the alphabet, so the next letter in the series would be K.
Q4. [+1, -0.25] Mathematics
What is the value of x in the equation: 2x + 5 = 11?
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
Correct Answer: B
2x + 5 = 11 => 2x = 6 => x = 3
Q5. [+1, -0.25] General Knowledge
Who is the current President of India?
A. Droupadi Murmu
B. Ram Nath Kovind
C. Pranab Mukherjee
D. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
Correct Answer: A
Droupadi Murmu is the current President of India, having taken office in July 2022.
Q6. [+1, -0.25] English
Which of the following is the correct form of the verb in the sentence: 'By the time I ______ (finish) my homework, it was midnight.'
A. had finished
B. have finished
C. finish
D. was finishing
Correct Answer: A
The correct form of the verb is 'had finished' because it is in the past perfect tense, indicating that one action (finishing homework) was completed before another action (it was midnight) in the past.
Q7. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
Which of the following is the next number in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30?
A. 40
B. 42
C. 44
D. 46
Correct Answer: B
The pattern of the series is obtained by adding 4, 6, 8, 10, ... to the previous term, so the next number in the series would be 30 + 12 = 42.
Q8. [+1, -0.25] Mathematics
A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. What is its average speed?
A. 50 km/h
B. 60 km/h
C. 55 km/h
D. 65 km/h
Correct Answer: A
Average speed = Total distance / Total time = 250 km / 5 h = 50 km/h
Q9. [+1, -0.25] Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a product at a 10% profit. If the cost price is Rs. 100, what is the selling price?
A. Rs. 90
B. Rs. 100
C. Rs. 110
D. Rs. 120
Correct Answer: C
The selling price is calculated by adding the profit to the cost price. 10% of Rs. 100 is Rs. 10, so the selling price is Rs. 100 + Rs. 10 = Rs. 110.
Q10. [+1, -0.25] English
Which of the following words is the correct antonym for 'Fast'?
A. Slow
B. Quick
C. Rapid
D. Speedy
Correct Answer: A
The antonym of 'Fast' is 'Slow' because they have opposite meanings in terms of speed or rate of movement.
Q11. [+1, -0.25] Mathematics
A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. What is its average speed?
A. 25 km/h
B. 50 km/h
C. 75 km/h
D. 100 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Average speed is calculated by dividing total distance by total time, so average speed = 250 km / 5 hours = 50 km/h.
Q12. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
A code language uses the following substitution: A=1, B=2, C=3, etc. What is the code for 'HELLO'?
A. 8 5 12 12 15
B. 7 5 11 11 14
C. 6 4 10 10 13
D. 5 3 9 9 12
Correct Answer: A
Using the substitution, H=8, E=5, L=12, L=12, O=15, so the code for 'HELLO' is 8 5 12 12 15.
Q13. [+1, -0.25] Reasoning
In a certain code, 'A' is represented by 'D', 'B' by 'E', and so on. What will 'Z' be represented by?
A. A
B. B
C. C
D. C
Correct Answer: D
Since each letter is shifted 3 places forward in the alphabet (A to D, B to E), 'Z' would be shifted to 'C' because the sequence wraps around the alphabet when it reaches 'Z'.
Q14. [+1, -0.25] General Knowledge
Which of the following countries is the largest producer of milk?
A. India
B. United States
C. China
D. Brazil
Correct Answer: A
India is the largest producer of milk in the world, accounting for over 18% of global milk production.
Q15. [+1, -0.25] General Knowledge
What is the chemical symbol for gold?
A. Ag
B. Au
C. Hg
D. Pb
Correct Answer: B
The chemical symbol 'Au' is derived from the Latin word 'Aurum', which means gold.
