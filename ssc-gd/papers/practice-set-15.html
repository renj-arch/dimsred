A certain sum amounts to ₹ 5,600 in 3 years and ₹ 6,800 in 6 years at simple interest. What is the principal?
A. ₹ 4,000
B. ₹ 4,200
C. ₹ 4,400
D. ₹ 4,600
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The difference in amounts (₹6,800−₹5,600=₹1,200) is the interest for 3 years (6−3), so annual interest = ₹400. Interest for 3 years = ₹1,200, thus principal = ₹5,600−₹1,200 = ₹4,400. Full: Let P be principal, R rate. Amount after 3 years = P+3PR/100 = 5600; after 6 years = P+6PR/100 = 6800. Subtracting gives 3PR/100 = 1200 => PR/100 = 400. Substitute back: P+3*400 = 5600 => P = 4400.

Q13. Elementary Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-23
The radius of a circle is increased by 50%. What is the percentage increase in its area?
A. 50%
B. 125%
C. 100%
D. 150%
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Area ∝ r². New radius = 1.5r, so new area = (1.5)² = 2.25 times original, i.e., a 125% increase. Full: Original area = πr². New area = π(1.5r)² = 2.25πr². Increase = 2.25πr² − πr² = 1.25πr², which is 125% of original.

A man travels from point A to point B at a speed of 40 km/h and returns from B to A at a speed of 60 km/h. What is his average speed for the entire round trip?
A. 45 km/h
B. 48 km/h
C. 50 km/h
D. 55 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For equal distances, average speed = 2ab/(a+b) = 2×40×60/(40+60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h. Full method: Let distance be d km. Time for onward = d/40 h, return = d/60 h. Total distance = 2d, total time = d/40 + d/60 = (3d+2d)/120 = 5d/120 = d/24 h. Average speed = 2d ÷ (d/24) = 48 km/h.
