Q1. English
Which of the following sentences is in the passive voice?
A. The dog bites the man
B. The man is bitten by the dog
C. The dog is biting the man
D. The man bites the dog
Correct Answer: B
The sentence 'The man is bitten by the dog' is in the passive voice, where the subject 'man' receives the action of the verb 'bites'.
Q2. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a product at a 10% profit. If the cost price is Rs. 100, what is the selling price?
A. Rs. 90
B. Rs. 100
C. Rs. 110
D. Rs. 120
Correct Answer: C
The selling price is calculated by adding the profit to the cost price. 10% of Rs. 100 is Rs. 10, so the selling price is Rs. 100 + Rs. 10 = Rs. 110.
Q3. English
What is the meaning of the word 'Ennui'?
A. A feeling of listlessness and boredom
B. A feeling of excitement and energy
C. A feeling of happiness and joy
D. A feeling of anger and frustration
Correct Answer: A
The word 'Ennui' refers to a feeling of listlessness and boredom, which is a state of mind characterized by a lack of interest and excitement.
Q4. Reasoning
Which of the following is the next number in the series: 2, 4, 8, 16, ?
A. 32
B. 36
C. 40
D. 48
Correct Answer: A
The pattern of the series is doubling the previous number, so the next number in the series is 32.
Q5. Mathematics
What is the ratio of the perimeter of a rectangle to its area?
A. 1:2
B. 2:1
C. Cannot be determined
D. 1:1
Correct Answer: C
The ratio of the perimeter of a rectangle to its area cannot be determined without knowing the length and width of the rectangle.
Q6. English
Which of the following sentences is grammatically correct?
A. Me and my friend goes to the store
B. My friend and I go to the store
C. My friend and me go to the store
D. I and my friend goes to the store
Correct Answer: B
The correct sentence should have the subject-verb agreement and the correct order of pronouns, which is 'My friend and I go to the store'.

B. The tense is incorrect

Q13. Mathematics
What is the value of x in the equation: x/4 + 2 = 9?
