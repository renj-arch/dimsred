Q1. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Statements: All pens are pencils. Some pencils are erasers. No eraser is a sharpener. Conclusions: I. Some pens are erasers. II. No pencil is a sharpener. III. Some pencils are not sharpeners. Which of the given conclusions logically follows from the statements?
A. Only I follows
B. Only II follows
C. Only III follows
D. None follows
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Since some pencils are erasers and no eraser is a sharpener, those pencils that are erasers cannot be sharpeners; hence some pencils are not sharpeners (III follows). I does not follow because pens are a subset of pencils and the erasers may lie outside the pen subset. II does not follow because only erasers are excluded from sharpeners, other pencils could be sharpeners. Full solution: Draw Venn diagrams – Pens inside Pencils; overlap Pencils–Erasers; Eraser and Sharpener disjoint. The overlapping region of Pencils and Erasers is non‑empty and disjoint from Sharpener, proving III. No region forces I or II.
Q2. English/Hindi
Choose the correct Indirect Speech for the given sentence: He said to me, "I will help you in your project tomorrow."
A. He told me that he would help me in my project the next day.
B. He told me that he will help me in my project tomorrow.
C. He said to me that he would help me in my project the next day.
D. He told me that he would help you in your project the next day.
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut (STOP Rule): **S**aid to → **T**old; **T**ense backshift (will → would); **O**ther words (tomorrow → the next day); **P**ronouns (I → he, you → me, your → my). Step-by-step: 1. Reporting verb 'said to' changes to 'told'. 2. Conjunction 'that' is added. 3. Pronoun 'I' changes to 'he' (subject of reporting verb), 'you' changes to 'me' (object), 'your' changes to 'my'. 4. Modal 'will' backshifts to 'would'. 5. Time expression 'tomorrow' changes to 'the next day'. Option A satisfies all rules.
Q3. English/Hindi
Select the most appropriate meaning of the given idiom. A bolt from the blue
A. A pleasant surprise
B. A sudden unexpected event
C. A thunderstorm
D. A planned strategy
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Associate the word 'blue' with the open sky. A 'bolt' (lightning) striking from a clear blue sky is sudden, shocking, and unannounced. Eliminate 'pleasant surprise' (positive connotation), 'thunderstorm' (literal/weather), and 'planned strategy' (opposite of sudden). Step-by-step: The idiom 'a bolt from the blue' originates from the imagery of a lightning bolt appearing unexpectedly from a clear blue sky. It metaphorically describes a sudden, unforeseen event or piece of news that causes great surprise or shock. Option B captures this exact meaning.
Q4. Elementary Mathematics
A train 150 metres long crosses a pole in 10 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
A. 45 km/h
B. 48 km/h
C. 54 km/h
D. 60 km/h
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Speed = length/time = 150/10 = 15 m/s. Convert to km/h by multiplying by 18/5: 15 × 18/5 = 54 km/h. Stepwise: Distance = 150 m, Time = 10 s ⇒ Speed = 15 m/s. 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h, so 15 × 3.6 = 54 km/h.
Q5. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Who was the first Governor General of India?
A. Warren Hastings
B. William Bentinck
C. Lord Canning
D. Lord Mountbatten
Correct Answer: B
Warren Hastings was the first Governor General of Bengal (1773). The Charter Act of 1833 redesignated the post as Governor General of India, making William Bentinck the first holder. Shortcut: Distinguish 'Bengal' (Hastings) from 'India' (Bentinck).
Q6. Elementary Mathematics
A sum of money amounts to ₹13,200 in 2 years at 10% per annum simple interest. Find the principal.
A. ₹11,000
B. ₹12,000
C. ₹10,000
D. ₹11,500
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: At 10% for 2 years the interest is 20%, so amount = 120% of principal. Principal = 13200 × 100/120 = ₹11,000. Stepwise: SI = P×R×T/100 = P×10×2/100 = 0.2P. Amount = P + 0.2P = 1.2P = 13200 ⇒ P = 13200/1.2 = 11000.

Q10. English/Hindi
Choose the correct passive voice of the given sentence. The committee has approved the new policy.
A. The new policy is approved by the committee.
B. The new policy was approved by the committee.
C. The new policy has been approved by the committee.
D. The new policy had been approved by the committee.
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Identify the tense of the active verb ('has approved' = Present Perfect). The passive equivalent must retain the same tense: 'has/have + been + V3'. Scan options for 'has been approved'. Eliminate Simple Present ('is approved'), Simple Past ('was approved'), and Past Perfect ('had been approved'). Step-by-step: 1. Identify Subject (The committee), Verb (has approved - Present Perfect), Object (the new policy). 2. Make Object the new Subject: 'The new policy'. 3. Apply Present Perfect Passive structure: 'has/have + been + Past Participle' -> 'has been approved'. 4. Add 'by + original subject' -> 'by the committee'. Result: 'The new policy has been approved by the committee.'
Q11. Elementary Mathematics
A and B together can finish a work in 12 days. A alone can finish it in 20 days. How many days will B alone take to finish the work?
A. 24 days
B. 30 days
C. 28 days
D. 36 days
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: 1/B = 1/12 – 1/20 = (5–3)/60 = 2/60 = 1/30 ⇒ B = 30 days. Detailed: A's 1 day work = 1/20, (A+B)'s 1 day work = 1/12. So B's 1 day work = 1/12 – 1/20 = 1/30. Hence B alone takes 30 days.
Q12. General Intelligence & Reasoning
A person walks 10 km North, turns right and walks 5 km, turns right again and walks 10 km, then turns left and walks 5 km. What is the straight‑line distance from the starting point?
A. 5 km
B. 15 km
C. 10 km
D. 20 km
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Plot the moves on a coordinate grid; net displacement is 10 km East, 0 km North → distance 10 km. Step‑by‑step: Start (0,0); N10 → (0,10); E5 → (5,10); S10 → (5,0); E5 → (10,0); distance = √(10²+0²)=10 km.
Q13. General Intelligence & Reasoning
In a certain code, each letter of a word is replaced by its opposite letter in the alphabet (A↔Z, B↔Y, …). The resulting string is then reversed. What will be the code for the word "LION"?
A. MLRO
B. MLRP
C. MLOR
D. MOLR
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Opposite letters have positions summing to 27; after conversion, reverse the string. Step‑by‑step: L(12)→O(15), I(9)→R(18), O(15)→L(12), N(14)→M(13) gives ORLM; reversing yields MLRO.
Q14. General Knowledge & General Awareness
Which of the following metals remains in liquid state at room temperature?
A. Sodium
B. Lead
C. Mercury
D. Zinc
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut/Trick: Associate Mercury with clinical thermometers which use a silvery liquid metal; Sodium is stored in kerosene (solid), while Lead and Zinc are hard solids used in batteries/galvanization. Worked Solution: Mercury (Hg) has a melting point of -38.83°C, so it is liquid at standard room temperature (~25°C). Sodium melts at 97.8°C, Lead at 327.5°C, and Zinc at 419.5°C, making them solids at room temperature.
Q15. Elementary Mathematics
In a 60 litre mixture the ratio of milk to water is 2:1. How many litres of water should be added to make the ratio 1:2?
A. 40 litres
B. 30 litres
C. 50 litres
D. 60 litres
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Milk = 2/3 × 60 = 40 L (constant). For final ratio 1:2, water must be 2 × milk = 80 L. Extra water needed = 80 – 20 = 60 L. Detailed: Initial water = 20 L. Let x be added water. Then 40/(20+x) = 1/2 ⇒ 80 = 20+x ⇒ x = 60 L.
