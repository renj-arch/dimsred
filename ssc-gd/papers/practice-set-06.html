Q1. Mathematics
What is the value of x in the equation: x/4 + 2 = 9?
A. 20
B. 28
C. 30
D. 32
Correct Answer: B
x/4 + 2 = 9 => x/4 = 7 => x = 28
Q2. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a book at a 10% discount. If the marked price is Rs. 200, what is the selling price?
A. Rs. 180
B. Rs. 190
C. Rs. 200
D. Rs. 210
Correct Answer: A
To find the selling price after a 10% discount, we calculate 10% of the marked price and subtract it from the marked price: 10% of Rs. 200 = 0.10 * Rs. 200 = Rs. 20. Selling price = Rs. 200 - Rs. 20 = Rs. 180.

Q5. English
Which of the following is the correct form of the possessive adjective 'its'?
A. it's
B. its
C. its'
D. its's
Correct Answer: B
The correct form of the possessive adjective is 'its', without an apostrophe. 'It's' is a contraction of 'it is' or 'it has'.

Q7. English
Which of the following is the correct form of the verb in the sentence: 'By the time I ______ (finish) my homework, it was midnight.'
A. had finished
B. have finished
C. finish
D. was finishing
Correct Answer: A
The correct form of the verb is 'had finished' because it is in the past perfect tense, indicating that one action (finishing homework) was completed before another action (it was midnight) in the past.
Q8. Reasoning
If it takes 5 machines 5 minutes to make 5 widgets, how long would it take 100 machines to make 100 widgets?
A. 5 minutes
B. 10 minutes
C. 50 minutes
D. 100 minutes
Correct Answer: A
The rate of production per machine does not change, so if 5 machines can make 5 widgets in 5 minutes, 100 machines can make 100 widgets in the same amount of time, which is 5 minutes.
Q9. Reasoning
A bat and a ball together cost Rs. 100. The bat costs Rs. 90 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?
A. Rs. 5
B. Rs. 10
C. Rs. 15
D. Rs. 20
Correct Answer: A
Let's denote the cost of the ball as x. Since the bat costs Rs. 90 more than the ball, the bat's cost is x + 90. The sum of their costs is Rs. 100: x + (x + 90) = 100. Simplifying, we get 2x + 90 = 100, then 2x = 10, and x = 5.
Q10. English
Which of the following sentences is in the correct grammatical form?
A. Me and my friend goes to the store.
B. My friend and I go to the store.
C. I and my friend goes to the store.
D. My friend and me go to the store.
Correct Answer: B
The correct sentence is 'My friend and I go to the store' because the subject-verb agreement is correct, and 'I' is used as the subject, not 'me'.

Q15. Mathematics
A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. What is its average speed?
A. 50 km/h
B. 60 km/h
C. 70 km/h
D. 80 km/h
Correct Answer: A
To find the average speed, we divide the total distance traveled by the total time taken: 250 km / 5 hours = 50 km/h.
