Q1. Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a product at a 10% profit. If the cost price is Rs. 100, what is the selling price?
A. Rs. 90
B. Rs. 100
C. Rs. 110
D. Rs. 120
Correct Answer: C
The selling price is calculated by adding the profit to the cost price. 10% of Rs. 100 is Rs. 10, so the selling price is Rs. 100 + Rs. 10 = Rs. 110.
Q2. General Knowledge
Which of the following rivers is known as the 'Ganga of the South'?
A. Godavari
B. Krishna
C. Kaveri
D. Tungabhadra
Correct Answer: A
The river Godavari is known as the 'Ganga of the South' due to its cultural and spiritual significance in southern India.
Q3. Reasoning
Which of the following is the next number in the series: 2, 4, 8, 16, ?
A. 32
B. 36
C. 40
D. 48
Correct Answer: A
The pattern of the series is doubling the previous number, so the next number in the series is 32.
Q4. Mathematics
A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. What is its average speed?
A. 25 km/h
B. 50 km/h
C. 75 km/h
D. 100 km/h
Correct Answer: B
Average speed is calculated by dividing total distance by total time, so average speed = 250 km / 5 hours = 50 km/h.
Q5. Reasoning
If 'a' denotes addition, 'b' denotes subtraction, 'c' denotes multiplication, and 'd' denotes division, then what does 'a b c d' stand for?
A. Add Subtract Multiply Divide
B. Subtract Multiply Divide Add
C. Multiply Divide Add Subtract
D. Divide Add Subtract Multiply
Correct Answer: A
Given the denotations, 'a b c d' directly translates to 'Add Subtract Multiply Divide'.
Q6. General Knowledge
Which of the following rivers flows through the Grand Canyon?
A. Colorado River
B. Mississippi River
C. Rio Grande
D. Missouri River
Correct Answer: A
The Colorado River is the river that flows through the Grand Canyon, one of the most famous natural wonders in the United States.
Q7. English
Which of the following is the correct form of the verb in the sentence: 'If I ______ (know) the answer, I would tell you.'
A. know
B. knew
C. have known
D. was knowing
Correct Answer: B
The correct form of the verb is 'knew' because the sentence is in the second conditional form, which requires the past simple tense in the if-clause.
Q8. General Knowledge
Which of the following rivers flows through the Satpura range?
A. Narmada
B. Godavari
C. Krishna
D. Kaveri
Correct Answer: A
The Narmada river flows through the Satpura range.
Q9. Reasoning
In a certain code, 'A' is represented by 'D', 'B' by 'E', and so on. What will 'Z' be represented by?
A. A
B. B
C. C
D. C
Correct Answer: D
Since each letter is shifted 3 places forward in the alphabet (A to D, B to E), 'Z' would be shifted to 'C' because the sequence wraps around the alphabet when it reaches 'Z'.
Q10. Mathematics
A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. What is its average speed?
A. 50 km/h
B. 60 km/h
C. 55 km/h
D. 65 km/h
Correct Answer: A
Average speed = Total distance / Total time = 250 km / 5 h = 50 km/h
Q11. Reasoning
Which of the following is the next letter in the series: A, C, E, G, I?
A. K
B. J
C. L
D. M
Correct Answer: A
The pattern of the series is obtained by adding 2 to the previous letter in the alphabet, so the next letter in the series would be K.
Q12. Mathematics
What is the value of x in the equation: 2x + 5 = 11?
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
Correct Answer: B
To solve for x, we first subtract 5 from both sides of the equation: 2x = 11 - 5, which simplifies to 2x = 6. Then, we divide both sides by 2: x = 6 / 2 = 3.
Q13. Mathematics
What is the area of a triangle with a base of 10 cm and a height of 6 cm?
A. 20 sq. cm
B. 30 sq. cm
C. 40 sq. cm
D. 50 sq. cm
Correct Answer: B
The area of a triangle is calculated using the formula 1/2 * base * height. Substituting the given values, we get 1/2 * 10 * 6 = 30 sq. cm.
Q14. Reasoning
Which of the following is the correct analogy: 'Finger : Hand :: ? : Foot'
A. Toe : Foot
B. Ankle : Foot
C. Knee : Foot
D. Leg : Foot
Correct Answer: A
The correct analogy is 'Finger : Hand :: Toe : Foot' because just as a finger is a part of a hand, a toe is a part of a foot.
Q15. General Knowledge
Who is the founder of Facebook?
A. Mark Zuckerberg
B. Bill Gates
C. Steve Jobs
D. Sergey Brin
Correct Answer: A
Mark Zuckerberg co-founded Facebook in 2004 while he was a student at Harvard University.
