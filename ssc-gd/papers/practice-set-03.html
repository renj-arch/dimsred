Q4. [+1, -0.25] Mathematics
What is the value of x in the equation 2x + 5 = 11?
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
Correct Answer: B
To solve the equation, we need to isolate x. Subtracting 5 from both sides gives 2x = 6, then dividing both sides by 2 gives x = 3.
Q5. [+1, -0.25] Mathematics
A shopkeeper sells a product at a 10% profit. If the cost price is Rs. 100, what is the selling price?
A. Rs. 90
B. Rs. 100
C. Rs. 110
D. Rs. 120
Correct Answer: C
The selling price is calculated by adding the profit to the cost price. 10% of Rs. 100 is Rs. 10, so the selling price is Rs. 100 + Rs. 10 = Rs. 110.
