How to use these pages

  • Scripts first — every page opens with the writing system; read the chart aloud before anything else.
  • Sound pairs — pronunciation notes flag sounds your mouth has never made (Cyrillic ы, Navajo tone, Arabic ع). Practice them slowly.
  • Grammar in small doses — cases, agglutination, and word order are explained with plain-English tables, not jargon.
  • Phrasebook every time — each page ends with real phrases you can use immediately while travelling or studying.
  • Numbers included — knowing how to count to 100 in each language is the fastest confidence builder.

Which language should I start with?

If you already read a Romance or Germanic language, Russian/Slavic gives you the most familiar grammar concepts wrapped in a new alphabet. If you love logic and puzzles, Korean's Hangul was designed to be learned in a day. If you want the biggest real-world payoff per hour, Mandarin or Arabic reaches the most speakers. Icelandic and Navajo are the "challenge modes" — learn them because they are fascinating, not because they are easy.
