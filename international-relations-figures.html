International Relations — Figure Pack Withheld

WITHHELD PENDING DATA FIX

The auto-suggested figures for this subject have been temporarily withdrawn. They were not wrong by accident at the image level — the topic selection itself was wrong.

Why

These packs picked each subject's figures from the highest-count subtopics in the question shards. Those shards are contaminated: each subject's set is a near-complete A–Z English Wikipedia sweep rather than subject-specific material. So the "top" subtopics were generic, high-traffic topics, and the figures inherited them. A scan of the Animal Husbandry shards alone returned thousands of hits for unrelated topics (e.g. "Democratic Party"), which is how that topic was offered as a figure here.

Status

  • Every figure in this pack was unverified auto-suggested material — 0 had been checked.
  • The build script is now gated off and will not regenerate these packs (see scripts/build-subject-figures.js, requires FIGURES_ALLOW_UNVERIFIED=1).
  • The curated Geography figure pack is unaffected and remains live.

Returning

These packs will be rebuilt from subject-specific topic data once the shards are corrected and each candidate figure is verified. Nothing here should be treated as study material in the meantime.
