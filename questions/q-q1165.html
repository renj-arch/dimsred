Indian Premier League
Cricket is by far the most popular sport in India. The India national cricket team won the 1983 Cricket World Cup, the 2011 Cricket World Cup, the 2007 T20 World Cup, the 2024 T20 World Cup, the 2002 ICC Champions Trophy, the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy and the 2025 ICC Champions Trophy. Domestic competitions include the Ranji Trophy, the Duleep Trophy, the Deodhar Trophy, the Irani Trophy and the Challenger Series. In addition, BCCI conducts the Indian Premier League, a Twenty20 competition. Football is popular in the Indian states of Kerala, Goa, West Bengal and the Northeastern states.
