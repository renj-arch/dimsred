_____ scientists were found to justify scientific censorship with harm avoidance
Answer
Some
Science has been described as "the most important tool" for separating valid claims from invalid ones. Sometimes, research can be well-intended but is incorrect, obsolete, incomplete, or over-simplified expositions of scientific ideas. Academic freedom can be reduced by scientific censorship caused by governments, institutions, other scientists or self-censorship. Some scientists were found to justify scientific censorship with harm avoidance. There can also be an element of political bias or ideological bias in science.
