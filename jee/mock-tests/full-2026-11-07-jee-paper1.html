Q1Physics
The Doppler effect in sound is observed when there is relative motion between source and:
Answer: C

Doppler effect occurs when source or observer is in motion relative to each other.

Q2Physics
SI unit of resistance is:
Answer: D

Q3Physics
The photon energy of light with wavelength 500 nm is (h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ Js):
Answer: B

E = hc/λ = (6.63×10⁻³⁴ × 3×10⁸)/(500×10⁻⁹) = 3.98×10⁻¹⁹ J.

Q4Physics
The unit of electric current in SI is:
Answer: B

Electric current is measured in Amperes (A).

Q5Physics
The speed of sound is maximum in:
Answer: C

Sound travels fastest in solids (steel ~5000 m/s) due to close molecular arrangement.

Q6Physics
A convex lens has focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens should an object be placed to get a real image twice the size of object?
Answer: B

Magnification m = -2 (real image). m = v/u = -2 ⇒ v = -2u. Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u. 1/20 = 1/(-2u) - 1/u = -3/(2u). u = -30 cm. So object distance = 30 cm.

Q7Physics
First law of thermodynamics is based on:
Answer: B

ΔU = Q - W. First law is the law of conservation of energy for thermodynamic systems.

Q8Physics
The efficiency of a Carnot engine operating between 500 K and 300 K is:
Answer: B

η = 1 - T2/T1 = 1 - 300/500 = 0.4 = 40%.

Q9Physics
A wire of resistance R is stretched to double its length. New resistance will be:
Answer: C

When length is doubled (L' = 2L), cross-sectional area halves (A' = A/2). R' = ρL'/A' = ρ(2L)/(A/2) = 4ρL/A = 4R.

Q10Physics
A particle is moving with velocity v = 2t² + 3t. Find its acceleration at t = 2s.
Answer: A

a = dv/dt = d(2t² + 3t)/dt = 4t + 3. At t = 2: a = 4(2) + 3 = 11 m/s².

Q11Physics
The equivalent resistance of three 6 Ω resistors connected in parallel is:
Answer: C

1/Rp = 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 ⇒ Rp = 2 Ω.

Q12Physics
A ray of light passes from air into a glass slab with a refractive index of 1.5. If the angle of incidence is 30 degrees, what is the angle of refraction?
Answer: A

Using Snell's law, n1 sin(θ1) = n2 sin(θ2), where n1 = 1 (air) and n2 = 1.5 (glass). Rearranging the equation to solve for θ2, we get sin(θ2) = (n1 / n2) * sin(θ1) = (1 / 1.5) * sin(30) = (2/3) * 0.5 = 1/3. Then θ2 = arcsin(1/3) ≈ 19.5 degrees.

Q13Physics
Two resistors 4 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in series. The total resistance is:
Answer: B

Rₛ = 4 + 6 = 10 Ω.

Q14Physics
In SHM, the acceleration is maximum at:
Answer: C

a = -ω²x. Acceleration is maximum at extreme positions (x = ±A) and zero at mean position.

Q15Physics
The escape velocity of a body from Earth is 11.2 km/s. What is the escape velocity from a planet having mass 4 times and radius twice that of Earth?
Answer: C

v_e = sqrt(2GM/R). For new planet: v_e' = sqrt(2G.4M/2R) = sqrt(2.2GM/R) = sqrt2.sqrt(2GM/R) = sqrt2 x 11.2 = 15.84 km/s.

Q16Physics
A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m/s in 10 s. The acceleration is:
Answer: B

a = (v-u)/t = (20-0)/10 = 2 m/s².

Q17Physics
The phenomenon of splitting of white light into its components is called:
Answer: C

Dispersion is the splitting of white light into its constituent colors (VIBGYOR).

Q18Physics
Hooke's law relates stress and:
Answer: B

Within elastic limit, stress ∝ strain (Hooke's law).

Chemistry 21 Q · 100 marks

Q20Chemistry
What is the oxidation number of chromium in the compound Cr2O7^2-?
Answer: C

Let's denote the oxidation number of chromium as x. The oxidation number of oxygen is -2. The compound has a charge of -2, so the sum of the oxidation numbers of all the atoms must be equal to -2. Therefore, 2x + 7(-2) = -2. Simplifying the equation, we get 2x - 14 = -2. Then 2x = 12, and x = 6.

Q21Chemistry
How many moles of O2 are required for complete combustion of 1 mole of C4H10?
Answer: C

C4H10 + 6.5O2 → 4CO2 + 5H2O. So 6.5 moles of O2 are needed.

Q30Chemistry
What is the pH of a 0.1 M solution of NaOH?
Answer: C

NaOH is a strong base, so it completely dissociates in water to give OH- ions. The concentration of OH- ions is equal to the concentration of NaOH, which is 0.1 M. Using the equation pOH = -log[OH-], we get pOH = -log(0.1) = 1. Then, pH + pOH = 14, so pH = 14 - pOH = 14 - 1 = 13.

Q31Chemistry
