Q12Physics

Q15Physics
A convex lens has focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens should an object be placed to get a real image twice the size of object?
Answer: B

Magnification m = -2 (real image). m = v/u = -2 ⇒ v = -2u. Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u. 1/20 = 1/(-2u) - 1/u = -3/(2u). u = -30 cm. So object distance = 30 cm.

Q16Physics

Q18Physics
The escape velocity of a body from Earth is 11.2 km/s. What is the escape velocity from a planet having mass 4 times and radius twice that of Earth?
Answer: C

v_e = v(2GM/R). For new planet: v_e' = v(2G·4M/2R) = v(2·2GM/R) = v2·v(2GM/R) = v2 × 11.2 = 15.84 km/s.

Q20Physics

Q21Physics
In a low‑supporting‑electrolyte solution, the limiting current at an electrode is not purely diffusion‑controlled. Which additional transport mechanism contributes significantly to the limiting current under these conditions?
Answer: B

Correct answer: Migration.

Q22Physics
A wire of resistance R is stretched to double its length. New resistance will be:
Answer: C

When length is doubled (L' = 2L), cross-sectional area halves (A' = A/2). R' = ρL'/A' = ρ(2L)/(A/2) = 4ρL/A = 4R.

Q23Physics

Answer: B

E = hc/λ = (6.63×10⁻³⁴ × 3×10⁸)/(500×10⁻⁹) = 3.98×10⁻¹⁹ J.

a = -ω²x. Acceleration is maximum at extreme positions (x = ±A) and zero at mean position.

Q40Chemistry
What is the molecular formula of the compound with the empirical formula CH2O and a molecular weight of 120 g/mol?
Answer: B

The empirical formula is CH2O, and its molecular weight is 30 g/mol. To find the molecular formula, we need to multiply the empirical formula by a factor that will give us the molecular weight of 120 g/mol. The factor is 120 / 30 = 4. So, the molecular formula is (CH2O) * 4 = C4H8O4 * (3/4) = C3H6O3.

Q44Chemistry
What is the pH of a 0.1 M solution of NaOH?
Answer: C

NaOH is a strong base, so it completely dissociates in water to give OH- ions. The concentration of OH- ions is equal to the concentration of NaOH, which is 0.1 M. Using the equation pOH = -log[OH-], we get pOH = -log(0.1) = 1. Then, pH + pOH = 14, so pH = 14 - pOH = 14 - 1 = 13.
