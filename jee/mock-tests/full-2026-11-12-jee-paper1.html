Q1Physics
The de Broglie wavelength of an electron moving with velocity v is . If velocity is doubled, wavelength becomes:
Answer: B

= h/p = h/(mv). If v doubles, ' = h/(m.2v) = /2.

Q2Physics
First law of thermodynamics is based on:
Answer: B

ΔU = Q - W. First law is the law of conservation of energy for thermodynamic systems.

Q3Physics
The equivalent resistance of three 6 Ω resistors connected in parallel is:
Answer: C

1/Rp = 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 ⇒ Rp = 2 Ω.

Q4Physics

Answer: B

H = u^2/2g = 400/20 = 20 m.

Q5Physics

Answer: D

Q6Physics
The escape velocity of a body from Earth is 11.2 km/s. What is the escape velocity from a planet having mass 4 times and radius twice that of Earth?
Answer: C

v_e = sqrt(2GM/R). For new planet: v_e' = sqrt(2G.4M/2R) = sqrt(2.2GM/R) = sqrt2.sqrt(2GM/R) = sqrt2 x 11.2 = 15.84 km/s.

Q7Physics
A body of mass 1 kg has weight approximately:
Answer: B

W = mg = 1 × 10 = 10 N (approx).

Q8Physics
A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m/s in 10 s. The acceleration is:
Answer: B

a = (v-u)/t = (20-0)/10 = 2 m/s^2.

Q9Physics
A particle is moving with velocity v = 2t² + 3t. Find its acceleration at t = 2s.
Answer: A

a = dv/dt = d(2t² + 3t)/dt = 4t + 3. At t = 2: a = 4(2) + 3 = 11 m/s².

Q10Physics
The speed of sound is maximum in:
Answer: C

Sound travels fastest in solids (steel ~5000 m/s) due to close molecular arrangement.

Q11Physics
If the distance between two charges is halved, the force between them becomes:
Answer: C

F ∝ 1/r². If r → r/2, F → 4F.

Q12Physics
A wire of resistance R is stretched to double its length. New resistance will be:
Answer: C

When length is doubled (L' = 2L), cross-sectional area halves (A' = A/2). R' = L'/A' = (2L)/(A/2) = 4L/A = 4R.

Q13Physics
A convex lens has focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens should an object be placed to get a real image twice the size of object?
Answer: B

Magnification m = -2 (real image). m = v/u = -2 ⇒ v = -2u. Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u. 1/20 = 1/(-2u) - 1/u = -3/(2u). u = -30 cm. So object distance = 30 cm.

Q14Physics
A ball dropped from height h hits the ground with speed v. The value of v is proportional to:
Answer: B

v = sqrt(2gh) -> v sqrth.

Q15Physics
The efficiency of a Carnot engine operating between 500 K and 300 K is:
Answer: B

= 1 - T/T = 1 - 300/500 = 0.4 = 40%.

Q16Physics
Young's modulus is defined for which type of deformation?
Answer: B

Y = stress/strain for longitudinal (tensile/compressive) deformation.

Q17Physics
A block of mass 5 kg is moving with a velocity of 2 m/s. What is the magnitude of its momentum?
Answer: B

The momentum of an object is given by the product of its mass and velocity. So, the magnitude of the momentum is p = mv = 5 kg * 2 m/s = 10 kg m/s.

Q18Physics

Q19Physics

Q20Physics

Q21Physics
The phenomenon of splitting of white light into its components is called:

Q22Physics
The temperature at which Celsius and Fahrenheit scales read the same is:
Answer: C

C/5 = (F-32)/9. Set C = F = x: x/5 = (x-32)/9 ⇒ 9x = 5x-160 ⇒ x = -40.

Q23Physics
A Carnot engine operates between two reservoirs at temperatures 500 K and 300 K. What is the efficiency of the engine?
Answer: B

The efficiency of a Carnot engine is given by the equation eta = 1 - (T2/T1), where T1 and T2 are the temperatures of the two reservoirs. For the given temperatures, eta = 1 - (300/500) = 1 - 0.6 = 0.4.

Q24Physics
A thermodynamic system undergoes a process where the internal energy change is 100 J and the heat added to the system is 200 J. What is the work done by the system?
Answer: A

The first law of thermodynamics states that ΔU = Q - W, where ΔU is the internal energy change, Q is the heat added, and W is the work done. Rearranging the equation to solve for W, we get W = Q - ΔU = 200 J - 100 J = 100 J. However, the work done 'by' the system is in the opposite direction, so the work done by the system is -100 J.

Q25Physics

Answer: B

R = 4 + 6 = 10 .

Q40Chemistry
What is the pH of a 0.1 M solution of NaOH?
Answer: C

NaOH is a strong base, so it completely dissociates in water to give OH- ions. The concentration of OH- ions is equal to the concentration of NaOH, which is 0.1 M. Using the equation pOH = -log[OH-], we get pOH = -log(0.1) = 1. Then, pH + pOH = 14, so pH = 14 - pOH = 14 - 1 = 13.

Q41Chemistry
The pH of 10 M HCl solution is:

Q68Mathematics
If the function f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3 is given, what is the value of x for which f(x) is maximum or minimum?
Answer: C

To find the maximum or minimum of the function f(x), we need to find the critical points by taking the derivative of f(x) and setting it equal to zero. The derivative of f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3 is f'(x) = 2x - 4. Setting f'(x) = 0, we get 2x - 4 = 0, which gives x = 2.

Q72Mathematics
If the equation x^2 + 2x + 1 = 0 has two equal roots, what is the value of the discriminant?
Answer: A

The discriminant of a quadratic equation ax^2 + bx + c = 0 is given by b^2 - 4ac. For the given equation x^2 + 2x + 1 = 0, a = 1, b = 2, and c = 1. The discriminant is 2^2 - 4*1*1 = 0.
