Key Concepts

What are Vectors & 3D Geometry?

Vectors are mathematical quantities with both magnitude and direction. 3D geometry extends coordinate geometry to three dimensions. In JEE, this topic covers vector algebra, dot/cross products, and lines/planes in 3D space. Weightage is approximately 8-10% in JEE Main.

Key Concepts

1. Vector Basics: A vector AB has magnitude |AB|. Position vector of point P(x,y,z): r = xî + yĵ + zk̂. Unit vector â = a/|a|. Zero vector has magnitude 0. Collinear vectors have same/opposite direction.

2. Dot Product: a·b = |a||b|cosθ = a₁b₁ + a₂b₂ + a₃b₃. If a·b = 0, vectors are perpendicular. Projection of a on b = (a·b)/|b|.

3. Cross Product: a×b = |a||b|sinθ n̂ = determinant|î ĵ k̂; a₁ a₂ a₃; b₁ b₂ b₃|. Direction: perpendicular to both a and b (right-hand rule). Area of parallelogram = |a×b|. Area of triangle = |a×b|/2.

4. Scalar Triple Product: [a b c] = a·(b×c) = determinant. Volume of parallelepiped = |[a b c]|. Coplanar if [a b c] = 0.

5. Lines in 3D: Vector form: r = a + λb. Cartesian: (x-x₁)/l = (y-y₁)/m = (z-z₁)/n where (l,m,n) are direction ratios. Angle between lines: cosθ = (l₁l₂+m₁m₂+n₁n₂)/√(l₁²+m₁²+n₁²)√(l₂²+m₂²+n₂²). Shortest distance between two skew lines = |(a₂-a₁)·(b₁×b₂)|/|b₁×b₂|.

6. Planes in 3D: Equation: ax + by + cz + d = 0. Vector: r·n = d. Angle between planes: cosine of angle between normals. Distance from point to plane: d = |ax₁+by₁+cz₁+d|/√(a²+b²+c²). Family of planes through intersection of two planes: P₁ + λP₂ = 0.

Example 3 (3D Plane)
Find the equation of the plane passing through the points A(1,0,0), B(0,1,0), and C(0,0,1).

a) x + y + z = 1 b) x + y + z = 0 c) x + y - z = 1 d) x - y + z = 1
Solution: a) x + y + z = 1. The plane through A(1,0,0), B(0,1,0), C(0,0,1) has intercept form: x/1 + y/1 + z/1 = 1, i.e., x + y + z = 1. This is the plane with intercepts 1 on all three axes.

Shortcut Techniques

  • For coplanarity of three vectors, check if scalar triple product is zero — this is faster than solving equations.
  • Shortest distance between skew lines can be found by checking if lines intersect first — if they do, SD = 0.
  • For plane through three points, use determinant form:|x y z 1; x₁ y₁ z₁ 1; x₂ y₂ z₂ 1; x₃ y₃ z₃ 1| = 0.
  • Area of triangle in 3D can be found using cross product of two side vectors — avoids Heron's formula.
  • Vector approach is often simpler than Cartesian for JEE problems — use vector forms whenever possible.
Pro Tip
In JEE, vector and 3D geometry questions are among the most scoring — they are formula-based with low computation. Master the vector approach for lines and planes (using r = a + λb and r·n = d) as it simplifies complex geometry into algebraic manipulation.
