Q1. Mathematics
If z = (1 + i)^6, then the value of
A. 4
B. 8
C. 16
D. 2
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: |1+i| = √2, so |(1+i)^6| = (√2)^6 = 2^3 = 8. Full: (1+i)^2 = 2i, then (1+i)^6 = (2i)^3 = 8i^3 = -8i, whose modulus is 8.
Q2. Mathematics
The area of the triangle formed by the lines x = 0, y = 0 and 3x + 4y = 12 is:
A. 3
B. 12
C. 6
D. 24
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The line 3x+4y=12 cuts axes at (4,0) and (0,3). With coordinate axes, it forms a right triangle. Area = ½ × x-intercept × y-intercept = ½ × 4 × 3 = 6 sq units. Step-by-step: Find x-intercept by setting y=0 ⇒ 3x=12 ⇒ x=4. Find y-intercept by setting x=0 ⇒ 4y=12 ⇒ y=3. Vertices are (0,0), (4,0), (0,3). Use right triangle area formula ½ × base × height.
Q3. Chemistry
The decreasing order of reactivity towards SN2 reaction for the following alkyl bromides is:
A. CH₃Br > CH₃CH₂Br > (CH₃)₂CHBr > (CH₃)₃CBr
B. (CH₃)₃CBr > (CH₃)₂CHBr > CH₃CH₂Br > CH₃Br
C. CH₃CH₂Br > CH₃Br > (CH₃)₂CHBr > (CH₃)₃CBr
D. (CH₃)₂CHBr > CH₃Br > CH₃CH₂Br > (CH₃)₃CBr
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: SN2 reactivity decreases with increasing steric hindrance: methyl > primary > secondary > tertiary. Option A follows this order exactly. Stepwise: CH₃Br (methyl) least hindered, fastest; CH₃CH₂Br (primary) next; (CH₃)₂CHBr (secondary) slower; (CH₃)₃CBr (tertiary) essentially no SN2. Hence the correct sequence is CH₃Br > CH₃CH₂Br > (CH₃)₂CHBr > (CH₃)₃CBr.
Q4. Chemistry
The standard electrode potentials are: Zn²⁺/Zn = –0.76 V, Cu²⁺/Cu = +0.34 V. For the cell Zn(s)
A. 1.10 V
B. 1.07 V
C. 1.03 V
D. 0.99 V
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: E°cell = 0.34 – (–0.76) = 1.10 V. Since the reaction quotient Q = [Zn²⁺]/[Cu²⁺] = 10, the Nernst term at 298 K is (0.0591/2)log10 = 0.02955 V. Subtracting gives 1.10 – 0.02955 ≈ 1.07 V. Stepwise: E°cell = 1.10 V; n = 2; E = E° – (0.0591/n) log Q = 1.10 – 0.02955 × 1 = 1.07045 V ≈ 1.07 V.

Q6. Mathematics
Two fair dice are rolled simultaneously. The probability that the sum of the numbers appearing is a prime number is:
A. 5/12
B. 1/3
C. 7/18
D. 5/18
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Total outcomes = 36. Prime sums possible: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11. Favourable pairs: Sum 2 (1), Sum 3 (2), Sum 5 (4), Sum 7 (6), Sum 11 (2). Total favourable = 15. Probability = 15/36 = 5/12. Step-by-step: List all 36 ordered pairs. Identify pairs giving prime sums (2,3,5,7,11). Count frequency: (1,1); (1,2),(2,1); (1,4),(2,3),(3,2),(4,1); (1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1); (5,6),(6,5). Divide 15 by 36.
Research Team · ~4s · Symmetry Shortcut
  1. Prime sums: 2,3,5,7,11. Their counts symmetric: 1,2,4,6,2
  2. Sum counts = 15. Probability = 15/36 = 5/12
Q7. Physics
One mole of a monoatomic ideal gas (γ = 5/3) expands adiabatically from volume V to 2V. If the initial temperature is T0, the final temperature is:
A. T0 / 2^(2/3)
B. T0 / 2^(3/2)
C. T0 / 2^(1/3)
D. T0 / 2^(5/3)
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For adiabatic process TV^(γ-1)=constant. Here γ-1 = 2/3, so T_f = T0 (V_i/V_f)^(2/3) = T0/2^(2/3). Full solution: Adiabatic relation T V^(γ-1) = constant. With γ = 5/3, γ-1 = 2/3. Initial state: T0 V^(2/3). Final state: T_f (2V)^(2/3). Equating gives T_f = T0 (V/(2V))^(2/3) = T0/2^(2/3).
Research Team · ~3s · Exponent elimination
  1. Adiabatic expansion cools gas, T_f < T0.
  2. For monoatomic γ=5/3, exponent is γ-1=2/3.
  3. Only option A has exponent 2/3.
  4. Confirm: T_f = T0 / 2^(2/3).
  5. Answer A.
Q8. Physics
A particle is moving with velocity v = 2t² + 3t. Find its acceleration at t = 2s.
A. 11 m/s²
B. 10 m/s²
C. 8 m/s²
D. 14 m/s²
Correct Answer: A
a = dv/dt = d(2t² + 3t)/dt = 4t + 3. At t = 2: a = 4(2) + 3 = 11 m/s².
Q9. Physics
A uniform disc of mass M and radius R has a concentric circular hole of radius R/2 cut out from it. The moment of inertia of the remaining part about an axis perpendicular to its plane and passing through its centre is:
A. (1/2) MR^2
B. (3/8) MR^2
C. (15/32) MR^2
D. (7/16) MR^2
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The removed disc has mass M/4 (area ratio) and MI = 1/2*(M/4)*(R/2)^2 = MR^2/32. Full disc MI = 1/2 MR^2 = 16/32 MR^2. Subtracting gives 15/32 MR^2. Full solution: MI of full disc = ½MR². Mass of hole = M*(π(R/2)²)/(πR²) = M/4. MI of hole about same axis = ½*(M/4)*(R/2)² = MR²/32. MI remaining = ½MR² - MR²/32 = (16/32 - 1/32)MR² = 15/32 MR².

Q14. Mathematics
The coefficient of x^2 in the expansion of (1 + x)^7 is:
A. 7
B. 14
C. 35
D. 21
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: The coefficient of x^r in (1+x)^n is nCr; here n=7, r=2 gives 7C2 = 21. Full: Expand using binomial theorem: (1+x)^7 = Σ_{k=0}^7 7Ck x^k; the term with x^2 is 7C2 x^2 = 21 x^2.
Q15. Chemistry
For a first-order reaction, the half-life is 20 minutes. The time required for 75% completion of the reaction is:
A. 40 min
B. 20 min
C. 60 min
D. 80 min
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: 75% completion corresponds to two half-lives (50% in first, 25% in second). Time = 2 × t₁/₂ = 2 × 20 = 40 min. Full Solution: For a first-order reaction, t₁/₂ = 0.693/k. Fraction remaining after time t is (1/2)^(t/t₁/₂). For 75% completion, fraction remaining = 0.25 = (1/2)², so t/t₁/₂ = 2 → t = 40 min.
