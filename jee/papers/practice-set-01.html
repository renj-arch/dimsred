Q5. [+4, -1] Physics
A wire of resistance R is stretched to double its length. New resistance will be:
A. R/2
B. R
C. 4R
D. 2R
Correct Answer: C
When length is doubled (L' = 2L), cross-sectional area halves (A' = A/2). R' = ρL'/A' = ρ(2L)/(A/2) = 4ρL/A = 4R.

Q12. [+4, -1] Physics
The escape velocity of a body from Earth is 11.2 km/s. What is the escape velocity from a planet having mass 4 times and radius twice that of Earth?
A. 22.4 km/s
B. 5.6 km/s
C. 15.8 km/s
D. 11.2 km/s
Correct Answer: C
v_e = √(2GM/R). For new planet: v_e' = √(2G·4M/2R) = √(2·2GM/R) = √2·√(2GM/R) = √2 × 11.2 = 15.84 km/s.

Q14. [+4, -1] Physics
A particle is moving with velocity v = 2t² + 3t. Find its acceleration at t = 2s.
A. 11 m/s²
B. 10 m/s²
C. 8 m/s²
D. 14 m/s²
Correct Answer: A
a = dv/dt = d(2t² + 3t)/dt = 4t + 3. At t = 2: a = 4(2) + 3 = 11 m/s².

Q16. [+4, -1] Mathematics
If sin θ = 3/5, then cos θ is:
A. 4/5
B. 4/5
C. 3/5

Correct Answer: B
