Q1. Mathematics
Find the derivative of f(x) = sin(x^2).
A. cos(x^2)
B. 2x cos(x)
C. 2x cos(x^2)
D. x^2 cos(x^2)
Correct Answer: C
Using chain rule: d/dx[sin(x^2)] = cos(x^2) . d/dx(x^2) = 2x cos(x^2).
Q2. Physics
If the distance between two charges is halved, the force between them becomes:
A. Half
B. Double
C. Four times
D. Same
Correct Answer: C
F ∝ 1/r². If r → r/2, F → 4F.
Q3. Mathematics
The number of ways to arrange letters of word CAT is:
A. 3
B. 4
C. 6
D. 9
Correct Answer: C
3! = 6 ways (CAT, CTA, ACT, ATC, TCA, TAC).
Q4. Chemistry
Which element has the highest ionization energy?
A. Li
B. Na
C. He
D. Ne
Correct Answer: C
Helium has the highest ionization energy due to its full 1s^2 configuration and small atomic size.

Q7. Mathematics

Q8. Chemistry
The coordination number in NaCl crystal structure is:
A. 4
B. 6
C. 8
D. 12
Correct Answer: B
NaCl has face-centered cubic (FCC) structure with each Na⁺ surrounded by 6 Cl⁻ and vice versa.

Q10. Chemistry
Which of the following shows +R effect?
A. -OH
B. -NO2
C. -CN
D. -COOH
Correct Answer: A
-OH, -NH2, -OR show +R (resonance) effect as they donate electrons. -NO2, -CN, -COOH show -R effect.

Q12. Physics
The equivalent resistance of three 6 Ω resistors connected in parallel is:
A. 18 Ω
B. 6 Ω
C. 2 Ω
D. 3 Ω
Correct Answer: C
1/Rp = 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 ⇒ Rp = 2 Ω.
Q13. Physics
The speed of sound is maximum in:
A. Air
B. Water
C. Steel
D. Vacuum
Correct Answer: C
Sound travels fastest in solids (steel ~5000 m/s) due to close molecular arrangement.
Q14. Physics
A block of mass 2 kg is moving with a velocity of 3 m/s on a frictionless surface. What is the kinetic energy of the block?
A. 9 J
B. 6 J
C. 12 J
D. 4 J
Correct Answer: A
Kinetic energy = (1/2)mv^2 = (1/2)*2*3^2 = 9 J.

Fractional distillation separates petroleum into fractions based on boiling points.
