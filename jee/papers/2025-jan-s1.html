Q1. Physics
A ball dropped from height h hits the ground with speed v. The value of v is proportional to:
A. h
B. √h
C. h²
D. 1/h
Correct Answer: B
v = √(2gh) → v ∝ √h.
Q2. Physics
If the distance between two charges is halved, the force between them becomes:
A. Half
B. Double
C. Four times
D. Same
Correct Answer: C
F ∝ 1/r². If r → r/2, F → 4F.
Q3. Physics
SI unit of resistance is:
A. Volt
B. Ampere
C. Coulomb
D. Ohm
Correct Answer: D
Resistance is measured in Ohms (Ω).
Q4. Physics
Hooke's law relates stress and:
A. Force
B. Strain
C. Area
D. Volume
Correct Answer: B
Within elastic limit, stress ∝ strain (Hooke's law).
Q5. Physics
The Doppler effect in sound is observed when there is relative motion between source and:
A. Observer only
B. Medium only
C. Observer or source
D. Neither
Correct Answer: C
Doppler effect occurs when source or observer is in motion relative to each other.
Q6. Physics
The SI unit of magnetic flux is:
A. Tesla
B. Gauss
C. Weber
D. Henry
Correct Answer: C
Magnetic flux is measured in Webers (Wb).
Q7. Physics
A body of mass 1 kg has weight approximately:
A. 1 N
B. 10 N
C. 100 N
D. 0.1 N
Correct Answer: B
W = mg = 1 × 10 = 10 N (approx).
Q8. Physics
The phenomenon of splitting of white light into its components is called:
A. Reflection
B. Refraction
C. Dispersion
D. Diffraction
Correct Answer: C
Dispersion is the splitting of white light into its constituent colors (VIBGYOR).
Q9. Physics
Transformer works on the principle of:
A. Self induction
B. Mutual induction
C. Electromagnetic induction
D. Both B and C
Correct Answer: D
Transformer works on electromagnetic induction via mutual induction between coils.
Q10. Physics
The speed of sound is maximum in:
A. Air
B. Water
C. Steel
D. Vacuum
Correct Answer: C
Sound travels fastest in solids (steel ~5000 m/s) due to close molecular arrangement.

Which of the following is a strong acid?

The gas used in welding is:
A. Methane

Q14. Chemistry

A. 50 g/mol
B. 84 g/mol
C. 100 g/mol
D. 150 g/mol

Ca(40) + C(12) + 3O(48) = 100 g/mol.

Q18. Chemistry
Which process is used to separate crude oil into useful fractions?

Q19. Chemistry
The pH of 0.001 M NaOH is approximately:
A. 3
B. 7
C. 10
D. 11
Correct Answer: D
[OH⁻] = 10⁻³, pOH = 3, pH = 14 - 3 = 11.
Q20. Chemistry
Which of these is a noble gas?
A. Cl₂
B. N₂
C. Xenon
D. O₂

Xenon (Xe) is a noble gas. Cl₂, N₂, O₂ are diatomic gases.
Q21. Mathematics

Q29. Mathematics
The range of the function f(x) = x² is:
A. All real numbers

D. (0, ∞)
Correct Answer: B
x² ≥ 0 for all real x → range [0, ∞).
Q30. Mathematics
The sum of first n odd natural numbers is:
A. n
B. 2n
C. n²
D. n(n+1)/2
Correct Answer: C
1 + 3 + 5 + ... + (2n-1) = n².
