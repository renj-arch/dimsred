A convex lens has focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens should an object be placed to get a real image twice the size of object?
A. 40 cm
B. 30 cm
C. 20 cm
D. 60 cm
Correct Answer: B
Magnification m = -2 (real image). m = v/u = -2 -> v = -2u. Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u. 1/20 = 1/(-2u) - 1/u = -3/(2u). u = -30 cm. So object distance = 30 cm.

Q4. [+4, -1] Chemistry
What is the oxidation number of chromium in the compound Cr2O7^2-?
A. +3
B. +4
C. +6
D. +8
Correct Answer: C
Let's denote the oxidation number of chromium as x. The oxidation number of oxygen is -2. The compound has a charge of -2, so the sum of the oxidation numbers of all the atoms must be equal to -2. Therefore, 2x + 7(-2) = -2. Simplifying the equation, we get 2x - 14 = -2. Then 2x = 12, and x = 6.

Q9. [+4, -1] Physics
A block of mass 2 kg is placed on a horizontal surface. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the block and the surface is 0.5. If a horizontal force of 3 N is applied on the block, what is the acceleration of the block?
A. 0.5 m/s^2
B. 1 m/s^2
C. 1.5 m/s^2
D. 2 m/s^2
Correct Answer: A
The force of kinetic friction is given by F = μN = μmg = 0.5 * 2 * 9.8 = 9.8 N. The net force acting on the block is F_net = 3 N - 9.8 N (friction) = -6.8 N (in the opposite direction). Therefore, the acceleration is given by a = F_net / m = -6.8 / 2 = -3.4 m/s^2. However, the applied force is not enough to move the block, so we consider the force of static friction instead, which is less than or equal to the kinetic friction. Since the applied force is less than the maximum static friction (which is also 9.8 N), the block does not move. But if we were to consider the case where the block does move (for example, with a greater applied force), we would use the kinetic friction value to calculate the acceleration, which in this case is not the correct scenario.
Q10. [+4, -1] Physics
A ray of light passes from air into a glass slab with a refractive index of 1.5. If the angle of incidence is 30 degrees, what is the angle of refraction?
A. 19.5 degrees
B. 20 degrees
C. 45 degrees
D. 60 degrees
Correct Answer: A
Using Snell's law, n1 sin(θ1) = n2 sin(θ2), where n1 = 1 (air) and n2 = 1.5 (glass). Rearranging the equation to solve for θ2, we get sin(θ2) = (n1 / n2) * sin(θ1) = (1 / 1.5) * sin(30) = (2/3) * 0.5 = 1/3. Then θ2 = arcsin(1/3) ≈ 19.5 degrees.
