Q1. Physicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
One mole of an ideal monoatomic gas undergoes a reversible adiabatic expansion such that its volume becomes 8 times the initial volume. The ratio of final temperature to initial temperature (T₂/T₁) is:
A. 1/2
B. 1/4
C. 1/8
D. 1/16
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a reversible adiabatic process, TV^(γ-1) = constant. For a monoatomic gas, γ = 5/3, so γ-1 = 2/3. Since V₂ = 8V₁, (V₂/V₁)^(2/3) = 8^(2/3) = (2³)^(2/3) = 2² = 4. Thus T₁/T₂ = 4, giving T₂/T₁ = 1/4. Full Solution: Using the adiabatic relation T₁V₁^(γ-1) = T₂V₂^(γ-1). Substitute γ = Cp/Cv = 5/3 for monoatomic gas. T₂/T₁ = (V₁/V₂)^(γ-1) = (1/8)^(2/3) = 1/4.
Q2. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
Let \(A\) be a \(3 \times 3\) matrix with \(\det(A) = 2\). Find \(\det(\operatorname{adj}(\operatorname{adj}(A)))\).
A. 4
B. 16
C. 8
D. 32
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For any \(n \times n\) matrix, \(\det(\operatorname{adj} A) = (\det A)^{n-1}\). For \(n=3\), \(\det(\operatorname{adj} A) = (\det A)^2 = 4\). Applying the property again, \(\det(\operatorname{adj}(\operatorname{adj} A)) = (\det(\operatorname{adj} A))^2 = 4^2 = 16\). Full solution: Given \(\det A = 2\). Since \(\operatorname{adj} A = (\det A) A^{-1}\), \(\det(\operatorname{adj} A) = (\det A)^{3-1} = 2^2 = 4\). Then \(\operatorname{adj}(\operatorname{adj} A) = \det(\operatorname{adj} A) (\operatorname{adj} A)^{-1}\), so its determinant is \((\det(\operatorname{adj} A))^{3-1} = 4^2 = 16\).
Q3. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
Let ω be a complex cube root of unity (ω ≠ 1). The value of the product (1+ω)(1+ω²)(1+ω⁴)(1+ω⁸) ... up to 2n factors is:
A. 1
B. -1
C. ω
D. ω²
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use 1+ω+ω²=0 ⇒ 1+ω = -ω² and 1+ω² = -ω. The product of each consecutive pair (1+ω)(1+ω²) = (-ω²)(-ω) = ω³ = 1. Since ω³=1, the sequence of factors repeats every two terms: (1+ω), (1+ω²), (1+ω), (1+ω²)... For 2n factors, there are n such pairs. Hence the total product is 1ⁿ = 1. Step-by-step: Given ω³=1 and 1+ω+ω²=0. The factors are (1+ω), (1+ω²), (1+ω⁴), (1+ω⁸)... Since ω⁴=ω and ω⁸=ω², the factors alternate between (1+ω) and (1+ω²). Compute one pair: (1+ω)(1+ω²) = 1 + ω + ω² + ω³ = (1+ω+ω²) + 1 = 0 + 1 = 1. There are 2n factors, forming n pairs. Final product = 1ⁿ = 1.

A satellite is revolving around the Earth in a circular orbit of radius R with orbital speed v. If the radius of the orbit is increased to 4R by firing rockets, the new orbital speed of the satellite will be:
A. v/4
B. v/2
C. v/√2
D. 2v
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a circular orbit, orbital speed v = √(GM/r). Speed is inversely proportional to the square root of radius (v ∝ 1/√r). If radius becomes 4 times (r₂ = 4r₁), speed becomes 1/√4 = 1/2 times the original. New speed = v/2. Full Solution: Centripetal force is provided by gravity: mv²/r = GMm/r² ⇒ v = √(GM/r). For initial orbit: v₁ = √(GM/R) = v. For final orbit: v₂ = √(GM/4R) = (1/2)√(GM/R) = v/2.

C. 760.4 mm Hg
D. 748.0 mm Hg
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For dilute solutions, relative lowering of vapour pressure (ΔP/P°) ≈ moles of solute / moles of solvent. Moles glucose = 18/180 = 0.1; moles water = 178.2/18 = 9.9. Ratio ≈ 0.0101. Q8. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
If the circles x² + y² + 2x + 2ky + 6 = 0 and x² + y² + 2kx + 2y + 6 = 0 cut orthogonally, then the value of k is:
A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Orthogonality condition for circles x²+y²+2g₁x+2f₁y+c₁=0 and x²+y²+2g₂x+2f₂y+c₂=0 is 2g₁g₂ + 2f₁f₂ = c₁ + c₂. Here g₁=1, f₁=k, c₁=6; g₂=k, f₂=1, c₂=6. Substitute: 2(1)(k) + 2(k)(1) = 6+6 ⇒ 4k = 12 ⇒ k = 3. Step-by-step: Circle 1: centre (-1, -k), radius² = 1+k²-6 = k²-5. Circle 2: centre (-k, -1), radius² = k²+1-6 = k²-5. Distance between centres² = (k-1)² + (1-k)² = 2(k-1)². For orthogonal intersection, d² = r₁² + r₂² ⇒ 2(k-1)² = 2(k²-5) ⇒ (k-1)² = k²-5 ⇒ k²-2k+1 = k²-5 ⇒ -2k = -6 ⇒ k = 3.

A. 9/2

Correct Answer: C

Q13. Physicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
A block of mass m is attached to two identical springs of spring constant k each, arranged in series. The block is displaced slightly and released. The time period of oscillation is T. If the same springs are rearranged in parallel with the same mass attached, the new time period of oscillation becomes:
A. T/2
B. T/√2
C. T√2
D. 2T
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For two identical springs, the effective spring constant in series is k/2 and in parallel is 2k. Since time period T ∝ 1/√k, the ratio T_parallel/T_series = √(k_series/k_parallel) = √((k/2)/(2k)) = √(1/4) = 1/2. Hence T_parallel = T/2. Full solution: Series combination gives 1/k_eq = 1/k + 1/k = 2/k ⇒ k_eq = k/2. Then T = 2π√(m/(k/2)) = 2π√(2m/k). Parallel combination gives k_eq = k + k = 2k. Then T' = 2π√(m/(2k)) = 2π√(m/(2k)) = (1/2)·2π√(2m/k) = T/2.
Q14. Physicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
The threshold wavelength for photoelectric emission from a metal surface is λ₀. Light of wavelength λ₀/2 is incident on the surface. The maximum kinetic energy of the emitted photoelectrons is: (Take work function = φ)
A. φ
B. 2φ
C. φ/2
D. 3φ/2
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Work function φ = hc/λ₀. Incident photon energy E = hc/(λ₀/2) = 2hc/λ₀ = 2φ. By Einstein's photoelectric equation: K_max = E - φ = 2φ - φ = φ. Full Solution: Threshold wavelength λ₀ implies hc/λ₀ = φ. Incident wavelength λ = λ₀/2. Energy of incident photon = hc/λ = hc/(λ₀/2) = 2hc/λ₀ = 2φ. Maximum kinetic energy K_max = Photon Energy - Work Function = 2φ - φ = φ.
