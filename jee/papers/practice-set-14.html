Q1. Physicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
One mole of an ideal monoatomic gas undergoes a reversible adiabatic expansion such that its volume becomes 8 times the initial volume. The ratio of final temperature to initial temperature (T₂/T₁) is:
A. 1/2
B. 1/4
C. 1/8
D. 1/16
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a reversible adiabatic process, TV^(γ-1) = constant. For a monoatomic gas, γ = 5/3, so γ-1 = 2/3. Since V₂ = 8V₁, (V₂/V₁)^(2/3) = 8^(2/3) = (2³)^(2/3) = 2² = 4. Thus T₁/T₂ = 4, giving T₂/T₁ = 1/4. Full Solution: Using the adiabatic relation T₁V₁^(γ-1) = T₂V₂^(γ-1). Substitute γ = Cp/Cv = 5/3 for monoatomic gas. T₂/T₁ = (V₁/V₂)^(γ-1) = (1/8)^(2/3) = 1/4.
Q2. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
Let \(A\) be a \(3 \times 3\) matrix with \(\det(A) = 2\). Find \(\det(\operatorname{adj}(\operatorname{adj}(A)))\).
A. 4
B. 16
C. 8
D. 32
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For any \(n \times n\) matrix, \(\det(\operatorname{adj} A) = (\det A)^{n-1}\). For \(n=3\), \(\det(\operatorname{adj} A) = (\det A)^2 = 4\). Applying the property again, \(\det(\operatorname{adj}(\operatorname{adj} A)) = (\det(\operatorname{adj} A))^2 = 4^2 = 16\). Full solution: Given \(\det A = 2\). Since \(\operatorname{adj} A = (\det A) A^{-1}\), \(\det(\operatorname{adj} A) = (\det A)^{3-1} = 2^2 = 4\). Then \(\operatorname{adj}(\operatorname{adj} A) = \det(\operatorname{adj} A) (\operatorname{adj} A)^{-1}\), so its determinant is \((\det(\operatorname{adj} A))^{3-1} = 4^2 = 16\).

What is the maximum number of electrons that can be accommodated in a subshell with principal quantum number n = 4 and azimuthal quantum number l = 2?
A. 2
B. 6
C. 10
D. 14
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For a given azimuthal quantum number (l), maximum electrons = 2(2l+1). Here l=2 (d-subshell), so 2(5)=10. Full Solution: The quantum numbers n=4, l=2 define the 4d subshell. Q7. Chemistrymodel-generated · 2026-11-16
18 g of glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) is dissolved in 178.2 g of water. The vapour pressure of pure water at 100°C is 760 mm Hg. The vapour pressure of the solution is approximately: (Molar mass: glucose = 180 g mol⁻¹, water = 18 g mol⁻¹)
A. 759.6 mm Hg
B. 752.4 mm Hg
C. 760.4 mm Hg
D. 748.0 mm Hg
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For dilute solutions, relative lowering of vapour pressure (ΔP/P°) ≈ moles of solute / moles of solvent. Moles glucose = 18/180 = 0.1; moles water = 178.2/18 = 9.9. Ratio ≈ 0.0101. ΔP ≈ 760 × 0.0101 ≈ 7.68 mm Hg. P_solution ≈ 760 - 7.68 = 752.3 mm Hg. Option B is closest. Full Solution: Mole fraction of water = (9.9) / (9.9 + 0.1) = 0.99. Applying Raoult's law, P_solution = 0.99 × 760 = 752.4 mm Hg.
Q8. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
If the circles x² + y² + 2x + 2ky + 6 = 0 and x² + y² + 2kx + 2y + 6 = 0 cut orthogonally, then the value of k is:
A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Orthogonality condition for circles x²+y²+2g₁x+2f₁y+c₁=0 and x²+y²+2g₂x+2f₂y+c₂=0 is 2g₁g₂ + 2f₁f₂ = c₁ + c₂. Here g₁=1, f₁=k, c₁=6; g₂=k, f₂=1, c₂=6. Substitute: 2(1)(k) + 2(k)(1) = 6+6 ⇒ 4k = 12 ⇒ k = 3. Step-by-step: Circle 1: centre (-1, -k), radius² = 1+k²-6 = k²-5. Circle 2: centre (-k, -1), radius² = k²+1-6 = k²-5. Distance between centres² = (k-1)² + (1-k)² = 2(k-1)². For orthogonal intersection, d² = r₁² + r₂² ⇒ 2(k-1)² = 2(k²-5) ⇒ (k-1)² = k²-5 ⇒ k²-2k+1 = k²-5 ⇒ -2k = -6 ⇒ k = 3.
Q9. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-16
Evaluate the limit: \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\tan 3x - \sin 3x}{x^3}\).
A. 9/2
B. 9
C. 27/2
D. 27
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use the standard limit \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\tan x - \sin x}{x^3} = \frac{1}{2}\). Substitute \(x \to 3x\) to get \(\frac{\tan 3x - \sin 3x}{(3x)^3} = \frac{1}{2}\), hence the required limit is \(\frac{27}{2}\). Full solution: Expand \(\tan 3x = 3x + 9x^3 + \cdots\) and \(\sin 3x = 3x - \frac{9}{2}x^3 + \cdots\) using Maclaurin series; their difference is \(\frac{27}{2}x^3 + \cdots\). Dividing by \(x^3\) and taking the limit gives \(\frac{27}{2}\).

Q13. Physics
A convex lens has focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens should an object be placed to get a real image twice the size of object?
A. 40 cm
B. 30 cm
C. 20 cm
D. 60 cm
Correct Answer: B
Magnification m = -2 (real image). m = v/u = -2 ⇒ v = -2u. Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u. 1/20 = 1/(-2u) - 1/u = -3/(2u). u = -30 cm. So object distance = 30 cm.
Q14. Physics
The escape velocity of a body from Earth is 11.2 km/s. What is the escape velocity from a planet having mass 4 times and radius twice that of Earth?
A. 22.4 km/s
B. 5.6 km/s
C. 15.8 km/s
D. 11.2 km/s
Correct Answer: C
v_e = v(2GM/R). For new planet: v_e' = v(2G·4M/2R) = v(2·2GM/R) = v2·v(2GM/R) = v2 × 11.2 = 15.84 km/s.
