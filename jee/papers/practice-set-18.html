Q1. Physics
In a photoelectric effect experiment, the stopping potential V_s is plotted against the frequency f of incident light. The slope of the straight line obtained gives:
A. Work function (φ)
B. Planck's constant (h)
C. Ratio h/e
D. Ratio e/h
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Einstein's equation eV_s = hf - φ ⇒ V_s = (h/e)f - φ/e. This is a straight line y = mx + c where slope m = h/e. Full Solution: The graph of stopping potential vs frequency is linear. Comparing V_s = (h/e)f - φ/e with y = mx + c, the slope is h/e. The intercept on the frequency axis gives threshold frequency f0 = φ/h, and the intercept on the V_s axis gives -φ/e.
Q2. Chemistry
The spin-only magnetic moment (in Bohr Magneton) of the complex ion [Fe(H₂O)₆]²⁺ is closest to:
A. 0 (Diamagnetic)
B. 2.84 BM
C. 4.90 BM
D. 5.92 BM
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Fe²⁺ is d⁶. H₂O is a weak field ligand → high spin configuration (t₂g⁴ eg²) → 4 unpaired electrons. Use μ = √[n(n+2)] = √[4×6] = √24 ≈ 4.90 BM. Full Solution: Electronic configuration of Fe²⁺ is [Ar] 3d⁶. In an octahedral field with weak field ligand H₂O, pairing energy > CFSE, so high spin: t₂g⁴ eg². Number of unpaired electrons n = 4. Spin-only formula μ = √[n(n+2)] BM = √[4(6)] = √24 = 4.899 ≈ 4.90 BM.
Q3. Physics
A particle is moving with velocity v = 2t² + 3t. Find its acceleration at t = 2s.
A. 11 m/s²
B. 10 m/s²
C. 8 m/s²
D. 14 m/s²
Correct Answer: A
a = dv/dt = d(2t² + 3t)/dt = 4t + 3. At t = 2: a = 4(2) + 3 = 11 m/s².
Q4. Chemistry
Which of the following compounds will NOT give a positive iodoform test (yellow precipitate of CHI₃) with I₂/NaOH?
A. Ethanol (CH₃CH₂OH)
B. Acetaldehyde (CH₃CHO)
C. Acetone (CH₃COCH₃)
D. Benzaldehyde (C₆H₅CHO)
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Iodoform test is given by compounds containing CH₃CO– (methyl ketone) or CH₃CH(OH)– (secondary alcohol with methyl adjacent to carbinol carbon) which oxidize to methyl ketones. Ethanol → CH₃CHO (gives test). Acetaldehyde → CH₃CHO (gives test). Acetone → CH₃COCH₃ (gives test). Benzaldehyde (PhCHO) lacks CH₃CO– group → No test. Full Solution: The iodoform reaction requires a CH₃C=O or CH₃CH(OH) group. Ethanol oxidizes to acetaldehyde (CH₃CHO) which has CH₃CO–. Acetaldehyde has CH₃CO–. Acetone is CH₃COCH₃. Benzaldehyde is C₆H₅CHO; the carbonyl carbon is attached to H and Ph, not CH₃. Hence, it does not respond.
Q5. Chemistry
Which of the following molecules has a net dipole moment of zero?
A. NH₃
B. BF₃
C. H₂O
D. SO₂
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Check molecular geometry symmetry. BF₃ is trigonal planar (sp², 120°) with three identical B–F bonds → vector sum = 0. NH₃ (pyramidal), H₂O (bent), SO₂ (bent) are asymmetrical → net μ > 0. Full Solution: Dipole moment is a vector quantity. BF₃ has trigonal planar geometry (D₃h symmetry); the three B–F bond dipoles are at 120° and cancel each other resulting in μ = 0. NH₃ (C₃v, pyramidal), H₂O (C₂v, bent), and SO₂ (C₂v, bent) have resultant dipole moments due to their asymmetric shapes and lone pairs.
Q6. Mathematics
Using the first two terms of the binomial expansion, the approximate value of (1.01)^5 is:
A. 1.05
B. 1.051
C. 1.0505
D. 1.052
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For small x, (1+x)^n ≈ 1 + nx. Here x=0.01, n=5, so approximation = 1 + 5×0.01 = 1.05. Full solution: Binomial expansion (1+x)^5 = 1 + 5x + 10x^2 + 10x^3 + 5x^4 + x^5. With x=0.01, the first two terms give 1 + 0.05 = 1.05. The next term 10x^2 = 10×0.0001 = 0.001, so actual value ≈ 1.051. The question asks for the approximation using only the first two terms, which is 1.05.

Q8. Chemistry
For a reaction at 298 K, ΔH = +50 kJ mol⁻¹ and ΔS = +100 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹. The minimum temperature (in K) above which the reaction becomes spontaneous is:
A. 300 K
B. 400 K
C. 500 K
D. 600 K
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Spontaneity requires ΔG < 0. At equilibrium ΔG = 0 = ΔH - TΔS → T = ΔH/ΔS. Convert ΔH to J: 50,000 J mol⁻¹. T = 50,000 / 100 = 500 K. Reaction spontaneous for T > 500 K. Full Solution: Gibbs free energy ΔG = ΔH - TΔS. For spontaneity, ΔG < 0 ⇒ ΔH - TΔS < 0 ⇒ T > ΔH/ΔS. Given ΔH = +50 kJ mol⁻¹ = 50,000 J mol⁻¹ and ΔS = +100 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹. T = 50,000 J mol⁻¹ / 100 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ = 500 K. Thus, above 500 K the reaction is spontaneous.
Q9. Physics
A convex lens has focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens should an object be placed to get a real image twice the size of object?
A. 40 cm
B. 30 cm
C. 20 cm
D. 60 cm
Correct Answer: B
Magnification m = -2 (real image). m = v/u = -2 ⇒ v = -2u. Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u. 1/20 = 1/(-2u) - 1/u = -3/(2u). u = -30 cm. So object distance = 30 cm.
Q10. Physics
In a potentiometer experiment, a cell of emf 1.5 V gives a balancing length of 60 cm. If this cell is replaced by another cell of emf 2.0 V, the new balancing length (in cm) will be:
A. 45
B. 80
C. 90
D. 75
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Balancing length L is directly proportional to emf E (L ∝ E) for a constant potential gradient. L2 = L1 × (E2/E1) = 60 × (2.0/1.5) = 60 × (4/3) = 80 cm. Full Solution: Potential gradient k = E1/L1 = 1.5/60 = 0.025 V/cm. For the second cell, E2 = k × L2 ⇒ L2 = E2/k = 2.0 / 0.025 = 80 cm.
Q11. Mathematics
What is the unit digit of 7^{2023}?
A. 7
B. 9
C. 3
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: The unit digits of powers of 7 repeat every 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. Since 2023 mod 4 = 3, the unit digit is the third in the cycle, which is 3. Full solution: 7^1=7, 7^2=49→9, 7^3=343→3, 7^4=2401→1. The cycle length is 4. 2023 = 4×505 + 3, so 7^{2023} has the same unit digit as 7^3 = 3.
Q12. Physics
The escape velocity of a body from Earth is 11.2 km/s. What is the escape velocity from a planet having mass 4 times and radius twice that of Earth?
A. 22.4 km/s
B. 5.6 km/s
C. 15.8 km/s
D. 11.2 km/s
Correct Answer: C
v_e = v(2GM/R). For new planet: v_e' = v(2G·4M/2R) = v(2·2GM/R) = v2·v(2GM/R) = v2 × 11.2 = 15.84 km/s.
Q13. Mathematics
The sum of the infinite series \( 1 + \frac{4}{5} + \frac{7}{5^2} + \frac{10}{5^3} + \dots \) is:
A. 35/16
B. 33/16
C. 37/16
D. 39/16
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Recognize as an Arithmetico-Geometric Progression (AGP) with first term \(a=1\), common difference \(d=3\), and common ratio \(r=1/5\). Use the direct formula \(S_\infty = \frac{a}{1-r} + \frac{dr}{(1-r)^2} = \frac{1}{4/5} + \frac{3(1/5)}{(4/5)^2} = \frac{5}{4} + \frac{15}{16} = \frac{35}{16}\). Full Solution: Let \(S = 1 + \frac{4}{5} + \frac{7}{25} + \frac{10}{125} + \dots\). Multiply by \(r=1/5\): \(\frac{S}{5} = \frac{1}{5} + \frac{4}{25} + \frac{7}{125} + \dots\). Subtract: \(S - \frac{S}{5} = 1 + \frac{3}{5} + \frac{3}{25} + \frac{3}{125} + \dots = 1 + \frac{3/5}{1-1/5} = 1 + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{7}{4}\). Thus \(\frac{4S}{5} = \frac{7}{4} \Rightarrow S = \frac{35}{16}\).
Q14. Chemistry
An electron is accelerated from rest through a potential difference of 150 V. The de Broglie wavelength associated with the electron is approximately: (Use h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s, mₑ = 9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kg, e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)
A. 1.00 Å
B. 1.23 Å
C. 0.85 Å
D. 1.50 Å
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Use the direct formula λ(Å) = 12.27/√V. For V = 150 V, λ = 12.27/√150 ≈ 12.27/12.25 ≈ 1.00 Å. Full Solution: Kinetic energy E = eV = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ × 150 = 2.4×10⁻¹⁷ J. Momentum p = √(2mE) = √(2 × 9.1×10⁻³¹ × 2.4×10⁻¹⁷) ≈ 6.62×10⁻²⁴ kg m/s. λ = h/p = 6.63×10⁻³⁴ / 6.62×10⁻²⁴ ≈ 1.00×10⁻¹⁰ m = 1.00 Å.
Q15. Mathematics
The number of distinct real roots of the cubic equation x^3 - 3x + 1 = 0 is:
A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 0
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use calculus. f(x)=x^3-3x+1, f'(x)=3x^2-3=3(x-1)(x+1). Critical points at x=±1. f(-1)=3>0, f(1)=-1<0. Since the cubic goes from -∞ to +∞ and has a local maximum positive and local minimum negative, it crosses the x-axis three times. Hence three distinct real roots. Full solution: The discriminant Δ = -4(-3)^3 - 27(1)^2 = 108 - 27 = 81 > 0, confirming three distinct real roots.
