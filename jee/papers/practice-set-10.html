Q7. Physics
An electron in the ground state of a hydrogen atom has an energy of -13.6 eV. What is the energy of the electron in the first excited state?
A. -3.4 eV
B. -6.8 eV
C. -10.2 eV
D. -13.6 eV
Correct Answer: A
The energy of the electron in the nth state of a hydrogen atom is given by En = -13.6 eV / n^2. For the first excited state, n = 2. So, E2 = -13.6 eV / 2^2 = -13.6 eV / 4 = -3.4 eV.
Q8. Chemistry
What is the molecular formula of the compound with the empirical formula CH2O and a molecular weight of 180 g/mol?
A. C6H12O6
B. C3H6O3
C. C2H4O2
D. C4H8O4
Correct Answer: A
The empirical formula of the compound is CH2O, and its molecular weight is 180 g/mol. The empirical formula weight is 30 g/mol (12 for C, 2 for H, and 16 for O). To find the molecular formula, we divide the molecular weight by the empirical formula weight: 180/30 = 6. Therefore, the molecular formula is (CH2O)6, which is C6H12O6.
Q9. Physics
A wire of resistance R is stretched to double its length. New resistance will be:
A. R/2
B. R
C. 4R
D. 2R
Correct Answer: C
When length is doubled (L' = 2L), cross-sectional area halves (A' = A/2). R' = L'/A' = (2L)/(A/2) = 4L/A = 4R.

Q12. Mathematics
If the function f(x) = x^3 - 2x^2 - 5x + 1 is given, what is the value of f(-1)?
A. 1
B. 3
C. 5
D. 7
Correct Answer: D
To find the value of f(-1), we substitute x = -1 into the function: f(-1) = (-1)^3 - 2(-1)^2 - 5(-1) + 1 = -1 - 2 + 5 + 1 = 3.

Q15. Physics
A thermodynamic system undergoes a process where the internal energy change is 100 J and the heat added to the system is 200 J. What is the work done by the system?
A. -100 J
B. 100 J
C. 200 J
D. 300 J
Correct Answer: A
The first law of thermodynamics states that ΔU = Q - W, where ΔU is the internal energy change, Q is the heat added, and W is the work done. Rearranging the equation to solve for W, we get W = Q - ΔU = 200 J - 100 J = 100 J. However, the work done 'by' the system is in the opposite direction, so the work done by the system is -100 J.
