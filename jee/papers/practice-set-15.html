\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1 - x - \frac{x^2}{2}}{x^3}

B. \frac{1}{3}
C. \frac{1}{2}

Correct Answer: A

An electric dipole of moment 2 × 10⁻²⁹ C·m is placed in vacuum. The magnitude of the electric field at a point on the axial line at a distance 0.1 m from the centre of the dipole is:
A. 1.8 × 10⁻¹⁶ N/C
B. 7.2 × 10⁻¹⁶ N/C
C. 3.6 × 10⁻¹⁶ N/C
D. 9.0 × 10⁻¹⁶ N/C
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: On the axial line, E = (1/4πϵ₀)(2p/r³). 1/4πϵ₀ ≈ 9×10⁹, 2p = 4×10⁻²⁹, r³ = (0.1)³ = 10⁻³. E ≈ 9×10⁹ × 4×10⁻²⁹ / 10⁻³ = 3.6×10⁻¹⁶ N/C. Full solution: Using the formula for the electric field of a dipole on its axis, E = (1/4πϵ₀) * (2p/r³). Substituting p = 2×10⁻²⁹ C·m, r = 0.1 m, and 1/4πϵ₀ = 9×10⁹ N·m²/C² gives E = 9×10⁹ × (2×2×10⁻²⁹) / (0.1)³ = 9×10⁹ × 4×10⁻²⁹ / 10⁻³ = 3.6×10⁻¹⁶ N/C.

If ω is a non-real cube root of unity, then the value of (1 - ω + ω²)(1 + ω - ω²) is:
A. -4
B. 4

Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use 1 + ω + ω² = 0 ⇒ 1 + ω² = -ω and 1 + ω = -ω². The expression becomes (-ω - ω)(-ω² - ω²) = (-2ω)(-2ω²) = 4ω³ = 4. Full: Substitute ω³ = 1 and 1 + ω + ω² = 0 to simplify each bracket to -2ω and -2ω² respectively; their product is 4ω³ = 4.

B. O₂⁺

Correct Answer: B

Q5. Physicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-17
The molar mass of gas A is 4 g/mol and that of gas B is 64 g/mol. Both gases are maintained at the same temperature. The ratio of the root mean square speed of gas A to that of gas B is:
A. 1:4
B. 2:1
C. 4:1
D. 1:2
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: RMS speed is inversely proportional to the square root of molar mass (v_rms ∝ 1/√M). Ratio v_A:v_B = √(M_B/M_A) = √(64/4) = √16 = 4. Hence, ratio is 4:1. Full Solution: The formula for RMS speed is v_rms = √(3RT/M). Since R and T are constant for both gases, v_A/v_B = √(M_B/M_A). Substituting M_A = 4 g/mol and M_B = 64 g/mol gives v_A/v_B = √(64/4) = √16 = 4. Therefore, the required ratio is 4:1.

C. 1000
D. 1
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: At 300 K, 2.303RT ≈ 2.303 × 8.314 × 300 ≈ 5740 J/mol = 5.74 kJ/mol. Use ΔG° = -2.303RT log K. Substituting -5.74 = -5.74 log K ⇒ log K = 1 ⇒ K = 10. Step-by-step: Convert ΔG° to J/mol (-5740 J/mol). Use formula ΔG° = -RT ln K = -2.303RT log₁₀ K. Calculate 2.303 × 8.314 × 300 = 5740 J/mol. Solve -5740 = -5740 log K → log K = 1 → K = 10¹ = 10.

B. Square Planar
C. See-saw
D. Trigonal Bipyramidal
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Xe has 8 valence electrons. 4 bonds to F use 4 electrons. Remaining 4 electrons = 2 lone pairs. Steric Number = 4 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs = 6 (sp³d² hybridization). Lone pairs occupy axial positions to minimize lp-lp repulsion. Resulting shape: Square Planar.

A bag contains 4 red and 6 black balls. Two balls are drawn at random without replacement. Given that the second ball drawn is red, the probability that the first ball was also red is:
A. 3/10
B. 3/5
C. 1/3
D. 2/5
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: P(R₁|R₂) = P(R₁∩R₂) / P(R₂). By symmetry, P(R₂) = 4/10 = 2/5. P(R₁∩R₂) = (4/10)(3/9) = 2/15. Required probability = (2/15) / (2/5) = 1/3. Full: Use Bayes' theorem or conditional probability definition. Compute joint probability of both red and marginal probability of second red (using law of total probability), then divide.

Correct Answer: A

The Crystal Field Stabilization Energy (CFSE) for a low-spin d⁶ octahedral complex in terms of Δo is:
A. -0.4 Δo
B. -1.2 Δo
C. -2.4 Δo
D. -0.6 Δo
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Low-spin d⁶ configuration is t2g⁶ eg⁰. CFSE = [(-0.4 × n_t2g) + (0.6 × n_eg)] Δo = (-0.4 × 6) Δo = -2.4 Δo. Step-by-step: In an octahedral field, d-orbitals split into t2g (lower, -0.4 Δo each) and eg (higher, +0.6 Δo each). For low-spin d⁶, electrons fill t2g completely before occupying eg (strong field ligand). All 6 electrons occupy t2g orbitals. CFSE = 6 × (-0.4 Δo) + 0 × (0.6 Δo) = -2.4 Δo.
Q12. Chemistrymodel-generated · 2026-11-17
Which of the following compounds is the weakest Brønsted acid?
A. Acetic acid (CH₃COOH)
B. Phenol (C₆H₅OH)
C. Water (H₂O)
D. Ethanol (C₂H₅OH)
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Acidic strength order: Carboxylic acid > Phenol > Water > Alcohol. Stability of conjugate base: Carboxylate (resonance, 2 equiv. structures) > Phenoxide (resonance, charge on O/C) > Hydroxide (small size, high charge density) > Alkoxide (destabilized by +I effect of alkyl group). Weakest acid has least stable conjugate base → Ethanol. Step-by-step: Compare conjugate bases: CH₃COO⁻ (resonance stabilized), C₆H₅O⁻ (resonance stabilized but less than carboxylate), OH⁻ (no resonance, high charge density), C₂H₅O⁻ (electron-donating ethyl group increases electron density on O, destabilizing anion). Least stable anion = strongest base = weakest acid = Ethanol.

Q14. Physicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-17
A series LCR circuit is connected to an AC source. At resonance, the voltage across the inductor is 80 V and the voltage across the capacitor is 80 V. The resistance of the circuit is 10 Ω and the current flowing at resonance is 2 A. The RMS voltage of the AC source is:
A. 20 V
B. 80 V
C. 100 V
D. 160 V
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: At resonance, inductive reactance equals capacitive reactance (X_L = X_C). The voltages across L and C are equal in magnitude but 180° out of phase, cancelling each other. The circuit behaves as purely resistive. Source voltage V_S = V_R = I × R = 2 A × 10 Ω = 20 V. Full Solution: At resonance, impedance Z = R. The voltage across the resistor is V_R = I R = 2 × 10 = 20 V. The voltages across the inductor and capacitor are V_L = I X_L = 80 V and V_C = I X_C = 80 V. Since V_L and V_C are in phase opposition, their phasor sum is zero. The source voltage is the phasor sum of V_R, V_L, and V_C: V_S = √[V_R² + (V_L - V_C)²] = √[20² + 0²] = 20 V.
Q15. Physicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-17
A diffraction grating has 5000 lines per centimetre. Light of wavelength 600 nm is incident normally. The angle of the first-order principal maximum is approximately:
A. 17.5°
B. 30°
C. 45°
D. 60°
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Grating spacing d = 1/(5000 cm⁻¹) = 2×10⁻⁶ m. For first order, sinθ = λ/d = 600×10⁻⁹ / 2×10⁻⁶ = 0.3 → θ ≈ 17.5°. Full solution: Using the grating equation d sinθ = mλ with m=1, d = 1/(5×10⁵ m⁻¹) = 2×10⁻⁶ m, λ = 600×10⁻⁹ m. sinθ = (1×600×10⁻⁹)/(2×10⁻⁶) = 0.3, giving θ = sin⁻¹(0.3) ≈ 17.5°.
