Q1. Physics
The speed of sound is maximum in:
A. Air
B. Water
C. Steel
D. Vacuum
Correct Answer: C
Sound travels fastest in solids (steel ~5000 m/s) due to close molecular arrangement.

A. 2.48 x 10 J
B. 3.98 x 10 J
C. 1.99 x 10 J
D. 4.97 x 10 J
Correct Answer: B
E = hc/ = (6.63x10 x 3x10)/(500x10) = 3.98x10 J.
Q3. Mathematics
The value of x^2 dx is:
A. 0
B. 1/2
C. 1/3
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
x^2 dx = [x/3] = 1/3 - 0 = 1/3.

Q5. Chemistry
How many moles of O are required for complete combustion of 1 mole of CH?
A. 4
B. 5

CH + 6.5O -> 4CO + 5HO. So 6.5 moles of O are needed.

Q8. Physics
A capacitor of 10 F is charged to 100 V. The energy stored is:
A. 0.05 J
B. 0.05 J
C. 0.5 J
D. 5 J
Correct Answer: B
E = 1/2CV^2 = 1/2 x 10x10 x 100^2 = 0.05 J.

Q10. Physics
A particle is moving with velocity v = 2t^2 + 3t. Find its acceleration at t = 2s.
A. 11 m/s^2
B. 10 m/s^2
C. 8 m/s^2
D. 14 m/s^2
Correct Answer: A
a = dv/dt = d(2t^2 + 3t)/dt = 4t + 3. At t = 2: a = 4(2) + 3 = 11 m/s^2.

Q13. Mathematics
The value of 5! is:
A. 60
B. 100
C. 120
D. 24
Correct Answer: C
5! = 5x4x3x2x1 = 120.
Q14. Mathematics
The equation of line with slope 2 passing through (1, 3) is:
A. y = 2x + 1
B. y = 2x + 1
C. y = 2x - 1
D. y = x + 2
Correct Answer: B
Point-slope form: y - 3 = 2(x - 1) -> y = 2x + 1.
Q15. Mathematics
Find the derivative of f(x) = sin(x^2).
A. cos(x^2)
B. 2x cos(x)
C. 2x cos(x^2)
D. x^2 cos(x^2)
Correct Answer: C
Using chain rule: d/dx[sin(x^2)] = cos(x^2) . d/dx(x^2) = 2x cos(x^2).
