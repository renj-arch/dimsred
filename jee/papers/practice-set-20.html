If A is a non-singular square matrix of order 3 such that
A. 125
B. 625
C. 25
D. 3125
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a non-singular matrix A of order n, |adj(adj A)| = |A|^((n-1)^2). Here n=3, |A|=5, so value = 5^4 = 625. Full: We know adj(adj A) = |A|^(n-2) * A. The half-life of a first order reaction is 20 minutes. The time required for 99% completion of the reaction is approximately:
A. 100 min
B. 133 min
C. 150 min
D. 200 min
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For a first order reaction, t_99% ≈ 6.64 × t_1/2 (since ln(100)/ln(2) ≈ 4.605/0.693 = 6.64). 20 min × 6.64 ≈ 133 min. Full solution: k = ln2 / t_1/2 = 0.693/20 min⁻¹. For 99% completion, fraction remaining = 0.01, so t = (1/k) ln(1/0.01) = (20/0.693) × 4.605 ≈ 132.8 min ≈ 133 min.

The locus of the point of intersection of perpendicular tangents to the parabola y² = 8x is:
A. x = 2
B. y = 2
C. x = -2
D. y = -2
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For the parabola y² = 4ax, the locus of intersection of perpendicular tangents (Director Circle) is the directrix x = -a. Here 4a = 8 ⇒ a = 2, so the locus is x = -2. Full Solution: Equation of tangent to y² = 8x is y = mx + 2/m. If tangents from P(h, k) are perpendicular, m₁m₂ = -1. The combined equation of pair of tangents from (h, k) gives the condition h = -2. For a diatomic ideal gas (γ = 7/5), what is the ratio of adiabatic bulk modulus to isothermal bulk modulus?
A. 1.0
B. 1.2
C. 1.4
D. 1.6
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Isothermal bulk modulus B_iso = P. Adiabatic bulk modulus B_ad = γP. Ratio = γ = Cp/Cv = 7/5 = 1.4. Full: Bulk modulus B = -V(dP/dV). For isothermal process PV=constant => dP/P = -dV/V => B_iso = P. For adiabatic process PV^γ=constant => dP/P = -γ dV/V => B_ad = γP. Ratio B_ad/B_iso = γ = 1.4.

A solid sphere, a solid cylinder, and a thin ring, each of mass M and radius R, are released from rest at the top of a rough inclined plane of inclination θ. They roll without slipping. Which object reaches the bottom first?
A. Solid sphere
B. Solid cylinder
C. Thin ring
D. All reach simultaneously
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Acceleration down an incline for rolling is a = g sinθ / (1 + k²/R²), where k is radius of gyration. The object with smallest k²/R² (sphere: 0.4) has maximum acceleration. Full: For rolling without slipping, mg sinθ - f = ma and fR = Iα = I(a/R). Solving gives a = g sinθ / (1 + I/mR²). Moments of inertia: Sphere = 2/5 mR², Cylinder = 1/2 mR², Ring = mR². Denominators are 1.4, 1.5, 2.0 respectively. Sphere has highest acceleration and reaches first.

A. 2x + 3y = 18
B. 3x + 2y = 9
C. 2x - 3y = 9
D. 2x + 3y = 9
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: The polar of (x₁, y₁) w.r.t. circle x²+y²=a² is T=0 i.e., xx₁+yy₁=a². For (2,3) and a²=9: 2x+3y=9. Full: The given circle is x²+y²=9. The equation of the polar (chord of contact) of an external point (x₁, y₁) is obtained by replacing x²→xx₁, y²→yy₁ in the circle equation (T=0 form). Thus, x(2) + y(3) = 9 ⇒ 2x + 3y = 9.

A satellite is orbiting very close to the Earth's surface (height h << R). What is the ratio of the escape velocity (v_e) from the Earth's surface to the orbital velocity (v_o) of this satellite?
A. 1
B. √2
C. 2
D. 1/√2
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Escape velocity v_e = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR). Orbital velocity near surface v_o = √(GM/R) = √(gR). Ratio v_e/v_o = √2. Full: Escape velocity is derived from energy conservation: 1/2 mv_e² = GMm/R. Orbital velocity from centripetal force: mv_o²/R = GMm/R². Dividing the two equations gives v_e²/v_o² = 2, so v_e/v_o = √2.
