Q2. Chemistry
Butanamide (CH₃CH₂CH₂CONH₂) is treated with Br₂ and NaOH (Hofmann Bromamide reaction). The major organic product formed is:
A. Butanamine (CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂NH₂)
B. Propanamine (CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂)
C. Propanoic acid (CH₃CH₂COOH)
D. Butanoic acid (CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH)
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Hofmann rearrangement degrades amide to amine with ONE LESS carbon (loss of carbonyl carbon as CO₂). Butanamide (C4) → Propanamine (C3). Full Solution: R-CONH₂ + Br₂ + 4NaOH → R-NH₂ + Na₂CO₃ + 2NaBr + 2H₂O. The alkyl group R migrates to nitrogen. For Butanamide, R = CH₃CH₂CH₂⁻ (propyl). Product is CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂ (Propanamine).
Q3. Physics
A point charge q is placed at one corner of a cube of side a. The electric flux through each of the three faces meeting at that corner is zero. What is the electric flux through each of the remaining three faces of the cube?
A. q/(24ε₀)
B. q/(8ε₀)
C. q/(6ε₀)
D. q/(3ε₀)
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Enclose the charge symmetrically by imagining 8 such cubes forming a larger cube with the charge at the centre. Total flux = q/ε₀. Flux through one small cube = q/(8ε₀). The 3 faces touching the charge have zero flux (field lines parallel). The remaining flux q/(8ε₀) is shared equally by the 3 opposite faces. Flux per face = q/(24ε₀). Full Solution: By Gauss's law, total flux from charge q is q/ε₀. Placing the charge at the corner of a cube means it is shared by 8 identical cubes meeting at that corner. Flux entering the given cube = q/(8ε₀). The electric field is radial; hence it is parallel to the three faces meeting at the corner, giving zero flux through them. The entire flux q/(8ε₀) must exit through the three opposite faces. By symmetry, each of these three faces gets one-third: (1/3) × q/(8ε₀) = q/(24ε₀).

Q5. Physics
Two long straight parallel wires separated by a distance d carry currents I and 2I respectively in the same direction. The magnetic force per unit length experienced by the wire carrying current I is F. If the current in the first wire is doubled and the separation between the wires is halved, the new force per unit length on the first wire becomes:
A. 2F
B. 4F
C. 8F
D. 16F
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Force per unit length f ∝ (I₁ I₂)/d. Initially f = F ∝ (I × 2I)/d = 2I²/d. New: I₁' = 2I, I₂' = 2I (unchanged), d' = d/2. New force f' ∝ (2I × 2I)/(d/2) = 4I² × (2/d) = 8I²/d. Ratio f'/F = (8I²/d) / (2I²/d) = 4. Answer is 4F. Full Solution: The force per unit length between two parallel currents is given by f = (μ₀/2π) (I₁ I₂/d). Initial force F = (μ₀/2π) (I × 2I)/d = (μ₀/2π) (2I²/d). After changes: I₁ = 2I, I₂ = 2I, d = d/2. New force F' = (μ₀/2π) (2I × 2I)/(d/2) = (μ₀/2π) (4I² × 2/d) = (μ₀/2π) (8I²/d) = 4 × [(μ₀/2π) (2I²/d)] = 4F.
Q6. Mathematics
Evaluate the determinant \[\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 4 & k \\ 3 & k & 9 \end{vmatrix}=0\] Find the value of \(k\).
A. 6
B. 9
C. 12
D. 15
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Notice rows are proportional when k=6 because second row =2×first row except last entry: 2×3=6, so set k=6 makes determinant zero instantly. Full solution: Expand determinant or use row operations: R2→R2-2R1 gives (0,0,k-6). R3→R3-3R1 gives (0,k-6,0). Determinant = (k-6)·(k-6)·1 = (k-6)^2. Setting to zero gives k=6.

Q8. Mathematics
Evaluate the definite integral \( \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{\sin^3 x}{\sin^3 x + \cos^3 x} \, dx \).
A. \frac{\pi}{2}
B. \frac{\pi}{4}
C. \frac{\pi}{6}
D. \frac{\pi}{3}
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Apply King's Property \( I = \int_a^b f(x) dx = \int_a^b f(a+b-x) dx \). Replacing \( x \) by \( \pi/2 - x \) converts the integrand to \( \cos^3 x/(\cos^3 x + \sin^3 x) \). Adding the original and transformed integrals gives \( 2I = \int_0^{\pi/2} 1 \, dx = \pi/2 \), so \( I = \pi/4 \). Full: Let \( I = \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{\sin^3 x}{\sin^3 x + \cos^3 x} dx \). Using \( x \to \pi/2 - x \), \( I = \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{\cos^3 x}{\cos^3 x + \sin^3 x} dx \). Adding: \( 2I = \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{\sin^3 x + \cos^3 x}{\sin^3 x + \cos^3 x} dx = \int_0^{\pi/2} dx = \frac{\pi}{2} \). Thus \( I = \frac{\pi}{4} \).
Q9. Mathematics
A fair coin is tossed 5 times. Given that at least 3 heads have appeared, the probability that exactly 4 heads appear is:
A. \frac{5}{32}
B. \frac{1}{4}
C. \frac{5}{16}
D. \frac{1}{2}
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use conditional probability \( P(A|B) = P(A)/P(B) \) since \( A \subset B \). \( P(\text{exactly 4 heads}) = {}^5C_4/2^5 = 5/32 \). \( P(\text{at least 3 heads}) = (^{5}C_3 + ^{5}C_4 + ^{5}C_5)/2^5 = 16/32 = 1/2 \). Required probability \( = (5/32) \div (1/2) = 5/16 \). Full: Total outcomes \( = 2^5 = 32 \). Event A (exactly 4 heads): \( n(A) = {}^5C_4 = 5 \). Event B (at least 3 heads): \( n(B) = {}^5C_3 + {}^5C_4 + {}^5C_5 = 10 + 5 + 1 = 16 \). Since \( A \subset B \), \( P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)} = \frac{P(A)}{P(B)} = \frac{5/32}{16/32} = \frac{5}{16} \).
Q10. Physics
A convex lens has focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens should an object be placed to get a real image twice the size of object?
A. 40 cm
B. 30 cm
C. 20 cm
D. 60 cm
Correct Answer: B
Magnification m = -2 (real image). m = v/u = -2 ⇒ v = -2u. Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u. 1/20 = 1/(-2u) - 1/u = -3/(2u). u = -30 cm. So object distance = 30 cm.
Q11. Mathematics
If \(\displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\sin 5x}{\tan 3x}=k\), then \(k\) equals:
A. \(\frac{5}{3}\)
B. \(\frac{3}{5}\)
C. 5
D. 3
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Near zero, \(\sin θ≈θ\) and \(\tan θ≈θ\). So \(\frac{\sin 5x}{\tan 3x}\approx\frac{5x}{3x}=\frac{5}{3}\). Full solution: Using the standard limits \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin ax}{ax}=1\) and \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\tan bx}{bx}=1\), rewrite the expression as \(\frac{5x}{3x}\cdot\frac{\sin 5x}{5x}\cdot\frac{3x}{\tan 3x}\). Both fractions tend to 1, leaving \(\frac{5}{3}\).

Correct Answer: B

Q14. Physics
In a Young's double slit experiment using light of wavelength λ, the central bright fringe is observed at point O on the screen. A thin transparent sheet of refractive index μ = 1.5 and thickness t is introduced in front of the upper slit. Consequently, the central maximum shifts to a position originally occupied by the 5th order bright fringe. The thickness t of the sheet in terms of λ is:
A. 5λ
B. 20λ
C. 10λ
D. 15λ
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Shift of central maximum by n fringes implies path difference introduced = nλ. Path difference by sheet = (μ - 1)t. Equate: (μ - 1)t = nλ. Here n=5, μ=1.5. (0.5)t = 5λ ⇒ t = 10λ. Full Solution: Introduction of a sheet of thickness t and refractive index μ in front of a slit increases the optical path by (μ - 1)t. For the central maximum to shift to the position of the 5th bright fringe, the path difference must equal 5λ. Thus, (μ - 1)t = 5λ. Substituting μ = 1.5 gives (1.5 - 1)t = 5λ ⇒ 0.5t = 5λ ⇒ t = 10λ.
