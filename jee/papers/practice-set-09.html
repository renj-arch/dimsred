Q1. Mathematics
The roots of equation x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 are:
A. 2, 3
B. 2, 3
C. -2, -3
D. 1, 6
Correct Answer: B
x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3) = 0 -> x = 2 or x = 3.
Q2. Chemistry
Which process is used to separate crude oil into useful fractions?
A. Distillation
B. Crystallization
C. Fractional distillation
D. Chromatography
Correct Answer: C
Fractional distillation separates petroleum into fractions based on boiling points.
Q3. Mathematics
The value of log 1000 is:
A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Correct Answer: C
log 1000 = log 10 = 3.
Q4. Mathematics
The value of x^2 dx is:
A. 0
B. 1/2
C. 1/3
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
x^2 dx = [x/3] = 1/3 - 0 = 1/3.

Q6. Chemistry
The oxidation state of Mn in KMnO is:
A. +5
B. +6
C. +7
D. +4
Correct Answer: C
K(+1) + Mn + 4O(-2) = 0 -> +1 + Mn -8 = 0 -> Mn = +7.
Q7. Chemistry
Which gas is known as "Laughing Gas"?
A. NO
B. NO
C. CO
D. CO
Correct Answer: B
Nitrous oxide (NO) is called laughing gas due to its euphoric effects when inhaled.

Q9. Chemistry
The color of phenolphthalein in basic medium is:
A. Colorless
B. Pink
C. Blue
D. Yellow
Correct Answer: B
Phenolphthalein is colorless in acid and pink in base (pH > 8.3).
Q10. Chemistry
The molar mass of CaCO is:
A. 50 g/mol
B. 84 g/mol
C. 100 g/mol
D. 150 g/mol
Correct Answer: C
Ca(40) + C(12) + 3O(48) = 100 g/mol.
Q11. Mathematics
If sin = 3/5, then cos is:
A. 4/5
B. 4/5
C. 3/5
D. 5/4
Correct Answer: B
sin^2 + cos^2 = 1 -> cos^2 = 1 - 9/25 = 16/25 -> cos = 4/5 ( in first quadrant).
Q12. Chemistry
The pH of 10 M HCl solution is:
A. 1
B. 3
C. 7
D. 11
Correct Answer: B
[H] = 10 M. pH = -log[H] = -log(10) = 3.
Q13. Mathematics
The quadratic equation whose roots are 1 and 2 is:
A. x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0
B. x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0
C. x^2 + 3x + 2 = 0
D. x^2 - x + 2 = 0
Correct Answer: B
Sum = 3, Product = 2 -> x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0.
Q14. Chemistry
The number of valence electrons in carbon is:
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
Correct Answer: C
Carbon (Z=6) has electronic configuration 2,4 -> 4 valence electrons.
Q15. Chemistry
What is the hybridization of the central atom in the molecule CH4?
A. sp
B. sp2
C. sp3
D. dsp2
Correct Answer: C
The central atom in CH4 is carbon, which is bonded to four hydrogen atoms. Since carbon has four equivalent bonds, its hybridization is sp3.
