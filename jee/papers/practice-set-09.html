A. All real numbers

Correct Answer: B

Q5. [+4, -1] Mathematics
If f(x) = 2x + 1, find f(3):
A. 5
B. 6
C. 7
D. 8
Correct Answer: C
f(3) = 2(3) + 1 = 7.
Q6. [+4, -1] Mathematics
If the function f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3 is given, what is the value of x for which f(x) is maximum or minimum?
A. 0
B. 1
C. 2
D. 3
Correct Answer: C
To find the maximum or minimum of the function f(x), we need to find the critical points by taking the derivative of f(x) and setting it equal to zero. The derivative of f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3 is f'(x) = 2x - 4. Setting f'(x) = 0, we get 2x - 4 = 0, which gives x = 2.
Q7. [+4, -1] Mathematics
The median of 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 is:
A. 3
B. 5
C. 5
D. 7
Correct Answer: C
Middle value of sorted 5 numbers = 5.
Q8. [+4, -1] Physics
A body of mass 1 kg has weight approximately:
A. 1 N
B. 10 N
C. 100 N
D. 0.1 N
Correct Answer: B
W = mg = 1 x 10 = 10 N (approx).
Q9. [+4, -1] Physics
Hooke's law relates stress and:
A. Force
B. Strain
C. Area
D. Volume
Correct Answer: B
Within elastic limit, stress strain (Hooke's law).
Q10. [+4, -1] Physics
A thermodynamic system undergoes a process where the internal energy change is 100 J and the heat added to the system is 200 J. What is the work done by the system?
A. -100 J
B. 100 J
C. 200 J
D. 300 J
Correct Answer: A
The first law of thermodynamics states that ΔU = Q - W, where ΔU is the internal energy change, Q is the heat added, and W is the work done. Rearranging the equation to solve for W, we get W = Q - ΔU = 200 J - 100 J = 100 J. However, the work done 'by' the system is in the opposite direction, so the work done by the system is -100 J.
Q11. [+4, -1] Physics
The efficiency of a Carnot engine operating between 500 K and 300 K is:
A. 60%
B. 40%
C. 50%
D. 30%
Correct Answer: B
= 1 - T/T = 1 - 300/500 = 0.4 = 40%.
Q12. [+4, -1] Chemistry
The number of isomeric pentanes (CH) possible is:
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
Correct Answer: B
Pentane has 3 isomers: n-pentane, isopentane, neopentane.
Q13. [+4, -1] Chemistry
Which metal does not react with dilute HCl?
A. Zn
B. Mg
C. Cu
D. Fe
Correct Answer: C
Copper is below hydrogen in reactivity series, so does not react with dilute HCl.
Q14. [+4, -1] Chemistry
The IUPAC name of CHCHCH(CH)COOH is:
A. 2-Methylbutanoic acid
B. 2-Methylbutanoic acid
C. 3-Methylbutanoic acid
D. Pentanoic acid
Correct Answer: B
Longest chain has 4 C with COOH at C1 and methyl at C2 -> 2-Methylbutanoic acid.
Q15. [+4, -1] Physics
First law of thermodynamics is based on:
A. Conservation of momentum
B. Conservation of energy
C. Conservation of mass
D. Conservation of charge
Correct Answer: B
U = Q - W. First law is the law of conservation of energy for thermodynamic systems.
