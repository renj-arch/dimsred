6 g of a non-electrolyte solute dissolved in 100 g of water elevates the boiling point by 0.26 K. The molar mass of the solute is: (Kb for water = 0.52 K kg mol⁻¹)
A. 30 g/mol
B. 60 g/mol
C. 120 g/mol
D. 240 g/mol
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: ΔTb = Kb × m → m = 0.26/0.52 = 0.5 mol/kg. Moles of solute = 0.5 × 0.1 kg = 0.05 mol. Molar mass = 6 g / 0.05 mol = 120 g/mol. Full: ΔTb = i·Kb·m (i=1 for non-electrolyte). Molality m = ΔTb/Kb = 0.26/0.52 = 0.5 mol kg⁻¹. Amount of solute = m × mass of solvent(kg) = 0.5 × 0.1 = 0.05 mol. Molar mass = mass/moles = 6 g / 0.05 mol = 120 g/mol.
Q2. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-15
The vectors a = i + 2j + 3k, b = 2i + 3j + 4k, c = 3i + 4j + 5k are:
A. Mutually perpendicular
B. Coplanar
C. Non-coplanar
D. Collinear
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Check linear dependence via row differences. b-a = (1,1,1), c-b = (1,1,1). Since (b-a) and (c-b) are parallel, the three vectors lie in the same plane (coplanar). Step-by-step: Scalar triple product [a b c] = determinant of rows (1,2,3), (2,3,4), (3,4,5). R₂→R₂-R₁, R₃→R₃-R₂ gives rows (1,1,1) and (1,1,1). Two identical rows ⇒ Determinant = 0 ⇒ Vectors are coplanar.

Q4. Physicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-15
A uniform disc of mass M and radius R has a circular hole of radius R/2 cut out from it, touching the disc's circumference. The moment of inertia of the remaining part about an axis passing through the centre of the original disc and perpendicular to its plane is:
A. MR²/2
B. 13MR²/32
C. 3MR²/8
D. MR²/4
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Mass of removed part m = M/4 (area ratio 1:4). Its MI about disc centre via parallel axis: I_rem = m[(R/2)²/2 + (R/2)²] = (M/4)(3R²/8) = 3MR²/32. Subtract from full disc MI (MR²/2 = 16MR²/32) to get 13MR²/32. Full: I_disc = ½ MR². Mass of cut portion m = M × (π(R/2)²)/(πR²) = M/4. MI of cut portion about its own centre = ½ m(R/2)² = MR²/32. Distance between axes = R/2. By parallel axis theorem, MI of cut portion about disc centre = MR²/32 + m(R/2)² = 3MR²/32. Required MI = ½ MR² - 3MR²/32 = 13MR²/32.

B. 4F
C. 8F
D. 16F
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Force per unit length f ∝ I₁I₂/d. New currents 2I₁, 2I₂ (factor 4). New distance d/2 (factor 2). Total factor = 4 × 2 = 8. So F' = 8F. Full: F = μ₀ I₁ I₂ / (2π d). F' = μ₀ (2I₁)(2I₂) / (2π (d/2)) = 8 μ₀ I₁ I₂ / (2π d) = 8F.

A. 4.44
B. 4.74

Correct Answer: B

Correct Answer: A

Correct Answer: A

For a first-order reaction, the rate constant is 2.303 × 10⁻³ s⁻¹. The time required for 90% completion of the reaction is approximately:
A. 1000 s
B. 500 s
C. 2000 s
D. 2303 s
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For 90% completion in 1st order, fraction remaining = 0.1, so t = (2.303/k) log(1/0.1) = 2.303/k. Substituting k = 2.303×10⁻³ gives t = 1000 s directly. Full: Integrated rate law t = (2.303/k) log(a/(a-x)). For 90% completion, (a/(a-x)) = 100/10 = 10. log 10 = 1. Thus t = 2.303 / (2.303 × 10⁻³) = 10³ s = 1000 s.
Q12. Mathematicsmodel-generated · 2026-11-15
The value of the definite integral ∫₀^(π/2) (sin x)/(sin x + cos x) dx is:
A. π/2
B. π/4
C. π/8
D. π
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Apply King's Property (∫₀ᵃ f(x)dx = ∫₀ᵃ f(a-x)dx). Let I be the integral. Replace x by π/2-x to get I = ∫₀^(π/2) (cos x)/(sin x+cos x) dx. Add both: 2I = ∫₀^(π/2) 1 dx = π/2 ⇒ I = π/4. Step-by-step: I = ∫ sin/(sin+cos). I = ∫ cos/(sin+cos). 2I = ∫ (sin+cos)/(sin+cos) = ∫ 1 dx = π/2. I = π/4.

10 g of H2 and 64 g of O2 are allowed to react in a 10 L vessel at 300 K to form water vapour. What is the pressure inside the vessel after the reaction goes to completion? (Assume ideal gas behaviour, R = 0.0821 L atm K⁻¹ mol⁻¹)
A. 6.15 atm
B. 12.3 atm
C. 18.45 atm
D. 24.6 atm
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Compare stoichiometric mole ratios (n_H2/2 = 2.5 vs n_O2/1 = 2); O2 is limiting. Remaining gas moles = excess H2 (5-4=1 mol) + product H2O (4 mol) = 5 mol. Full: Moles H2 = 5, O2 = 2. Reaction 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O consumes 4 mol H2 and 2 mol O2, producing 4 mol H2O. Total moles = 1 + 4 = 5. P = nRT/V = 5 × 0.0821 × 300 / 10 = 12.315 atm ≈ 12.3 atm.

Four point charges +q, +q, -q, -q are placed at the corners A, B, C, D of a square of side 'a' in clockwise order. The electric potential at the centre O of the square is:
A. Zero
B. 2kq/a
C. 4kq/a
D. kq/a
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Potential is a scalar quantity. Net charge sum = +q + q - q - q = 0. Since distance from centre to each corner is same (a/√2), V_net = k(Σq)/r = 0. Full: Distance from centre to corner r = a/√2. Potential due to +q charges = 2kq/r. Potential due to -q charges = -2kq/r. Total V = 2kq/r - 2kq/r = 0.
