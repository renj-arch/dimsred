Q1. Physics
A particle is moving with velocity v = 2t² + 3t. Find its acceleration at t = 2s.
A. 11 m/s²
B. 10 m/s²
C. 8 m/s²
D. 14 m/s²
Correct Answer: A
a = dv/dt = d(2t² + 3t)/dt = 4t + 3. At t = 2: a = 4(2) + 3 = 11 m/s².
Q2. Physics
A convex lens has focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens should an object be placed to get a real image twice the size of object?
A. 40 cm
B. 30 cm
C. 20 cm
D. 60 cm
Correct Answer: B
Magnification m = -2 (real image). m = v/u = -2 → v = -2u. Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u. 1/20 = 1/(-2u) - 1/u = -3/(2u). u = -30 cm. So object distance = 30 cm.
Q3. Physics
The escape velocity of a body from Earth is 11.2 km/s. What is the escape velocity from a planet having mass 4 times and radius twice that of Earth?
A. 22.4 km/s
B. 5.6 km/s
C. 15.8 km/s
D. 11.2 km/s
Correct Answer: C
v_e = √(2GM/R). For new planet: v_e' = √(2G·4M/2R) = √(2·2GM/R) = √2·√(2GM/R) = √2 × 11.2 = 15.84 km/s.
Q4. Physics
A wire of resistance R is stretched to double its length. New resistance will be:
A. R/2
B. R
C. 4R
D. 2R
Correct Answer: C
When length is doubled (L' = 2L), cross-sectional area halves (A' = A/2). R' = ρL'/A' = ρ(2L)/(A/2) = 4ρL/A = 4R.
Q5. Physics
The de Broglie wavelength of an electron moving with velocity v is λ. If velocity is doubled, wavelength becomes:
A. 2λ
B. λ/2
C. 4λ
D. λ/4
Correct Answer: B
λ = h/p = h/(mv). If v doubles, λ' = h/(m·2v) = λ/2.
Q6. Chemistry
The IUPAC name of CH₃CH₂CH(CH₃)COOH is:
A. 2-Methylbutanoic acid

C. 3-Methylbutanoic acid
D. Pentanoic acid
Correct Answer: B
Longest chain has 4 C with COOH at C1 and methyl at C2 → 2-Methylbutanoic acid.
Q7. Chemistry
How many moles of O₂ are required for complete combustion of 1 mole of C₄H₁₀?
A. 4
B. 5
C. 6.5
D. 8
Correct Answer: C
C₄H₁₀ + 6.5O₂ → 4CO₂ + 5H₂O. So 6.5 moles of O₂ are needed.
Q8. Chemistry
Which of the following shows +R effect?
A. -OH
B. -NO₂
C. -CN
D. -COOH
Correct Answer: A
-OH, -NH₂, -OR show +R (resonance) effect as they donate electrons. -NO₂, -CN, -COOH show -R effect.
Q9. Chemistry
The pH of 10⁻³ M HCl solution is:
A. 1
B. 3
C. 7
D. 11
Correct Answer: B
[H⁺] = 10⁻³ M. pH = -log[H⁺] = -log(10⁻³) = 3.
Q10. Chemistry
The coordination number in NaCl crystal structure is:
A. 4
B. 6
C. 8
D. 12
Correct Answer: B
NaCl has face-centered cubic (FCC) structure with each Na⁺ surrounded by 6 Cl⁻ and vice versa.
Q11. Mathematics
If A = {1, 2, 3} and B = {2, 3, 4}, find A ∩ B.
A. {1}
B. {2, 3}
C. {2, 3, 4}
D. {1, 2, 3, 4}
Correct Answer: B
A ∩ B = elements common to both sets = {2, 3}.
Q12. Mathematics
Find the derivative of f(x) = sin(x²).
A. cos(x²)
B. 2x cos(x)
C. 2x cos(x²)
D. x² cos(x²)
Correct Answer: C
Using chain rule: d/dx[sin(x²)] = cos(x²) · d/dx(x²) = 2x cos(x²).
Q13. Mathematics
The value of ∫₀¹ x² dx is:
A. 0
B. 1/2
C. 1/3
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
∫₀¹ x² dx = [x³/3]₀¹ = 1/3 - 0 = 1/3.
Q14. Mathematics
If the probability of an event A is 0.4 and of B is 0.5, and A and B are independent, then P(A ∩ B) is:
A. 0.9
B. 0.1
C. 0.2
D. 0.02
Correct Answer: C
For independent events: P(A ∩ B) = P(A)·P(B) = 0.4 × 0.5 = 0.2.
Q15. Mathematics
The equation of line with slope 2 passing through (1, 3) is:
A. y = 2x + 1
B. y = 2x + 1
C. y = 2x - 1
D. y = x + 2
Correct Answer: B
Point-slope form: y - 3 = 2(x - 1) → y = 2x + 1.
Q16. Physics
A capacitor of 10 µF is charged to 100 V. The energy stored is:
A. 0.05 J
B. 0.05 J
C. 0.5 J
D. 5 J
Correct Answer: B
E = ½CV² = ½ × 10×10⁻⁶ × 100² = 0.05 J.
Q17. Physics
The equivalent resistance of three 6 Ω resistors connected in parallel is:
A. 18 Ω
B. 6 Ω
C. 2 Ω
D. 3 Ω
Correct Answer: C
1/Rp = 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 → Rp = 2 Ω.
Q18. Physics
The photon energy of light with wavelength 500 nm is (h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ Js):
A. 2.48 × 10⁻¹⁹ J
B. 3.98 × 10⁻¹⁹ J
C. 1.99 × 10⁻¹⁹ J
D. 4.97 × 10⁻¹⁹ J
Correct Answer: B
E = hc/λ = (6.63×10⁻³⁴ × 3×10⁸)/(500×10⁻⁹) = 3.98×10⁻¹⁹ J.
Q19. Physics
In SHM, the acceleration is maximum at:
A. Mean position
B. Equilibrium position
C. Extreme position
D. Same everywhere
Correct Answer: C
a = -ω²x. Acceleration is maximum at extreme positions (x = ±A) and zero at mean position.
Q20. Physics
First law of thermodynamics is based on:
A. Conservation of momentum
B. Conservation of energy
C. Conservation of mass
D. Conservation of charge
Correct Answer: B
ΔU = Q - W. First law is the law of conservation of energy for thermodynamic systems.
Q21. Chemistry

Q22. Chemistry
The oxidation state of Mn in KMnO₄ is:

Q27. Mathematics
If sin θ = 3/5, then cos θ is:
A. 4/5
B. 4/5
C. 3/5
D. 5/4
Correct Answer: B
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 → cos²θ = 1 - 9/25 = 16/25 → cos θ = 4/5 (θ in first quadrant).
Q28. Mathematics

B. 5
C. 6
D. 7
Correct Answer: C
Mean = (2+4+6+8+10)/5 = 30/5 = 6.
Q29. Mathematics
The roots of equation x² - 5x + 6 = 0 are:
A. 2, 3
B. 2, 3
C. -2, -3
D. 1, 6
Correct Answer: B
x² - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3) = 0 → x = 2 or x = 3.
