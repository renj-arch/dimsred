Q3. [+4, -1] Chemistry
What is the molecular formula of the compound with the empirical formula CH2O and a molecular weight of 180 g/mol?
A. C6H12O6
B. C3H6O3
C. C2H4O2
D. C4H8O4
Correct Answer: A
The empirical formula of the compound is CH2O, and its molecular weight is 180 g/mol. The empirical formula weight is 30 g/mol (12 for C, 2 for H, and 16 for O). To find the molecular formula, we divide the molecular weight by the empirical formula weight: 180/30 = 6. Therefore, the molecular formula is (CH2O)6, which is C6H12O6.

Q6. [+4, -1] Physics
A wire of resistance R is stretched to double its length. New resistance will be:
A. R/2
B. R
C. 4R
D. 2R
Correct Answer: C
When length is doubled (L' = 2L), cross-sectional area halves (A' = A/2). R' = L'/A' = (2L)/(A/2) = 4L/A = 4R.
Q7. [+4, -1] Mathematics
If the equation of a circle is x^2 + y^2 + 4x - 6y + 9 = 0, what is the radius of the circle?
A. 2
B. 4
C. sqrt(5)
D. sqrt(5)
Correct Answer: D
To find the radius of the circle, we need to rewrite the equation of the circle in the standard form (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2, where (h, k) is the center of the circle and r is the radius. Completing the square for x and y, we get (x + 2)^2 - 4 + (y - 3)^2 - 9 + 9 = 0, which simplifies to (x + 2)^2 + (y - 3)^2 = 4. Comparing this with the standard form, we see that the radius is sqrt(4) = 2.
