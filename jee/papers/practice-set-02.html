Q1. Physics
Young's modulus is defined for which type of deformation?
A. Shear
B. Longitudinal strain
C. Volume strain
D. Torsion
Correct Answer: B
Y = stress/strain for longitudinal (tensile/compressive) deformation.
Q2. Physics
A convex lens has focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens should an object be placed to get a real image twice the size of object?
A. 40 cm
B. 30 cm
C. 20 cm
D. 60 cm
Correct Answer: B
Magnification m = -2 (real image). m = v/u = -2 -> v = -2u. Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u. 1/20 = 1/(-2u) - 1/u = -3/(2u). u = -30 cm. So object distance = 30 cm.
Q3. Physics
A wire of resistance R is stretched to double its length. New resistance will be:
A. R/2
B. R
C. 4R
D. 2R
Correct Answer: C
When length is doubled (L' = 2L), cross-sectional area halves (A' = A/2). R' = L'/A' = (2L)/(A/2) = 4L/A = 4R.
Q4. Mathematics
The sum of first 10 natural numbers is:
A. 45
B. 55
C. 50
D. 60
Correct Answer: B
Sum = n(n+1)/2 = 10x11/2 = 55.
Q5. Chemistry
Which element has the highest ionization energy?
A. Li
B. Na
C. He
D. Ne
Correct Answer: C
Helium has the highest ionization energy due to its full 1s^2 configuration and small atomic size.
Q6. Chemistry
Which gas is known as "Laughing Gas"?
A. NO
B. NO
C. CO
D. CO
Correct Answer: B
Nitrous oxide (NO) is called laughing gas due to its euphoric effects when inhaled.
Q7. Mathematics
Find the derivative of f(x) = sin(x^2).
A. cos(x^2)
B. 2x cos(x)
C. 2x cos(x^2)
D. x^2 cos(x^2)
Correct Answer: C
Using chain rule: d/dx[sin(x^2)] = cos(x^2) . d/dx(x^2) = 2x cos(x^2).
Q8. Mathematics
The probability of getting a head when a fair coin is tossed is:
A. 0
B. 1/4
C. 1/2
D. 1
Correct Answer: C
P(Head) = 1/2 (favorable outcomes / total outcomes).
