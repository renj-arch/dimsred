About JEE

Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main is conducted for admission to NITs, IIITs, and CFTIs. JEE Advanced is for IIT admission. JEE Main consists of 90 questions (300 marks) across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with negative marking for incorrect answers.
