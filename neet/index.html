About NEET

National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) UG is the single largest medical entrance exam in India for MBBS, BDS, and other medical courses. It consists of 200 questions (720 marks) from Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Botany + Zoology) with negative marking.
