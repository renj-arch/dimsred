Key Concepts

What is Plant Physiology?

Plant physiology is the study of how plants function, including their metabolism, growth, development, and responses to environmental stimuli. For NEET, key topics include photosynthesis, respiration, transpiration, mineral nutrition, plant growth regulators (phytohormones), and photoperiodism. Understanding how plants convert light energy into chemical energy is fundamental.

Key Concepts

Photosynthesis: 6CO2 + 12H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2 + 6H2O. Light reactions produce ATP and NADPH; dark reactions (Calvin cycle) fix CO2. C3 vs C4 Plants: C3 plants use RuBisCO and have photorespiration; C4 plants use PEP carboxylase and have Kranz anatomy. Transpiration: Loss of water vapor through stomata, driven by the transpiration pull-cohesion tension mechanism. Plant Hormones: Auxin (cell elongation), Gibberellin (stem growth), Cytokinin (cell division), Abscisic acid (stress response), Ethylene (fruit ripening).

Solved Examples

Example 1
Which pigment is the primary light-absorbing pigment in photosynthesis?

a) Chlorophyll a b) Chlorophyll b c) Xanthophyll d) Carotene
Solution: Chlorophyll a is the primary photosynthetic pigment that directly participates in the light reactions. It acts as the reaction center in both photosystem I and photosystem II. Accessory pigments (chlorophyll b, carotenoids) absorb light and transfer energy to chlorophyll a. Hence option (a) is correct.
Example 2
Which plant hormone promotes fruit ripening?

a) Auxin b) Gibberellin c) Cytokinin d) Ethylene
Solution: Ethylene (C2H4) is the plant hormone responsible for fruit ripening. It is a gaseous hormone that also promotes senescence and abscission. It is commercially used to ripen fruits like bananas and tomatoes. Hence option (d) is correct.
Example 3
The opening and closing of stomata is regulated by:

a) Auxin b) Abscisic acid c) Gibberellin d) Cytokinin
Solution: Abscisic acid (ABA) is the stress hormone that causes stomatal closure under water-deficit conditions. ABA triggers the efflux of potassium ions from guard cells, reducing turgor pressure and closing the stomatal pore. Hence option (b) is correct.

Shortcut Techniques

  • For C3 vs C4 comparison: C4 plants have higher photosynthetic efficiency at high temperatures and low CO2 concentrations.
  • Photorespiration occurs only in C3 plants and reduces photosynthetic efficiency by consuming O2 instead of CO2.
  • Casparian strips in the endodermis of roots regulate the apoplastic movement of water and minerals.
  • Essential plant nutrients are classified as macronutrients (C, H, O, N, P, K, Ca, Mg, S) and micronutrients (Fe, Mn, Cu, Zn, Mo, B, Cl, Ni).
Pro Tip
For NEET, remember the key differences between C3 and C4 plants: C4 plants have Kranz anatomy, higher optimum temperature, higher water use efficiency, and include crops like maize, sugarcane, and sorghum.
