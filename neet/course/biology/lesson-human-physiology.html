Key Concepts

What is Human Physiology?

Human physiology is the study of how the human body functions, including the mechanisms of various organ systems and their coordination. For NEET, key topics include digestion and absorption, respiration and gas exchange, circulation (heart and blood vessels), excretion and osmoregulation, nervous system, endocrine system, and reproduction. Understanding homeostasis and feedback mechanisms is essential.

Key Concepts

Digestive System: Mouth (amylase), stomach (pepsin, HCl), small intestine (trypsin, lipase, maltase). Villi and microvilli increase surface area for absorption. Respiratory System: Trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli. Oxygen transport: 97% bound to hemoglobin, 3% dissolved. CO2 transport: 70% as bicarbonate, 23% bound to hemoglobin, 7% dissolved. Circulatory System: SA node is the pacemaker. Cardiac output = stroke volume × heart rate. Excretory System: Nephron is the functional unit. Ultrafiltration, reabsorption, and secretion. Endocrine System: Hypothalamus-pituitary axis, feedback regulation. Nervous System: Neuron structure, synapse, action potential propagation.

Solved Examples

Example 1
Which enzyme is responsible for the digestion of proteins in the stomach?

a) Trypsin b) Pepsin c) Lipase d) Amylase
Solution: Pepsin is the proteolytic enzyme secreted by chief cells of the gastric glands in the stomach. It acts on proteins in an acidic medium (pH 1.5-2.0) and breaks them down into peptides. Trypsin acts in the small intestine. Hence option (b) is correct.
Example 2
Which part of the brain is responsible for maintaining balance and coordination?

a) Cerebrum b) Cerebellum c) Medulla oblongata d) Hypothalamus
Solution: The cerebellum is the part of the hindbrain that coordinates voluntary movements, balance, posture, and motor learning. It receives input from sensory systems and the spinal cord to fine-tune motor activity. Hence option (b) is correct.
Example 3
The hormone that regulates blood sugar level is secreted by which gland?

a) Thyroid b) Adrenal c) Pancreas d) Pituitary
Solution: The pancreas secretes insulin (by beta cells of islets of Langerhans) which lowers blood glucose, and glucagon (by alpha cells) which raises blood glucose. These hormones maintain blood sugar homeostasis. Hence option (c) is correct.

Shortcut Techniques

  • For the cardiac cycle: remember Lub (atrioventricular valve closure) and Dub (semilunar valve closure).
  • For the nephron: Proximal convoluted tubule is the major site of reabsorption (glucose, amino acids, 65% of Na+, water).
  • In the nervous system, the resting membrane potential is -70 mV maintained by Na+/K+ ATPase pump.
  • For hormone classification: Peptide hormones act via second messengers (cAMP); steroid hormones act directly on DNA.
Pro Tip
For NEET, focus on the hypothalamus-pituitary-target organ axis. Understand the feedback mechanisms: negative feedback (most common) and positive feedback (e.g., oxytocin in childbirth, LH surge in ovulation).
