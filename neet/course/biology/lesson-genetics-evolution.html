Key Concepts

What is Genetics and Evolution?

Genetics is the study of genes, heredity, and genetic variation in living organisms. Evolution explains how populations of organisms change over generations through the process of natural selection. For NEET, key topics include Mendelian genetics, molecular basis of inheritance (DNA replication, transcription, translation), gene regulation, mutation, and the evidence and mechanisms of evolution.

Key Concepts

Mendel's Laws: Law of Segregation (alleles separate during gamete formation), Law of Independent Assortment (genes on different chromosomes assort independently). DNA Structure: Double helix by Watson and Crick, complementary base pairing (A-T, G-C), antiparallel strands. Central Dogma: DNA → mRNA → Protein. Transcription: DNA to mRNA by RNA polymerase. Translation: mRNA to protein by ribosomes. Hardy-Weinberg Principle: p + q = 1, p2 + 2pq + q2 = 1. Natural Selection: Darwin's theory - organisms with favorable traits survive and reproduce more.

Solved Examples

Example 1
In a cross between two heterozygous tall pea plants (Tt × Tt), what proportion of offspring will be homozygous tall?

a) 1/4 b) 1/2 c) 3/4 d) 1
Solution: Using a Punnett square for Tt × Tt: TT (homozygous tall), Tt (heterozygous tall), Tt (heterozygous tall), tt (dwarf). The genotypes are in the ratio 1:2:1. Homozygous tall (TT) = 1/4. Hence option (a) is correct.
Example 2
If a population has 16% recessive phenotype, what is the frequency of the dominant allele? (Assuming Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium)

a) 0.4 b) 0.6 c) 0.8 d) 0.2
Solution: Recessive phenotype frequency q2 = 0.16, so q = √0.16 = 0.4. Since p + q = 1, p = 1 - 0.4 = 0.6. The frequency of the dominant allele is 0.6. Hence option (b) is correct.
Example 3
Which enzyme is responsible for unwinding the DNA double helix during replication?

a) DNA polymerase b) DNA helicase c) DNA ligase d) RNA primase
Solution: DNA helicase is the enzyme that unwinds the DNA double helix by breaking hydrogen bonds between complementary base pairs, creating the replication fork. DNA polymerase synthesizes new strands, DNA ligase joins Okazaki fragments, and RNA primase synthesizes RNA primers. Hence option (b) is correct.

Shortcut Techniques

  • For monohybrid cross problems, the phenotypic ratio is 3:1 and genotypic ratio is 1:2:1 in F2 generation.
  • For dihybrid cross, the phenotypic ratio is 9:3:3:1 if genes are on different chromosomes.
  • In DNA, the number of hydrogen bonds: A-T pair has 2 bonds, G-C pair has 3 bonds.
  • For evolution, remember homology (same structure, different function) indicates common ancestry; analogy (different structure, same function) indicates convergent evolution.
Pro Tip
For NEET, master Punnett square problems by practicing various cross types (monohybrid, dihybrid, test cross, back cross). Also understand pedigree analysis as it is a common application question in genetics.
