Q1. Botany
According to Lindeman's 10% Law of energy transfer in an ecosystem, if the total energy captured by producers (Trophic Level 1) is 10,000 Joules, what is the approximate energy available to secondary consumers (Trophic Level 3)?
A. 100 J
B. 10 J
C. 1,000 J
D. 10,000 J
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Energy at Trophic Level n = Energy at T1 × (0.1)^(n-1). Secondary Consumers are at T3. Number of transfers = 2 (T1→T2, T2→T3). Calculation: 10,000 × 0.1 × 0.1 = 100 J. Eliminate 1,000 J (T2, 1 transfer), 10 J (T4, 3 transfers), 10,000 J (T1, 0 transfers). Worked: Producers (T1) = 10,000 J. Primary Consumers/Herbivores (T2) receive 10% = 1,000 J. Secondary Consumers/Primary Carnivores (T3) receive 10% of T2 = 10% of 1,000 J = 100 J. Only ~1% of producer energy reaches secondary consumers.

Q3. Zoology
In which subphylum of Phylum Chordata does the notochord persist throughout life and extend the entire length of the body, including the anterior region?
A. Cephalochordata
B. Urochordata
C. Vertebrata
D. Hemichordata
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: 'Cephalo' means head; in Cephalochordata (e.g., Branchiostoma), the notochord extends into the head region (rostrum) and persists lifelong. Urochordates have notochord only in the larval tail; Vertebrates replace it with a vertebral column; Hemichordates possess a stomochord, not a true notochord. Full Solution: The defining feature of Chordata is the notochord. In subphylum Cephalochordata, the notochord is persistent, extends the full body length (including anterior to the nerve cord), and is contractile. In Urochordata, it is restricted to the larval tail and lost during metamorphosis. In Vertebrata, the embryonic notochord is largely replaced by the vertebral column. Hemichordata aren on-chordates with a buccal diverticulum (stomochord).
Q4. Botany
The Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) is a cyclic process used to amplify DNA. What is the correct sequential order of the three main steps in a single standard PCR cycle?
A. Denaturation → Annealing → Extension
B. Annealing → Denaturation → Extension
C. Extension → Denaturation → Annealing
D. Denaturation → Extension → Annealing
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Logical flow of DNA synthesis: 1. Separate strands (Denaturation, ~94-92°C). 2. Bind primers (Annealing, ~50-65°C). 3. Extend primers (Extension, ~72°C). Only Option A follows this mandatory thermodynamic and enzymatic sequence. Options starting with Annealing/Extension are physically impossible on dsDNA template. Worked: Cycle 1: Denaturation (94–92°C) melts dsDNA template into single strands. Cycle 2: Annealing (50–65°C) allows forward/reverse primers to anneal to complementary sequences on ssDNA. Cycle 3: Extension (72°C) - Thermus aquaticus (Taq) DNA polymerase extends primers in 5'→3' direction synthesizing new complementary strands. This 3-step sequence constitutes one cycle.
Q5. Botany
Which plant hormone specifically induces bolting in rosette plants and breaks seed dormancy by stimulating the synthesis of hydrolytic enzymes (like α-amylase) in the aleurone layer of germinating cereal grains?
A. Auxin (IAA)
B. Gibberellin (GA₃)
C. Cytokinin (Kinetin)
D. Abscisic Acid (ABA)
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Keywords 'bolting' (rapid stem elongation in rosette plants) and 'aleurone layer/amylase induction' (mobilizing endosperm reserves during germination) are exclusive functional hallmarks of Gibberellins. Auxin regulates apical dominance; Cytokinin delays senescence; ABA enforces dormancy (antagonist to GA). Worked: Gibberellins (GA₃) promote internodal elongation causing bolting in long-day/rosette plants. In germinating seeds, GA diffuses from embryo to aleurone layer, triggering DNA transcription/translation for α-amylase and protease synthesis to hydrolyze starch/proteins. ABA inhibits this process. Thus, Gibberellin is the correct hormone.
Q6. Chemistry
Which of the following complex ions exhibits optical isomerism?
A. [Co(en)₃]³⁺ (en = ethylenediamine)
B. [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺
C. [CoCl₄]²⁻
D. [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Optical activity in octahedral complexes requires the absence of a plane of symmetry or center of inversion. Complexes with three bidentate chelating ligands (like [M(AA)₃]ⁿ⁺) are inherently chiral (exist as Λ and Δ enantiomers). Monodentate ligand complexes like [Ma₄b₂] or [Ma₆] possess symmetry elements. Tetrahedral [Ma₄] is also symmetrical. Full Solution: [Co(en)₃]³⁺ contains three bidentate ethylenediamine ligands forming three chelate rings in an octahedral geometry. This arrangement lacks any plane of symmetry or center of inversion, making it chiral and optically active (exists as d and l forms). [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺ (cis and trans) has planes of symmetry. [CoCl₄]²⁻ is tetrahedral with four identical ligands (Td symmetry). [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ has Oh symmetry. Only option A shows optical isomerism.
Q7. Physics
A Carnot engine operates with a sink at 300 K and has an efficiency of 40%. If the temperature of the source is increased by 100 K while the sink temperature remains unchanged, the new efficiency of the engine will be:
A. 45%
B. 48%
C. 49%
D. 50%
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Use η = 1 - T₂/T₁. From η₁ = 0.4, find T₁ = T₂/(1-η₁) = 300/0.6 = 500 K. New T₁' = 600 K. New η₂ = 1 - 300/600 = 0.5 = 50%. Full Solution: Initial efficiency η₁ = 1 - T₂/T₁ ⇒ 0.4 = 1 - 300/T₁ ⇒ 300/T₁ = 0.6 ⇒ T₁ = 500 K. New source temperature T₁' = 500 + 100 = 600 K. New efficiency η₂ = 1 - T₂/T₁' = 1 - 300/600 = 1 - 0.5 = 0.5 = 50%.

Q9. Zoology
In a diploid organism with a somatic chromosome number of 2n = 24, what is the chromosome number and DNA content (relative to 2C G1 somatic cell) in a secondary spermatocyte immediately after the completion of Meiosis I?
A. 12 chromosomes, 1C DNA
B. 12 chromosomes, 2C DNA
C. 24 chromosomes, 1C DNA
D. 24 chromosomes, 2C DNA
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Meiosis I is the reductional division (chromosome number halves: 2n → n). Meiosis II is the equational division (DNA content halves: 2C → 1C). Secondary spermatocyte is formed *after* Meiosis I. Therefore, chromosome number = n = 12. DNA content is still 2C (each chromosome consists of two chromatids). Option Elimination: Eliminate options with 24 chromosomes (C, D) because number halves in Meiosis I. Eliminate option with 1C DNA (A) because DNA halves in Meiosis II. Correct answer is 12 chromosomes, 2C DNA. Full Solution: Somatic cell (G1): 2n=24, 2C. After S-phase: 2n=24, 4C. End of Meiosis I (Secondary Spermatocyte): Homologous chromosomes separate. Chromosome number reduces to n=12. Each chromosome still has 2 chromatids, so DNA content = 2C. End of Meiosis II (Spermatid): Sister chromatids separate. Chromosome number n=12, DNA content = 1C.
Q10. Physics
In a potentiometer experiment, a cell of emf 1.5 V gives a balancing length of 60 cm. The cell is replaced by another cell of emf 2.0 V, keeping the potential gradient along the wire constant. The new balancing length is:
A. 80 cm
B. 45 cm
C. 90 cm
D. 120 cm
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: At constant potential gradient (k), emf E ∝ balancing length l. So l₂ = l₁ × (E₂/E₁) = 60 × (2.0/1.5) = 60 × (4/3) = 80 cm. Full Solution: Potential gradient k = E₁/l₁ = 1.5 V / 0.6 m = 2.5 V/m. For the second cell, E₂ = k × l₂ ⇒ l₂ = E₂/k = 2.0 / 2.5 = 0.8 m = 80 cm.
Q11. Zoology
The Juxtaglomerular Apparatus (JGA) regulates renal blood flow and filtration rate. Which statement accurately describes the stimulus and secretory response of the juxtaglomerular (JG) cells?
A. Decreased renal perfusion pressure stimulates JG cells to secrete renin.
B. Increased NaCl concentration at the macula densa stimulates JG cells to secrete renin.
C. Decreased sympathetic stimulation inhibits JG cells from secreting erythropoietin.
D. Increased atrial natriuretic peptide (ANP) stimulates JG cells to secrete angiotensinogen.
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: JG cells secrete Renin (not EPO or Angiotensinogen). Renin release is triggered by Low BP (baroreceptor), Low NaCl (macula densa), and Sympathetic stimulation. Eliminate options with wrong hormone (C, D) or inverse stimulus (B: High NaCl inhibits renin; D: ANP inhibits renin). Step-by-step: JG cells are modified afferent arteriolar smooth muscle cells acting as baroreceptors. A drop in renal perfusion pressure directly stimulates renin secretion. The macula densa senses low tubular NaCl (not high) to stimulate renin. Erythropoietin is produced by peritubular fibroblasts, not JG cells. Angiotensinogen is a hepatic protein; ANP suppresses renin release.
Q12. Chemistry
For a first-order reaction, the time required for 75% completion is 40 minutes. The time required (approximately) for 99.9% completion will be:
A. 80 min
B. 120 min
C. 200 min
D. 400 min
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: For 1st order, t₇₅% = 2×t₁/₂ (so t₁/₂ = 20 min) and t₉₉.₉% ≈ 10×t₁/₂ = 200 min. Full: k = ln2/20; t = (1/k)ln(1000) = 20×ln1000/ln2 ≈ 200 min.
Q13. Physics
Unpolarised light of intensity I₀ is incident on a polaroid P₁. The transmitted light passes through a second polaroid P₂ whose pass axis makes an angle of 45° with that of P₁. The intensity of light emerging from P₂ is:
A. I₀/8
B. I₀/2
C. I₀/4
D. 3I₀/8
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Unpolarised light through first polaroid reduces intensity to half (I₁ = I₀/2). Malus' Law for second polaroid: I₂ = I₁ cos²θ. For θ = 45°, cos²45° = 1/2. Thus I₂ = (I₀/2) × (1/2) = I₀/4. Full Solution: Intensity after P₁: I₁ = I₀/2. Intensity after P₂: I₂ = I₁ cos²(45°) = (I₀/2) × (1/√2)² = (I₀/2) × (1/2) = I₀/4.
Q14. Botany
In the embryo sac of a typical flowering plant (Polygonum type), double fertilization involves the fusion of two male gametes. What is the ploidy level of the Primary Endosperm Nucleus (PEN) formed by the fusion of the second male gamete with the central cell?
A. Haploid (n)
B. Diploid (2n)
C. Triploid (3n)
D. Tetraploid (4n)
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: PEN ploidy = Male gamete (n) + Two Polar Nuclei (n + n) = 3n (Triploid). The Zygote is 2n (n+n). Eliminate n (gamete), 2n (zygote), 4n (no standard 4n fusion in angiosperms). Worked: The central cell contains two haploid polar nuclei (n+n). The second male gamete (n) fuses with these two nuclei (triple fusion). Resulting Primary Endosperm Nucleus (PEN) has three haploid sets of chromosomes (3n), making it triploid. This nucleus develops into the typically triploid endosperm.
Q15. Chemistry
For an ionic compound AX, the radius ratio (r⁺/r⁻) is found to be 0.55. The coordination number of the cation A⁺ and the likely crystal structure type are:
A. 4, Tetrahedral (ZnS type)
B. 8, Cubic (CsCl type)
C. 3, Trigonal planar
D. 6, Octahedral (NaCl type)
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Memorize the four standard radius ratio ranges: 0.155–0.225 (CN=3, Trigonal), 0.225–0.414 (CN=4, Tetrahedral), 0.414–0.732 (CN=6, Octahedral), 0.732–1.0 (CN=8, Cubic). Since 0.55 lies between 0.414 and 0.732, CN = 6 (Octahedral), structure = NaCl type. Full Solution: The radius ratio rule predicts the coordination number (CN) and structure of ionic crystals based on the relative sizes of cation (r⁺) and anion (r⁻). For a radius ratio r⁺/r⁻ = 0.55: The limiting range for Octahedral coordination (CN=6) is 0.414 to 0.732. Since 0.55 falls within this range, the cation fits into an octahedral void. The corresponding crystal structure for AX type compounds with CN=6 is the Rock Salt (NaCl) structure. Options A (0.225–0.414), B (0.732–1.0), and C (0.155–0.225) correspond to different ranges.
