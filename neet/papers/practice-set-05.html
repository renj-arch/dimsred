Correct Answer: B
v = c/n = 3x10/1.5 = 2x10 m/s.
Q4. [+4, -1] Physics
A particle of mass m is released from rest at a height h above a horizontal surface. If it falls under gravity, and experiences a resistive force proportional to its velocity (F_r = -kv), where k is a positive constant, what is the terminal velocity it approaches?
A. mg/k
B. sqrt(2gh)
C. k/mg
D. mgh/k
Correct Answer: A
Terminal velocity is reached when the net force on the particle is zero. The forces acting on the particle are gravity (mg downwards) and the resistive force (-kv upwards). Thus, mg - kv_t = 0, which gives v_t = mg/k.

Q9. [+4, -1] Chemistry
The catalyst used in the contact process for HSO is:
A. VO
B. Fe
C. Ni
D. Pt
Correct Answer: A
Contact process uses VO (vanadium pentoxide) as catalyst.
Q10. [+4, -1] Physics
A convex lens of focal length 20 cm is placed in contact with a concave lens of focal length 30 cm. The focal length of the combination is:
A.
B.
C.
D.
Correct Answer: C
For a combination of thin lenses in contact, the reciprocal of the equivalent focal length (F) is given by 1/F = 1/f + 1/f. Given f = +20 cm (convex lens) and f = -30 cm (concave lens). So, 1/F = 1/20 + 1/(-30) = 1/20 - 1/30. To combine, find a common denominator, which is 60. 1/F = (3/60) - (2/60) = 1/60. Therefore, F = +60 cm.
Q11. [+4, -1] Physics
A lens of power +2 D has focal length:
A. 0.5 m
B. 0.5 m
C. 2 m
D. 5 m
Correct Answer: B
f = 1/P = 1/2 = 0.5 m. Positive power -> convex lens.
Q12. [+4, -1] Physics
The de Broglie wavelength of an electron accelerated through a potential difference V is given by:
A. h / sqrt(mV)
B. h / sqrt(2meV)
C. h / sqrt(2mVe)
D. h / sqrt(V/2me)
Correct Answer: B
When an electron is accelerated through a potential difference V, it gains kinetic energy KE = eV. The kinetic energy is also given by KE = p^2 / 2m, where p is the momentum and m is the mass of the electron. Therefore, p = sqrt(2mKE) = sqrt(2meV). The de Broglie wavelength is given by = h/p. Substituting the expression for p, we get = h / sqrt(2meV).
Q13. [+4, -1] Physics
The energy equivalent of 1 amu is:
A. 931 eV
B. 931 MeV
C. 931 keV
D. 931 J
Correct Answer: B
1 amu = 931.5 MeV/c^2.
Q14. [+4, -1] Chemistry
What is the type of bond that occurs between two atoms with a large difference in electronegativity?
A. Covalent bond
B. Ionic bond
C. Hydrogen bond
D. Van der Waals bond
Correct Answer: B
Ionic bonds occur between two atoms with a large difference in electronegativity, resulting in the transfer of electrons and the formation of ions.
Q15. [+4, -1] Biology
The process of cell division for growth is:
A. Meiosis
B. Mitosis
C. Binary fission
D. Budding
Correct Answer: B
Mitosis produces identical daughter cells for growth and repair.
