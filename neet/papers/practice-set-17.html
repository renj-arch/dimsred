Q2. Chemistrymodel-generated · 2026-11-19
Which of the following compounds will NOT undergo aldol condensation but WILL undergo Cannizzaro reaction?
A. Acetaldehyde
B. Propanone
C. Butanal
D. Benzaldehyde
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Aldol condensation requires α-hydrogen; Cannizzaro reaction occurs for non-enolizable aldehydes (no α-H). Benzaldehyde (C₆H₅CHO) has no α-H. Full Solution: Acetaldehyde (CH₃CHO), Propanone (CH₃COCH₃), and Butanal (CH₃CH₂CH₂CHO) possess α-hydrogens and undergo aldol condensation. Benzaldehyde lacks α-hydrogens, so it cannot form an enolate for aldol but undergoes Cannizzaro reaction (disproportionation) in concentrated alkali.

Correct Answer: A

A Carnot engine operates between a hot reservoir at 500 K and a cold reservoir at 300 K. If the temperature of the cold reservoir is increased by 50 K while the hot reservoir temperature remains unchanged, the new efficiency of the engine becomes:
A. 0.30
B. 0.35
C. 0.40
D. 0.45
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Efficiency η = 1 - Tc/Th. New Tc = 350 K, Th = 500 K → η = 1 - 350/500 = 150/500 = 0.30. Step-by-step: Original η = 1 - 300/500 = 0.40. After increase, Tc = 350 K, so η_new = (500-350)/500 = 150/500 = 0.30.

For a first-order reaction, the time required for 75% completion is 60 minutes. The time required for 93.75% completion (in minutes) is:
A. 90
B. 120
C. 150
D. 100
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: 75% completion = 2 half-lives (t₁/₂). 93.75% completion = 4 half-lives. Given 2 t₁/₂ = 60 min → t₁/₂ = 30 min → 4 t₁/₂ = 120 min. Full Solution: For 1st order, fraction remaining = (1/2)ⁿ. 75% done → 25% left = (1/2)² → n=2. 93.75% done → 6.25% left = (1/2)⁴ → n=4. Time is proportional to number of half-lives. t₉₃.₇₅% = (4/2) × 60 = 120 min.

The electric field at a point on the axial line of a short dipole at distance r from its centre is E. If the distance is doubled to 2r, the electric field becomes:
A. E/2
B. E/4
C. E/8
D. E/16
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Axial field of a dipole varies as 1/r³. Doubling r reduces field by factor 2³ = 8 → E_new = E/8. Step-by-step: E ∝ 1/r³ ⇒ E_new/E = (r/(2r))³ = (1/2)³ = 1/8 ⇒ E_new = E/8.
