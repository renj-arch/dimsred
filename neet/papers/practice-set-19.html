Q6. Chemistrymodel-generated · 2026-11-23
Which of the following molecules has a net dipole moment of zero?
A. NH₃
B. H₂O
C. BF₃
D. SO₂
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: A molecule has zero net dipole moment if its geometry is symmetrical and all terminal atoms are identical. BF₃ is trigonal planar (sp² hybridised, no lone pairs on B) with three identical B–F bonds; the bond dipoles cancel vectorially. NH₃ (pyramidal), H₂O (bent), and SO₂ (bent) have lone pairs causing asymmetry and net dipole moments. Step-by-step: Determine hybridisation and shape using VSEPR. BF₃: Steric number 3, trigonal planar, symmetrical → μ = 0. NH₃: Steric number 4 (1 lp), pyramidal → μ > 0. H₂O: Steric number 4 (2 lp), bent → μ > 0. SO₂: Steric number 3 (1 lp), bent → μ > 0.

Correct Answer: D

Light of frequency 1.5ν₀ is incident on a photosensitive metal with threshold frequency ν₀. The stopping potential is measured as V₀. If the frequency of incident light is changed to 2.5ν₀, the new stopping potential will be:
A. 2V₀
B. 3V₀
C. 4V₀
D. 1.5V₀
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Einstein's equation eVₛ = h(ν - ν₀). Stopping potential is proportional to (ν - ν₀). Ratio = (2.5ν₀ - ν₀) / (1.5ν₀ - ν₀) = 1.5ν₀ / 0.5ν₀ = 3. New Vₛ = 3V₀. Full Solution: eV₀ = h(0.5ν₀). For ν = 2.5ν₀, eVₛ' = h(1.5ν₀) = 3 × h(0.5ν₀) = 3eV₀ ⇒ Vₛ' = 3V₀.
