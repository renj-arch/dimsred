A linear DNA fragment of 5 kb is completely digested with the restriction enzyme EcoRI (recognition site: 5'-GAATTC-3'). The digestion yields three fragments of sizes 2 kb, 2 kb, and 1 kb. How many EcoRI recognition sites were present in the original linear fragment?
A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For LINEAR DNA: Number of Sites = Number of Fragments - 1. (For Circular DNA: Sites = Fragments). Here, 3 fragments → 3 - 1 = 2 sites. Worked Solution: A linear DNA molecule has two ends. Each cut at a restriction site increases the number of fragments by one. Starting with 1 fragment (uncut), 1 cut produces 2 fragments, and 2 cuts produce 3 fragments. Since the digestion produced 3 fragments (2 kb + 2 kb + 1 kb = 5 kb), exactly 2 cuts occurred, indicating 2 recognition sites for EcoRI in the original fragment.

B. 2f₀

Correct Answer: A

One mole of a monoatomic ideal gas (γ = 5/3) undergoes an adiabatic expansion until its volume becomes 8 times the initial volume. The final temperature T_f as a fraction of the initial temperature T_i is:
A. 1/2
B. 1/8
C. 1/16
D. 1/4
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: For adiabatic process TV^(γ-1) = constant. With γ = 5/3, γ-1 = 2/3. Volume ratio V_f/V_i = 8 ⇒ T_f/T_i = (V_i/V_f)^(γ-1) = (1/8)^(2/3) = (2⁻³)^(2/3) = 2⁻² = 1/4. Full solution: T_i V_i^(2/3) = T_f V_f^(2/3) → T_f = T_i (V_i/V_f)^(2/3) = T_i (1/8)^(2/3) = T_i/4.

A. Binds to the CAP site upstream of the promoter, facilitating RNA polymerase binding
B. Binds to the operator region, preventing repressor protein binding
C. Acts as an allosteric inducer by binding directly to the repressor protein
D. Is synthesized in high amounts when glucose concentration in the medium is high
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: **CAP-cAMP = **C**atabolite **A**ctivator **P**rotein**. **Low Glucose → High cAMP → CAP-cAMP binds CAP site (upstream) → Helps RNA Pol bind Promoter → Transcription ON**. High Glucose → Low cAMP → CAP inactive → Transcription LOW (Catabolite Repression). This binding induces a bend in the DNA, which facilitates the binding of RNA Polymerase to the promoter, significantly increasing the transcription rate of structural genes (lac Z, Y, A). It does not bind the operator (Option B), nor does it bind the repressor (Option C - allolactose does). Option D is factually incorrect as cAMP levels are *low* when glucose is high.
Q13. Botanymodel-generated · 2026-11-20
A chloroplast suspension is illuminated with light of wavelength greater than 680 nm (activating only Photosystem I) in the absence of an external electron acceptor. Which set of products will be generated?
A. ATP, NADPH, and O₂
B. ATP and NADPH only
C. ATP only
D. NADPH only
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Wavelength > 680 nm → Only PSI active → Cyclic Photophosphorylation → ATP only (No NADPH, No O₂). Worked Solution: Photosystem II (P680) absorbs light < 680 nm. Light > 680 nm excites only Photosystem I (P700). Without PSII activity, water is not split (no O₂, no electrons for NADP+ reduction). Electrons from PSI cycle back via ferredoxin and plastoquinone to P700 (Cyclic flow). This generates a proton gradient for ATP synthesis only. NADPH is not produced as NADP+ reductase requires electrons from the non-cyclic chain.

A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Calculate theoretical ΔTf for i=1: ΔTf = Kf × m. Molality m = (0.5/100) / 0.05 = 0.1 m. ΔTf = 1.86 × 0.1 = 0.186 K. Matches observed ΔTf exactly, so i = 1. Step-by-step: Moles of solute = 0.5 g / 100 g mol⁻¹ = 0.005 mol. Mass of solvent = 50 g = 0.05 kg. Molality = 0.005 / 0.05 = 0.1 mol kg⁻¹. Observed ΔTf = 0.186 K. Using ΔTf = i × Kf × m → 0.186 = i × 1.86 × 0.1 → i = 0.186 / 0.186 = 1.
