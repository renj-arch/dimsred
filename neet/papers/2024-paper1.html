v = p/m = 20/5 = 4 m/s.

A. Q1/W
B. Q2/W

D. (Q1-Q2)/W
Correct Answer: B
COP = Q2/W where Q2 is heat extracted from cold reservoir and W is work input.
Q10. [+4, -1] Physics
The reason of mirage in desert is:
A. Reflection
B. Total internal reflection
C. Diffraction
D. Interference
Correct Answer: B
Mirage occurs due to total internal reflection of light in layers of different refractive indices.
Q11. [+4, -1] Chemistry

C. Group 16

Correct Answer: B

Q12. [+4, -1] Chemistry
Which of the following shows paramagnetism?
A. H2
B. N2
C. O2
D. F2
Correct Answer: C
O2 has two unpaired electrons in its MO configuration ? paramagnetic.
Q13. [+4, -1] Chemistry
The IUPAC name of CH3COOH is:
A. Formic acid
B. Ethanoic acid
C. Acetic acid
D. Methanoic acid
Correct Answer: B
CH3COOH ? 2 carbons ? Ethanoic acid (common: acetic acid).
Q14. [+4, -1] Chemistry
The shape of NH3 molecule is:
A. Trigonal planar
B. Trigonal pyramidal
C. Tetrahedral
D. Linear
Correct Answer: B
NH3: 3 bond pairs + 1 lone pair ? trigonal pyramidal shape.
Q15. [+4, -1] Chemistry
Which of the following is NOT a polymer?
A. Nylon
B. Cellulose
C. Glucose
D. PVC
Correct Answer: C
Glucose is a monomer (monosaccharide), not a polymer. Nylon, cellulose, PVC are polymers.
Q16. [+4, -1] Chemistry
In a redox reaction, oxidation involves:
A. Loss of electrons
B. Gain of electrons
C. Gain of hydrogen
D. Loss of oxygen
Correct Answer: A
Oxidation = loss of electrons, Reduction = gain of electrons (OIL RIG).
Q17. [+4, -1] Chemistry
The bond angle in water is approximately:
A. 90°
B. 109.5°
C. 104.5°
D. 120°
Correct Answer: C
Water has bent shape with 104.5° angle due to lone pair repulsion.
Q18. [+4, -1] Chemistry
Which of these is a double displacement reaction?
A. 2H2 + O2 ? 2H2O
B. NaCl + AgNO3 ? AgCl + NaNO3
C. Zn + H2SO4 ? ZnSO4 + H2
D. CaCO3 ? CaO + CO2
Correct Answer: B
Double displacement: two compounds exchange ions ? new compounds.
Q19. [+4, -1] Chemistry
The number of moles in 11 g of CO2 is:
A. 0.5
B. 0.25
C. 1
D. 2
Correct Answer: B
Molar mass CO2 = 44 g/mol. n = 11/44 = 0.25.
Q20. [+4, -1] Chemistry
The catalyst used in the contact process for H2SO4 is:
A. V2O5
B. Fe
C. Ni
D. Pt
Correct Answer: A
Contact process uses V2O5 (vanadium pentoxide) as catalyst.
Q21. [+4, -1] Biology
Which vitamin is water soluble?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin D
C. Vitamin C
D. Vitamin K
Correct Answer: C
Vitamin C and B-complex are water-soluble. A, D, E, K are fat-soluble.
Q22. [+4, -1] Biology
The largest gland in the human body is:
A. Pancreas
B. Liver
C. Salivary gland
D. Thyroid
Correct Answer: B
Liver is the largest gland (~1.5 kg) and performs 500+ functions.
Q23. [+4, -1] Biology
Which of these is a prokaryote?
A. Amoeba
B. Bacteria
C. Yeast
D. Paramecium
Correct Answer: B
Bacteria are prokaryotes (no membrane-bound nucleus/organelles).
Q24. [+4, -1] Biology
The universal donor blood group is:
A. A
B. B
C. AB
D. O
Correct Answer: D
O negative is universal donor (no A/B antigens, no Rh factor).
Q25. [+4, -1] Biology
The respiratory pigment in human blood is:
A. Chlorophyll
B. Hemoglobin
C. Melanin
D. Myoglobin
Correct Answer: B
Hemoglobin in RBCs transports O2 from lungs to tissues.
Q26. [+4, -1] Biology
Which part of the brain controls balance and coordination?
A. Cerebrum
B. Cerebellum
C. Medulla
D. Hypothalamus
Correct Answer: B
Cerebellum coordinates voluntary movements, balance, and posture.
Q27. [+4, -1] Biology
The number of chromosomes in human cells is:
A. 23
B. 46
C. 48
D. 44
Correct Answer: B
Humans have 23 pairs = 46 chromosomes (22 autosomes + 1 sex chromosome pair).
Q28. [+4, -1] Biology
The disease caused by deficiency of insulin is:
A. Goiter
B. Diabetes mellitus
C. Anemia
D. Rickets
Correct Answer: B
Diabetes mellitus is caused by insufficient insulin production or insulin resistance.
Q29. [+4, -1] Biology
The DNA structure was discovered by:
A. Mendel
B. Darwin
C. Watson and Crick
D. Pasteur
Correct Answer: C
Watson and Crick proposed the double helix structure of DNA in 1953.
Q30. [+4, -1] Biology
Which chamber of the heart pumps blood to the lungs?
A. Left atrium
B. Left ventricle
C. Right atrium
D. Right ventricle
Correct Answer: D
Right ventricle pumps deoxygenated blood to lungs via pulmonary artery.
