A buffer solution is prepared by mixing 100 mL of 0.1 M CH₃COOH (pKa = 4.74) and 100 mL of 0.1 M CH₃COONa. To this solution, 1 mmol of solid NaOH is added. The new pH of the buffer is approximately: (Assume volume change negligible)
A. 4.66
B. 4.74
C. 4.82
D. 4.90
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Initial mmol acid = 10, salt = 10. Adding 1 mmol OH⁻ converts 1 mmol acid to salt. New acid = 9 mmol, salt = 11 mmol. pH = pKa + log(11/9) = 4.74 + log(1.222) ≈ 4.74 + 0.087 = 4.827 ≈ 4.83. Option C (4.82) is closest. Full Solution: Henderson-Hasselbalch equation: pH = pKa + log([Salt]/[Acid]). Since total volume is constant, concentration ratio equals mole ratio. Initial moles: CH₃COOH = 10 mmol, CH₃COO⁻ = 10 mmol. NaOH (1 mmol) reacts with CH₃COOH: CH₃COOH + OH⁻ → CH₃COO⁻ + H₂O. Final moles: CH₃COOH = 9 mmol, CH₃COO⁻ = 11 mmol. pH = 4.74 + log(11/9) = 4.74 + 0.087 = 4.827.

In a large, randomly mating population in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, the incidence of an autosomal recessive disorder is 1 in 2500 individuals. What is the approximate frequency of heterozygous carriers in this population?
A. 1/25
B. 1/50
C. 1/100
D. 1/125
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: For rare recessive disorders (q² << 1), p ≈ 1. Carrier frequency (2pq) ≈ 2q = 2√(q²). Here q² = 1/2500, so q = 1/50. Approximate carrier frequency = 2 × (1/50) = 1/25. Step-by-step: Given q² = 1/2500. Calculate q = √(1/2500) = 1/50. Calculate p = 1 - q = 1 - 1/50 = 49/50. Carrier frequency (2pq) = 2 × (49/50) × (1/50) = 98/2500 = 49/1250 ≈ 0.0392. 1/25 = 0.04. The approximation 2q = 1/25 = 0.04 is very close and the standard exam shortcut.

The de Broglie wavelength of an electron in the nth Bohr orbit of a hydrogen atom is λₙ. What is the ratio λ₃ : λ₁?
A. 1:3
B. 3:1
C. 1:9
D. 9:1
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: In Bohr's model, the circumference of the nth orbit equals nλₙ (2πrₙ = nλₙ). Since radius rₙ ∝ n², we get λₙ ∝ n. Thus λ₃/λ₁ = 3/1. Full Solution: Bohr's quantization condition: mvr = nh/2π. de Broglie wavelength λ = h/mv = h/p. Substituting p = mv = nh/2πr gives λ = 2πr/n. For hydrogen, rₙ = n²a₀. Therefore λₙ = 2πn²a₀/n = 2πna₀ ∝ n. Hence λ₃ : λ₁ = 3 : 1.
