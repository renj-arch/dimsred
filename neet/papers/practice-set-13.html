Q1. Zoology
Which of the following hormones acts as a counter-regulatory hormone to insulin by raising blood glucose levels?
A. Insulin
B. Glucagon
C. Thyroxine
D. Melatonin
Correct Answer: B
Glucagon is secreted by the pancreas when blood sugar is low and stimulates glycogen breakdown to raise glucose levels. Insulin lowers blood sugar; Thyroxine regulates metabolism but does not directly oppose insulin's immediate effect on glucose; Melatonin regulates sleep cycles.
Q2. Botany
How many ATP molecules are generated by substrate-level phosphorylation in the Krebs cycle per molecule of glucose oxidised aerobically?
A. 4
B. 2
C. 6
D. 8
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: One glucose yields 2 Acetyl CoA. Each Krebs cycle turn produces 1 GTP (≡ ATP) at succinyl-CoA synthetase step. Total = 2 turns × 1 ATP = 2 ATP. Worked: Glycolysis gives 2 ATP (substrate-level), Krebs gives 2 GTP (substrate-level), and the bulk (~32-34 ATP) comes from oxidative phosphorylation via NADH/FADH₂.
Q3. Chemistry
For a reaction, ΔH = +50 kJ mol⁻¹ and ΔS = +150 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹. The minimum temperature (in K) above which the reaction becomes spontaneous is:
A. 333 K
B. 33.3 K
C. 300 K
D. 500 K
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Spontaneity requires ΔG < 0. At equilibrium ΔG = 0 = ΔH - TΔS → T = ΔH/ΔS. Convert ΔH to J: 50,000 J mol⁻¹. T = 50,000 / 150 = 333.33 K. Reaction is spontaneous for T > 333 K. Full Solution: ΔG = ΔH - TΔS. For spontaneity, ΔG < 0 ⇒ TΔS > ΔH ⇒ T > ΔH/ΔS. ΔH = 50 kJ mol⁻¹ = 50,000 J mol⁻¹. ΔS = 150 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹. T = 50,000 J mol⁻¹ / 150 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹ = 1000/3 K ≈ 333.33 K.
Q4. Physics
The radius of the first Bohr orbit of hydrogen atom is a₀. The radius of the second orbit (n=2) of Li²⁺ ion (Z=3) is:
A. (4/3) a₀
B. (3/4) a₀
C. (2/3) a₀
D. (9/4) a₀
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Bohr radius r_n ∝ n²/Z. For H (n=1, Z=1) r=a₀. For Li²⁺ (n=2, Z=3) r = a₀ × (2²/3) / (1²/1) = 4a₀/3. Full Solution: Using the formula r_n = (n²/Z) a₀, substituting n=2 and Z=3 for Li²⁺ gives r = (4/3) a₀.
Q5. Botany
In a dihybrid cross between two heterozygous parents (AaBb × AaBb), the F₂ population consists of 1600 plants. How many plants are expected to be homozygous dominant for both traits (AABB)?
A. 100
B. 200
C. 300
D. 400
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The F₂ phenotypic ratio is 9:3:3:1 (sum = 16 parts). The genotype AABB constitutes 1 part out of 16. Number = 1600 ÷ 16 = 100. Worked: A 4×4 Punnett square yields 16 genotypic combinations; AABB appears only once. Thus, frequency = 1/16. 1/16 × 1600 = 100 plants.
Q6. Physics
In a photoelectric effect experiment, the stopping potential (V_s) is plotted against the frequency (ν) of incident light. The slope of the straight line obtained gives:
A. Work function (φ)
B. Planck's constant (h)
C. Ratio h/e
D. Threshold frequency (ν₀)
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Einstein's equation eV_s = hν - φ rearranges to V_s = (h/e)ν - φ/e; the slope is the coefficient of ν, which is h/e. Full Solution: The graph of stopping potential versus frequency is linear with slope h/e and intercept -φ/e on the V_s axis, directly yielding the ratio of Planck's constant to electronic charge.

Q8. Zoology
Which of the following organisms exhibits a life cycle with distinct larval and adult stages involving metamorphosis?
A. Earthworm
B. Frog
C. Snake
D. Shark
Correct Answer: B
Frogs undergo complete metamorphosis from tadpole to adult frog. Earthworms, snakes (reptiles), and sharks generally do not exhibit such distinct metamorphic stages in their life cycles.
Q9. Chemistry
The molar conductivity of a 0.01 M solution of a weak electrolyte is 20 S cm² mol⁻¹. If its limiting molar conductivity (Λ°_m) is 400 S cm² mol⁻¹, the degree of dissociation (α) is:
A. 0.005
B. 0.05
C. 0.5
D. 0.02
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: For weak electrolytes, degree of dissociation α = Λ_m / Λ°_m (Ostwald's dilution law approximation). α = 20 / 400 = 0.05. Full Solution: The relationship between molar conductivity at concentration 'c' (Λ_m) and limiting molar conductivity (Λ°_m) for a weak electrolyte is given by α = Λ_m / Λ°_m. Substituting values: α = 20 S cm² mol⁻¹ / 400 S cm² mol⁻¹ = 1/20 = 0.05.
Q10. Physics
A force of 10 N acts on a body of mass 2 kgf or 5 s. The impulse is:
A. 10 Ns
B. 50 Ns
C. 2 Ns
D. 25 Ns
Correct Answer: B
Impulse = F × t = 10 × 5 = 50 Ns.
Q11. Botany
In a test cross between a heterozygous tall pea plant (Tt) and a homozygous dwarf plant (tt), 240 offspring are produced. As per the table below showing the expected phenotypic ratio, how many dwarf phenotype plants are expected?
A. 60
B. 120
C. 180
D. 240
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: A test cross (Tt × tt) always yields a 1:1 phenotypic ratio. Simply halve the total offspring (240/2 = 120). Step-by-step: The heterozygous parent produces gametes T and t (50% each). The recessive parent produces only t gametes. Offspring genotypes: Tt (tall) and tt (dwarf) in equal proportion. 50% of 240 = 120 dwarf plants.
Q12. Chemistry
The half-life of a first-order reaction is 20 minutes. The time required for the concentration of the reactant to drop to 12.5% of its initial value is:
A. 20 minutes
B. 40 minutes
C. 60 minutes
D. 80 minutes
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: 12.5% = 1/8 = (1/2)³, meaning 3 half-lives have passed. Time = 3 × 20 min = 60 min. Full Solution: For a first-order reaction, fraction remaining = (1/2)ⁿ where n = number of half-lives. (1/2)ⁿ = 1/8 = (1/2)³ ⇒ n = 3. Total time t = n × t₁/₂ = 3 × 20 min = 60 min.
Q13. Zoology
Which of the following blood groups is considered the universal donor for red blood cell transfusions?
A. A+
B. B-
C. AB+
D. O-
Correct Answer: D
Blood group O-negative lacks A, B, and Rh antigens on the red blood cells, making it safe to transfuse into recipients of any blood type. A+ has A antigen and Rh factor; B- has B antigen; AB+ has both A and B antigens, limiting their compatibility.
Q14. Botany
If 250 microspore mother cells in an anther undergo meiosis successfully, how many viable pollen grains will be formed?
A. 250
B. 500
C. 750
D. 1000
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: 1 Microspore Mother Cell (2n) → Meiosis → 4 haploid microspores (pollen grains). No degeneration in male gametogenesis. Total = 250 × 4 = 1000. Worked: Microsporogenesis involves one meiotic division (Meiosis I & II) producing a tetrad of 4 microspores. Each develops into a pollen grain. Contrast with megasporogenesis where 3 of 4 megaspores degenerate.
Q15. Physics
A series LCR circuit has a resonant frequency ω₀ = 1000 rad/s and a quality factor Q = 50. The bandwidth (Δω) of the circuit is:
A. 50 rad/s
B. 200 rad/s
C. 50000 rad/s
D. 20 rad/s
Correct Answer: D
Shortcut: Bandwidth Δω = ω₀/Q = 1000/50 = 20 rad/s. Full Solution: By definition, the quality factor for a series resonant circuit is Q = ω₀/Δω. Rearranging gives Δω = ω₀/Q = 1000/50 = 20 rad/s.
