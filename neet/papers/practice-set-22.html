Correct Answer: A

EPP spreads -> Voltage-gated Na+ channels open -> Muscle Action Potential -> T-tubules -> SR Ca2+ release. Option A describes Step 3-4 directly.

Q4. Botany

B. Hydrogen
C. Oxygen
D. Nitrogen
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Nitrogenase is extremely O2-labile; leghemoglobin in root nodules maintains low O2 concentration. Eliminate CO2, H2, N2 as they do not inactivate nitrogenase. Q5. Zoology
What is the primary function of the stomata found on the epidermis of plant leaves?
A. Gas exchange and transpiration
B. Photosynthesis only
C. Water absorption from soil
D. Defense against herbivores
Correct Answer: A
Stomata are microscopic pores that open to allow carbon dioxide intake for photosynthesis and release oxygen and water vapor (transpiration). Photosynthesis occurs in chloroplasts within cells, not the pore itself; water absorption is done by roots; defense involves trichomes or chemical compounds.
Q6. Chemistry
An element crystallizes in an FCC lattice with an atomic mass of 60 g mol⁻¹ and a density of 6.23 g cm⁻³. The approximate edge length of the unit cell is: (Given Nₐ = 6.02 × 10²³ mol⁻¹)
A. 200 pm
B. 300 pm
C. 400 pm
D. 500 pm
Correct Answer: C
Shortcut: Use the density formula ρ = (Z × M) / (Nₐ × a³). For FCC, Z = 4. Rearrange for a³ = (4 × 60) / (6.23 × 6.02 × 10²³). Approximate denominator: 6.23 × 6.02 ≈ 37.5. Numerator = 240. a³ ≈ 240 / 37.5 × 10⁻²³ = 6.4 × 10⁻²³ cm³. Cube root of 6.4 ≈ 1.86 (since 1.8³=5.8, 1.9³=6.86), so a ≈ 1.86 × 10⁻⁸ cm = 386 pm ≈ 400 pm. Option elimination: 200 pm gives too high density, 500 pm too low. Full Solution: a³ = (Z × M) / (ρ × Nₐ) = (4 × 60 g mol⁻¹) / (6.23 g cm⁻³ × 6.02 × 10²³ mol⁻¹) = 240 / (37.5046 × 10²³) cm³ = 6.399 × 10⁻²³ cm³. a = (6.399 × 10⁻²³)^(1/3) cm = 3.997 × 10⁻⁸ cm = 399.7 pm ≈ 400 pm.

Q10. Chemistry
The correct decreasing order of reactivity towards nucleophilic addition of HCN for the following carbonyl compounds is:
A. Acetone > Acetaldehyde > Benzaldehyde > Benzophenone
B. Acetaldehyde > Benzaldehyde > Acetone > Benzophenone
C. Benzaldehyde > Acetaldehyde > Benzophenone > Acetone
D. Benzophenone > Acetone > Benzaldehyde > Acetaldehyde
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Reactivity order: Aldehydes > Ketones (steric + electronic). Among aldehydes: Aliphatic (Acetaldehyde) > Aromatic (Benzaldehyde) because -R effect of phenyl reduces electrophilicity. Among ketones: Aliphatic (Acetone) > Aromatic (Benzophenone) due to steric hindrance and -R effect of two phenyl groups. Eliminate options starting with Ketone (A, D). Between B and C: Acetaldehyde is more reactive than Benzaldehyde. Option B matches. Full Solution: Nucleophilic addition reactivity depends on +I effect (decreases reactivity), -I/-R effect (increases), and steric hindrance (decreases). Acetaldehyde (CH₃CHO) has one electron-donating alkyl group and small H. Benzaldehyde (C₆H₅CHO) has phenyl with -I but +R (resonance donation dominates, decreasing δ⁺ on carbonyl C) and is bulkier. Acetone (CH₃COCH₃) has two alkyl groups (+I, more steric). Benzophenone ((C₆H₅)₂CO) has two phenyl groups (max steric, resonance donation). Order: CH₃CHO > C₆H₅CHO > CH₃COCH₃ > (C₆H₅)₂CO.

Q12. Botany
In a dihybrid cross between two heterozygous parents (AaBb × AaBb), the F₂ population consists of 1600 plants. How many plants are expected to be homozygous dominant for both traits (AABB)?
A. 100
B. 200
C. 300
D. 400
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: The F₂ phenotypic ratio is 9:3:3:1 (sum = 16 parts). The genotype AABB constitutes 1 part out of 16. Number = 1600 ÷ 16 = 100. Worked: A 4×4 Punnett square yields 16 genotypic combinations; AABB appears only once. Thus, frequency = 1/16. 1/16 × 1600 = 100 plants.

Q14. Botany
Which of the following is the sole product of cyclic photophosphorylation in chloroplasts?
A. ATP and NADPH
B. ATP only
C. NADPH only
D. O2 and ATP
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Cyclic photophosphorylation involves only Photosystem I, generating a proton gradient for ATP synthesis without splitting water or reducing NADP+. Thus NADPH and O2 are not produced. Q15. Zoology
The Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase pump maintains the resting membrane potential in a mammalian neuron. For each ATP molecule hydrolyzed, what is the correct ratio of Na⁺ ions ejected out of the cell to K⁺ ions imported into the cell?
A. 2:3
B. 3:2
C. 1:1
D. 2:1
Correct Answer: B
Shortcut: Use the '3-2-1' mnemonic (3 Na⁺ out, 2 K⁺ in, 1 ATP). The pump is electrogenic (net +1 charge out), so Na⁺ exported must exceed K⁺ imported. Only option B (3:2) satisfies this. Full Solution: The Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase is a P-type ATPase that actively transports 3 Na⁺ ions out of the cell and 2 K⁺ ions into the cell per ATP hydrolyzed. This 3:2 stoichiometry creates the electrochemical gradient essential for action potentials and secondary active transport.
