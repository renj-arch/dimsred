In a series RL circuit, the resistance is 3 Ω and the inductive reactance is 4 Ω. The power factor of the circuit is:
A. 0.6
B. 0.8
C. 0.5
D. 1.0
Correct Answer: A

Q8. Zoologymodel-generated · 2026-11-07
The process by which water evaporates from the leaves of a plant, creating a suction force that pulls water up from the roots, is called:
A. Translocation
B. Photosynthesis
C. Transpiration
D. Osmosis
Correct Answer: C
Transpiration is the evaporation of water from plant surfaces (mainly stomata) which generates negative pressure to pull water upward. Translocation refers to sugar transport; photosynthesis converts light to chemical energy; osmosis is passive water movement across a membrane.

A circular loop of radius R carries a current I. The magnetic field at its centre is B. If the current is doubled and the radius is halved, the new magnetic field at the centre becomes:
A. 4B
B. 2B
C. 8B
D. B/2
Correct Answer: A
Shortcut: Magnetic field at centre of a loop is proportional to I/R. Doubling I and halving R multiplies B by 2/(1/2)=4. Full solution: B = μ₀I/(2R). New B = μ₀(2I)/(2(R/2)) = μ₀(2I)/R = 4 × μ₀I/(2R) = 4B.
