Q4. [+4, -1] Physics
The coefficient of performance of a refrigerator is given by:
A. Q/W
B. Q/W
C. W/Q
D. (Q-Q)/W
Correct Answer: B
COP = Q/W where Q is heat extracted from cold reservoir and W is work input.
