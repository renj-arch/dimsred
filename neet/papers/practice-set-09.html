Q13. Chemistry
Which of the following reagents can be used to distinguish between propanal and propanone?
A. Tollen's reagent
B. Fehling's solution
C. Benedict's solution
D. All of the above
Correct Answer: D
Propanal is an aldehyde (CHCHCHO), and propanone is a ketone (CHCOCH). Aldehydes are generally more easily oxidized than ketones. They can reduce mild oxidizing agents, while ketones typically cannot (except under harsh conditions or specific structural features like alpha-hydroxy ketones). 1. **Tollen's reagent (ammoniacal silver nitrate):** Aldehydes reduce Tollen's reagent to metallic silver, forming a 'silver mirror' on the test tube. Ketones do not react. 2. **Fehling's solution (alkaline solution of copper(II) sulfate complexed with tartrate):** Aldehydes reduce the blue Cu^2 ions to red precipitate of cuprous oxide (CuO). Ketones do not react. 3. **Benedict's solution (alkaline solution of copper(II) sulfate complexed with citrate):** Similar to Fehling's solution, aldehydes reduce the blue Cu^2 ions to red precipitate of cuprous oxide. Ketones do not react. Since propanal (an aldehyde) will give a positive test with all three reagents, while propanone (a ketone) will not, all of them can be used to distinguish between the two compounds.

Q15. Physics
A particle of mass 'm' is projected with velocity 'v' at an angle '' with the horizontal. The magnitude of the change in momentum of the particle from the time of projection till it reaches the maximum height is:
A. mv cos
B. mv sin
C.
D.
Correct Answer: B
At the time of projection, the momentum components are Px = mv cos (horizontal) and Py = mv sin (vertical). At the maximum height, the vertical component of velocity becomes zero, so the vertical component of momentum is zero. The horizontal component of momentum remains mv cos. Change in momentum (P) = Final momentum - Initial momentum. P_x = mv cos - mv cos = 0. P_y = 0 - mv sin = -mv sin. The magnitude of the change in momentum is |P_y| = mv sin.
